Theme A · India and the World: Land and the People

Locating Places on the Earth

Map, latitude-longitude aur time zones — dharti par kisi bhi jagah ka 'address' nikalna.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Naye shehar mein raasta kaise dhoondhoge?

Socho tum pehli baar kisi naye shehar ke station par utre ho aur bank jaana hai. Kisi se poochh sakte ho — ya ek map (naksha) dekh sakte ho! Map ek khazaane ka naksha jaisa hai: batata hai kaunsi cheez kahan hai aur wahan kaise pahunchein. Map mein hum zameen ko aise dekhte hain jaise upar se (chidiya ki tarah) dekh rahe hon. Bahut saare maps ki kitaab ko atlas kehte hain.

Teen tarah ke map

Physical map — prakriti ki cheezein: pahad, nadi, samundar. Political map — insaan ki banaayi seemaayein: desh, rajya, raajdhani, shehar. Thematic map — kisi ek khaas jaankaari ke liye, jaise barish kahan kitni hoti hai, ya kaunsi fasal kahan ugti hai.

Map ke 3 zaroori hisse — doori, disha, nishaan

1. Scale (doori) — itna bada shehar chhote kaagaz par kaise aa gaya? Scale se! Agar scale 1 cm = 500 m hai, to map par 2 cm ki doori asal mein 1,000 m (1 km) hogi. 2. Disha (direction) — upar North, phir ghadi ki disha mein East, South, West — ye 4 cardinal directions. Beech wali 4: NE, SE, SW, NW. Zyaadatar map par sirf ek teer 'N' bana hota hai jo North batata hai. 3. Symbols (nishaan) — har building ka chitra banana mumkin nahi, isliye chhote nishaan: school, post office, railway line, nadi. India ke liye ye nishaan Survey of India (sarkari sanstha) ne tay kiye hain.

Dharti gol hai — to globe kyon?

Dharti lagbhag ek gend (sphere) jaisi hai, bas dhruvon (poles) par thodi chapti. Gol cheez ko chapte kaagaz par dikhaane mein thoda bigaad aata hai, isliye globe (gol map) zyada sahi hota hai. Par globe jeb mein nahi aata, isliye hum chapte map bhi use karte hain.

Latitude — dharti par khinchi 'aadi' lakeerein

Dharti ke beech mein ek kalpanik lakeer socho — Equator (bhoomadhya rekha), 0° — ye sabse bada gola hai aur dharti ko uttari aur dakshini golaardh mein baant-ta hai. Iske upar-neeche aur gol lakeerein — parallels of latitude — jo dhruvon ki taraf chhoti hoti jaati hain. North Pole 90°N, South Pole 90°S. Latitude batata hai ki koi jagah Equator se kitni door hai. Isse mausam bhi samajh aata hai: Equator ke paas garam (torrid), beech mein samsheetoshn (temperate), dhruvon ke paas bahut thanda (frigid).

Longitude — upar se neeche khinchi lakeerein

Ab North Pole se South Pole tak aadhe gole socho — inhe meridians of longitude kehte hain; sab ki lambaai barabar hoti hai. Inki ginti Prime Meridian (0°) se hoti hai jo London ke Greenwich se guzarti hai (1884 mein tay hua). Phir 0° se 180°E aur 180°W. Latitude + longitude milkar ek pata (address) bante hain — jaise Delhi ≈ 29°N, 77°E. Mazedaar baat: Bharat ki apni prime meridian thi — 'Madhya Rekha', Ujjayini (Ujjain) se, jahan ~1,500 saal pehle khagolshaastri Varahamihira kaam karte the. India 8°N–37°N aur 68°E–97°E ke beech faila hai.

Samay kaise badalta hai? — 1° = 4 minute

Dharti 24 ghante mein ek chakkar (360°) lagaati hai. To 360 ÷ 24 = 15° har ghante, yani 1° = 4 minute. Sooraj pehle poorv (East) mein ugta hai, isliye East wali jagahon ka samay aage, West ka peeche. Har shehar apna-apna samay rakhe to gadbad ho jaaye — isliye desh ek Standard Time rakhta hai. IST = GMT + 5 ghante 30 minute. Bade desh mein kai time zone — USA mein 6, Russia mein 11!

International Date Line — ek kadam mein kal ya parson!

180° ke aas-paas ek tedhi-medhi lakeer hai — International Date Line. Ise paar karo to taareekh badal jaati hai: poorv ki taraf (East) jaate hue paar karo to ek din ghatao, pashchim ki taraf (West) jaate hue ek din jodo. Yaani jahaz mein baithe-baithe tumhara Monday, Sunday ban sakta hai!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Map = kisi area ki drawing, upar se dekhi hui (top view). Atlas = maps ka collection (book).
  • Map ke 3 prakaar: Physical (pahad, nadi, samudra), Political (desh, rajya, boundary, capital), Thematic (ek khaas jaankari).
  • Map ke 3 components: Distance (Scale), Direction, Symbols. Example: scale 1 cm = 500 m.
  • 4 cardinal directions: North (upar), East, South, West — clockwise. 4 intermediate: NE, SE, SW, NW. Map par 'N' arrow north dikhata hai.
  • India ke maps ke standard symbols Survey of India (sarkari sanstha) ne fix kiye hain.
  • Earth lagbhag sphere hai — poles par thodi chapti. Globe = sphere par bana map, flat map se zyada sahi.
  • Latitude = Equator se doori. Equator = 0° (sabse bada circle), poles = 90°N aur 90°S. Parallels poles ki taraf chhote hote jaate hain.
  • Latitude aur climate: Equator ke paas torrid (garam), beech mein temperate, poles ke paas frigid (thanda).
  • Longitude = Prime Meridian se doori. Meridians pole se pole tak half-circles, sab ki length barabar. 0° se 180° E/W.
  • Prime Meridian (0°) Greenwich (London) se guzarti hai — 1884 mein tay hua. Delhi ≈ 29°N, 77°E; New York 74°W; Tokyo 140°E.
  • Bharat ki apni prime meridian thi — Madhya Rekha, Ujjayini (Ujjain) se. Astronomer Varahamihira ~1,500 saal pehle wahin kaam karte the.
  • India ka vistaar: latitude 8°N–37°N, longitude 68°E–97°E.
  • Earth 24 ghante mein 360° ghoomti hai → 15° = 1 ghanta, 1° = 4 minute. East mein time aage, West mein peeche.
  • IST = GMT + 5 ghante 30 minute. USA mein 6 time zones, Russia mein 11.
  • International Date Line ≈ 180°. Eastward cross karo → ek din ghatao; Westward cross karo → ek din jodo.

Yaad karne ki trick

LAT = Ladder ki seedhi

Latitude lines seedhi ke 'paidaan' jaisi aadi (horizontal). LONGitude = LONG lines, pole se pole khadi.

15-1-4 formula

15° = 1 ghanta, 1° = 4 minute. Do jagah ka longitude farak × 4 = minute ka farak.

Date Line: 'Pashchim jao, din badhao'

Westward cross → +1 din. Eastward cross → −1 din.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Prime Meridian kis jagah se guzarti hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Greenwich — 1884 mein kai deshon ne Greenwich (London) wali meridian ko 0° longitude maana.

  2. Q2. Indian Standard Time, GMT se kitna aage hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 5 ghante 30 minute — IST = GMT + 5:30. Isliye London mein dopahar 12 baje India mein shaam 5:30 hote hain.

  3. Q3. Pracheen Bharat ki prime meridian 'Madhya Rekha' kis shahar se guzarti thi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Ujjayini — Ujjayini (Ujjain) astronomy ka bada kendra tha; saare Indian astronomical texts isi meridian ko reference maante the.

  4. Q4. Porbandar aur Tinsukia mein longitude ka farak 30° maana jaaye, to local time ka farak kitna hoga?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 2 ghante — 15° = 1 ghanta, to 30° = 2 ghante. Tinsukia (east) mein suraj pehle doobta hai.

  5. Q5. Delhi (29°N) aur Bengaluru (13°N) dono ka longitude ~77°E hai. Unke local time mein farak?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Lagbhag zero — Local time longitude se tay hota hai, latitude se nahi. Longitude same hai to local time lagbhag same.

  6. Q6. International Date Line ko eastward cross karne par kya karte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ek din ghatate hain — Eastward → ek din ghatao (Monday → Sunday). Westward → ek din jodo.
