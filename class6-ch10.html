Theme D · Governance and Democracy

Grassroots Democracy – Part 1: Governance

Niyam kyun, sarkaar kya, uske 3 ang aur 3 star, aur democracy ka arth.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Niyam kyon chahiye?

Socho class mein sab ek saath bolein, ya sadak par sab jahan chahein gaadi chalaayein — gadbad! Jab bahut log saath rehte hain to niyam zaroori hain — ghar, school, sadak, daftar har jagah. Governance = faisle lena, niyam banaana aur unka paalan karwaana. Ye kaam karne wala system = Government (sarkar). Bade aur zaroori niyam = laws (kaanoon) — aur zaroorat padne par badle bhi ja sakte hain.

Ek misaal — cybercrime

Online dhokha (cybercrime) naya apraadh tha. To pehle naya kaanoon bana, phir cyber police ne pakda, aur court ne saza di. Yahan sarkar ke teeno ang ne kaam kiya!

Sarkar ke 3 ang — 3 alag kaam

Legislature (vidhaayika) — kaanoon banaati/badalti hai; janta ke chune pratinidhi. Executive (kaaryapaalika) — kaanoon lagu karti hai; rashtra pramukh, mantri, police. Judiciary (nyaypaalika) — adaalatein; kaanoon toota ya nahi, saza; executive ke faisle aur kaanoon ki jaanch. Ye alag kyon? Taaki koi ek bahut taaqatwar na ho jaaye — separation of powers → checks and balances (har ang doosre par nazar rakhe), jaise cricket mein khiladi, umpire aur third umpire!

Teen star — gaon se desh tak

Local (gaon/shehar), State (rajya), Central/Union (kendra). Baadh ki misaal: chhoti baadh → local sarkar; badi → rajya sarkar; bahut vishaal → kendra (sena, raahat). Kendra ke kaam: raksha (defence), videsh neeti, parmaanu urja, sanchaar, mudra (currency), rajyon ke beech vyapaar, shiksha, rashtriya neeti. Rajya ke kaam: police/kaanoon vyavastha, kendra ke kaanoon lagu karna, swasthya, shiksha, kheti, sinchaai, sthaaniya sarkar.

Kaun kya hai? — desh aur rajya

Desh: Judiciary — Supreme Court; Legislature — Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha; Executive — President (naam-maatra pramukh, sena ke Supreme Commander) aur Prime Minister (asli karyakari pramukh). Rajya: High Court; Vidhan Sabha (zyaadatar rajyon mein ek sadan, kuch mein do); Governor (naam-maatra) aur Chief Minister (asli pramukh). Motto: Bharat Sarkar — 'Satyameva Jayate' (satya ki hi jeet); Supreme Court — 'Yato Dharmastato Jayah' (jahan dharm, wahan vijay).

Kalam Sir ki seekh

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam — janm 1931, Rameswaram (TN); 'Missile Man of India'; 11th President (2002–2007). Unki mazedaar seekh: F.A.I.L. = First Attempt In Learning (pehli koshish seekhne ki), E.N.D. = Effort Never Dies (mehnat kabhi nahi marti), N.O. = Next Opportunity (agla mauka). Yaani haar se mat daro!

Loktantra — janta ka raaj

Democracy = Greek demos (log) + kratos (shaasan) = 'janta ka raaj'. Crore log seedhe raaj nahi chala sakte, isliye hum pratinidhi chunte hain — representative democracy: rajya ke liye MLA, desh ke liye MP. India = duniya ka sabse bada loktantra — 2024 mein ~97 crore voter; 18+ naagrik vote dete hain. Direct democracy — sab seedhe vote karein (class picnic ki jagah haath utha kar tay karna!). Grassroots democracy = aam logon ki bhaagidaari — loktantra ke pyramid ka aadhaar.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Bahut log saath rahein to niyam zaroori — ghar, school, sadak, naukri sab jagah.
  • Governance = faisle lena, niyam banana aur unka paalan karwana. Government = ye kaam karne wala system/samooh. Mahatvapurna niyam = laws. Niyam badle bhi ja sakte hain.
  • Udaharan — cybercrime: naya kanoon bana, cyber police ne pakda, court ne saza di.
  • 3 organs: Legislature — kanoon banana/badalna (janta ke pratinidhi). Executive — kanoon lagu karna (head of state, mantri, police). Judiciary — adaalatein; kanoon toota ya nahi, saza; executive ke faisle aur kanoon ki jaanch.
  • Separation of powers → checks and balances: har ang doosre par nazar rakhe.
  • 3 levels: Local (gaon/shahar), State, Central/Union. Baadh ka udaharan: chhoti → local, badi → State, vishaal → Centre (sena, rahat).
  • Mottos: Bharat Sarkar — 'Satyameva Jayate' (satya ki hi jeet); Supreme Court — 'Yato Dharmastato Jayah' (jahan dharma, wahan vijay).
  • National: Judiciary — Supreme Court; Legislature — Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha; Executive — President (nominal head, Supreme Commander of Armed Forces), Prime Minister (executive head).
  • State: High Court; Vidhan Sabha (zyaadatar rajyon mein ek sadan, kuch mein do); Governor (nominal), Chief Minister (executive head).
  • Kendra ke kaam: defence, foreign affairs, atomic energy, communications, currency, inter-state commerce, education, national policies. Rajya ke kaam: police/law & order, kendriya kanoon lagu karna, public health, education, agriculture, irrigation, local government.
  • Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam: janm 1931, Rameswaram (TN); 'Missile Man of India'; 11th President (2002–2007). F.A.I.L. = First Attempt In Learning; E.N.D. = Effort Never Dies; N.O. = Next Opportunity.
  • Democracy = Greek dēmos (log) + kratos (shaasan) = 'janta ka raaj'. Sab log seedhe raaj nahi kar sakte → representative democracy: MLA (rajya), MP (desh).
  • India = duniya ka sabse bada loktantra — 2024 mein ~97 crore (970 million) voters; 18+ nagrik vote kar sakte hain.
  • Direct democracy: har vyakti seedhe vote kare (class picnic ka haath uthaakar faisla). Grassroots democracy: aam nagrikon ki bhaagidaari — pyramid ka aadhaar.

Yaad karne ki trick

L-E-J

Legislature = Law banaye, Executive = Execute (lagu) kare, Judiciary = Judge kare.

Do motto, do jagah

Sarkar = Satya (Satyameva Jayate); Court = Dharma (Yato Dharmastato Jayah).

Kalam = 31-11-02

Janm 1931, 11th President, 2002 se 2007.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Supreme Court ka motto kya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Yato Dharmastato Jayah — 'Jahan dharma hai, wahan vijay hai.' Satyameva Jayate Bharat Sarkar ka motto hai.

  2. Q2. Kanoon lagu (implement) karne wala ang kaun sa hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Executive — Executive — head of state, mantri aur police jaise agencies.

  3. Q3. Bharat ke nominal head kaun hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) President — President nominal head aur Supreme Commander hain; PM executive head.

  4. Q4. 'Democracy' kin Greek shabdon se bana hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Demos + Kratos — Dēmos = people, kratos = rule/power.

  5. Q5. Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Bharat ke kaun se President the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 11th — 11th President, 2002–2007.

  6. Q6. 2024 mein India mein lagbhag kitne voters the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 97 crore — Lagbhag 970 million — duniya ka sabse bada loktantra.
