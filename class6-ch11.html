Grassroots Democracy – Part 2: Local Government in Rural Areas

Panchayati Raj — gaon ka apna shaasan, teen manzil ka system.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Asli Bharat gaon mein

Gandhi ji ne kaha — 'The real India lives in its villages' (asli Bharat gaon mein basta hai). India mein ~6 lakh gaon, ~8,000 kasbe aur 4,000+ shehar; 1.4 arab mein se lagbhag do-tihaai log gaon mein. Misaal ka gaon Lakshmanpur (Himalaya ki talhati) — 200 ghar, ~700 log. Itne gaon ko Delhi se chalaana mushkil, isliye har gaon ka apna chhota 'sarkar' — Panchayat.

Panchayati Raj — apna raaj, apne haath

Panchayat = gaon ki parishad. Panchayati Raj = swashaasan — sarkar logon ke paas, aur yojanaayein seedhe zameen tak. Ye teen manzil ki imaarat jaisi hai (neeche se upar): Gram Panchayat (gaon) → Panchayat Samiti / Block Panchayat / Mandal Parishad (block) → Zila Parishad (zila).

Gram Sabha aur Gram Panchayat — farak samjho

Gram Sabha = gaon (ya paas ke kuch gaon) ke sabhi registered vayask voter — yani gaon ke saare bade log! Gram Sabha Gram Panchayat ke sadasya chunti hai. Panchayat ka mukhiya = Sarpanch / Pradhan. Madadgaar: Panchayat Secretary (meeting bulaana, record rakhna) aur Patwari (zameen ke record, kabhi peedhiyon purane naksha). Socho — Gram Sabha 'class ke saare bachche', Gram Panchayat 'chuni hui class committee'!

Prerna dene wale Sarpanch

Dnyaneshwar Kamble — transgender Sarpanch, Tarangfal (Solapur, Maharashtra), 2017; motto 'lok seva, gram seva'. Vandana Bahadur Maida — Bhil samudaay, Khankhandvi (MP) ki pehli mahila Sarpanch. Popatrao Pawar — Hiware Bazar (Ahmednagar): Anna Hazare model se barish ka paani rokna, watershed, laakhon ped → sookha gaon hara-bhara; Padma Shri 2020.

Bachchon ki bhi Panchayat!

Child-Friendly Panchayat: Bal Sabha, Bal Panchayat — Maharashtra mein bachche baal shram aur baal vivah ke khilaaf awaaz uthaate hain. Sangkhu Radhu Khandu GP (West Sikkim) — bachchon ki maang par school ki deewaar aur rasoi bani. Children's Parliament — Bunker Roy ka Barefoot College (Rajasthan); 8–14 saal ke bachche; World's Children's Honorary Award 2001. Yaani tum bhi apne gaon-shehar ke faislon mein hissa le sakte ho!

Upar ki do manzilein aur khaas niyam

Panchayat Samiti = Gram Panchayat aur Zila Parishad ke beech ki kadi; kai gaon ki yojanaayein jodti hai; Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana jaisi schemes. Teeno star par vanchit vargon ke liye khaas niyam; mahilaaon ke liye 1/3 seatein aarakshit. Har rajya mein dhaancha thoda alag, kyonki Panchayati Raj rajya ka vishay hai. Purana itihaas: Kautilya ke Arthashastra (~2,300 saal pehle) mein — har 10 gaon par sangrahana, 100 par karvatika, 400 par dronamukha, 800 par sthaniya!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Gandhi ji: 'The real India lives in its villages.' India: ~6 lakh gaon, ~8,000 towns, 4,000+ cities; 1.4 billion mein se lagbhag do-tihai gramin.
  • Udaharan gaon Lakshmanpur (Himalaya ki talhati) — 200 ghar, ~700 log.
  • Panchayat = village council. Panchayati Raj = self-government; shaasan logon ke kareeb, schemes zameen tak.
  • Three-tier system (neeche se upar): Gram Panchayat (village) → Panchayat Samiti / Block Panchayat / Mandal Parishad (block) → Zila Parishad / District Panchayat (district).
  • Gram Sabha = gaon (ya paas ke gaanvon) ke sabhi registered adult voters. Gram Sabha Gram Panchayat ke sadasya chunti hai. Mukhiya = Sarpanch / Pradhan.
  • Panchayat Secretary — meetings bulana, records rakhna. Patwari — zameen ke records (kabhi peedhiyon purane naksha).

  • Hiware Bazar (Ahmednagar, MH): Sarpanch Popatrao Pawar — Anna Hazare model (rainwater harvesting, watershed, lakhon ped) → hara-bhara gaon; Padma Shri 2020.
  • Child-Friendly Panchayat: Bal Sabha, Bal Panchayat (Maharashtra — child labour, child marriage ke khilaaf). Sangkhu Radhu Khandu GP (West Sikkim) — school deewar, rasoi.

Yaad karne ki trick

G-P-Z = 'Gaon Pe Zor'

Gram Panchayat → Panchayat Samiti → Zila Parishad.

Kautilya ka 10-100-400-800

'Sab Kaam Dron Sthaan par' — Sangrahana, Karvatika, Dronamukha, Sthaniya.

Sabha vs Panchayat

Sabha = Sab voters (badi). Panchayat = chune hue panch (chhoti).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Gram Sabha mein kaun hote hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Gaon ke sabhi registered adult voters — Gram Sabha = sabhi adult voters; ye Gram Panchayat ke sadasya chunti hai.

  2. Q2. Panchayati Raj ka beech wala (block) star kaun sa hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Panchayat Samiti — Panchayat Samiti / Block Panchayat / Mandal Parishad.

  3. Q3. Patwari ka mukhya kaam?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Zameen ke records rakhna — Patwari villagers ke land records rakhta hai.

  4. Q4. Hiware Bazar ko hara-bhara kisne banaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Popatrao Pawar — Sarpanch Popatrao Pawar ne Anna Hazare ka model apnaya; Padma Shri 2020.

  5. Q5. Panchayati Raj mein mahilaon ke liye kitni seats reserved?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1/3 — Ek-tihai (one-third).

  6. Q6. Arthashastra ke anusaar 'sangrahana' kitne gaanvon par?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) 10 — 10 → sangrahana, 100 → karvatika, 400 → dronamukha, 800 → sthaniya.
