Theme E · Economic Life Around Us

The Value of Work

Economic aur non-economic activities, bhugtaan ke tareeke aur value addition.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Kaam bhi pooja hai

Swami Vivekananda: 'Har kaam ko pooja ki tarah karo.' Hum din bhar kai kaam karte hain — kuch paise ke liye, kuch pyaar ke liye. Is chapter mein dono ka mulya samjhenge.

Economic vs non-economic — farak kya?

Economic activity = jo kaam paise ya paise ke barabar cheez ke badle kiya jaaye — dukaandaar, kisaan ka fasal bechna, vakeel ki fees, truck driver, factory mazdoor. Non-economic activity = jo kaam prem, dekhbhaal, aabhaar ya samman se kiya jaaye, paise ke liye nahi — maa ka parivaar ke liye khaana banaana, bhai ka homework mein madad, dada-dadi ki seva, ghar ki marammat mein haath batana.

Anu-Kabir ki kahaani

Geeta Aunty — Indian Air Force pilot, tankhwah milti hai → economic. Kabir ke dada (retired BSF) bachchon ko muft geography padhaate → non-economic. Rohan — hafte bhar software engineer (economic) aur weekend par volunteer (non-economic). Yaani ek hi insaan dono tarah ke kaam kar sakta hai!

Bazaar aur bhugtaan ke 4 tareeke

Market (bazaar) = jahan saamaan aur sevaaon ka len-den, zyaadatar paise se. Bhugtaan: Salary — tay aur niyamit, aam taur par har mahine. Wage — tay samay ke kaam ka (jaise roz ki dihaadi). Fee — professional salaah/seva (doctor, vakeel, tuition). Payment in kind — paise ki jagah cheez. Kavya ki kahaani: chacha bulldozer technician (salary), chachi post office (salary) + online tuition (fee), aur Sahil khet mazdoor — roz ki dihaadi, kuch nakad + kuch aam (payment in kind)!

Value addition — badhai ki kursi

Rajesh badhai ne ₹600 ki lakdi khareedi aur kursi banaakar ₹1,000 mein bechi. Badhe ₹400 = uske hunar, samay aur mehnat ka mulya — ise value addition kehte hain. Har stage par cheez ki keemat badhti hai.

Bina paise ke kaam — sabse anmol

Seva — Golden Temple ka langar (laakhon log roz muft khaana), mandir ka prasad. Swachh Bharat Abhiyan — milkar safaai. Van Mahotsav — ped lagaane ka utsav. Saath milkar tyohaar manaana. Ye kaam samaaj ko jodte hain — inki keemat paise se nahi naapi ja sakti.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Swami Vivekananda: 'Har kaam ko pooja ki tarah karo.'
  • Economic activities: paise ya money's worth ke badle kiye jaane wale kaam — dukaandaar, kisaan ka bechna, vakeel ki fees, truck driver, factory worker.
  • Non-economic activities: paisa nahi, balki prem, dekhbhaal, kritagyata, samman se — parivaar ke liye khaana banana, homework mein madad, dada-dadi ki seva, ghar ki marammat mein haath.
  • Anu-Kabir kahani: Geeta Aunty (IAF pilot) — salary, economic. Kabir ke dada (retired BSF) — bachchon ko muft geography → non-economic. Rohan — software engineer (economic) + weekend volunteer (non-economic).
  • Market = jahan saamaan aur sevaon ka len-den hota hai, zyaadatar paise se.
  • Bhugtaan ke prakaar: Salary — fixed, niyamit (aam taur par monthly). Wage — tay samay ke kaam ka bhugtaan (jaise daily wage). Fee — professional salah/seva (doctor, vakeel, tuition). Payment in kind — nakad ki jagah vastu.
  • Kavya ki kahani: chacha bulldozer technician (salary), chachi post office (salary) + online tuition (weekly fee), Sahil farm labourer — daily wage, kuch cash + kuch aam (payment in kind).
  • Value addition: har stage par mulya badhna. Rajesh carpenter — lakdi ₹600, kursi ₹1,000 → ₹400 = uske hunar, samay, mehnat ka mulya.
  • Non-economic ka mahatva: Sevā — gurudwara ka langar (Golden Temple), mandir ka prasad. Swachh Bharat Abhiyan — saamuhik safai. Van Mahotsav — ped lagane ka utsav. Tyohaar saath manana.

Yaad karne ki trick

SWiFt + Kind

Salary = mahine ki pakki; Wage = waqt/dihaadi; Fee = farmaish wali professional seva; Kind = cheez ke roop mein.

Value addition formula

Bechne ki keemat − kachche maal ki keemat = value added. (1000 − 600 = 400)

Teacher test

School teacher ko salary → economic. Dada ka free tuition → non-economic. Kaam same, bhugtaan alag.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Sahil ko mazdoori ka kuch hissa aam ke roop mein mila. Ise kya kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Payment in kind — Nakad ki jagah vastu ke roop mein bhugtaan = payment in kind.

  2. Q2. Lakdi ₹600 ki, kursi ₹1,000 mein biki. Value addition kitna?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) ₹400 — 1,000 − 600 = ₹400 — carpenter ke hunar aur mehnat ka mulya.

  3. Q3. In mein non-economic activity kaun si hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Mata-pita ka parivaar ke liye khaana banana — Prem aur dekhbhaal se kiya gaya kaam, paisa nahi.

  4. Q4. Doctor ya vakeel ko professional seva ke badle kya milta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Fee — Fee = professional salah/seva ka bhugtaan.

  5. Q5. Van Mahotsav kis cheez se juda hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ped lagana aur van sanrakshan — Festival of forests — tree plantation drives.

  6. Q6. Gurudwara ka langar kis mool-moolya ka udaharan hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Sevā (selfless service) — Har aagantuk ko muft bhojan — nishkaam seva.
