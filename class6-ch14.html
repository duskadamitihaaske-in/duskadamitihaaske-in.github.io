Economic Activities Around Us

Primary, Secondary, Tertiary — teen sector aur unki aapsi nirbharta (AMUL ki kahani).

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Kaamon ko teen 'dabbon' mein rakho

Kautilya (Arthashastra): 'Samriddhi ki jad aarthik gatividhi hai.' Duniya ke hazaaron kaamon ko samajhne ke liye unhe milti-julti baaton ke hisaab se teen sectors mein baant-te hain: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary. Socho ek roti ka safar: gehu ugaana (primary) → aata pisaana (secondary) → dukaan tak pahunchaana aur bechna (tertiary)!

Primary sector — seedhe prakriti se

Jo kaam prakriti se seedhe cheez lete hain: kheti, khaan khodna (mining), machhli paalan, pashupaalan, jungle (forestry), poultry (ande). Greenhouse kheti bhi.

Secondary sector — badlo aur banaao

Primary ki cheezon ko badal kar naya roop dena: manufacturing, construction (imaarat, sadak), utilities (paani, bijli, gas), solar bijli. Misaal: aata chakki, moongphali se tel, chai patti, lakdi se furniture aur kaagaz, kapaas se kapda, loha → steel → car. India 2022 (SIAM): ~45 lakh passenger gaadiyan, 10.3 lakh commercial, 8.6 lakh three-wheeler aur 2 crore do-pahiya!

Tertiary sector — sahaara dene wali sevaayein

Pehle do sectors ko sahaara dene wali sevaayein (services): transport, bank, vyapaar, dukaan (retail), aspataal, padhaana, sanchaar, software, hotel, godaam, marammat. Isliye ise service sector bhi kehte hain.

AMUL — kisaanon ki apni jeet

AMUL = Anand Milk Union Limited, Anand zila (Gujarat). 1940s mein doodh wale kisaan beech ke dalaalon (middlemen) se pareshaan the — kam daam milte. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel ne salaah di — milkar cooperative (sahkaari samiti) banao. 1946 mein AMUL bana — netritva: Tribhuvandas Patel (vakeel, swatantrata senani) aur Dr. Verghese Kurien (engineer). Kisaanon (mahilaaon samet) ko niyantran mila. Pasteurisation = doodh ko tay taapmaan tak garam karke bacteria maarna. Phir makkhan, milk powder, ghee, cheese — export bhi!

AMUL mein teeno sector ek saath

Gaay-bhains ka doodh nikaalna → primary; makkhan/powder banana → secondary; transport, vyapaar, dukaan → tertiary. Doosre doodh sahkaari: Nandini (Karnataka), Mother Dairy (Delhi-NCR), Aavin (Tamil Nadu), Vijaya (Andhra), Kevi (Nagaland), Sudha (Bihar), Verka (Punjab).

Kitaab ka safar aur recycling

Tumhari kitaab: ped se pulp (primary) → kaagaz aur chhapai (secondary) → transport aur dukaan (tertiary). Recycling ka jaadu: 1 ton kaagaz recycle karo to 17 ped bachte hain, 2.5 cubic metre landfill (kachra-ghar) ki jagah bachti hai, aur 70% kam urja aur paani lagta hai. Purani copiyan raddi mein do, phenko mat!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Kautilya (Arthashastra): 'Samriddhi ki jad aarthik gatividhi hai.'
  • Economic activities ko milti-julti visheshtaon ke aadhaar par economic sectors mein baanta: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary.
  • Primary sector: seedhe prakriti se — kheti, mining, machhli paalan, pashupaalan, forestry, poultry (ande). Greenhouse farming bhi.
  • Secondary sector: primary ke output ko process/transform karna — manufacturing, construction (imarat, sadak), utilities (paani, bijli, gas), solar power. Udaharan: atta chakki, moongphali se tel, chai patti, lakdi se furniture/kaagaz, cotton se kapda, iron se steel → cars.
  • India 2022 (SIAM): passenger vehicles 45 lakh, commercial 10.3 lakh, three-wheelers 8.6 lakh, two-wheelers 2 crore.
  • Tertiary sector (service sector): pehle do sectors ko sahara — transport, banking, trade, retail, healthcare, teaching, communication, software, hotels, warehouses, repair.
  • AMUL = Anand Milk Union Limited, Anand district (Gujarat). 1940s mein kisaan middlemen se pareshaan; Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel ne cooperative banane ki salaah di.
  • AMUL 1946 — netritva: Tribhuvandas Patel (vakeel, swatantrata senani) aur Dr. Verghese Kurien (engineer). Mahilaon samet kisaanon ko niyantran mila.
  • Pasteurisation = doodh ko tay taapmaan tak garam karke bacteria maarna. Factory mein makkhan, milk powder; aaj ghee, cheese aadi; export bhi.
  • AMUL mein teeno sector: doodh nikaalna (primary) → makkhan/powder banana (secondary) → transport, trading, retail (tertiary).
  • Doosre milk co-ops: Nandini (Karnataka), Mother Dairy (Delhi-NCR), Aavin (Tamil Nadu), Vijaya (Andhra Pradesh), Kevi (Nagaland), Sudha (Bihar), Verka (Punjab).
  • Kitaab ka safar: ped se pulp (primary) → kaagaz aur chhapai (secondary) → transport aur dukaan (tertiary).
  • Recycling: 1 ton kaagaz recycle → 17 ped, 2.5 cubic metre landfill bachta; 70% kam urja aur paani.

Yaad karne ki trick

P-S-T = Prakriti, Sanyantra, Teeri Sewa

Primary = Prakriti se; Secondary = Sanyantra/factory; Tertiary = Tumhari sewa (service).

AMUL 46 = Patel squad

1946; salaah Sardar Patel, netritva Tribhuvandas Patel + Kurien.

Dairy-State jodi

Nandini-Karnataka, Aavin-TN, Verka-Punjab, Sudha-Bihar, Kevi-Nagaland, Vijaya-AP, Mother Dairy-Delhi.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. AMUL ki sthapana kab hui?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1946 — 1946, Anand (Gujarat), Tribhuvandas Patel aur Dr. Verghese Kurien ke netritva mein.

  2. Q2. AMUL ka full form?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Anand Milk Union Limited — Anand Milk Union Limited.

  3. Q3. Kisaanon ko cooperative banane ki salaah kisne di?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel — Sardar Patel ne middlemen se mukti ke liye cooperative ki salaah di.

  4. Q4. Atta chakki mein gehun pisna kis sector mein?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Secondary — Primary output (gehun) ko process karke naya product (atta) → secondary.

  5. Q5. Banking kis sector ki activity hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Tertiary — Service sector = tertiary.

  6. Q6. Verka milk cooperative kis rajya ka hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Punjab — Verka — Punjab; Sudha — Bihar; Nandini — Karnataka.

  7. Q7. 1 ton kaagaz recycle karne se kitne ped bachte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 17 — 17 ped, 2.5 m³ landfill; 70% kam urja aur paani.
