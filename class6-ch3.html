Chapter03

Theme A · India and the World: Land and the People

Landforms and Life

Pahad, pathaar aur maidan — har landform ka apna mausam, jeevan, chunautiyan aur avsar.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Landform kya hota hai?

Dharti ki satah kahin oonchi, kahin samtal, kahin gehri hai. In aakritiyon ko landforms (bhoo-aakritiyaan) kehte hain; ye laakhon saalon mein bante hain. Teen mukhya: pahad (mountains), pathaar (plateaus), maidaan (plains). Aur sookhe registan (deserts) bhi.

Pahad — chauda neeche, patla upar

Mountain: chauda aadhaar, khadi dhalaan, patli choti. Kam ooncha aur gol choti wala = hill (pahaadi). Badi shrinkhlaayein: Himalaya (Asia), Alps (Europe), Andes (South America). Sabse oonchi choti Mount Everest — 8,848 m (Nepal-Tibet seema); Tibetan naam Chomolungma ('duniya ki maa devi'), Nepali naam Sagarmatha ('aakaash ki devi'). Kanchenjunga (Nepal-Sikkim), Kilimanjaro (Africa — akela pahad, kisi shrinkhla ka hissa nahi), Mont Blanc (Alps), Aconcagua (Andes), Anamudi (Kerala — dakshin India ki sabse oonchi). Himalaya jawaan pahad hain — nukile, oonche, ab bhi badh rahe; Aravalli bahut boodhe — ghis kar gol ho gaye.

Pahad par zindagi

Oonchaai par conifer ped — pine, fir, spruce, deodar (inke patte suiyon jaise, barf phisal jaaye); aur upar sirf ghaas, moss, lichen. Log seedhidaar kheti (terrace farming), pashupaalan, paryatan aur teerth yatra se jeete hain. Mushkilein: landslide (zameen khisakna), avalanche (barf ka toofan), flash flood, baadal fatna, kadi thand. Ganga (~2,500 km) Himalaya se; sahaayak — Yamuna, Ghaghara; Son Vindhya se. Kalidasa ki Kumarasambhava Himalaya ki vandana se shuru hoti hai. Mount Kailash — Hindu, Buddh, Jain aur Bon chaaron ke liye pavitra.

Himmat ki do misaalein

Bachendri Pal — Everest chadhne wali pehli Bharatiya mahila (1984); Padma Shri 1984, Padma Bhushan 2019. Arunima Sinha — ek haadse mein pair kho diya, phir bhi 2013 mein Everest chadhi aur har mahadweep ki sabse oonchi choti bhi; Padma Shri 2015. Seekh: himmat ho to koi pahad bada nahi!

Pathaar — upar se samtal 'mez'

Plateau = aas-paas se ooncha, upar se lagbhag samtal, kinaare khade — jaise ek badi mez! Tibetan Plateau — sabse bada aur ooncha (~4,500 m), 'Roof of the World'. Deccan Plateau — duniya ke sabse purane pathaaron mein, jwalamukhi ke lava se bana — isliye upjaau kaali mitti. Pathaar = 'khanijon ka bhandaar': East Africa — sona, heera; Chhota Nagpur — loha, koyla, manganese. Sundar jharne: Hundru (Subarnarekha), Jog (Sharavati), Nohkalikai — 340 m (Meghalaya), Africa ka Victoria (Zambezi).

Maidaan — jahan sabse zyada log

Plains = samtal zameen, aam taur par samundar tal se 300 m se kam. Nadiyaan baadh mein apne saath laayi mitti (sediment) bichha deti hain — bahut upjaau! Isliye kheti aasaan aur aabadi zyada: Ganga ke maidaan mein ~40 crore log (India ka 1/4 se zyada). Fasal: chaawal, gehu, makka, jau, millets; resha: kapaas, jute. Par zyada sinchaai se zameen ka paani ghat raha hai.

Registan aur Tamil kaviyon ke 5 'tinai'

Garam registan — Sahara, Thar; thanda — Gobi; kuch vidwaan Antarctica ko bhi registan kehte hain (barish/barf bahut kam). 2,000 saal purani Tamil Sangam kavita mein zameen ke 5 prakaar (tinai) aur unka jeevan: Kurinji (pahad — shikaar), Mullai (jungle/ghaas — pashupaalan), Marudam (upjaau maidaan — kheti), Neydal (samundar tat — machhli, yaatra), Palai (sookha — yaatra, yuddh). Yani bhoogol hi jeevan ka dhang tay karta hai!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Landform = dharti ki satah ka physical feature; laakhon saalon mein bante hain. 3 mukhya: Mountains, Plateaus, Plains.
  • Mountain: chauda base, khadi dhalaan, patli choti. Kam oonchai, golai wali choti = hill.
  • Ranges: Himalayas (Asia), Alps (Europe), Andes (South America).
  • Mount Everest 8,848 m (Tibet-China/Nepal); Kanchenjunga (Nepal-Sikkim); Aconcagua (Andes ki sabse oonchi); Kilimanjaro (East Africa — akela, kisi range ka hissa nahi); Mont Blanc (Alps); Anamudi / Anai Peak (Kerala, South India ki sabse oonchi).
  • Himalayas = young mountains (oonche, nukeele, abhi bhi badh rahe). Aravalli = bahut purane, erosion se gol.
  • Montane forest: conifer ped — pine, fir, spruce, deodar. Aur oonchai par grasses, moss, lichen.
  • Kalidasa ki Kumarasambhava Himalaya ki vandana se shuru hoti hai.
  • Ganga ~2,500 km. Tributaries: Yamuna, Ghaghara (Himalaya se); Son (Vindhya se).
  • Pahadon mein: terrace farming, herding, tourism, teerth yatra. Chunautiyan: landslide, avalanche, flash flood, cloudburst, thand.
  • Bachendri Pal — Everest chadhne wali pehli Indian mahila (1984; Padma Shri 1984, Padma Bhushan 2019). Arunima Sinha — ek pair khone ke baad Everest 2013, har continent ki sabse oonchi choti; Padma Shri 2015.
  • Everest ke naam: Tibetan Chomolungma ('Mother Goddess of the World'), Nepali Sagarmatha ('Goddess of the Sky'). Mount Kailash — Hindu, Buddhist, Jain aur Bon ke liye pavitra.
  • Plateau: aas-paas se ooncha, upar lagbhag samtal, kinare khade. Tibetan Plateau — sabse bada aur ooncha, avg 4,500 m, 'Roof of the World', ~2,500 km lamba.
  • Deccan Plateau — duniya ke sabse purane plateaus mein, jwalamukhi se bana. Plateaus = 'storehouses of minerals'.
  • East African Plateau: sona, heera. Chhota Nagpur: iron, coal, manganese. Lava plateau mein upjaau kaali mitti.
  • Jharne: Victoria (Zambezi), Hundru (Subarnarekha, Chhota Nagpur), Jog (Sharavati, Western Ghats), Nohkalikai — 340 m (Cherrapunji Plateau, Meghalaya).
  • Plains: samtal, aam taur par sea level se 300 m se kam. Floodplains mein nadiyaan sediments bichhati hain → upjaau mitti.
  • Ganga plain mein ~40 crore log (India ki 1/4 se zyada aabadi). Fasal: chawal, gehun, makka, jau, millets; fibre: cotton, jute, hemp. Irrigation se groundwater ghat raha hai.
  • Desert: hot — Sahara, Thar; cold — Gobi. Antarctica ko bhi kuch experts desert kehte hain.
  • Tamil Sangam ke 5 tinai: Kurinji (pahad – shikaar), Mullai (ghaas/jungle – pashupalan), Marudam (upjaau maidan – kheti), Neydal (tat – machhli/samudri yatra), Palai (sookha – yatra aur yuddh).

Yaad karne ki trick

Tinai ka desi code

Kurinji = Kursi oonchi (pahad), Mullai = Maal-mavesi, Marudam = Mitti-kheti, Neydal = Neer (samudra), Palai = Pyaas (registaan).

Kili akeli

Kilimanjaro kisi range mein nahi — akela khada pahad.

Jharna-Nadi jodi

Jog–Sharavati, Hundru–Subarnarekha, Victoria–Zambezi, Nohkalikai–Meghalaya (340 m).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 'Roof of the World' kise kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Tibetan Plateau — Tibetan Plateau ki ausat oonchai 4,500 m hai — sabse bada aur sabse ooncha plateau.

  2. Q2. Anamudi (Anai Peak) kis rajya mein hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Kerala — Anamudi Kerala mein hai aur South India ki sabse oonchi choti hai.

  3. Q3. Mount Everest chadhne wali pehli Indian mahila?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Bachendri Pal — Bachendri Pal ne 1984 mein Everest fateh kiya; usi saal Padma Shri mila.

  4. Q4. Nohkalikai Falls kis plateau se girta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Cherrapunji — Nohkalikai Falls Meghalaya ke Cherrapunji Plateau se 340 m neeche girta hai.

  5. Q5. Sangam ke kis tinai mein mukhya pesha kheti tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Marudam — Marudam = upjaau krishi maidan → farming.

  6. Q6. Kaun sa pahad kisi range ka hissa nahi hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Kilimanjaro — Kilimanjaro East Africa ka akela (isolated) pahad hai.
