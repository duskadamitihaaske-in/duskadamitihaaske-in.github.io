Chapter04

Theme B · Tapestry of the Past

Timeline and Sources of History

Itihaas ka samay kaise naapein, kin sources se jaanein, aur shuruaati insaan kaise jeete the.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Itihaas — beete kal se baat-cheet

Itihaasakar E.H. Carr ne kaha — itihaas 'aaj aur beete kal ke beech kabhi na khatam hone wala samvaad' hai. Hum aaj ke sawaal lekar purane zamaane se poochhte hain. Isme kai jaankaar madad karte hain: Geologist (mitti, pathar, pahad, nadi), Palaeontologist (fossils — pathar mein bane purane jeevon ke nishaan), Anthropologist (insaani samaaj-sanskriti), Archaeologist (khudai mein mili cheezein), Epigraphist (purane shilalekh padhne wale).

Calendar ka hisaab

Aaj duniya Gregorian calendar chalaati hai — 12 mahine, 365 din, har 4 saal mein leap year (366 din). Chupi baat: century wala saal tabhi leap jab 400 se poora bate — isliye 2000 leap tha, 1900 nahi. Bharat ka Panchang sooraj-chaand ki sthiti se banta hai — grahan, sooryoday-sooryast aur tyohaar ka muhurat batata hai.

BCE aur CE — ulti aur seedhi ginti

Pehle BC/AD kehte the, ab BCE (Before Common Era) aur CE (Common Era). BCE mein ginti ulti chalti hai: 500 BCE, 400 BCE, 300 BCE… (300 BCE, 500 BCE ke baad aaya). CE mein seedhi. Dhyaan rakho — 'saal zero' nahi hota: 1 BCE ke turant baad 1 CE. Isliye BCE se CE tak kitne saal? Dono jodo, phir 1 ghatao. Misaal: Buddha ~560 BCE → 2024 tak = 560 + 2024 − 1 = 2,583 saal.

Century aur millennium

Century (sadi) = 100 saal. 21st century = 2001 se 2100 (haan, 2000 nahi, 2001 se!). 3rd century BCE = 300 BCE se 201 BCE. Millennium (sahasraabdi) = 1,000 saal: 3rd millennium CE = 2001–3000; 1st millennium BCE = 1000 BCE se 1 BCE. Trick: century number = saal ke pehle ank + 1 (2024 → 20 + 1 = 21st).

Itihaas ke 'saboot' — sources

Itihaasakar ek jigsaw puzzle jodne wala detective hai — tukde (sources) kabhi milte hain, kabhi aapas mein takraate hain. Sources: Literary (Veda, Itihasa, pandulipi, kavita-natak); Archaeological (sikke, shilalekh, taamrapatra, imaaratein, auzaar, bartan, kabr); Artistic (chitra, moortiyan); Oral (lok-kathaayein, geet); Foreign accounts (videshi yaatriyon ke vritaant); Scientific (DNA/genetics, purane mausam ki jaanch). Haal ke itihaas ke liye akhbaar aur TV-internet.

Pehle insaan kaise jeete the?

Aaj jaise insaan (Homo sapiens) ~3,00,000 saal se dharti par hain. Shuru mein wo shikaari-sangrahak (hunter-gatherers) the — chhote dal (bands) mein, chattaanon ke neeche ya gufaon mein; aag jalaana, pathar ke auzaar, gufa ki deewaaron par chitra (rock paintings), moti (beads) banaana seekha. Last Ice Age (1 lakh saal se pehle se ~12,000 saal pehle tak) khatam hua to mausam garam hua — log kheti, pashupaalan karne lage aur nadiyon ke paas bas gaye.

Basti se shehar tak

Kheti ke saath: sardaar (chieftain), zameen sab ki saajhi (niji maalikaana nahi). Chhoti basti (hamlet) → gaon (village) → kasba/shehar (town). Mitti ke bartan (pottery), aur dhaatu mein pehle taamba (copper), baad mein loha (iron).

Exam ke liye short notes

  • E.H. Carr: History = 'present aur past ke beech kabhi na khatam hone wala samvaad'.
  • Geologists — mitti, pathar, pahad, nadi. Palaeontologists — fossils. Anthropologists — maanav samaaj aur sanskriti. Archaeologists — khudai se mile avshesh. Epigraphists — pracheen inscriptions.
  • Aaj duniya mein Gregorian calendar: 12 mahine, 365 din, har 4 saal leap year. Century year sirf tab leap jab 400 se divide ho — 2000 leap, 1900 nahi.
  • AD → ab CE (Common Era); BC → ab BCE (Before Common Era). BCE ulti ginti, CE seedhi.
  • Year zero nahi hota: 1 BCE ke turant baad 1 CE. BCE se CE tak saal = dono jodo − 1. Example: Buddha ~560 BCE → 2024 tak = 560 + 2024 − 1 = 2,583 saal.
  • Century = 100 saal. 21st century = 2001–2100. 3rd century BCE = 300 BCE se 201 BCE.
  • Millennium = 1,000 saal. 3rd millennium CE 2001 se 3000 tak. 1st millennium BCE = 1000 BCE se 1 BCE.
  • Panchanga — sooraj-chaand ki sthiti par aadhaarit; grahan, sooryoday-sooryast, tyohaar ke muhurat batata hai.
  • Sources of history: Literary (Vedas, Itihasas, manuscripts, kavita-naatak), Archaeological (coins, inscriptions, copper plates, monuments, tools, pottery, burials), Artistic (paintings, sculptures), Oral (lok-kathayein), Foreign accounts (travelogues), Scientific (genetics, pracheen climate studies). Haal ke itihaas ke liye newspapers aur electronic media.
  • Historian = jigsaw puzzle jodne wala; sources kabhi milte hain, kabhi takraate hain.
  • Homo sapiens (modern insaan) ~3,00,000 saal se dharti par.
  • Shuruaati insaan: hunter-gatherers, groups (bands) mein, rock shelters/gufaon mein; aag ka use, pathar ke auzaar, rock paintings, beads.
  • Last Ice Age: 1 lakh saal se pehle se lekar ~12,000 saal pehle tak. Uske baad kheti, pashupaalan, nadiyon ke paas basna.
  • Chieftains, saamuhik zameen (niji malikana nahi). Hamlet → village → town. Pottery; dhaatu mein pehle copper, baad mein iron.

Yaad karne ki trick

BCE + CE − 1

BCE aur CE ke beech ke saal: dono ko jodo, phir 1 ghatao (year zero nahi hai).

Century nikalna

Saal ke pehle do ank + 1. 1828 → 18 + 1 = 19th century. (Jo saal 00 par khatam ho — 1900 — wo 19th mein hi rahega.)

4 '-logist' ka shortcut

Geo = Ground, Palaeo = Patthar mein fossil, Anthropo = Aadmi ka samaaj, Archaeo = Avshesh ki khudai.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 2 BCE se 2 CE tak kitne saal beete?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 3 — Year zero nahi hai: 2 + 2 − 1 = 3 saal.

  2. Q2. Rani of Jhansi ka janm 1828 mein hua. Wo kis century ki thin?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 19th — 1801–1900 = 19th century. Trick: 18 + 1 = 19.

  3. Q3. Fossils ka adhyayan kaun karta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Palaeontologist — Palaeontologists laakhon saal purane paudhon, jaanwaron, insaanon ke fossils padhte hain.

  4. Q4. In mein se kaun sa saal leap year tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (D) 2000 — Century years tabhi leap hote hain jab 400 se divide hon. 2000 ÷ 400 = 5.

  5. Q5. Agar Chandragupta ka janm 320 CE mein hua, to Buddha (560 BCE) ke kitne saal baad?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 879 — 560 + 320 − 1 = 879 saal. Aur 320 CE = 4th century CE.

  6. Q6. Modern insaan (Homo sapiens) lagbhag kab se dharti par hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 3 lakh saal — Lagbhag 3,00,000 (teen lakh) saal se.
