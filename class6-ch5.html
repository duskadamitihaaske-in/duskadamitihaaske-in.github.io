Chapter05

Theme B · Tapestry of the Past

India, That Is Bharat

Bharat ke pracheen naam — apne logon ne kya kaha, videshiyon ne kya kaha.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Ek desh, kai naam — kyon?

Jaise ghar mein tumhe maa ek naam se, dost doosre naam se bulaate hain, waise hi hamare desh ko alag-alag samay aur log alag naamon se bulaate rahe. Is chapter mein hum dekhenge — hamare apne log desh ko kya kehte the, aur videshi kya kehte the.

Sabse purana naam — Sapta Sindhava

Duniya ke sabse purane granthon mein se ek Rig Veda ne uttar-pashchim ke ilaake ko 'Sapta Sindhava' kaha — saat nadiyon ki dharti. 'Sindhu' ka matlab Indus nadi bhi hai aur aam taur par 'nadi' bhi.

Mahabharata mein ilaakon ke naam

Mahabharata mein kai kshetron ke naam hain jo aaj bhi pehchaane jaate hain: Kashmira (Kashmir), Kurukshetra (Haryana), Vanga (Bengal), Pragjyotisha (Assam), Kaccha (Kutch), Kerala. Aur poore upmahadweep ke liye do naam: 'Bharatavarsha' aur 'Jambudvipa'.

Bharatavarsha aur Jambudvipa ka matlab

Bharatavarsha = 'Bharaton ka desh'; 'Bharata' naam pehli baar Rig Veda mein ek pramukh Vedic jan-samooh ke liye aaya. Jambudvipa = 'jamun ke phal ka dweep'! Samrat Ashoka (~250 BCE) ne apne shilalekh mein poore India ke liye 'Jambudvipa' likha — usme aaj ke Bangladesh, Pakistan aur Afghanistan ke hisse bhi aate the. Vishnu Purana: 'samundar ke uttar aur Himalaya ke dakshin wala desh Bharata hai'. ~2,000 saal purani Tamil kavita: 'dakshin mein Kumari se uttar ke mahaan parvat tak, poorv-pashchim ke samundaron tak'.

Samvidhan kya kehta hai?

Hamara Samvidhan (1950 mein lagu) shuru mein hi kehta hai: 'India, that is Bharat'; Hindi mein 'Bharat arthat India'. Yaani dono naam hamare.

Videshiyon ne kya kaha?

Persians (purana Iran) ne 6th sadi BCE mein Sindhu ka ilaaka jeeta. Unki bhasha mein 's' ki jagah 'h' bolte the — isliye 'Sindhu' → 'Hindu', 'Hind'. Yahan 'Hindu' sirf jagah ka naam tha, dharm nahi! Greeks ki bhasha mein 'h' tha hi nahi, isliye unhone use bhi gira diya → 'Indoi' / 'Indike' → aage chal kar 'India'. Chinese ne kaha 'Yintu' / 'Yindu', aur samman se 'Tianzhu' ('swargiya guru' — Buddha ki dharti).

Xuanzang aur 'Hindustan'

Chinese bhikshu Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang) 7th sadi CE mein India aaye, 17 saal rahe, Buddh granth China le gaye aur Sanskrit se Chinese mein anuvaad kiya. 'Hindustan' naam pehli baar ~1,800 saal pehle ek Persian shilalekh mein aaya; baad mein zyaadatar aakrantaon ne yahi naam use kiya. Seekh: naam badalte rahe, par desh ki pehchaan bani rahi.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Rig Veda (sabse pracheen granth) ne North-West ko kaha 'Sapta Sindhava' = saat nadiyon ki dharti. 'Sindhu' = Indus nadi ya aam nadi.
  • Mahabharata mein kshetra: Kashmira (Kashmir), Kurukshetra (Haryana), Vanga (Bengal), Pragjyotisha (Assam), Kaccha (Kutch), Kerala aadi.
  • Mahabharata ne poore upmahadweep ke liye 'Bharatavarsha' aur 'Jambudvipa' shabd use kiye.
  • Bharatavarsha = 'Bharaton ka desh'. 'Bharata' pehli baar Rig Veda mein — ek pramukh Vedic jan-samooh.
  • Jambudvipa = 'jamun ke phal ka dweep'. Ashoka (~250 BCE) ne inscription mein poore India ke liye Jambudvipa likha — jismein aaj ka Bangladesh, Pakistan aur Afghanistan ka hissa bhi tha.
  • Vishnu Purana: 'samudra ke uttar aur himalaya ke dakshin wala desh Bharata kehlata hai'.
  • Tamil kavita (~2,000 saal purani): 'dakshin mein Kumari se, uttar mein mahaan parvat tak, purab-pashchim ke samudron tak'.
  • Samvidhan (lagu 1950): 'India, that is Bharat'; Hindi mein 'Bharat arthat India'.
  • Persians (pracheen Iran) — 6th century BCE mein Indus kshetra jeeta; 'Sindhu' ko kaha 'Hind', 'Hidu', 'Hindu'. Yahan 'Hindu' sirf bhaugolik shabd — dharm nahi.
  • Greeks: 'Indoi' / 'Indike' — Greek mein 'h' akshar nahi tha, isliye gira diya.
  • Chinese: 'Yintu' / 'Yindu'; ek aur shabd 'Tianzhu' = 'heavenly master' — Buddha ki dharti ke liye samman.
  • Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang) 7th century CE mein China se aaya, 17 saal raha, Buddhist granth le gaya aur Sanskrit se Chinese mein anuvaad kiya.
  • 'Hindustan' pehli baar ek Persian inscription mein ~1,800 saal pehle; baad mein zyaadatar aakrantaon ne yahi naam use kiya.

Yaad karne ki trick

S → H → I chain

Sindhu → Hindu (Persian, S ki jagah H) → Indoi (Greek, H gira) → India. Chinese: Sindhu → Hindu → Indu → Yindu.

Ashoka = Jambu

'A' se Ashoka, 'J' se Jambudvipa — samraat ne jamun ka naam chuna.

Xuanzang = 7 aur 17

7th century mein aaya, 17 saal raha.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 'Sapta Sindhava' ka arth hai —

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Saat nadiyon ki dharti — Rig Veda ne North-West kshetra ko 'land of the seven rivers' kaha.

  2. Q2. Kis samraat ne inscription mein India ke liye 'Jambudvipa' likha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ashoka — Ashoka (~250 BCE) ke inscription mein Jambudvipa — poora upmahadweep.

  3. Q3. Pracheen Persian mein 'Hindu' shabd kis cheez ke liye tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Bhaugolik kshetra (Indus ke paar) — Persian 'Hindu' = Sindhu ka roop, purely geographical term.

  4. Q4. Greeks ne 'Hindu' se 'Indoi' kyun banaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Greek mein 'h' akshar nahi tha — Greek bhasha mein shuruaati 'h' nahi tha, isliye gira diya.

  5. Q5. 'Hindustan' shabd pehli baar kahan mila?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Persian inscription, ~1800 saal pehle — NCERT: ek Persian inscription mein ~1,800 saal pehle. (Textbook ka True/False sawaal!)

  6. Q6. Xuanzang India kab aaya aur kitne saal raha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 7th century CE, 17 saal — 7th century CE, 17 saal; Buddhist granth Chinese mein anuvaad kiye.
