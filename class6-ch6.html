Theme B · Tapestry of the Past

The Beginnings of Indian Civilisation

Harappan / Indus-Sarasvati sabhyata (c. 2600–1900 BCE) — planned cities, water management, vyapaar aur patan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Sabhyata (civilisation) kise kehte hain?

Ek gaon ko shehar aur phir sabhyata banne ke liye kya chahiye? Socho: sarkar/prashasan jo niyam chalaaye; town planning — sadkein, paani, naaliyan; kaarigari (crafts); vyapaar — desh ke andar aur bahar; likhaavat (writing); saajhe saanskritik vichaar; aur itni achchhi kheti ki shehron ka pet bhar sake. Duniya ki pehli sabhyataayein: Mesopotamia (Iraq-Syria, ~6,000 saal pehle), phir Egypt.

Hamari pehli shehri sabhyata — naam kai, sabhyata ek

Indus-Sarasvati sabhyata, c. 2600–1900 BCE. ~3500 BCE se gaon kasbe bane, ~2600 BCE tak kasbe shehar — ise 'First Urbanisation of India' (pehla shehrikaran) kehte hain. Naam: Indus, Harappan, Indus-Sarasvati, Sindhu-Sarasvati. 'Harappan' isliye kyonki Harappa (Pakistan ka Punjab) 1920–21 mein sabse pehle khoda gaya. 'Indus Valley' naam ab purana pad gaya, kyonki sabhyata Indus se bahut door tak faili thi. Sarasvati nadi = aaj ki Ghaggar (India) / Hakra (Pakistan) — ab sirf barsaati; Rig Veda mein devi aur nadi dono.

Kaunsa shehar kahan?

Harappa — Punjab (Pakistan); Mohenjo-daro — Sindh (Pakistan); Dholavira — Gujarat (Rann of Kutch); Rakhigarhi — Haryana; Kalibangan — Rajasthan; Ganweriwala — Cholistan (Pakistan); Lothal — Gujarat. Sarasvati ke kinaare Haryana mein Farmana, Bhirrana, Banawali bhi.

Smart shehar — 4,500 saal pehle!

Sadkein chaudi aur disha (N-S, E-W) ke hisaab se; shehar ke chaaron or deewaar (fortification); upper town (khaas log) aur lower town (aam log). Dholavira mein 3 hisse, neev pathar ki. Mazedaar — chhote aur bade gharon ki eenton ki quality ek jaisi! Gande paani ki naaliyan sadkon ke neeche dhaki hui. Mohenjo-daro mein ~700 eent ke kuein. Dholavira mein kam se kam 6 jalaashay — sabse bada 73 m lamba; ek chattaan kaat kar bana (33 m).

Great Bath — ek pahelee

Mohenjo-daro ka Great Bath ~12 × 7 m, bitumen (taarkol jaisa padarth) se waterproof, kone mein naali. Pehle maana gaya ki ye sabke nahaane ka talaab tha, par zyaadatar gharon mein apne bathroom the — isliye ye vichaar chhoda gaya. Shaayad raaj parivaar ka ya dharmik anushthaan ka talaab tha — vidwaan abhi bhi bahas kar rahe hain.

Khaana, kheti aur kapaas

Jau, gehu, millets, kabhi chaawal, daalein, sabziyaan. Duniya (Eurasia) mein sabse pehle kapaas (cotton) yahin ugaaya gaya! Hal (plough) bhi tha — Banawali se mitti ka model mila. Bartanon mein haldi, adrak, kela aur doodh-dahi ke nishaan.

Vyapaar — samundar paar tak

Gujarat ke laal pathar carnelian ke moti, shankh ki choodiyan; bronze (taamba + tin); shaayad taamba bahar se. Haathi-daant ki kanghi Oman mein aur moti Susa (Iran) mein mile. Lothal mein vishaal basin — 217 m × 36 m (do football maidaan se thoda lamba) — maana jaata hai ki ye dockyard (jahaaz ghaat) tha. Ye India ki pehli tez samudri gatividhi thi.

Mohren (seals) aur mashhoor cheezein

Seals — steatite (naram pathar, garam karke sakht) ki, kuch cm ki; unicorn, bail, seengh wala baagh + likhaavat ke chinh. Ye lipi aaj tak koi padh nahi paaya! Seals shaayad vyapaar mein maal par thappa lagaane ke liye. Aur: kaansa ka darpan, pathar ke baat (weights), khel ka board, mitti ki seeti, 'Priest King', Pashupati seal, kaanse ki 'Dancing Girl' (10.8 cm, Mohenjo-daro), namaste mudra wali moorti, aur Lothal ka bartan jis par pyaase kauve ki kahaani!

Sabhyata dheemi kyon padi? (~1900 BCE)

Yuddh ya hamle ka koi saboot nahi — ye shaanti-priya sabhyata thi. Do mukhya kaaran: (1) 2200 BCE se jalvaayu badla — barish kam; (2) Sarasvati nadi beech ke hisse mein sookh gayi — Kalibangan, Banawali chhod diye gaye. Log shehar chhod kar gaon laut gaye — par unki sanskriti (kheti, kala, reeti) aage chalti rahi.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Civilisation ke lakshan: government/administration, urbanism (town planning, paani, drainage), crafts, trade (andar-bahar), writing, cultural ideas, aur shehron ko khilane laayak productive agriculture.
  • Mesopotamia (Iraq-Syria) ~6,000 saal pehle; Egypt kuch sadiyon baad.
  • Indus-Sarasvati civilisation: c. 2600–1900 BCE. ~3500 BCE se gaon → town; ~2600 BCE ke aas-paas town → city. Ise 'First Urbanisation of India' kehte hain.
  • Naam: Indus, Harappan, Indus-Sarasvati, Sindhu-Sarasvati. 'Harappan' isliye kyunki Harappa (Pakistan ka Punjab) pehla khoda gaya shahar — 1920-21. 'Indus Valley' naam ab purana (obsolete), kyunki sabhyata Indus se bahut aage faili thi.
  • Sarasvati nadi = aaj Ghaggar (India) / Hakra (Pakistan) — ab mausami. Rig Veda mein devi aur nadi dono roop mein; baad ke granth uske sookhne ka zikr karte hain.
  • Shahar → aaj ka sthaan: Harappa – Punjab (Pak), Mohenjo-daro – Sindh, Dholavira – Gujarat (Rann of Kutch), Rakhigarhi – Haryana, Kalibangan – Rajasthan, Ganweriwala – Cholistan (Pak), Lothal – Gujarat. Sarasvati basin: Farmana, Bhirrana, Banawali (Haryana).
  • Town planning: chaudi sadkein (cardinal directions mein), fortification, upper town (elite) aur lower town (aam log). Dholavira mein 3 zones, neenv pathar ki. Chhote-bade gharon ki construction quality ek jaisi, eenton se.
  • Great Bath (Mohenjo-daro) ~12 × 7 m, bitumen se waterproof, kone mein drain. Public bath wali vyakhya khaarij (zyaadatar gharon mein apne bathroom the); royal family ya dharmik anushthaan ka tank — bahas jaari.
  • Drains sadkon ke neeche. Mohenjo-daro mein ~700 eenton ke kuen. Dholavira mein kam se kam 6 reservoirs; sabse bada 73 m lamba; chattaan kaat ke bana ek reservoir 33 m.
  • Khaana: jau, gehun, millets, kabhi chawal, daalein, sabziyaan. Eurasia mein sabse pehle cotton ugaya. Plough (Banawali ka clay model). Bartanon mein haldi, adrak, kela, dairy ke nishaan.
  • Vyapaar: carnelian beads (laal pathar, Gujarat), shell bangles; shayad copper import. Bronze = copper + tin. Ivory comb Oman mein mila; beads Susa (Iran) mein. India ki pehli tez samudri gatividhi.
  • Lothal (Gujarat) ka vishaal basin — 217 m × 36 m, do football ground se thoda lamba — dockyard maana jaata hai.
  • Seals: steatite (naram pathar, garam karke sakht), kuch cm ke; unicorn, bull, horned tiger + likhawat ke chinh. Lipi abhi tak padhi nahi gayi; seals vyapaar se jude.
  • Vastuein: bronze mirror, stone weights, bronze chisel, gameboard, terracotta whistle (Karanpura); 'Priest King', swastika seal, Pashupati seal, 'Dancing Girl' (bronze, 10.8 cm, Mohenjo-daro), namaste mudra figurine, pyaase kauve ki kahani wala bartan (Lothal).
  • Patan ~1900 BCE: yuddh/aakraman ka koi saboot nahi; shaanti-priya sabhyata. Do maane hue kaaran: 2200 BCE se jalvaayu parivartan (kam baarish) aur Sarasvati ka central basin mein sookhna (Kalibangan, Banawali chhode gaye). Log gaanvon mein laute; sanskriti aage chalti rahi.

Yaad karne ki trick

Shahar-Rajya: 'Do Gujarati — Dholu aur Lotu'

Dholavira aur Lothal dono Gujarat. Rakhi Haryana mein bandhi, Kali Rajasthan ki, Harappa-Mohenjo Pakistan.

Number trio: 12-7 / 73 / 217

Great Bath 12×7 m, Dholavira reservoir 73 m, Lothal dock 217×36 m, Mohenjo-daro ~700 kuen.

Dates: '26 se 19'

2600 BCE shuru, 1900 BCE patan; 2200 BCE se sookha mausam.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Harappan civilisation ka samay-kaal kya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) c. 2600–1900 BCE — NCERT timeline: lagbhag 2600 se 1900 BCE (urban phase).

  2. Q2. 'Indus Valley Civilisation' naam ab kyun obsolete maana jaata hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Sabhyata Indus kshetra se bahut aage tak faili thi — Sarasvati basin aur Gujarat tak sainkdon sites mili hain.

  3. Q3. Dholavira kis rajya mein hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Gujarat — Dholavira — Rann of Kutch, Gujarat. Yahan 3 zones aur bade reservoirs the.

  4. Q4. Lothal ka vishaal basin kis kaam ka maana jaata hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Dockyard — 217 m × 36 m basin — boats ke aane-jaane ka dockyard.

  5. Q5. Harappan seals mostly kis pathar ke bane the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Steatite — Steatite naram pathar hai jise garam karke sakht kiya jaata tha. Carnelian se beads bante the.

  6. Q6. Patan ke do maane hue kaaran kaun se hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Jalvaayu parivartan aur Sarasvati ka sookhna — Yuddh/aakraman ka koi saboot nahi mila; 2200 BCE se sookha daur aur Sarasvati ka sookhna.

  7. Q7. 'Dancing Girl' kis dhaatu ki bani hai aur kahan mili?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Bronze, Mohenjo-daro — Bronze figurine, 10.8 cm oonchi, Mohenjo-daro se. Poori baanh mein bangles — aaj bhi Gujarat-Rajasthan mein dikhta hai.
