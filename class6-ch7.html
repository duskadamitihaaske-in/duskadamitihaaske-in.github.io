Theme C · Our Cultural Heritage and Knowledge Traditions

India’s Cultural Roots

Vedas, Upanishads, Buddhism, Jainism aur lok-janjaati parampara — Bharatiya sanskriti ke ped ki jadein.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Sanskriti — ek purana ped

Hamari sanskriti ko ek bahut purane ped jaisa socho: kai jadein (alag-alag srot — kuch Harappa tak jaati hain), ek mazboot tana (saajhe vichaar), aur anek shaakhaayein (alag-alag parampara). Is chapter mein hum jadein aur shaakhaayein dekhenge.

Veda — gyaan ka khazaana jo yaad rakha gaya

'Veda' Sanskrit ke 'vid' (jaanna) se — matlab gyaan. Chaar Veda: Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva. Inme hazaaron mantra hain jo Sapta Sindhava mein rache gaye. Hairat ki baat — ye likhe nahi gaye, 100–200 peedhiyon tak lagbhag bina badle munh-zubaani yaad rakhe gaye! Isliye UNESCO (2008) ne Vedic paath ko 'maanavta ki maukhik virasat ka masterpiece' kaha. Rig Veda ki tithi par alag andaaze: 5th se 2nd millennium BCE.

Rishi, rishika aur devta

Mantra rachne wale rishi (purush) aur rishika (mahila) the. Devta: Indra, Agni, Varuna, Mitra, Sarasvati, Ushas (bhor ki devi). Ritam = brahmaand ka satya aur vyavastha. Mashhoor vichaar: 'Ekam sat vipra bahudha vadanti' — satya ek hai, gyaani use anek naamon se pukaarte hain. Rig Veda ke aakhri mantra: 'saath chalo, saath bolo, tumhare man ek hon'. Roz Agni ko aahuti — yajna.

Vedic samaaj kaisa tha?

Log janas (kul/kabeele) mein — Rig Veda mein 30 se zyada: Bharatas, Purus, Kurus, Yadus, Turvashas. Shabd jo aaj bhi hain: raja, sabha, samiti. Pesha: kisaan, bunkar, kumhaar, badhai, vaidya, nartak, naai, purohit.

Upanishad — bade sawaal, gehre jawaab

Upanishadon mein naye vichaar aaye — punarjanm aur karma (jaise karm waisa phal). Vedanta: sab kuch ek divya tatva brahman se bana; har jeev mein wahi divya roop = atman. 'Aham brahmasmi' (main brahman hoon), 'Tat tvam asi' (tum wahi ho), 'Sarve bhavantu sukhinah' (sab sukhi hon). Teen kahaaniyan: Shvetaketu — 12 saal gurukul ke baad bhi ghamand, to pita Uddalaka Aruni ne bargad ke chhote beej se samjhaaya ki adrishya tatva hi sab mein hai; Nachiketa — Yama (mrityu ke devta) se poochha 'maut ke baad kya?' — jawab: atman amar hai; Gargi ne Raja Janaka ke darbaar mein Yajnavalkya se shastrarth kiya. Yoga — brahman ko apni chetna mein anubhav karne ke tareeke. Ye sab milkar aaj ke Hindu dharm ki neev bane.

Buddh dharm — dukh se mukti ka raasta

Siddhartha Gautama — Lumbini (Nepal), ~560 BCE (andaaza). 29 saal ki umar mein mahal se bahar ek boodha, ek beemaar, ek mrit vyakti aur ek shaant sanyasi dekha — aur mahal chhod diya. Bodh Gaya (Bihar) mein peepal ke neeche gyaan mila → Buddha = 'jaaga hua'. Dukh ki jad: avidya (agyaan) aur moh (attachment). Ahimsa = kisi ko chot na pahunchaana. Bhikshu-bhikshuniyon ka sangathan = Sangha. Jataka kathaayein — Buddha ke pichhle janm (bandar-raja ki kahaani, Bharhut).

Jain dharm — teen anmol siddhant

Vardhamana — Vaishali (Bihar) ke paas, early 6th sadi BCE. 30 saal par ghar chhoda, 12 saal tap → 'anant gyaan' → Mahavira ('mahaan veer'). 'Jain' = jina (vijeta — agyaan aur moh par jeet). Siddhant: Ahimsa (hinsa nahi), Anekantavada (satya ke kai pehlu — jaise andhon aur haathi ki kahaani!), Aparigraha (zaroorat se zyada sangrah nahi). Kahaani: chor Rohineya badal gaya — sabko doosra mauka. Ellora (6th–10th sadi CE) mein Hindu, Buddh, Jain — teeno ki gufaayein saath-saath!

Ek alag soch aur saajha 'tana'

Charvaka/Lokayata — sirf bhautik duniya sach, maut ke baad kuch nahi; ye lokpriya nahi hua. Baaki sab mein saajha tana: dharma, karma, punarjanm, aur dukh-agyaan se mukti.

Janjaatiyaan — hamari jadon ka hissa

2011: 705 janjaatiyaan, ~10.4 crore log (Australia + UK ki aabadi se zyada!). Purane Bharat mein 'tribe' shabd nahi tha — ye bhi janas the; samvidhan mein 'janjati'. Lena-dena: Jagannath (Puri) mool roop se janjaati devta; Toda (Nilgiris) 30+ chotiyon ko pavitra maante, ungli se ishaara nahi karte; Donyipolo (Arunachal — Surya + Chandra), Khandoba, Singbonga (Munda, Santhal). Samaajshaastri André Béteille: Hindu aur janjaati dharmon ne ek-doosre ko prabhaavit kiya.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Sanskriti ek pracheen ped jaisi: anek jadein, ek tana (saajhe vichaar), anek shaakhaein. Kuch jadein Harappan sabhyata tak jaati hain.
  • 'Veda' = Sanskrit 'vid' (jaanna) se — gyaan. 4 Vedas: Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva. Hazaaron hymns, maukhik (likhe nahi gaye), Sapta Sindhava mein rache gaye.
  • Rig Veda ki tithi par anumaan: 5th se 2nd millennium BCE. 100–200 peedhiyon tak lagbhag bina badlaav ke yaad rakha gaya.
  • UNESCO (2008): Vedic chanting = 'masterpiece of the oral and intangible heritage of humanity'.
  • Rishis (purush) aur rishikas (mahila). Devta: Indra, Agni, Varuna, Mitra, Sarasvati, Ushas. Ritam = satya aur vyavastha.
  • 'Ekam sat vipra bahudha vadanti' — satya ek hai, gyaani use anek naam dete hain. Rig Veda ke antim mantra: 'saath chalo, saath bolo, man ek ho'.
  • Vedic samaaj janas (clans) mein — Rig Veda mein 30+: Bharatas, Purus, Kurus, Yadus, Turvashas. Shabd: rājā, sabhā, samiti (sabha). Pesha: kisaan, bunkar, kumhaar, badhai, vaidya, nartak, naai, purohit.
  • Yajna — Agni ko roz ki aahuti; samay ke saath rituals jatil hue.
  • Upanishads: naye vichaar — rebirth (punarjanm) aur karma. Vedanta: sab kuch ek divya tatva brahman. 'Aham brahmasmi' (main brahman hoon), 'Tat tvam asi' (tu wahi hai). Atman = har jeev mein divya swaroop. 'Sarve bhavantu sukhinah'.
  • Kahaniyan: Shvetaketu (Chhandogya Up.) — pita Uddalaka Aruni, 12 saal gurukul, bargad ka beej aur mitti ke bartan. Nachiketa (Katha Up.) — Yama se 'maut ke baad kya?'; atman amar. Gargi–Yajnavalkya (Brihadaranyaka Up.) — Raja Janaka ke darbar mein shastrarth.
  • Yoga — chetna mein brahman ki anubhooti ke tareeke. Ye schools mil kar aaj ke Hinduism ki neenv bane.
  • Buddhism: Siddhartha Gautama, Lumbini (Nepal), ~560 BCE (anumaanit). 29 saal ki umr mein boodha, beemar, mrit aur ek shaant sanyasi dekha → mahal chhoda.
  • Bodh Gaya (Bihar) mein peepal ke neeche gyaan. Dukh ka kaaran: avidya (agyaan) aur attachment (moh). Buddha = 'enlightened/awakened'. Ahimsa = mool arth 'na chot pahunchana'. Sangha: bhikshu aur bhikshuni.
  • Jainism: Vardhamana, Vaishali (Bihar) ke paas, early 6th century BCE. 30 saal par ghar chhoda, 12 saal tap → 'infinite knowledge'; Mahavira = 'great hero'. 'Jain' = jina (vijeta — agyaan aur moh par).
  • Jain siddhant: Ahimsa, Anekantavada (satya ke anek pehlu), Aparigraha (sangrah na karna). Sabhi jeev ek-doosre par nirbhar.
  • Jataka tales — Buddha ke poorva-janm (monkey-king ki kahani, Bharhut panel, MP). Jain kahani: chor Rohineya ka badlaav — sabko doosra mauka.
  • Ellora (Maharashtra) gufaein, 6th–10th century CE — Hindu, Buddhist aur Jain teeno.
  • Charvaka / Lokayata: sirf bhautik sansaar sach, maut ke baad kuch nahi — lokpriya nahi hua. Saajha 'tana': dharma, karma, rebirth, dukh-agyaan se mukti.
  • Tribes (2011): 705 tribes, ~10.4 crore log (Australia + UK se zyada). Pracheen Bharat mein 'tribe' shabd nahi — ve bhi 'janas' the. Samvidhan: 'janjati'.
  • Aadaan-pradaan: Jagannath (Puri) mool roop se janjaati devta. Toda (Nilgiris) — 30+ chotiyan pavitra, ungli se ishaara nahi. Donyipolo (Arunachal — Surya+Chandra), Khandoba (central India), Singbonga (Munda, Santhal). Sociologist André Béteille: Hinduism aur tribal dharmon ne ek-doosre ko prabhavit kiya.

Yaad karne ki trick

Vedas = RYSA ('Raisa')

Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva — purane se naye.

Mahavira ke teen 'A'

Ahimsa, Anekantavada, Aparigraha.

Upanishad-kahani jodi

Chhandogya = Chhota beej (Shvetaketu); Katha = Kaal/Yama (Nachiketa); Brihadaranyaka = Bahas (Gargi).

Umr ka khel: 29 vs 30+12

Siddhartha 29 par nikle; Vardhamana 30 par nikle, 12 saal baad gyaan.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 'Veda' shabd kis Sanskrit dhaatu se bana hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Vid (jaanna) — 'Vid' = jaanna, gyaan; isi se 'vidya' bhi.

  2. Q2. UNESCO ne Vedic chanting ko kab 'intangible heritage' maana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 2008 — 2008 — masterpiece of the oral and intangible heritage of humanity.

  3. Q3. Nachiketa ki kahani kis Upanishad mein hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Katha — Katha Upanishad — Nachiketa Yama se maut ke baad ka sawaal poochta hai.

  4. Q4. 'Anekantavada' ka arth hai —

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Satya ke anek pehlu hote hain — Truth ke anek aspects hain; koi ek kathan poora satya nahi bata sakta. Aparigraha = non-possession.

  5. Q5. Buddha ko gyaan kahan mila?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Bodh Gaya — Bodh Gaya (Bihar), peepal ke ped ke neeche. Lumbini janmasthan hai.

  6. Q6. Singbonga kin janjaatiyon ke sarvochch devta hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Munda aur Santhal — Purvi Bharat ke Munda, Santhal aadi — Singbonga ne sansaar rachaa.

  7. Q7. 2011 ke anusaar India mein kitni tribes thin?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 705 — 705 tribes, lagbhag 104 million (10.4 crore) log.
