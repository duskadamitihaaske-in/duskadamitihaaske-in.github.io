Chapter08

Theme C · Our Cultural Heritage and Knowledge Traditions

Unity in Diversity, or ‘Many in the One’

Khaana, kapde, tyohaar aur mahakavya — alag roop, ek aatma.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Itne alag, phir bhi ek

India mein 1.4 arab se zyada log — duniya ka ~18%. Anthropological Survey of India ke 'People of India' project (director K.S. Singh) ne paaya: 4,635 samudaay, 325 bhaashaayein, 25 lipiyaan! Angrez itihaasakar Vincent Smith ne sawaal kiya — itni vividhta mein ek itihaas kaise likhein? Aur khud jawaab diya: 'India offers unity in diversity' — vividhta mein ekta. Socho ek maala: moti alag-alag rang ke, par dhaaga ek.

Khaana — wahi saamaan, hazaar pakwaan

Mukhya anaaj: chaawal, jau, gehu; millets: bajra, jowar, ragi; daalein, rajma; masaale: haldi, jeera, elaichi, adrak. Saamaan lagbhag sab jagah wahi (ekta), par har kshetra ke pakwaan alag (vividhta) — idli-dosa, roti-sabzi, maachh-bhaat, dal-baati!

Saadi — bina sile kapde ka kamaal

Saadi — ek bina sila lamba kapda, lagbhag poore India mein. Resham ki mashhoor saadiyan: Banarasi, Kanjivaram, Paithani, Patan Patola, Muga, Mysore. Vaishali (Bihar) ki pathar ki nakkaashi kuch sadi BCE purani hai jisme saadi jaisa vastra hai. Pehenne ke anek tareeke — aur upyog bhi: jhoola, thaila, chhaata! Kabhi India duniya ka sabse badhiya kapaas banata tha; chhapa hua kapaas 'chintz' 17th sadi ke Europe mein itna pasand ki England aur France ne iske aayaat par rok laga di!

Ek tyohaar, das naam — Makara Sankranti

~14 January ko fasal ka tyohaar poore desh mein, par naam alag: Lohri/Maghi (Punjab), Magh Bihu (Assam), Khichdi Parv (UP/Bihar), Uttarayan (Gujarat), Poush Sankranti (Bengal), Pongal (Tamil Nadu), Makaravilakku (Kerala), Pedda Panduga (Andhra), Makara Sankramana (Karnataka), Shishur Saenkraat (Kashmir). Mauka ek, rang anek!

Kahaaniyan jo poori duniya ghoomi

Panchatantra — Sanskrit mool kam se kam 2,200 saal purana; ~200 roop, 50+ bhaashaon mein; SE Asia, Arab desh, Europe tak pahuncha. Ramayana aur Mahabharata — mool mein ~7,000 prishth; dharma ki punahsthaapana ki kahaani. Sirf Tamil Nadu mein Mahabharata ke ~100 lok-roop! Bhil, Gond, Munda aur Northeast-Himalaya ki janjaatiyon ke apne Ramayana-Mahabharata. Nilgiris mein Irula janjaati ka 'Pancha Pandavar' pathar. K.S. Singh: 'shaayad hi koi jagah ho jahan lok-kathaaon ke hisaab se Pandav na gaye hon'. Nehru: anpadh gaon wale bhi mahakaavyon ke saikdon shlok jaante the.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • India: 1.4 billion+ log, duniya ki ~18% aabadi.
  • Anthropological Survey of India ka 'People of India' project (late 20th century, director K.S. Singh): 4,635 communities, 325 languages, 25 scripts.
  • British historian Vincent Smith: 'itni vividhta mein India ka itihaas kaise likhein? Jawab — India offers unity in diversity.'
  • Khaana: staple grains — chawal, jau, gehun; millets — bajra, jowar, ragi; daalein, rajma. Masale: haldi, jeera, ilaychi, adrak. Same ingredients (unity), anek pakwaan (diversity).
  • Sari — bina sila kapda, lagbhag poore India mein. Silk: Banarasi, Kanjivaram, Paithani, Patan Patola, Muga, Mysore. Vaishali (Bihar) ki stone relief kuch sadi BCE purani. Pehenne aur upyog ke anek tareeke.
  • India duniya ka sabse badhiya cotton banata tha. Chintz (printed cotton) 17th century Europe mein itna lokpriya ki England aur France ne iska import ban kar diya.
  • Makara Sankranti (~14 January, fasal ka mausam) — alag naam: Lohri/Maghi (Punjab), Magh Bihu (Assam), Khichdi Parv (UP/Bihar), Uttarayan (Gujarat), Poush Sankranti (Bengal), Pongal (Tamil Nadu), Makaravilakku (Kerala), Pedda Panduga (Andhra), Makara Sankramana (Karnataka), Shishur Saenkraat (Kashmir).
  • Panchatantra — Sanskrit mool kam se kam 2,200 saal purana; ~200 roopantar, 50+ bhashaon mein; SE Asia, Arab duniya, Europe tak.
  • Ramayana aur Mahabharata — mool mein ~7,000 prishth; dharma ki punahsthapana ki kahani. Sirf Tamil Nadu mein Mahabharata ke ~100 lok-roop.
  • Janjaatiyon ke apne roop: Bhil, Gond, Munda; North-East aur Himalaya (Kashmir samet). 'Pancha Pandavar' pathar, Nilgiris — Irula tribals ka shrine.
  • K.S. Singh: 'desh mein shayad hi koi jagah ho jahan lok-kathaon ke anusaar Pandav na gaye hon.' Nehru: anpadh gaanv-waale bhi mahakavyon ke sainkdon shlok jaante the.

Yaad karne ki trick

People of India = 4635-325-25

'Chhiyaalis-paintees samudaay, teen-do-paanch bhasha, pachchees lipi'.

Sankranti ka naksha

Punjab = Lohri, Assam = Bihu, Gujarat = Uttarayan (patang!), Tamil = Pongal, UP-Bihar = Khichdi.

Sari silk route

Banaras (UP) → Kanjivaram (TN) → Paithani (Maharashtra) → Patola (Gujarat) → Muga (Assam) → Mysore (Karnataka).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 'People of India' project ne kitni bhashaein ginin?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 325 — 325 languages, 25 scripts, 4,635 communities.

  2. Q2. 'India offers unity in diversity' kisne kaha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Vincent Smith — British historian Vincent Smith.

  3. Q3. 17th century mein kin deshon ne Indian chintz ka import ban kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) England aur France — Apne kapda udyog ko bachane ke liye England aur France ne ban lagaya.

  4. Q4. Tamil Nadu mein Makara Sankranti ko kya kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Pongal — Pongal — Tamil Nadu; Bihu — Assam; Lohri — Punjab; Uttarayan — Gujarat.

  5. Q5. Panchatantra ke lagbhag kitne roopantar hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 200, 50+ bhashaon mein — ~200 adaptations, 50 se zyada bhashaon mein.

  6. Q6. Nilgiris ke 'Pancha Pandavar' shrine ki dekh-rekh kaun karta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Irula — Irula tribals — Pandavon ke us kshetra se guzarne ki yaad mein.
