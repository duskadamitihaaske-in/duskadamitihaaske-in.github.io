Family and Community

Parivaar — samaaj ki pehli ikaai; community — ek-doosre ka sahara.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Parivaar — samaaj ki pehli 'eent'

Jaise deewaar eenton se banti hai, waise samaaj parivaaron se. Parivaar samaaj ki sabse buniyaadi aur sabse purani ikaai hai. Joint family — kai peedhiyan saath: dada-dadi, maata-pita, chacha-chachi, bhai-behen, cousins. Nuclear family — sirf pati-patni aur bachche (kabhi ek parent aur bachche). Bharatiya bhaashaon mein rishton ke kitne shabd — bua, tau, mausi, nana… Zyaadatar mein 'cousin' ka shabd hi nahi — sab 'bhai-behen'!

Parivaar ka gond — prem aur saath

Rishton ka aadhaar: prem, dekhbhaal, sahyog (cooperation) aur ek-doosre par nirbharta (interdependence). Parivaar ek school jaisa hai jahan hum moolya seekhte hain: ahimsa, daan (dena), seva, tyaag, aur apna dharma (kartavya) nibhaana.

Shalini ka Onam (Kerala)

Onam se pehle Chittappa (chacha) ki naukri chali gayi. Parivaar ne faisla kiya ki phir bhi sabke liye naye kapde aayenge — par kharcha sambhaal kar. Shalini ne khud resham ki jagah cotton ki dress chuni. Parivaar mein Acchamma (dadi), Chitti (chachi), Chinni (cousin). Seekh: parivaar mushkil mein saath khada hota hai, aur bachche bhi tyaag kar sakte hain.

Tenzing ka ghar (Meghalaya)

Tenzing ki maa handicraft cooperative mein vyast rehti hain, to pita ghar ka kaam sambhaalte hain; dada samaaj seva karte hain; bade kharche par maata-pita milkar faisla lete hain. Seekh: ghar ke kaam kisi ek ke nahi, sab ke hain.

Samudaay (community) — parivaar se bada parivaar

Community = aapas mein jude logon ka samooh. Saajhe sansaadhan — paani, charagaah, jungle ki upaj — ke apne niyam, aksar likhe nahi, par sabke kartavya tay. Halma parampara — Bhil samudaay, Jhabua (MP): log bina paise ke milkar hazaaron ped lagaate aur barish ka paani rokne ke liye khaaiyan khodte — Dharti Maa ki seva. Mahesh Sharma (Shivganga andolan) — Padma Shri 2019.

Madad ke anokhe udaharan

Chennai baadh (2015) — dharmik-aadhyaatmik sansthaon ne khaana banaakar baanta. Kamal Parmar (Ahmedabad) — auto workshop ke maalik, roz shaam 5:30–9:30 gareeb bachchon ko padhaate aur khaana dete — 150 bachche! Shehron mein naye samudaay: RWA (Residents' Welfare Association) — kachra, safaai, paaltu jaanwaron ke niyam. 'Community' ek lachila shabd — jaati, dharm, kshetra (Mumbai ka Parsi samudaay), pesha, ruchi; school mein NSS, NCC, clubs.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Family = samaaj ki sabse buniyaadi aur sabse pracheen ikaai.
  • Joint family: kai peedhiyan saath (dada-dadi, mata-pita, chacha-chachi, bhai-behen, cousins). Nuclear family: dampati aur bachche (kabhi ek parent aur bachche).
  • Indian bhashaon mein rishton ke bahut shabd (bua, tau, mausi, nana...). Zyaadatar mein 'cousin' ka shabd nahi — sab 'bhai-behen'.
  • Rishton ka aadhaar: prem, dekhbhaal, cooperation (saath kaam karna), interdependence.
  • Parivaar ek 'school' — mool-moolya: ahimsa, dāna (dena), sevā, tyāga. Apna dharma (kartavya) nibhaana.
  • Shalini (Kerala): Onam par chacha ki naukri gayi, to parivaar ne sabke liye kapde liye; Shalini ne silk ki jagah cotton dress li. Acchamma (dadi), Chittappa (chacha), Chitti (chachi), Chinni (cousin).
  • Tenzing (Meghalaya): maa handicraft cooperative mein busy, to pita ghar ke kaam karte; dada social work; bade kharch par mata-pita milkar faisla.
  • Community = jude hue logon ka samooh. Saajhe sansaadhan (paani, charagaah, van-upaj) ke niyam — aksar likhe nahi gaye, par sab ke kartavya tay.
  • Halma parampara — Bhil samudaay, Jhabua (MP): hazaaron ped, rainwater ke liye khaaiyan — bina paise, Dharti Maa ki seva. Mahesh Sharma (Shivganga movement) — Padma Shri 2019.
  • Chennai floods 2015: dharmik-adhyatmik sansthaon ne khaana banakar baanta.
  • Kamal Parmar (Ahmedabad): auto-fabrication workshop ke maalik; roz 5:30–9:30 pm garib bachchon ko padhaya aur khaana diya — 150 bachche.
  • Nayi communities: Residents' Welfare Associations (RWA) — kachra, safai, pets ke apne niyam. Communities ek-doosre par nirbhar.
  • 'Community' lachila shabd: jati, dharm, kshetra (Mumbai ka Parsi samudaay), pesha, ruchi; school mein NSS, NCC, clubs.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 'Halma' parampara kis samudaay se judi hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Bhil — Jhabua (MP) ke Bhil — sankat mein saamuhik madad; ped lagaye, paani bachaya.

  2. Q2. Shivganga movement ke Mahesh Sharma ko Padma Shri kab mila?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 2019 — 2019 — Bhil communities ke saath kaam ke liye.

  3. Q3. Zyaadatar Indian bhashaon mein kaun sa shabd nahi milta?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Cousin — Cousins ko bhi bhai-behen hi kehte hain — gehre rishte dikhata hai.

  4. Q4. Nuclear family mein kaun hote hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Dampati aur unke bachche — Couple aur unke bachche; kabhi ek parent aur bachche.

  5. Q5. Shalini ki kahani kis tyohaar ke samay ki hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Onam — Kerala ka Onam.

  6. Q6. Residents' Welfare Association kis cheez ka udaharan hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Urban community jo apne niyam banati hai — Pichhle 30–40 saal mein bani nayi communities — kachra, safai, pets ke niyam.
