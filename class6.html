Locating Places on the Earth

Map, latitude-longitude aur time zones — dharti par kisi bhi jagah ka 'address' nikalna.

Notes

  • Map = kisi area ki drawing, upar se dekhi hui (top view). Atlas = maps ka collection (book).
  • Map ke 3 prakaar: Physical (pahad, nadi, samudra), Political (desh, rajya, boundary, capital), Thematic (ek khaas jaankari).
  • Map ke 3 components: Distance (Scale), Direction, Symbols. Example: scale 1 cm = 500 m.
  • 4 cardinal directions: North (upar), East, South, West — clockwise. 4 intermediate: NE, SE, SW, NW. Map par 'N' arrow north dikhata hai.
  • India ke maps ke standard symbols Survey of India (sarkari sanstha) ne fix kiye hain.
  • Earth lagbhag sphere hai — poles par thodi chapti. Globe = sphere par bana map, flat map se zyada sahi.
  • Latitude = Equator se doori. Equator = 0° (sabse bada circle), poles = 90°N aur 90°S. Parallels poles ki taraf chhote hote jaate hain.
  • Latitude aur climate: Equator ke paas torrid (garam), beech mein temperate, poles ke paas frigid (thanda).
  • Longitude = Prime Meridian se doori. Meridians pole se pole tak half-circles, sab ki length barabar. 0° se 180° E/W.
  • Prime Meridian (0°) Greenwich (London) se guzarti hai — 1884 mein tay hua. Delhi ≈ 29°N, 77°E; New York 74°W; Tokyo 140°E.
  • Bharat ki apni prime meridian thi — Madhya Rekha, Ujjayini (Ujjain) se. Astronomer Varahamihira ~1,500 saal pehle wahin kaam karte the.
  • India ka vistaar: latitude 8°N–37°N, longitude 68°E–97°E.
  • Earth 24 ghante mein 360° ghoomti hai → 15° = 1 ghanta, 1° = 4 minute. East mein time aage, West mein peeche.
  • IST = GMT + 5 ghante 30 minute. USA mein 6 time zones, Russia mein 11.
  • International Date Line ≈ 180°. Eastward cross karo → ek din ghatao; Westward cross karo → ek din jodo.

Yaad karne ki trick

LAT = Ladder ki seedhi

Latitude lines seedhi ke 'paidaan' jaisi aadi (horizontal). LONGitude = LONG lines, pole se pole khadi.

15-1-4 formula

15° = 1 ghanta, 1° = 4 minute. Do jagah ka longitude farak × 4 = minute ka farak.

Date Line: 'Pashchim jao, din badhao'

Westward cross → +1 din. Eastward cross → −1 din.

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Greenwich — 1884 mein kai deshon ne Greenwich (London) wali meridian ko 0° longitude maana.

  2. Q2. Indian Standard Time, GMT se kitna aage hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 5 ghante 30 minute — IST = GMT + 5:30. Isliye London mein dopahar 12 baje India mein shaam 5:30 hote hain.

  3. Q3. Pracheen Bharat ki prime meridian 'Madhya Rekha' kis shahar se guzarti thi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Ujjayini — Ujjayini (Ujjain) astronomy ka bada kendra tha; saare Indian astronomical texts isi meridian ko reference maante the.

  4. Q4. Porbandar aur Tinsukia mein longitude ka farak 30° maana jaaye, to local time ka farak kitna hoga?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 2 ghante — 15° = 1 ghanta, to 30° = 2 ghante. Tinsukia (east) mein suraj pehle doobta hai.

  5. Q5. Delhi (29°N) aur Bengaluru (13°N) dono ka longitude ~77°E hai. Unke local time mein farak?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Lagbhag zero — Local time longitude se tay hota hai, latitude se nahi. Longitude same hai to local time lagbhag same.

  6. Q6. International Date Line ko eastward cross karne par kya karte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ek din ghatate hain — Eastward → ek din ghatao (Monday → Sunday). Westward → ek din jodo.

Oceans and Continents

Dharti ka teen-chauthai paani, ek-chauthai zameen — 5 mahasagar, 7 mahadweep aur unka jeevan par asar.

Notes

  • Earth ki satah ka lagbhag 3/4 hissa paani, 1/4 se thoda zyada zameen — isliye 'Blue Planet'.
  • Indian Navy ka motto 'Sham no Varunah' — Varuna, samudra aur jal se jude Vedic devta.
  • Northern Hemisphere mein zameen zyada hai; Southern Hemisphere mein paani zyada.
  • 5 oceans size mein: Pacific (sabse bada) > Atlantic > Indian > Southern (Antarctic) > Arctic (sabse chhota). Sab aapas mein jude hain.
  • Indian Ocean: North mein Asia, West mein Africa, East mein Australia, South mein Southern Ocean. India ke West mein Arabian Sea, East mein Bay of Bengal.
  • Samudra ka paani khaara; freshwater bahut kam — glaciers, nadiyaan, jheelein, vayumandal aur groundwater mein.
  • Monsoon ki baarish samudra se aati hai; cyclone aur tsunami bhi samudra se. Tsunami = bhookamp/jwalamukhi se bani vishal lehar.
  • 26 December 2004: Indonesia ke bhookamp se tsunami — India samet 14 desh, 2 lakh se zyada maut. Andaman-Nicobar, Tamil Nadu, Kerala prabhavit.
  • Indian Ocean Tsunami Warning System; India mein National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA).
  • Continents 4 se 7 tak gine ja sakte hain (Eurasia, Africa-Eurasia, America). Sabse prachalit ginti: 7.
  • Size: Asia > Africa > North America > South America > Antarctica > Europe > Australia (sabse chhota).
  • Olympic rings = 5 inhabited continents: Africa, America, Asia, Australia, Europe.
  • Greenland = duniya ka sabse bada island. India ke paas 1,300+ islands — Andaman & Nicobar (Bay of Bengal), Lakshadweep (Arabian Sea).
  • Indian Antarctic Programme 1981 se; pehla base Dakshin Gangotri (1983); baad mein 2 aur base. ~40 teams ne research ki.
  • Duniya ki aadhi se zyada oxygen samudri flora banati hai — oceans = 'planet's lungs'. World Oceans Day = 8 June (UN).
  • Khatre: plastic pollution (har saal lakhon ton), overfishing.

Yaad karne ki trick

Bade se chhote: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic.

Continents: 'Aaj Aloo Naan Saath, Achaar Ek Aur'

Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia (bade se chhote).

Antarctica: 81-83

Programme 1981, Dakshin Gangotri 1983 — pehle programme, do saal baad ghar.

Chapter03

Theme A · India and the World: Land and the People

Landforms and Life

Pahad, pathaar aur maidan — har landform ka apna mausam, jeevan, chunautiyan aur avsar.

Notes

  • Landform = dharti ki satah ka physical feature; laakhon saalon mein bante hain. 3 mukhya: Mountains, Plateaus, Plains.
  • Mountain: chauda base, khadi dhalaan, patli choti. Kam oonchai, golai wali choti = hill.
  • Ranges: Himalayas (Asia), Alps (Europe), Andes (South America).
  • Mount Everest 8,848 m (Tibet-China/Nepal); Kanchenjunga (Nepal-Sikkim); Aconcagua (Andes ki sabse oonchi); Kilimanjaro (East Africa — akela, kisi range ka hissa nahi); Mont Blanc (Alps); Anamudi / Anai Peak (Kerala, South India ki sabse oonchi).
  • Himalayas = young mountains (oonche, nukeele, abhi bhi badh rahe). Aravalli = bahut purane, erosion se gol.
  • Montane forest: conifer ped — pine, fir, spruce, deodar. Aur oonchai par grasses, moss, lichen.
  • Kalidasa ki Kumarasambhava Himalaya ki vandana se shuru hoti hai.
  • Ganga ~2,500 km. Tributaries: Yamuna, Ghaghara (Himalaya se); Son (Vindhya se).
  • Pahadon mein: terrace farming, herding, tourism, teerth yatra. Chunautiyan: landslide, avalanche, flash flood, cloudburst, thand.
  • Bachendri Pal — Everest chadhne wali pehli Indian mahila (1984; Padma Shri 1984, Padma Bhushan 2019). Arunima Sinha — ek pair khone ke baad Everest 2013, har continent ki sabse oonchi choti; Padma Shri 2015.
  • Everest ke naam: Tibetan Chomolungma ('Mother Goddess of the World'), Nepali Sagarmatha ('Goddess of the Sky'). Mount Kailash — Hindu, Buddhist, Jain aur Bon ke liye pavitra.
  • Plateau: aas-paas se ooncha, upar lagbhag samtal, kinare khade. Tibetan Plateau — sabse bada aur ooncha, avg 4,500 m, 'Roof of the World', ~2,500 km lamba.
  • Deccan Plateau — duniya ke sabse purane plateaus mein, jwalamukhi se bana. Plateaus = 'storehouses of minerals'.
  • East African Plateau: sona, heera. Chhota Nagpur: iron, coal, manganese. Lava plateau mein upjaau kaali mitti.
  • Jharne: Victoria (Zambezi), Hundru (Subarnarekha, Chhota Nagpur), Jog (Sharavati, Western Ghats), Nohkalikai — 340 m (Cherrapunji Plateau, Meghalaya).
  • Plains: samtal, aam taur par sea level se 300 m se kam. Floodplains mein nadiyaan sediments bichhati hain → upjaau mitti.
  • Ganga plain mein ~40 crore log (India ki 1/4 se zyada aabadi). Fasal: chawal, gehun, makka, jau, millets; fibre: cotton, jute, hemp. Irrigation se groundwater ghat raha hai.
  • Desert: hot — Sahara, Thar; cold — Gobi. Antarctica ko bhi kuch experts desert kehte hain.
  • Tamil Sangam ke 5 tinai: Kurinji (pahad – shikaar), Mullai (ghaas/jungle – pashupalan), Marudam (upjaau maidan – kheti), Neydal (tat – machhli/samudri yatra), Palai (sookha – yatra aur yuddh).

Yaad karne ki trick

Tinai ka desi code

Kurinji = Kursi oonchi (pahad), Mullai = Maal-mavesi, Marudam = Mitti-kheti, Neydal = Neer (samudra), Palai = Pyaas (registaan).

Kili akeli

Kilimanjaro kisi range mein nahi — akela khada pahad.

Jharna-Nadi jodi

Jog–Sharavati, Hundru–Subarnarekha, Victoria–Zambezi, Nohkalikai–Meghalaya (340 m).

Timeline and Sources of History

Itihaas ka samay kaise naapein, kin sources se jaanein, aur shuruaati insaan kaise jeete the.

Notes

  • E.H. Carr: History = 'present aur past ke beech kabhi na khatam hone wala samvaad'.
  • Geologists — mitti, pathar, pahad, nadi. Palaeontologists — fossils. Anthropologists — maanav samaaj aur sanskriti. Archaeologists — khudai se mile avshesh. Epigraphists — pracheen inscriptions.
  • Aaj duniya mein Gregorian calendar: 12 mahine, 365 din, har 4 saal leap year. Century year sirf tab leap jab 400 se divide ho — 2000 leap, 1900 nahi.
  • AD → ab CE (Common Era); BC → ab BCE (Before Common Era). BCE ulti ginti, CE seedhi.
  • Year zero nahi hota: 1 BCE ke turant baad 1 CE. BCE se CE tak saal = dono jodo − 1. Example: Buddha ~560 BCE → 2024 tak = 560 + 2024 − 1 = 2,583 saal.
  • Century = 100 saal. 21st century = 2001–2100. 3rd century BCE = 300 BCE se 201 BCE.
  • Millennium = 1,000 saal. 3rd millennium CE 2001 se 3000 tak. 1st millennium BCE = 1000 BCE se 1 BCE.
  • Panchanga — sooraj-chaand ki sthiti par aadhaarit; grahan, sooryoday-sooryast, tyohaar ke muhurat batata hai.
  • Sources of history: Literary (Vedas, Itihasas, manuscripts, kavita-naatak), Archaeological (coins, inscriptions, copper plates, monuments, tools, pottery, burials), Artistic (paintings, sculptures), Oral (lok-kathayein), Foreign accounts (travelogues), Scientific (genetics, pracheen climate studies). Haal ke itihaas ke liye newspapers aur electronic media.
  • Historian = jigsaw puzzle jodne wala; sources kabhi milte hain, kabhi takraate hain.
  • Homo sapiens (modern insaan) ~3,00,000 saal se dharti par.
  • Shuruaati insaan: hunter-gatherers, groups (bands) mein, rock shelters/gufaon mein; aag ka use, pathar ke auzaar, rock paintings, beads.
  • Last Ice Age: 1 lakh saal se pehle se lekar ~12,000 saal pehle tak. Uske baad kheti, pashupaalan, nadiyon ke paas basna.
  • Chieftains, saamuhik zameen (niji malikana nahi). Hamlet → village → town. Pottery; dhaatu mein pehle copper, baad mein iron.

Yaad karne ki trick

BCE + CE − 1

BCE aur CE ke beech ke saal: dono ko jodo, phir 1 ghatao (year zero nahi hai).

Century nikalna

Saal ke pehle do ank + 1. 1828 → 18 + 1 = 19th century. (Jo saal 00 par khatam ho — 1900 — wo 19th mein hi rahega.)

4 '-logist' ka shortcut

Geo = Ground, Palaeo = Patthar mein fossil, Anthropo = Aadmi ka samaaj, Archaeo = Avshesh ki khudai.

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (D) 2000 — Century years tabhi leap hote hain jab 400 se divide hon. 2000 ÷ 400 = 5.

India, That Is Bharat

Bharat ke pracheen naam — apne logon ne kya kaha, videshiyon ne kya kaha.

Notes

  • Rig Veda (sabse pracheen granth) ne North-West ko kaha 'Sapta Sindhava' = saat nadiyon ki dharti. 'Sindhu' = Indus nadi ya aam nadi.
  • Mahabharata mein kshetra: Kashmira (Kashmir), Kurukshetra (Haryana), Vanga (Bengal), Pragjyotisha (Assam), Kaccha (Kutch), Kerala aadi.
  • Mahabharata ne poore upmahadweep ke liye 'Bharatavarsha' aur 'Jambudvipa' shabd use kiye.
  • Bharatavarsha = 'Bharaton ka desh'. 'Bharata' pehli baar Rig Veda mein — ek pramukh Vedic jan-samooh.
  • Jambudvipa = 'jamun ke phal ka dweep'. Ashoka (~250 BCE) ne inscription mein poore India ke liye Jambudvipa likha — jismein aaj ka Bangladesh, Pakistan aur Afghanistan ka hissa bhi tha.
  • Vishnu Purana: 'samudra ke uttar aur himalaya ke dakshin wala desh Bharata kehlata hai'.
  • Tamil kavita (~2,000 saal purani): 'dakshin mein Kumari se, uttar mein mahaan parvat tak, purab-pashchim ke samudron tak'.
  • Samvidhan (lagu 1950): 'India, that is Bharat'; Hindi mein 'Bharat arthat India'.
  • Persians (pracheen Iran) — 6th century BCE mein Indus kshetra jeeta; 'Sindhu' ko kaha 'Hind', 'Hidu', 'Hindu'. Yahan 'Hindu' sirf bhaugolik shabd — dharm nahi.
  • Greeks: 'Indoi' / 'Indike' — Greek mein 'h' akshar nahi tha, isliye gira diya.
  • Chinese: 'Yintu' / 'Yindu'; ek aur shabd 'Tianzhu' = 'heavenly master' — Buddha ki dharti ke liye samman.
  • Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang) 7th century CE mein China se aaya, 17 saal raha, Buddhist granth le gaya aur Sanskrit se Chinese mein anuvaad kiya.
  • 'Hindustan' pehli baar ek Persian inscription mein ~1,800 saal pehle; baad mein zyaadatar aakrantaon ne yahi naam use kiya.

Yaad karne ki trick

S → H → I chain

Sindhu → Hindu (Persian, S ki jagah H) → Indoi (Greek, H gira) → India. Chinese: Sindhu → Hindu → Indu → Yindu.

Ashoka = Jambu

'A' se Ashoka, 'J' se Jambudvipa — samraat ne jamun ka naam chuna.

Xuanzang = 7 aur 17

7th century mein aaya, 17 saal raha.

The Beginnings of Indian Civilisation

Harappan / Indus-Sarasvati sabhyata (c. 2600–1900 BCE) — planned cities, water management, vyapaar aur patan.

Notes

  • Civilisation ke lakshan: government/administration, urbanism (town planning, paani, drainage), crafts, trade (andar-bahar), writing, cultural ideas, aur shehron ko khilane laayak productive agriculture.
  • Mesopotamia (Iraq-Syria) ~6,000 saal pehle; Egypt kuch sadiyon baad.
  • Indus-Sarasvati civilisation: c. 2600–1900 BCE. ~3500 BCE se gaon → town; ~2600 BCE ke aas-paas town → city. Ise 'First Urbanisation of India' kehte hain.
  • Naam: Indus, Harappan, Indus-Sarasvati, Sindhu-Sarasvati. 'Harappan' isliye kyunki Harappa (Pakistan ka Punjab) pehla khoda gaya shahar — 1920-21. 'Indus Valley' naam ab purana (obsolete), kyunki sabhyata Indus se bahut aage faili thi.
  • Sarasvati nadi = aaj Ghaggar (India) / Hakra (Pakistan) — ab mausami. Rig Veda mein devi aur nadi dono roop mein; baad ke granth uske sookhne ka zikr karte hain.
  • Shahar → aaj ka sthaan: Harappa – Punjab (Pak), Mohenjo-daro – Sindh, Dholavira – Gujarat (Rann of Kutch), Rakhigarhi – Haryana, Kalibangan – Rajasthan, Ganweriwala – Cholistan (Pak), Lothal – Gujarat. Sarasvati basin: Farmana, Bhirrana, Banawali (Haryana).
  • Town planning: chaudi sadkein (cardinal directions mein), fortification, upper town (elite) aur lower town (aam log). Dholavira mein 3 zones, neenv pathar ki. Chhote-bade gharon ki construction quality ek jaisi, eenton se.
  • Great Bath (Mohenjo-daro) ~12 × 7 m, bitumen se waterproof, kone mein drain. Public bath wali vyakhya khaarij (zyaadatar gharon mein apne bathroom the); royal family ya dharmik anushthaan ka tank — bahas jaari.
  • Drains sadkon ke neeche. Mohenjo-daro mein ~700 eenton ke kuen. Dholavira mein kam se kam 6 reservoirs; sabse bada 73 m lamba; chattaan kaat ke bana ek reservoir 33 m.
  • Khaana: jau, gehun, millets, kabhi chawal, daalein, sabziyaan. Eurasia mein sabse pehle cotton ugaya. Plough (Banawali ka clay model). Bartanon mein haldi, adrak, kela, dairy ke nishaan.
  • Vyapaar: carnelian beads (laal pathar, Gujarat), shell bangles; shayad copper import. Bronze = copper + tin. Ivory comb Oman mein mila; beads Susa (Iran) mein. India ki pehli tez samudri gatividhi.
  • Lothal (Gujarat) ka vishaal basin — 217 m × 36 m, do football ground se thoda lamba — dockyard maana jaata hai.
  • Seals: steatite (naram pathar, garam karke sakht), kuch cm ke; unicorn, bull, horned tiger + likhawat ke chinh. Lipi abhi tak padhi nahi gayi; seals vyapaar se jude.

  • Swami Vivekananda: 'Har kaam ko pooja ki tarah karo.'

Notes

  • Kautilya (Arthashastra): 'Samriddhi ki jad aarthik gatividhi hai.'
  • Economic activities ko milti-julti visheshtaon ke aadhaar par economic sectors mein baanta: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary.
  • Primary sector: seedhe prakriti se — kheti, mining, machhli paalan, pashupaalan, forestry, poultry (ande). Greenhouse farming bhi.
  • Secondary sector: primary ke output ko process/transform karna — manufacturing, construction (imarat, sadak), utilities (paani, bijli, gas), solar power. Udaharan: atta chakki, moongphali se tel, chai patti, lakdi se furniture/kaagaz, cotton se kapda, iron se steel → cars.
  • India 2022 (SIAM): passenger vehicles 45 lakh, commercial 10.3 lakh, three-wheelers 8.6 lakh, two-wheelers 2 crore.
  • Tertiary sector (service sector): pehle do sectors ko sahara — transport, banking, trade, retail, healthcare, teaching, communication, software, hotels, warehouses, repair.
  • AMUL = Anand Milk Union Limited, Anand district (Gujarat). 1940s mein kisaan middlemen se pareshaan; Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel ne cooperative banane ki salaah di.
  • AMUL 1946 — netritva: Tribhuvandas Patel (vakeel, swatantrata senani) aur Dr. Verghese Kurien (engineer). Mahilaon samet kisaanon ko niyantran mila.
  • Pasteurisation = doodh ko tay taapmaan tak garam karke bacteria maarna. Factory mein makkhan, milk powder; aaj ghee, cheese aadi; export bhi.
  • AMUL mein teeno sector: doodh nikaalna (primary) → makkhan/powder banana (secondary) → transport, trading, retail (tertiary).
  • Doosre milk co-ops: Nandini (Karnataka), Mother Dairy (Delhi-NCR), Aavin (Tamil Nadu), Vijaya (Andhra Pradesh), Kevi (Nagaland), Sudha (Bihar), Verka (Punjab).
  • Kitaab ka safar: ped se pulp (primary) → kaagaz aur chhapai (secondary) → transport aur dukaan (tertiary).
  • Recycling: 1 ton kaagaz recycle → 17 ped, 2.5 cubic metre landfill bachta; 70% kam urja aur paani.

C. Short-answer (model answers)

  1. 1Local time aur standard time mein kya farak hai?

    Local time har jagah ke longitude se tay hota hai — sooraj jab sir par ho tab dopahar. Ek desh mein anek local times asuvidhajanak hain, isliye desh ek meridian chun kar standard time apnata hai. India mein IST, jo GMT se 5:30 ghante aage hai. Isliye Gujarat aur Assam ka local time alag hote hue bhi ghadi mein ek hi samay (IST) chalta hai.

  2. 2Harappan sabhyata ke kai naam kyun hain?

    Harappan — pehla khoda gaya shahar Harappa (1920-21). Indus — shuru mein zyaadatar sites Indus ke maidan mein mili. Indus-Sarasvati / Sindhu-Sarasvati — baad mein Sarasvati (Ghaggar-Hakra) basin mein bahut saari sites (Rakhigarhi, Ganweriwala, Kalibangan) mili. 'Indus Valley' ab obsolete, kyunki sabhyata Gujarat aur Haryana tak faili thi.

  3. 3Buddhism ke mukhya vichaar likhiye.

    Dukh ka kaaran avidya (agyaan) aur attachment (moh) hai; inhe door karne ka maarg Buddha ne bataya. Ahimsa — kisi ko chot na pahunchana, vichaar mein bhi. Sachchi aantarik anushasan: 'Paani se koi pavitra nahi hota; jismein satya aur dharma ho wahi pavitra hai.' Sangha ki sthapana — bhikshu-bhikshuni shiksha phailaate the.

  4. 4Sari 'unity in diversity' ko kaise darshati hai?

    Sari ek hi tarah ka bina sila kapda hai (unity), jo lagbhag poore India mein pehna jaata hai. Par kapda (cotton, silk), bunai, design, rang aur pehenne ke tareeke har kshetra mein alag hain — Banarasi, Kanjivaram, Paithani, Patola, Muga, Mysore (diversity). Mahilaon ne iske anek upyog bhi khoje. Vaishali ki relief se pata chalta hai ki ye kuch sadi BCE se pehna ja raha hai.

  5. 5Sarkaar ke teen star kyun chahiye?

    Har samasya ka hal ek hi jagah se nahi ho sakta. Chhoti, sthaniya baatein (gaon ki sadak, naali) local government sambhalti hai; kai zilon ki samasya State; aur poore desh ke vishay (defence, currency, videsh neeti) ya vishaal aapda Central government. Baadh ka udaharan: chhoti baadh local, badi State, vishaal mein Centre sena aur rahat bhejta hai.

  6. 6Panchayati Raj ke teen star aur unke kaam batayiye.

    Gram Panchayat (village) — Gram Sabha dwara chuni; Sarpanch/Pradhan ke netritva mein gaon ke kaam. Panchayat Samiti (block) — Gram Panchayaton aur Zila Parishad ke beech kadi; sabki development plans jodti hai, funds aur schemes (PMGSY) dilati hai. Zila Parishad (district) — poore zile ki yojana aur samanvay. Teeno mein 1/3 seats mahilaon ke liye.

  7. 7Economic aur non-economic activities mein farak udaharan sahit.

    Economic activity paise ya money's worth ke badle hoti hai — Geeta Aunty ki pilot ki naukri (salary), Sahil ki mazdoori (wage + kind), doctor ki fee. Non-economic activity prem, dekhbhaal ya seva se hoti hai — mata-pita ka khaana banana, dada ka muft padhana, langar. Dono ka mahatva hai: ek kamaai deti hai, doosri samaaj aur parivaar ko jodti hai.

  8. 8Teeno sectors ki aapsi nirbharta AMUL se samjhaiye.

    Kisaan gaay-bhains se doodh nikaalte hain — primary. Doodh pasteurise karke makkhan, ghee, cheese, milk powder banate hain — secondary. Truck, rail, hawai jahaaz se transport aur retail dukaanon par bikri — tertiary. Koi ek sector ruk jaaye to poori chain ruk jaati hai: doodh hoga par factory nahi, ya product hoga par transport nahi.
