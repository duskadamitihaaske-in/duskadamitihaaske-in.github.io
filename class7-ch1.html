Theme A · India and the World: Land and the People

Geographical Diversity of India

Himalaya se islands tak — Bharat ke paanch bade bhaugolik kshetra aur unka jeevan par asar.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

'Saare jahan se achchha' — upar se kaisa dikhta hai India?

1984 mein Rakesh Sharma — antariksh jaane wale pehle Bharatiya — se PM Indira Gandhi ne poochha: upar se India kaisa dikhta hai? Jawab: 'Saare jahan se achchha!' India duniya ka 7th sabse bada desh hai. Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar ke saath milkar ye Indian Subcontinent (upmahadweep) banata hai. Isme 5 bade kshetra: pahad, maidaan, registan, dakshini pathaar aur dweep. Prakritik deewaarein: uttar mein Himalaya, pashchim mein Thar aur Arabian Sea, dakshin mein Indian Ocean, poorv mein Bay of Bengal.

Himalaya kaise bana? — carpet wali misaal

Laakhon saal pehle India Gondwana naam ke bade mahadweep ka hissa tha — padosi tha Africa! Dheere-dheere khisakta hua India ~5 crore saal pehle Asia (Eurasia) se takraaya. Socho farsh par ek carpet ko dono taraf se dhakelo — beech mein silvatein uth aayengi. Waise hi zameen sikud kar Himalaya bana! Aaj bhi India ~5 cm har saal Asia mein ghus raha hai aur Himalaya ~5 mm har saal ooncha ho raha hai. 'Himalaya' = hima (barf) + alaya (ghar) = barf ka ghar.

Himalaya — Asia ki paani ki tanki

Himalaya ~2,500 km lamba, 6 deshon mein (India, Nepal, Bhutan, China, Pakistan, Afghanistan); kai chotiyan 8,000 m se oonchi — 'Eight Thousanders'. Iski barf pighal kar Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra ko paani deti hai — isliye 'Water Tower of Asia'. Ganga ki mukhya dhaara Bhagirathi, Gaumukh (Gangotri glacier) se nikalti hai. Teen shrinkhla: Himadri (sabse oonchi — Everest, Kanchenjunga, saal bhar barf), Himachal (beech wali — Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling), Shivalik (sabse neechi — ghane jungle). Pashchimi Himalaya ke kath-kuni ghar (pathar + lakdi) garam rehte aur halke bhookamp sah lete. Ladakh — thanda registan, sardi mein −30°C; Pangong Tso khaari jheel; yak — doodh, oon, sawaari sab!

Uttar ke maidaan — mitti ka uphaar

Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra pahadon se mitti bahaa kar laati hain aur maidaan mein bichha deti hain — isliye ye zameen bahut upjaau, aabadi bahut zyada, aur samtal hone se sadak-rail bichhaana aasaan. Brahmaputra = 'Brahma ka putra' — kuch nadiyon mein ek jinhe purush naam diya gaya; garmi mein barf pighalne se badh jaati hai. Jaanwar: langur, baagh (Project Tiger), lambe munh wala gharial (vilupti ke kagaar par), aur mor (rashtriya pakshi).

Thar registan aur boodhi Aravalli

Thar — Rajasthan, Gujarat, Punjab, Haryana; ret ke tile 150 m tak oonche! Jaisalmer — 'Golden City'; Pushkar mela; paani bachaane ke liye taanka/kund (zameen mein bani tanki). Aravalli — duniya ke sabse purane pahadon mein (~2.5 arab saal); ghis kar neeche ho gaye; sabse oonchi choti Mount Abu; ye Thar ko poorv mein badhne se rokti hai. Yahan ki Zawar khaanon mein 800+ saal pehle Bharatiyon ne duniya mein sabse pehle zinc nikaalna seekha!

Dakshin ka pathaar aur do Ghats

Peninsular Plateau — teen taraf samundar. Pashchim mein Western Ghats (oonche, deewaar jaise, UNESCO; uttari hissa Sahyadri), poorv mein Eastern Ghats (neeche, toote-phoote). Beech mein Deccan Plateau, poorv ki taraf jhuka — isliye zyaadatar nadiyaan poorv behti hain: Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi → Bay of Bengal. Sirf Narmada aur Tapti pashchim → Arabian Sea. Jungle mein Santhal, Gond, Baiga, Bhil, Korku janjaatiyaan.

Samundar kinaare aur dweep

Tat-rekha 7,500 km se zyada. Pashchimi tat — patla, tez nadiyaan, estuary (Narmada, Tapti). Poorvi tat — chauda maidaan, delta (Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri); Chilika aur Pulicat lagoon. Lakshadweep — 36 coral dweep. Andaman & Nicobar — 500+ jwalamukhi se bane dweep; Cellular Jail; Barren Island — India ka ekmaatra sakriya jwalamukhi. Sundarbans — Ganga-Brahmaputra delta, mangrove jungle, Royal Bengal Tiger. Poorvottar: Garo-Khasi-Jaintia pahadiyan; Mawlynnong — 'Asia ka sabse saaf gaon'; pedon ki jadon se bane living root bridges!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Rakesh Sharma (1984) — pehle Indian astronaut; Indira Gandhi ke sawaal par bole: 'Saare jahan se achchha'.
  • India duniya ka 7th sabse bada desh. Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar ke saath mil kar Indian Subcontinent.
  • 5 kshetra: great mountain zone, Ganga-Indus plains, desert, southern peninsula, islands. Prakritik seema: uttar mein Himalaya, pashchim mein Thar aur Arabian Sea, dakshin mein Indian Ocean, purab mein Bay of Bengal.
  • Himalaya ~2,500 km lamba; kai chotiyan 8,000 m+ ('Eight Thousanders'); 6 desh: India, Nepal, Bhutan, China, Pakistan, Afghanistan. Barf pighal kar Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra ko paani → 'Water Tower of Asia'.
  • Bhagirathi (Ganga ki mukhya tributary) Gaumukh (Gangotri Glacier, Uttarakhand) se nikalti hai.
  • Banna: India pehle Gondwana ka hissa (padosi Africa!). ~5 crore (50 million) saal pehle Eurasia se takraya → zameen 'carpet ki tarah' sikud kar Himalaya bana. Aaj bhi India ~5 cm/saal Asia mein ghus raha hai; Himalaya ~5 mm/saal ooncha ho raha hai.
  • 'Himalaya' = hima (barf) + alaya (ghar) = barf ka ghar.
  • 3 range: Himadri (Greater — Everest, Kanchenjunga; saal bhar barf), Himachal (Lesser — Nainital, Darjeeling, Shimla, Mussoorie), Shivalik (Outer — sabse neeche, ghane jungle, plains se jodne wala zone).
  • Kath-kuni / dhajji-dewari — western Himalaya ka pathar+lakdi ka ghar; garam aur halke bhookamp mein majboot. Great Himalayan National Park (Himachal) — UNESCO World Heritage.
  • Ladakh = cold desert; sardi mein −30°C se neeche; Pangong Tso (khaara paani; tso = jheel); 'Moonland'. Yak — doodh, maas, oon, gobar, transport. Tyohaar: Losar, Hemis.
  • Gangetic (Northern) Plains: Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra ki mitti → bahut upjaau; badi aabadi; sadak-rail ka jaal aasaan. Brahmaputra = 'Brahma ka putra' (nadi ka purush naam), garmi mein badhti hai.
  • Wildlife: grey langur, tiger (Project Tiger), gharial (2.5–4.5 m, vilupti ke kagar par), mor (rashtriya pakshi).
  • Thar Desert: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Punjab, Haryana; sand dunes 150 m tak; Jaisalmer 'Golden City' (fort UNESCO); Pushkar Mela; paani bachane ke liye taanka / kund.
  • Aravalli — duniya ke sabse purane pahadon mein, ~2.5 billion saal; sabse oonchi choti Mount Abu (1,700 m+); Thar ko purab badhne se rokti hai. Marble, granite, zinc, copper; Zawar ki khaanein — 800+ saal pehle Indians ne duniya mein sabse pehle zinc nikaalna seekha. Forts: Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Ranthambore.
  • Peninsular Plateau — teen taraf paani; Western Ghats (oonche, deewar jaise; UNESCO; uttari bhaag = Sahyadri) aur Eastern Ghats (neeche, toote hue). Beech mein Deccan Plateau, purab ki taraf jhuka.
  • Purab bahne wali: Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi (Bay of Bengal). Pashchim bahne wali: Narmada, Tapti (Arabian Sea). Plateau ke jungle: Santhal, Gond, Baiga, Bhil, Korku.
  • Coastline 7,500 km+. West coast (Gujarat–Kerala): tez nadiyan, estuaries (Narmada, Tapti sabse badi). East coast: chaude maidan, deltas (Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri), Chilika aur Pulicat (lagoon).
  • Lakshadweep — Arabian Sea, 36 coral islands. Andaman & Nicobar — 500+ volcanic islands; Cellular Jail; Barren Island = India ka ekmatra active volcano.
  • Sundarbans — Ganga-Brahmaputra delta; aadha India aadha Bangladesh; mangroves, Royal Bengal Tiger, UNESCO.
  • North-East: Garo, Khasi, Jaintia hills (Meghalaya Plateau); Mawlynnong — 'Asia ka sabse saaf gaon'; living root bridges.

Yaad karne ki trick

Himalaya ki 3 manzil: 'Hi-Hi-Shi'

Upar se neeche — Himadri (Greater), Himachal (Lesser, hill stations), Shivalik (Outer).

5-5 ka jodi

India 5 cm/saal aage, Himalaya 5 mm/saal oonche, 5 crore saal pehle takkar.

Nadi ki disha: 'NaTa ulta'

Narmada-Tapti pashchim jaati hain (estuary); baaki Godavari-Krishna-Kaveri-Mahanadi purab (delta).

Islands: 36 vs 500

Lakshadweep = 36 coral; Andaman-Nicobar = 500+ volcanic, Barren Island active volcano.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Himalaya ko 'Water Tower of Asia' kyun kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Iski barf pighal kar Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra jaisi nadiyon ko paani deti hai — Garmi mein barf pighal kar badi nadiyon ko paani deti hai, jo karodon logon ka jeevan chalaati hain.

  2. Q2. Hill stations jaise Shimla, Darjeeling Himalaya ki kis range mein hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Himachal (Lesser) — Himachal ka mausam narm hai — isliye yahan basaavat aur hill stations hain.

  3. Q3. India ka ekmatra active volcano kaun sa hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Barren Island — Barren Island, Andaman & Nicobar.

  4. Q4. Duniya mein sabse pehle zinc nikaalne ke saboot kahan mile?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Zawar (Aravalli) — Zawar ki pracheen khaano se pata chalta hai ki 800+ saal pehle Indians ne zinc nikaalna seekha.

  5. Q5. Kaun si nadi pashchim ki taraf bahkar Arabian Sea mein girti hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Narmada — Narmada aur Tapti pashchim bahti hain; baaki peninsular nadiyan purab.

  6. Q6. Lakshadweep mein kitne coral islands hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 36 — 36 coral islands — Kerala ke Malabar tat ke paas.
