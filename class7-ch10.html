Theme D · Governance and Democracy

The Constitution of India — An Introduction

Samvidhan kya hai, kaise bana, kin cheezon se prerit hua, aur uski mukhya visheshtaayein — Preamble tak.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Samvidhan — desh ki 'rulebook'

Kabaddi khelne ke liye niyam chahiye — kitne khiladi, line kahan, point kaise milega. Waise hi desh chalaane ki rulebook hai Samvidhan (Constitution): sarkar ke teen ang kaise bane aur kya karein, unke beech checks & balances, naagrikon ke adhikaar aur kartavya, aur desh ke lambe lakshya. 26 January 1950 ko lagu hua — isliye Republic Day. Mool prati Parliament mein helium bhare kaanch ke case mein rakhi hai (helium kaagaz-syaahi se reaction nahi karti).

Kisne aur kab banaaya?

Samvidhan Sabha (Constituent Assembly) — 9 December 1946 ko pehli baar baithi; shuru mein 389 sadasya, vibhaajan ke baad 299; inme 15 mahilaayein. Asthaayi adhyaksh Dr. Sachidananda Sinha, phir adhyaksh Dr. Rajendra Prasad. Drafting Committee ke chairman Dr. B.R. Ambedkar — samaaj sudhaarak, pehle Law minister. ~3 saal ki mehnat ke baad 26 November 1949 ko apnaaya gaya (Constitution Day), 26 Jan 1950 ko lagu. Aaj ye duniya ka sabse bada likhit samvidhan — 25 parts, 12 schedules (1950 mein 22 parts, 8 schedules).

Prerna kahan se mili?

(1) Swatantrata andolan — samaanta, nyaay, azaadi, bhaichaara, sabko vote. (2) Hamari sabhyata — alag vichaaron ki sweekriti, prakriti ka samman, vasudhaiva kutumbakam, sarve bhavantu sukhinah, janapada-sangha, rajadharma. (3) Duniya — 'a no bhadrah kratavo yantu vishwatah' (har disha se achchhe vichaar aayein): France → liberty, equality, fraternity; Ireland → Directive Principles; USA → swatantra nyaypaalika; UK, Australia bhi. Prem Behari Narain Raizada ne haath se sundar likhaavat (calligraphy) mein likha; Nandalal Bose ki team ne Mohenjo-daro, gurukul, Ramayana, Gita, Nataraja, Nalanda tak ke chitra banaaye!

Teen zaroori hisse — Rights, DPSP, Duties

Fundamental Rights — court mein lagu karwa sakte ho: Art. 14 (kaanoon ke saamne samaanta), Art. 21 (jeevan aur swatantrata), shoshan ke khilaaf, Art. 21A (shiksha). Directive Principles (DPSP) — sarkar ke liye disha-nirdesh, court mein lagu nahi: Art. 38 (nyaay), 41 (kalyaan), 44 (Uniform Civil Code), 47 (poshan, swasthya), 48A (paryavaran, jeev), 49 (smarak). Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A, 1976) — samvidhan, jhanda, rashtragaan ka samman; desh ki raksha; mili-juli virasat; paryavaran; utkrishtata; 6–14 saal ke bachchon ko shiksha dilaana (maata-pita).

Jeevit dastavez — badalta bhi hai

Samvidhan 'living document' hai — sansad mein bahas ke baad amendment (sanshodhan). 1992 — 73rd Amendment se Panchayati Raj samvidhan mein. 2004 — Supreme Court: ghar par tiranga fehraana = abhivyakti ki azaadi! Begum Aizaz Rasul (1949): mahilaaon ki barabari India ke liye naya nahi, purana aadarsh hai.

Preamble — samvidhan ka 'parichay patra'

'We, the People of India' — shakti janta ki. Sovereign (koi bahari shakti hukm nahi de sakti), Socialist, Secular (koi sarkari dharm nahi, sab dharmon ka barabar samman), Democratic, Republic (rashtra pramukh chuna hua). Lakshya: Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity. 'Socialist', 'Secular', 'Integrity' — 42nd Amendment, 1976 mein jode gaye. President samvidhan ki 'raksha, sanrakshan aur bachaav' ki shapath leta hai.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • 26 January 1950 — Samvidhan lagu → Republic Day. Mool prati Parliament mein helium bhare kaanch ke case mein (helium kaagaz-syaahi se reaction nahi karti).
  • President samvidhan ki 'raksha, sanrakshan aur bachaav' ki shapath leta; PM, mantri, judges paalan ka vachan dete.
  • Constitution = desh ki 'rulebook': teen angon ka dhaancha aur kaam, unke beech checks & balances, nagrikon ke adhikaar aur kartavya, desh ke lambe lakshya. (Kabaddi rulebook udaharan.)
  • Aaj 25 parts, 12 schedules — duniya ka sabse bada likhit samvidhan. 1950 mein 22 parts, 8 schedules the.
  • Constituent Assembly — 9 December 1946 ko bani; shuru mein 389 sadasya, Partition ke baad 299; inme 15 mahilaayein. Sadasya praanton ki vidhan sabhaon ne chune. Shuruaati adhyaksh Dr. Sachidananda Sinha (asthaayi); chairman Dr. Rajendra Prasad.
  • Drafting Committee ke chairman Dr. B.R. Ambedkar — samaj sudhaarak, swatantra India ke pehle Law & Justice minister.
  • 26 November 1949 — adopt hua (lagbhag 3 saal ki mehnat); 26 January 1950 — lagu.
  • Prerna 1: Freedom movement — samaanta, nyaay, swatantrata, bandhutva, virasat ka sanrakshan; har vayask ko vote; Centre-State sambandh; amendment ki prakriya.
  • Prerna 2: Sabhyata ki virasat — alag vichaaron ki sweekriti, prakriti pavitra, gyaan, mahilaon ka samman, vasudhaiva kutumbakam, sarve bhavantu sukhinah; janapada-sangha, saptanga, rajdharma → Fundamental Duties.
  • Prerna 3: Duniya — 'ā no bhadrāh kratavo yantu viśhwatah' (har disha se achhe vichaar aayein). France → liberty, equality, fraternity (1789 kranti); Ireland → DPSP; USA → independent judiciary. UK, Australia bhi.
  • Kala: Prem Behari Narain Raizada ne haath se likha (calligraphy); Nandalal Bose ki team ne chitra banaaye — Mohenjo-daro se gurukul, Ramayana, Gita, Nataraja, Mahabalipuram, Akbar, Nalanda tak.
  • Fundamental Rights — court mein lagu karwa sakte: Right to Equality (Art. 14 — kanoon ke saamne samaanta), Right to Freedom (Art. 21 — jeevan aur vyaktigat swatantrata), Right against Exploitation, Art. 21-A (shiksha ka adhikaar).
  • DPSP — sarkar ke liye disha-nirdesh, court mein lagu nahi: Art. 38 (saamajik-rajnitik-aarthik nyaay), Art. 41 (welfare), Art. 44 (Uniform Civil Code), Art. 47 (poshan, jeevan star, swasthya), Art. 48-A (paryavaran, wildlife), Art. 49 (smarak).
  • Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A, 1976 mein jude): samvidhan, jhanda, rashtragaan ka samman; desh ki raksha; mili-juli sanskriti ki virasat; paryavaran; utkrishtata; 6–14 saal ke bachche ko shiksha ka avsar dena (mata-pita).
  • Begum Aizaz Rasul (22 Nov 1949): mahilaon ki samaanta India ke liye naya vichaar nahi, purana aadarsh hai.
  • Living document — amendments sansad mein bahas ke baad. 2004: Supreme Court — ghar par tiranga fehrana = freedom of expression. 1992: 73rd Amendment se Panchayati Raj samvidhan mein.
  • Preamble ke shabd: We, the People; Sovereign (koi bahari shakti hukm nahi de); Socialist; Secular (koi sarkari dharm nahi, sab dharmon ka barabar samman); Democratic; Republic (head of state chuna hua); Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity.
  • 'Socialist', 'Secular', 'Integrity' — 42nd Amendment, 1976 mein Preamble mein jode gaye.

Yaad karne ki trick

Teen tareekhein: '9-26-26'

9 Dec 1946 sabha bani → 26 Nov 1949 adopt (Constitution Day) → 26 Jan 1950 lagu (Republic Day).

Videshi udhaar: 'FIA'

France = liberty-equality-fraternity, Ireland = DPSP, America = independent judiciary.

1976 ka double dhamaka

42nd Amendment: Preamble mein Socialist-Secular-Integrity + Part IV-A Fundamental Duties.

Rights vs DPSP

Rights = 'Right hai to court jao'; DPSP = 'Disha hai, dheere-dheere'.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Samvidhan kab lagu hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 26 January 1950 — 26 Jan 1950 — isliye Republic Day. 26 Nov 1949 ko adopt hua tha.

  2. Q2. Drafting Committee ke chairman kaun the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar — Dr. B.R. Ambedkar; Rajendra Prasad Constituent Assembly ke chairman the.

  3. Q3. Partition ke baad Constituent Assembly mein kitne sadasya rahe?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 299 — 389 se ghat kar 299; inme 15 mahilaayein thi.

  4. Q4. Directive Principles (DPSP) ka vichaar kis desh ke samvidhan se prerit hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Ireland — Ireland. France → liberty-equality-fraternity; USA → independent judiciary.

  5. Q5. Fundamental Duties samvidhan mein kab jode gaye?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1976 — 1976 — Part IV-A.

  6. Q6. Panchayati Raj kis amendment se samvidhan mein aaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 73rd, 1992 — 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992.

  7. Q7. Mool samvidhan kis gas mein surakshit rakha gaya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Helium — Helium — kaagaz-syaahi se reaction nahi karti.
