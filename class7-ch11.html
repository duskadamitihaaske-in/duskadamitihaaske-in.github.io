Theme E · Economic Life Around Us

From Barter to Money

Barter se sikke, kaagazi note aur UPI tak — paise ka safar aur uske kaam.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Jab paisa nahi tha — barter

Socho tumhare paas gehu hai aur tumhe joote chahiye. Paisa nahi, to tum mochi ko gehu doge — ye hai barter system: bina paise ke saamaan/seva ki adla-badli. Duniya ka sabse pehla tareeka. Kaudi (cowrie), namak, chai, kapda, pashu, beej — sab 'paise' ki tarah chale. Ajeeb paise bhi: Yap dweep ke vishaal Rai pathar ke pahiye, Mexico ka taambe ka Tajadero, Solomon dweep ka laal pankhon wala Tevau!

Barter ki 5 mushkilein — kisaan aur bail

Ek kisaan apna bail deke kapda, namak aur bartan lena chahta hai: (1) Double coincidence of wants — kapde wale ko bhi bail chahiye, ye zaroori nahi! (2) Keemat naapne ka ek paimaana nahi — ek bail = kitna kapda? (3) Divisibility — aadha bail nahi de sakte! (4) Portability — bail har jagah le jaana mushkil. (5) Durability — gehu sad jaata hai. Isliye paisa aaya.

Aaj bhi barter — Junbeel Mela

Junbeel Mela (Morigaon, Assam; jun = chaand, beel = jheel) — 3 din, Agni Pooja se shuru; 15th sadi se Tiwa, Karbi, Khasi, Jaintia samudaay milte hain aur pahaad ki upaj ko maidaan ke chaawal ke pithe se badalte hain — bina paise! Schools ke book exchange club bhi barter hi hain.

Paise ke 4 kaam

(1) Medium of exchange — len-den ka zariya. (2) Store of value — aaj kamaao, baad mein kharcho (gehu jaisa sadta nahi!). (3) Measure of value — har cheez ki keemat ek ikaai (rupaye) mein — tulna aasaan. (4) Standard of deferred payment — udhaar lo, baad mein lautaao.

Sikkon ka safar — 'rupa' se 'rupaya'

Kaudi → dhaatu ke sikke (~600 BCE) → kaagazi note (official 1861) → card → UPI (2016). Purane sikke raja jaari karte (minting). Naam: karshapana / pana; sikke par thappa maara chinh = 'rupa' → isi se 'rupaya'! 'Pana' aaj bhi — Tamil/Telugu/Malayalam mein panam, Kannada mein hana = paisa. Sikke ke do pehlu: obverse (head) aur reverse (tail). Chalukya sikke par Varaha, Chola chaandi sikke par baagh. Pudukkottai (TN) mein Roman sone ke sikke — vyapaar India ke paksh mein tha! 1947 mein 1 anna (1/16 rupaya) mein darjan kele milte the.

₹ ka nishaan aur kaagazi note

₹ — 2010; designer Udaya Kumar (IIT Bombay); Devanagari 'Ra' + Roman 'R'; upar ki do rekhaayein = tiranga aur 'barabar' ka nishaan. Kaagazi paisa pehle China mein; India mein late 18th sadi (Bank of Bengal, Bank of Bombay). Aaj note-sikke jaari karne ka haq sirf RBI ko — aur kisi ka chhaapna gair-kaanooni. Note par virasat ke chitra aur drishtiheen logon ke liye ubhre nishaan.

Digital paisa aur Arthashastra ka hisaab

Aaj paisa dikhta bhi nahi (intangible) — debit/credit card, net banking, UPI, QR code ('quick-response'); Krishnappa ke phal ke thele par bhi QR! Arthashastra: saalaana vetan 60 pana = roz ek adhaka (~3 kg) anaaj; padosi ki madad na karne par 100 pana jurmaana — paisa samaaj ke moolya bhi sikhaata tha.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Barter system = bina paise ke saamaan/seva ka len-den. Sabse pehla tareeka. Cowrie, namak, chai, tambaaku, kapda, pashu, beej use hote.
  • Duniya ke ajeeb paise: Rai stones (Yap Island, Micronesia — vishaal pathar ke pahiye), Aztec copper Tajadero (Mexico), Tevau (laal pankh ka coil, Solomon Islands).
  • Barter ki 5 dikkatein (kisaan aur bail ki kahani): Double coincidence of wants (dono ko ek-doosre ki cheez chahiye), common standard measure of value nahi, divisibility (aadha bail nahi), portability (bail har jagah nahi le ja sakte), durability (gehun sad jaata).
  • Aaj bhi barter: Junbeel Mela (Morigaon, Assam; jun = chaand, beel = wetland) — 3 din, Agni Puja se shuru; 15th century se Tiwa, Karbi, Khasi, Jaintia ke mukhiya milte; pahaad ki upaj ↔ maidan ke chawal ke pithe. Book exchange club, purane kapde ↔ bartan.
  • Paise ke kaam: Medium of exchange, Store of value (baad mein use), Measure of value (common denomination — keemat ki tulna), Standard of deferred payment (udhaar/baad mein bhugtaan).
  • Timeline: barter → cowrie → ~6000 BCE... dhaatu sikke (iron, silver, gold, copper) ~600 BCE → official kaagazi note 1861 → debit/credit card → UPI 2016.
  • Pracheen sikke raja jaari karte (minting); shaktishaali rajaon ke sikke doosre rajyon mein bhi chalte. Naam: kārṣhāpaṇa / paṇa; punch kiye chinh = rūpa (→ 'rupaya'!).
  • 'Paṇa' aaj bhi: Tamil/Telugu/Malayalam mein paṇaṁ, Kannada mein haṇa = paisa.
  • Sikke ke do taraf: obverse (head) aur reverse (tail). Chalukya sikke par Varaha + teen-tiered chhatra; Chola chaandi sikke par baagh (tiger).
  • Pudukkottai (TN) mein Roman sone ke sikke — samudri vyapaar India ke paksh mein tha.
  • 1 anna = 1/16 rupaya; 1947 mein ek anne mein darjan kele.
  • ₹ symbol — 2010; designer Udaya Kumar (IIT Bombay); Devanagari 'Ra' + Roman 'R', upar do rekhaayein = tiranga aur 'barabar' chinh.
  • Paper money pehle China mein; India mein late 18th century (Bank of Bengal, Bank of Bombay ke note). Sikke chhoti, note badi denomination ke liye.
  • Aaj currency jaari karne ka adhikaar sirf Reserve Bank of India (RBI) ko; kisi aur ka chhaapna gair-kanooni. Note par virasat ke chitra, drishtiheen logon ke liye ubhre nishaan.
  • Digital money (intangible): debit/credit card, net banking, UPI, QR code ('quick-response') — Krishnappa ka fruit cart.
  • Arthashastra: saalana vetan 60 paṇa = roz ek āḍhaka (~3 kg) anaaj; padosi ki madad na karne par 100 paṇa jurmaana → samaajik moolya.

Yaad karne ki trick

Barter ki 5 dikkat: 'DC-MV-D-P-D'

Double Coincidence, Measure of Value, Divisibility, Portability, Durability — 'Do Chaar Mein Vo Dil Pe Dard'.

Paise ke 4 kaam: 'MSMS'

Medium of exchange, Store of value, Measure of value, Standard of deferred payment.

Rupa → Rupaya

Sikke par punch kiye chinh 'rupa' — isi se 'rupaya'. Paṇa → paṇam/haṇa.

Teen saal: 1861 – 2010 – 2016

Official note 1861, ₹ chinh 2010, UPI 2016.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Barter mein 'double coincidence of wants' ka arth?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Dono logon ko ek-doosre ki cheez chahiye — Dono ke paas wo ho jo doosre ko chahiye — tabhi seedha len-den sambhav.

  2. Q2. Paise ke kis kaam se hum aaj ki kamaai kal use kar sakte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Store of value — Store of value — paisa sadta nahi, baad mein kharch ho sakta.

  3. Q3. ₹ symbol kab apnaaya gaya aur kisne design kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 2010, Udaya Kumar — 2010; Udaya Kumar, IIT Bombay.

  4. Q4. Junbeel Mela kis rajya mein lagta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Assam — Morigaon district, Assam — aaj bhi barter.

  5. Q5. India mein currency jaari karne ka ekmatra adhikaar kiske paas hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) RBI — Reserve Bank of India.

  6. Q6. 1 anna rupaye ka kitna hissa tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1/16 — 16 aane = 1 rupaya.

  7. Q7. Pudukkottai mein mile Roman sone ke sikke kya batate hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) India ka Rome ke saath samudri vyapaar tha — Vyapaar India ke paksh mein tha — sona India aata tha.
