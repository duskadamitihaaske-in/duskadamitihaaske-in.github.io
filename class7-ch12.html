Theme E · Economic Life Around Us

Understanding Markets

Market kya hai, keemat kaise tay hoti hai, market ke prakaar, supply chain, sarkar ki bhoomika aur quality marks.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Bazaar kya hai — needs aur wants

Market (bazaar) = jahan log saamaan aur sevaayein khareedte-bechte hain — haat, mandi, marukatte (Kannada), mall, ya phone ka app. Needs = jeene ke liye zaroori (khaana, paani, kapda, ghar); Wants = chaahat (naya video game) — zaroori nahi. Hampi Bazaar (Vijayanagara, 16th sadi, Virupaksha mandir ke saamne) — Portuguese Domingos Paes: 'duniya ka sabse sampann shehar'; Fernao Nuniz ne heere-moti-kapdon ka varnan kiya.

Keemat kaise tay hoti hai? — amrood ki kahaani

Bazaar ke liye chahiye: khareedaar, bechne wala, aur dono ko manzoor keemat; mol-bhaav (bargaining) se tay. Amrood ₹80 kilo — bahut mehenga, koi nahi khareedega; ₹20 — bechne wale ko ghaata; ₹40 — 'just right'! Demand (kitne log khareedna chahte) aur supply (kitna maal hai) milkar keemat tay karte hain. Isliye raat ko sabzi sasti (maal bacha, khareedaar kam) aur sardi ke ant mein woollen par discount.

Bazaar ke prakaar

Physical (haat, local market, mall) vs online (apps, websites; online classes bhi). Domestic (desh ke andar) vs international (export = bahar bechna, import = bahar se mangaana). India 2024 mein vanaspati tel ka sabse bada importer (palm oil — Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand). India bechta hai: N. America ko software, Africa ko dawaayein, Europe ko machinery, West Asia ko refined petrol; mangaata hai: Africa se heere, West Asia se kachcha tel aur khaad, S. America se copper ore.

Khet se thaali tak — supply chain

Kachcha maal → producer (banane wala) → wholesaler (thok mein khareede; godaam, cold storage, mandi) → distributor → retailer (mohalle ki dukaan) → consumer (tum!). Online mein aggregator (app) godaam se seedha ghar. Thok bazaar: Khari Baoli (Old Delhi — masaale, dry fruits), Bengaluru ka phool bazaar, Johari Bazaar (Jaipur — ratna-gehne). Surat — Asia ka sabse purana kapda bazaar aur duniya ka sabse bada heera udyog (~15 lakh kaarigar).

Bazaar sirf len-den nahi, rishta bhi

Darzi, sunaar, doctor se peedhiyon ka rishta; mahine ke ant mein kiraane ka hisaab. Ima Keithal ('Maa ka bazaar', Imphal, Manipur) — ~3,000 mahilaayein saari dukaanein chalaati hain! Dakshin mein dukaandaar muft haldi-kumkum dete — shubhkaamna.

Sarkar bazaar mein kya karti hai?

(1) Keemat niyantran — jeevanrakshak dawaon ki upari seema; gehu-dhaan-makka ka nyuntam samarthan mulya (kisaan ko ghaata na ho); minimum wages. (2) Quality-suraksha — dawaon ki jaanch. (3) Bura asar rokna — single-use plastic par rok, tarazu-baat ki jaanch. (4) Public goods — park, sadak, police. Kautilya bhi: ghee bechne wala 1/50 hissa zyada de, kyonki bartan mein chipak jaata hai — purana consumer protection!

Packet padhna seekho — label aur quality mark

Label par: net quantity, manufacture date, batch no., best before, MRP, nirmata ka pata, poshan jaankaari, saamagri, allergen. Quality marks: FSSAI — khaana; ISI (BIS) — bijli ka saamaan, construction, tyre; AGMARK — krishi upaj (daal, masaale, shahad); BEE Star — zyada star = kam bijli. Logon ki raay (word of mouth) aur online review bhi kharid ko prabhaavit karte hain — par soch-samajh kar!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Market = jahan log saamaan aur sevaayein kharidte-bechte hain; bazaar, haat (Hindi), mārukatté (Kannada); physical ya online.
  • Needs = jeene ke liye zaroori (khaana, paani, kapda, ghar); Wants = chaahat, zaroori nahi.
  • Hampi Bazaar (Karnataka) — Vijayanagara samrajya, 16th century, Virupaksha mandir ke saamne. Portuguese yaatri Domingos Paes: 'duniya ka sabse sampann shahar'; Fernao Nuniz ne ratn-heere-moti, kapde ka varnan kiya.
  • Market ke liye zaroori: buyer, seller aur sahmat price. Mol-bhaav (negotiation/bargaining) se keemat tay.
  • Guava udaharan: ₹80 — bahut mehenga, kharidaar nahi; ₹20 — bechne wale ko ghaata; ₹40 — 'just right'. Demand (kharidaar ki maang) aur supply (bechne wale ki uplabdhata) milkar keemat tay karte.
  • Raat mein sabzi sasti, sardi ke ant mein woollen par discount — supply zyada/maang kam.
  • Prakaar: Physical (haat, local market, mall) vs Online (apps/websites; online classes bhi). Stock/share market — saamaan-seva ka nahi.
  • Domestic (desh ke andar) vs International (export/import). India 2024 mein vegetable oil ka sabse bada importer (palm oil — Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand).
  • India ke export: N. America — software services; S. America — chemicals; Africa — dawaayein; Europe — machinery; West Asia — refined petroleum. Import: aircraft (N. America), copper ore (S. America), heere (Africa), electrical (Europe), crude oil & fertilizer (West Asia), vegetable oil (SE Asia).
  • Supply chain: kachcha maal → producer → finished goods → wholesaler (bulk, godown/cold storage, mandi) → distributor → retailer → consumer. Online mein aggregator (app) warehouse se seedha ghar.
  • Wholesale markets: Khari Baoli (Old Delhi — masale, dry fruits), flower market (Bengaluru), Johari Bazaar (Jaipur — ratn-gehne).
  • Surat (Gujarat) — Asia ka sabse purana textile market; Maharashtra/Gujarat ki cotton mandiyon se kapaas → power loom → dyeing → saree/readymade → wholesale. Duniya ka sabse bada diamond udyog — ~15 lakh kaarigar; 16th century se vyapaar; port, highway, railway.
  • Market ka saamaajik roop: tailor/jeweller/doctor se peedhiyon ka rishta; mahine ke ant mein kiraane ka hisaab. Ima Keithal ('Mother's Market', Imphal, Manipur) — ~3,000 mahilaayein saari dukaanein chalaati hain. Dakshin mein haldi-kumkum muft dena — shubhkaamna.
  • Sarkar ki bhoomika: (1) Price control — jeevanrakshak dawaon ki upari seema; gehun-dhaan-makka ka nyunatam mulya (kisaan ko ghaata na ho); minimum wages. (2) Quality-safety — dawaon ki jaanch. (3) Bahari asar rokna — single-use plastic par rok, weights & measures ki jaanch. (4) Public goods — park, sadak, police.
  • Kautilya: ghee bechne wale kharidaar ko 1/50 hissa zyada (mānasrāva) dein, kyunki bartan mein chipak jaata hai — pracheen consumer protection.
  • Packet label: net quantity, manufacture date, batch no., best before, MRP, nirmata ka pata, nutrition facts, FSSAI mark, ingredients, allergen.
  • Quality marks: FSSAI (Food Safety & Standards Authority of India) — khaana; ISI (BIS ka) — electrical, construction, tyre, kaagaz; AGMARK — krishi upaj (sabzi, daal, masale, shahad); BEE Star (Bureau of Energy Efficiency) — zyada star = kam bijli.
  • Word of mouth aur online reviews bhi kharid ko prabhavit karte.

Yaad karne ki trick

Quality marks ka 'Kaun-Kya'

FSSAI = Food, AGMARK = Agriculture, ISI = Industrial cheezein, BEE Star = Bijli bachat.

Supply chain: 'P-W-D-R-C'

Producer → Wholesaler → Distributor → Retailer → Consumer — 'Pehle Wala Dukaan Raste Chala'.

Hampi ke do Portuguese

Paes = 'best-Provided' city; Nuniz = Nagine (ratn) aur kapde.

Ima = Maa

Ima Keithal = Mother's Market, Imphal, ~3,000 mahilaayein.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Hampi ko 'best-provided city in the world' kisne kaha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Domingos Paes — Portuguese yaatri Domingos Paes — Vijayanagara samrajya.

  2. Q2. Agricultural products (sabzi, daal, masale, shahad) par kaun sa certification mark hota hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) AGMARK — AGMARK = agriculture; ISI = industrial goods; BEE = energy efficiency.

  3. Q3. Ima Keithal market kahan hai aur use kaun chalaata hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Imphal, ~3,000 mahilaayein — Imphal (Manipur) ka 'Mother's Market'.

  4. Q4. Asia ka sabse purana textile market kaun sa hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Surat — Surat, Gujarat — diamond udyog bhi duniya mein sabse bada.

  5. Q5. Online market mein bahut se bechne walon ke saamaan ek jagah dikhaane wala app kya kehlata hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Aggregator — Aggregator — warehouse se pack karke ghar tak pahunchata hai.

  6. Q6. Gehun-dhaan ka nyunatam mulya tay karne ka uddeshya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kisaan ko ghaata na ho — Minimum price kisaano ko suraksha deta hai.

  7. Q7. Kautilya ke anusaar ghee bechne wala kitna zyada de?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1/50 (mānasrāva) — Bartan mein chipakne ki bharpaai — 1/50 hissa zyada.
