Theme A · India and the World: Land and the People

India and Her Neighbours

Zameen aur samudra ke padosi — itihaas, sanskriti aur aaj ke rishte.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Padosi kaun hain?

Jaise tumhare ghar ke padosi hote hain, waise desh ke bhi. Zameeni padosi: Pakistan, Afghanistan (uttar-pashchim), China (Tibet), Nepal, Bhutan (uttar), Bangladesh, Myanmar (poorv) — zameeni seema 15,100 km+. Samudri (maritime) padosi — zameen nahi, samundar se jude: Sri Lanka, Maldives, aur Iran, Oman, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia. Indian Ocean — duniya ke aadhe container jahaaz aur 2/3 tel yahin se; ~2.7 arab log. India ki tat-rekha (dweep samet) ~11,100 km. Padosiyon ki madad aur saath = regionalism.

China aur Pakistan

China — seema Arunachal, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, Himachal, Ladakh se. Buddh dharm ~1st sadi CE China pahuncha; Chinese bhikshu Faxian, Xuanzang India aaye, Bharatiya Bodhidharma, Kumarajiva China gaye; Quanzhou ke mandir mein Vishnu-Shiv ki moortiyan! Vyapaar mein China ka palda bhaari. Pakistan — 1947 ke vibhaajan se pehle India ka hissa, dharm ke aadhaar par bana; yuddh 1948, 1965, 1971, Kargil 1999; aatanki hamle. Saajhi virasat: Katas Raj, Hinglaj Mata. Kartarpur Corridor (2019) — Guru Nanak ki 550vi jayanti par; bina visa; Gurdwara Darbar Sahib jahan Guru Nanak ne aakhri 18 saal bitaaye.

Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Myanmar

Bangladesh — pehle 'East Pakistan', 1971 ke yuddh se bana; saajhi Bangla bhasha; seema China se bhi lambi; Sundarbans (sabse bada mangrove, 2/3 Bangladesh mein). Nepal — Pashupatinath; Dashain, Tihar, Holi; 1950 ki sandhi → khuli seema (bina passport!); India sabse bada vyapaari saathi. Bhutan — 'Drukyul' (thunder dragon ki dharti); jal-vidyut sahyog (Tala); Guru Padmasambhava; 'Tiger's Nest' monastery; Gross National Happiness — khushi naapna! Myanmar — SE Asia ka darwaaza; India ne Bagan ka Ananda mandir theek kiya.

Buddh dharm ke 3 raaste

Theravada ('buzurgon ka', ~3rd sadi BCE — Sri Lanka, Thailand, Myanmar), Mahayana ('bada vaahan', ~1st sadi BCE — China, Japan, Korea; Zen), Vajrayana ('heere ka vaahan', ~6th sadi CE — Tibet).

Afghanistan, Sri Lanka, Maldives

Afghanistan — chaaron or zameen (landlocked); Uttarapatha se juda; pehle Buddh-Hindu kendra; Bamiyan Buddha 2001 mein nasht; India ne Afghan sansad bhavan aur Zaranj-Delaram highway banaaya. Sri Lanka — Palk Strait, sabse kam doori ~32 km; Ashoka ke bete-beti Mahendra aur Sanghamitra Buddh dharm laaye. Maldives — 1,100+ chhote dweep; Minicoy se ~130 km; 1965 mein azaad, India ne sabse pehle maanyata di; Dhivehi bhasha mein raajje (raja), mas (matsya); India ne 2004 tsunami, 2014 paani sankat, COVID mein madad ki; 2009 mein paani ke neeche cabinet meeting — climate change ki chetaavni!

Suvarnabhumi — South-East Asia

Purane naam: Java-Sumatra-Malaya = Suvarnabhumi / Suvarnadvipa ('sone ki dharti'). Thailand — Ayutthaya (Ayodhya se!); raja kehlaate 'Rama' (aaj Rama X); Bangkok ke Suvarnabhumi Airport par samudra manthan ki moorti. Malaysia — ~9% Bharatiya mool; Petronas Towers. Singapore = 'Singapuram' (sheron ka shehar); Tamil 4 official bhashaon mein; Little India. Indonesia — 17,000+ dweep; note par Garuda; Borobudur — duniya ka sabse bada Buddh smarak; Ramayana ka manchan. SAARC (1985): Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka.

Iran aur Oman

Iran — Kaanse ke yug se rishta; Avesta aur Rig Veda mein samaanta; Mahabharata mein Parasika; Persian Mughal darbaar ki bhasha; Parsi samudaay; India Chabahar bandargah bana raha hai — Afghanistan-Central Asia tak raasta. Oman — 'Land of Copper'; Harappa kaal se ~5,000 saal ka rishta; 10%+ aabadi Bharatiya mool; Muscat ka Motishwar Shiv mandir; Gulf mein India ka sabse kareebi raksha saathi.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Zameeni padosi: Pakistan, Afghanistan (NW), China ka Tibet, Nepal, Bhutan (N), Bangladesh, Myanmar (E). Zameeni seema 15,100 km+.
  • Maritime neighbour = bina zameeni seema ke, samudra se juda padosi: Sri Lanka, Maldives; aur Iran, Oman, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia.
  • Indian Ocean — teesra sabse bada; duniya ke aadhe container jahaaz, 1/3 bulk cargo, 2/3 tel yahin se; ~2.7 billion log. India ka coastline ~11,100 km (islands samet). Padosiyon ki madad = regionalism.
  • China: seema — Arunachal, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, Himachal, Ladakh. Buddhism ~1st century CE China pahuncha. Chinese bhikshu Faxian, Xuanzang India aaye; Indian bhikshu Bodhidharma, Dharmakshema, Kumarajiva China gaye. 13th century — Quanzhou ke Kaiyuan mandir mein Vishnu-Shiva (Gajendra moksham). Trade: India → iron ore, chemicals, cotton yarn; China → electronics, machinery; balance China ke paksh mein (~8 guna).
  • Pakistan: 1947 Partition se pehle India ka hissa; dharm ke aadhaar par bana. Yuddh: 1948, 1965, 1971, Kargil 1999; Pakistan sena ke samarthan se aatanki hamle. Seema: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab, J&K, Ladakh. Saajhi virasat: Katas Raj (Punjab, Mahabharata se juda), Hinglaj Mata (Balochistan).
  • Kartarpur Corridor — 2019 mein, Guru Nanak ki 550th jayanti par; visa-free (sirf permit); Gurdwara Darbar Sahib — Guru Nanak ne antim 18 saal yahin bitaaye. Pehle Dera Baba Nanak se doorbeen se darshan.
  • Bangladesh — pehle 'East Pakistan', 1971 ke India-Pak yuddh se bana; saajhi Bangla bhasha. Seema China se bhi lambi: WB, Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram. Sundarban — duniya ka sabse bada mangrove; ~2/3 Bangladesh mein; cyclone se dhaal.
  • Nepal: seema — Uttarakhand, UP, Bihar, WB, Sikkim. Pashupatinath (Kathmandu). Tyohaar: Dashain, Tihar, Holi. 1950 Treaty of Peace and Friendship → open border (bina passport/visa). India Nepal ka sabse bada trade partner.
  • Bhutan — 'Drukyul' (Land of the Thunder Dragon); seema: Sikkim, WB, Assam, Arunachal. Hydroelectric sahyog — Tala project (2008). Guru Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche) ne 8th century mein Vajrayana laaya. 'Tiger's Nest' monastery (Paro). Gross National Happiness index.
  • Buddhism ke 3 school: Theravada ('Elders', ~3rd c. BCE; Sri Lanka, Thailand, Myanmar), Mahayana ('Great Vehicle', ~1st c. BCE; China, Japan, Korea; Zen), Vajrayana ('Diamond Vehicle'/Tantric, ~6th c. CE; Tibetan ~7th c.).
  • Myanmar (Burma) — SE Asia ka gateway; seema: Arunachal, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram. Land Border Crossing Agreement 2018. India ne Bagan ka Ananda temple restore kiya; Shwedagon Pagoda ko Sarnath Buddha ki 16-ft pratikriti di.
  • Afghanistan — landlocked; Uttarapatha se juda; 7th c. se pehle Buddhist-Hindu kendra (Kapisha, Zabul). Bamiyan Buddha 2001 mein nasht. India ne Afghan Parliament bhavan aur Zaranj-Delaram highway banaya.
  • Pracheen naam: Java-Sumatra-Malaya = Suvarṇabhūmi / Suvarṇadvīpa ('sone ki dharti'). SAARC — 1985: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka.
  • Sri Lanka — Palk Strait, sabse kam doori ~32 km. Buddhism 3rd c. BCE — Mahendra aur Sanghamitra (Ashoka ke bete-beti). Civil war mid-1980s–~2010 (Sinhala vs Tamil).
  • Maldives — 1,100+ islets; Minicoy (Lakshadweep) se ~130 km. 1965 mein swatantra; India ne sabse pehle maanyata di. Dhivehi: raajje (raja), mas (matsya), dhoni (naav). Madad: 2004 tsunami, 2014 Male paani sankat, COVID. International Solar Alliance ka sadasya; 2009 mein cabinet ki paani ke neeche meeting.
  • Thailand: Dvaravati sanskriti (6th–11th c.) — Dvaraka se; Ayutthaya rajya (1351) — Ayodhya se. Chakri vansh ke raja 'Rama' — aaj Rama X. Bangkok ka Suvarnabhumi Airport — samudra manthan ki moorti. India-Myanmar-Thailand Trilateral Highway (Manipur se).
  • Malaysia: Srivijaya; ~4th c. CE Brahmi par aadhaarit lipi; 15th c. tak Islam pramukh. 19th–20th c. dakshin Indian rubber plantation mazdoor; ~9% Indian mool. Petronas Towers.
  • Singapore = 'Singapuram' (sheron ka shahar); 1965 mein alag desh. Tamil 4 official bhashaon mein; ~9% Indian mool; Little India. Shahri yojana, jaywalking par jurmaana; bada nivesh India mein.
  • Indonesia — 17,000+ islands; Nalanda aur Muara Jambi sahyog; mudra rupiah par Garuda. Borobudur — duniya ka sabse bada Buddhist smarak (8th–9th c., mandala aakaar, 500+ Buddha). Ramayana ka manchan. 2004 tsunami: India mein ~15,000, kul 2 lakh+ maut → Indian Tsunami Early Warning Centre, Hyderabad.
  • Iran: Bronze Age se rishta; Silk Route; Avesta aur Rig Veda mein samaanta; Mahabharata mein Parasika; Persian Mughal darbar ki bhasha; Parsi. India Chabahar Port (Shahid Beheshti) viksit kar raha — Afghanistan-Central Asia tak raasta.
  • Oman — 'Land of Copper'; Harappan kaal se 5,000 saal ka sambandh (copper ingot); 10%+ aabadi Indian mool; Muscat ka Motishwar Shiva mandir; Gulf mein India ka sabse kareebi raksha saathi — teeno senaon ke saath abhyaas.

Yaad karne ki trick

Naam ka safar: 'Dwaraka→Dvaravati, Ayodhya→Ayutthaya'

Thailand ke do rajya Indian shaharon ke naam par; raja aaj bhi Rama X.

Buddhism ke 3 vaahan

Thera = buzurg (Sri Lanka-Thai), Maha = bada vaahan (China-Japan), Vajra = heera (Tibet-Bhutan).

Distance duo: 32 & 130

Sri Lanka — Palk Strait 32 km; Maldives — Minicoy se 130 km.

Lion & Copper

Singapore = Sinha-pura (Lion City); Oman = Land of Copper; Bhutan = Thunder Dragon.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Kartarpur Corridor kab khula?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 2019 — 2019 — Guru Nanak Dev ki 550th jayanti par; visa-free, sirf permit.

  2. Q2. India-Nepal open border kis sandhi par aadhaarit hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Treaty of Peace and Friendship 1950 — 1950 ki Treaty of Peace and Friendship.

  3. Q3. Bhutan mein Vajrayana Buddhism kaun laaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Guru Padmasambhava — Guru Padmasambhava (Guru Rinpoche), 8th century CE.

  4. Q4. Thailand ka Ayutthaya rajya (1351) kis Indian shahar ke naam par hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ayodhya — Ayodhya — Rama ki janmabhoomi. Dvaravati — Dvaraka se.

  5. Q5. Duniya ka sabse bada Buddhist smarak Borobudur kis desh mein hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Indonesia — Indonesia — 8th–9th century, mandala aakaar.

  6. Q6. SAARC ki sthapana kab hui?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1985 — 1985 — 8 sadasya desh.

  7. Q7. India kis desh ka Chabahar Port viksit kar raha hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Iran — Iran — Afghanistan aur Central Asia tak behtar pahunch.
