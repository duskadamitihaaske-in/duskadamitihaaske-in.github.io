Empires and Kingdoms: 6th to 10th Centuries

Gupta ke baad ke kshetriya rajya — Harsha, tripartite struggle, Chalukya-Pallava-Chola, Bhakti, ganit aur Huna-Arab hamle.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Bade samrajya kam, raajya zyada

~600 se 1200 CE ke beech Maurya-Gupta jaise bade samrajya kam the, par kai taaqatwar raajya the — aur kala, sahitya, vichaar khoob phale. Socho ek bade ped ki jagah kai chhote ped — sabme phal!

Harshavardhana — kavi raja

606 CE mein Kannauj mein gaddi; Pushyabhuti vansh (purani raajdhani Thanesar). Khud kavi — 3 Sanskrit natak! Darbaari Banabhatta ne Kadambari (duniya ke pehle upanyaason mein) aur Harshacharita likhe. Shiv bhakt, par Buddh dharm se lagaav, sabka samman. Xuanzang (630–644 India mein) — 600+ pandulipiyan 20 ghodon par le gaya! Harsha har 5 saal Prayag mein daan sabha karte. Narmada ke dakshin Pulakeshin II (Chalukya) ne unhe roka. Banskhera taamrapatra par Nagari lipi (Devanagari ki purvaj).

Kannauj ke liye teen-tarfa ladaai

Tripartite Struggle (8th–9th sadi): Pala (poorv), Gurjara-Pratihara (pashchim), Rashtrakuta (Deccan) — Kannauj ke liye; koi sthaayi vijeta nahi. Pala: 750 CE mein janta ne Gopala ko chuna! Dharmapala ne Vikramashila (Bihar) aur Somapura banaaye; Vikramashila mein pravesh se pehle dvarapandita ('vidwaan pehredaar') pariksha leta! Pratihara: Nagabhata I — Arab hamle roke; Bhoja (9th sadi). Rashtrakuta: raajdhani Manyakheta; Krishna I — Ellora ka Kailashanatha mandir (ek chattaan se!); Amoghavarsha I — 64 saal raaj, Kannada-Sanskrit kavi.

Kashmir, Shankaracharya aur dakshin

Kalhana ki Rajatarangini ('rajaon ki nadi', 12th sadi) — 'itihaasakar prem-nafrat se mukt ho'. Lalitaditya ne Arabon ko haraaya; rani Didda; Abhinavagupta. Adi Shankaracharya (8th sadi) — Advaita: brahman hi param satya; chaar kone par 4 math — Badrinath, Puri, Dwarka, Sringeri. Chalukya: Pulakeshin II, raajdhani Vatapi (Badami); Aihole 100+ mandir. Pallava: Narasimhavarman I 'Mamalla' ne Pulakeshin ko haraaya; Mamallapuram ka Shore Temple aur ek-pathar ke rath. 9th sadi mein Chola phir uthe — Vijayalaya, Tanjavur; Chola kaanse ke Nataraja.

Raaj, vyapaar aur samaaj

Samanta (adheen raja) sena aur sthaaniya prashasan dete — par vafaadaari kamzor (Rashtrakuta pehle Chalukya ke samanta the!). Vibhaajan: bhukti/rashtra → mandala → gaon. Zameen daan badhe. Pallavon ke talaab aaj bhi chalu! Pashchimi tat se Iran, Iraq, East Africa; poorvi tat se SE Asia, China. Chera taamrapatron mein Isaai, Muslim, Yahudi vyapaari. Naye jaati bane; vyavastha lachili (Pala mool roop se Shudra, baad mein Kshatriya maane gaye); par 'asprishya' jaisa bhedbhaav bhi. Odisha ki rani Tribhuvana Mahadevi.

Bhakti ki chingaari aur ganit ka jaadu

Bhakti dakshin se: 12 Alvar (Vishnu; Andal mahila) aur 63 Nayanar (Shiv; Karaikal Ammaiyar). Jain — Bahubali (Shravanabelagola, 10th sadi). Ganit: Brahmagupta (598 CE, Bhillamala) — Brahmasphutasiddhanta: zero, negative numbers, fractions ke niyam — algebra ke sansthaapak! Bhaskara I — trigonometry; Virahanka — wahi sequence jise baad mein Fibonacci ke naam se jaana gaya; Mahavira (Jain) — khagol se alag pehla ganit granth. 'Monsoon' Arabi 'mawsim' se; 9th sadi Baghdad mein Sanskrit granthon ka anuvaad → zero aur decimal Arabon se Europe — 'Hindu-Arabic numerals'.

Huna aur Arab

Huna (Central Asia ke Huns) 6th sadi mein Ganga maidaan tak aaye; Yashodharma (Mandsaur) ne haraaya; baad mein samaaj mein ghul-mil gaye. Arab: 637 ke samudri hamle asafal; 712 mein Muhammad bin Qasim ne Sindh jeeta, raja Dahar maare gaye (Chachnama); par do saal mein Bharatiya mukhiyaon ne zyaadatar ilaaka wapas le liya; Nagabhata I aur Lalitaditya ne roka — 300 saal baad bhi Arab sirf Sindh-Punjab ke do chhote raajyon tak.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Kaal: ~600–1200 CE (yeh adhyaay 6th–10th c.). Bade samrajya kam, par kai shaktishaali rajya; nayi kala, sahitya, vichaar.
  • Harshavardhana — 606 CE mein Kannauj (Kanyakubja, UP) mein gaddi; Pushyabhuti (Vardhana) vansh, purani rajdhani Sthaneshvara (Thanesar, Haryana). Copper plate (Banskhera): 'svahasto mama...' — Nagari lipi (Devanagari ki purvaj).
  • Harsha — kavi-naatakkaar (3 Sanskrit naatak); darbari Banabhatta — Kadambari (duniya ke pehle upanyaason mein; beta ne poora kiya) aur Harshacharita. Shiva bhakt, Buddhism se lagaav, sab ka samman.
  • Xuanzang — 630–644 India mein; 600+ pandulipiyan, 20 ghodon par le gaya. Harsha ne Kannauj mein sabha ki; har 5 saal Prayaga mein daan sabha.
  • Harsha ko Narmada ke dakshin Pulakeshin II (Chalukya) ne roka. Kamarupa ke Varman (Bhaskaravarman) se gathbandhan. Maut 647.
  • Tripartite Struggle (8th–9th c.) — Kannauj ke liye: Pala (purab), Gurjara-Pratihara (pashchim), Rashtrakuta (Deccan); koi sthaayi vijeta nahi.
  • Pala: 750 CE mein janta ne Gopala ko chuna; Dharmapala — Mahayana ke sangrakshak; Vikramashila (Bihar) aur Somapura (Bangladesh) banaye; Nalanda ko sahaara; samudri vyapaar.
  • Vikramashila — late 8th c., Ganga kinare; 6 college, ~3,000 vidwaan; pravesh se pehle dvārapaṇḍita ('scholar gatekeeper') ki pariksha; Tibet se gehra rishta; 12th c. mein Bakhtiyar Khilji ne nasht kiya.
  • Gurjara-Pratihara: Nagabhata I (mid-8th c.); rajdhani Bhillamala (Bhinmal) phir Ujjayini; Arab hamle rokne ke liye mashhoor. Bhoja (9th c.) — 'Mihira', 'Adi Varaha'; Punjab-Kathiawar se Kannauj. 11th c. ki shuruaat mein Ghaznavids ne khatam kiya.
  • Rashtrakuta: Dantidurga ne Chalukyon ko hata kar shuru kiya; rajdhani Manyakheta (Malkheda, Karnataka). Krishna I — Ellora ka Kailashanatha mandir (sabse bada rock-cut, ek chattaan se 'moorti'). Amoghavarsha I 'Nripatunga' — 64 saal raaj, Jain jhukaav, Sanskrit-Kannada kavi (Bengaluru ki Nrupatunga Road). Arab yaatri Al-Masudi ne Muslimon ke samman ki tareef ki.
  • Kashmir: Kalhana ki Rajatarangini ('rajaon ki nadi', 12th c.) — 11 purane granth, inscriptions jaanche; 'itihaaskaar prem-nafrat se mukt ho'. Lalitaditya Muktapida (Karkota) — Arabon ko 3 baar haraaya. Rani Didda (late 10th c.). Kashmir Shaivism — Abhinavagupta.
  • Adi Shankaracharya (8th c.) — Advaita Vedanta: brahman hi param satya, jagat maya; 4 math: Badrinath, Puri, Dwarka, Sringeri (chaar kone).
  • Chalukya: Pulakeshin I (mid-6th c.); Pulakeshin II — rajdhani Vatapi (Badami); Aihole (Aryapura) — 100+ mandir; Meguti Jain mandir par kavi Ravikirti ki prashasti (Harsha par vijay; kuch atishayokti). Baad mein Western Chalukya (Kalyani) aur Eastern Chalukya (Vengi, AP).
  • Pallava (Kanchi): Mahendravarman I (kavi, vyangya naatak) aur Narasimhavarman I 'Mamalla' (pehalwaan) — Pulakeshin II ko haraaya, Badami jeeta; Tungabhadra anaupcharik seema; Sri Lanka nausena bheji. Mamallapuram — Shore Temple (Narasimhavarman II), monolithic mandir, Durga-Mahishasura relief; Chinese, Persian, Roman sikke. Kavi Dandin. Late 9th c. mein Chola Aditya I ne haraaya.
  • Pandya (Madurai, 6th c. se; bandargah Korkai; Mahabharata ka Tamil anuvaad). Chera Perumal (Kerala). Chola punarjanm — Vijayalaya (9th c.), rajdhani Tanjavur; Aditya I; nausena, mandir, Chola bronze Nataraja.
  • Prashasan: samanta (adheen raja) — sena aur local prashasan; vafaadari kamzor (Rashtrakuta pehle Chalukya ke samanta the). Vibhaajan: bhukti/rashtra (pradesh) → mandala (zila) → gaon. Uttaramerur sabha.
  • Arthvyavastha: land grants badhe (bichauliye dwara kisaan shoshan bhi); Pallavon ke talaab aaj bhi chalu; pashchimi tat — Iran, Iraq, East Africa; purvi tat — Sri Lanka, SE Asia, China. Chera copper plates mein Christian, Muslim, Yahudi vyapaari. Hero stone (Eduthanur, TN) — mukhiya aur uska kutta.
  • Samaaj: naye jati bane, varna-jati lachili (Pala, Kakatiya mool roop se Shudra, baad mein Kshatriya maane gaye); Kayastha 10th c. mein jati bane. 'Asprishya' shabd aur Xuanzang ka varnan — kuch samudaayon se bhedbhaav. Rani Tribhuvana Mahadevi I (Bhauma-Kara, Odisha, 9th c.).
  • Dharm: Tantric parampara (~6th c.); Pala kshetra mein Buddhism, anyatra ghata; Jain — pashchim aur Karnataka; Bahubali (Shravanabelagola, 10th c.). Bhakti ki chingaari dakshin se — 12 Alvar (Vishnu; Andal mahila) aur 63 Nayanar (Shiva; 3 mahilaayein, jaise Karaikal Ammaiyar).
  • Ganit-khagol: Brahmagupta (598 CE, Bhillamala) — Brahmasphutasiddhanta: zero, negative numbers, fractions ke niyam; algebra ke sansthaapak. Bhaskara I — trigonometry. Virahanka — Virahanka-Fibonacci sequence. Mahavira (Jain ganitagya, Amoghavarsha ke darbar) — khagol se alag pehla ganit granth.
  • Huna — Central Asia ke Huns ki shaakha; early 6th c. mein Ganga ke maidan tak; Aulikara raja Yashodharma (Dashapura/Mandsaur, MP) ne haraaya — vijay stambh. Baad mein Indian samaaj mein ghul-mil gaye (Toramana ke sikke Gupta jaise).
  • Arab: 637 mein Thana, Bharuch, Debal par nausena hamle — asafal. 712: Muhammad bin Qasim ne Sindh jeeta; raja Dahar maare gaye (Chachnama). Do saal mein Indian mukhiyaon ne zyaadatar ilaaka wapas liya; Nagabhata I aur Lalitaditya ne roka. 3 sadiyon baad bhi Arab sirf Sindh-Punjab ke do chhote rajyon tak.
  • Saanskritik len-den: 'monsoon' Arabic mawsim se; 9th c. mein Baghdad mein Sanskrit ganit-khagol-chikitsa granthon ka anuvaad; decimal system aur zero Arabon ke zariye Europe — 'Hindu-Arabic numerals'.

Yaad karne ki trick

Tripartite = 'PaGuRa'

Pala (purab) + Gurjara-Pratihara (pashchim) + Rashtrakuta (dakshin) — Kannauj ke liye.

Rajdhani jodiyan

Harsha–Kannauj, Rashtrakuta–Manyakheta, Pratihara–Bhillamala/Ujjayini, Chalukya–Vatapi, Pallava–Kanchi, Chola–Tanjavur.

Bhakti ke ank: 12 + 63

12 Alvar = Vishnu (Andal), 63 Nayanar = Shiva (Karaikal Ammaiyar).

Shankaracharya ke 4 math: 'BaPuDwaSri'

Badrinath (N), Puri (E), Dwarka (W), Sringeri (S).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Harshavardhana ki rajdhani kya thi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kannauj (Kanyakubja) — 606 CE se Kannauj; vansh ki purani rajdhani Sthaneshvara (Thanesar) thi.

  2. Q2. Tripartite Struggle kis shahar ke liye tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kannauj — Pala, Gurjara-Pratihara aur Rashtrakuta — 8th–9th century.

  3. Q3. Ellora ka Kailashanatha mandir kis raja ne banwaaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Krishna I (Rashtrakuta) — Rashtrakuta raja Krishna I — sabse bada rock-cut mandir.

  4. Q4. Vikramashila university kisne sthaapit ki?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Dharmapala — Pala raja Dharmapala, late 8th century.

  5. Q5. Zero aur negative numbers ke ganitiya niyam kisne diye?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Brahmagupta — Brahmagupta (598 CE, Bhillamala) — Brahmasphutasiddhanta.

  6. Q6. 712 CE mein Sindh par hamla kisne kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Muhammad bin Qasim — Muhammad bin Qasim; raja Dahar maare gaye. Chachnama mein varnan.

  7. Q7. Vishnu bhakt Tamil sant kya kehlaate the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Alvar — 12 Alvar — Vishnu; 63 Nayanar — Shiva.

  8. Q8. Pulakeshin II ki prashasti (Aihole) kisne likhi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Ravikirti — Kavi Ravikirti — Meguti Jain mandir par.
