Theme B · Tapestry of the Past

Turning Tides: 11th and 12th Centuries

Ghaznavid aur Ghurid hamle, al-Biruni, Bhaskaracharya, Sena-Ganga-Kakatiya-Hoysala-Chola-Paramara-Chauhan aur sanskritik ubhaar.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Itihaas ka mushkil panna — kaise padhein?

Arab hamlon ka asar seemit tha, par 11th–12th sadi mein Turkic shaktiyan uttar India mein gehre tak aayin. NCERT ki seekh: andhere daur ko bhi samjho, par aaj ke kisi insaan ko uske liye zimmedaar mat thehraao. Khyber Pass (Hindu Kush) — 2,500 saal se hamlavaron aur vyapaariyon ka raasta; Hindu Shahi uski rakhwali karte the.

Mahmud Ghazni ke 17 hamle

Ghazni ke Mahmud ne Hindu Shahi raja Jayapala, phir 1008 mein Anandapala ko haraaya; 'Sultan' (Arabi — shakti) kehlaaya; India par 17 abhiyaan. 1018 — Mathura ka bhavya mandir aur Kannauj loota; 1026 — Somnath (Saurashtra) ka Shiv mandir toda. Aaj ka Somnath mandir 1951 mein janta ke daan se bana (udghaatan Rajendra Prasad). Mahmud ki maut 1030; bhatija Salar Masud ko Bahraich mein raja Suheldev ne roka. Loot, hatya, bandi bana kar bechna — maqsad loot aur dharm-prasaar dono.

Al-Biruni aur Bhaskaracharya

Al-Biruni (Khwarizm, aaj Uzbekistan) Mahmud ke saath aaya, par Sanskrit seekhi aur India par vishwakosh likha; Aryabhata-Varahamihira-Brahmagupta ka gyaan sameta, Yoga Sutra ka Arabi anuvaad; likha — vigyaan Kashmir-Varanasi jaisi jagah 'bhaag gaya'. Bhaskaracharya (Bhaskara II), janm 1114 — Lilavati (paheliyon se ganit!), Bijaganita (algebra), Siddhanta Shiromani (khagol).

Is daur ke kshetriya raajya

Sena (Bengal) — kavi Jayadeva, Gitagovindam. Eastern Ganga — Puri Jagannath mandir shuru; baad mein Konark. Kakatiya — Warangal, pathar ke torana, Thousand Pillar Temple. Hoysala — 'Poy, Sala!' (maaro, Sala); Belur, Halebidu ki baareek nakkaashi (UNESCO). Paramara — raja Bhoja (1010–55, Dhara): vishaal jheel → Bhopal ('Bhojpal'), adhoora Bhojeshwar mandir, granth Samarangana Sutradhara (vaastu, yantra) — 9th sadi ke Pratihara Bhoja se alag!

Chola — samundar ke sardaar

Rajaraja I (985 se) — Maldives, uttari Sri Lanka; Thanjavur ka Brihadishvara (Rajarajeshvaram); Shrivijaya raja ko Nagapattinam mein Buddh vihar banaane diya. Rajendra I (1014–44) — Ganga tak pahunche → 'Gangaikondachola', nayi raajdhani Gangaikondacholapuram; 1025 mein Shrivijaya (Malaysia-Indonesia) par nausena abhiyaan — Malacca ke vyapaar ke liye, qabza nahi kiya. China ko doot bheje.

Ghuri, Prithviraj aur Tarain

Muhammad Ghuri 1178 mein Mount Abu ke paas Chaulukya raja Mularaja II (rani Naikidevi) se haara! Chauhan raajdhani Ajmer; Delhi = 'Dhillika' → Dhilli → Delhi. Prithviraj III ne 1191 Tarain-I mein Ghuri ko haraaya, par 1192 Tarain-II mein haare. Ghuri ne ilaaka rakha; general Qutb-ud-din Aibak → aage Delhi Sultanate. Ghuri ke sikke par Lakshmi!

Nalanda ki aag aur naye vichaar

Bakhtiyar Khilji ne Nalanda aur Vikramashila nasht kiye; Nalanda ki library mahinon jalti rahi → Buddh dharm ka patan tez. Par 30 saal baad bhi 90 saal ke Rahula Shribhadra 70 chhaatron ko padha rahe the — gyaan ki lau bujhi nahi! 1205 mein Kamarupa (Assam) mein Bakhtiyar buri tarah haara. Vichaar: Ramanujacharya — Vishishtadvaita (bhakti aur sharanagati se mukti); Basavanna — Lingayat, jaati-bhed ka virodh, Kannada vachana, Anubhava Mandapa — sabke liye charcha sabha. Khajuraho ke Kandariya Mahadeva (Chandella). Cambodia ka Angkor Wat — duniya ka sabse bada dharmik smarak, Vishnu ko samarpit!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Arab hamlon ka asar seemit tha; 11th–12th c. mein Turkic shaktiyan uttar India mein gehre tak aayin. NCERT ki salaah: andhere daur ka bhi vishleshan karo, par aaj kisi ko unke liye zimmedaar mat thehraao.
  • Hindu Shahi — Afghanistan se Punjab tak; Khyber Pass (Hindu Kush) par niyantran — 2,500 saal se hamlavaron aur vyapaar ka raasta.
  • Ghaznavid (rajdhani Ghazna/Ghazni): Mahmud ne Jayapala, phir 1008 mein Anandapala ko haraaya. 'Sultan' (Arabic — adhikaar/shakti) upaadhi. India par 17 abhiyaan; Chandella jaisi kadi takkar.
  • 1018: Mathura ka mandir (al-Utbi: 'koi kalam varnan na kar sake') nasht aur loot; phir Kannauj. 1026: Somnath (Saurashtra) Shiva mandir nasht. Aaj ka Somnath mandir 1950 mein bana, 1951 mein Rajendra Prasad ne udghaatan kiya — janta ke daan se.
  • Mahmud ki maut 1030. Bhatija Salar Masud ko Bahraich (UP) mein raja Suheldev ne roka. Abhiyaanon mein loot, hatyaayein, bandi bana kar Central Asia mein bechna; itihaaskaar maqsad par bahas karte — loot aur dharm-prasaar dono.
  • Al-Biruni (Khwarizm, aaj Uzbekistan) — ~1017 mein Mahmud ke saath aaya; Sanskrit seekhi; India par vishwakosh; Aryabhata-Varahamihira-Brahmagupta ka gyaan sankalit; Yoga Sutras ka Arabic anuvaad. Likha: vigyaan 'Kashmir, Varanasi' jaisi jagah bhaag gaya.
  • Bhaskaracharya (Bhaskara II) — janm 1114 (shayad Maharashtra); Lilavati (paheliyon se ganit, ~1150), Bijaganita (algebra), Siddhanta Shiromani (khagol). Mughal kaal mein Persian anuvaad.
  • Sena (Bengal) — rajdhani Nadiya; Hindu vichaar ke sangrakshak; kavi Jayadeva — Gitagovindam (Krishna-Radha).
  • Eastern Ganga (Kalinga) — 'Ganga se Godavari tak'; Puri Jagannath mandir shuru kiya; mid-13th c. mein Konark Sun Temple.
  • Kakatiya (12th c., Telangana-AP) — rajdhani Orugallu (Warangal), pathar ke torana; Thousand Pillar Temple (Hanamkonda); Telugu sahitya; gaon swa-shaasan, sinchai.
  • Hoysala (Karnataka) — katha: Jain sanyasi ne kaha 'Poy, Sala!' (maaro Sala) → 'Hoysala'. Rajdhani Belur (Velapura), Halebidu (Dvarasamudra); raja Vishnuvardhana; baariki nakkaashi wale mandir (3 UNESCO; Somanathapura).
  • Chola: Rajaraja I (985 se ~30 saal) — 'samudra ke dweep' (Maldives), uttari Sri Lanka (Anuradhapura); Brihadishvara (Rajarajeshvaram) mandir, Thanjavur; Shrivijaya raja ko Nagapattinam mein Buddhist vihar banane diya.
  • Rajendra I (1014–44) — Odisha-Bengal tak → upaadhi 'Gangaikondachola'; nayi rajdhani Gangaikondacholapuram; 1025 — Shrivijaya (Malaysia-Indonesia) par nausena abhiyaan (Strait of Malacca vyapaar ke liye; guilds ka saath; kabza nahi kiya). China ko doot. Sri Lanka ke Vijayabahu I ne Cholon ko nikaala. 13th c. tak Pandyon mein vileen.
  • Paramara (Malwa, rajdhani Dhara) — Bhoja (1010–55): Bhojpur, vishaal jheel → Bhopal ('Bhojpal'), aaj Bhojtal ('taal to Bhopal taal...'); adhoora Bhojeshwar mandir; granth Samarangana Sutradhara (vaastu, town planning, yantra), Yoga Sutra tika. (9th c. ke Pratihara Bhoja se alag!)
  • Ghurid (Ghor, Afghanistan): Muhammad Ghuri — 1178 mein Mount Abu ke paas Chaulukya (Solanki) raja Mularaja II (/ rani Naikidevi) se haara. Chaulukya rajdhani Anahilavada (Patan); Deccan ke Chalukya se alag.
  • Chahamana / Chauhan — rajdhani Ajayameru (Ajmer); Delhi = 'Dhillika' → Dhilli → Delhi. Prithviraja III: 1191 First Tarain mein Ghuri ko haraaya; 1192 Second Battle of Tarain (Taraori, Haryana) mein haare aur maare gaye.
  • Ghuri ne Delhi jeeta, ilaaka rakhna chaaha (Mahmud ki tarah sirf loot nahi); general Qutb-ud-din Aibak — baad mein Delhi Sultanate. Ghuri ke sikke par Lakshmi.
  • Bakhtiyar Khilji — Bihar-Bengal; Nalanda aur Vikramashila nasht, bhikshuon ki hatya; Nalanda ki library mahinon jali → Buddhism ke patan mein tezi. Minhaj-us-Siraj ki Tabakat-i-Nasiri mein varnan. Tibetan bhikshu Dharmasvamin ne 3 dashak baad 90 saal ke Rahula Shribhadra ko 70 chhaatron ko padhaate dekha.
  • 1203–04: Nadiya mein Sena raja par achanak hamla. 1205: Tibet jaate hue Kamarupa mein buri tarah haara (janta ne khaana-chaara nasht kiya). 1206: Ghuri aur Bakhtiyar dono ki maut.
  • Arthvyavastha: China-SE Asia vyapaar badha; west coast par shipyard; mukhya aadhaar kheti (Kashmir saffron, Kerala masale, Bengal adrak, west cotton). Chalukya Someshvara III — Manasollasa (vishwakosh). Khajuraho — Kandariya Mahadeva, Lakshmana mandir (Chandella).
  • Ramanujacharya — Vishishtadvaita: jagat aur jeev asli, bhakti aur sharanagati se mukti. Basaveshvara (Basavanna) — Kalyani ke mantri; Lingayat andolan, jaati-bhed aur karmkaand ka virodh; Kannada vachana; Anubhava Mandapa — sab ke liye charcha sabha.
  • Angkor Wat (Cambodia, 12th c., Khmer raja Suryavarman II) — duniya ka sabse bada dharmik smarak; Vishnu ko samarpit, 5 shikhar = Meru; baad mein Buddhist.

Yaad karne ki trick

Teen tareekh: 1026-1192-1206

Somnath loot 1026, Second Tarain 1192, Ghuri-Bakhtiyar ki maut 1206.

Tarain ka 91-92

1191 Prithviraj jeete, 1192 haare — 'ek saal mein palta paasa'.

Dynasty-Mandir match

Chola–Brihadishvara, Hoysala–Belur, Chandella–Kandariya Mahadeva, Paramara–Bhojeshwar, Eastern Ganga–Konark.

Do Bhoja, do Chalukya!

Bhoja Pratihara (9th c.) ≠ Bhoja Paramara (11th c.). Chalukya (Deccan) ≠ Chaulukya/Solanki (Gujarat).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Mahmud of Ghazni ne Somnath mandir par kab hamla kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1026 — 1026. 1018 mein Mathura, 1008 mein Anandapala ki haar.

  2. Q2. Second Battle of Tarain (1192) mein kaun haara?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Prithviraja III — Prithviraja III — 1191 mein jeete the, 1192 mein haare.

  3. Q3. 'Gangaikondachola' upaadhi kis Chola raja ki thi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Rajendra I — Rajendra I — uttar abhiyaan aur Ganga ki pavitrata ki yaad mein.

  4. Q4. Lilavati ki paheli: aadha + aadhe ka 1/3 gufa mein; 1/6 + 1/6 ka 1/7 nadi par; 1/8 + 1/8 ka 1/9 taalaab mein; aur raja 3 hathiniyon ke saath. Kul haathi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1008 — 2/3 + 4/21 + 5/36 = 251/252 → bache 1/252 = 4 haathi → x = 1008.

  5. Q5. Nalanda aur Vikramashila ko kisne nasht kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Bakhtiyar Khilji — Bakhtiyar Khilji, ~12th century ke ant mein.

  6. Q6. Bhopal shahar ka naam kis raja se juda hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Bhoja Paramara — Paramara Bhoja ki jheel/baandh — 'Bhojpal'.

  7. Q7. Lingayat andolan kisne shuru kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Basaveshvara — Basavanna — Kalyani; jaati-bhed ka virodh, vachana kavitaayein.

  8. Q8. Hoysala mandir kahan hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Belur aur Halebidu — Belur, Halebidu, Somanathapura — UNESCO.
