Theme C · Our Cultural Heritage and Knowledge Traditions

India, a Home to Many

Bahar se aakar India ko ghar banaane wale samudaay — Yahudi, Syriac Christian, Parsi, Arab, Siddi, Armenian, Baha'i, Polish bachche, Tibetan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

'Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam' — poori duniya ek parivaar

Hitopadesha: 'ye mera, wo paraaya — ye chhote mann ki soch hai; udaar logon ke liye vasudhaiva kutumbakam'. Sri Sarada Devi: 'poori duniya tumhaari apni hai'. India ne sadiyon tak sataaye gaye logon ko sharan di. Swami Vivekananda (Chicago, 1893): 'mujhe garv hai us desh par jisne har dharm aur desh ke peedit sharnaarthiyon ko aashray diya'.

Yahudi — Bene Israel aur Kochi

Bene Israel — Konkan tat par, kuch vidwaanon ke anusaar ~175 BCE mein jahaaz doobne se pahunche; granth kho gaye par 'ek Ishwar' ki praarthana yaad rahi; India ka sabse bada Yahudi samudaay. Baad mein Yahudi Kochi aaye; raja ne zameen di — 'jab tak duniya, suraj aur chaand rahein'; synagogue bana.

Syriac Christian aur Parsi

Syriac Christian — Roman samrajya mein 'heretic' aur Persia mein jasoos maane gaye → Malabar (Kerala) aaye. Parsi (Zoroastrian) — 7th sadi mein Persia par Arab vijay ke baad zabardasti dharm-parivartan, jizya, agni mandir tode gaye → 8th–10th sadi mein Gujarat tat. Mashhoor kahaani: Sanjan ke raja Jadi Rana ne doodh se bhara jug dikhaaya — 'yahan jagah nahi'. Parsi buzurg ne usme shakkar ghol di — doodh chhalka nahi! Matlab: 'hum ghul-mil jaayenge aur mithaas badhaayenge'. Udvada mein aaj bhi pavitra agni. Duniya mein sabse zyada Parsi India mein. Paigambar Zarathushtra, Ishwar Ahura Mazda, granth Avesta; Vedic samaanta: soma = haoma, yajna = yasna.

Arab vyapaari, Siddi, Armenian, Baha'i

Arab vyapaari 7th sadi se Kerala, Gujarat — sthaaniya shaadiyan; Kerala ke Mappila Muslim; Cheraman Juma Masjid — India ki sabse purani masjid; ye shaantipurn vyapaari the, vijeta nahi. Siddi — African mool, gulaam bana kar laaye gaye; aaj Scheduled Tribe; Hirabai Lobi — Padma Shri 2023. Armenian — Akbar ne Agra mein church ki anumati di; Abdul Hai Chief Justice, Lady Juliana mahal ki doctor; Chennai ki Armenian Street. Baha'i — Persia mein Baha'u'llah (1800s); New Delhi ka Lotus Temple.

'Good Maharaja' aur Tibet

Nawanagar (Jamnagar) ke Digvijaysinhji Jadeja ne WWII mein (1942–46) ~1,000 Polish anaath bachchon ko bachaaya — Warsaw mein 'Good Maharaja Square'! Tibet — 7th sadi mein Buddh dharm; Nalanda ke vinaash ke baad bhikshu pandulipiyan lekar Tibet gaye. 1950 se China ka qabza; 1959 mein 14th Dalai Lama India aaye — Dharamshala (Central Tibetan Administration); Karnataka ka Bylakuppe. Dalai Lama ki 4 pratibaddhataayein: karuna-kshama jaise moolya, dharmik sauhaard, Tibetan sanskriti ka sanrakshan, India ki ahimsa-karuna ki virasat ka punarjeevan. Sowa Rigpa (Tibetan chikitsa) ab AYUSH mein (Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, Sowa Rigpa, Homoeopathy).

Hamare mool moolya

Sarve bhavantu sukhinah, atithi devo bhava (mehmaan bhagwaan jaisa), Karaniya Metta Sutta (maa jaisi karuna sab jeevon par). Kai hamlavar bhi India ki sanskriti se 'jeete' gaye — Indo-Greek, Kushana yahin ke rang mein rang gaye.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Hitopadesha: 'yeh mera, woh paraaya — chhote mann ki soch; udaar logon ke liye vasudhaiva kutumbakam'. Sri Sarada Devi: 'poori duniya tumhaari apni hai'.
  • Bene Israel (Yahudi) — Konkan tat (Mumbai ke dakshin); ~175 BCE (kuch vidwaanon ke anusaar) jahaaz doobne se; dharmgranth kho gaye par 'ek Ishwar' ki praarthana yaad rahi; aazaadi ke baad 25,000+ — India ka sabse bada Yahudi samudaay.
  • 12th–19th c. mein aur Yahudi Kochi aaye; Raja ne muft zameen di — 'jab tak duniya, suraj, chaand rahein'; synagogue bana. Travancore Maharaja ka Torah ke liye upahaar.
  • Swami Vivekananda (Chicago, 1893, World Parliament of Religions): 'mujhe garv hai us desh par jisne har dharm aur desh ke peedit sharnaarthiyon ko aashray diya' — Israelites aur Zoroastrian.
  • Syriac (Syrian) Christians — 4th c. CE se Roman samrajya mein 'heretic' maane gaye, Persia mein Romanon ke jasoos samjhe gaye → Malabar (Kerala) aaye; kai sampradaay.
  • Parsi (Zoroastrian) — 7th c. mein Persia par Arab-Islamic vijay ke baad (Sassanid samrajya ka patan) jabran dharm-parivartan, jizya, agni mandir nasht → 8th–10th c. mein Gujarat tat. Sangh mein pavitra agni.
  • Katha: Sanjan ke raja Jadi Rana ne doodh se bhara jug dikhaaya ('jagah nahi'); Parsi buzurg ne usme shakkar ghol di, doodh nahi chhalka → 'hum ghul-mil jaayenge, mithaas badhaayenge'. Udvada mein aaj bhi pavitra agni. Duniya mein sabse zyada Parsi India mein. Raja Ravi Varma ka chitra 'Decking the Bride'.
  • Zoroastrianism — paigambar Zarathushtra, ek Ishwar Ahura Mazda; granth Avesta. Vedic samaanta: soma = haoma, hotar = haotar, yajna = yasna; ulta arth: Vedic 'deva' achhe, Avesta ke 'daeva' bure; Avesta ka 'Ahura' bada devta, India mein 'asura' bure.
  • Arab vyapaari — 7th c. se Kerala, Gujarat, Karnataka; sthaniya shaadiyan; Kerala mein Mappila Muslim; Cheraman Juma Masjid — India ki sabse purani masjid. Vijeta nahi, shaantipurn vyapaari.
  • Siddi — African mool; 7th–19th c. mein Arab, Portuguese, British vyapaariyon ne gulaam banaakar laaye; 18th c. mein kuch ne Bengal ke hisse sambhaale. African drum wala nritya; Hindu/Muslim/Christian dharm. Scheduled Tribe; aarthik sthiti kamzor. Hirabai Lobi (Gujarat) — Padma Shri 2023.
  • Armenian — Malabar mein 8th c. ki pehli basti ke sanket; Mughal kaal mein Agra — Akbar ne church ki anumati di. Abdul Hai — Akbar ke darbar mein Chief Justice; Lady Juliana — mahal ki doctor. Kolkata (Armenian College, 18th c.), Chennai (Armenian Street, St. Mary's Church).
  • Baha'i — mid-1800s Persia mein Baha'u'llah; ekta ka sandesh; 'heretic' kehkar sataaye gaye → late 19th c. India. Aaj zyaadatar Indian Baha'i; Lotus Temple, New Delhi.
  • 'Good Maharaja' — Digvijaysinhji Ranjitsinhji Jadeja, Nawanagar (Jamnagar) — WWII mein 1942–46 ~1,000 Polish anath bachche bachaaye; Warsaw mein Good Maharaja Square smarak.
  • Tibet — 7th c. mein Buddhism; Nalanda ke vinaash ke baad bhikshu pandulipiyan lekar Tibet gaye; 15th c. se Dalai Lama, mid-17th c. se shaasak. 1950 se China ne kabza kiya; 1959 Lhasa vidroh ke baad 14th Dalai Lama India aaye — Dharamshala (Central Tibetan Administration). Karnataka (Bylakuppe) mein monasteries.
  • Dalai Lama ki 4 pratibaddhataayein: maanav moolya (karuna, kshama, sahishnuta), dharmik sauhaard, Tibetan sanskriti ka sanrakshan, India ki virasat (karuna, ahimsa) ka punarjeevan.
  • Sowa Rigpa (Tibetan chikitsa) — 8th c. Lhasa ke paas sankalit; Ayurveda + China, Central Asia, Persia, Greece; Men-Tsee-Khang (Dharamshala); AYUSH mein shaamil (Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa Rigpa, Homoeopathy).
  • Mool moolya: sarve bhavantu sukhinah, atithi devo bhava, Karaniya Metta Sutta (maa jaisi karuna sab jeevon par). Kai hamlavar bhi India ki sanskriti se 'jeete' gaye (Indo-Greek, Kushana).

Yaad karne ki trick

Doodh mein shakkar = Parsi

Sanjan, Jadi Rana, Udvada — 'S-J-U'; ghul-milne ka prateek.

Kaun kahan: 'Bene-Konkan, Yahudi-Kochi, Syriac-Malabar, Parsi-Gujarat'

Siddi — Gujarat/Karnataka; Armenian — Agra/Kolkata/Chennai; Tibetan — Dharamshala/Bylakuppe.

Avesta ↔ Veda

Soma–Haoma, Hotar–Haotar, Yajna–Yasna. Deva/Asura ke arth ulte!

Polish bachche = Jamnagar

Good Maharaja Digvijaysinhji — ~1,000 bachche, Warsaw mein square.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Parsi kis rajya ke tat par pehle aaye?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Gujarat — Gujarat — Sanjan; pavitra agni Udvada mein.

  2. Q2. 'Doodh mein shakkar' ki katha kis raja se judi hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Jadi Rana — Sanjan ke raja Jadi Rana — Parsiyon ne ghul-milne ka vaada dikhaaya.

  3. Q3. India ki sabse purani masjid kaun si hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Cheraman Juma Masjid, Kerala — Cheraman Juma Masjid — Arab vyapaariyon aur Mappila samudaay se judi.

  4. Q4. Polish anath bachchon ko WWII mein kisne bachaaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Digvijaysinhji Jadeja (Nawanagar) — Jamnagar ke 'Good Maharaja' — ~1,000 bachche, 1942–46.

  5. Q5. 14th Dalai Lama India kab aaye?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1959 — 1959 — Lhasa vidroh ke baad; Dharamshala mein.

  6. Q6. Siddi samudaay ka mool kahan hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Africa — Africa — gulaam banaakar laaye gaye; aaj Scheduled Tribe.

  7. Q7. Avesta mein Vedic 'yajna' ka samaan shabd?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Yasna — Yajna = Yasna; soma = haoma; hotar = haotar.
