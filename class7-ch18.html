Theme D · Governance and Democracy

The State, the Government, and You

State vs government, democracy vs republic, teen ang, political vs permanent executive, aur nagrik sarkar se kaise kaam karwaaye.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

State aur Government — farak samjho

Socho tumhara school = State (rashtra) — wo hamesha rehta hai; aur principal-teachers = Government (sarkar) — jo badal sakte hain. State ke 4 ang: People (sthaayi aabadi), Territory (tay seema), Government, Sovereignty (bina bahari dakhal ke kaanoon banaane ki poori shakti). Sarkar chunaav se badalti hai, state wahi rehta hai. India mein 'State' ka matlab desh bhi, aur Kerala jaisa rajya bhi! Socrates: 'raaj na karne ki sabse badi saza — kisi badtar ke raaj mein rehna'.

Democracy aur Republic — dono ek nahi

Democracy = janta ka raaj. Republic = rashtra pramukh chuna hua, khaandaani nahi. Har democracy republic nahi — UK, Canada, Sweden mein loktantra hai par raja/rani bhi; India aur USA dono hain. Republic mein bahumat ki manmaani se bhi bachaav: Dongria Kondh ki pavitra pahaadiyon par mining nahi hui; colony ki RWA ne raat 9 baje ke baad delivery band ki, par beemaar buzurg ke liye dawa ko chhoot mili. George Washington ne raja banne se inkaar kiya. Kautilya: sarkar na ho to 'matsya nyaya' — badi machhli chhoti ko kha jaaye!

Teen ang aur do tarah ke executive

Legislature (kaanoon — Parliament, Vidhan Sabha), Executive (lagu karna), Judiciary (paalan aur samvidhan ki raksha). Executive do tarah: Political — chune hue PM, CM, mantri (aam taur par 5 saal); Permanent — UPSC jaisi pariksha se chune civil servants (IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS, IFoS) jo chunaav se nahi badalte. Zameeni star par sarkari teacher, anganwadi, health worker, bus conductor. Police rajya sarkar ki — kaanooni taur par bal prayog kar sakti, par adhikaaron ka samman zaroori.

C.B. Muthamma — pehli mahila IFS

C.B. Muthamma (1924–2009, Karnataka) — 1948 mein civil services pariksha paas karne wali pehli mahila, IFS mein pehli, pehli mahila rajdoot. Shaadi par istifa jaise bhedbhaav wale niyam ke khilaaf Supreme Court gayin — Justice V.R. Krishna Iyer ki bench ne niyam radd kiye. Himmat se badlaav!

Sarkar ki 3 bhoomika aur hamari taakat

Protector (raksha, kaanoon), Provider (kalyaan, sadak-school), Regulator (arthvyavastha, nyaay). Naagrik kya kar sakte hain: (1) shikaayat — pgportal.gov.in; (2) RTI (Right to Information) — sarkar se jaankaari maango (Bagepalli ki sadak isi se bani!); (3) media/social media; (4) NGO; (5) pratinidhi ko chitthi; (6) vote. Silent Valley (Kerala) — logon ke andolan se 1983 mein bijli yojana radd, 1985 mein National Park!

Teen star aur decentralisation

Union, State, Local. India: 28 rajya, 8 UT. Mool samvidhan mein do star the; 1990s ke 73rd/74th Amendment se teesra star (panchayat, nagar palika). Decentralisation = shakti ek jagah na ho — zyada loktantrik, sthaaniya gyaan, zyada jawabdehi. Gandhi ka gram swaraj: har gaon ek 'poorn republic'. Misaal — shiksha: sansad ne RTE banaaya → executive ne Samagra Shiksha, PM Poshan chalaaye → judiciary aur NCPCR nigraani karte hain.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Socrates (Plato ki 'Republic'): 'raaj na karne ki sabse badi saza — kisi badtar ke raaj mein rehna'.
  • State (rashtra) ke 4 ang: People (sthaayi aabadi), Land / Territory (tay seema), Government, Sovereignty (bina bahari dakhal ke kanoon banane ki poorn shakti).
  • Government = state ka ek hissa — kanoon banana, lagu karna, vivaad sulajhana. Chunaav se badalti hai, state wahi rehta hai (school = state, principal-teachers = government).
  • Dono shabd mil jaate hain kyunki sarkar state ki taraf se kaam karti hai aur hum roz sarkar se hi milte hain. India mein 'State' = desh bhi, aur Kerala jaisa rajya bhi.
  • Police — rajya sarkar ka hissa; kanooni taur par bal prayog kar sakti; court-jail ke saath; road safety, nasha, mahila suraksha jaagrukta; par samvidhan ke adhikaaron ka samman zaroori.
  • Democracy = janta ka raaj (demos + kratos). Republic = head of state chuna hua (seedha ya apratyaksh), vanshaanugat nahi; samvidhan se shakti par seema. India ka President chune pratinidhi chunte.
  • Har democracy republic nahi: UK, Canada, Sweden — democracy, par raja/rani. India aur USA — dono.
  • Republic mein 'dominance of the majority' se bachaav: Dongria Kondh (Odisha) ki pavitra pahaadiyon par mining nahi — kanoon ne alpsankhyak ke adhikaar bachaaye. RWA ka 9 baje ke baad delivery ban — buzurg mahila ki tabiyat bigdi → dawa-khaane ko chhoot.
  • George Washington (1782) ne America ka raja banne se inkaar kiya; 1789 mein pehle President.
  • Kautilya: sarkar na ho to 'matsya nyaya' — badi machhli chhoti ko kha jaati. Raja ko rajadharma.
  • Teen ang: Legislature (kanoon — Parliament; rajyon ki Vidhan Sabha), Executive (lagu karna aur policy), Judiciary (paalan aur samvidhan ki raksha).
  • Political executive — chune hue: PM, CM, mantri (+ President, Governor formal); aam taur par 5 saal. Permanent executive — UPSC jaisi pariksha se chune bureaucrats / civil servants; chunaav se nahi badalte; salaah, yojana lagu karna, janta se kadi, record.
  • Mukhya sevaayein: IFS (Foreign), IAS, IPS, IRS, IFoS (Forest). Zameeni star par: sarkari teacher, anganwadi, health worker, bus conductor. Census, chunaav, aapda rahat, VB-G RAM G (2026), COVID vaccination.
  • C.B. Muthamma (1924–2009, Karnataka) — 1948 mein civil services pariksha paas karne wali pehli mahila; 1949 mein IFS mein pehli mahila; pehli mahila rajdoot. Shaadi par istifa ki shart jaisi bhedbhaavpurn neeti ke khilaaf Supreme Court gayi — Justice V.R. Krishna Iyer ki bench ne niyam radd kiye.
  • Sarkar ki teen bhoomika: Protector (kanoon, raksha), Provider (kalyaan, infrastructure), Regulator (arthvyavastha, saamajik nyaay).
  • Nagrik kya karein: (1) Grievance redressal — pgportal.gov.in, vigilance; (2) RTI (Right to Information) — transparency aur accountability; Bagepalli (Bengaluru ke paas) ki sadak ka kissa; (3) Media/social media (@RailwaySeva); (4) Civil society / NGO; (5) pratinidhi ko chitthi; (6) vote.
  • Silent Valley (Kerala): 1970 ki hydroelectric yojana ke khilaaf andolan; 1983 mein Kendra ne radd ki; 1985 mein National Park.
  • Judiciary: kanoon ki raksha aur vyakhya, adhikaar aur swatantrata ki raksha, judicial review; rule of law — sarkar bhi kanoon ke neeche; independent.
  • Teen star: Union, State, Local. India: 28 states, 8 UTs. Mool samvidhan mein do star; local body State List mein; DPSP ne gram panchayat ki baat ki; 1990s ke amendments (73rd/74th) se third tier.
  • Decentralisation = shakti ek jagah na ho. Fayde: zyada loktantrik, behtar sthaniya gyaan, majboot local sarkar, sthaniya charcha, zyada jawabdehi. Gandhi — gram swaraj: gaon ek 'poorn republic'.
  • Shiksha ka udaharan: Parliament/Vidhan Sabha → RTE Act; executive → Samagra Shiksha, PM Poshan, District/Block Education Officer; judiciary → NCPCR / SCPCR, courts.

Yaad karne ki trick

State ke 4 ang = 'P-L-G-S'

People, Land, Government, Sovereignty — 'Pyaara Lagta Gaon Sabko'.

Democracy vs Republic

Democracy = kaun chunta (janta). Republic = head of state chuna hua, raja nahi. UK = democracy, republic nahi.

Executive ke do roop

Political = 5 saal ka mehmaan (chuna hua); Permanent = UPSC wala, tika rehta hai.

Nagrik ke 6 hathiyaar: 'G-R-M-N-L-V'

Grievance, RTI, Media, NGO, Letter, Vote.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. State ke chaar ang kaun se hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Log, zameen, sarkar, sovereignty — People, Territory, Government, Sovereignty.

  2. Q2. Kaun sa desh democracy hai par republic nahi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (D) UK — UK mein raja head of state hai (vanshaanugat), isliye republic nahi.

  3. Q3. IAS, IPS jaise adhikaari kis executive ka hissa hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Permanent executive — UPSC se chune bureaucrats — chunaav se nahi badalte.

  4. Q4. IFS mein shaamil hone wali pehli mahila?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) C.B. Muthamma — C.B. Muthamma — 1949 mein IFS; baad mein Supreme Court mein bhedbhaav ke khilaaf jeet.

  5. Q5. Silent Valley National Park kab bana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1985 — 1983 mein yojana radd, 1985 mein National Park.

  6. Q6. Kautilya ke 'matsya nyaya' ka arth?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Bina sarkar ke badi machhli chhoti ko kha jaati — Law & order na ho to taakatwar kamzor ko daba dete hain.

  7. Q7. Sarkari kaamon ki jaankari maangne ka kanooni adhikaar?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) RTI — Right to Information Act — transparency aur accountability.
