Theme E · Economic Life Around Us

Infrastructure: Engine of India's Development

Sadak, rail, metro, hawai, bandargah aur communication — desh ke vikaas ka engine; uski dekhbhaal sab ki zimmedaari.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Infrastructure — desh ki 'nason' jaisa

Jaise shareer mein nason se khoon pahunchta hai, waise desh mein infrastructure se sab kuch: transport (sadak, pul, rail), utilities (bijli, paani), communication (internet, tower), energy (solar park, pipeline). Kisaan Satish ki kahaani: nahar, bijli pump, sadak, cold storage, internet — sab puzzle ke tukde; ek bhi na ho to fasal ka poora daam nahi milta. Udaharan: Chenab Rail Bridge (duniya ka sabse ooncha rail pul), Atal Setu (sabse lamba samudri pul), Bhakra Nangal Dam, Cochin Airport (solar se chalne wala!).

Sadkein aur pul

India ka sadak network duniya mein 2nd (USA ke baad). State highway — rajya ka PWD; National Highway — Kendra; 2025 mein NH ~1,50,000 km. Sabse lamba NH44 — 4,112 km, Srinagar se Kanyakumari! Golden Quadrilateral — Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata ko jodta chaukor. Bhupen Hazarika Setu (Dhola-Sadiya) — Assam-Arunachal, 9.15 km, safar 4 ghante kam. Meghalaya ke living root bridges (Khasi mein jingkieng jri) — rubber ped ki jadon se bane, sadiyon tak tikte!

Railway, metro, hawai jahaaz, bandargah

Angrezon ne railway 1853 mein shuru ki — kapaas-chai bandargah tak le jaane aur sena ke liye. Aaj roz 2 crore+ yaatri; duniya ka 4th sabse bada rail system; sabse bada employer (~12 lakh). Safar: 1895 steam → 1957 diesel → 1970 electric → 2019 Vande Bharat. Maalgaadi sadak se 75–90% kam urja khaati hai. Metro — 23 shehar, 1,000+ km. Hawai yaatra mein India 3rd; 159 airport (2025). Bandargah: 12 major, 217 minor; container ki ikaai TEU.

Sanchaar ka safar — J.C. Bose ki kahaani

1780 — pehla chhapa akhbaar Bengal Gazette; 1844 telegraph; 1876 telephone; 1930 All India Radio… aaj 5G aur AI. J.C. Bose (1858–1937) ne 1895 mein Calcutta mein microwave se deewaar ke paar signal bheja; naya coherer, galena detector banaya — par patent nahi karaaya. Marconi ne 1901 mein patent liya aur 1909 mein Nobel paaya. Bose Institute — 1917. 1990s mein mobile call ₹17/minute (incoming bhi!); 2025 mein 116 crore mobile subscriber. Tumhara ek voice note: tower (bijli), fibre cable, satellite, data centre — sab ka kamaal!

Social infrastructure aur hamari zimmedari

Social infrastructure: school, aspataal, police-fire station, court, park, library. Chunautiyaan: kachra, gaddhe, smaarakon par naam likhna; behtar sewer-traffic-paani; bachchon, buzurgon, divyangon ke liye design; jaanwaron ke paar karne ke raaste. Arthashastra mein bhi niyam: rajmarg 16 m chauda, rath ki sadak 2.5 m; baandh todne par kadi saza! Sarkari sampatti = hamari sampatti.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Physical infrastructure = shahar-gaon chalaane wala thos dhaancha: transport (sadak, pul, rail), utilities (bijli, paani), communication (internet, tower), energy (windmill, solar park, pipeline).
  • Udaharan: Chenab Rail Bridge (J&K, duniya ka sabse ooncha rail pul), Atal Setu (Maharashtra, sabse lamba samudri pul), Delhi-Meerut Expressway, Bhakra Nangal Dam, Visakhapatnam port, Cochin Airport (solar), Muppandal Wind Farm (TN).
  • Rishabh aur kisaan Satish ki kahani: nahar, bijli pump, sadak, cold storage, internet — sab puzzle ke tukde. Fayde: vyapaar, tourism, door-daraaz ilaake, aapda mein madad, raksha.
  • Sadak: India ka network duniya mein 2nd (USA ke baad, 2024). State highways — rajya ka PWD; National highways aur expressways — Kendra. 2025 mein NH ~1,50,000 km. NH44 sabse lamba — 4,112 km, Srinagar se Kanyakumari. Golden Quadrilateral — Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata.
  • Dhola Sadiya Bridge (Bhupen Hazarika Setu) — Assam-Arunachal, Lohit nadi par, 9.15 km, India ka doosra sabse lamba pul; safar 4 ghante kam. Living root bridges (Meghalaya; Khasi mein jingkieng jri) — Ficus elastica ki jadon se, Khasi-Jaintia; 15–30 m, sadiyon tak tikte.
  • Railway: British ne 1853 mein shuru ki — cotton, chai bandargah tak, British maal ka bazaar, sena ki tez aavajahi. Aaj roz 2 crore+ yaatri; maalgaadi sadak se 75–90% kam urja; 100% electrification ka lakshya (2025).
  • Indian Railways — sabse bada employer (~12.1 lakh, 2024); duniya ka 4th sabse bada rail system (USA, China, Russia ke baad). Timeline: 1895 steam → 1957 diesel (WDM-1) → 1970 electric → 2019 Vande Bharat.
  • Metro — 23 shahar, 1,000+ km; jaldi duniya mein 3rd sabse bada; Delhi Metro mein solar bhi.
  • Hawai: domestic air traffic mein India 3rd (USA, China ke baad); 2024-25 mein ~37.6 crore yaatri; 2025 mein 159 airports. Kempegowda Airport (Bengaluru) — 'garden city' design.
  • Shipping: coastline ~11,100 km; 12 major aur 217 minor ports; dashak mein cargo 50% badha. TEU = Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit (container capacity).
  • Communication: Rani ka voice note — tower (bijli), fibre cable, satellite, data centre. E-governance (DigiLocker), e-commerce, online classes, SMS aapda alert.
  • Timeline: 1780 — India ka pehla chhapa akhbaar Bengal Gazette; 1844 telegraph (Morse); 1876 telephone (Bell); 1899 J.C. Bose ka wireless; 1930 All India Radio; 1947 budget AIR par; 1994 WWW... aaj 5G, AI.
  • J.C. Bose (1858–1937) — 1895 Calcutta mein microwave se deewar ke paar signal; naya coherer, galena detector (shuruaati semiconductor); patent nahi karaaye. Marconi ne 1901 mein patent, 1909 Nobel. Bose Institute — 1917.
  • 1990s mein mobile call ₹17/minute tak (incoming bhi); 2025 mein 116 crore wireless subscriber, ~90 crore internet connections.
  • Social infrastructure: school, hospital, police/fire station, court, park, library.
  • Chunautiyan: kachra, gaddhe, smarakon par likhna; behtar waste-sewer-traffic-paani; clean energy, bachchon-buzurgon-divyangon ke liye design; jaanwar paar karne par alarm.
  • Arthashastra: rajmarg, gaon ki sadak, bandargah ki sadak 16 m; jungle aur shahar ki sadak 8 m; rath ki sadak 2.5 m; baandh todne par kadi saza, park ki baad todne par jurmaana.

Yaad karne ki trick

NH44 = 'Shri-Kanya 4112'

Srinagar se Kanyakumari, 4,112 km — sabse lamba NH.

Rank yaad karo: '2-4-3-3'

Sadak 2nd, Rail 4th, Domestic air 3rd, Metro jaldi 3rd.

Ports = 12 + 217

12 major, 217 minor; coastline 11,100 km.

Bose vs Marconi

Bose 1895 (demo, patent nahi) → Marconi 1901 (patent) → 1909 Nobel.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. India ka sabse lamba National Highway kaun sa hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) NH44 — NH44 — 4,112 km, Srinagar se Kanyakumari.

  2. Q2. India mein railway kab shuru hui?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1853 — 1853 — British ne kachcha maal bandargah tak le jaane ke liye.

  3. Q3. Meghalaya ke living root bridges kis ped ki jadon se bante hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ficus elastica (Indian rubber tree) — Ficus elastica; Khasi aur Jaintia janjaatiyaan banaati hain.

  4. Q4. J.C. Bose ne wireless transmission ka pradarshan kab kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1895 — 1895, Calcutta. Marconi ne 1901 mein patent liya.

  5. Q5. India mein kitne major ports hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 12 — 12 major aur 217 minor ports.

  6. Q6. Dhola Sadiya Bridge kis nadi par hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Lohit — Lohit (Brahmaputra ki tributary); 9.15 km, Assam–Arunachal.

  7. Q7. Arthashastra ke anusaar rajmarg kitna chauda ho?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 16 m — 16 m; jungle/shahar ki sadak 8 m, rath ki sadak 2.5 m.
