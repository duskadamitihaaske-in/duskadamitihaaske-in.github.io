Understanding the Weather

Mausam ke 5 tatva, unhe naapne ke yantra, weather station aur forecast.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Mausam (weather) kya hai?

Aaj dhoop hai, kal barish — ye hai weather (mausam): kisi jagah aur samay par vaayumandal (atmosphere) ki haalat. Atmosphere = dharti ke chaaron or gaison ki chaadar ('hawa'). Sabse neeche ki parat troposphere — yahin hum rehte hain aur lagbhag saara mausam banta hai; ye 6–18 km oonchi (dhruvon par patli, equator ke paas moti). Upar stratosphere — jisme ozone parat hamein suraj ki haanikaarak kirnon se bachaati hai.

Mausam ke 5 tatva

(1) Temperature (garmi-thand), (2) Precipitation (barish, barf, ole, sleet), (3) Atmospheric pressure (hawa ka dabaav), (4) Wind (hawa ki gati aur disha), (5) Humidity (hawa mein bhaap/nami). Mausam ka vaigyanik adhyayan = meteorology. Purane log bhi sanket padhte the: cheentiyan ande oonchi jagah le jaayein, mendhak tarraayein, pine cone band ho jaaye → barish aane wali!

Garmi naapo — thermometer

Thermometer se temperature; ikaai Celsius ya Fahrenheit (15°C = 59°F). Din ka range = sabse zyada − sabse kam. Ausat (mean) din ka taapmaan = (max + min) ÷ 2. Misaal: max 30°C, min 20°C → mean 25°C, range 10°C.

Barish naapo — rain gauge

Rain gauge = keep (funnel) + cylinder + scale. Agar cylinder mein 5 mm paani jama hua to 5 mm barish. Tum ghar par bhi botal aur keep se bana sakte ho! India Meteorological Department (IMD) — 1875 se; motto 'adityat jayate vrishti' — 'Surya se varsha hoti hai'.

Hawa ka dabaav — barometer

Atmospheric pressure = hamare upar hawa ke stambh ka bojh. Samundar ke kinaare zyada, pahad par kam — isliye pahad par oxygen kam aur saans phoolti hai. Barometer se naapte hain, ikaai millibar (mb). Samundar tal par ~1013 mb; 1000 mb se kam = 'depression' / low pressure → toofan ya cyclone ka sanket. Khardung La (Ladakh, 5,600 m+) par sirf ~650 mb!

Hawa ki gati, disha aur nami

Wind hamesha zyada dabaav se kam dabaav ki taraf chalti hai — jaise gubbaare se hawa bahar nikalti hai. Disha — wind vane (airport par kapde ka wind sock); gati — anemometer (3–4 katoriyan ghoomti hain), km/h mein. Humidity — relative humidity %: sookha 20–40%, nami wala 60–80%. Zyada nami mein kapde der se sookhte, paseena zyada aata hai. Yantra: hygrometer. Garmi mein uttar India ki garam, dhool bhari hawa = Loo.

Weather station aur chetaavni ke rang

Saare yantra ek jagah = weather station. Automated Weather Station (AWS) — sensor khud record karte hain; 2023 mein NDMA ne Sikkim ki glacier jheel par 4,800 m+ par AWS lagaya. IMD ki chetaavni: Watch (jaankaari rakho), Alert (taiyaar raho), Warning (kadam uthao). Forecast se machhuaare samundar mein nahi jaate aur cyclone se pehle tat khaali karaaye jaate hain — jaanein bachti hain!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Weather = kisi jagah aur samay par vayumandal ki sthiti. Atmosphere = gas ki parat ('air').
  • Sabse neeche ki parat troposphere — yahin jeevan aur lagbhag saara mausam. Oonchai 6–18 km: poles par patli, tropics mein moti. Upar stratosphere (ozone layer).
  • Mausam ke 5 tatva: Temperature, Precipitation (baarish, barf, sleet, ole), Atmospheric pressure, Wind (gati + disha), Humidity (hawa mein bhaap).
  • Paramparik sanket: cheentiyan ande oonchi jagah le jaayein, mendhak tarraayein, pine cones band (nami) / khule (sookha) → baarish ka andaaza. Meteorology = mausam ka vaigyanik adhyayan.
  • Thermometer — temperature. Celsius/Fahrenheit: 15°C = 59°F. Range = max − min. Mean daily temp = (max + min) ÷ 2.
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) — 1875. Motto: 'ādityāt jāyate vṛiṣhṭi' — 'Surya se varsha hoti hai' (Manusmriti).
  • Rain gauge — funnel + cylinder + scale; 5 mm paani = 5 mm baarish.
  • Atmospheric pressure = upar ki hawa ka bhaar. Tat par zyada, pahaad par kam (kam oxygen → saans phoolna). Achanak giravat = 'depression' / low-pressure system → toofan/cyclone.
  • Barometer — pressure, unit millibar (mb). Tat par normal ~1013 mb; 1000 mb se kam = depression. Khardung La (Ladakh, 5,600 m+) par ~650 mb.
  • Wind = high pressure se low pressure ki taraf hawa. Wind vane — disha (airport par 'wind sock'). Anemometer — 3–4 cups, gati km/h mein.
  • Humidity → relative humidity (%): sookha 20–40%, nami wala 60–80%. Zyada nami mein paani dheere bhaap banta hai — kapde der se sookhte, paseena zyada. Yantra: Hygrometer. Food processing aur museums mein zaroori.
  • Weather station — saare yantra ek jagah. Automated Weather Station (AWS) — sensors se khud record. 2023: NDMA ne Sikkim ki glacial lake par 4,800 m+ par AWS lagaya.
  • IMD warning rang: Watch (Be Updated), Alert (Be Prepared), Warning (Take Action). Forecast se machhuaaron ko chetavani, cyclone mein tat khaali karwana.
  • Loo = uttar India ki garmi ki tez, garam, dhool bhari hawa.

Yaad karne ki trick

5 tatva: 'TP-PWH' = 'Tapu Pe Wah'

Temperature, Precipitation, Pressure, Wind, Humidity.

Yantra-jodi

Thermo = tapmaan, Baro = bhaar (pressure), Anemo = aandhi (hawa), Hygro = humidity, Rain gauge = baarish.

1013 ka funda

Normal 1013 mb; 1000 se neeche gira = depression; Khardung La sirf 650.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Lagbhag saara mausam vayumandal ki kis parat mein hota hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Troposphere — Troposphere — zameen ke sabse paas, 6–18 km tak.

  2. Q2. Hawa ki gati naapne ka yantra?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Anemometer — Anemometer ke cups jitni tez ghoomte hain, hawa utni tez.

  3. Q3. India Meteorological Department kab bana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1875 — 1875; motto 'ādityāt jāyate vṛiṣhṭi'.

  4. Q4. Kisi din max 32°C aur min 18°C hai. Mean daily temperature?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 25°C — (32 + 18) ÷ 2 = 25°C. Range = 32 − 18 = 14°C.

  5. Q5. Tat par atmospheric pressure kitne mb se neeche ho to 'depression' kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1000 — Normal ~1013 mb; 1000 mb se kam = depression.

  6. Q6. Delhi mein humidity 52%, Kochi mein 84%. Geele kapde kahan jaldi sookhenge?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Delhi — Kam humidity mein paani tezi se bhaap banta hai.
