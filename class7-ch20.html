Banks and the Magic of Finance

Bank kya karte hain, compound interest ka jaadu, RBI, payment systems (UPI), stock market aur fraud se bachaav.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Bank — paise ka 'pul'

Bank ek pul jaisa hai: ek taraf bachat karne wale (Navdeep ne ₹3,000 jama kiye), doosri taraf udhaar lene wale (Rima ne baans ke business ke liye loan liya). Bank jama (deposit) leta hai aur loan deta hai. Account ke prakaar: Savings (aam log; byaaj milta; nikaasi ki seema), Current (vyapaari; byaaj nahi; len-den ki seema nahi), Fixed Deposit (ek baar jama, 3–5 saal, zyada byaaj). Passbook mein har len-den — Debit = paisa nikla, Credit = paisa aaya.

Byaaj par byaaj — compounding ka jaadu

Interest (byaaj) = paise udhaar dene/lene ka 'kiraaya'. Compounding = pichhle byaaj par bhi byaaj! ₹1,000 @ 6%: 1st saal ₹1,060, 2nd saal ₹1,123.60, aur 12 saal mein ₹2,012 — lagbhag doguna! Kerala ke Ambalappuzha ki kahaani: sadhu ne shatranj ke har khaane mein chaawal doguna maanga — 8th khaane mein 128, 16th mein 32,768, 32nd tak 210 crore+! Ise exponential growth kehte hain.

Bank kamaata kaise hai?

Jama par kam byaaj deta, loan par zyada byaaj leta. Anand ne ₹200 @ 2% jama kiye (₹4 mila), Shreya ne ₹200 @ 5% loan liya (₹10 diya) → bank ki kamaai ₹6. Bank saara paisa loan mein nahi deta — kuch reserve rakhta hai. PM Jan Dhan Yojana (2014) — zero balance account; pehle sirf ~15 crore ke account the, ab 50 crore+ (zyaadatar mahilaayein); paisa seedha khaate mein. Post office ki yojanaayein: NSC, Kisan Vikas Patra, Sukanya Samriddhi; NABARD — gaon aur kheti ke loan.

RBI — bankon ka bank

Reserve Bank of India — 1935 mein bana, 1949 se sarkari; 'banker to banks' (central bank): note chhaapna-baantna, byaaj dar tay karna, bankon aur sarkar ko loan. Delhi daftar ke darwaaze par yaksha-yakshini — Kubera ke khazaane ke rakshak! Purane India mein mandir bhi bank jaise — kaarigaron, vyapaariyon ko udhaar; Kodumbalur (TN) mandir se byaaj par karz ke record.

Paisa bhejne ke tareeke aur UPI

Withdrawal slip, ATM (debit card + PIN, 24×7), cheque (tareekh, naam, rakam shabdon aur ankon mein, hastaakshar), POS machine, net banking, mobile (BHIM). UPI — 2016, NPCI ne banaaya; QR ya phone number se turant; COVID mein khoob chala; Nepal (2022) pehla videshi desh, phir UAE, France, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Mauritius — 'India's gift to the world'!

Share bazaar aur dhokhe se bachaav

Share = company mein hissedaari — jaise poori chapati ka ek tukda! Stock exchange par khareed-bikri; BSE (1875) duniya ke sabse purane exchange mein. Daam badhe = boom, gire = crash — company ke kaam, sarkari neeti, yuddh se utaar-chadhaav. Fraud se bacho: OTP, PIN, password kabhi share mat karo, anjaan link mat dabao. Shikaayat: 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Financial infrastructure = banks, payment systems, stock market, financial institutions — paise ka len-den aur prabandhan.
  • Bank — logon se deposit leta, udhaar lene walon ko loan deta. Navdeep (₹3,000 bachat jama) aur Rima (bamboo business ke liye loan) ki kahani.
  • Account ke prakaar: Savings (vyakti, byaaj milta, nikaasi ki seema), Current (vyapaari, byaaj nahi, len-den ki seema nahi), Fixed Deposit (ek baar, 3–5 saal, savings se zyada byaaj).
  • Interest = paise udhaar dene/lene ka kiraaya (%). Compounding = pichhle byaaj par bhi byaaj. ₹1,000 @ 6%: 1st saal ₹1,060, 2nd saal ₹1,123.60; 12 saal mein ₹2,012.20.
  • Kahani: Ambalappuzha (Kerala) ke raja aur sadhu — shatranj ke har khaane mein chawal doguna: 8th khaana 128, 16th 32,768, 32nd tak 210 crore+ — exponential growth!
  • Passbook — saare len-den ka record. Debit = paisa nikla, Credit = paisa aaya.
  • Bank ki kamaai: jama par kam byaaj, loan par zyada byaaj. Anand ₹200 @ 2% (₹4 mila), Shreya loan ₹200 @ 5% (₹10 diya) → bank ki kamaai ₹6. Bank saara jama loan mein nahi deta — reserve rakhta.
  • PM Jan Dhan Yojana — 2014: zero balance account; pehle sirf ~15 crore ke account, ab 50 crore+ (zyaadatar mahilaayein); seedha wage/scholarship transfer, bichauliye kam.
  • Post office: NSC, Kisan Vikas Patra, Sukanya Samriddhi. IFCI — udyog (bijli, kapda); NABARD — gramin vikaas, krishi loan.
  • RBI — 1935 mein bana; 1949 se sarkari 'banker to banks' (central bank): currency chhaapna-baantna, benchmark interest rate, banko aur sarkar ko loan. Delhi office ke dwaar par yaksha-yakshini (Kubera ke khazane ke rakshak).
  • Pracheen India mein mandir bank jaise — kaarigar, vyapaari, sthaniya sarkar ko udhaar; copper plates par anubandh. Kodumbalur (TN, 13th c.) — Tirumudukunramudaiya-Nayanar mandir se byaaj par karz.
  • Paisa nikaalna: withdrawal slip, debit card + ATM (24×7) + PIN. Transfer: cheque (tareekh, naam, rakam shabdon aur ankon mein, hastaakshar, MICR code), POS machine, net banking, mobile (BHIM).
  • UPI — 2016, NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India); QR ya phone number se turant; COVID mein lokpriya; kai bhashaein. Nepal (2022) pehla videshi desh; UAE, France, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Mauritius bhi → 'India's gift to the world'.
  • Stock market: share = company mein hissedari (chapati ka ek tukda); stock exchange par kharid-bikri. BSE — 1875, duniya ke sabse purane exchange mein. Daam badhe = boom, gire = crash; company ka pradarshan, sarkari neeti, tax, yuddh, economic shock se utaar-chadhaav.
  • Fraud se bachaav: OTP, PIN, password kabhi share mat karo; anjaan link mat dabao; device par bank details mat rakho. Shikayat: helpline 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in.

Yaad karne ki trick

3 account: 'S-C-F'

Savings = Sabke liye, byaaj; Current = Commerce (vyapaari), byaaj nahi; Fixed = Fix samay, sabse zyada byaaj.

Bank ki kamaai = spread

Loan ka byaaj − jama ka byaaj. (5% − 2% = 3% → ₹6 on ₹200)

Saal-ladder

BSE 1875 → RBI 1935 → RBI sarkari 1949 → Jan Dhan 2014 → UPI 2016 → Nepal mein UPI 2022.

Fraud helpline = 1930

'Unnees-sau-tees' — wahi saal jab Dandi March hua tha!

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Kis account mein aam taur par sabse zyada byaaj milta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Fixed Deposit — Fixed Deposit — paisa tay samay ke liye bandha rehta hai.

  2. Q2. ₹1,000 par 6% saalana compound interest. 2 saal baad kitna?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) ₹1,123.60 — 1,000 × 1.06 = 1,060; 1,060 × 1.06 = 1,123.60.

  3. Q3. UPI kisne aur kab launch kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) NPCI, 2016 — NPCI ne 2016 mein.

  4. Q4. UPI apnaane wala pehla videshi desh?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Nepal — Nepal — 2022.

  5. Q5. RBI kab sthaapit hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) 1935 — 1935; 1949 se sarkar ke adheen central bank.

  6. Q6. Sahil ko ₹10,000 par 12% saalana compound interest. 3 saal baad?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) ₹14,049.28 — 10,000 × 1.12 × 1.12 × 1.12 = 14,049.28.

  7. Q7. Cyber fraud ki shikayat kis helpline par?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1930 — 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in.

  8. Q8. Bombay Stock Exchange kab bana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1875 — 1875 — duniya ke sabse purane stock exchanges mein.
