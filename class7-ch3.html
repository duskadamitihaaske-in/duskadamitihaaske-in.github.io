Theme A · India and the World: Land and the People

Climates of India

Weather vs climate, India ke climate prakaar, unke kaaran, monsoon, aapdaayein aur climate change.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Weather, season, climate — teen alag cheezein

Weather = aaj ka mausam (roz badle). Season (ritu) = har saal laut kar aane wala samay (garmi, barsaat, sardi). Climate (jalvaayu) = kisi jagah ka lambe samay (kam se kam 30 saal) ka ausat pattern. Socho: tumhara aaj ka mood = weather, tumhara swabhaav = climate! Bharat ki paramparik 6 ritu: vasanta, grishma, varsha, sharad, hemanta, shishir — tyohaar bhi: Vasanta Panchami, Sharad Purnima.

India mein kitne tarah ki jalvaayu?

Himalaya — alpine (bahut thandi); neeche ke pahad — temperate (hill stations); uttari maidaan — subtropical (gehu); Thar — arid (sookha); pashchimi tat — tropical wet (chaawal, masaale); Deccan — semi-arid; poorv-dakshin — tropical. Ek desh, kai mausam!

Jalvaayu kaun tay karta hai? — 5 kaaran

(1) Latitude — equator par suraj ki kirne seedhi (garmi kendrit), dhruvon par tirchhi (garmi phaili) → Kanniyakumari garam, Srinagar thanda. (2) Altitude (oonchaai) — oonchaai badhe to hawa patli aur thandi: Ooty (10–25°C) vs Coimbatore (25–38°C) — latitude lagbhag same! (3) Samundar se nazdeeki — samundar taapmaan ko santulit rakhta hai: Mumbai (32°/18°, range ~14°) vs Nagpur (44°/10°, range 34°). (4) Hawa — garmi mein Arabia-Afghanistan se garam hawa → Punjab-Rajasthan mein heat wave; sardi mein Himalaya paar ki thandi hawa → cold wave. (5) Topography — Himalaya-Karakoram Central Asia ki thandi hawa rokte hain. Chhote ilaake ki apni jalvaayu = microclimate; concrete wala shehar aas-paas se garam = urban heat island.

Monsoon — hawa ka ulta-pulta khel

Monsoon Arabi shabd 'mausim' (mausam) se. Garmi mein zameen samundar se jaldi garam hoti hai → zameen par kam dabaav → samundar se nami wali hawa zameen ki taraf → barish! Sardi mein ulta. South-west monsoon: June ki shuruaat mein Kerala se, mid-July tak poore desh mein; Western Ghats ke pashchimi dhalaan par bahut barish, uske peeche Deccan par kam (rain shadow). North-east monsoon (sardi): Bay of Bengal se nami lekar Tamil Nadu jaise ilaakon mein barish. Mawsynram (Meghalaya) — duniya mein sabse zyada ausat barish, ~11,000 mm (11 metre!). Raag Megh Malhar barish ka raag hai.

Prakriti ke sanket aur monsoon ki 'naakaami'

Konkan ke machhuaare machhli ko satah par dekh kar barish bhaanp lete; Golden Shower (amaltas) ke khilne ke ~50 din baad monsoon; kauve ka ghonsla ooncha → kam barish. Agar monsoon fail ho jaaye: fasal barbaad, paani ki kami, log palaayan karte, aur cheezon ke daam badhte (mehngaai/inflation).

Aapdaayein — cyclone, baadh, landslide, aag

Cyclone — tez ghoomti hawa ka kam-dabaav; beech ka shaant, bina badal wala hissa = 'eye of the cyclone'; poorvi tat par zyada; NDRF ki 12 battalion. Baadh — UP, Bihar, Kerala, Andhra, Assam; Himalaya mein glacier jheel phatna; 2013 Uttarakhand (Kedarnath) — ~6,000 maut; shehron mein kharab naali aur qabze se baadh. Landslide — Himachal, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal, Western Ghats; jungle kaatna, galat nirmaan. Jungle ki aag — Uttarakhand, Himachal, MP, Chhattisgarh.

Climate change — dharti ko bukhaar

19th sadi se insaan koyla, petrol, gas (fossil fuels) jala raha hai aur jungle kaat raha hai. Isse greenhouse gases (jaise CO₂) badhti hain jo garmi ko kambal ki tarah rok leti hain → global warming. 2025 ki shuruaat mein India ka taapmaan normal se 1–3°C upar raha. Ilaaj (mitigation): pradooshan kam, ped lagaao, solar-wind jaisi renewable urja.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Weather = roz badalne wala; Season = har saal lautne wala; Climate = lambe samay (kam se kam 3 dashak) ka mausam pattern.
  • Paramparik 6 ritu: vasanta (spring), grishma (summer), varsha (rainy), sharad (autumn), hemanta (pre-winter), shishir (winter). Tyohaar: Vasanta Panchami, Sharad Purnima.
  • India ke climate: Himalaya — alpine; neeche pahaad — temperate (hill stations); uttari maidan — subtropical (gehun); Thar — arid; pashchimi tat — tropical wet (chawal, masale); Deccan — semi-arid; purvi/dakshini — tropical.
  • Climate ke 5 kaaran: Latitude, Altitude, Proximity to sea, Winds, Topography.
  • Latitude: Equator par surya ki kirne seedhi (kendrit), poles par tirchhi (phaili) → poles thande. Kanniyakumari garam, Srinagar thanda.
  • Altitude: oonchai badhe → hawa ka ghanatva kam → thanda. Ooty (10–25°C) vs Coimbatore (25–38°C), latitude same.
  • Samudra ki nazdeeki: samudra tapmaan sambhaalta hai. Mumbai (32°C / 18°C, range ~14°C) vs Nagpur (44°C / 10°C, range 34°C).
  • Winds: Arabia-Afghanistan se aati sookhi garam hawa → Punjab-Rajasthan mein heat wave; sardi mein Himalaya paar ki thandi hawa → cold wave.
  • Topography: Himalaya-Karakoram Central Asia ki thandi hawa se bachaate; samtal Thar ke paas koi bachaav nahi.
  • Microclimate — chhote kshetra ka alag climate; urban heat island = concrete wale shahar aas-paas se garam.
  • Monsoon — Arabic 'mausim' = season. Garmi mein zameen tezi se garam → low pressure → samudra se nami wali hawa → baarish. Sardi mein ulta.
  • Southwest (summer) monsoon: June ki shuruaat mein dakshini sire se, mid-July tak poore desh mein. Western Ghats ke pashchimi dhalaan par bahut baarish, Deccan par kam. Northeast (winter) monsoon: Bay of Bengal se nami → purv aur dakshin ke hisson mein baarish.
  • Mawsynram (Meghalaya) — duniya mein sabse zyada saalana ausat baarish, ~11,000 mm (11 metre!). Raag: Megh Malhar, Amritavarshini.
  • Paramparik gyaan: Konkan ke machhuaare machhli satah par dekhein; Golden Shower tree (Cassia fistula) khilne ke ~50 din mein monsoon; kauve ka ghonsla oonchaa → kam baarish.
  • 'Monsoon failure' → kheti barbaad, paani ki kami, palayan, mehngai (inflation).
  • Cyclone: tez low-pressure, ghoomti hawa; kendra = 'eye of the cyclone' (badal rahit). Zyada purvi tat par. NDRF — 12 jagah battalions.
  • Flood: UP, Bihar, Kerala, Andhra, Assam. Himalaya mein glacial burst; 2013 Uttarakhand (Kedarnath) — ~6,000 maut. Shehri baadh — kharab drainage, encroachment.
  • Landslide: Himachal, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal, Western Ghats; jungal katna, galat nirmaan. Forest fire: Uttarakhand, Himachal, MP, Chhattisgarh.
  • Climate change: 19th century se insaani kaaran — fossil fuels (coal, petroleum, natural gas), deforestation. Greenhouse gases (CO₂) garmi rokti → global warming. 2025 ki shuruaat mein India ka ausat temp normal se 1–3°C upar. Upaay = mitigation: emission kam, ped, renewable energy.

Yaad karne ki trick

5 kaaran: 'LAP-WiT'

Latitude, Altitude, Proximity to sea, Winds, Topography.

6 ritu: 'Va-Gri-Var-Sha-He-Shi'

Vasanta → Grishma → Varsha → Sharad → Hemanta → Shishir.

Monsoon ka naksha

Garmi = SW (samudra → zameen, geela); Sardi = NE (zameen → samudra, sookha; Bay paar karke dakshin mein thodi baarish).

Mumbai vs Nagpur = 14 vs 34

Samudra paas = chhota range; door = bada range.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Climate tay karne ke liye kam se kam kitne samay ka mausam dekha jaata hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 3 dashak (30 saal) — Climate = teen dashak ya usse zyada ka pattern.

  2. Q2. Ooty aur Coimbatore ka latitude lagbhag same hai, phir bhi Ooty thanda kyun?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Oonchai (altitude) zyada hai — Altitude badhne se hawa patli aur thandi hoti hai.

  3. Q3. 'Monsoon' shabd kis bhasha ke kis shabd se aaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Arabic 'mausim' — Arabic 'mausim' = season.

  4. Q4. Duniya mein sabse zyada saalana ausat baarish kahan?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Mawsynram — Mawsynram, Meghalaya — ~11,000 mm.

  5. Q5. Cyclone ke badal-rahit kendra ko kya kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Eye of the cyclone — Hawa kendra ki taraf ghoomti hai, kendra (eye) shaant aur badal rahit hota hai.

  6. Q6. Mumbai ka temperature range ~14°C, Nagpur ka 34°C. Kaaran?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Samudra ki nazdeeki — Samudra tapmaan ko moderate karta hai; Nagpur andar hai.

  7. Q7. 2013 mein Kedarnath ki aapda kis kaaran hui?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Glacial burst aur bhaari baarish — Lagaataar baarish se glacial burst, landslides; ~6,000 maut.
