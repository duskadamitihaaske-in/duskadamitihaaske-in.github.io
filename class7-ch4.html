Theme B · Tapestry of the Past

New Beginnings: Cities and States

Second Urbanisation — janapada, 16 mahajanapada, gana-sangha, iron, sikke aur varna-jati vyavastha.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Shehar gaye, phir lautey — 'Second Urbanisation'

Harappa ke shehar (First Urbanisation) 2nd millennium BCE ki shuruaat mein bikhar gaye, aur lagbhag 1,000 saal tak bade shehar dikhe hi nahi. Phir 1st millennium BCE mein Ganga ke maidaan aur aas-paas naye shehar bane — Second Urbanisation — jo aaj tak chal raha hai! Jaankaari khudai aur late Vedic, Buddh, Jain granthon se milti hai.

Janapada se Mahajanapada

Janapada = 'jahan jana (log) ne pada (pair) rakha' — ek kul ka ilaaka, mukhiya raja. 8th–7th sadi BCE tak janapada milkar bade Mahajanapada bane — sabse prachalit soochi mein 16: uttar-pashchim mein Gandhara, poorv mein Anga, Godavari ke paas Ashmaka… Sabse taaqatwar: Magadha (raajdhani Rajagriha — aaj Rajgir, Bihar), Kosala (Shravasti), Vatsa (Kaushambi), Avanti (Ujjayini). Kai raajdhaniyan aaj bhi jeevit shehar — ~2,500 saal purane!

Ganga ke maidaan hi kyon?

Upjaau zameen (khoob anaaj), aas-paas pahadon mein loha (iron ore), aur naye vyapaari raaste. Raajdhaniyon ke chaaron or mitti/pathar ki deewaar (rampart), bahar paani bhari khaai (moat), aur patle darwaaze taaki aana-jaana niyantrit rahe.

Raja bhi, ganrajya bhi

Har janapada mein sabha/samiti — buzurgon ki salaah; kuch granthon ke anusaar ayogya raja hataaya ja sakta tha. Zyaadatar mahajanapada monarchy (raja peedhi-dar-peedhi; tax, kaanoon, qile, sena). Par Vajji (Vrijji) aur Malla mein gana/sangha — sabha charcha aur vote se faisle karti, raja bhi chunti! Ye duniya ke sabse shuruaati ganrajyon (republics) mein the.

Lohe ka zamaana aur pehle sikke

Harappa = taamba/kaansa; Second Urbanisation = loha. Lohe ke auzaaron se badi kheti aur tez-halke hathiyaar. India ke pehle sikke — chaandi ke punch-marked coins (thappa maar kar chinh); baad mein taamba, sona; har mahajanapada ke apne sikke.

Jaati aur varna — samaaj ki banawat

Jati = khaas pesha wala samudaay — hunar peedhi dar peedhi, apne riwaaj. Varna (Vedic granth) = 4: Brahmin (gyaan, anushthaan), Kshatriya (raksha), Vaishya (vyapaar, kheti), Shudra (kaarigar, mazdoor). 'Caste' shabd Portuguese 'casta' se (16th sadi). Shuru mein vyavastha lachili thi — log pesha badal lete; samay ke saath, khaas kar angrezi raaj mein, kathor hui aur bhedbhaav badha.

Raaste aur dakshin-poorv ke shehar

Do bade raaste: Uttarapatha (uttar-pashchim → Ganga maidaan → poorv) aur Dakshinapatha (Kaushambi se Vindhya paar karke dakshin). Shishupalgarh (Bhubaneswar; 1948 mein khudai) — Kalinga ki raajdhani, chaukor naksha. Dakshin mein ~400 BCE se shehar; Chola, Chera, Pandya. Kodumanal (Erode, TN) — shankh aur ratna udyog. 300–200 BCE tak poora upmahadweep jud gaya aur mahajanapadon ka daur khatam hua.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Harappan shehar (First Urbanisation) 2nd millennium BCE ki shuruaat mein bikhre; lagbhag ek hazaar saal tak shehri jeevan gaayab raha.
  • 1st millennium BCE mein Ganga ke maidan, Indus basin aur aas-paas naya shehrikaran — 'Second Urbanisation', jo aaj tak chal raha hai. Saboot: khudai + late Vedic, Buddhist, Jain granth.
  • Janapada = 'jahan jan (log) ne pada (pair) rakha' — ek clan ka ilaaka, mukhiya rājā. 2nd millennium BCE ke ant tak bane.
  • 8th–7th century BCE tak janapada mil kar mahājanapada; sabse prachalit soochi = 16. Uttar-pashchim mein Gandhara, purab mein Anga, Godavari ke paas Ashmaka (central/south).
  • Sabse shaktishaali: Magadha (Rajagriha — aaj Rajgir, Bihar), Kosala (Shravasti), Vatsa (Kaushambi), Avanti (Ujjayini). Kai rajdhaniyan aaj bhi zinda shahar — ~2,500 saal purane!
  • Zyaadatar mahajanapada Ganga plains mein — upjaau zameen, aas-paas pahaadon mein iron ore, naye trade networks.
  • Rajdhaniyan: kile ki deewar (rampart), bahar moat (paani bhari khaai), patle darwaaze taaki aana-jaana control ho.
  • Har janapada mein sabhā / samiti (sabha) — buzurgon ki salaah; kuch granth kehte hain ayogya raja hataaya ja sakta tha.
  • Zyaadatar mahajanapada monarchy — raja peedhi-dar-peedhi; kar vasoolna, law & order, kile, sena.
  • Vajji (Vrijji) aur Malla — gaṇa / sangha: sabha charcha aur vote se faisle leti, raja bhi chunti → 'early republics', duniya ke sabse shuruaati.
  • Takneek: Harappa = copper/bronze; Second Urbanisation = iron. Late 2nd millennium BCE tak lohe ke auzaar aam → badi kheti, tez halke hathiyaar.
  • India ke pehle sikke — chaandi ke punch-marked coins (chinh 'punch' kiye); baad mein tamba, sona. Har mahajanapada ke apne sikke.
  • Samaaj: jāti = khaas pesha wala samudaay (hunar peedhi-dar-peedhi; sub-jati ke apne riwaaj). Varṇa (Vedic granthon se) = 4: Brahmin (gyaan, anushthaan), Kshatriya (raksha), Vaishya (vyapaar, kheti), Shudra (kaarigar, mazdoor).
  • 'Caste' shabd Portuguese 'casta' se (16th century). Shuruaat mein vyavastha lachili thi — log halaat ke hisaab se pesha badalte. Samay ke saath, khaas kar British raj mein, kathor hui aur bhedbhaav badha.
  • Do bade raaste: Uttarapatha (uttar-pashchim → Ganga plains → purab) aur Dakshinapatha (Kaushambi, Prayagraj ke paas, se Vindhya paar karke dakshin).
  • Shishupalgarh (aaj Bhubaneswar ka hissa, 1948 mein khudai) — Kalinga ki rajdhani, square plan. Dakshin mein shahar ~400 BCE se; Chola, Chera, Pandya. Kodumanal (Erode, TN) — shell aur gemstone udyog.
  • 300–200 BCE tak poora upmahadweep jud gaya; mahajanapada khatam hue.

Yaad karne ki trick

Top-4 ka 'MaKoVA'

Magadha–Rajagriha, Kosala–Shravasti, Vatsa–Kaushambi, Avanti–Ujjayini.

Republic jodi: 'VaMa'

Vajji + Malla = gana-sangha, sabha raja chunti — early republics.

Do raaste

Uttarapatha = uttar ka highway; Dakshinapatha = Kaushambi se dakshin.

1st vs 2nd Urbanisation

Pehla = Harappa, bronze, Indus; Doosra = Ganga, iron, sikke, monasteries.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 'Second Urbanisation' kis kaal aur kshetra mein shuru hui?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1st millennium BCE, Ganga plains — 1st millennium BCE mein Ganga ke maidan aur aas-paas.

  2. Q2. 'Janapada' shabd ka arth hai —

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Jahan logon ne pair rakha (basaavat) — Jana (log) + pada (pair) = jahan log bas gaye.

  3. Q3. Kin mahajanapadon ko 'early republics' kaha jaata hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Vajji aur Malla — Vajji aur Malla mein sabha vote se faisle aur raja ka chunaav karti thi.

  4. Q4. India ke sabse pehle sikke kis dhaatu ke the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Chaandi (punch-marked) — Chaandi naram hoti hai, ispe chinh punch kiye jaate the.

  5. Q5. Dakshinapatha raasta kahan se shuru hota tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kaushambi — Kaushambi (Prayagraj ke paas) se Vindhya paar karke dakshin.

  6. Q6. Second Urbanisation mein kaun si nayi takneek sabse ahem thi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Iron metallurgy — Lohe ke auzaar se badi kheti aur behtar hathiyaar.
