Theme B · Tapestry of the Past

The Age of Reorganisation

Maurya ke baad (c. 185 BCE – 3rd century CE): Shunga, Satavahana, Chedi, Sangam ke teen raj, Indo-Greek, Shaka, Kushana.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

'Punargathan' ka daur kyon?

~185 BCE mein aakhri Maurya samraat ko uske senapati Pushyamitra Shunga ne maar diya. Bada samrajya toot kar kai naye raajya ban gaye — jaise ek badi kitaab ke panne bikhar kar alag-alag chhoti kitaabein ban jaayein. Isliye ise 'Age of Reorganisation' kehte hain. Raajya badhaane ke do tareeke: shaadi se gathbandhan ya yuddh. Is daur mein kala, vaastu aur sahitya khoob phale.

Shunga aur Satavahana

Shunga: Pushyamitra ne ashvamedha yajna kiya (ghoda chhoda jaata — jahan wo bina roke ghoome, wo ilaaka raja ka). Patanjali ne Yoga Sutra isi samay sankalit kiye. Bharhut stupa ki nakkaashidaar railing. Satavahana ('Andhra') — 2nd sadi BCE se Deccan; raajdhani Amaravati, Pratishthana (Paithan); sikkon par do mastool wala jahaaz → Rome tak vyapaar (masaale, kapda, chandan, moti). Raja maa ke naam se jaane jaate — Gautamiputra Satakarni (maa Gautami Balashri, Nashik shilalekh). Naneghat gufaon ke Brahmi ankon mein kuch aaj ke ankon jaise — yaani aaj ke numerals ki jad India mein! Karla gufaayein, sunaar Kanhadasa ka Pitalkhora yaksha.

Kharavela — 'bhikshu-raja'

Chedi vansh (Kalinga) ke raja Kharavela — Jain bhakt. Udayagiri-Khandagiri gufaayein (Bhubaneswar). Hathigumpha shilalekh mein saal-dar-saal unke kaam: 'har sampradaay ka samman, har mandir ki marammat'.

Dakshin ke 'teen mukutdhaari' aur Sangam

Chera, Chola, Pandya — Ashoka ke samay bhi azaad. Inki kavitaon ka sangrah = Sangam sahitya (sangam = sabha) — prem, veerta, udaarta. Chola raja Karikala ne Kaveri par Kallanai (Grand Anicut) banaya — aaj bhi chalu, ~2,000 saal baad! Isse Kaveri delta = 'dakshin ka chaawal katora'. Silappadikaram ('paayal ki kahaani'): Kannagi ke pati Kovalan par Madurai mein jhootha chori ka ilzaam laga kar maar diya gaya; Kannagi ne apni doosri paayal se sach saabit kiya — sandesh: raja ko nyaay karna hi hoga. Chera (Vanji/Karur) — Rome se vyapaar; Pandya (Madurai) — moti ke liye mashhoor.

Bahar se aaye log — Indo-Greek, Shaka, Kushana

Indo-Greek — Alexander ke satrapon ke vanshaj. Heliodorus pillar (Vidisha) — Greek doot ne Vasudeva (Krishna) ko 'devon ka dev' kaha; teen amrit niyam: aatm-sanyam, daan, chetna. Shaka — Shaka Samvat Gregorian se 78 saal peeche; 1957 mein yahi Indian National Calendar bana. Kushana — Kanishka sabse shaktishaali ('maharaja rajadhiraja devaputra'); sikkon par Buddha aur Shiva-Nandi; Silk Route par niyantran. Bahar se aaye log yahin ke rang mein rang gaye!

Moortikala ke do school

Gandhara — Greek-Roman + Bharatiya mel; sleti-kaala pathar (schist); asli shareer jaisi banawat, lehraate vastra. Mathura — desi shaili; laal balua pathar; bhare-poore roop (Kubera ki moonchh!). Isi kaal mein Sanskrit sahitya phala; Mahabharata-Ramayana ki rachna aage badhi.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • ~185 BCE: antim Maurya samraat ki hatya senapati Pushyamitra Shunga ne ki → samrajya toota, kai naye rajya — isliye 'Age of Reorganisation'.
  • Rajya badhaane ke tareeke: matrimonial alliance (shaadi se gathbandhan) ya yuddh. Kala, sthapatya, sahitya mein ubhaar.
  • Desi vansh: Shunga, Chedi, Satavahana, Chola, Chera, Pandya. Bahar se aaye: Indo-Greeks, Shakas, Kushanas.
  • Shunga: Pushyamitra ne ashvamedha yajna kiya (ghoda chhoda — jahan bina rok ghooma, wo ilaaka raja ka). Vedic anushthaanon ka punarjagran; ~ek sadi chale. Patanjali ne Yoga Sutras isi kaal mein sankalit kiye.
  • Bharhut Stupa (MP) — Shungon ne nakkaashidaar railings aur Buddha ke jeevan ke drishya jode; shuruaati Buddhist kala.
  • Satavahana ('Andhra') — 2nd century BCE se Deccan: aaj ka Andhra, Telangana, Maharashtra; rajdhani Amaravati, Pratishthana (Paithan). Sikkon par do mastool wala jahaaz → samudri vyapaar; Roman Empire tak trade (masale, kapda, chandan, moti, haathi-daant; import: kaanch, itr).
  • Satavahana raja maa ke naam se: Gautamiputra Satakarni (maa Gautami Balashri — Nashik inscription). Naneghat (Pune ke paas) gufaein — toll/tax, aur ek vidhwa rani ke ashvamedha ka inscription; Brahmi mein kuch ank aaj ke ankon jaise → modern numerals ka mool India.
  • Satavahana Vasudeva (Krishna) ke bhakt, par Vedic vidwaan, Jain aur Buddhist bhikshuon ko kar-mukt zameen di. Karla caves (Lonavala), Pitalkhora yaksha (sunaar Kanhadasa ne banaya). 3rd century CE mein toote.
  • Chedi (Kalinga): raja Kharavela — Jain bhakt, 'bhikshu-raja'; Udayagiri-Khandagiri gufaein (Bhubaneswar); Hathigumpha inscription (Brahmi) — saal-dar-saal uplabdhiyan, 'har sampradaay ka samman, har mandir ki marammat'.
  • Dakshin mein Chera, Chola, Pandya — 'teen mukutdhaari raja'. Ashoka ke samay bhi swatantra. Kavitaon ka sangrah = Sangam literature (sangam = sangha = sabha) → 'Sangam Age'; prem, veerta, udaarta.
  • Chola (3rd c. BCE – 13th c. CE): raja Karikala ne Chera-Pandya ko haraaya; Kallanai / Grand Anicut — Kaveri par, Srirangam ke neeche; aaj bhi chalu → Kaveri delta 'rice bowl of the South'.
  • Silappadikaram ('paayal ki kahani'): Kannagi aur Kovalan, Chola rajdhani Puhar se Madurai (Pandya); jhootha chori ka aarop → Kannagi ne doosri paayal se sach saabit kiya, Madurai jalaaya; aaj TN-Kerala mein devi roop mein pooja. Sandesh: nyaay aur raja ka dharma.
  • Chera ('Keralaputra') — rajdhani Vanji (Karur); Rome aur West Asia se vyapaar (masale, lakdi, haathi-daant, moti); Sangam kaviyon ke sangrakshak.
  • Pandya — rajdhani Madurai; Megasthenes ne samriddh bataya; moti ke liye mashhoor; nausena shakti.
  • Indo-Greeks — Alexander ke chhode satrapon ke vanshaj. Heliodorus pillar (Vidisha, MP) — Indo-Greek doot ne Vasudeva ko 'devon ka dev' kaha; teen amrit niyam: aatm-sanyam, daan, chetna. Sikkon par Vasudeva-Krishna, Lakshmi bhi.
  • Shakas (Indo-Scythians) — late 2nd c. BCE se 5th c. CE. Shaka Samvat — Gregorian se 78 saal peeche (Jan–Mar mein 79); 1957 mein Indian National Calendar bana.
  • Kushanas — Central Asia se, shayad 2nd c. CE; Kanishka sabse shaktishaali — sir rahit pratima (1.85 m): 'maharaja rajadhiraja devaputra'. Sikkon par Buddha (Greek 'BOΔΔO') aur Shiva-Nandi. Silk Route par niyantran.
  • Kala ke do school: Gandhara — Greco-Roman + Indian, grey-black schist, asli jaisi anatomy, behte vastra. Mathura — desi shaili, laal balua pathar (red sandstone), bhare-poore roop (Kubera ki moonchh!).
  • Isi kaal mein Sanskrit sahitya phala-phula — Mahabharata aur Ramayana ki rachna aage badhi.

Yaad karne ki trick

Gandhara vs Mathura: 'G = Grey, Greek'

Gandhara = Grey schist + Greek asar. Mathura = laal pathar, Maati ka desi roop.

Shaka Samvat = 78

Gregorian − 78 = Shaka saal; 1957 se rashtriya calendar.

Sangam ke 3 raja + rajdhani

Chola–Puhar/Uraiyur, Chera–Vanji (Karur), Pandya–Madurai. 'Cho-Che-Pa'.

Maa ke naam wala raja

Gautamiputra Satakarni — putra = 'Gautami ka beta'.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Maurya vansh ka ant ~185 BCE mein kisne kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Pushyamitra Shunga — Senapati Pushyamitra Shunga ne antim Maurya ki hatya ki aur Shunga vansh shuru kiya.

  2. Q2. Hathigumpha inscription kis raja se juda hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kharavela — Kalinga ke Chedi raja Kharavela — Udayagiri gufa mein.

  3. Q3. Grand Anicut (Kallanai) kis raja ne banwaaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Karikala — Chola raja Karikala — Kaveri par, aaj bhi upyog mein.

  4. Q4. Shaka Samvat Gregorian calendar se kitne saal peeche hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 78 — 78 saal (Jan–Mar mein 79). 1957 mein Indian National Calendar bana.

  5. Q5. Gandhara school ki moortiyan mukhyatah kis pathar ki hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Grey-black schist — Gandhara = grey schist + Greco-Roman asar; Mathura = red sandstone.

  6. Q6. Silappadikaram ki naayika kaun hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kannagi — Kannagi — paayal se pati Kovalan ki begunahi saabit ki.

  7. Q7. Heliodorus pillar mein kis devta ko 'devon ka dev' kaha gaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Vasudeva — Indo-Greek doot Heliodorus ne Vasudeva (Krishna) ki stuti ki.
