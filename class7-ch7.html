The Gupta Era: An Age of Tireless Creativity

Gupta samrajya (3rd–6th century CE) — 'classical age': vigyan, sahitya, kala; saath mein Vakataka, Pallava, Kamarupa.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Gupta kaal — 'sunehra' kyon kehte hain?

Socho ek lamba shaanti ka samay — log khush, vyapaar badha, kala-vigyaan khoob phale. Gupta kaal aisa hi tha, isliye ise 'classical age' kaha jaata hai. Us samay aur raajya bhi the: Vakataka (raajdhani Nandivardhana, Nagpur ke paas), Pallava (Kanchipuram), Kamarupa (Assam). Ek mazedaar saboot: Delhi ke Mehrauli ka Iron Pillar — 1,600+ saal purana, 6 ton, phir bhi aaj tak jang nahi lagi! Raaz: oxygen se bani patli suraksha parat. Ye Chandragupta II ke samay ka hai.

Teen bade Gupta raja

Chandragupta I — sikke aur gathbandhan; sikke par rani Kumaradevi, peeche Lakshmi. Samudragupta — kavi Harisena ki Prayaga Prashasti (Prayagraj stambh) mein unki tareef; lakshya 'dharani-bandha' (dharti ko ek karna); haare rajaon ko aksar tribute lekar gaddi lauta di; sikke par veena bajaate dikhte hain! Chandragupta II 'Vikramaditya' — Vishnu bhakt (Garuda chinh); darbar mein vidwaan aur kavi.

Prabhavati Gupta — 'do rajaon ki maa'

Chandragupta II ki beti Prabhavati ki shaadi Vakataka raajkumar se hui (shaadi se gathbandhan). Pati ki maut ke baad wo regent (chhote beton ki jagah raaj chalaane wali) bani; Vishnu ke 7 mandir banwaaye (Ramagiri/Ramtek, Maharashtra).

Raaj aur vyapaar

Raajya pradeshon mein bata; zameen ke daan taamrapatron par likhe jaate. Mukhya aamdani zameen ka tax; jurmaana, khaan, sinchaai, vyapaar kar bhi. Vyapaar Mediterranean, SE Asia, China tak — kapda, masaale, haathi-daant, ratna. Arabian Sea ke Socotra dweep par Brahmi shilalekh — Bharatiya vyapaari wahan tak gaye! Vishnu bhakt hote hue bhi Guptas ne Nalanda aur Buddh viharon ki madad ki — udaar soch. Chinese yaatri Faxian (Fa-Hien): log khush, shehar ameer, daan aur muft dawaakhaane; par chandalon se kathor vyavhaar bhi likha — isliye itihaas ke liye kai sources dekhne chahiye.

Aryabhata — dharti ghoomti hai!

Aryabhata (~500 CE, Kusumapura, Patna ke paas) — granth Aryabhatiya. Unhone bataaya ki dharti apni dhuri par ghoomti hai, isliye din-raat hote hain; saal ki lambaai 365 din 6 ghante 12 min ke kareeb (aaj ka maan 365 d 5 h 48 m); grahan ka sahi kaaran; dharti ka size. Varahamihira (Ujjayini) — Brihat Samhita: khagol, mausam, vaastu, nagar yojana, kheti.

Kavi, vaidya aur kalaakaar

Kalidasa — Meghadutam ('baadal doot'): ek yaksha baadal se apni priya ko sandesh bhejta hai, aur raaste ke uttar India ke drishya ka sundar varnan; Raghuvansham bhi. Ayurveda ke granth Charaka Samhita aur Sushruta Samhita ko antim roop — rog, ilaaj, aahaar, surgery; mann-shareer-prakriti ka rishta. Kala: Sarnath ki Buddha moortiyan, Ajanta ke chitra (Bodhisattva Padmapani), Udayagiri (MP) gufaayein, Deogarh Dashavatara mandir (Vishnu Sheshnaag par), Ahichchhatra ki terracotta (Ganga makar par, Yamuna kachhue par).

Ant aur dakshin-poorv ke raajya

6th sadi CE mein Central Asia ke Huna hamlon aur kshetriya rajaon ke ubharne se Gupta kamzor hue. Pallava — Shiv bhakt; Kanchipuram = 'city of a thousand temples'; ghatika (shiksha kendra). Kamarupa — Brahmaputra ghaati, purana naam Pragjyotisha. Samudragupta ne Pallava aur Kamarupa ko haraaya par seedha raaj nahi kiya — tribute lekar gaddi lautaayi.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Samkaaleen rajya: Gupta (rajdhani Pataliputra), Vakataka (rajdhani Nandivardhana, Nagpur ke paas), Pallava (rajdhani Kanchipuram, 9th c. ke uttarardh tak), Kamarupa (Assam, Varman vansh).
  • Iron Pillar, Mehrauli (Delhi) — 1,600+ saal purana, 6 ton, aaj tak jang nahi; Chandragupta II ke samay, Vishnu ko samarpit, shayad pehle Udayagiri (MP) mein. Raaz: oxygen se bani patli suraksha parat.
  • Guptas shayad aaj ke UP kshetra ke regional raja the; Kushanas kamzor hue to ubhre.
  • Chandragupta I — sikke aur gathbandhan; sikke par rani Kumaradevi, peeche Lakshmi.
  • Samudragupta — Prayaga Prashasti (Prayagraj stambh) — kavi Harisena; lakshya 'dharani-bandha' (dharti ko ek karna). Haare raja kai baar wapas gaddi par, tribute dekar. Sikke par veena bajaate; ashvamedha ke sikke bhi.
  • Chandragupta II 'Vikramaditya' — Vishnu bhakt (Garuda chinh); darbar mein vidwaan, kavi, kalakaar. Titles: maharajadhiraja, samrat, chakravartin.
  • Vishnu Purana: Gupta raaj — Anuganga, Prayaga, Saketa (Ayodhya), Magadha. Shikhar par zyaadatar uttar-pashchim India + madhya-purv ke hisse.
  • Faxian (Fa-Hien) — Chinese yaatri, early 5th century CE: log khush, ameer shahar, Vaishya parivaar daan aur dawaakhaane chalaate, garib-beemaar ki dekhbhaal. Par chandalon ke saath kathor vyavhaar bhi likha — ek source ka nazariya seemit, kai sources se jaanch zaroori.
  • Prabhavati Gupta — Chandragupta II ki beti, Vakataka rajkumar se vivaah (matrimonial alliance); pati ki maut ke baad regent; 'do rajaon ki maa'; Vishnu ke 7 mandir, Ramagiri (Ramtek, Maharashtra).
  • Prashasan: pradesh mein baanta; land grants — copper plates par record. Mukhya aay = land tax; aur jurmaana, khaan, sinchai, vyapaar kar.
  • Vyapaar: Mediterranean, SE Asia, China — kapda, masale, haathi-daant, ratn. Socotra Island (Arabian Sea) par Brahmi inscriptions, stupa design — Indian vyapaari.
  • Vishnu bhakt hote hue bhi Nalanda aur Buddhist viharon ko sahaara → udaar soch. Lambi shaanti → 'classical age'.
  • Aryabhata — Kusumapura (Patna ke paas), ~500 CE, Aryabhatiya; dharti apni dhuri par ghoomti hai (din-raat); saal = 365 din 6 ghante 12 min 30 sec (aaj ka maan 365 d 5 h 48 m 45 s); grahan ka sahi kaaran; dharti ka size.
  • Varahamihira — Ujjayini; Brihat Samhita — khagol, jyotish, mausam forecast, vaastu, town planning, kheti.
  • Kalidasa — Meghadutam ('badal doot'): yaksha badal se priya ko sandesh bhejta; uttar India ke landscape ka varnan. (Chapter ka quote — Raghuvansham.)
  • Ayurveda ka codification: Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita ko antim roop — rog, ilaaj, aahaar, dawa, surgery; mann-sharir-prakriti ka sambandh.
  • Kala kendra: Sarnath (Buddha moortiyan), Ajanta (Gupta + Vakataka sahayog; Bodhisattva Padmapani chitra), Udayagiri (MP) gufaein, Deogarh Dashavatara mandir (UP; Vishnu Sheshnaag par). Ahichchhatra ki terracotta: Ganga makara par, Yamuna kachhue par.
  • Patan: 6th century CE — Central Asia ke Huna hamle + regional rajaon ka ubhaar.
  • Pallava — Satavahano ke adheen the, phir swatantra; Shiva bhakt; Kanchipuram = 'city of a thousand temples'; ghatika (shiksha kendra). Kamarupa — Brahmaputra ghaati, pracheen naam Pragjyotisha, Bhagadatta (Mahabharata).
  • Samudragupta ne Pallava aur Kamarupa ko haraaya par seedha raaj nahi kiya — tribute lekar gaddi lautaayi.

Yaad karne ki trick

Gupta family tree: 'CS-C'

Chandragupta I → Samudragupta (veena, Prayaga Prashasti) → Chandragupta II Vikramaditya (Iron Pillar) → beti Prabhavati.

Vigyaan ki jodi = 'AK-VU'

Aryabhata–Kusumapura; Varahamihira–Ujjayini.

Kalidasa = 'Megh ka Dakiya'

Meghadutam = cloud messenger; Raghuvansham = quote wala granth.

Kanchi = 1000

Kanchipuram — hazaar mandiron ka shahar, Pallava rajdhani.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Mehrauli ka Iron Pillar kis Gupta raja ke samay ka hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Chandragupta II — Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya); Vishnu ko samarpit; 1,600+ saal se jang nahi.

  2. Q2. Prayaga Prashasti kisne likhi aur kis raja ki stuti hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Harisena, Samudragupta — Darbari kavi Harisena — Samudragupta ki vijayon ka varnan.

  3. Q3. Aryabhata kis jagah se jude the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kusumapura — Kusumapura (Patna ke paas), ~500 CE; Aryabhatiya.

  4. Q4. Faxian (Fa-Hien) ne India ki yatra kab ki?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Early 5th century CE — Early 5th century CE — Buddhist sthal aur granth ke liye.

  5. Q5. Prabhavati Gupta ka vivaah kis vansh mein hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Vakataka — Vakataka — Guptas ke dakshini padosi; pati ki maut ke baad regent bani.

  6. Q6. 'City of a thousand temples' kise kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kanchipuram — Kanchipuram — Pallava rajdhani.

  7. Q7. Gupta samrajya ke patan ka ek bada bahari kaaran?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Huna hamle — Central Asia ke Huna — 6th century CE.
