Theme C · Our Cultural Heritage and Knowledge Traditions

How the Land Becomes Sacred

Teerth, teerth-yatra aur pavitra bhugol — kaise poora Bharat ek pavitra bhoomi bana.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Zameen 'pavitra' kaise banti hai?

Kuch jagahein, raaste, nadiyaan, pahad ya ped hamare liye bahut gehra dharmik-aadhyaatmik mahatva rakhte hain — ise sacredness (pavitrata) kehte hain. India mein har dharm ke pavitra sthal: Sanchi stupa, Mahabodhi (Bodh Gaya) — saal mein 40 lakh+ yaatri; Sikh takht — Patna Sahib, Akal Takht (Amritsar), Keshgarh Sahib (Anandpur); Ajmer Dargah, Velankanni Church (TN) — jahan doosre dharm ke log bhi jaate hain. Guru Nanak ne Haridwar, Prayag, Mathura, Varanasi, Ayodhya, Puri ki yaatraayein kin.

Tirtha — 'paar utarne' ki jagah

Tirtha = asal mein wo jagah jahan nadi paar ki ja sake; prateek roop mein — saadhaaran jeevan se aadhyaatmik jeevan mein 'paar' jaana. Tirthayatra ~3,000 saal purani parampara — baahari yaatra bhi aur bheetari bhi. Lucknow ke gaon se log 3 mahine paidal chal kar Rameswaram se Haridwar tak jaate the! Jain Tirthankara ('tirtha banaane wale') ke sthal: Mount Abu, Girnar, Shatrunjaya. Sabarimala (Kerala, Ayyappa) — saal mein 1 crore+; kathin pahaadi raasta = bheetari raaste ki kathinai. Pandharpur Wari — 800 saal purani, 21 din paidal, Vithoba mandir.

Poora desh ek 'pavitra jaal'

Char Dham chaaron dishaon mein; 12 Jyotirlinga; 51 Shakti Peetha (Bangladesh-Pakistan tak) — kahaani: devi Sati ke shareer ke ang jahan-jahan gire, wahan peeth bane → poori dharti devi maa ka shareer! Yaatra mein log alag bhasha, khaana, kapde dekhte, par saajhapan bhi mehsoos karte; saath mein vyapaar, shastrarth, shiksha → saanskritik ekta. Nehru (1961): Badrinath-Kedarnath se Kanyakumari tak ki yaatraon ne 'ek desh, ek sanskriti' ki bhaavna di. Teerth aur vyapaar ke raaste aksar ek hi the — Uttarapatha, Dakshinapatha.

Nadiyaan aur Kumbh

Rig Veda ka Nadistuti Sukta — 19 nadiyon ki stuti. Mantra 'gange cha yamune chaiva…' mein 7 nadiyaan: Ganga, Yamuna, Godavari, Sarasvati, Narmada, Sindhu, Kaveri. Kumbh Mela — Prayagraj mein Ganga + Yamuna + adrishya Sarasvati ka sangam; UNESCO amoort virasat; 2025 mein ~66 crore log! Katha: samudra manthan ka amrit-kumbh, boondein 4 jagah gireen — Haridwar, Prayagraj, Nashik, Ujjain.

Pahad, ped aur pavitra jungle

Pahad = dharti se swarg ka dwaar: Kailash, Vaishno Devi, Tiruvannamalai, Tirumala. Peepal — Hindu, Buddh, Sikh, Jain sabke liye pavitra; Mohenjo-daro ki mohar par bhi peepal ke patte; Bodh Gaya ka Bodhi vriksh. Sacred groves (devvan) — devta ke jungle jahan shikaar-kataai mana → jaiv-vividhta aur paani bachta hai. Naam: kavu (Malayalam), devarai (Marathi), oran (Rajasthan), sarna (Jharkhand), dev van (Himachal), Mawphlong (Meghalaya)… Thanjavur ke grove mein devta fruit bats ki raksha karte — jo parag aur beej failaate hain! Niyam Dongar (Odisha) — Dongria Khond ke devta Niyam Raja; ped kaatna mana. Bhudevi (dharti maa).

Duniya bhar mein aur aaj ki zimmedari

Greece, Native Americans, aur New Zealand ke Maori — Taranaki Maunga pahad ko kaanoon ne insaan jaise adhikaar diye! Par aaj hamari pavitra nadiyaan — Yamuna, Mahanadi, Kaveri — pradooshit hain. Samvidhan bhi virasat bachaane ka kartavya batata hai. David Suzuki: 'Hum duniya ko jaise dekhte hain, waise hi uske saath vyavhaar karte hain' — agar nadi ko maa maante ho, to use ganda mat karo!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Sacredness = gehri dharmik/aadhyatmik mahatta wali cheez — sthaan, yatra, raasta ya poori zameen.
  • Bahar se aaye dharmon ke pavitra sthal bhi: Dargah Sharif, Ajmer; Velankanni Church (TN) — doosre dharm ke log bhi jaate.
  • Buddhist: Sanchi Great Stupa (relic stupa), Mahabodhi, Bodh Gaya — saal mein 40 lakh+ yaatri.
  • Sikh takht (adhyatmik adhikaar ke kendra): Takht Sri Patna Sahib, Akal Takht (Golden Temple, Amritsar), Takht Sri Keshgarh Sahib (Anandpur). Guru Nanak ne Haridwar, Prayag, Mathura, Varanasi, Ayodhya, Puri ki yatraayein kin.
  • Nehru (1961): Badrinath-Kedarnath-Amarnath se Kanyakumari tak yatra — 'ek desh, ek sanskriti ki bhaavna'.
  • Tīrtha = jahan nadi paar ki ja sake; prateek roop mein — saamaanya jeevan se aadhyatmik jeevan mein 'paar'. Tīrthayātrā — kam se kam 3,000 saal purani parampara; baahari aur aantarik dono yatra.
  • Itihaaskar Dharampal ka kissa: Lucknow ke gaanvon se yaatri — 3 mahine, Rameswaram se Haridwar.
  • Jain: Tirthankara ('teerth banaane wala') se jude sthal — Mount Abu, Girnar, Shatrunjaya (Saurashtra, Gujarat).
  • Sabarimala (Kerala, Ayyappa) — saal mein 1 crore+ bhakt; kathin pahaadi raasta = aantarik maarg ki kathinai ka prateek. Pandharpur Wari (Maharashtra) — 800 saal purani, 21 din paidal, Vithoba mandir.
  • Prakriti pavitra: Bhudevi (Dharti Maa; Varaha avatar, Belur). Niyam Dongar (Niyamgiri, Odisha) — Dongria Khond ke devta Niyam Raja; ped kaatna mana. Sikkim sarkar (2000s) ne pavitra pahaad, gufa, jheel, garam srot suraksha mein liye. Toda (Nilgiris) — chotiyan, Shola jungle.
  • Pavitra network: Char Dham (chaaron dishaon mein), 12 Jyotirlinga (Shiva), 51 Shakti Peetha (Bangladesh-Pakistan tak). Kahani: Sati ke deh ke ang jahan gire → poori dharti devi maa ka sharir.
  • Yatra se: alag bhasha, khaana, kapde dekhe; samaanta bhi; vyapaar, shastrarth, shiksha → sanskritik ekikaran. Pavitra prakritik kshetra = puṇyakṣhetra.
  • Nadiyan: Rig Veda ka Nadistuti Sukta — 19 nadiyon ki stuti. Mantra 'gange cha yamune chaiva...' — 7 nadiyan: Ganga, Yamuna, Godavari, Sarasvati, Narmada, Sindhu, Kaveri.
  • Kumbh Mela — Prayagraj (Ganga + Yamuna + adrishya Sarasvati ka sangam); UNESCO intangible heritage; 2025 mein ~66 crore (660 million) log. Katha: amrit manthan, Mohini ne kumbh liya, boondein 4 jagah gireen — Haridwar, Prayagraj, Nashik, Ujjain.
  • Pahaad = dharti se swarg ka dwaar: Mount Kailash, Vaishno Devi (Katra), Tiruvannamalai, Tirumala (Balaji).
  • Peepal (ashvattha, Ficus religiosa) — Hindu, Buddhist, Sikh, Jain sab ke liye pavitra; Mohenjo-daro ki seal par bhi patte; Bodh Gaya ka Bodhi vriksh.
  • Sacred groves (devtaon ke jungle, shikaar/katai mana) → jaiv-vividhata aur jal sanrakshan. Naam: kāvu (Malayalam), kovilkādu (Tamil), devare kādu (Kannada), devarāī (Marathi), khlaw kyntang (Khasi), dev van (Himachal), sarnā (Jharkhand), devgudi (Chhattisgarh), oraṇ (Rajasthan). Meghalaya: Ryngkew/Basa; Mawphlong.
  • Thanjavur ke grove — devta fruit bats ki raksha karte; bats parag aur beej phailaate.
  • Teerth aur vyapaar ke raaste aksar ek: Uttarapatha, Dakshinapatha (Kaushambi → Ujjayini → Pratishthana).
  • Duniya mein bhi: Greece, Native Americans; Maori (New Zealand) — Taranaki Maunga pahaad ko kanoon ne insaan jaise adhikaar diye.
  • Aaj: Yamuna, Mahanadi, Kaveri pradooshit — Samvidhan bhi virasat bachane ka kartavya batata hai. David Suzuki: 'Hum duniya ko jaise dekhte hain, waise hi vyavhaar karte hain.'

Yaad karne ki trick

Network numbers: 4-12-51

4 Dham, 12 Jyotirlinga, 51 Shakti Peetha — '4 dishaayein, 12 mahine, 51 akshar'.

Kumbh ke 4 = 'HaPra-NaU'

Haridwar, Prayagraj, Nashik, Ujjain.

Grove ke naam

Kerala = Kaavu, Maharashtra = Devrai, Jharkhand = Sarna, Rajasthan = Oran — 'Kavu Devrai Sarna Oran'.

Takht = 'PAK'

Patna Sahib, Akal Takht, Keshgarh Sahib.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 'Tīrtha' shabd ka shaabdik arth?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Jahan nadi/jal paar kiya ja sake — Shaabdik: paar utarne ki jagah; prateek: saansaarik se aadhyatmik jeevan mein paar.

  2. Q2. Kumbh Mela kin 4 jagahon par hota hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Haridwar, Prayagraj, Nashik, Ujjain — Amrit manthan katha ke anusaar boondein in 4 jagahon par gireen.

  3. Q3. Niyam Dongar hill kis janjaati ke liye pavitra hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Dongria Khond — Odisha ki Niyamgiri range — Niyam Raja ka ghar.

  4. Q4. Pandharpur Wari mein yaatri kitne din paidal chalte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 21 — 21 din — Vithoba mandir, ~800 saal purani parampara.

  5. Q5. Rajasthan mein sacred grove ko kya kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Oran — Oran — Rajasthan; Kaavu — Kerala; Devrai — Maharashtra; Sarna — Jharkhand.

  6. Q6. Peepal ka vaigyanik naam?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ficus religiosa — Ficus religiosa = 'pavitra' fig; banyan = Ficus benghalensis.

  7. Q7. Rig Veda ka Nadistuti Sukta kitni nadiyon ki stuti karta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 19 — 19 nadiyan (pracheen NW India). Roz ke mantra mein 7 nadiyan aati hain.
