Theme D · Governance and Democracy

From the Rulers to the Ruled: Types of Governments

Sarkar ke kaam, democracy ke prakaar (direct, parliamentary, presidential) aur monarchy, theocracy, dictatorship, oligarchy.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Sarkar kyon chahiye? — Kautilya ke 3 kaam

Kautilya ke anusaar raja ke 3 kartavya: raksha (baahari hamle se bachaav), paalana (kaanoon-vyavastha), yogakshema (janta ka kalyaan). Aaj ki sarkar bhi yahi karti hai — shaanti, suraksha, videsh sambandh, shiksha, swasthya, sadkein, arthvyavastha. Sarkar ke 3 kaam: legislative (niyam banana), executive (lagu karna), judicial (paalan sunishchit karna).

School committee ki misaal

School Student Committee kaise banaao? (1) Sab bachche sadasya — itni bheed mein kaam hi ruk jaaye! (2) Head Teacher chun dein — par sabki awaaz nahi pahunchegi. (3) Vote se pratinidhi chuno — sabse achchha, kyonki sabki baat bhi aur kaam bhi. Yahi hai democracy! Abraham Lincoln: loktantra = 'janta ki, janta dwaara, janta ke liye sarkar'.

Sarkaron mein farak kaise pehchaanein?

4 sawaal poochho: (1) Adhikaar kahan se — janta se ya dharm/vansh se? (2) Kaise banti — chunaav se ya khaandaan se? (3) Hisse aur kaam — samvidhan tay karta hai ya raja? (4) Lakshya — sabki samriddhi ya kuch parivaaron ki? Loktantra ke mool siddhant: barabari, azaadi, pratinidhi bhaagidaari, sabko vote, Fundamental Rights, swatantra nyaypaalika. India ne 1950 se hi sabhi vayaskon ko vote diya — jabki Switzerland mein mahilaaon ko 1971, UK mein poora vote 1928, USA mein 1965 tak!

Direct, representative; parliamentary, presidential

Direct democracy — sab naagrik khud faisla karein (Switzerland ke kuch hisson mein); bade desh mein mushkil. Representative — hum pratinidhi chunte hain; chunaav India mein har 5 saal, USA mein har 4 saal. Parliamentary (India, Australia) — PM aur mantri legislature ka hi hissa; Lok Sabha ka vishwas zaroori. Presidential (USA, South Korea) — President ko janta seedha chunti; legislature ke vishwas ki zaroorat nahi. Legislature: India — Parliament (Lok Sabha zyada taaqatwar + Rajya Sabha); USA — Congress (Senate + House barabar); South Korea — ek sadan.

Hamare purane ganrajya aur Uttaramerur

Vajji (Lichchhavi) — yogyata aur chunaav se neta → shuruaati republic (jahan rashtra pramukh chuna jaata hai, khaandaani nahi). Uttaramerur shilalekh (Chola, 10th sadi, TN) — gaon sabha ka chunaav: seal kiya ballot box, ummeedwar ki yogyata, kartavya, aur bhrashtachar par turant barkhaast! Greece-Rome ke ganrajyon mein mahila, mazdoor, gulam vote nahi de sakte the.

Raja bhi dharma se bandha

Bharat mein raja rajadharma se bandha tha — use poori manmaani ka haq nahi. Mahabharata ke Shanti Parva mein Bhishma ne Yudhishthira ko rajadharma sikhaaya. Rajatarangini (Kalhana, 12th sadi): raja Chandrapida ko mandir ke liye ek mochi ki jhopdi chahiye thi; raja ne izzat se anumati maangi aur muaavza diya!

Aaj ki duniya mein aur kaunsi sarkarein?

Absolute monarchy — raja ke paas sab shakti (Saudi Arabia). Constitutional monarchy — raja naam ka, asli shakti PM-Parliament ki (UK, King Charles III). Theocracy — dharm ke niyam aur dharmguru (Iran, Afghanistan, Vatican City). Dictatorship — ek vyakti/samooh ki poori shakti: Hitler (Holocaust mein 60 lakh Yahudi), Idi Amin (Uganda — Bharatiyon ko bhaagna pada); North Korea mein baal, kapde, internet tak sarkar tay kare. Oligarchy — kuch ameer-taaqatwar logon ka raaj (oligos = kuch). Loktantra ki chunautiyaan: bhrashtachar, ameeri-gareebi ka antar, jhoothi soochna. Duniya ke aadhe se zyada desh loktantra hain.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Kautilya: raja ke 3 kartavya — rakṣha (baahari hamle se bachaav), pālana (law & order), yogakṣhema (janta ka kalyaan).
  • Sarkar ke kaam: law & order, shaanti-suraksha, videsh sambandh, raksha, shiksha-swasthya-infrastructure, arthvyavastha, kalyaan.
  • School Student Committee udaharan: sab member (kaam ruk jaaye), Head Teacher chune (sab ki awaaz nahi), ya vote se representative (democratic) — teesra sabse achha.
  • Sarkar ke 3 functions: legislative (niyam banana), executive (lagu karna), judicial (paalan sunishchit karna).
  • Abraham Lincoln: democracy = 'government of the people, by the people, for the people'.
  • Sarkaron mein 4 farak: (1) adhikaar kahan se (janta / dharm), (2) kaise banti (chunaav / vansh), (3) hisse aur unke kaam (constitution / raja), (4) lakshya kya (sab ki samriddhi / kuch parivaar).
  • Democracy ke mool siddhant: Equality, Freedom, Representative participation, Universal adult franchise, Fundamental rights, Independent judiciary.
  • India ne 1950 se hi sab vayaskon ko vote diya; Switzerland mein mahilaon ko vote 1971 mein. UK mein effective universal suffrage 1928, USA 1965 tak.
  • Democracy sthaapna: USA 1787, Switzerland 1848, India 1947, Germany 1949, Kenya 1964, Nepal 2008.
  • Direct democracy — sab nagrik seedhe faisle; aaj kuch roop Switzerland mein; bade desh mein mushkil.
  • Representative democracy — chune pratinidhi; sarkar janta ke prati accountable. India mein chunaav har 5 saal, USA mein har 4 saal.
  • Parliamentary (India, Australia): executive (PM + council of ministers) legislature ka hissa; Lok Sabha ka vishwas zaroori; janta legislature chunti, executive nahi.
  • Presidential (USA, South Korea): president seedha janta chunti; legislature ke vishwas ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Legislature: India = Parliament, USA = Congress. India: Lower = Lok Sabha (zyada shaktishaali), Upper = Rajya Sabha. USA: Senate + House of Representatives (barabar). South Korea: ek sadan (National Assembly).
  • Separation of powers = teeno ang swatantra, ek-doosre ke kaam mein dakhal nahi.
  • Itihaas: Vajji — Lichchhavi clan; yogyata se neta, chunaav → early republic. Republic = head of state chuna hua, vanshaanugat nahi.
  • Uttaramerur inscriptions (Chola, 10th century CE, TN, Vaikuntha Perumal mandir) — gaon sabha ka chunaav: sealed ballot box, yogyata, kartavya, bhrashtachar par turant barkhaast.
  • Rome-Greece (5th–4th c. BCE) republics — par mahila, mazdoor, gulaam vote nahi.
  • Monarchy: India mein raja rajadharma se bandha — poorn shakti nahi. Mahabharata Shanti Parva — Bhishma ne Yudhishthira ko rajdharma sikhaaya. Rajatarangini (Kalhana, 12th c.) — raja Chandrapida aur mochi ki jhopdi: raja ne izzat se anumati maangi, muavza diya.
  • Aaj: Absolute monarchy — Saudi Arabia (raja ke paas sab shakti). Constitutional monarchy — UK (King Charles III nominal; asli shakti PM aur Parliament).
  • Theocracy — dharm ke niyam aur dharmguru: Iran (Supreme Leader jeevan bhar, clerics chunte; chuna president aur sansad bhi), Afghanistan, Vatican City.
  • Dictatorship — ek vyakti/samooh ki poorn shakti: Hitler (Chancellor 1933; 60 lakh Yahudi — Holocaust; WWII 1939–45), Idi Amin (Uganda; Indians ko bhaagna pada). Roma log mool roop se India se. North Korea mein 'Shane' ki kahani — baal, kapde, internet sab sarkar tay kare.
  • Oligarchy — Greek oligos (kuch) + arkho (raaj): chhota ameer/prabhaavshaali samooh faisle kare; democracy bhi oligarchy jaisi ho sakti hai.
  • Democracy ki chunautiyan: bhrashtachar, ameeri-gareebi ka antar, kuch logon ka niyantran, nyaypaalika ki swatantrata ghatna, soochna mein hera-pheri. Duniya ke aadhe se zyada desh democracy.

Yaad karne ki trick

Kautilya ke 3: 'Ra-Pa-Yo'

Raksha (bahar se), Palana (andar law & order), Yogakshema (kalyaan).

Parliamentary vs Presidential

Parliamentary = PM sansad ke andar (India). Presidential = President alag, seedha chuna (USA).

Sarkar ke 4 roop + misaal

Absolute monarchy = Saudi, Constitutional = UK, Theocracy = Iran/Vatican, Dictatorship = Hitler/Idi Amin.

Oligarchy = 'Oli = Only few'

Oligos = kuch, arkho = raaj.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. India kis prakaar ka loktantra hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Parliamentary democracy — PM aur council of ministers sansad ke sadasya hain aur Lok Sabha ke prati jawabdeh.

  2. Q2. Kis desh mein mahilaon ko vote ka adhikaar 1971 mein mila?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Switzerland — Switzerland — 1971. India ne 1950 se sab vayaskon ko vote diya.

  3. Q3. Uttaramerur inscriptions kis vansh aur sadi ke hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Chola, 10th century CE — Chola, 10th century CE — gaon sabha ka chunaav, sealed ballot box.

  4. Q4. UK kis prakaar ki sarkar ka udaharan hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Constitutional monarchy — Raja nominal; asli shakti chuni sansad aur PM ke paas.

  5. Q5. 'Oligarchy' ka arth?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kuch logon ka raaj — Greek oligos (kuch) + arkho (raaj).

  6. Q6. Kautilya ke anusaar 'yogakshema' ka arth?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Janta ka kalyaan — Raksha = bahar se, Palana = andar law & order, Yogakshema = kalyaan.

  7. Q7. Rajatarangini kisne likhi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kalhana — Kashmiri vidwaan Kalhana, 12th century — raja Chandrapida aur mochi ki kahani.
