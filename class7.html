Geographical Diversity of India

Himalaya se islands tak — Bharat ke paanch bade bhaugolik kshetra aur unka jeevan par asar.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

'Saare jahan se achchha' — upar se kaisa dikhta hai India?

1984 mein Rakesh Sharma — antariksh jaane wale pehle Bharatiya — se PM Indira Gandhi ne poochha: upar se India kaisa dikhta hai? Jawab: 'Saare jahan se achchha!' India duniya ka 7th sabse bada desh hai. Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar ke saath milkar ye Indian Subcontinent (upmahadweep) banata hai. Isme 5 bade kshetra: pahad, maidaan, registan, dakshini pathaar aur dweep. Prakritik deewaarein: uttar mein Himalaya, pashchim mein Thar aur Arabian Sea, dakshin mein Indian Ocean, poorv mein Bay of Bengal.

Himalaya kaise bana? — carpet wali misaal

Laakhon saal pehle India Gondwana naam ke bade mahadweep ka hissa tha — padosi tha Africa! Dheere-dheere khisakta hua India ~5 crore saal pehle Asia (Eurasia) se takraaya. Socho farsh par ek carpet ko dono taraf se dhakelo — beech mein silvatein uth aayengi. Waise hi zameen sikud kar Himalaya bana! Aaj bhi India ~5 cm har saal Asia mein ghus raha hai aur Himalaya ~5 mm har saal ooncha ho raha hai. 'Himalaya' = hima (barf) + alaya (ghar) = barf ka ghar.

Himalaya — Asia ki paani ki tanki

Himalaya ~2,500 km lamba, 6 deshon mein (India, Nepal, Bhutan, China, Pakistan, Afghanistan); kai chotiyan 8,000 m se oonchi — 'Eight Thousanders'. Iski barf pighal kar Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra ko paani deti hai — isliye 'Water Tower of Asia'. Ganga ki mukhya dhaara Bhagirathi, Gaumukh (Gangotri glacier) se nikalti hai. Teen shrinkhla: Himadri (sabse oonchi — Everest, Kanchenjunga, saal bhar barf), Himachal (beech wali — Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling), Shivalik (sabse neechi — ghane jungle). Pashchimi Himalaya ke kath-kuni ghar (pathar + lakdi) garam rehte aur halke bhookamp sah lete. Ladakh — thanda registan, sardi mein −30°C; Pangong Tso khaari jheel; yak — doodh, oon, sawaari sab!

Uttar ke maidaan — mitti ka uphaar

Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra pahadon se mitti bahaa kar laati hain aur maidaan mein bichha deti hain — isliye ye zameen bahut upjaau, aabadi bahut zyada, aur samtal hone se sadak-rail bichhaana aasaan. Brahmaputra = 'Brahma ka putra' — kuch nadiyon mein ek jinhe purush naam diya gaya; garmi mein barf pighalne se badh jaati hai. Jaanwar: langur, baagh (Project Tiger), lambe munh wala gharial (vilupti ke kagaar par), aur mor (rashtriya pakshi).

Thar registan aur boodhi Aravalli

Thar — Rajasthan, Gujarat, Punjab, Haryana; ret ke tile 150 m tak oonche! Jaisalmer — 'Golden City'; Pushkar mela; paani bachaane ke liye taanka/kund (zameen mein bani tanki). Aravalli — duniya ke sabse purane pahadon mein (~2.5 arab saal); ghis kar neeche ho gaye; sabse oonchi choti Mount Abu; ye Thar ko poorv mein badhne se rokti hai. Yahan ki Zawar khaanon mein 800+ saal pehle Bharatiyon ne duniya mein sabse pehle zinc nikaalna seekha!

Dakshin ka pathaar aur do Ghats

Peninsular Plateau — teen taraf samundar. Pashchim mein Western Ghats (oonche, deewaar jaise, UNESCO; uttari hissa Sahyadri), poorv mein Eastern Ghats (neeche, toote-phoote). Beech mein Deccan Plateau, poorv ki taraf jhuka — isliye zyaadatar nadiyaan poorv behti hain: Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi → Bay of Bengal. Sirf Narmada aur Tapti pashchim → Arabian Sea. Jungle mein Santhal, Gond, Baiga, Bhil, Korku janjaatiyaan.

Samundar kinaare aur dweep

Tat-rekha 7,500 km se zyada. Pashchimi tat — patla, tez nadiyaan, estuary (Narmada, Tapti). Poorvi tat — chauda maidaan, delta (Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri); Chilika aur Pulicat lagoon. Lakshadweep — 36 coral dweep. Andaman & Nicobar — 500+ jwalamukhi se bane dweep; Cellular Jail; Barren Island — India ka ekmaatra sakriya jwalamukhi. Sundarbans — Ganga-Brahmaputra delta, mangrove jungle, Royal Bengal Tiger. Poorvottar: Garo-Khasi-Jaintia pahadiyan; Mawlynnong — 'Asia ka sabse saaf gaon'; pedon ki jadon se bane living root bridges!

Understanding the Weather

Mausam ke 5 tatva, unhe naapne ke yantra, weather station aur forecast.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Mausam (weather) kya hai?

Aaj dhoop hai, kal barish — ye hai weather (mausam): kisi jagah aur samay par vaayumandal (atmosphere) ki haalat. Atmosphere = dharti ke chaaron or gaison ki chaadar ('hawa'). Sabse neeche ki parat troposphere — yahin hum rehte hain aur lagbhag saara mausam banta hai; ye 6–18 km oonchi (dhruvon par patli, equator ke paas moti). Upar stratosphere — jisme ozone parat hamein suraj ki haanikaarak kirnon se bachaati hai.

Mausam ke 5 tatva

(1) Temperature (garmi-thand), (2) Precipitation (barish, barf, ole, sleet), (3) Atmospheric pressure (hawa ka dabaav), (4) Wind (hawa ki gati aur disha), (5) Humidity (hawa mein bhaap/nami). Mausam ka vaigyanik adhyayan = meteorology. Purane log bhi sanket padhte the: cheentiyan ande oonchi jagah le jaayein, mendhak tarraayein, pine cone band ho jaaye → barish aane wali!

Garmi naapo — thermometer

Thermometer se temperature; ikaai Celsius ya Fahrenheit (15°C = 59°F). Din ka range = sabse zyada − sabse kam. Ausat (mean) din ka taapmaan = (max + min) ÷ 2. Misaal: max 30°C, min 20°C → mean 25°C, range 10°C.

Barish naapo — rain gauge

Rain gauge = keep (funnel) + cylinder + scale. Agar cylinder mein 5 mm paani jama hua to 5 mm barish. Tum ghar par bhi botal aur keep se bana sakte ho! India Meteorological Department (IMD) — 1875 se; motto 'adityat jayate vrishti' — 'Surya se varsha hoti hai'.

Hawa ka dabaav — barometer

Atmospheric pressure = hamare upar hawa ke stambh ka bojh. Samundar ke kinaare zyada, pahad par kam — isliye pahad par oxygen kam aur saans phoolti hai. Barometer se naapte hain, ikaai millibar (mb). Samundar tal par ~1013 mb; 1000 mb se kam = 'depression' / low pressure → toofan ya cyclone ka sanket. Khardung La (Ladakh, 5,600 m+) par sirf ~650 mb!

Hawa ki gati, disha aur nami

Wind hamesha zyada dabaav se kam dabaav ki taraf chalti hai — jaise gubbaare se hawa bahar nikalti hai. Disha — wind vane (airport par kapde ka wind sock); gati — anemometer (3–4 katoriyan ghoomti hain), km/h mein. Humidity — relative humidity %: sookha 20–40%, nami wala 60–80%. Zyada nami mein kapde der se sookhte, paseena zyada aata hai. Yantra: hygrometer. Garmi mein uttar India ki garam, dhool bhari hawa = Loo.

Weather station aur chetaavni ke rang

Saare yantra ek jagah = weather station. Automated Weather Station (AWS) — sensor khud record karte hain; 2023 mein NDMA ne Sikkim ki glacier jheel par 4,800 m+ par AWS lagaya. IMD ki chetaavni: Watch (jaankaari rakho), Alert (taiyaar raho), Warning (kadam uthao). Forecast se machhuaare samundar mein nahi jaate aur cyclone se pehle tat khaali karaaye jaate hain — jaanein bachti hain!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Weather = kisi jagah aur samay par vayumandal ki sthiti. Atmosphere = gas ki parat ('air').
  • Sabse neeche ki parat troposphere — yahin jeevan aur lagbhag saara mausam. Oonchai 6–18 km: poles par patli, tropics mein moti. Upar stratosphere (ozone layer).
  • Mausam ke 5 tatva: Temperature, Precipitation (baarish, barf, sleet, ole), Atmospheric pressure, Wind (gati + disha), Humidity (hawa mein bhaap).
  • Paramparik sanket: cheentiyan ande oonchi jagah le jaayein, mendhak tarraayein, pine cones band (nami) / khule (sookha) → baarish ka andaaza. Meteorology = mausam ka vaigyanik adhyayan.
  • Thermometer — temperature. Celsius/Fahrenheit: 15°C = 59°F. Range = max − min. Mean daily temp = (max + min) ÷ 2.
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) — 1875. Motto: 'ādityāt jāyate vṛiṣhṭi' — 'Surya se varsha hoti hai' (Manusmriti).
  • Rain gauge — funnel + cylinder + scale; 5 mm paani = 5 mm baarish.
  • Atmospheric pressure = upar ki hawa ka bhaar. Tat par zyada, pahaad par kam (kam oxygen → saans phoolna). Achanak giravat = 'depression' / low-pressure system → toofan/cyclone.
  • Barometer — pressure, unit millibar (mb). Tat par normal ~1013 mb; 1000 mb se kam = depression. Khardung La (Ladakh, 5,600 m+) par ~650 mb.
  • Wind = high pressure se low pressure ki taraf hawa. Wind vane — disha (airport par 'wind sock'). Anemometer — 3–4 cups, gati km/h mein.
  • Humidity → relative humidity (%): sookha 20–40%, nami wala 60–80%. Zyada nami mein paani dheere bhaap banta hai — kapde der se sookhte, paseena zyada. Yantra: Hygrometer. Food processing aur museums mein zaroori.
  • Weather station — saare yantra ek jagah. Automated Weather Station (AWS) — sensors se khud record. 2023: NDMA ne Sikkim ki glacial lake par 4,800 m+ par AWS lagaya.
  • IMD warning rang: Watch (Be Updated), Alert (Be Prepared), Warning (Take Action). Forecast se machhuaaron ko chetavani, cyclone mein tat khaali karwana.
  • Loo = uttar India ki garmi ki tez, garam, dhool bhari hawa.

Climates of India

Weather vs climate, India ke climate prakaar, unke kaaran, monsoon, aapdaayein aur climate change.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Weather, season, climate — teen alag cheezein

Weather = aaj ka mausam (roz badle). Season (ritu) = har saal laut kar aane wala samay (garmi, barsaat, sardi). Climate (jalvaayu) = kisi jagah ka lambe samay (kam se kam 30 saal) ka ausat pattern. Socho: tumhara aaj ka mood = weather, tumhara swabhaav = climate! Bharat ki paramparik 6 ritu: vasanta, grishma, varsha, sharad, hemanta, shishir — tyohaar bhi: Vasanta Panchami, Sharad Purnima.

India mein kitne tarah ki jalvaayu?

Himalaya — alpine (bahut thandi); neeche ke pahad — temperate (hill stations); uttari maidaan — subtropical (gehu); Thar — arid (sookha); pashchimi tat — tropical wet (chaawal, masaale); Deccan — semi-arid; poorv-dakshin — tropical. Ek desh, kai mausam!

Jalvaayu kaun tay karta hai? — 5 kaaran

(1) Latitude — equator par suraj ki kirne seedhi (garmi kendrit), dhruvon par tirchhi (garmi phaili) → Kanniyakumari garam, Srinagar thanda. (2) Altitude (oonchaai) — oonchaai badhe to hawa patli aur thandi: Ooty (10–25°C) vs Coimbatore (25–38°C) — latitude lagbhag same! (3) Samundar se nazdeeki — samundar taapmaan ko santulit rakhta hai: Mumbai (32°/18°, range ~14°) vs Nagpur (44°/10°, range 34°). (4) Hawa — garmi mein Arabia-Afghanistan se garam hawa → Punjab-Rajasthan mein heat wave; sardi mein Himalaya paar ki thandi hawa → cold wave. (5) Topography — Himalaya-Karakoram Central Asia ki thandi hawa rokte hain. Chhote ilaake ki apni jalvaayu = microclimate; concrete wala shehar aas-paas se garam = urban heat island.

Monsoon — hawa ka ulta-pulta khel

Monsoon Arabi shabd 'mausim' (mausam) se. Garmi mein zameen samundar se jaldi garam hoti hai → zameen par kam dabaav → samundar se nami wali hawa zameen ki taraf → barish! Sardi mein ulta. South-west monsoon: June ki shuruaat mein Kerala se, mid-July tak poore desh mein; Western Ghats ke pashchimi dhalaan par bahut barish, uske peeche Deccan par kam (rain shadow). North-east monsoon (sardi): Bay of Bengal se nami lekar Tamil Nadu jaise ilaakon mein barish. Mawsynram (Meghalaya) — duniya mein sabse zyada ausat barish, ~11,000 mm (11 metre!). Raag Megh Malhar barish ka raag hai.

Prakriti ke sanket aur monsoon ki 'naakaami'

Konkan ke machhuaare machhli ko satah par dekh kar barish bhaanp lete; Golden Shower (amaltas) ke khilne ke ~50 din baad monsoon; kauve ka ghonsla ooncha → kam barish. Agar monsoon fail ho jaaye: fasal barbaad, paani ki kami, log palaayan karte, aur cheezon ke daam badhte (mehngaai/inflation).

Aapdaayein — cyclone, baadh, landslide, aag

Climate change — dharti ko bukhaar

Ilaaj (mitigation): pradooshan kam, ped lagaao, solar-wind jaisi renewable urja.

Shehar gaye, phir lautey — 'Second Urbanisation'

Janapada se Mahajanapada

Janapada = 'jahan jana (log) ne pada (pair) rakha' — ek kul ka ilaaka, mukhiya raja. 8th–7th sadi BCE tak janapada milkar bade Mahajanapada bane — sabse prachalit soochi mein 16: uttar-pashchim mein Gandhara, poorv mein Anga, Godavari ke paas Ashmaka… Sabse taaqatwar: Magadha (raajdhani Rajagriha — aaj Rajgir, Bihar), Kosala (Shravasti), Vatsa (Kaushambi), Avanti (Ujjayini). Ganga ke maidaan hi kyon?

Upjaau zameen (khoob anaaj), aas-paas pahadon mein loha (iron ore), aur naye vyapaari raaste. Raajdhaniyon ke chaaron or mitti/pathar ki deewaar (rampart), bahar paani bhari khaai (moat), aur patle darwaaze taaki aana-jaana niyantrit rahe.

Raja bhi, ganrajya bhi

Lohe ka zamaana aur pehle sikke

Harappa = taamba/kaansa; Second Urbanisation = loha. Lohe ke auzaaron se badi kheti aur tez-halke hathiyaar. India ke pehle sikke — chaandi ke punch-marked coins (thappa maar kar chinh); baad mein taamba, sona; har mahajanapada ke apne sikke.

Jaati aur varna — samaaj ki banawat

Samrajya (empire) kya hota hai?

Empire Latin 'imperium' (sarvochch shakti) se. Jab ek shaktishaali raja (samraat) kai chhote rajyon par raaj kare, aur chhote raja use tribute (nazraana/kar) dein, to wo samrajya hai. Sanskrit: samraj, adhiraja, rajadhiraja. Samrajya ko chahiye: sena, prashasan, sadak-nadi-samudri jaal, ek jaise kaanoon-sikke-naap tol, kala-gyaan ko badhaava. Vyapaari-kaarigaron ke guilds (shreni) apne niyam khud banaate — raja dakhal nahi deta tha.

Magadha — taaqat ka khazaana

Magadha (dakshini Bihar) 6th–4th sadi BCE mein sabse taaqatwar bana kyonki: upjaau Ganga maidaan, jungle (lakdi, haathi — sena ke tank!), paas mein loha, aur Ganga-Son nadiyaan. Raja Ajatashatru — Buddha aur Mahavira ke samay ke. Phir Mahapadma Nanda — Nanda vansh, badi sena; aakhri raja Dhana Nanda — ameer par janta ko sataane wala. Isi daur mein Panini ki Ashtadhyayi — Sanskrit vyakaran ke 3,996 sutra!

Alexander aur Porus

Macedonia ka Alexander Persian samrajya jeet kar 327–325 BCE mein India aaya aur Punjab mein raja Porus (Paurava) ko haraaya. Jab poochha 'tumhare saath kaisa vyavhaar karoon?', Porus bola — 'jaisa ek raja doosre raja se karta hai'! Alexander ne prabhaavit hokar use satrap (governor) bana kar raajya lauta diya. Zameen 'pavitra' kaise banti hai?

Sarkar kyon chahiye? — Kautilya ke 3 kaam

Kautilya ke anusaar raja ke 3 kartavya: raksha (baahari hamle se bachaav), paalana (kaanoon-vyavastha), yogakshema (janta ka kalyaan). Sarkar ke 3 kaam: legislative (niyam banana), executive (lagu karna), judicial (paalan sunishchit karna).

Direct, representative; parliamentary, presidential

Direct democracy — sab naagrik khud faisla karein (Switzerland ke kuch hisson mein); bade desh mein mushkil. Representative — hum pratinidhi chunte hain; chunaav India mein har 5 saal, USA mein har 4 saal. Parliamentary (India, Australia) — PM aur mantri legislature ka hi hissa; Lok Sabha ka vishwas zaroori. Presidential (USA, South Korea) — President ko janta seedha chunti; legislature ke vishwas ki zaroorat nahi. Legislature: India — Parliament (Lok Sabha zyada taaqatwar + Rajya Sabha); USA — Congress (Senate + House barabar); South Korea — ek sadan.

Hamare purane ganrajya aur Uttaramerur

Vajji (Lichchhavi) — yogyata aur chunaav se neta → shuruaati republic (jahan rashtra pramukh chuna jaata hai, khaandaani nahi). Rajatarangini (Kalhana, 12th sadi): raja Chandrapida ko mandir ke liye ek mochi ki jhopdi chahiye thi; raja ne izzat se anumati maangi aur muaavza diya!

Aaj ki duniya mein aur kaunsi sarkarein?

Absolute monarchy — raja ke paas sab shakti (Saudi Arabia). Constitutional monarchy — raja naam ka, asli shakti PM-Parliament ki (UK, King Charles III). Theocracy — dharm ke niyam aur dharmguru (Iran, Afghanistan, Vatican City). Dictatorship — ek vyakti/samooh ki poori shakti: Hitler (Holocaust mein 60 lakh Yahudi), Idi Amin (Uganda — Bharatiyon ko bhaagna pada); North Korea mein baal, kapde, internet tak sarkar tay kare. Asthaayi adhyaksh Dr. Sachidananda Sinha, phir adhyaksh Dr. Rajendra Prasad. Drafting Committee ke chairman Dr. B.R. Ambedkar — samaaj sudhaarak, pehle Law minister. ~3 saal ki mehnat ke baad 26 November 1949 ko apnaaya gaya (Constitution Day), 26 Jan 1950 ko lagu. Aaj ye duniya ka sabse bada likhit samvidhan — 25 parts, 12 schedules (1950 mein 22 parts, 8 schedules).

Prerna kahan se mili?

(1) Swatantrata andolan — samaanta, nyaay, azaadi, bhaichaara, sabko vote. (2) Hamari sabhyata — alag vichaaron ki sweekriti, prakriti ka samman, vasudhaiva kutumbakam, sarve bhavantu sukhinah, janapada-sangha, rajadharma. (3) Duniya — 'a no bhadrah kratavo yantu vishwatah' (har disha se achchhe vichaar aayein): France → liberty, equality, fraternity; Ireland → Directive Principles; USA → swatantra nyaypaalika; UK, Australia bhi. Prem Behari Narain Raizada ne haath se sundar likhaavat (calligraphy) mein likha; Nandalal Bose ki team ne Mohenjo-daro, gurukul, Ramayana, Gita, Nataraja, Nalanda tak ke chitra banaaye!

Teen zaroori hisse — Rights, DPSP, Duties

Fundamental Rights — court mein lagu karwa sakte ho: Art. 14 (kaanoon ke saamne samaanta), Art. 21 (jeevan aur swatantrata), shoshan ke khilaaf, Art. 21A (shiksha). Directive Principles (DPSP) — sarkar ke liye disha-nirdesh, court mein lagu nahi: Art. 38 (nyaay), 41 (kalyaan), 44 (Uniform Civil Code), 47 (poshan, swasthya), 48A (paryavaran, jeev), 49 (smarak). Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A, 1976) — samvidhan, jhanda, rashtragaan ka samman; desh ki raksha; mili-juli virasat; paryavaran; utkrishtata; 6–14 saal ke bachchon ko shiksha dilaana (maata-pita).

Jeevit dastavez — badalta bhi hai

Samvidhan 'living document' hai — sansad mein bahas ke baad amendment (sanshodhan). 1992 — 73rd Amendment se Panchayati Raj samvidhan mein. 2004 — Supreme Court: ghar par tiranga fehraana = abhivyakti ki azaadi! Begum Aizaz Rasul (1949): mahilaaon ki barabari India ke liye naya nahi, purana aadarsh hai.

Preamble — samvidhan ka 'parichay patra'

'We, the People of India' — shakti janta ki. Sovereign (koi bahari shakti hukm nahi de sakti), Socialist, Secular (koi sarkari dharm nahi, sab dharmon ka barabar samman), Democratic, Republic (rashtra pramukh chuna hua). Lakshya: Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity. 'Socialist', 'Secular', 'Integrity' — 42nd Amendment, 1976 mein jode gaye. President samvidhan ki 'raksha, sanrakshan aur bachaav' ki shapath leta hai.

From Barter to Money

Barter se sikke, kaagazi note aur UPI tak — paise ka safar aur uske kaam.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Jab paisa nahi tha — barter

Socho tumhare paas gehu hai aur tumhe joote chahiye. Paisa nahi, to tum mochi ko gehu doge — ye hai barter system: bina paise ke saamaan/seva ki adla-badli. Duniya ka sabse pehla tareeka. Kaudi (cowrie), namak, chai, kapda, pashu, beej — sab 'paise' ki tarah chale. Ajeeb paise bhi: Yap dweep ke vishaal Rai pathar ke pahiye, Mexico ka taambe ka Tajadero, Solomon dweep ka laal pankhon wala Tevau!

Barter ki 5 mushkilein — kisaan aur bail

Ek kisaan apna bail deke kapda, namak aur bartan lena chahta hai: (1) Double coincidence of wants — kapde wale ko bhi bail chahiye, ye zaroori nahi! (2) Keemat naapne ka ek paimaana nahi — ek bail = kitna kapda? (3) Divisibility — aadha bail nahi de sakte! (4) Portability — bail har jagah le jaana mushkil. (5) Durability — gehu sad jaata hai. Isliye paisa aaya.

Aaj bhi barter — Junbeel Mela

Junbeel Mela (Morigaon, Assam; jun = chaand, beel = jheel) — 3 din, Agni Pooja se shuru; 15th sadi se Tiwa, Karbi, Khasi, Jaintia samudaay milte hain aur pahaad ki upaj ko maidaan ke chaawal ke pithe se badalte hain — bina paise! Schools ke book exchange club bhi barter hi hain.

Paise ke 4 kaam

(1) Medium of exchange — len-den ka zariya. (2) Store of value — aaj kamaao, baad mein kharcho (gehu jaisa sadta nahi!). (3) Measure of value — har cheez ki keemat ek ikaai (rupaye) mein — tulna aasaan. (4) Standard of deferred payment — udhaar lo, baad mein lautaao.

Sikkon ka safar — 'rupa' se 'rupaya'

Kaudi → dhaatu ke sikke (~600 BCE) → kaagazi note (official 1861) → card → UPI (2016). Purane sikke raja jaari karte (minting). Naam: karshapana / pana; sikke par thappa maara chinh = 'rupa' → isi se 'rupaya'! 'Pana' aaj bhi — Tamil/Telugu/Malayalam mein panam, Kannada mein hana = paisa. Sikke ke do pehlu: obverse (head) aur reverse (tail). Chalukya sikke par Varaha, Chola chaandi sikke par baagh. Pudukkottai (TN) mein Roman sone ke sikke — vyapaar India ke paksh mein tha! 1947 mein 1 anna (1/16 rupaya) mein darjan kele milte the.

₹ ka nishaan aur kaagazi note

₹ — 2010; designer Udaya Kumar (IIT Bombay); Devanagari 'Ra' + Roman 'R'; upar ki do rekhaayein = tiranga aur 'barabar' ka nishaan. Kaagazi paisa pehle China mein; India mein late 18th sadi (Bank of Bengal, Bank of Bombay). Aaj note-sikke jaari karne ka haq sirf RBI ko — aur kisi ka chhaapna gair-kaanooni. Note par virasat ke chitra aur drishtiheen logon ke liye ubhre nishaan.

Digital paisa aur Arthashastra ka hisaab

Aaj paisa dikhta bhi nahi (intangible) — debit/credit card, net banking, UPI, QR code ('quick-response'); Krishnappa ke phal ke thele par bhi QR! Arthashastra: saalaana vetan 60 pana = roz ek adhaka (~3 kg) anaaj; padosi ki madad na karne par 100 pana jurmaana — paisa samaaj ke moolya bhi sikhaata tha.

Understanding Markets

Market kya hai, keemat kaise tay hoti hai, market ke prakaar, supply chain, sarkar ki bhoomika aur quality marks.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Bazaar kya hai — needs aur wants

Market (bazaar) = jahan log saamaan aur sevaayein khareedte-bechte hain — haat, mandi, marukatte (Kannada), mall, ya phone ka app. Needs = jeene ke liye zaroori (khaana, paani, kapda, ghar); Wants = chaahat (naya video game) — zaroori nahi. Hampi Bazaar (Vijayanagara, 16th sadi, Virupaksha mandir ke saamne) — Portuguese Domingos Paes: 'duniya ka sabse sampann shehar'; Fernao Nuniz ne heere-moti-kapdon ka varnan kiya.

Keemat kaise tay hoti hai? — amrood ki kahaani

Bazaar ke liye chahiye: khareedaar, bechne wala, aur dono ko manzoor keemat; mol-bhaav (bargaining) se tay. Amrood ₹80 kilo — bahut mehenga, koi nahi khareedega; ₹20 — bechne wale ko ghaata; ₹40 — 'just right'! Demand (kitne log khareedna chahte) aur supply (kitna maal hai) milkar keemat tay karte hain. Isliye raat ko sabzi sasti (maal bacha, khareedaar kam) aur sardi ke ant mein woollen par discount.

Bazaar ke prakaar

Physical (haat, local market, mall) vs online (apps, websites; online classes bhi). Domestic (desh ke andar) vs international (export = bahar bechna, import = bahar se mangaana). India 2024 mein vanaspati tel ka sabse bada importer (palm oil — Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand). India bechta hai: N. America ko software, Africa ko dawaayein, Europe ko machinery, West Asia ko refined petrol; mangaata hai: Africa se heere, West Asia se kachcha tel aur khaad, S. America se copper ore.

Khet se thaali tak — supply chain

Kachcha maal → producer (banane wala) → wholesaler (thok mein khareede; godaam, cold storage, mandi) → distributor → retailer (mohalle ki dukaan) → consumer (tum!). Online mein aggregator (app) godaam se seedha ghar. Thok bazaar: Khari Baoli (Old Delhi — masaale, dry fruits), Bengaluru ka phool bazaar, Johari Bazaar (Jaipur — ratna-gehne). Surat — Asia ka sabse purana kapda bazaar aur duniya ka sabse bada heera udyog (~15 lakh kaarigar).

Bazaar sirf len-den nahi, rishta bhi

Darzi, sunaar, doctor se peedhiyon ka rishta; mahine ke ant mein kiraane ka hisaab. Ima Keithal ('Maa ka bazaar', Imphal, Manipur) — ~3,000 mahilaayein saari dukaanein chalaati hain! Dakshin mein dukaandaar muft haldi-kumkum dete — shubhkaamna.

Sarkar bazaar mein kya karti hai?

(1) Keemat niyantran — jeevanrakshak dawaon ki upari seema; gehu-dhaan-makka ka nyuntam samarthan mulya (kisaan ko ghaata na ho); minimum wages. (2) Quality-suraksha — dawaon ki jaanch. (3) Bura asar rokna — single-use plastic par rok, tarazu-baat ki jaanch. (4) Public goods — park, sadak, police. Kautilya bhi: ghee bechne wala 1/50 hissa zyada de, kyonki bartan mein chipak jaata hai — purana consumer protection!

Packet padhna seekho — label aur quality mark

Label par: net quantity, manufacture date, batch no., best before, MRP, nirmata ka pata, poshan jaankaari, saamagri, allergen. Quality marks: FSSAI — khaana; ISI (BIS) — bijli ka saamaan, construction, tyre; AGMARK — krishi upaj (daal, masaale, shahad); BEE Star — zyada star = kam bijli. Logon ki raay (word of mouth) aur online review bhi kharid ko prabhaavit karte hain — par soch-samajh kar!

Kheti — sirf hal-bail nahi

The State, the Government, and You

State vs government, democracy vs republic, teen ang, political vs permanent executive, aur nagrik sarkar se kaise kaam karwaaye.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

State aur Government — farak samjho

Socho tumhara school = State (rashtra) — wo hamesha rehta hai; aur principal-teachers = Government (sarkar) — jo badal sakte hain. State ke 4 ang: People (sthaayi aabadi), Territory (tay seema), Government, Sovereignty (bina bahari dakhal ke kaanoon banaane ki poori shakti). Sarkar chunaav se badalti hai, state wahi rehta hai. India mein 'State' ka matlab desh bhi, aur Kerala jaisa rajya bhi! Socrates: 'raaj na karne ki sabse badi saza — kisi badtar ke raaj mein rehna'.

Democracy aur Republic — dono ek nahi

Democracy = janta ka raaj. Republic = rashtra pramukh chuna hua, khaandaani nahi. Har democracy republic nahi — UK, Canada, Sweden mein loktantra hai par raja/rani bhi; India aur USA dono hain. Republic mein bahumat ki manmaani se bhi bachaav: Dongria Kondh ki pavitra pahaadiyon par mining nahi hui; colony ki RWA ne raat 9 baje ke baad delivery band ki, par beemaar buzurg ke liye dawa ko chhoot mili. George Washington ne raja banne se inkaar kiya. Kautilya: sarkar na ho to 'matsya nyaya' — badi machhli chhoti ko kha jaaye!

Teen ang aur do tarah ke executive

Legislature (kaanoon — Parliament, Vidhan Sabha), Executive (lagu karna), Judiciary (paalan aur samvidhan ki raksha). Executive do tarah: Political — chune hue PM, CM, mantri (aam taur par 5 saal); Permanent — UPSC jaisi pariksha se chune civil servants (IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS, IFoS) jo chunaav se nahi badalte. Zameeni star par sarkari teacher, anganwadi, health worker, bus conductor. Police rajya sarkar ki — kaanooni taur par bal prayog kar sakti, par adhikaaron ka samman zaroori.

C.B. Muthamma — pehli mahila IFS

C.B. Muthamma (1924–2009, Karnataka) — 1948 mein civil services pariksha paas karne wali pehli mahila, IFS mein pehli, pehli mahila rajdoot. Shaadi par istifa jaise bhedbhaav wale niyam ke khilaaf Supreme Court gayin — Justice V.R. Krishna Iyer ki bench ne niyam radd kiye. Himmat se badlaav!

Sarkar ki 3 bhoomika aur hamari taakat

Protector (raksha, kaanoon), Provider (kalyaan, sadak-school), Regulator (arthvyavastha, nyaay). Naagrik kya kar sakte hain: (1) shikaayat — pgportal.gov.in; (2) RTI (Right to Information) — sarkar se jaankaari maango (Bagepalli ki sadak isi se bani!); (3) media/social media; (4) NGO; (5) pratinidhi ko chitthi; (6) vote. Union, State, Local. India: 28 rajya, 8 UT. Mool samvidhan mein do star the; 1990s ke 73rd/74th Amendment se teesra star (panchayat, nagar palika). Byaaj par byaaj — compounding ka jaadu

Interest (byaaj) = paise udhaar dene/lene ka 'kiraaya'. Compounding = pichhle byaaj par bhi byaaj! ₹1,000 @ 6%: 1st saal ₹1,060, 2nd saal ₹1,123.60, aur 12 saal mein ₹2,012 — lagbhag doguna! RBI — bankon ka bank

Delhi daftar ke darwaaze par yaksha-yakshini — Kubera ke khazaane ke rakshak! C. Short-answer (model answers)

  1. 1India ko 'mini-continent' kyun kehte hain?

    India mein ek hi desh ke andar barf wale Himalaya, Ladakh ka cold desert, upjaau Gangetic plains, Thar ka garam registaan, purana Aravalli, Peninsular plateau, do lambi coastlines, deltas, coral aur volcanic islands, aur Meghalaya ki duniya ki sabse zyada baarish — sab hai. Itni bhaugolik, jalvaayu aur jaiv vividhata aam taur par poore mahadweep mein milti hai.

  2. 2Monsoon kaise banta hai?

    Garmi mein zameen samudra se jaldi garam hoti hai, isliye Asia par low pressure banta hai. Hawa high pressure (thande samudra) se low pressure (garam zameen) ki taraf chalti hai aur nami laati hai — yahi southwest monsoon (June se mid-July tak poore desh mein). Sardi mein ulta: zameen thandi, hawa zameen se samudra ki taraf — northeast monsoon, jo Bay of Bengal paar karke dakshin-purv mein baarish deta hai.

  3. 3Kautilya ke Saptanga ko samjhaaiye.

    Rajya ke saat ang: Swami (raja), Amatya (mantri-adhikaari), Janapada (ilaaka aur log), Durga (kile wale shahar), Kosha (khazana), Danda (sena, law & order) aur Mitra (mitra rajya). Saaton milkar surakshit aur samriddh rajya banaate hain. Mool mantra: 'praja ke sukh mein raja ka sukh'.

  4. 4Gupta kaal ko 'classical age' kyun kehte hain?

    Lambi shaanti aur samriddhi mein vigyaan (Aryabhata — dharti ka ghoomna, saal ki lambai; Varahamihira — Brihat Samhita), sahitya (Kalidasa — Meghadutam), chikitsa (Charaka-Sushruta Samhita ko antim roop), dhaatu-vigyaan (jang na lagne wala Iron Pillar) aur kala (Ajanta, Sarnath, Deogarh) apne shikhar par pahunche. Purane gyaan ko sankalit karke granth bane, jo sadiyon tak maanak rahe.

  5. 5Fundamental Rights aur DPSP mein farak.

    Fundamental Rights (jaise Art. 14 samaanta, Art. 21 jeevan-swatantrata, Art. 21-A shiksha) vaade hain — ullanghan par court jaa sakte hain. DPSP (Art. 38 nyaay, 44 UCC, 47 poshan, 48-A paryavaran) sarkar ke liye disha-nirdesh hain — court se lagu nahi karwa sakte. Samvidhan nirmataon ne jaan-boojh kar kuch turant adhikaar aur kuch dheere-dheere poore hone wale lakshya rakhe.

  6. 6Democracy aur Republic mein farak, udaharan sahit.

    Democracy = janta free aur fair chunaav se sarkar chunti hai. Republic = head of state chuna hua (seedha ya apratyaksh), vanshaanugat raja nahi, aur samvidhan shakti par seema lagata hai. India aur USA dono hain. UK, Canada, Sweden democracy hain par republic nahi, kyunki wahan raja/rani head of state hai.

  7. 7Kheti mein paramparik aur aadhunik tareekon ka santulan kyun zaroori hai?

    Green Revolution (HYV beej, rasayanik khaad, machine) se anaaj mein aatmanirbharta aayi, par mitti kamzor hui, groundwater ghata, pradooshan aur swasthya par asar pada. Paramparik tareeke (crop rotation, panchagavya, phad, terrace farming) prakriti ke saath chalte hain par upaj kam ho sakti hai. ICAR ne 85%+ paramparik tareeke vigyan se sahi paaye — isliye drip irrigation, neem keetnashak, organic kheti (Sikkim) jaisa mel sabse achha raasta hai.

  8. 8Compound interest ka jaadu udaharan se samjhaaiye.

    Compound interest mein pichhle byaaj par bhi byaaj milta hai. ₹1,000 @ 6% → 1st saal ₹60 byaaj (₹1,060), 2nd saal ₹63.60 (₹1,123.60), aur 12 saal mein ₹2,012.20 — lagbhag doguna. Ambalappuzha ke raja ki shatranj kahani mein har khaane par chawal doguna hone se 32nd khaane tak 210 crore se zyada daane ho gaye. Jitna lamba samay, utni tez badhat.
