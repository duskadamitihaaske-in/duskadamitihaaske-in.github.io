Geographical Diversity of India

Himalaya se islands tak — Bharat ke paanch bade bhaugolik kshetra aur unka jeevan par asar.

Notes

  • Rakesh Sharma (1984) — pehle Indian astronaut; Indira Gandhi ke sawaal par bole: 'Saare jahan se achchha'.
  • India duniya ka 7th sabse bada desh. Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar ke saath mil kar Indian Subcontinent.
  • 5 kshetra: great mountain zone, Ganga-Indus plains, desert, southern peninsula, islands. Prakritik seema: uttar mein Himalaya, pashchim mein Thar aur Arabian Sea, dakshin mein Indian Ocean, purab mein Bay of Bengal.
  • Himalaya ~2,500 km lamba; kai chotiyan 8,000 m+ ('Eight Thousanders'); 6 desh: India, Nepal, Bhutan, China, Pakistan, Afghanistan. Barf pighal kar Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra ko paani → 'Water Tower of Asia'.
  • Bhagirathi (Ganga ki mukhya tributary) Gaumukh (Gangotri Glacier, Uttarakhand) se nikalti hai.
  • Banna: India pehle Gondwana ka hissa (padosi Africa!). ~5 crore (50 million) saal pehle Eurasia se takraya → zameen 'carpet ki tarah' sikud kar Himalaya bana. Aaj bhi India ~5 cm/saal Asia mein ghus raha hai; Himalaya ~5 mm/saal ooncha ho raha hai.
  • 'Himalaya' = hima (barf) + alaya (ghar) = barf ka ghar.
  • 3 range: Himadri (Greater — Everest, Kanchenjunga; saal bhar barf), Himachal (Lesser — Nainital, Darjeeling, Shimla, Mussoorie), Shivalik (Outer — sabse neeche, ghane jungle, plains se jodne wala zone).
  • Kath-kuni / dhajji-dewari — western Himalaya ka pathar+lakdi ka ghar; garam aur halke bhookamp mein majboot. Great Himalayan National Park (Himachal) — UNESCO World Heritage.
  • Ladakh = cold desert; sardi mein −30°C se neeche; Pangong Tso (khaara paani; tso = jheel); 'Moonland'. Yak — doodh, maas, oon, gobar, transport. Tyohaar: Losar, Hemis.
  • Gangetic (Northern) Plains: Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra ki mitti → bahut upjaau; badi aabadi; sadak-rail ka jaal aasaan. Brahmaputra = 'Brahma ka putra' (nadi ka purush naam), garmi mein badhti hai.
  • Wildlife: grey langur, tiger (Project Tiger), gharial (2.5–4.5 m, vilupti ke kagar par), mor (rashtriya pakshi).
  • Thar Desert: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Punjab, Haryana; sand dunes 150 m tak; Jaisalmer 'Golden City' (fort UNESCO); Pushkar Mela; paani bachane ke liye taanka / kund.
  • Aravalli — duniya ke sabse purane pahadon mein, ~2.5 billion saal; sabse oonchi choti Mount Abu (1,700 m+); Thar ko purab badhne se rokti hai. Marble, granite, zinc, copper; Zawar ki khaanein — 800+ saal pehle Indians ne duniya mein sabse pehle zinc nikaalna seekha. Forts: Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Ranthambore.
  • Peninsular Plateau — teen taraf paani; Western Ghats (oonche, deewar jaise; UNESCO; uttari bhaag = Sahyadri) aur Eastern Ghats (neeche, toote hue). Beech mein Deccan Plateau, purab ki taraf jhuka.
  • Purab bahne wali: Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi (Bay of Bengal). Pashchim bahne wali: Narmada, Tapti (Arabian Sea). Plateau ke jungle: Santhal, Gond, Baiga, Bhil, Korku.
  • Coastline 7,500 km+. West coast (Gujarat–Kerala): tez nadiyan, estuaries (Narmada, Tapti sabse badi). East coast: chaude maidan, deltas (Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri), Chilika aur Pulicat (lagoon).
  • Lakshadweep — Arabian Sea, 36 coral islands. Andaman & Nicobar — 500+ volcanic islands; Cellular Jail; Barren Island = India ka ekmatra active volcano.
  • Sundarbans — Ganga-Brahmaputra delta; aadha India aadha Bangladesh; mangroves, Royal Bengal Tiger, UNESCO.
  • North-East: Garo, Khasi, Jaintia hills (Meghalaya Plateau); Mawlynnong — 'Asia ka sabse saaf gaon'; living root bridges.

Understanding the Weather

Mausam ke 5 tatva, unhe naapne ke yantra, weather station aur forecast.

Notes

  • Weather = kisi jagah aur samay par vayumandal ki sthiti. Atmosphere = gas ki parat ('air').
  • Sabse neeche ki parat troposphere — yahin jeevan aur lagbhag saara mausam. Oonchai 6–18 km: poles par patli, tropics mein moti. Upar stratosphere (ozone layer).
  • Mausam ke 5 tatva: Temperature, Precipitation (baarish, barf, sleet, ole), Atmospheric pressure, Wind (gati + disha), Humidity (hawa mein bhaap).
  • Paramparik sanket: cheentiyan ande oonchi jagah le jaayein, mendhak tarraayein, pine cones band (nami) / khule (sookha) → baarish ka andaaza. Meteorology = mausam ka vaigyanik adhyayan.
  • Thermometer — temperature. Celsius/Fahrenheit: 15°C = 59°F. Range = max − min. Mean daily temp = (max + min) ÷ 2.
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD) — 1875. Motto: 'ādityāt jāyate vṛiṣhṭi' — 'Surya se varsha hoti hai' (Manusmriti).
  • Rain gauge — funnel + cylinder + scale; 5 mm paani = 5 mm baarish.
  • Atmospheric pressure = upar ki hawa ka bhaar. Tat par zyada, pahaad par kam (kam oxygen → saans phoolna). Achanak giravat = 'depression' / low-pressure system → toofan/cyclone.
  • Barometer — pressure, unit millibar (mb). Tat par normal ~1013 mb; 1000 mb se kam = depression. Khardung La (Ladakh, 5,600 m+) par ~650 mb.
  • Wind = high pressure se low pressure ki taraf hawa. Wind vane — disha (airport par 'wind sock'). Anemometer — 3–4 cups, gati km/h mein.
  • Humidity → relative humidity (%): sookha 20–40%, nami wala 60–80%. Zyada nami mein paani dheere bhaap banta hai — kapde der se sookhte, paseena zyada. Yantra: Hygrometer. Food processing aur museums mein zaroori.
  • Weather station — saare yantra ek jagah. Automated Weather Station (AWS) — sensors se khud record. 2023: NDMA ne Sikkim ki glacial lake par 4,800 m+ par AWS lagaya.
  • IMD warning rang: Watch (Be Updated), Alert (Be Prepared), Warning (Take Action). Forecast se machhuaaron ko chetavani, cyclone mein tat khaali karwana.
  • Loo = uttar India ki garmi ki tez, garam, dhool bhari hawa.

Climates of India

Weather vs climate, India ke climate prakaar, unke kaaran, monsoon, aapdaayein aur climate change.

Notes

  • Weather = roz badalne wala; Season = har saal lautne wala; Climate = lambe samay (kam se kam 3 dashak) ka mausam pattern.
  • Paramparik 6 ritu: vasanta (spring), grishma (summer), varsha (rainy), sharad (autumn), hemanta (pre-winter), shishir (winter). Tyohaar: Vasanta Panchami, Sharad Purnima.
  • India ke climate: Himalaya — alpine; neeche pahaad — temperate (hill stations); uttari maidan — subtropical (gehun); Thar — arid; pashchimi tat — tropical wet (chawal, masale); Deccan — semi-arid; purvi/dakshini — tropical.
  • Climate ke 5 kaaran: Latitude, Altitude, Proximity to sea, Winds, Topography.
  • Latitude: Equator par surya ki kirne seedhi (kendrit), poles par tirchhi (phaili) → poles thande. Kanniyakumari garam, Srinagar thanda.
  • Altitude: oonchai badhe → hawa ka ghanatva kam → thanda. Ooty (10–25°C) vs Coimbatore (25–38°C), latitude same.
  • Samudra ki nazdeeki: samudra tapmaan sambhaalta hai. Mumbai (32°C / 18°C, range ~14°C) vs Nagpur (44°C / 10°C, range 34°C).
  • Winds: Arabia-Afghanistan se aati sookhi garam hawa → Punjab-Rajasthan mein heat wave; sardi mein Himalaya paar ki thandi hawa → cold wave.
  • Topography: Himalaya-Karakoram Central Asia ki thandi hawa se bachaate; samtal Thar ke paas koi bachaav nahi.
  • Microclimate — chhote kshetra ka alag climate; urban heat island = concrete wale shahar aas-paas se garam.
  • Monsoon — Arabic 'mausim' = season. Garmi mein zameen tezi se garam → low pressure → samudra se nami wali hawa → baarish. Sardi mein ulta.
  • Southwest (summer) monsoon: June ki shuruaat mein dakshini sire se, mid-July tak poore desh mein. Western Ghats ke pashchimi dhalaan par bahut baarish, Deccan par kam. Northeast (winter) monsoon: Bay of Bengal se nami → purv aur dakshin ke hisson mein baarish.
  • Mawsynram (Meghalaya) — duniya mein sabse zyada saalana ausat baarish, ~11,000 mm (11 metre!). Raag: Megh Malhar, Amritavarshini.
  • Paramparik gyaan: Konkan ke machhuaare machhli satah par dekhein; Golden Shower tree (Cassia fistula) khilne ke ~50 din mein monsoon; kauve ka ghonsla oonchaa → kam baarish.
  • 'Monsoon failure' → kheti barbaad, paani ki kami, palayan, mehngai (inflation).
  • Cyclone: tez low-pressure, ghoomti hawa; kendra = 'eye of the cyclone' (badal rahit). Zyada purvi tat par. NDRF — 12 jagah battalions.
  • Flood: UP, Bihar, Kerala, Andhra, Assam. Himalaya mein glacial burst; 2013 Uttarakhand (Kedarnath) — ~6,000 maut. Shehri baadh — kharab drainage, encroachment.
  • Landslide: Himachal, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal, Western Ghats; jungal katna, galat nirmaan. Forest fire: Uttarakhand, Himachal, MP, Chhattisgarh.
  • Climate change: 19th century se insaani kaaran — fossil fuels (coal, petroleum, natural gas), deforestation. Greenhouse gases (CO₂) garmi rokti → global warming. 2025 ki shuruaat mein India ka ausat temp normal se 1–3°C upar. Upaay = mitigation: emission kam, ped, renewable energy.

New Beginnings: Cities and States

Second Urbanisation — janapada, 16 mahajanapada, gana-sangha, iron, sikke aur varna-jati vyavastha.

Notes

  • Harappan shehar (First Urbanisation) 2nd millennium BCE ki shuruaat mein bikhre; lagbhag ek hazaar saal tak shehri jeevan gaayab raha.
  • 1st millennium BCE mein Ganga ke maidan, Indus basin aur aas-paas naya shehrikaran — 'Second Urbanisation', jo aaj tak chal raha hai. Saboot: khudai + late Vedic, Buddhist, Jain granth.
  • Janapada = 'jahan jan (log) ne pada (pair) rakha' — ek clan ka ilaaka, mukhiya rājā. 2nd millennium BCE ke ant tak bane.
  • 8th–7th century BCE tak janapada mil kar mahājanapada; sabse prachalit soochi = 16. Uttar-pashchim mein Gandhara, purab mein Anga, Godavari ke paas Ashmaka (central/south).
  • Sabse shaktishaali: Magadha (Rajagriha — aaj Rajgir, Bihar), Kosala (Shravasti), Vatsa (Kaushambi), Avanti (Ujjayini). Kai rajdhaniyan aaj bhi zinda shahar — ~2,500 saal purane!
  • Zyaadatar mahajanapada Ganga plains mein — upjaau zameen, aas-paas pahaadon mein iron ore, naye trade networks.
  • Rajdhaniyan: kile ki deewar (rampart), bahar moat (paani bhari khaai), patle darwaaze taaki aana-jaana control ho.
  • Har janapada mein sabhā / samiti (sabha) — buzurgon ki salaah; kuch granth kehte hain ayogya raja hataaya ja sakta tha.
  • Zyaadatar mahajanapada monarchy — raja peedhi-dar-peedhi; kar vasoolna, law & order, kile, sena.
  • Vajji (Vrijji) aur Malla — gaṇa / sangha: sabha charcha aur vote se faisle leti, raja bhi chunti → 'early republics', duniya ke sabse shuruaati.
  • Takneek: Harappa = copper/bronze; Second Urbanisation = iron. Late 2nd millennium BCE tak lohe ke auzaar aam → badi kheti, tez halke hathiyaar.
  • India ke pehle sikke — chaandi ke punch-marked coins (chinh 'punch' kiye); baad mein tamba, sona. Har mahajanapada ke apne sikke.
  • Samaaj: jāti = khaas pesha wala samudaay (hunar peedhi-dar-peedhi; sub-jati ke apne riwaaj). Varṇa (Vedic granthon se) = 4: Brahmin (gyaan, anushthaan), Kshatriya (raksha), Vaishya (vyapaar, kheti), Shudra (kaarigar, mazdoor).
  • 'Caste' shabd Portuguese 'casta' se (16th century). Shuruaat mein vyavastha lachili thi — log halaat ke hisaab se pesha badalte. Samay ke saath, khaas kar British raj mein, kathor hui aur bhedbhaav badha.
  • Do bade raaste: Uttarapatha (uttar-pashchim → Ganga plains → purab) aur Dakshinapatha (Kaushambi, Prayagraj ke paas, se Vindhya paar karke dakshin).
  • Shishupalgarh (aaj Bhubaneswar ka hissa, 1948 mein khudai) — Kalinga ki rajdhani, square plan. Dakshin mein shahar ~400 BCE se; Chola, Chera, Pandya. Kodumanal (Erode, TN) — shell aur gemstone udyog.
  • 300–200 BCE tak poora upmahadweep jud gaya; mahajanapada khatam hue.

The Rise of Empires

Magadha, Nanda, Alexander, Maurya — Chandragupta-Kautilya, Ashoka aur samrajyon ki kamzori.

Notes

  • Empire — Latin 'imperium' (supreme power). Chhote rajya tributary / vassal ban kar samraat ko kar (tribute) dete. Sanskrit: samrāj, adhirāja, rājādhirāja.
  • Empire ki visheshtaayein: sena, administration, sadak-nadi-samudri jaal, kanoon-sikke-naap tol, kala aur gyaan ko badhaava, sansaadhan par niyantran.
  • Guilds (śhreṇī) — vyapaari, kaarigar, sahukaar, kisaano ke sangathan; chuna hua mukhiya; apne niyam bana sakte the, raja dakhal nahi deta → samaaj ki self-organising kshamta.
  • Vyapaar: kapda, masale, kheti upaj, ratn, hastshilp, jaanwar — desh aur videsh dono.
  • Magadha (dakshini Bihar) — 6th–4th century BCE; upjaau Ganga plains, jungle (lakdi, haathi), paas mein iron ore, Ganga-Son nadiyan. Raja Ajatashatru — Buddha aur Mahavira ke samkaaleen.
  • ~5th century BCE: Mahapadma Nanda — Nanda vansh, sikke chalaaye, badi sena. Antim raja Dhana Nanda — ameer par janta ko satane wala, aprasiddh.
  • Panini (~5th century BCE) — Ashtadhyayi: Sanskrit vyakaran ke 3,996 sutra.
  • Alexander (Macedonia): 334–331 BCE Persian empire jeeta; 327–325 BCE India aaya, Punjab mein Porus (Paurava) ko haraaya. Porus: 'Raja jaisa vyavhaar' → satrap bana kar raaj lautaaya. Sainik aage Ganga ki taraf jaane se mana; Babylon mein 32 saal ki umr mein maut.
  • Satrap = Persian/Greek pradesh ke governor. Gymnosophists ('naked philosophers') — Alexander ke paheliyon ka shaant jawab.
  • ~321 BCE: Chandragupta Maurya ne Maurya empire banaya, rajdhani Pataliputra (Patna). Guru Kautilya / Chanakya / Vishnugupta — Takshashila ke shikshak; Dhana Nanda ne apmaan kiya → Nanda raaj khatam karne ki pratigya.

From Barter to Money

Barter se sikke, kaagazi note aur UPI tak — paise ka safar aur uske kaam.

Notes

  • Barter system = bina paise ke saamaan/seva ka len-den. Sabse pehla tareeka. Cowrie, namak, chai, tambaaku, kapda, pashu, beej use hote.
  • Duniya ke ajeeb paise: Rai stones (Yap Island, Micronesia — vishaal pathar ke pahiye), Aztec copper Tajadero (Mexico), Tevau (laal pankh ka coil, Solomon Islands).
  • Barter ki 5 dikkatein (kisaan aur bail ki kahani): Double coincidence of wants (dono ko ek-doosre ki cheez chahiye), common standard measure of value nahi, divisibility (aadha bail nahi), portability (bail har jagah nahi le ja sakte), durability (gehun sad jaata).
  • Aaj bhi barter: Junbeel Mela (Morigaon, Assam; jun = chaand, beel = wetland) — 3 din, Agni Puja se shuru; 15th century se Tiwa, Karbi, Khasi, Jaintia ke mukhiya milte; pahaad ki upaj ↔ maidan ke chawal ke pithe. Book exchange club, purane kapde ↔ bartan.
  • Paise ke kaam: Medium of exchange, Store of value (baad mein use), Measure of value (common denomination — keemat ki tulna), Standard of deferred payment (udhaar/baad mein bhugtaan).
  • Timeline: barter → cowrie → ~6000 BCE... dhaatu sikke (iron, silver, gold, copper) ~600 BCE → official kaagazi note 1861 → debit/credit card → UPI 2016.
  • Pracheen sikke raja jaari karte (minting); shaktishaali rajaon ke sikke doosre rajyon mein bhi chalte. Naam: kārṣhāpaṇa / paṇa; punch kiye chinh = rūpa (→ 'rupaya'!).
  • 'Paṇa' aaj bhi: Tamil/Telugu/Malayalam mein paṇaṁ, Kannada mein haṇa = paisa.
  • Sikke ke do taraf: obverse (head) aur reverse (tail). Chalukya sikke par Varaha + teen-tiered chhatra; Chola chaandi sikke par baagh (tiger).
  • Pudukkottai (TN) mein Roman sone ke sikke — samudri vyapaar India ke paksh mein tha.
  • 1 anna = 1/16 rupaya; 1947 mein ek anne mein darjan kele.
  • ₹ symbol — 2010; designer Udaya Kumar (IIT Bombay); Devanagari 'Ra' + Roman 'R', upar do rekhaayein = tiranga aur 'barabar' chinh.
  • Paper money pehle China mein; India mein late 18th century (Bank of Bengal, Bank of Bombay ke note). Sikke chhoti, note badi denomination ke liye.
  • Aaj currency jaari karne ka adhikaar sirf Reserve Bank of India (RBI) ko; kisi aur ka chhaapna gair-kanooni. Note par virasat ke chitra, drishtiheen logon ke liye ubhre nishaan.
  • Digital money (intangible): debit/credit card, net banking, UPI, QR code ('quick-response') — Krishnappa ka fruit cart.
  • Arthashastra: saalana vetan 60 paṇa = roz ek āḍhaka (~3 kg) anaaj; padosi ki madad na karne par 100 paṇa jurmaana → samaajik moolya.

Understanding Markets

Banks and the Magic of Finance

Bank kya karte hain, compound interest ka jaadu, RBI, payment systems (UPI), stock market aur fraud se bachaav.

Notes

  • Financial infrastructure = banks, payment systems, stock market, financial institutions — paise ka len-den aur prabandhan.
  • Bank — logon se deposit leta, udhaar lene walon ko loan deta. Navdeep (₹3,000 bachat jama) aur Rima (bamboo business ke liye loan) ki kahani.
  • Account ke prakaar: Savings (vyakti, byaaj milta, nikaasi ki seema), Current (vyapaari, byaaj nahi, len-den ki seema nahi), Fixed Deposit (ek baar, 3–5 saal, savings se zyada byaaj).
  • Interest = paise udhaar dene/lene ka kiraaya (%). Compounding = pichhle byaaj par bhi byaaj. ₹1,000 @ 6%: 1st saal ₹1,060, 2nd saal ₹1,123.60; 12 saal mein ₹2,012.20.
  • Kahani: Ambalappuzha (Kerala) ke raja aur sadhu — shatranj ke har khaane mein chawal doguna: 8th khaana 128, 16th 32,768, 32nd tak 210 crore+ — exponential growth!
  • Passbook — saare len-den ka record. Debit = paisa nikla, Credit = paisa aaya.
  • Bank ki kamaai: jama par kam byaaj, loan par zyada byaaj. Anand ₹200 @ 2% (₹4 mila), Shreya loan ₹200 @ 5% (₹10 diya) → bank ki kamaai ₹6. Bank saara jama loan mein nahi deta — reserve rakhta.
  • PM Jan Dhan Yojana — 2014: zero balance account; pehle sirf ~15 crore ke account, ab 50 crore+ (zyaadatar mahilaayein); seedha wage/scholarship transfer, bichauliye kam.
  • Post office: NSC, Kisan Vikas Patra, Sukanya Samriddhi. IFCI — udyog (bijli, kapda); NABARD — gramin vikaas, krishi loan.
  • RBI — 1935 mein bana; 1949 se sarkari 'banker to banks' (central bank): currency chhaapna-baantna, benchmark interest rate, banko aur sarkar ko loan. Delhi office ke dwaar par yaksha-yakshini (Kubera ke khazane ke rakshak).
  • Pracheen India mein mandir bank jaise — kaarigar, vyapaari, sthaniya sarkar ko udhaar; copper plates par anubandh. Kodumbalur (TN, 13th c.) — Tirumudukunramudaiya-Nayanar mandir se byaaj par karz.
  • Paisa nikaalna: withdrawal slip, debit card + ATM (24×7) + PIN. Transfer: cheque (tareekh, naam, rakam shabdon aur ankon mein, hastaakshar, MICR code), POS machine, net banking, mobile (BHIM).
  • UPI — 2016, NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India); QR ya phone number se turant; COVID mein lokpriya; kai bhashaein. Nepal (2022) pehla videshi desh; UAE, France, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Mauritius bhi → 'India's gift to the world'.
  • Stock market: share = company mein hissedari (chapati ka ek tukda); stock exchange par kharid-bikri. BSE — 1875, duniya ke sabse purane exchange mein. Daam badhe = boom, gire = crash; company ka pradarshan, sarkari neeti, tax, yuddh, economic shock se utaar-chadhaav.
  • Fraud se bachaav: OTP, PIN, password kabhi share mat karo; anjaan link mat dabao; device par bank details mat rakho. Shikayat: helpline 1930 ya cybercrime.gov.in.

C. Short-answer (model answers)

  1. 1India ko 'mini-continent' kyun kehte hain?

    India mein ek hi desh ke andar barf wale Himalaya, Ladakh ka cold desert, upjaau Gangetic plains, Thar ka garam registaan, purana Aravalli, Peninsular plateau, do lambi coastlines, deltas, coral aur volcanic islands, aur Meghalaya ki duniya ki sabse zyada baarish — sab hai. Itni bhaugolik, jalvaayu aur jaiv vividhata aam taur par poore mahadweep mein milti hai.

  2. 2Monsoon kaise banta hai?

    Garmi mein zameen samudra se jaldi garam hoti hai, isliye Asia par low pressure banta hai. Hawa high pressure (thande samudra) se low pressure (garam zameen) ki taraf chalti hai aur nami laati hai — yahi southwest monsoon (June se mid-July tak poore desh mein). Sardi mein ulta: zameen thandi, hawa zameen se samudra ki taraf — northeast monsoon, jo Bay of Bengal paar karke dakshin-purv mein baarish deta hai.

  3. 3Kautilya ke Saptanga ko samjhaaiye.

    Rajya ke saat ang: Swami (raja), Amatya (mantri-adhikaari), Janapada (ilaaka aur log), Durga (kile wale shahar), Kosha (khazana), Danda (sena, law & order) aur Mitra (mitra rajya). Saaton milkar surakshit aur samriddh rajya banaate hain. Mool mantra: 'praja ke sukh mein raja ka sukh'.

  4. 4Gupta kaal ko 'classical age' kyun kehte hain?

    Lambi shaanti aur samriddhi mein vigyaan (Aryabhata — dharti ka ghoomna, saal ki lambai; Varahamihira — Brihat Samhita), sahitya (Kalidasa — Meghadutam), chikitsa (Charaka-Sushruta Samhita ko antim roop), dhaatu-vigyaan (jang na lagne wala Iron Pillar) aur kala (Ajanta, Sarnath, Deogarh) apne shikhar par pahunche. Purane gyaan ko sankalit karke granth bane, jo sadiyon tak maanak rahe.

  5. 5Fundamental Rights aur DPSP mein farak.

    Fundamental Rights (jaise Art. 14 samaanta, Art. 21 jeevan-swatantrata, Art. 21-A shiksha) vaade hain — ullanghan par court jaa sakte hain. DPSP (Art. 38 nyaay, 44 UCC, 47 poshan, 48-A paryavaran) sarkar ke liye disha-nirdesh hain — court se lagu nahi karwa sakte. Samvidhan nirmataon ne jaan-boojh kar kuch turant adhikaar aur kuch dheere-dheere poore hone wale lakshya rakhe.

  6. 6Democracy aur Republic mein farak, udaharan sahit.

    Democracy = janta free aur fair chunaav se sarkar chunti hai. Republic = head of state chuna hua (seedha ya apratyaksh), vanshaanugat raja nahi, aur samvidhan shakti par seema lagata hai. India aur USA dono hain. UK, Canada, Sweden democracy hain par republic nahi, kyunki wahan raja/rani head of state hai.

  7. 7Kheti mein paramparik aur aadhunik tareekon ka santulan kyun zaroori hai?

    Green Revolution (HYV beej, rasayanik khaad, machine) se anaaj mein aatmanirbharta aayi, par mitti kamzor hui, groundwater ghata, pradooshan aur swasthya par asar pada. Paramparik tareeke (crop rotation, panchagavya, phad, terrace farming) prakriti ke saath chalte hain par upaj kam ho sakti hai. ICAR ne 85%+ paramparik tareeke vigyan se sahi paaye — isliye drip irrigation, neem keetnashak, organic kheti (Sikkim) jaisa mel sabse achha raasta hai.

  8. 8Compound interest ka jaadu udaharan se samjhaaiye.

    Compound interest mein pichhle byaaj par bhi byaaj milta hai. ₹1,000 @ 6% → 1st saal ₹60 byaaj (₹1,060), 2nd saal ₹63.60 (₹1,123.60), aur 12 saal mein ₹2,012.20 — lagbhag doguna. Ambalappuzha ke raja ki shatranj kahani mein har khaane par chawal doguna hone se 32nd khaane tak 210 crore se zyada daane ho gaye. Jitna lamba samay, utni tez badhat.
