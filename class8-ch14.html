Theme E · Economic Life Around Us

India's Urban Landscape

Shehar kaise bante hain, aarthik aur saanskritik kendra kyon hain, unki chunautiyaan, aur Jaipur-Singapore jaisi achchhi planning.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Shehar vs gaon — farak kya hai?

India ki shehri parampara bahut purani hai — Harappa ke Dholavira, Kalibangan, Rakhigarhi se lekar Vijayanagara, Varanasi, Ujjain tak. Urban (shehri) ilaake = zyada aabadi, alag-alag dhandhe aur naukriyan, sarkari sansthaayein, bada transport-communication jaal; chune hue local bodies (nagar palika/nigam) chalaate hain. Rural (graamin) = gaon, kam aabadi, kareebi samudaay, zyaadatar kheti; gram panchayat chalaati hai. Shehar ki paribhasha har desh mein alag: Denmark — 220 log se zyada, Japan — 50,000, India — 5,000!

Population density — kamre mein kitne log?

Ghar par kuch dost aaye to aaram se ghoom sakte ho; par usi kamre mein 30 log thoons do to bheed! Kisi jagah kitni 'bheed' hai, ye maapta hai population density = kul aabadi ÷ kshetrafal (sq km) — yani prati sq km kitne log. Haryana ke gaon Asadpur Khera ki satellite photo mein hare-bhoore khule khet; Gurugram mein bhoore-grey imaaraton ka jaal. 2025 ke sabse bade shehron mein Delhi (~4.19 crore — sabse upar!) aur Kolkata bhi hain.

Shehar badhte kyon hain?

Itihaas mein shehar wahan bane jahan nadi (aasaan transport), vyapaari raaston ka chauraaha (bazaar), aur kheti ki upaj aur kachcha maal paas ho (kaarigar aur udyog). UN ke anusaar 2050 tak duniya ke 68% log shehron mein (abhi 55%). India mein shehri aabadi 17% (1951) → ~40% (2025). Urbanisation ka safar: kheti wala gaon → nayi factory, hotel, bank, sarkari daftar → doosre-teesre sector (udyog, transport, sevaayein) → log aate hain, kaam ki vividhta → chhota kasba/vyapaari kendra → metropolitan shehar (oonchi imaaratein, mall, metro).

Sakchi kaise bana Jamshedpur?

Chota Nagpur Plateau ka chhota gaon Sakchi — paas mein loha (iron ore) — isliye yahan Asia ka pehla steel plant laga. Tata parivaar ka sapna tha ki karmachaariyon ke liye aadhunik suvidhaon wala shehar bane; Sir Dorabji Tata ne steel banne se pehle hi aspataal bana diya! 1919 mein naam Jamshedpur — aaj Jharkhand ka sabse bada audyogik shehar; chaudi ped-lagi sadkein, baag, khuli jagah.

Har shehar ki apni pehchaan

Mumbai — Arabian Sea ka bandargah; 1875 mein Bombay Stock Exchange → desh ki aarthik raajdhani; film udyog 1910s mein shuru, 1930s tak chamka; Bandra-Worli Sea Link, Coastal Road, BKC. Kolkata — poorvi tat ka bandargah. Bengaluru — 'Silicon Valley of India'; software, startups, university; AI tak. Hyderabad — technology. Chhote vishesh shehar: Moradabad (peetal), Ludhiana, Tirupur (kapda), Jaipur (hastkala, ratna-gehne), Channapatna (lakdi ke khilone), Kanchipuram (resham saadi). Kochi, Indore, Lucknow, Nagpur — kharcha kam, isliye companiyan godaam-daftar kholti hain. Paryatan/teerth: Agra (Taj — 70–80 lakh log/saal), Amritsar, Varanasi.

Delhi aur NCR

Delhi Iron Age se sanskriti, vyapaar aur prashasan ka kendra — Rajput, Turk, Mughal ne qile-sadak-bazaar banaaye; angrezon ne nayi raajdhani, rail, bijli. Azaadi ke baad mantralay, udyog, university — log umad pade; ghar, paani, sewer par dabaav. Isliye National Capital Region (NCR) — NOIDA (New Okhla Industrial Development Authority), Ghaziabad; highway aur metro. 1990s se Gurugram — IT aur BPO (Business Process Outsourcing — kisi company ka kaam doosri company se karwaana) ka hub.

Shehar = sanskritiyon ki khichdi

Alag-alag ilaakon ke log aate hain to bhasha, khaana, tyohaar mil jaate hain. Vada pav — Maharashtrian aaloo vada + pav (pav aur aaloo Portuguese laaye the!); Mumbai ke kapda mill mazdooron ka pasandeeda — banana, le jaana, khaana sab aasaan. Kolkata — Indian Museum, Victoria Memorial; Durga Puja — 2021 mein UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage. Shehar library, museum, stadium, music festival banaate hain; Delhi ka Pradhanmantri Sangrahalaya — pradhan mantriyon ki kahani.

Shehron ki chunautiyaan

Zyada log → khaana, paani, bijli, ghar ki zyada maang. Tooti-patli sadkein, bina planning ke bastiyaan, gaadiyon ki bheed, 'heat islands' (shehar gaon se zyada garam), kachra. NCR mein AQI aksar 400 se upar, jabki surakshit seema 50! (AQI = PM2.5, PM10, CO, SO₂ jaise pradooshakon ko ek aasaan number mein batata hai — jitna zyada, utna khatarnaak.) Zyada ghanatva aur aarthik asmaanta se apraadh bhi zyada. Slums (jhuggi) — bheed, kachche ghar, paani-safaai ki kami; railway line, factory ke paas khaali zameen par, kyonki sasta ghar nahi. Par jhuggi wale hi sabzi bechte, ghar ka kaam, kapde dhona, construction, plumbing karte hain — shehar chalaate hain! Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana — gareeb parivaaron ko sasta ghar.

Bengaluru ki jheelein — milkar bachaao

Bengaluru mein garmi mein paani ki kami aur barish mein baadh — kharab planning se. Zyada groundwater nikaalna, rainwater harvesting na karna, jheelon par qabza. Kabhi ~1,000 jheelein thin, ab ~200; kai mein sewage-chemical; Bellandur aur Varthur jheelon par jhaag chha gaya, zehreeli gas, yahan tak ki aag lag gayi! Ab NGOs, RWAs (Resident Welfare Associations) aur log milkar gaad nikaal rahe, prakritik cheezon se baandh sudhaar rahe, naaliyan saaf, desi paudhe, machhliyan wapas — jaise Yelahanka Lake.

Jaipur — 'Pink City' ki planning (1727)

Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II ne 1727 mein Vidyadhar Bhattacharya ko mukhya vaastukaar banaya — vaastu shastra aur shilpa shastra ke gyaata. Shehar grid pattern mein — aayataakaar block (chowkri), disha ke hisaab se. Chaudi sadkein, khuli jagah, baag, 9 public chowk (vyapaar-milan, mandir). Suraksha ke liye 20 foot oonchi deewaar, 7 darwaaze. Mukhya sadkon ki imaaratein gulaabi sandstone se — ek jaisi khoobsurat pehchaan → 'Pink City'. Dhoop, taazi hawa, jalaashay-nahar se paani (sookha mausam); bazaaron mein chhaaya wale baraamde; Panna Meena Kund stepwell. Aaj bhi waise hi kaam karta hai!

Singapore — badalti planning ka model

Chhota dweep-desh, 8,000+ log prati sq km, phir bhi achchhi zindagi. Concept Plan — 40 saal aage tak zameen ka upyog; Master Plan — har 5 saal mein update. 1960s ke bheed bhare bandargah se aadhunik shehar. Mixed-use — ghar, daftar, park, museum paas-paas (Marina Bay Sands aur Raffles Place kuch hi km door). MRT metro, bus, cycle path, paidal hare raaste → car kam. Chhaton aur deewaaron par vertical garden → 'garden city', garmi kam. Buzurgon ke ghar aspataal ke paas, childcare, milan-jagah — ise 'blue zone' (jahan log lambi, swasth zindagi jeete hain, jaise Okinawa, Japan) maana jaata hai.

India ke achchhe udaharan aur kal ka shehar

Sabarmati Riverfront (Ahmedabad) — do-manzila nadi kinaare ki sair, park, bazaar, naukaayan, paidal-cycle wala Atal Bridge. M.G. Road (Gangtok) — sirf paidal zone, bahut saaf. Achchhe shehar ke liye: technology (smart traffic, online shikaayat, solar), naagrik bhaagidaari, kachra prabandhan, paani, public transport, sasta ghar, e-governance. Shehar 'jeevit' hote hain — badhte, badalte. Kal ka shehar wo jo vikas aur prakriti ka santulan rakhe, kamzor logon ko na chhode, aur sansaadhan khatam kiye bina suvidha de.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Shehri virasat: Dholavira (48 ha), Kalibangan, Rakhigarhi, Vijayanagara, Varanasi, Ujjain.
  • Urban — local bodies; rural — gram panchayat. Paribhasha: Denmark 220, Japan 50,000, India 5,000.
  • Population density = aabadi ÷ kshetrafal (sq km).
  • 2025 bade shehar: New Delhi (~4.19 crore), Shanghai, Tokyo, Dhaka, Cairo, Kolkata…
  • Shehar ke kaaran: nadi, vyapaari raaste, kachcha maal.
  • UN: 2050 tak 68% shehri (abhi 55%). India: 17% (1951) → ~40% (2025).
  • Sakchi → Jamshedpur (1919); Asia ka pehla steel plant; Dorabji Tata.
  • Mumbai — BSE 1875, aarthik raajdhani, film 1910s. Bengaluru — Silicon Valley. Hyderabad — tech.
  • Vishesh: Moradabad (brass), Ludhiana/Tirupur (textile), Jaipur (handicraft, gems), Channapatna (toys), Kanchipuram (silk). Agra 70–80 lakh paryatak.
  • Delhi → NCR (NOIDA, Ghaziabad), metro; Gurugram — IT/BPO (1990s se).
  • Saanskritik: vada pav (Portuguese pav), Kolkata Durga Puja — UNESCO 2021; Pradhanmantri Sangrahalaya.
  • Chunautiyaan: bheed, unplanned bastiyaan, traffic, heat islands, kachra; NCR AQI 400+ (safe 50); apraadh; slums; PM Awas Yojana.
  • Bengaluru: ~1,000 → ~200 jheelein; Bellandur-Varthur (jhaag, aag); Yelahanka punarjeevan (RWAs, NGOs).
  • Jaipur 1727 — Sawai Jai Singh II + Vidyadhar Bhattacharya; grid, chowkri, 9 chowk, 20 ft deewaar, 7 gate, gulaabi sandstone.
  • Singapore — 8,000+/sq km; Concept Plan 40 saal, Master Plan har 5 saal; mixed-use; MRT; vertical garden; 'garden city'; blue zone (Okinawa ki tarah).
  • India: Sabarmati Riverfront (Atal Bridge), M.G. Road Gangtok (pedestrian-only).

Yaad karne ki trick

Shehar ki paribhasha = "220-50K-5K"

Denmark 220, Japan 50,000, India 5,000.

Shehri aabadi = 17 → 40, duniya 55 → 68

India 1951 mein 17%, 2025 mein ~40%; duniya abhi 55%, 2050 mein 68%.

Jaipur = "27 mein 9-7-20"

1727 — 9 chowk, 7 darwaaze, 20 foot deewaar.

Singapore = "40 ka sapna, 5 ka sudhaar"

Concept Plan 40 saal aage; Master Plan har 5 saal.

Bengaluru jheel = 1000 → 200

Hazaar mein se sirf do sau bachi — 'paanch mein se ek'.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. India mein kisi jagah ko shehar maanne ke liye kam se kam kitni aabadi chahiye?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 5,000 — Denmark mein 220, Japan mein 50,000.

  2. Q2. Population density kaise nikaalte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Aabadi ÷ kshetrafal — Prati sq km log.

  3. Q3. Sakchi gaon ka naya naam kya pada?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Jamshedpur — 1919 mein; Asia ka pehla steel plant.

  4. Q4. Bombay Stock Exchange kab bana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1875 — Isse Mumbai aarthik raajdhani bana.

  5. Q5. Kolkata ki Durga Puja UNESCO Intangible Heritage list mein kab aayi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 2021 — 2021 mein.

  6. Q6. Surakshit AQI seema kitni maani gayi hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) 50 — NCR mein aksar 400 se upar.

  7. Q7. Jaipur ke mukhya vaastukaar kaun the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Vidyadhar Bhattacharya — 1727 mein vaastu aur shilpa shastra ke aadhar par.

  8. Q8. Singapore ka Master Plan kitne saal mein update hota hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 5 — Concept Plan 40 saal aage dekhta hai.

  9. Q9. Gangtok ka M.G. Road kis baat ke liye prasiddh hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Sirf paidal chalne wala saaf zone — Pedestrian-only shopping hub.
