Reshaping India's Political Map

13th–17th sadi: Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Mughal, aur unka mukabla karne wale Rajput, Ahom, Sikh, Gond — naksha kaise badalta raha.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Ye chapter kis samay ki baat karta hai?

Socho ek bada naksha (map) hai jis par har kuch saal mein seemaayein mitaayi aur phir se kheenchi ja rahi hain — yahi is chapter ki kahani hai. Samay hai lagbhag 11th se 17th sadi — ise aksar 'medieval' (madhyakaal = 'do yugon ke beech') kehte hain. Ye shabd asal mein Europe ke itihaas ke liye bana tha, isliye India ke liye poori tarah theek nahi — hamare liye iska seedha matlab hai 11th–17th sadi. Is daur mein Hindu Kush pahadon ke paar se — Central Asia ke Turkic aur Afghan log — baar-baar hamle karne aaye: daulat, zameen aur apne dharm ko failane ke liye.

Delhi Sultanate — 5 vansh, ek ke baad ek

1192 mein Prithviraj Chauhan ki haar ke baad 1206 mein Delhi Sultanate bani. 'Sultan' ek padvi (title) thi. Paanch vansh ne raaj kiya: Mamluk (Slave), Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi. Gaddi ke liye bahut khoon-kharaba hota tha — lagbhag har 3 mein se 2 sultan pichhle ko hata kar gaddi par baithe, isliye ek sultan ka raaj ausatan sirf ~9 saal chala.

Ala-ud-din Khilji aur Muhammad bin Tughlaq

Ala-ud-din Khilji (14th sadi ki shuruaat) ne uttar aur madhya India mein bade abhiyaan chalaye, aur Mongol hamlon ko bhi roka. Usne apne sikke par likhwaya 'Sikander Sani' = doosra Alexander. Uska general Malik Kafur dakshin tak gaya (Srirangam, Madurai, Chidambaram). Muhammad bin Tughlaq ke samay Mauryon ke baad pehli baar lagbhag poora upmahadweep ek shaasak ke neeche aaya. Par uske plan ulte pade: (1) raajdhani Delhi se Daulatabad (Devagiri) le gaya — logon ko 1,000 km se zyada chalna pada, phir wapas Delhi! (2) token currency — taambe ke sikke ko chaandi jitni keemat di; idea aage ka tha (aaj ke note bhi 'token' hain), par log nakli sikke banane lage aur vyapaar bigad gaya.

Timur ka hamla aur Sultanate ka sikudna

1398 mein Central Asia ka kroor vijeta Timur aaya aur Delhi ko loot kar barbaad kar diya. Uske baad Lodi vansh aaya — Sultanate ka aakhri vansh — par tab tak uska ilaaka bahut chhota ho chuka tha. Kuch sultanon ne gair-Muslimon par jizya tax lagaya. Mandiron ki moortiyon ko todna bhi hua — ise iconoclasm kehte hain.

Jinhone haar nahi maani — Sultanate ka virodh

Eastern Ganga raja Narasimhadeva I (Kalinga/Odisha, 13th sadi) ne Sultanate ko roka aur jeet ki yaad mein Konark ka Surya Mandir banwaya. Musunuri Nayakas ne 75 se zyada sardaaron ko jod kar Tughlaq ki sena ko Warangal se bhagaya (~1330–36) — socho, us zamaane mein 75 netaon ko ek karna kitna mushkil hoga! Hoysala (Karnataka; Belur, Halebidu ke mandir) dakshin ke akele azaad raajya rahe; unke naam ki kahani: Sala ne sher se ladkar guru ko bachaya — 'Hoy Sala!' (maaro, Sala!). Mewar ke Rana Kumbha ne Kumbhalgarh qila banaya jiski deewar 36 km lambi hai.

Vijayanagara — kharagosh ne kutton ko bhagaya!

1336 mein do bhai Harihara aur Bukka (pehle Tughlaq ke governor) ne Delhi ko nakaar kar apna raajya banaya. Kahani: Hampi mein unhone dekha ki ek kharagosh mud kar shikari kutton ko daudaa raha hai! Guru Vidyaranya ne kaha — ye himmat ka sanket hai, yahin raajdhani banao. Uttar mein Bahmani Sultanate (1347) dushman tha, jo baad mein 5 Deccan Sultanates mein toot gaya — Bijapur, Golconda, Berar, Ahmednagar, Bidar. Padviyan: Vijayanagara raja = Narapati, Bahmani = Ashwapati, Odisha = Gajapati, Maratha = Chhatrapati ('pati' = maalik).

Krishnadevaraya aur Talikota ki tabaahi

16th sadi mein Krishnadevaraya ke samay Vijayanagara apne shikhar par tha. Wo khud kavi the — Telugu mein 'Amuktamalyada' likha (sant kavi Andal ki kahani; isme Rajaniti — achchhe shaasan ke vichaar). Tirupati aur Vitthala mandir ko daan diya — Vitthala mandir ke 'musical pillars' thapthapaane par sangeet ke sur nikaalte hain! Portuguese yatri Domingo Paes ne likha — shehar Rome jitna bada, 'duniya ka sabse bhara-poora shehar'. Krishnadevaraya ki 1529 mein maut ke baad 1565 mein Deccan Sultanates ne milkar Talikota ki ladaai mein Ramaraya ko haraya; shehar mahinon loota gaya. Baad mein Nayaka chhote-chhote raaj chalaane lage.

Mughal — Babur se Akbar tak

Samarkand se nikaale gaye Babur (Timur ka vanshaj) ne 1526 mein Panipat ki pehli ladaai mein Ibrahim Lodi ko baarood, top aur bandook se haraya — Sultanate khatam, Mughal shuru. Babur ki aatmakatha Baburnama — usme chidiyon, phalon ka shauk bhi aur kroor qatl-e-aam bhi. Bete Humayun ko Sher Shah Suri ne hata diya, par Humayun wapas aaya. Himu (Hemchandra Vikramaditya) ne Delhi pakdi, par 1556 ki Panipat ki doosri ladaai mein ghayal hua aur Akbar ne uska sir kalam karwa diya.

Akbar — pehle kathor, phir sahishnu

13 saal ki umar mein badshah bane. Jawani mein Chittor ke qile ko 5 mahine se zyada ghera; jeet ke baad ~30,000 logon ka qatl karwaya; wahan ki auraton ne jauhar kiya (gulam banne se bachne ke liye aag mein koodna). Baad mein Akbar ne neeti badli: Rajput raajkumariyon se shaadi, Rajputon ko darbar mein jagah, jizya hataya, 'sulh-i-kul' (sabse shaanti) ka vichaar. Khud padh-likh nahi sakte the par Fatehpur Sikri mein anuvaad ghar banaya — Mahabharata ka Persian roop 'Razmnama', Ramayana (176 chitron ke saath), Gita, Panchatantra. Raaj 1556–1605 (~50 saal).

Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb

Jahangir ko kala pasand thi; Shah Jahan ne Taj Mahal banwaya. Aurangzeb ne bade bhai Dara Shikoh ko maar kar, pita Shah Jahan ko Agra Fort mein qaid karke 1658 mein gaddi li, naam rakha 'Alamgir' (duniya ka vijeta). Uske samay Mughal samrajya sabse bada hua, par aakhri 25 saal Deccan ki ladaaiyon mein beete aur khazaana khaali ho gaya. Usne jizya aur teerth-yatra tax wapas lagaya; 1669 ke farman mein mandir-paathshaala todne ka hukm diya (Banaras, Mathura, Somnath). 1707 mein maut ke baad Mughal taakat tezi se giri. Yaad rakho: ye beete zamaane ke logon ki baat hai — aaj ke kisi insaan ko iska dosh nahi diya ja sakta.

Mughalon ka virodh — veer kahaniyaan

Jat kisaanon ki bagawat (20,000 log). Bhil, Gond, Santhal, Koch jaise adivasi samooh. Rani Durgavati (Garha, Gond raajya) — 20,000 sainik, 1,000 haathi; 1564 mein Akbar ke general se ladeen, ghayal hokar bhi aatmasamarpan nahi kiya — sirf 40 saal ki thin. Rana Sanga — Khanwa mein Babur se haare. Maharana Pratap — 1576 Haldighati; bach nikle aur saalon tak Aravalli se guerrilla yuddh (chhupkar achaanak hamla) kiya; Bhil teerandaazon ne madad ki, isliye Mewar ke chinh mein ek Bhil yoddha hai. Durga Das Rathore ne Jodhpur ki azaadi bachaayi.

Ahom aur Sikh

Ahom 13th sadi mein Myanmar se Brahmaputra ghaati aaye. Paik system: har tandurust aadmi raajya ke liye mazdoori ya sena ki seva karta, badle mein zameen milti — bina sthaayi sena ke badi fauj! 1671 Saraighat (Guwahati ke paas, Brahmaputra par) — Lachit Borphukan ke 10,000 sainikon ne 30,000 ki Mughal sena ko haraya. Guru Nanak (15th sadi) — Ik Onkar, barabari, daya. Guru Arjan ne Guru Granth Sahib sankalit kiya; Jahangir ne unhe yaatna dekar maara. Guru Hargobind ne sena banayi. 1675 — Guru Tegh Bahadur ne Kashmiri Pandits ki raksha ke liye dharm badalne se mana kiya; Chandni Chowk mein sir kaata gaya (Gurudwara Sis Ganj Sahib). Guru Gobind Singh ne 1699 mein Khalsa banaya. Maharaja Ranjit Singh ne 1799 mein Sikh samrajya banaya.

Raaj kaise chalta tha aur log kaise jeete the?

Sultanate: iqta system — sardaaron (iqtadar) ko ilaaka diya, wo tax wasool kar sena chalaate; pad khandaani nahi tha. Akbar: mantri — Diwan (paisa), Mir Bakhshi (sena), Khan-i-Saman (public works, vyapaar, raaj ghar), Sadr (nyay, dharm, shiksha); 12 subah (praant). Mansabdari: har afsar (mansabdar) ka rank (mansab) tay karta ki wo kitne ghode-haathi-sainik rakhega; tankhwah zameen (jagir) ke roop mein. Todar Mal ne zameen ka survey karke lagaan system sudhara. Kheti mukhya — lagaan aam taur par 1/5, kabhi 1/2 tak. Persian wheel se sinchaai. Mughal sikke: chaandi ka rupaya, taambe ka dam. Hundi = likhit bhugtaan aadesh, bina paisa uthaye paisa bhejna — aaj ki banking ka purvaj. Mandir bhi bazaar, karz aur dharmshaala ke kendra the.

  • Sultanate ke 5 vansh: Mamluk/Slave → Khilji → Tughlaq → Sayyid → Lodi (Turkic-Afghan). Sultan ka ausat raaj ~9 saal.
  • Ala-ud-din Khilji — 'Sikander Sani'; Mongol hamle roke; Malik Kafur dakshin tak.
  • Muhammad bin Tughlaq — raajdhani Delhi → Daulatabad (Devagiri) → Delhi; token currency (taamba); Mauryon ke baad pehli baar lagbhag poora upmahadweep.
  • Timur (1398) — Delhi loot; uske baad Lodi antim vansh.
  • Virodh: Narasimhadeva I (Eastern Ganga) → Konark Surya mandir; Musunuri Nayakas (75+ sardar, Warangal ~1330–36); Hoysala (Belur, Halebidu); Rana Kumbha → Kumbhalgarh (36 km deewar).
  • Vijayanagara — Harihara & Bukka, guru Vidyaranya, raajdhani Hampi; Virupaksha mandir.
  • Bahmani → 5 Deccan Sultanates: Bijapur, Golconda, Berar, Ahmednagar, Bidar. Padvi: Narapati, Ashwapati, Gajapati, Chhatrapati.
  • Krishnadevaraya — Amuktamalyada (Telugu, Andal); Vitthala mandir ke musical pillars; Domingo Paes. Maut 1529; Talikota 1565 (Ramaraya haare).
  • Babur — Panipat-I 1526, Ibrahim Lodi ko haraya (baarood, field artillery, matchlock); Baburnama. Sher Shah Suri — Sur Empire. Himu = Hemchandra Vikramaditya, Panipat-II 1556.
  • Akbar (1556–1605) — Chittor; phir sulh-i-kul, jizya khatam, Fatehpur Sikri (Panch Mahal), anuvaad: Razmnama (Mahabharata). Itihaskar Abul Fazl — Ain-i-Akbari.
  • Shah Jahan — Taj Mahal. Aurangzeb 1658 'Alamgir'; Dara Shikoh ko maara; jizya + pilgrimage tax wapas; 1669 farman; maut 1707.
  • Rani Durgavati (Garha, Gond; 1564). Rana Sanga — Khanwa (Babur). Maharana Pratap — Haldighati 1576, guerrilla yuddh, Bhil saathi. Durga Das Rathore (Marwar).
  • Ahom — Myanmar se; paik system; Lachit Borphukan, Saraighat 1671 (10,000 vs 30,000); raajdhani Garhgaon.
  • Sikh: Guru Nanak (Ik Onkar) → Guru Arjan (Guru Granth Sahib; Jahangir) → Guru Hargobind (sena) → Guru Tegh Bahadur 1675 (Sis Ganj Sahib) → Guru Gobind Singh Khalsa 1699 → Ranjit Singh.
  • Prashasan: iqta (Sultanate); Akbar ke Diwan, Mir Bakhshi, Khan-i-Saman, Sadr; 12 subah; mansabdari/jagirdari; Todar Mal ka revenue survey.
  • Arthvyavastha: shrenis (guilds), jatis, hundi, Marwari vyapaari; bandargah — Calicut, Mangalore, Surat, Masulipatnam, Hooghly; Mughal rupaya (chaandi) + dam (taamba); mandir arthik kendra (daan, dharmshaala, chhatram).

Yaad karne ki trick

Sultanate ke 5 vansh = "MaKhi TuSaLo"

Mamluk, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi — 'Makhi Tu Sa Lo' (makkhi tu sa lo!).

5 Deccan Sultanates = "BiGo BAB"

Bijapur, Golconda, Berar, Ahmednagar, Bidar.

Teen ladaaiyan, teen 6

Panipat-I 1526, Panipat-II 1556, Talikota 1565 — sab mein 6!

Akbar ke 4 mantri = "DMKS"

Diwan (paisa), Mir Bakhshi (military), Khan-i-Saman (kaam-kaaj), Sadr (nyay-shiksha).

Saraighat: 1-3 ka khel

Lachit ke 10 hazaar ne 30 hazaar ko haraya, saal 1671.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Delhi Sultanate ka pehla vansh kaunsa tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Mamluk (Slave) — Kram: Mamluk → Khilji → Tughlaq → Sayyid → Lodi.

  2. Q2. Kis sultan ne raajdhani Delhi se Daulatabad le gaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Muhammad bin Tughlaq — Plan fail hua aur raajdhani wapas Delhi aayi.

  3. Q3. Konark ka Surya mandir kisne banwaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Narasimhadeva I — Eastern Ganga raja Narasimhadeva I ne, jeet ki yaad mein.

  4. Q4. Vijayanagara ke sansthapak kaun the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Harihara aur Bukka — 1336 mein; guru Vidyaranya ki salaah par Hampi.

  5. Q5. Talikota ki ladaai kab hui?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1565 — 1565 mein Deccan Sultanates ne Vijayanagara ko haraya.

  6. Q6. 'Sulh-i-kul' kis shaasak ki neeti thi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Akbar — Arth: sabse shaanti / sabhi dharmon ke prati sahishnuta.

  7. Q7. Saraighat ki ladaai mein Ahom senapati kaun the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Lachit Borphukan — 1671 mein Brahmaputra par Mughalon ko haraya.

  8. Q8. Mansabdari system kisne shuru kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Akbar — Mansab = rank; vetan jagir ke roop mein.

  9. Q9. Khalsa ki sthaapna kab hui?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1699 — Guru Gobind Singh ne 1699 mein.
