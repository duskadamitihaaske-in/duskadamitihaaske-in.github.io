Theme E · Economic Life Around Us

Factors of Production

Koi bhi cheez banane ke 4 input — Land, Labour, Capital, Entrepreneurship — aur Technology jo inhe aur taaqatwar banati hai.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Ratna ka dhaba — production ki poori kahani

Tumhare kapde, joote, bag, phone — sab kisi production process se guzar kar tum tak aaye. Jo cheezein (inputs) maal ya seva banane mein lagti hain, unhe factors of production kehte hain. Misaal: Ratna ka highway par chhota restaurant 'Pause Point'. Shuru karne ke liye use chahiye tha: ek jagah (land), khaana banane-parosne wale log (labour — ab 7 log), bartan-furniture-gas aur kiraaye ke liye paisa (capital), aur ye sab jodne wala dimaag aur himmat — khud Ratna (entrepreneur). Aise kaam karne wali dukaan, factory, firm ko business / enterprise kehte hain.

1. Land — sirf zameen nahi!

Economics mein 'land' ka matlab sirf zameen ka tukda nahi — mitti, jungle, paani, hawa, dhoop, khanij, tel, gas — saare prakritik sansaadhan. (Chapter 1 yaad karo!) Business zameen ya to khareedta hai ya kiraaye (rent) par leta hai.

2. Labour aur Human Capital — mehnat vs hunar

Labour = production mein lagne wali shaaririk aur maansik mehnat — kisaan, mazdoor, badhai, teacher, doctor. Human capital = us mehnat ki quality — khaas skill, gyaan, kaabiliyat, anubhav. Do log ek jaisi mehnat karein par jiske paas behtar hunar hai, wo zyada achchha aur tez kaam karega. Human capital badhaane wale 3 facilitators: (a) shiksha aur training — civil engineering ka student design padhta hai, phir site par training se seekhta hai; (b) swasthya — swasth bachcha roz school aata aur behtar seekhta hai (cognitive vikas), swasth mazdoor zyada kaam karta; (c) samaajik-saanskritik maahaul — Japan ka kaizen ('lagaataar sudhaar', 1940s se), Germany ka work ethic (samay ki paabandi, detail, quality).

India ki takat aur chunautiyaan

Adult literacy (15+ umar, rozmarra ki chhoti baat padh-likh sake) — 2023 mein purush ~85%, mahila ~70% (World Bank). Economic Survey 2024: ~65% Bharatiya 35 saal se kam umar ke! Isse demographic dividend mil sakta hai — jab kaam karne wale zyada aur un par nirbhar kam hon to desh tezi se badhta hai. Par shart: achchhi shiksha, swasthya, training. Socho Harsh ne pita ki naukri jaane par school chhoda — badaa hokar uske hunar par kya asar padega? Aur yaad rakho — har kaam zaroori hai: safaai karmachari, kisaan, doctor, koi na ho to kya hoga?

Hamari purani 'skill' parampara

Purane India mein kaam ek saadhna tha — poori lagan se, devta ya grahak ko arpan ki tarah. Auzaaron ki pooja hoti thi — aaj bhi Vishwakarma puja / Ayudha puja. Kaam = kala (art) + vidya (knowledge), pidhi dar pidhi. Shilpa shastra mein moorti, chitra, imaarat, lakdi aur gehno ke naap, mudra, rang tak likhe the. Mandir banane wale shilpi parivaar aksar tayyar mandir dekh bhi nahi paate the, phir bhi utkrishtata ke liye kaam karte. Stitched shipbuilding — 2,000 saal se zyada purani takneek: lakdi ke takhte keelon (nails) ki jagah rassi se silkar jahaz banaye jaate, jo lachiley hone se Hind Mahasagar mein aaram se chalte. 16th sadi mein Europeans ke aane ke baad ye ghata; aaj sirf chhoti machhli-naav mein.

3. Capital — paisa aur machine

Capital = paisa + insaan ki banayi tikaau cheezein jo production mein kaam aayein: machine, auzaar, gaadi, thela, computer, dukaan, factory. Paisa kahan se? Pehle apni bachat, parivaar, dost. Kam pade to bank loan — Ratna ne bhi liya aur kisht ke saath interest (byaaj) chukaaya. Badi companiyan stock market mein shares bech kar janta se paisa uthaati hain, aur munaafe mein se dividend deti hain.

4. Entrepreneurship — sapna + jokhim

Entrepreneur (udyami) wo jo: ek samasya pehchaane aur naye hal ke liye adig rahe, paisa-samay laga kar risk le, baaki teen factors ko jode, zaroori faisle le, aur apne naye vichaar se samaaj ka bhala kare. Wo naukri bhi paida karta hai. Startup = kam saadhanon se, technology ke sahare tezi se badhne wala naya udyam. Misaal: J.R.D. Tata (janm 1904) — 1932 mein India ki pehli airline Tata Airlines (aage chal kar Air India); steel, car, bijli, chemicals; mazdooron ka khayal; 1992 Bharat Ratna.

Technology — madadgaar (enabler)

Technology = vaigyanik gyaan ka istemal. Isse kam input mein zyada maal banta hai. UPI se ek click mein payment, mausam ki pehle soochna, GPS se chhota raasta, drone se khaad ka chhidkaav, surgery mein robot. Chitthi ki jagah email. Par har purani cheez nahi hat-ti — pulley aur wheelbarrow aaj bhi kaam aate hain. Online seekhne ke liye SWAYAM (Class 9 se, muft MOOCs — robotics, aquaculture, textile printing), naukri dhoondhne ke liye National Career Service portal.

Sab factors kaise jude hain?

Ye sab puzzle ke tukdon jaise milkar kaam karte hain; ek bhi gaayab ho to kaam ruk jaaye. Kheti, construction, hastkala — zyada mazdoor chahiye (labour-intensive); semiconductor chip, satellite — zyada machine-paisa (capital-intensive). Nayi takneek anupaat badal deti hai — kheti mein machine se mazdoor kam; 3-D printing se marti hastkalaon ka bada utpaadan. Supply chain = utpaadan se bikri tak ka poora network; door ke inputs par nirbharta se COVID-19 mein utpaadan ruk gaya. 2025 mein India duniya ka doosra sabse bada mobile phone nirmaata (China ke baad) — R&D → zameen/factory → kachcha maal → assembly + software → testing → mass production → dukaanon tak.

Zimmedari — prakriti aur mazdoor dono ke prati

Tamil Nadu ki chamda factories naukri deti hain par kachra nadi-mitti ganda karta hai; puraane phone recycle na hon to lead aur mercury zameen-paani mein. Isliye producer ko sansaadhan sambhal kar, kachra kam, pradooshan rokna chahiye. Mazdooron ke liye: uchit vetan aur surakshit maahaul, skill training, adhikaar aur suraksha (bhedbhaav nahi, health care, chhutti). CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) — 2014 mein India duniya ka pehla desh bana jisne kaanoon banaya: companiyan pichhle 3 saal ke ausat munaafe ka 2% samaaj-kalyan par kharch karein.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Factors of production = Land, Labour, Capital, Entrepreneurship; Technology = enabler/facilitator.
  • Caselet: Ratna ka restaurant Pause Point (7 log) — location, paisa, staff, saamaan, yojana.
  • Land = zameen + sabhi prakritik sansaadhan (mitti, jungle, paani, hawa, dhoop, khanij, tel, gas); khareedo ya rent.
  • Labour = shaaririk + maansik mehnat; Human capital = skill, gyaan, expertise — mehnat ki quality aur efficiency.
  • Human capital ke facilitators: education & training, healthcare, social-cultural (Japan — kaizen, mid-1940s; Germany — work ethic).
  • Adult literacy 2023: purush ~85%, mahila ~70% (World Bank).
  • Economic Survey 2024: ~65% log 35 saal se kam → demographic dividend.
  • Prachin parampara: Vishwakarma/Ayudha puja, kala + vidya, shilpa shastra; stitched shipbuilding (2000+ saal; rassi se silaai, keelon (nails) ke bina) — 16th c. ke baad girawat.
  • Capital = paisa + tikaau maanav-nirmit asset (machine, auzaar, gaadi, computer, factory). Srot: bachat, parivaar, bank loan (interest), stock market (shares, dividend).
  • Entrepreneur: samasya pehchaane, risk le, factors jode, faisle le, samaaj ka bhala kare. Startup.
  • J.R.D. Tata — 1904; Tata Airlines 1932 → Air India; Bharat Ratna 1992.
  • Technology: UPI, GPS, mausam alert, drone, robot surgery; pulley-wheelbarrow abhi bhi. SWAYAM (MOOCs, Class 9+), National Career Service.
  • Labour-intensive (kheti, construction, hastkala) vs capital-intensive (chips, satellite). 3-D printing se handloom.
  • Supply chain — COVID-19 mein rukaawat. India — 2nd largest mobile maker (2025).
  • Zimmedari: Tamil Nadu chamda udyog ka pradooshan; e-waste (lead, mercury); mazdoor — fair pay, training, adhikaar.
  • CSR law 2014 — duniya mein pehla; pichhle 3 saal ke ausat munaafe ka 2%.

Enterprise

Human capital
Logon ka gyaan, hunar, anubhav jo kaam ki quality badhaaye.
Training
Kisi khaas kaam ke liye zaroori skill seekhna.
Cognitive
Seekhne, jaanne aur samajhne se juda.
Kaizen
Japanese vichaar — lagaataar chhote sudhaar.
Productivity

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Economics mein 'land' mein kya shaamil NAHI hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Machine — Machine capital hai; land = prakritik sansaadhan.

  2. Q2. 'Human capital' ka sahi arth kya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Logon ka hunar, gyaan aur anubhav — Ye mehnat ki quality aur efficiency hai.

  3. Q3. 'Kaizen' kis desh ka vichaar hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Japan — Arth: lagaataar sudhaar; mid-1940s se.

  4. Q4. Economic Survey 2024 ke anusaar kitne pratishat Bharatiya 35 se kam umar ke hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 65% — Isse demographic dividend ki sambhavna.

  5. Q5. Shareholder ko company ke munaafe se diya gaya hissa kya kehlaata hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Dividend — Loan par byaaj = interest; share par munaafa = dividend.

  6. Q6. J.R.D. Tata ne India ki pehli airline kab shuru ki?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1932 — Tata Airlines 1932 — baad mein Air India.

  7. Q7. SWAYAM kya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Free online courses ka sarkari platform — Study Webs of Active Learning for Young Aspiring Minds.

  8. Q8. Satellite banana kis tarah ka utpaadan hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Capital-intensive — Zyada paisa aur specialised machinery chahiye.

  9. Q9. CSR kaanoon ke anusaar companiyon ko munaafe ka kitna hissa kharch karna hota hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 2% — 2014 ka kaanoon — pichhle 3 saal ke ausat munaafe ka 2%.
