Theme B · Tapestry of the Past

India's Long Road to Independence

1858 se 1947 tak — sudhaarak, Congress, Swadeshi, krantikaari, Gandhi ke andolan, Bose ki INA, Quit India, aur Azaadi ke saath Vibhajan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Itni bhashaaein, itne dharm — phir bhi ek!

Socho ek desh jahan saikdon bhashaayein, alag-alag dharm aur reeti-riwaaj hon — phir bhi sab milkar duniya ke sabse taaqatwar samrajya ko chunauti dein. Yahi hai Bharat ki azaadi ki kahani. ICHR ki 'Dictionary of Martyrs' mein ~14,000 shaheedon ke naam hain — is chapter mein bahut kam naam hain, par jo naam nahi hain wo kam mahaan nahi. Sabka lakshya ek tha, par raaste alag: Moderates, Nationalists, krantikaari, Gandhi ka satyagraha.

1858 ki 'shaahi ghoshna' — vaade aur sachchaai

1858 mein Queen Victoria ke naam se raaj: London mein Secretary of State for India, India mein Viceroy (Governor General). Vaada kiya — ~560 rajaon ka samman (par 'paramountcy' angrezon ki), praja ki barabar dekhbhaal, zabardasti Isaai dharm nahi. Sachchaai: rajaon ke darbar mein Resident dakhal deta; shoshan, akaal jaari; Bharatiyon ko kam tankhwah, club-train mein alag; 1878 — Arms Act (Bharatiya hathiyaar nahi rakh sakte, angrez rakh sakte) aur Vernacular Press Act (Bharatiya bhasha ke akhbaaron par sensor).

Samaaj sudhaarak — andar se badlaav

Brahmo Samaj (1828, Calcutta) — Raja Ram Mohan Roy: baal vivah aur jaati-bhed ka virodh, vidhvaaon ke adhikaar, ladkiyon ki shiksha. Arya Samaj (1875, Bombay) — Swami Dayananda Saraswati: 'Vedon ki or lauto', janm-aadharit jaati ka virodh, vidhva punarvivah, stri shiksha. Shuddhi andolan. Aur Iswar Chandra Vidyasagar, Syed Ahmad Khan, Jyotirao aur Savitribai Phule — ladkiyon aur pichhde vargon ke liye school.

Do prernaayein — 'Vande Mataram' aur Vivekananda

Bankim Chandra ne 1875 mein 'Bande Mataram' likha (Bengali mein 'va' nahi hota, isliye 'Ba'), 1882 ke Anandamath mein joda. Tagore ne 1896 Congress session mein gaaya; 1950 mein rashtriya geet. Swami Vivekananda (Narendranath Datta, janm 1863; guru Sri Ramakrishna) — 1893 Chicago ki Parliament of World's Religions mein prabhaavi bhaashan; Ramakrishna Mission; 'Utho, jaago aur lakshya tak mat ruko'; Jamsetji Tata ko research sanstha ki salaah di — wahi aage Indian Institute of Science bani. Janmdin 12 January = National Youth Day.

Congress ka janm aur badhta gussa

Angrez afsar A.O. Hume ne naukri chhodi aur 1885 mein Bombay mein Indian National Congress ka pehla session hua — Dadabhai Naoroji saath, pehle adhyaksh W.C. Bonnerjee, 72 delegates. Shuru mein Congress British raaj ki 'wafaadaar' thi aur sirf chhote sudhaar maangti thi — angrezon ne zyaadatar ansuna kiya. Gussa badha: Kuka/Namdhari andolan (Baba Ram Singh, Punjab) — videshi maal ka bahishkaar, apni daak aur adaalat (baad ke boycott se pehle hi!); 1872 mein 65 Kukas ko bina mukadme top se udaya gaya, Baba Ram Singh ko Burma bhej diya. 1897 Pune mein plague ke zulm par Chapekar bhaiyon ne Plague Commissioner ko maara. Tilak ne 'Kesari' akhbaar, Ganesh Chaturthi aur Shivaji utsav se logon ko jagaaya — 'Lokmanya'; 'Gita Rahasya'. Birsa Munda — Ulgulaan (1899–1900), 'Mahaan Halchal'; 24 saal ki umar mein jail mein mrityu.

1905 — Bengal ka bantwara aur Swadeshi

Viceroy Curzon ne 1905 mein Bengal baanta — bahaana 'achchha prashasan', asal mein divide and rule (Muslim-bahul poorv, Hindu-bahul pashchim). Logon ne ek-doosre ko rakhi baandhi, sadkon par Vande Mataram gaaya (angrezon ne rok lagaayi!). Do hathiyaar: Swadeshi (apne desh ka maal) aur Boycott (videshi kapda, cheeni ki holi; sarkari school-court ka bahishkaar). Swadeshi pehle se: Jamsetji Tata — Swadeshi Mills 1886; Punjab National Bank 1895 (Lala Lajpat Rai); P.C. Ray — Bengal Chemicals 1901. 1906: Bipin Chandra Pal ka akhbaar 'Bande Mataram' (Aurobindo sampaadak) — 'absolute national autonomy'; Bengal National College (principal Aurobindo; ab Jadavpur University); Calcutta Congress (Naoroji) ne Swaraj, Swadeshi, Boycott, National Education apnaaya. 'Bharat Mata' — Abanindranath Tagore ki painting, Subramania Bharati ki kavitaayein. Usi saal Dhaka mein All-India Muslim League bani. 1911 mein bantwara radd, raajdhani Calcutta se Delhi.

Surat 1907 — Moderates vs Nationalists

Surat session (1907) mein Congress do tukdon mein bant gayi. Moderates (Naoroji, Gokhale, Pherozeshah Mehta, Surendranath Banerjee, Rashbehari Ghosh) — chhote sudhaar, British samrajya ke andar dheere swashaasan. Nationalists — Lal-Bal-Pal (Lala Lajpat Rai 'Lion of Punjab', Bal Gangadhar Tilak, Bipin Chandra Pal) aur Aurobindo — poori azaadi. Angrez unhe 'Extremists' kehte, par wo khud ko bas 'Nationalists' kehte the.

Krantikaari — jaan hatheli par

Anushilan Samiti (1902), Abhinav Bharat (1904, V.D. Savarkar), akhbaar 'Yugantar'. 1908: Khudiram Bose (18 saal) aur Prafulla Chaki ne jaj Kingsford par bomb phenka (galti se do angrez mahilaayein mareen); Khudiram ko phaansi — muskurate hue. Alipore Bomb Case — Aurobindo ka mukadma C.R. Das ne lada, Aurobindo bari; Barin ko Cellular Jail. Tilak ko 6 saal Mandalay (vakeel Jinnah). Tilak: 'Swaraj mera janmsiddh adhikaar hai aur main use lekar rahunga.' 1910 mein Aurobindo Pondicherry gaye — 'Sri Aurobindo', integral yoga. 1909 Nashik (Anant Kanhere), 1911 Vanchinathan (V.O. Chidambaram Pillai ki Swadeshi Steam Navigation ke liye), 1912 Viceroy Hardinge par Delhi mein bomb. Ghadar Party (America; Lala Hardayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna) — 1914–15 sena mein vidroh ki koshish; Kartar Singh Sarabha (19 saal) ko phaansi. Rash Behari Bose Japan chale gaye. Bagha Jatin — 9 Sept 1915, Balasore mein 75 minute ki golibaari. Cellular Jail ('Kala Pani') — Andaman mein ekaant qaid, haath se tel nikalwaana.

Poorvottar ka yogdaan

Khasi vidroh (1829–33) — Tirot Sing; Singpho vidroh (Assam); 1857 mein Maniram Dewan aur Piyali Baruah ko phaansi; 1942 mein Kanaklata Barua aur Kushal Konwar shaheed; Manipur ki Rani Gaidinliu — 13 saal ki umar se angrezon se ladeen.

Home Rule aur Gandhi ka aagman

1916: Lucknow mein Congress phir ek; Lucknow Pact — Congress ne Muslim League ki separate electorates maani. Tilak aur Annie Besant ka Home Rule Movement — samrajya ke andar swashaasan. M.K. Gandhi (janm 1869, Porbandar) — South Africa mein rang-bhed jhela, satyagraha ('satya ka aagrah' — ahinsa + kasht sehna) banaaya; 1915 mein India lautey. Local satyagraha: Champaran (Bihar, neel kisaan), Ahmedabad mill mazdoor, Kheda (lagaan). Bardoli 1928 — Vallabhbhai Patel → 'Sardar'.

1919 — Rowlatt Act aur Jallianwala Bagh

World War I ke baad azaadi ki jagah Rowlatt Act (1919) — police kisi ko bhi bina mukadme qaid kar sake. 13 April 1919, Baisakhi, Amritsar: shaant bheed par General Dyer ne mukhya raasta band karke ~10 minute mein 1,650 goliyaan chalwaayin; log kuein mein koode. Angrezi aankda ~400 mare, 1,200+ ghayal; Bharatiya andaaza 1,000+. Dyer = 'Butcher of Amritsar'. Tagore ne knighthood lauta di. Britain ne aaj tak maafi nahi maangi. 20 saal baad Udham Singh ne London mein O'Dwyer ko maara.

Non-Cooperation aur Khilafat (1920–22)

Gandhi ne Rowlatt ke khilaaf upvaas-prarthana, phir Non-Cooperation — videshi maal, school, court ka bahishkaar, khadi (haath ka suta-buna kapda) — 'ek saal mein Swaraj'. Aug 1920 mein Tilak ki mrityu; Nagpur session (Dec 1920) mein aupchaarik shuruaat. Khilafat (1919) — Ali bhai aur Maulana Abul Kalam Azad; Turkey ke Khalifa ke samarthan mein; Gandhi ne Hindu-Muslim ekta ke liye joda. Jinnah ne 1920 mein Congress chhodi (dharm aur azaadi ko mat milao). Feb 1922 Chauri Chaura (UP) — bheed ne thana jalaaya, policewale mare → Gandhi ne andolan wapas le liya (Motilal, Jawaharlal, C.R. Das, Subhas naaraaz). Motilal-Das ki Swaraj Party. 1924 mein Turkey ne Khilafat hi khatam kar di.

Simon Go Back, Bhagat Singh aur Purna Swaraj

1927 Simon Commission — ek bhi Bharatiya sadasya nahi → 'Simon Go Back!'. Lahore 1928 mein Lala Lajpat Rai par lathi — 'mujh par pada har vaar angrezi raaj ke taboot ki aakhri keel hoga'; 2 hafte baad mrityu. Kakori (1925) — HRA ke Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Chandrashekhar Azad ne sarkari khazaana loota; Bismil-Ashfaq ko phaansi. HSRA — Bhagat Singh ne Assembly mein 'behron ko sunaane' ke liye bina nuksaan wale bomb phenke; 1931 mein Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev, Rajguru ko phaansi (22–23 saal). Chhaatraayein bhi — Suniti Chowdhury, Shanti Ghosh, Bina Das. 1928 Nehru Report — Dominion status. Dec 1929 Lahore session — Purna Swaraj (poorn azaadi) ka prastaav (Jawaharlal Nehru).

Namak se hili sarkar — Dandi March

Namak par sarkar ka ekaadhikaar aur bhaari tax tha. March–April 1930 — Gandhi ki 380 km ki Dandi yatra (dakshin Gujarat) aur mutthi bhar namak — Civil Disobedience shuru. Hazaaron giraftaar; mahilaayein parde se nikal kar aayin. Sarojini Naidu — Congress ki pehli Bharatiya mahila adhyaksh (1925). Peshawar, 23 April 1930, Qissa Khwani Bazaar — Abdul Ghaffar Khan ki giraftaari par shaant bheed par goli; Chandra Singh Garhwali ke Garhwal Rifles ne goli chalaane se inkaar kar diya. 1931 Gandhi-Irwin Pact → Round Table Conference (London). 1934 andolan wapas. 1935 Government of India Act — prantiya swayatta. Poona Pact — Ambedkar separate electorate chahte the, Gandhi ne anshan kiya; samjhauta: aarakshit seatein badhin.

Do-rashtra ka vichaar, World War II aur Netaji

1930 mein Muhammad Iqbal ne uttar-pashchim mein Muslimon ke alag kshetra ki baat ki; 1940 Lahore Resolution (Jinnah) — Muslim-bahul rajyon ki maang. Congress ke Maulana Azad, Abdul Ghaffar Khan do-rashtra ke khilaaf. World War II (1939) — India se poochhe bina shaamil; 25 lakh+ Bharatiya sainik (itihaas ki sabse badi swayamsevi sena), 87,000 mare; Churchill ke hukm se anaaj bahar → 1943 Bengal akaal, laakhon mare. Subhas Chandra Bose — 1941 mein nazarbandi se nikal kar Afghanistan-Russia se Germany, phir 1943 Singapore: Indian National Army (INA) aur Azad Hind sarkar; Burma, Manipur, Nagaland mein lade, haare (1944).

Quit India — 'Karo ya Maro' (1942)

Cripps Mission (1942) — yuddh ke baad dominion status, par praant alag ho sakte → asweekaar. August 1942 — Gandhi: 'Do or Die', angrez turant jaayein. Saare bade neta giraftaar, phir bhi janta ne andolan chalaaya; Ram Manohar Lohia, Jayaprakash Narayan ne bhoomigat netritva kiya. Aruna Asaf Ali ne Bombay ke Gowalia Tank Maidan mein jhanda fehraaya. Assam ki Kanaklata Barua (17) Gohpur thane par jhanda lagaate shaheed — 'Birangana'. Viceroy Linlithgow: '1857 ke baad sabse gambhir vidroh'. 1 lakh+ jail, ~10,000 mare.

Azaadi aur Vibhajan (1947)

1945 ke baad Britain thak gaya; Jan 1946 Air Force aur Feb 1946 Royal Indian Navy (Bombay) mein vidroh — sena par bharosa khatam. INA trials. Cabinet Mission 1946 — gatirodh. 16 Aug 1946 — Jinnah ka 'Direct Action Day': 'ya to bataa hua India, ya barbaad India'; Calcutta mein bhayanak danga. Sept 1946 — Nehru ki Interim Government. Mountbatten ne Cyril Radcliffe ko sirf 5 hafte mein seema kheenchne ko diya — Radcliffe Line ne Punjab-Bengal ko cheer diya; Pakistan = West Pakistan + East Pakistan (1971 se Bangladesh). Laakhon visthaapit, bhayanak hinsa. 15 August 1947 — aadhi raat ko azaadi; Nehru: 'Tryst with destiny'. Sardar Patel ne 560+ riyasaton ko samjha-bujha kar (zaroorat par sena se) India mein milaaya — 'Iron Man of India'. Azaadi sirf ek vyakti ya tareeke se nahi — Gandhi, krantikaari, vidroh, sena ke mutiny, World War ke baad Britain ki kamzori — sab milkar.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Indian National Congress ka pehla session kahan hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Bombay — 1885, Bombay; pehle adhyaksh W.C. Bonnerjee.

  2. Q2. Bengal ka vibhajan kisne kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Lord Curzon — 1905 mein; 1911 mein radd.

  3. Q3. Surat split (1907) kin do dhadon ke beech hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Moderates aur Nationalists — Nationalists — Lal-Bal-Pal aur Aurobindo.

  4. Q4. 'Swaraj mera janmsiddh adhikaar hai' kisne kaha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Tilak — Bal Gangadhar Tilak — 'Lokmanya'.

  5. Q5. Jallianwala Bagh hatyakaand kab hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) 13 April 1919 — Baisakhi ke din, General Dyer ne goli chalwaayi.

  6. Q6. Non-Cooperation Movement kis ghatna ke baad wapas liya gaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Chauri Chaura — Feb 1922 mein policewalon ki hatya ke baad.

  7. Q7. Purna Swaraj ka prastaav kis session mein paas hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Lahore 1929 — December 1929, Lahore.

  8. Q8. Dandi March kitne km ka tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 380 km — March–April 1930, namak kaanoon todne ke liye.

  9. Q9. INA aur Azad Hind sarkar kahan ghoshit hui?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Singapore — 1943 mein Subhas Chandra Bose ne Singapore mein.

  10. Q10. Riyasaton ko India mein milaane wale 'Iron Man' kaun the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Sardar Patel — Samjhaane aur zaroorat par sena se.
