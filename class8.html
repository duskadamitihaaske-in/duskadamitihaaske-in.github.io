Part 1 · Ch01

Theme A · India and the World: Land and the People

Natural Resources and Their Use

Prakriti kab 'resource' banti hai, resources ke prakaar, unka asmaan bantwara aur unhe samajhdaari se use karna.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Pehle ek sawaal — tumhari shirt ka button kahan se aaya?

Apne aas-paas dekho — pencil, kursi, kapde, bijli, yahan tak ki plastic ka button bhi. Agar hum peeche jaate jaayein ki ye cheez kis se bani, to aakhir mein har cheez Prakriti (Nature) par pahunchti hai. Pencil lakdi se (ped), plastic petroleum se (zameen ke neeche), kapda kapaas se (khet). Yani hamari poori zindagi Prakriti ke tohfon par tiki hai.

Ped kab 'resource' ban jaata hai?

Jungle mein khada ped apne aap mein Prakriti ka hissa hai — usse insaan ne nahi banaya. Jab hum us ped ki lakdi kaat kar kursi banate hain, tab wahi ped hamare liye resource (sansaadhan) ban jaata hai. Lekin har cheez resource nahi banti. Teen sharten hain: (1) technology ho — samundar ke bahut neeche chhupa petrol tab tak bekaar hai jab tak use nikaalne ki machine na ho; (2) kharcha theek ho — nikaalne mein itna paisa lage ki fayda hi na ho, to bekaar; (3) samaaj maane — jaise sacred grove (pavitra jungle) ke ped log nahi kaat-te.

Resources ko groups mein baantna

Jaise school mein bachchon ko class ke hisaab se baant-te hain, waise hi resources ko bhi baant-te hain. Pehla tareeka — kaam ke hisaab se: (a) zindagi ke liye zaroori — hawa, paani, khana (mitti se); inhe hum bana nahi sakte; (b) cheezein banane ke liye — lakdi, marble, sona; (c) energy ke liye — koyla, petrol, gas, dhoop, hawa, behta paani. Doosra tareeka — dobara bante hain ya nahi: renewable aur non-renewable.

Renewable — jo phir se ban jaate hain (lekin ek shart ke saath!)

Socho tumhe ungli mein chhota sa cut lag gaya — kuch din mein apne aap bhar jaata hai. Prakriti bhi aisi hi hai: jungle aag ke baad phir ug aata hai. Ise restoration (purani haalat mein laut-na) kehte hain. Regeneration usse aage hai — nayi zindagi paida karna, jaise ujde ilaake mein wahan ke asli ped lagao to chidiya, gilahri sab wapas aa jaati hain. Prakriti mein koi kachra nahi — gira hua ped sad kar mitti ban jaata hai, mitti se naye paudhe. Dhoop, hawa, behta paani, jungle ki lakdi = renewable. Par shart: agar hum ped itni tezi se kaatein ki jungle ug hi na paaye, to renewable bhi khatam ho jaayega. Jaise tuna machhli — zyada pakadne se kam ho rahi hai.

Non-renewable — ek baar khatam, to khatam

Koyla, petrol (fossil fuels) aur loha, taamba, sona jaise khanij laakhon saal mein bante hain. Hum unhe jitni tezi se use karte hain, utni tezi se wo nahi bante. Ek andaaza hai ki India ka koyla lagbhag 50 saal aur chalega. Isliye inhe sambhal kar (judiciously) kharch karna hai — bilkul gullak ke paise ki tarah.

Prakriti hamein muft mein kya kaam karke deti hai?

Jungle paani saaf karta hai, mitti ko behne se rokta hai, jaanwaron ko ghar deta hai — ye ecosystem functions hain. Jab inka fayda insaan ko milta hai (saaf paani, surakshit khet, phoolon ka pollination), to unhe ecosystem services kehte hain. Ek mazedaar fact: ek bada ped din mein lagbhag 275 litre oxygen banata hai, aur ek insaan ko roz lagbhag 350 litre chahiye!

Resources har jagah barabar kyon nahi?

Koyla Jharkhand-Chhattisgarh mein, tel Assam (Digboi) aur Mumbai High mein, loha Odisha-Karnataka mein — yani resources asmaan (uneven) bante hain. Jahan resource hota hai, wahan factory lagti hai, naukri milti hai, shehar basta hai. Lekin kai baar wahan ke logon ko ghar chhodna padta hai. Nadiyaan bhi seema nahi maanti — Kaveri ka paani Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Puducherry mein baantna padta hai; Brahmaputra kai deshon se behti hai.

'Paradox of Plenty' — bahut hona bhi musibat?

Socho kisi ke paas bahut saara kapaas hai par wo kapda banana nahi jaanta — wo sasta kapaas bechega aur mehenga kapda khareedega. Isi tarah kuch desh resources se bhare hain par phir bhi gareeb hain, kyonki wo resource se mehengi cheez banane wale udyog nahi bana paaye. Ise 'resource curse' / 'paradox of plenty' kehte hain. India ne udyog bana kar is curse se khud ko bachaya.

Punjab ki kahani — paani ka khazaana khaali

1960s mein Green Revolution aayi — gehu aur dhaan ke naye beej jo zyada fasal dete the par zyada paani bhi peete the. Kisaan zameen ke neeche ka paani (groundwater) pump karne lage; muft bijli ki wajah se aur zyada pump hua. Saath mein chemical khaad aur keetnaashak. Natija: Punjab ka lagbhag 80% ilaaka 'over-exploited', kai jagah paani 30 metre tak neeche nahi milta, aur paani mein chemical ghul gaye. Desh ka pet bhara, par lambe samay ki keemat chukaani pad rahi hai.

Sikkim ki kahani — Pema ka khet

Pema ka parivaar mehengi chemical khaad se karz mein tha. Sarkar ne organic kheti badhaai to unhone compost, neem aur lehsun se bani keet-dawa aur alag-alag fasalein shuru kin. Pehle fasal kam hui, par lagbhag 5 saal mein khet lehlaha utha. 2016 mein Sikkim 100% organic state bana — chidiya aur keede wapas aaye, tourist badhe, kisaanon ki kamaai ~20% badhi.

Hum kya kar sakte hain? — 'Steward' bano

Steward matlab rakhwala — jo cheez ka maalik nahi, uski dekhbhaal karta hai. Purane tareeke (barish ka paani jama karna, talaab theek karna, gobar ki khaad, mulching, multi-cropping), Vrikshayurveda ka gyaan, mitti ki imaaratein (Auroville), aur solar energy (Bhadla Solar Park, International Solar Alliance 2015) — ye sab raaste hain. Bhagavad Gita ka lokasangraha kehta hai: apni ichchha se upar uth kar sabki bhalaai ke liye kaam karo.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Natural resources = Prakriti mein milne wale padarth jo insaan ke liye keemti hain.
  • Resource banne ki 3 sharten: technologically accessible, economically feasible, culturally acceptable.
  • Upyog ke aadhar par 3 group: essential for life (hawa, paani, mitti/khana), materials (lakdi, marble, sona), energy (koyla, paani, petrol, gas, dhoop, hawa).
  • Renewable: solar, wind, behta paani, jungle ki lakdi — tabhi tak jab tak sustainable tareeke se use ho.
  • Non-renewable: fossil fuels (koyla, petroleum) + minerals/metals (loha, taamba, sona). India ka koyla ~50 saal aur.
  • Himalayan glaciers tezi se pighal rahe → plains ki water security par khatra ('water tower').
  • Ecosystem functions (jungle paani filter kare) → jab insaan ko fayda mile = ecosystem services. Ped ~275 L oxygen/din; insaan ko ~350 L/din.
  • Map: koyla — Jharia, Raniganj, Bokaro, Korba, Talcher, Singareni, Neyveli; tel — Digboi, Naharkatia, Ankleshwar, Kalol, Mumbai High; loha — Bailadila, Keonjhar, Kudremukh, Ballari.
  • Sharing ke jhagde: Kaveri (Karnataka, TN, Kerala, Puducherry; Mettur dam TN); Brahmaputra (Yarlung Tsangpo → Brahmaputra → Jamuna, Bangladesh).
  • Wootz steel — natural resource + insaani hunar se bana anokha utpaad.
  • Paradox of plenty / natural resource curse: resource zyada par vikas dheema; India ne udyog bana kar bachaav kiya.
  • Punjab caselet: 1960s Green Revolution, HYV gehu-dhaan, muft bijli → ~80% area over-exploited, ~30 m tak paani nahi; chemicals groundwater mein.
  • Cement sabse pradooshak udyogon mein; Central Pollution Control Board ke guidelines; vikalp — pathar, mitti, plant-based, recycled plastic. Auroville Earth Institute (UNESCO Chair for Earthen Architecture).
  • Vrikshayurveda (vriksha = ped, ayurveda = jeevan ka vigyan) — Surapala ka granth ~10th c. CE: beej, sinchaai, natural keet-niyantran, crop rotation, mixed cropping.
  • Sikkim — 2016 mein 100% organic state; kisaanon ki aay ~20% badhi.
  • International Solar Alliance — India + France, 2015. Bhadla Solar Park (Rajasthan) — Rajasthan ki ~15% bijli ki zaroorat.
  • Lokasangraha (Bhagavad Gita) — sabki bhalaai. Hamari bhoomika: stewards of Nature.

Theme B · Tapestry of the Past

Reshaping India's Political Map

13th–17th sadi: Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Mughal, aur unka mukabla karne wale Rajput, Ahom, Sikh, Gond — naksha kaise badalta raha.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Ye chapter kis samay ki baat karta hai?

Socho ek bada naksha (map) hai jis par har kuch saal mein seemaayein mitaayi aur phir se kheenchi ja rahi hain — yahi is chapter ki kahani hai. Samay hai lagbhag 11th se 17th sadi — ise aksar 'medieval' (madhyakaal = 'do yugon ke beech') kehte hain. Ye shabd asal mein Europe ke itihaas ke liye bana tha, isliye India ke liye poori tarah theek nahi — hamare liye iska seedha matlab hai 11th–17th sadi. Is daur mein Hindu Kush pahadon ke paar se — Central Asia ke Turkic aur Afghan log — baar-baar hamle karne aaye: daulat, zameen aur apne dharm ko failane ke liye.

Delhi Sultanate — 5 vansh, ek ke baad ek

1192 mein Prithviraj Chauhan ki haar ke baad 1206 mein Delhi Sultanate bani. 'Sultan' ek padvi (title) thi. Paanch vansh ne raaj kiya: Mamluk (Slave), Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi. Gaddi ke liye bahut khoon-kharaba hota tha — lagbhag har 3 mein se 2 sultan pichhle ko hata kar gaddi par baithe, isliye ek sultan ka raaj ausatan sirf ~9 saal chala.

Ala-ud-din Khilji aur Muhammad bin Tughlaq

Ala-ud-din Khilji (14th sadi ki shuruaat) ne uttar aur madhya India mein bade abhiyaan chalaye, aur Mongol hamlon ko bhi roka. Usne apne sikke par likhwaya 'Sikander Sani' = doosra Alexander. Uska general Malik Kafur dakshin tak gaya (Srirangam, Madurai, Chidambaram). Muhammad bin Tughlaq ke samay Mauryon ke baad pehli baar lagbhag poora upmahadweep ek shaasak ke neeche aaya. Par uske plan ulte pade: (1) raajdhani Delhi se Daulatabad (Devagiri) le gaya — logon ko 1,000 km se zyada chalna pada, phir wapas Delhi! (2) token currency — taambe ke sikke ko chaandi jitni keemat di; idea aage ka tha (aaj ke note bhi 'token' hain), par log nakli sikke banane lage aur vyapaar bigad gaya.

Timur ka hamla aur Sultanate ka sikudna

1398 mein Central Asia ka kroor vijeta Timur aaya aur Delhi ko loot kar barbaad kar diya. Uske baad Lodi vansh aaya — Sultanate ka aakhri vansh — par tab tak uska ilaaka bahut chhota ho chuka tha. Kuch sultanon ne gair-Muslimon par jizya tax lagaya. Mandiron ki moortiyon ko todna bhi hua — ise iconoclasm kehte hain.

Jinhone haar nahi maani — Sultanate ka virodh

Eastern Ganga raja Narasimhadeva I (Kalinga/Odisha, 13th sadi) ne Sultanate ko roka aur jeet ki yaad mein Konark ka Surya Mandir banwaya. Musunuri Nayakas ne 75 se zyada sardaaron ko jod kar Tughlaq ki sena ko Warangal se bhagaya (~1330–36) — socho, us zamaane mein 75 netaon ko ek karna kitna mushkil hoga! Hoysala (Karnataka; Belur, Halebidu ke mandir) dakshin ke akele azaad raajya rahe; unke naam ki kahani: Sala ne sher se ladkar guru ko bachaya — 'Hoy Sala!' (maaro, Sala!). Mewar ke Rana Kumbha ne Kumbhalgarh qila banaya jiski deewar 36 km lambi hai.

Vijayanagara — kharagosh ne kutton ko bhagaya!

1336 mein do bhai Harihara aur Bukka (pehle Tughlaq ke governor) ne Delhi ko nakaar kar apna raajya banaya. Kahani: Hampi mein unhone dekha ki ek kharagosh mud kar shikari kutton ko daudaa raha hai! Guru Vidyaranya ne kaha — ye himmat ka sanket hai, yahin raajdhani banao. Uttar mein Bahmani Sultanate (1347) dushman tha, jo baad mein 5 Deccan Sultanates mein toot gaya — Bijapur, Golconda, Berar, Ahmednagar, Bidar. Padviyan: Vijayanagara raja = Narapati, Bahmani = Ashwapati, Odisha = Gajapati, Maratha = Chhatrapati ('pati' = maalik).

Krishnadevaraya aur Talikota ki tabaahi

16th sadi mein Krishnadevaraya ke samay Vijayanagara apne shikhar par tha. Wo khud kavi the — Telugu mein 'Amuktamalyada' likha (sant kavi Andal ki kahani; isme Rajaniti — achchhe shaasan ke vichaar). Tirupati aur Vitthala mandir ko daan diya — Vitthala mandir ke 'musical pillars' thapthapaane par sangeet ke sur nikaalte hain! Portuguese yatri Domingo Paes ne likha — shehar Rome jitna bada, 'duniya ka sabse bhara-poora shehar'. Krishnadevaraya ki 1529 mein maut ke baad 1565 mein Deccan Sultanates ne milkar Talikota ki ladaai mein Ramaraya ko haraya; shehar mahinon loota gaya. Baad mein Nayaka chhote-chhote raaj chalaane lage.

Mughal — Babur se Akbar tak

Samarkand se nikaale gaye Babur (Timur ka vanshaj) ne 1526 mein Panipat ki pehli ladaai mein Ibrahim Lodi ko baarood, top aur bandook se haraya — Sultanate khatam, Mughal shuru. Babur ki aatmakatha Baburnama — usme chidiyon, phalon ka shauk bhi aur kroor qatl-e-aam bhi. Bete Humayun ko Sher Shah Suri ne hata diya, par Humayun wapas aaya. Himu (Hemchandra Vikramaditya) ne Delhi pakdi, par 1556 ki Panipat ki doosri ladaai mein ghayal hua aur Akbar ne uska sir kalam karwa diya.

Akbar — pehle kathor, phir sahishnu

13 saal ki umar mein badshah bane. Jawani mein Chittor ke qile ko 5 mahine se zyada ghera; jeet ke baad ~30,000 logon ka qatl karwaya; wahan ki auraton ne jauhar kiya (gulam banne se bachne ke liye aag mein koodna). Baad mein Akbar ne neeti badli: Rajput raajkumariyon se shaadi, Rajputon ko darbar mein jagah, jizya hataya, 'sulh-i-kul' (sabse shaanti) ka vichaar. Khud padh-likh nahi sakte the par Fatehpur Sikri mein anuvaad ghar banaya — Mahabharata ka Persian roop 'Razmnama', Ramayana (176 chitron ke saath), Gita, Panchatantra. Raaj 1556–1605 (~50 saal).

Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb

Jahangir ko kala pasand thi; Shah Jahan ne Taj Mahal banwaya. Aurangzeb ne bade bhai Dara Shikoh ko maar kar, pita Shah Jahan ko Agra Fort mein qaid karke 1658 mein gaddi li, naam rakha 'Alamgir' (duniya ka vijeta). Uske samay Mughal samrajya sabse bada hua, par aakhri 25 saal Deccan ki ladaaiyon mein beete aur khazaana khaali ho gaya. Usne jizya aur teerth-yatra tax wapas lagaya; 1669 ke farman mein mandir-paathshaala todne ka hukm diya (Banaras, Mathura, Somnath). 1707 mein maut ke baad Mughal taakat tezi se giri. Yaad rakho: ye beete zamaane ke logon ki baat hai — aaj ke kisi insaan ko iska dosh nahi diya ja sakta.

Mughalon ka virodh — veer kahaniyaan

Jat kisaanon ki bagawat (20,000 log). Bhil, Gond, Santhal, Koch jaise adivasi samooh. Rani Durgavati (Garha, Gond raajya) — 20,000 sainik, 1,000 haathi; 1564 mein Akbar ke general se ladeen, ghayal hokar bhi aatmasamarpan nahi kiya — sirf 40 saal ki thin. Rana Sanga — Khanwa mein Babur se haare. Maharana Pratap — 1576 Haldighati; bach nikle aur saalon tak Aravalli se guerrilla yuddh (chhupkar achaanak hamla) kiya; Bhil teerandaazon ne madad ki, isliye Mewar ke chinh mein ek Bhil yoddha hai. Durga Das Rathore ne Jodhpur ki azaadi bachaayi.

Ahom aur Sikh

Ahom 13th sadi mein Myanmar se Brahmaputra ghaati aaye. Paik system: har tandurust aadmi raajya ke liye mazdoori ya sena ki seva karta, badle mein zameen milti — bina sthaayi sena ke badi fauj! 1671 Saraighat (Guwahati ke paas, Brahmaputra par) — Lachit Borphukan ke 10,000 sainikon ne 30,000 ki Mughal sena ko haraya. Guru Nanak (15th sadi) — Ik Onkar, barabari, daya. Guru Arjan ne Guru Granth Sahib sankalit kiya; Jahangir ne unhe yaatna dekar maara. Guru Hargobind ne sena banayi. 1675 — Guru Tegh Bahadur ne Kashmiri Pandits ki raksha ke liye dharm badalne se mana kiya; Chandni Chowk mein sir kaata gaya (Gurudwara Sis Ganj Sahib). Guru Gobind Singh ne 1699 mein Khalsa banaya. Maharaja Ranjit Singh ne 1799 mein Sikh samrajya banaya.

Raaj kaise chalta tha aur log kaise jeete the?

Sultanate: iqta system — sardaaron (iqtadar) ko ilaaka diya, wo tax wasool kar sena chalaate; pad khandaani nahi tha. Akbar: mantri — Diwan (paisa), Mir Bakhshi (sena), Khan-i-Saman (public works, vyapaar, raaj ghar), Sadr (nyay, dharm, shiksha); 12 subah (praant). Mansabdari: har afsar (mansabdar) ka rank (mansab) tay karta ki wo kitne ghode-haathi-sainik rakhega; tankhwah zameen (jagir) ke roop mein. Todar Mal ne zameen ka survey karke lagaan system sudhara. Kheti mukhya — lagaan aam taur par 1/5, kabhi 1/2 tak. Persian wheel se sinchaai. Mughal sikke: chaandi ka rupaya, taambe ka dam. Hundi = likhit bhugtaan aadesh, bina paisa uthaye paisa bhejna — aaj ki banking ka purvaj. Mandir bhi bazaar, karz aur dharmshaala ke kendra the.

The Rise of the Marathas

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj ka Swarajya, Maratha navy, Peshwa, Tarabai-Ahilyabai aur Thanjavur — angrezon se pehle sabse bada Bharatiya samrajya.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Marathe kaun the?

Marathe Deccan pathaar — khaas kar aaj ke Maharashtra — ke log hain jo Marathi bolte hain. Marathi ka sahitya 12th sadi se lagatar likha ja raha hai. 13th sadi mein yahan Yadava vansh ka raaj tha (raajdhani Devagiri), jise 14th sadi mein Khilji ne hara diya. Raajnaitik uthal-puthal ke beech bhi bhakti ki parampara chalti rahi — Dnyaneshwar, Namdev, Tukaram, Ramdas jaise santon ne Gita aur Upanishad ko Marathi mein samjhaya. Isse samaaj ki ek mazboot saanskritik neev bani, jis par aage chalkar Maratha shakti khadi hui.

Bachpan — maa Jijabai ki seekh

1630 mein Bhonsle kul mein Shahji aur Jijabai ke ghar Shivaji ka janm hua. Pita Deccan sultanon ki naukri mein aksar bahar rehte, isliye Pune ki jagir mein maa Jijabai ne unhe achchhe sanskaar aur shiksha di. Us waqt Deccan ke sultan aapas mein ladte rehte aur aam log pareshaan the. Sirf 16 saal ki umar mein Shivaji ne veeraan pade qile jeet kar mazboot kiye. Dheere-dheere unka sapna bana — 'Swarajya' = apna raaj, jahan log surakshit aur khush hon.

Navy — us zamaane ka anokha kadam

Jab raajya pashchimi tat (samundar kinaare) tak pahuncha to Shivaji ne samjha ki samundar ki raksha ke liye navy chahiye (1657). Ye kranti jaisa kadam tha — Bijapur ke paas sirf vyapaari jahaz the aur Mughalon ki navy bhi kamzor thi. Sindhudurg jaise samudri qile bane. Ek baar 1665 mein Oman (Muscat) ke shaasak ne Shivaji ke 4 jahaz pakad liye, par jab suna ki Shivaji ki navy mein 100 se zyada jahaz hain, to wo darr kar bandargah mein chhup gaya!

Afzal Khan aur 'waagh nakh'

Shivaji guerrilla yuddh ladte the — chhote dal, tez raftaar, achaanak hamla, pahadon-jungle ki poori jaankaari. Bijapur ne anubhavi general Afzal Khan ko bheja. Ghane jungle mein Pratapgad qile ke neeche dono ki akele mein mulaqat tay hui. Wahan Shivaji ne waagh nakh (sher ke panje jaisa chhota hathiyaar) se Afzal Khan ko maar giraya, aur pahadon mein chhupe Maratha sainikon ne uski sena ko bhaga diya.

Shaista Khan ki ungliyan aur Surat

Mughal sardaar Shaista Khan 3 saal tak hamle karta raha. Ek raat Shivaji kuch hi sainikon ke saath uske camp mein ghus gaye! Shaista Khan bach to gaya, par kuch ungliyan kat gayin aur wo Maharashtra chhod kar bhaag gaya — ye aaj ke 'surgical strike' jaisa tha. Badle mein Shivaji ne Mughalon ke ameer bandargah Surat ko loota — lagbhag 1 crore rupaye! Par dharmik sthalon ko haath nahi lagaya, aur daani Mohandas Parekh ka ghar bhi chhod diya. Ye khabar London ke akhbaar 'London Gazette' tak mein chhapi.

Purandar, Agra aur mithai ki tokri!

Aurangzeb ne Rajput general Jai Singh ko bheja. Purandar mein Shivaji ko sandhi karni padi — kaafi ilaaka dena pada. Jai Singh ke kehne par wo Agra darbar gaye (1666), jahan unhe ek haare hue Mughal general ke saath khada karke apmaan kiya gaya. Gusse mein wo darbar chhod gaye to Aurangzeb ne unhe nazarband kar diya. Shivaji ne chaal chali — saadhuon aur sardaaron ko phal-mithai ki badi tokriyan bhejne lage. Pehracharon ne kuch din jaancha, phir jaanchna band kar diya... aur ek din Shivaji aur beta Sambhaji tokriyon mein chhup kar nikal gaye!

Raigad mein Rajyabhishek aur dakshin vijay

1674 mein Raigad qile par Vedic vidhi se rajyabhishek hua; padvi — 'Shri Raja Shiva Chhatrapati'; naya samvat — Rajyabhisheka shaka. Phir dakshina digvijaya (1677) — aaj ka uttari Tamil Nadu aur Karnataka ka hissa jeeta, jo baad mein Mughalon ke khilaaf 'peeche ki surakshit zameen' bana. Dakshin mein unhone Dutch ko gulamon ka vyapaar karne se roka — us zamaane mein European log Bharatiyon ko pakad kar bechte the aur koi Bharatiya shakti rokti nahi thi. 1680 mein bukhaar se unki maut hui. Unse prerit hokar Chhatrasal ne Bundelkhand banaya; kavi Bhushan ne unki prashansa mein kavitaayein likhin.

Shivaji ke baad — Sambhaji, Rajaram, Tarabai, Peshwa

Bade bete Sambhaji Chhatrapati bane; Aurangzeb ne Bijapur aur Golconda jeet kar Sambhaji ko pakda aur yaatna dekar maar diya, Raigad bhi le liya. Chhote bete Rajaram Gingee (Tamil Nadu) chale gaye — ladaai dakshin tak fail gayi. Aurangzeb Deccan se nikal hi nahi paaya aur haare bina Marathon ko jeete mar gaya. Phir Tarabai (Rajaram ki rani) ne bade lashkar uttar bheje — wo uttar ki taraf failaav ki 'architect' thin. Dheere-dheere Peshwa (pradhan mantri) sabse taaqatwar ho gaye — Bajirao I aur Nanasaheb ne Maratha raaj poore India mein failaaya: Lahore, Attock, Peshawar tak!

Panipat 1761 se angrezon tak

1761 mein Panipat ki teesri ladaai mein Afghanon se badi haar hui, par Peshwa Madhavrao I ke samay jaldi sambhal gaye. Mahadji Shinde (Scindia) ne 1771 mein Delhi wapas li. 18th sadi ke doosre hisse mein angrezon ke sabse bade dushman Marathe hi the. Teen Anglo-Maratha yuddh (1775–1818) hue — pehla Marathon ne jeeta, par aakhir mein aapsi phoot aur angrezon ki behtar technology se 1818 mein Maratha shakti khatam hui. Isliye kehte hain — angrezon ne India Mughalon se zyada Marathon se liya. Nana Phadnavis ne Hyder Ali aur Nizam tak ko jod kar pehla akhil-Bharatiya angrez-virodhi gathbandhan banaya.

Raaj kaise chalaya? — 'ghaas ka tinka bhi mat cheeno'

Shivaji ne khandaani pad aur zameen dena band kiya; har afsar ko khazaane se tankhwah di aur baar-baar transfer kiya taaki koi bahut taaqatwar na ho. Shaheed sainikon ki vidhvaaon ko pension, beton ko naukri. Afsaron ko likha: navy ke liye sagwan (teak) kaato to ijaazat lekar, par aam aur kathal ke ped mat kaatna — log unhe bachchon ki tarah paalte hain. Ashta Pradhan (8 mantri) unki madad karte. Doosre praanton se chauth (25%) aur sardeshmukhi (+10%) lete, badle mein unki raksha karte. Nyay panchayat karti, maut ki saza bahut kam; shehar mein kotwal (police). Sena: paidal, ghudsawar, navy; ghudsawar mein bargir (ghoda sarkar ka) aur shiledar (ghoda apna). Rocket bhi istemal hote the!

Qile, Kanhoji Angre aur 'cartaz'

Finance mantri Ramachandrapant Amatya ne 'Adnyapatra' mein likha — 'qile raajya ka dil hain'; Aurangzeb jaisa dushman bhi aaya, par qilon ki wajah se raajya bacha. 18th sadi mein Kanhoji Angre ne samudri ladaaiyaan jeetin. European log Bharatiya jahazon se zabardasti 'cartaz' (pass) khareedwaate the; Marathon ne ulta Europeans se pass maangna shuru kar diya — chidh kar Europeans ne Kanhoji ko 'pirate' (samudri daaku) keh diya! Shivaji ke jahaz Mocha (Yemen), Muscat (Oman), Malacca (Malaysia) tak jaate the.

Sanskriti, mahaan mahilaayein aur Thanjavur

Shivaji ki mohar (seal) Sanskrit mein thi (Persian ki jagah) — 'naye chaand ki tarah badhne wali, logon ke kalyan ke liye'. 'Rajya-Vyavahara-Kosha' se Persian shabdon ki jagah Sanskrit/Marathi shabd. Bhagwa dhwaj, mandiron ka punarnirman. Chitthiyon ke liye Modi lipi. Ahilyabai Holkar (Indore) ne pati-bete ko khone ke baad bhi 30 saal raaj kiya; Kedarnath se Rameswaram tak mandir, ghaat, kuein banwaye; Kashi Vishwanath aur Somnath ka punarnirman; Maheshwar ki bunai. Thanjavur mein Shivaji ke sautele bhai Ekoji ka raaj; Serfoji II ne 'Devendra Kuravanji' natak likha, Carnatic sangeet aur Bharatanatyam ko roop mila, Dhanwantari Mahal (muft ilaaj), aur kisi Bharatiya raja ka pehla printing press.

The Colonial Era in India

Portuguese, Dutch, French aur British — vyapaari kaise shaasak bane, India ki daulat kaise gayi, aur logon ne kaise virodh kiya (1857 tak).

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Colonialism kya hota hai? — ek seedhi misaal

Socho tumhare mohalle mein ek bahar ka aadmi dukaan kholne aaya. Pehle wo vyapaar karta hai, phir dheere-dheere jhagdon mein ek paksh ki madad karke sabka 'sarpanch' ban jaata hai, tumhare khet ki fasal le jaata hai aur tumhe apni banayi cheezein mehenge daam par bechta hai. Yahi hai colonialism (upniveshvaad): ek desh doosre ilaake par qabza karke wahan apni basti, apna raaj, apni arthvyavastha aur sanskriti thop de. 15th sadi se Spain, Portugal, Britain, France, Netherlands ne Africa, Asia, America, Australia tak colony banaayi. Wajah: ek-doosre se hod, naye resources-bazaar-raaste, loot, Isaai dharm failana, aur thoda vigyaan ki jigyaasa. Wo khud ko 'civilising mission' (sabhya banane wale) kehte the, par asal mein azaadi chhini, shoshan hua.

India itna 'aakarshak' kyon tha?

India 2,000 saal pehle bhi Greece-Rome se vyapaar karta tha — masaale, kapaas, haathi-daant, ratna, chandan, sagwan, wootz steel. Arthshaastri Angus Maddison ke andaaze se 16th sadi tak duniya ki kul kamaai (GDP) ka kam se kam 1/4 hissa India ka tha — China ke saath duniya ki do sabse badi arthvyavasthaon mein. Itna ameer desh hi lalchi taakaton ka nishana bana.

Portuguese — vyapaar bhi, atyachaar bhi

May 1498 mein Vasco da Gama Kappad (Kozhikode/Calicut ke paas) pahuncha. Doosri yaatra mein usne vyapaariyon ko maara aur Calicut par samundar se gole barsaaye. 1510 mein Goa liya. Cartaz system — bina Portuguese pass ke jahaz zabt. 1560 mein Goa mein Inquisition (dharmik adaalat) — Hindu, Muslim, Yahudi aur 'galat' Isaaiyon par atyachaar, zabardasti dharm-parivartan, mandir tode; ye 1812 tak chala. Par Ullal (Karnataka) ki Rani Abbakka I ne padosi raajaon se milkar Portuguese ko roka; Rani Abbakka II ne nariyal ke khol se aag ke gole bana kar unke jahaz jalaaye! Unki kahani aaj bhi Yakshagana mein gaayi jaati hai.

Dutch aur French

Dutch (17th sadi) — sirf vyapaar, khaas kar masaale; Surat, Bharuch, Kochi, Nagapattinam, Masulipatnam. 1741 mein Colachel ki ladaai mein Travancore ke raja Marthanda Varma ne Dutch ko zameen aur samundar dono par haraya — ek Asian shakti ki European par durlabh jeet! French: Surat (1668), Pondicherry (1674). Governor Dupleix (1742–54) ne do chaalein shuru kin jo baad mein angrezon ne apnaayi: (1) Bharatiyon ko European tareeke se train karke sepoy (sipahi) banana, (2) kathputli raja bitha kar parde ke peeche raaj. Carnatic Wars (1746–63) mein angrezon se haar kar French sirf Pondicherry tak simat gaye. 1748 mein Dupleix ne Pondicherry ka Vedapurishwaran mandir tudwaya.

Angrez — vyapaari se raja kaise bane?

English East India Company ko Queen Elizabeth I ne charter diya — apni sena rakhne tak ki chhoot! 17th sadi mein Surat, Madras, Bombay, Calcutta mein 'sirf vyapaari' ban kar kothiyan banayin. Phir chaal: 'divide and rule' (phoot daalo, raaj karo) — rajaon ke aapsi jhagdon aur gaddi ke vivaad mein ek ko sena dekar 'kingmaker' ban jaana; dharmik samudaayon ke beech tanaav ko hawa dena.

Plassey 1757 — 'Mir Jafar' = gaddaar

Bengal ke Nawab Siraj-ud-daulah se jhagda hua to Robert Clive ne Nawab ke senapati Mir Jafar se saazish ki — 'tum dhoka do, hum tumhe Nawab banayenge'. Palashi (Plassey) (Kolkata se ~150 km) mein Mir Jafar ki sena — jo Nawab ki zyaadatar sena thi — khadi tamasha dekhti rahi, aur chhoti angrez sena jeet gayi. Aaj bhi 'Mir Jafar' ka matlab gaddaar hai!

Doctrine of Lapse aur Subsidiary Alliance — do chaalaak jaal

Doctrine of Lapse: agar raja ka apna saga beta na ho to raajya angrezon ka — god liye bete ko nahi maana, jabki Hindu parampara mein god lena jaayaz tha. Subsidiary Alliance: raja ke darbar mein angrez 'Resident' baithega, raja apne kharche par angrez sena rakhega, aur videsh se baat sirf angrezon ke zariye — yani raja apni ghulaami ka bill khud bhare! Hyderabad (1798) pehla tha. Ise 'empire on the cheap' (sasta samrajya) kaha gaya. Azaadi ke samay 500+ princely states the, upmahadweep ka ~40%.

'Swarg' se 'narak' — bhayankar akaal (famine)

Clive ne Bengal ko 'paradise of the earth' kaha. Phir Company ko Bengal, Bihar, Odisha ka lagaan wasoolne ka haq mila. Do saal fasal kharaab, upar se zabardasti nakad tax — 1770–72 ka Bengal akaal: lagbhag 1/3 aabadi, ~1 crore log mare, aur Company ne tax badha diya! Log beej, bail, auzaar, yahan tak ki bachche bech kar ped ki pattiyan khaane ko majboor hue. 1876–78 ka Great Famine — ~80 lakh mare (zyaadatar Deccan), phir bhi har saal ~10 lakh tonne chaawal Britain bheja gaya. Viceroy Lytton ne kaha — khaane ke daam ghataane mein sarkar dakhal nahi degi, aur usi saal Delhi mein 68,000 logon ki hafte bhar ki daawat! Angrezi raaj mein kul 5–10 crore log akaal se mare — lagbhag World War II jitne.

Daulat ka 'drain' — paisa India se Britain

US itihaaskar Brooks Adams ne kaha — Plassey ke baad Bengal ki loot London pahunchi aur 1760 se Britain ki Industrial Revolution shuru hui. Dadabhai Naoroji (British House of Commons mein chune gaye pehle Bharatiya) ne 1901 mein 'Poverty and Un-British Rule in India' likhi; R.C. Dutt ne 'Economic History of India'. Utsa Patnaik ke anusaar 1765–1938 mein ~45 trillion US dollar nikaale gaye — Britain ki 2023 GDP ka ~13 guna! Railway, telegraph, yahan tak ki angrezon ki ladaaiyon ka kharcha bhi Bharatiyon se wasoola — 'Indians funded their own subjugation'.

Kapda, gaon aur school — sab badal diya

Udyog barbaad: Bharatiya kapde par Britain mein bhaari tax, par British maal India mein lagbhag bina tax — bunkar gareeb ho gaye. William Bentinck (1834): 'bunkaron ki haddiyan India ke maidaanon mein safed pad rahi hain'. Azaadi tak India ka world GDP hissa ~5% reh gaya. Gaon ki panchaayat: Charles Metcalfe ne kaha — gaon 'little republics' hain jo har raajvansh ke girne ke baad bhi bane rehte; angrezon ne inhe tod kar tax wasoolne wali afsarshahi banayi; adaalatein mehengi, dheemi aur videshi bhasha mein. Shiksha: Bengal-Bihar mein 1830 tak 1–1.5 lakh gaon ke school the. 1835 mein Macaulay ki 'Minute' — 'achchhi European library ki ek shelf poore Bharatiya aur Arabi sahitya ke barabar' (jabki wo Sanskrit-Arabi jaante bhi nahi the!). Lakshya: aise log jo 'khoon aur rang se Indian, par soch se English' hon — sasta clerk varg.

Railway — vardaan ya chaal?

Railway ne logon ko joda, par asli maqsad tha andar se kachcha maal bandargah tak le jaana aur British maal poore desh mein failana, plus sena ko tezi se bagawat ki jagah pahunchana. Aur iska kharcha bhi Bharatiya tax se hua. India ko angrez 'jewel in the crown' kehte the aur dava karte the ki unke samrajya mein 'sooraj kabhi nahi doobta'.

Virodh — sanyasi, adivasi, kisaan

Sannyasi-Fakir Rebellion (1770 ke akaal ke baad, Bengal) — ispe Bankim Chandra ka upanyas 'Anandamath' (1882), jisme 'Vande Mataram' hai — aaj hamara rashtriya geet. Adivasiyon se jungle chheena, nakad tax, karz ka jaal, aur 'criminal tribes' ka kaanoon. Kol Uprising (1831–32), Chota Nagpur — Munda, Oraon. Khasi Uprising (1829–33), Meghalaya. Santhal Rebellion (1855–56) — bhai Sidhu aur Kanhu Murmu, 'khoon ki aakhri boond tak'. Indigo Revolt (1859–62) — Bengal ke kisaanon se zabardasti neel ugwaya jaata tha; Bengali akhbaaron ke saath se kuch zulm ruke.

1857 — Mahaan Vidroh

Angrez ise 'Sepoy Mutiny' kehte the; azaadi ke baad itihaskaaron ne ise 'Great Rebellion of 1857' kaha. Pehle 1806 Vellore — tilak aur daadhi par rok se sipahi bhadke. 1857 mein afwaah — cartridge par gaay aur suar ki charbi. Barrackpore mein Mangal Pandey ne afsaron par hamla kiya; unki phaansi se gussa faila. Meerut ke sipahi Delhi gaye aur boodhe Mughal Bahadur Shah Zafar ko neta ghoshit kiya. Kanpur, Lucknow, Jhansi tak faila. Nana Saheb (Kanpur), Rani Lakshmibai (Jhansi; Tatia Tope ke saath Gwalior jeeta; 18 June 1858 ko shaheed — angrez afsar ne bhi kaha 'best and bravest'), Begum Hazrat Mahal (Awadh/Lucknow; Nepal mein sharan). Angrezon ka badla behad kroor tha. Vidroh ek kendriya netritva na hone se haara, par azaadi ka beej bo gaya. 1858 — Company ka raaj khatam, British Crown ka seedha raaj (British Raj); Queen Victoria ki ghoshna.

Anjaane mein hue kuch asar

Angrezon ne survey kiye, smaarakon ka adhyayan kiya, archaeology shuru ki — par hazaaron moortiyan, chitra, gehne, pandulipiyan Europe le gaye; aaj inhe wapas laane (repatriation) ki koshish chal rahi hai. 1785 mein Charles Wilkins ne Bhagavad Gita ka pehla angrezi anuvaad kiya. Hegel ne Sanskrit ki khoj ko 'naye mahadweep ki khoj' kaha. Seekh: raajneeti ek taraf bahti hai, par sanskriti ulti disha mein bhi beh sakti hai.

Universal Franchise and India's Electoral System

Har 18+ naagrik ka ek vote, ek mulya — Election Commission chunaav kaise karwata hai: Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, President, Vice President.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

'Bas voting hi to hai!' — itna josh kyon?

Chunaav ko 'Festival of Democracy' (loktantra ka tyohaar) kehte hain, kyonki is din desh ka har bada aadmi — ameer ho ya gareeb, padha-likha ho ya nahi — barabar ho jaata hai. Sabka vote ek jaisa ginta hai. Franchise / suffrage = vote dene ka adhikaar. Universal adult franchise = har 18 saal ya usse bade naagrik ko vote ka haq, jaati, dharm, ling, shiksha ya kamaai ki parwaah kiye bina. Ye Article 326 mein hai. Pehle umar 21 thi; 1988 mein 18 ki gayi. Koi kisi doosre ki jagah vote nahi de sakta, aur vote dene ke liye apne constituency (chunaav kshetra) ki voter list mein naam hona zaroori hai.

Hamare samvidhan nirmaata itne himmati kyon the?

1947 mein sirf ~14% log padhe-likhe the (mahilaayein ~8%). Kuch log kehte the ki vote sirf padhe-likhon ko do. Par samvidhan ne pehle din se sabko vote diya — aam aadmi par poora bharosa! Azaadi se pehle sirf ~13% Bharatiya vote de sakte the. Duniya mein kai jagah auraton ko vote ke liye lambi ladaai ladni padi (USA mein 1913 ki parade); Switzerland mein auraton ko vote 1971 mein mila, jabki India mein shuru se. 10th sadi ke Uttaramerur shilalekh mein bhi chunaav ka paardarshi tareeka tha — taad ke patton par naam likh kar ghade mein, aur ek chhota bachcha parchi nikaalta tha!

Kitna bada hai hamara chunaav?

2024 Lok Sabha: 96.8 crore se zyada voter, 543 seat, 10 lakh se zyada polling station. India ke voter UK ki poori aabadi ke 15 guna! Desh mein 31 lakh chune hue pratinidhi (13 lakh mahilaayein) aur 2.5 lakh+ local bodies. Phir bhi 2024 mein ~34% logon ne vote nahi diya — socho kyon?

Koi voter peeche na chhoote

Election Commission doordaraz ilaakon tak jaata hai — ek akeli mahila voter ke liye bhi polling station banaya gaya! 2024 mein pehli baar buzurg aur divyang log ghar se vote de sake; kuch logon ke liye postal vote; braille voter card, wheelchair aur ramp ke liye app. Shyam Saran Negi (Himachal ke teacher) — 1951 ke pehle general election ke pehle voter; 2017 mein 100 saal ki umar mein bhi vote diya!

Class ka chunaav — poora system samajh lo

Suryodaya School ki Class 8 mein Class Rep ka chunaav: Ahmed (safaai), Gurmat (peer coaching), Ravi (arts). Teacher Ms Usha = Election Officer. Secret ballot — kisi ko pata nahi kisne kise vote diya. Neha ke liye braille ballot. 33 vote — 1 khaali (invalid); Ahmed 8, Gurmat 12, Ravi 10. Gurmat jeeti — jabki use aadhe se kam vote mile! Yahi hai 'First-Past-the-Post' — jisko sabse zyada vote, wo jeeta. Agar koi kisi ko pasand na kare? NOTA (None Of The Above) — result nahi badalta, par sandesh jaata hai ki 'behtar ummeedwar chahiye'.

Election Commission of India (ECI) — chunaav ka referee

ECI ek swatantra samvaidhanik sanstha hai, 1950 mein bani. Ye Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, Vidhan Sabha, President aur Vice President ke chunaav karwata hai. Pehla aam chunaav 1951–52. Kaam: tareekhein tay karna, political party register karna, Model Code of Conduct lagu karna, poori prakriya dekhna. Dhaancha: Chief Election Commissioner + 2 Election Commissioners (desh); Chief Electoral Officer (state); District Election Officer, Returning Officer (chunaav karwata hai), Electoral Registration Officer (voter list). Panchayat aur nagar nigam ke chunaav State Election Commission karwata hai.

Polling booth ke andar kya hota hai?

1st polling officer: list mein naam aur ID check. 2nd: ungli par syaahi, parchi, signature. 3rd: parchi lekar ungli dekhta hai. Phir EVM (Electronic Voting Machine) par button dabao — beep! Saath mein VVPAT mein chhapi parchi dekh lo ki vote sahi gaya. India ki EVM-VVPAT Namibia aur Bhutan mein bhi use hui hai.

Model Code of Conduct — chunaav ke 'niyam-kaanoon'

Chunaav ke dauraan: sarkar nayi yojana ghoshit nahi kar sakti jo voter ko lalchaaye; sarkari afsar party ka prachaar nahi kar sakte; ummeedwar shaanti rakhein, gaali-galauch nahi; vote ke badle saadi, mixer ya note baantna saza laayak. Ye code 1960 mein Kerala mein partiyon ki sahmati se shuru hua, 1962 mein ECI ne failaaya, 1991 se sakhti. T.N. Seshan (CEC, 1990) ne voter ID, prachaar ke niyam aur kharche par nigraani laakar chunaav ko nidar aur saaf banaya.

Direct aur indirect chunaav — MP, MLA, PM, CM

Direct: hum seedhe vote dete hain — Lok Sabha ke MP, Vidhan Sabha ke MLA. Lok Sabha mein 84 seat SC aur 47 ST ke liye aarakshit. Jis party ya coalition (gathbandhan) ko Lok Sabha mein bahumat mile, uska neta Prime Minister; rajya mein Chief Minister. Indirect: chune hue log aage chunte hain — jaise class reps milkar school council chunein. Rajya Sabha: 245 mein se 233 MLAs chunte hain, 12 President nominate karte hain; seat aabadi ke hisaab se (UP zyada, Arunachal kam); kabhi bhang nahi hoti (Permanent House); term 6 saal, har 2 saal mein 1/3 retire; single transferable vote.

President aur Vice President kaise chune jaate hain?

President: Electoral College — dono sadan ke chune hue MPs + sabhi rajyon aur Delhi-Puducherry ke chune hue MLAs; single transferable vote. Nominated sadasya aur Legislative Council ke sadasya vote nahi dete — taaki sirf janta ke seedhe chune log hi President chunein. Vice President: dono sadanon ke chune aur nominated sabhi sadasya; wo Rajya Sabha ke Chairperson hote hain aur zaroorat par President ka kaam sambhalte hain.

Chunautiyaan — aur hamara kaam

Chunaav mein paise ka badhta asar, aapraadhik record wale ummeedwar, aur shehron mein voter apathy (vote ke prati udaaseenta). Ilaaj: jaagruk voter. Sahi sawaal poochho, soch-samajh kar vote do — ek jaagruk voter hi loktantra ka sabse bada rakshak hai.

Nalanda Vidyalaya ke bachche Delhi mein naye Parliament building gaye. Ye na gol hai na chaukor — tikona (triangle) jaisa, jo santulan aur sabko saath lene ka sanket hai. Isme green construction se bijli ~30% kam lagti hai. Deewaron par mor, kamal, adivasi kala, mandir ke design aur swatantrata senani; Ashokan emblem bhi. Purana bhavan (1920s mein bana) ab 'Samvidhan Sadan' kehlaata hai — wahin Samvidhan Sabha baithi thi.

Parliament kisse banti hai?

Parliament = President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha. Do sadan hone ko bicameral kehte hain (bi = do, cameral = kamra/sadan). Lok Sabha (House of the People / Lower House) — janta seedhe chunti hai; samvidhan mein adhiktam 550 sadasya ki baat. Rajya Sabha (Council of States / Upper House) — indirect chunaav. Dono mein seatein rajya ki aabadi ke hisaab se. 1952 se ab tak 17 Lok Sabha; 18th Lok Sabha June 2024 mein bani. Do sadan kyon? Kyonki India federal hai — taakat Centre, State aur local sarkar mein bant-ti hai, to rajyon ki awaaz ke liye alag sadan chahiye.

Hamara parliamentary system kahan se aaya?

Britain ke system se prerna li, par saath mein hamare apne anubhav bhi the: angrezi raaj mein thodi bhaagidaari, purane mahajanapadon ke ganrajya, aur gaon ki panchayat jahan buzurg milkar faisle lete the.

Sadan ko kaun chalata hai? — Speaker aur Sengol

Har sadan ka ek presiding officer hota hai. Lok Sabha ke sadasya Speaker chunte hain — jo bolne ki ijaazat deta, anushaasan rakhta, niyam lagu karta. Rajya Sabha ki adhyakshata Vice President karte hain. Speaker ki kursi ke paas Sengol rakha hai — sone ki parat wala chaandi ka raajdand, jo 14 August 1947 ko Pt. Nehru ko satta hastantaran ke prateek ke roop mein diya gaya. Ye Chola kaal ki parampara hai — yaad dilaata hai ki satta dharm se chale; upar Nandi nyay ka prateek. Sansad mein pehle 12 bhashaon mein anuvaad tha; ab Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Manipuri, Urdu, Sanskrit bhi — kul 18.

Legislature aur Executive — kaanoon banana vs lagu karna

Socho school mein ek committee niyam banaati hai (legislature) aur principal-teachers unhe lagu karte hain (executive). Union Executive = President, Vice President, aur Prime Minister ki adhyakshata mein Council of Ministers. Mantri dono sadanon ke MPs mein se chune jaate hain aur milkar (collectively) Lok Sabha ke prati jawabdeh hote hain. Parliament ke 4 kaam: (1) samvaidhanik — President/VP ka chunaav, samvidhan sanshodhan, separation of powers, federalism, Fundamental Rights aur Directive Principles ki raksha; (2) kaanoon banana; (3) executive ki jawabdehi; (4) paise ka hisaab.

Bill se Act — RTE ki apni zubaani

Bill = prastaavit kaanoon ka draft; paas hone par Act. RTE kehta hai: meri jad Directive Principles mein thi, par 10 saal mein lagu hona tha, nahi hua. 1990s mein court mein tark diya gaya ki shiksha Right to Life ka hissa hai. 86th Amendment, 2002 ne Article 21A joda — 6–14 saal ke bachchon ko muft aur anivarya shiksha. Bill Rajya Sabha mein aaya, committee ne jaancha, sabse bada sawaal — paisa! August 2009 mein Lok Sabha ne paas kiya, President ki manzoori mili — RTE Act 2009. Raasta: dono mein se kisi sadan mein introduction & reading → (kabhi-kabhi) Standing Committee → clause-by-clause charcha aur sanshodhan → vote → doosre sadan mein yahi → Presidential assent → Gazette mein soochna. Money Bill (tax, udhaar) sirf Lok Sabha mein, wo bhi President ki purv sifarish se.

Sarkar se sawaal kaise poochhe jaate hain?

Question Hour — aam taur par baithak ka pehla ghanta, jab MPs mantriyon se sawaal poochhte hain aur mantri ko jawab dena padta hai. Standing committees (alag-alag partiyon ke MPs) mantralayon se report maangti hain — jaise Health committee ne Ayush vibhag ke baare mein sifarish ki aur mantralay ne bataya ki 24 rajyon mein directorate hai. Parliament budget paas karke dekhti hai ki janta ka paisa kahan kharch hua. Kabhi-kabhi sadan mein kavita aur mazaak bhi — Nirmala Sitharaman ne Tirukkural ka doha padha ('jaise jeev barish ki aas mein, praja achchhe shaasan ki aas mein'); Sushma Swaraj aur Dr. Manmohan Singh ki shayari wali nok-jhonk (2011).

President, PM aur 'naitik zimmedari'

President = Head of State, executive ke naam-maatra (nominal) pramukh: PM aur mantri niyukt karte, sansad bulaate, bill par manzoori dete. Mantri parishad unhe salaah deti hai; par jab kisi ko spasht bahumat na mile, tab President apne vivek (discretion) se faisla le sakte hain. Prime Minister = asli (de facto) karyakari pramukh — Lok Sabha ke bahumat dal/gathbandhan ka neta; mantri parishad ki aguvaai, mantralayon mein taalmel, President ko salaah, rashtriya neetiyaan. Zyaadatar bill sarkar laati hai. Civil servants (afsar) neetiyaan lagu karte hain. 1956 mein rail mantri Lal Bahadur Shastri ne train haadse ki naitik zimmedari lekar istifa de diya — Nehru ne mana kiya, par wo nahi maane.

Teesra stambh — Judiciary aur 'checks and balances'

Judiciary (adaalatein) kaanoon ki vyakhya karti hai, jhagde suljhaati hai, Fundamental Rights bachaati hai — Samvidhan ki rakhwali. Agar Parliament ka banaya kaanoon ya executive ka kaam samvidhan ke khilaaf ho, to court rok sakti hai. Teen ang — legislature, executive, judiciary — ek-doosre par nazar rakhte hain taaki koi ek bahut taaqatwar na ho; ise separation of powers aur checks and balances kehte hain.

Rajyon mein bhi aisa hi dhaancha

Rajya mein Governor (President dwara niyukt) naam-maatra pramukh aur Chief Minister asli. CM Vidhan Sabha ke bahumat ka neta; mantri parishad Vidhan Sabha ke prati jawabdeh. Term 5 saal. Rajya ki vidhayika unicameral (sirf Vidhan Sabha) ya bicameral (Vidhan Sabha + Vidhan Parishad) — bicameral sirf 6: Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh. Vishay teen list mein: Union List (sirf Centre), State List (sirf rajya), Concurrent List (dono; takraav ho to Centre ka kaanoon chalega — jaise shiksha, isliye RTE poore desh mein). Karnataka ka Vidhana Soudha — Kengal Hanumanthaiah ka sapna.

Chunautiyaan — sansad kam kyon chal rahi?

Sansad saal mein 3 session: Budget, Monsoon, Winter; din mein ~6 ghante. Par baithakein ghat rahi hain: 1st Lok Sabha mein 677 sittings, 13th mein sirf 356. M. Venkaiah Naidu (2021): Rajya Sabha ki productivity 2004–14 mein ~78%, phir ~65%; 2018 mein sabse kam 35.75%. Kaaran: gair-haaziri, hungama, Question Hour mein rukaawat, aapraadhik record wale sadasya. Ilaaj: jaagruk naagrik. Atal Bihari Vajpayee: 'Sarkaarein aayengi, jaayengi... lekin yeh desh rehna chaahiye, is desh ka loktantra amar rehna chaahiye.'

Factors of Production

Koi bhi cheez banane ke 4 input — Land, Labour, Capital, Entrepreneurship — aur Technology jo inhe aur taaqatwar banati hai.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Ratna ka dhaba — production ki poori kahani

Tumhare kapde, joote, bag, phone — sab kisi production process se guzar kar tum tak aaye. Jo cheezein (inputs) maal ya seva banane mein lagti hain, unhe factors of production kehte hain. Misaal: Ratna ka highway par chhota restaurant 'Pause Point'. Shuru karne ke liye use chahiye tha: ek jagah (land), khaana banane-parosne wale log (labour — ab 7 log), bartan-furniture-gas aur kiraaye ke liye paisa (capital), aur ye sab jodne wala dimaag aur himmat — khud Ratna (entrepreneur). Aise kaam karne wali dukaan, factory, firm ko business / enterprise kehte hain.

1. Land — sirf zameen nahi!

Economics mein 'land' ka matlab sirf zameen ka tukda nahi — mitti, jungle, paani, hawa, dhoop, khanij, tel, gas — saare prakritik sansaadhan. (Chapter 1 yaad karo!) Business zameen ya to khareedta hai ya kiraaye (rent) par leta hai.

2. Labour aur Human Capital — mehnat vs hunar

Labour = production mein lagne wali shaaririk aur maansik mehnat — kisaan, mazdoor, badhai, teacher, doctor. Human capital = us mehnat ki quality — khaas skill, gyaan, kaabiliyat, anubhav. Do log ek jaisi mehnat karein par jiske paas behtar hunar hai, wo zyada achchha aur tez kaam karega. Human capital badhaane wale 3 facilitators: (a) shiksha aur training — civil engineering ka student design padhta hai, phir site par training se seekhta hai; (b) swasthya — swasth bachcha roz school aata aur behtar seekhta hai (cognitive vikas), swasth mazdoor zyada kaam karta; (c) samaajik-saanskritik maahaul — Japan ka kaizen ('lagaataar sudhaar', 1940s se), Germany ka work ethic (samay ki paabandi, detail, quality).

India ki takat aur chunautiyaan

Adult literacy (15+ umar, rozmarra ki chhoti baat padh-likh sake) — 2023 mein purush ~85%, mahila ~70% (World Bank). Economic Survey 2024: ~65% Bharatiya 35 saal se kam umar ke! Isse demographic dividend mil sakta hai — jab kaam karne wale zyada aur un par nirbhar kam hon to desh tezi se badhta hai. Par shart: achchhi shiksha, swasthya, training. Socho Harsh ne pita ki naukri jaane par school chhoda — badaa hokar uske hunar par kya asar padega? Aur yaad rakho — har kaam zaroori hai: safaai karmachari, kisaan, doctor, koi na ho to kya hoga?

Hamari purani 'skill' parampara

Purane India mein kaam ek saadhna tha — poori lagan se, devta ya grahak ko arpan ki tarah. Auzaaron ki pooja hoti thi — aaj bhi Vishwakarma puja / Ayudha puja. Kaam = kala (art) + vidya (knowledge), pidhi dar pidhi. Shilpa shastra mein moorti, chitra, imaarat, lakdi aur gehno ke naap, mudra, rang tak likhe the. Mandir banane wale shilpi parivaar aksar tayyar mandir dekh bhi nahi paate the, phir bhi utkrishtata ke liye kaam karte. Stitched shipbuilding — 2,000 saal se zyada purani takneek: lakdi ke takhte keelon (nails) ki jagah rassi se silkar jahaz banaye jaate, jo lachiley hone se Hind Mahasagar mein aaram se chalte. 16th sadi mein Europeans ke aane ke baad ye ghata; aaj sirf chhoti machhli-naav mein.

3. Capital — paisa aur machine

Capital = paisa + insaan ki banayi tikaau cheezein jo production mein kaam aayein: machine, auzaar, gaadi, thela, computer, dukaan, factory. Paisa kahan se? Pehle apni bachat, parivaar, dost. Kam pade to bank loan — Ratna ne bhi liya aur kisht ke saath interest (byaaj) chukaaya. Badi companiyan stock market mein shares bech kar janta se paisa uthaati hain, aur munaafe mein se dividend deti hain.

4. Entrepreneurship — sapna + jokhim

Entrepreneur (udyami) wo jo: ek samasya pehchaane aur naye hal ke liye adig rahe, paisa-samay laga kar risk le, baaki teen factors ko jode, zaroori faisle le, aur apne naye vichaar se samaaj ka bhala kare. Wo naukri bhi paida karta hai. Startup = kam saadhanon se, technology ke sahare tezi se badhne wala naya udyam. Misaal: J.R.D. Tata (janm 1904) — 1932 mein India ki pehli airline Tata Airlines (aage chal kar Air India); steel, car, bijli, chemicals; mazdooron ka khayal; 1992 Bharat Ratna.

Technology — madadgaar (enabler)

Technology = vaigyanik gyaan ka istemal. Isse kam input mein zyada maal banta hai. UPI se ek click mein payment, mausam ki pehle soochna, GPS se chhota raasta, drone se khaad ka chhidkaav, surgery mein robot. Chitthi ki jagah email. Par har purani cheez nahi hat-ti — pulley aur wheelbarrow aaj bhi kaam aate hain. Online seekhne ke liye SWAYAM (Class 9 se, muft MOOCs — robotics, aquaculture, textile printing), naukri dhoondhne ke liye National Career Service portal.

Sab factors kaise jude hain?

Ye sab puzzle ke tukdon jaise milkar kaam karte hain; ek bhi gaayab ho to kaam ruk jaaye. Kheti, construction, hastkala — zyada mazdoor chahiye (labour-intensive); semiconductor chip, satellite — zyada machine-paisa (capital-intensive). Nayi takneek anupaat badal deti hai — kheti mein machine se mazdoor kam; 3-D printing se marti hastkalaon ka bada utpaadan. Supply chain = utpaadan se bikri tak ka poora network; door ke inputs par nirbharta se COVID-19 mein utpaadan ruk gaya. 2025 mein India duniya ka doosra sabse bada mobile phone nirmaata (China ke baad) — R&D → zameen/factory → kachcha maal → assembly + software → testing → mass production → dukaanon tak.

Zimmedari — prakriti aur mazdoor dono ke prati

Tamil Nadu ki chamda factories naukri deti hain par kachra nadi-mitti ganda karta hai; puraane phone recycle na hon to lead aur mercury zameen-paani mein. Isliye producer ko sansaadhan sambhal kar, kachra kam, pradooshan rokna chahiye. Mazdooron ke liye: uchit vetan aur surakshit maahaul, skill training, adhikaar aur suraksha (bhedbhaav nahi, health care, chhutti). CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) — 2014 mein India duniya ka pehla desh bana jisne kaanoon banaya: companiyan pichhle 3 saal ke ausat munaafe ka 2% samaaj-kalyan par kharch karein.

World Geography: Some Glimpses

Duniya ki sair — map projection, mahasagar, reef, aur har mahadweep ki khaas bhoo-aakritiyaan (landforms) aur unse judi zindagi.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Dharti — Bhudevi, Vasudha

Adivasi samudaay aur India ki parampara dharti ko maa maanti hai — Bhudevi ya Vasudha. Hamari zindagi door-door ki cheezon se judi hai: door dakshini Hind Mahasagar tak Ganga ke maidaan ke monsoon par asar daalta hai! Chalo 'blue planet' ki ek tez sair karte hain.

Map kabhi poora sach kyon nahi dikhata? — santre ka chhilka

Dharti poori gol nahi — oblate spheroid hai: equator par thodi phooli, dhruvon (poles) par thodi chapti (ghoomne ki wajah se). Ek santra lo, chhilka utaaro aur bina phaade zameen par seedha bichhao — namumkin! Isi tarah gol dharti ko chapte kaagaz par dikhane (map projection) mein kuch na kuch bigaad (distortion) hota hi hai. Mercator projection — aakaar (shape) aur disha sahi, isliye jahaz chalane mein kaam ka; par dhruvon ke paas size bahut bada — Greenland Africa jitna lagta hai aur India se bada, jabki asal mein India se chhota hai! Robinson projection — size aur shape dono mein santulan. Isliye kai projection dekho. Professional log latitude-longitude wale sateek map use karte hain. Physical map ki bhasha: neela = paani, hara = maidaan, peela/bhoora = pathaar, gehra bhoora = oonche pahad, halka peela = registan, safed = barf.

Mahasagar — dharti ka neela rang

Mahasagar (khaara paani) dharti ki satah ka ~71% aur dharti ke kul paani ka 97%. Ye jalvaayu ko santulit karte hain — garmi sokh kar dheere chhodte hain, baadal-barish mein madad. Hamari lagbhag aadhi oxygen mahasagar ke nanhe plankton banaate hain (chlorophyll + dhoop + CO₂ se khaana). Sabse bada jeev blue whale — 30 m se lamba, ~1,50,000 kg! Paanch mahasagar: Pacific (sabse bada aur gehra), Atlantic (vyapaar ka vyast raasta), Indian (garam paani, vyapaar), Arctic (sabse chhota-uthla, polar bear, walrus), Southern (Antarctica ko ghere, penguin-seal). Samundar ka tal bhi pahad-ghaati-maidaan wala hai!

Samundar ki 'nadiyaan', gehri khaai aur reef

Ocean currents samundar mein behti vishaal nadiyon jaise — garam (equator se dhruv ki taraf) aur thandi (dhruv se equator ki taraf). Gulf Stream garam paani Western Europe le jaati hai, isliye UK usi latitude ki doosri jagahon se garam rehta hai. Mariana Trench — sabse gehri khaai; uska Challenger Deep 11,000 m se zyada gehra — Everest se bhi 2,000 m zyada! Wahan andhera hai, isliye jeev dhoop ki jagah chemicals se urja lete hain. Great Barrier Reef (Australia ke uttar-poorvi tat) — 2,000 km lamba, ~3,50,000 sq km, UNESCO site; reefs = 'rainforests of the sea' — ~1/4 samudri jeevon ka ghar. Pradooshan, zyada machhli pakadna, climate change se coral bleaching.

Sea, Gulf, Bay, Strait, Canal — farak samjho

Sea — bada, aanshik ghira khaara paani (Arabian Sea). Gulf — zameen mein gehra ghusa bada hissa (Persian Gulf — hazaaron saal India-Europe vyapaar). Bay — chhota, chauda inlet; bandargah ke liye surakshit (Bay of Bengal — duniya ki sabse badi bay). Strait — do samundaron ko jodne wala patla raasta (Strait of Malacca). Canal — insaan ka banaya raasta (Suez Canal, 1869 — Red Sea ko Mediterranean se jodta; India se Europe ka sabse chhota samudri raasta).

Asia — Tibet, Gobi, Steppes, Urals

Tibetan Plateau — duniya ka sabse ooncha aur bada pathaar, 'Roof of the world'; Indo-Australian aur Eurasian tectonic plates ki takkar se bana (Himalaya bhi); ~2,500 km × 1,000 km, ausat oonchaai 4,500 m+; Indus, Brahmaputra, Yangtze ka srot; dhruvon ke baad sabse zyada meetha paani — 'Third Pole'. Registan = saal mein 25 cm se kam barish; zameen ka ~1/5; ~1 arab log rehte hain. Gobi (Mongolia + uttar China) — Mongolia = 'Land of the Eternal Blue Sky' (8–9 mahine dhoop), sardi −30°C; solar-wind urja. Eurasian Steppes — 8,000 km lamba ghaas ka maidaan, carbon sink; Siberian crane ka vishraam; yurt (ghumantu ki jhopdi); steppe tortoise 1968 mein chaand ki yaatra karne wale pehle jeevon mein! Ural — purane pahad, 2,500 km, Europe aur Asia ki seema; loha, taamba, platinum, sona, tel, gas; taiga (coniferous jungle) aur tundra (jamii mitti — permafrost; pighle to greenhouse gas nikalti).

Pahad = 'water towers'

Pahad tectonic shakti, jwalamukhi aur kataav se bante hain. Ye jalvaayu tay karte, jaiv-vividhta ke hotspot hote, deshon ki seema bante, aur oonchaai par barf-glacier pighal kar meetha paani dete hain — isliye 'water towers'.

Europe aur Africa

European Plain — duniya ke sabse bade maidaanon mein; upjaau mitti — gehu, jau, rye, aaloo (duniya ka sabse bada gehu hissa Europe se); koyla-tel-gas; Moscow, Berlin, Paris, Amsterdam, Warsaw; kai ladaaiyon ka maidaan. Alps — 1,200 km, ardh-chandra aakaar; Mont Blanc; phool edelweiss (Austria ka rashtriya phool, sunflower parivaar); Rhine aur Danube ko paani. Sahara — sabse bada garam registan; 30–46°C (kabhi 50°C+), barish 25 cm se kam; oasis, khajoor, cactus, bade kaan wala fennec fox, zameen ke neeche ghar; sona-namak ke purane vyapaari raaste. Savannah — ushnakatibandhiya ghaas maidaan; haathi, sher, zebra, giraffe; geela-sookha mausam. Nile — duniya ki sabse lambi nadi ~6,650 km; Misr (Egypt) ki sabhyata ka paalna.

South America

Andes — mahadweep ki sabse badi parvat shrinkhla (pashchimi tat); Aconcagua — Western Hemisphere ki sabse oonchi choti; Bolivia ka Uyuni — duniya ka sabse bada salt flat; taamba, zinc, chaandi, lithium; sirf 1/4 mool vanaspati-jeev bache — conservation hotspot; Andean condor (pankh 3.2 m). Amazon — paani ki maatra mein sabse badi nadi; Peru ke Andes se Atlantic tak; Amazon rainforest — 'Lungs of the Earth', ~400 adivasi samudaay; mazedaar — Sahara ki dhool udkar Amazon tak phosphorus pahunchaati hai! Atacama (Chile) — duniya ki sabse sookhi jagahon mein, ~1,000 km; din 40°C, raat 5°C; taare dekhne ki behtareen jagah; NASA ne Mars rover test kiye; par fast fashion ke phenke kapdon ka dher — UN: 'environmental and social emergency'.

North America aur Australia-Antarctica

Colorado Plateau (USA) — 3,30,000 sq km; avsaadi chattaanon ki parten, laakhon saal ke kataav se Grand Canyon (~50 lakh paryatak/saal); koyla, uranium. Rockies — 4,800 km; Native Americans ka ghar, ab 'reservations' mein. Yellowstone — Old Faithful geyser; 1995 mein bhediye (wolves) chhode gaye jinhone elk hiran ki aabadi ghataayi aur vanaspati laut aayi — ecosystem restoration. Appalachian — duniya ke sabse purane pahadon mein; Cherokee samudaay. Great Lakes — 5 vishaal meethe paani ki jheelein; pradooshan ki samasya. Australia — zyaadatar Great Australian Desert (Great Victoria Desert); dingo, wallaby, jungli oont (19th sadi mein India-Afghanistan se laaye gaye); Spinifex log 25,000 saal se. Antarctica — duniya ka sabse bada aur sabse thanda registan (14 million sq km, India ka 4 guna se zyada)!

Pichhe mud kar — Odisha ke do vidroh (Paika aur Sambalpur)

Paika Sangram (1817), Khorda (Odisha): 1803 mein Company ki madad karne ke baad bhi Raja ka ilaaka chheena gaya aur Jagannath mandir par adhikaar bhi. Rajguru Jayee Rajguru ko 1806 mein phaansi. Paika (paaramparik kisaan-sainik) ki lagaan-mukt zameen par tax, namak mehenga, kaudi (cowrie) mudra band. Buxi Jagabandhu ne Paika aur adivasiyon ke saath Puri par qabza kiya; Lord Jagannath Odia sampravutta ka prateek bane. May 1817 tak dabaaya gaya, par Buxi ne guerrilla yuddh jaari rakha, 1825 mein samarpan. Sambalpur Uprising (1827–1862) — angrezon ne Surendra Sai ko raja nahi maana aur raajya hadap liya. 1857 mein jail se chhoot kar bhaai Udanta Sai, Chhabila Sai ke saath pahadi qilon se guerrilla hamle; Kudopali (Dec 1857) mein Chhabila Sai ke saath 57 yoddha shaheed. Dhoke se pakde gaye Surendra Sai 37 saal qaid mein rahe aur wahin mrityu.

India's Long Road to Independence

1858 se 1947 tak — sudhaarak, Congress, Swadeshi, krantikaari, Gandhi ke andolan, Bose ki INA, Quit India, aur Azaadi ke saath Vibhajan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Itni bhashaaein, itne dharm — phir bhi ek!

Socho ek desh jahan saikdon bhashaayein, alag-alag dharm aur reeti-riwaaj hon — phir bhi sab milkar duniya ke sabse taaqatwar samrajya ko chunauti dein. Yahi hai Bharat ki azaadi ki kahani. ICHR ki 'Dictionary of Martyrs' mein ~14,000 shaheedon ke naam hain — is chapter mein bahut kam naam hain, par jo naam nahi hain wo kam mahaan nahi. Sabka lakshya ek tha, par raaste alag: Moderates, Nationalists, krantikaari, Gandhi ka satyagraha.

1858 ki 'shaahi ghoshna' — vaade aur sachchaai

1858 mein Queen Victoria ke naam se raaj: London mein Secretary of State for India, India mein Viceroy (Governor General). Vaada kiya — ~560 rajaon ka samman (par 'paramountcy' angrezon ki), praja ki barabar dekhbhaal, zabardasti Isaai dharm nahi. Sachchaai: rajaon ke darbar mein Resident dakhal deta; shoshan, akaal jaari; Bharatiyon ko kam tankhwah, club-train mein alag; 1878 — Arms Act (Bharatiya hathiyaar nahi rakh sakte, angrez rakh sakte) aur Vernacular Press Act (Bharatiya bhasha ke akhbaaron par sensor).

Samaaj sudhaarak — andar se badlaav

Brahmo Samaj (1828, Calcutta) — Raja Ram Mohan Roy: baal vivah aur jaati-bhed ka virodh, vidhvaaon ke adhikaar, ladkiyon ki shiksha. Arya Samaj (1875, Bombay) — Swami Dayananda Saraswati: 'Vedon ki or lauto', janm-aadharit jaati ka virodh, vidhva punarvivah, stri shiksha. Shuddhi andolan. Aur Iswar Chandra Vidyasagar, Syed Ahmad Khan, Jyotirao aur Savitribai Phule — ladkiyon aur pichhde vargon ke liye school.

Do prernaayein — 'Vande Mataram' aur Vivekananda

Bankim Chandra ne 1875 mein 'Bande Mataram' likha (Bengali mein 'va' nahi hota, isliye 'Ba'), 1882 ke Anandamath mein joda. Tagore ne 1896 Congress session mein gaaya; 1950 mein rashtriya geet. Swami Vivekananda (Narendranath Datta, janm 1863; guru Sri Ramakrishna) — 1893 Chicago ki Parliament of World's Religions mein prabhaavi bhaashan; Ramakrishna Mission; 'Utho, jaago aur lakshya tak mat ruko'; Jamsetji Tata ko research sanstha ki salaah di — wahi aage Indian Institute of Science bani. Janmdin 12 January = National Youth Day.

Congress ka janm aur badhta gussa

Angrez afsar A.O. Hume ne naukri chhodi aur 1885 mein Bombay mein Indian National Congress ka pehla session hua — Dadabhai Naoroji saath, pehle adhyaksh W.C. Bonnerjee, 72 delegates. Shuru mein Congress British raaj ki 'wafaadaar' thi aur sirf chhote sudhaar maangti thi — angrezon ne zyaadatar ansuna kiya. Gussa badha: Kuka/Namdhari andolan (Baba Ram Singh, Punjab) — videshi maal ka bahishkaar, apni daak aur adaalat (baad ke boycott se pehle hi!); 1872 mein 65 Kukas ko bina mukadme top se udaya gaya, Baba Ram Singh ko Burma bhej diya. 1897 Pune mein plague ke zulm par Chapekar bhaiyon ne Plague Commissioner ko maara. Tilak ne 'Kesari' akhbaar, Ganesh Chaturthi aur Shivaji utsav se logon ko jagaaya — 'Lokmanya'; 'Gita Rahasya'. Birsa Munda — Ulgulaan (1899–1900), 'Mahaan Halchal'; 24 saal ki umar mein jail mein mrityu.

1905 — Bengal ka bantwara aur Swadeshi

Viceroy Curzon ne 1905 mein Bengal baanta — bahaana 'achchha prashasan', asal mein divide and rule (Muslim-bahul poorv, Hindu-bahul pashchim). Logon ne ek-doosre ko rakhi baandhi, sadkon par Vande Mataram gaaya (angrezon ne rok lagaayi!). Do hathiyaar: Swadeshi (apne desh ka maal) aur Boycott (videshi kapda, cheeni ki holi; sarkari school-court ka bahishkaar). Swadeshi pehle se: Jamsetji Tata — Swadeshi Mills 1886; Punjab National Bank 1895 (Lala Lajpat Rai); P.C. Ray — Bengal Chemicals 1901. 1906: Bipin Chandra Pal ka akhbaar 'Bande Mataram' (Aurobindo sampaadak) — 'absolute national autonomy'; Bengal National College (principal Aurobindo; ab Jadavpur University); Calcutta Congress (Naoroji) ne Swaraj, Swadeshi, Boycott, National Education apnaaya. 'Bharat Mata' — Abanindranath Tagore ki painting, Subramania Bharati ki kavitaayein. Usi saal Dhaka mein All-India Muslim League bani. 1911 mein bantwara radd, raajdhani Calcutta se Delhi.

Surat 1907 — Moderates vs Nationalists

Surat session (1907) mein Congress do tukdon mein bant gayi. Moderates (Naoroji, Gokhale, Pherozeshah Mehta, Surendranath Banerjee, Rashbehari Ghosh) — chhote sudhaar, British samrajya ke andar dheere swashaasan. Nationalists — Lal-Bal-Pal (Lala Lajpat Rai 'Lion of Punjab', Bal Gangadhar Tilak, Bipin Chandra Pal) aur Aurobindo — poori azaadi. Angrez unhe 'Extremists' kehte, par wo khud ko bas 'Nationalists' kehte the.

Krantikaari — jaan hatheli par

Anushilan Samiti (1902), Abhinav Bharat (1904, V.D. Savarkar), akhbaar 'Yugantar'. 1908: Khudiram Bose (18 saal) aur Prafulla Chaki ne jaj Kingsford par bomb phenka (galti se do angrez mahilaayein mareen); Khudiram ko phaansi — muskurate hue. Alipore Bomb Case — Aurobindo ka mukadma C.R. Das ne lada, Aurobindo bari; Barin ko Cellular Jail. Tilak ko 6 saal Mandalay (vakeel Jinnah). Tilak: 'Swaraj mera janmsiddh adhikaar hai aur main use lekar rahunga.' 1910 mein Aurobindo Pondicherry gaye — 'Sri Aurobindo', integral yoga. 1909 Nashik (Anant Kanhere), 1911 Vanchinathan (V.O. Chidambaram Pillai ki Swadeshi Steam Navigation ke liye), 1912 Viceroy Hardinge par Delhi mein bomb. Ghadar Party (America; Lala Hardayal, Sohan Singh Bhakna) — 1914–15 sena mein vidroh ki koshish; Kartar Singh Sarabha (19 saal) ko phaansi. Rash Behari Bose Japan chale gaye. Bagha Jatin — 9 Sept 1915, Balasore mein 75 minute ki golibaari. Cellular Jail ('Kala Pani') — Andaman mein ekaant qaid, haath se tel nikalwaana.

Poorvottar ka yogdaan

Khasi vidroh (1829–33) — Tirot Sing; Singpho vidroh (Assam); 1857 mein Maniram Dewan aur Piyali Baruah ko phaansi; 1942 mein Kanaklata Barua aur Kushal Konwar shaheed; Manipur ki Rani Gaidinliu — 13 saal ki umar se angrezon se ladeen.

Home Rule aur Gandhi ka aagman

1916: Lucknow mein Congress phir ek; Lucknow Pact — Congress ne Muslim League ki separate electorates maani. Tilak aur Annie Besant ka Home Rule Movement — samrajya ke andar swashaasan. M.K. Gandhi (janm 1869, Porbandar) — South Africa mein rang-bhed jhela, satyagraha ('satya ka aagrah' — ahinsa + kasht sehna) banaaya; 1915 mein India lautey. Local satyagraha: Champaran (Bihar, neel kisaan), Ahmedabad mill mazdoor, Kheda (lagaan). Bardoli 1928 — Vallabhbhai Patel → 'Sardar'.

1919 — Rowlatt Act aur Jallianwala Bagh

World War I ke baad azaadi ki jagah Rowlatt Act (1919) — police kisi ko bhi bina mukadme qaid kar sake. 13 April 1919, Baisakhi, Amritsar: shaant bheed par General Dyer ne mukhya raasta band karke ~10 minute mein 1,650 goliyaan chalwaayin; log kuein mein koode. Angrezi aankda ~400 mare, 1,200+ ghayal; Bharatiya andaaza 1,000+. Dyer = 'Butcher of Amritsar'. Tagore ne knighthood lauta di. Britain ne aaj tak maafi nahi maangi. 20 saal baad Udham Singh ne London mein O'Dwyer ko maara.

Non-Cooperation aur Khilafat (1920–22)

Gandhi ne Rowlatt ke khilaaf upvaas-prarthana, phir Non-Cooperation — videshi maal, school, court ka bahishkaar, khadi (haath ka suta-buna kapda) — 'ek saal mein Swaraj'. Aug 1920 mein Tilak ki mrityu; Nagpur session (Dec 1920) mein aupchaarik shuruaat. Khilafat (1919) — Ali bhai aur Maulana Abul Kalam Azad; Turkey ke Khalifa ke samarthan mein; Gandhi ne Hindu-Muslim ekta ke liye joda. Jinnah ne 1920 mein Congress chhodi (dharm aur azaadi ko mat milao). Feb 1922 Chauri Chaura (UP) — bheed ne thana jalaaya, policewale mare → Gandhi ne andolan wapas le liya (Motilal, Jawaharlal, C.R. Das, Subhas naaraaz). Motilal-Das ki Swaraj Party. 1924 mein Turkey ne Khilafat hi khatam kar di.

Simon Go Back, Bhagat Singh aur Purna Swaraj

A Journey Through Indian Architecture

Harappa ke shehron se colonial imaaraton tak — stupa, gufaayein, baavdi, mandir, masjid, qile, maqbare, haveli aur church.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Architecture kya hai?

Architecture (vaastukala) = imaarat ka design aur nirmaan, jisme kaam ki baat (functional) aur sundarta (aesthetic) dono hon. Ye kala bhi hai aur bahut technical vigyaan bhi — aur India hazaaron saal se isme maahir hai. Chalo ek yaatra par chalte hain — har padaav par ek khaas tarah ki imaarat. Ek mazedaar baat yaad rakhna: is chapter ki kisi bhi purani imaarat mein cement nahi laga!

Shuruaat — Harappa ke smart shehar

Harappan sabhyata bharatiya upmahadweep mein sabse pehli town planning ka udaharan hai — sadkein disha ke hisaab se, lagbhag har ghar mein nahaane ki jagah, aur gande paani ke liye naaliyon ka jaal. Zyaadatar shehar eent ke bane (nadi ke maidaan mein mitti khoob thi). Mohenjo-daro (Sindh) ke bade ghar mein shaayad naukar neeche aur maalik upar rehte — dhool-shor se door! Dholavira (Rann of Kachchh) mein pathar ka istemal (wahin milta tha); sabse oonche hisse 'Castle' ka darwaza, pehredaar ka kamra, chamakdaar pathar ka khambha, bade jalaashay.

Stupa — Buddha ki yaad mein gumbad

4th/3rd sadi BCE se Buddh dharm ke saath stupa bane — gumbad jaise dhaanche jinme Buddha ya bhikshuon ke avshesh (relics) rakhe jaate. Shuru mein Buddha ko insaan ke roop mein nahi, prateekon se dikhaate the — pairon ke nishaan, dharma chakra, ya stupa. Sanchi (MP) ka mukhya stupa — Ashoka ne shuru karwaya; chaar bade torana (sajaavati darwaaze, jin par shehar-gaon ke drishya) aur vedika (railing) ke andar parikrama. Bharhut (MP) mein stupa ki pooja ke chitra aur daan-daataaon ke naam. Jain dharm mein bhi stupa hote hain.

Pahad kaat kar bani gufaayein

Rock-cut architecture = chattaan ko seedha kaat kar gufa banana — tikaau aur kaam ki. Udayagiri–Khandagiri (Bhubaneswar ke paas) — 2nd sadi BCE se, zyaadatar Jain muniyon ke rehne ke liye; ek mein Kharavela ka shilalekh. Maharashtra ki Ajanta aur Ellora — hazaaron tonne pathar chhaant kar Kailasha mandir ek hi chattaan se! Ellora ki gufa no. 10 — stupa ke saath Buddha ki badi moorti, chhat lakdi ke shahteeron jaisi taraashi; bagal ke galiyaare mein bhikshu pradakshina (ghadi ki disha mein parikrama) karte — ye parampara mandiron mein bhi aayi. Socho, sirf hathode aur chheni se ye sab!

Kuch khaas mandir

Sharada Peetha (J&K) — devi Sharada (Saraswati), 6th–12th sadi ka gyaan kendra, Sharada lipi; isliye Kashmir = 'Sharada Desh'. Hidimba Devi mandir (Manali, 16th sadi) — Bheem ki patni Hidimba; paas mein beta Ghatotkacha ka mandir. Jageshwar (Almora, Uttarakhand) — ~124 pathar ke mandir, 8th–18th sadi. Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib) — Guru Ramdas ne neev rakhi, Guru Arjan Dev ne 1588 mein nirmaan aage badhaaya; Sikhon ka sabse pavitra sthal.

Paani ki imaaratein — monsoon wale desh ki samajh

Monsoon ki wajah se India mein paani jama karne ke anokhe tareeke bane — gaon ke talaab se lekar Grand Anicut tak. Harappa walon ne trapezoid aakaar ki eenton se aise kuein banaaye jo andar ki taraf dhah nahi sakte — eentein ek-doosre mein phans jaati thin! Shringaverpur (UP; Ramayana ka Shringaverapura, jahan Ram-Sita-Lakshman ne Ganga paar ki) — Ganga ki baadh se bharne wale jude hue jalaashay; pehle hisse mein gaad (silt) baith jaati. Stepwell (vav/baori) — Gujarat-Rajasthan; Rani ki Vav — kai manzil, saikdon moortiyan; paani bhi aur thandi jagah par baith kar gup-shup bhi! Mandiron ke saath pushkarini (Vijayanagara). Aaj kai pradooshan aur laaparwaahi ke shikaar.

Mandir ki banawat — ek insaan ke shareer jaisi

Classical mandir Gupta kaal mein shuru hue; niyam shilpashastra mein. Hisse: pravesh dwar (gopuram — dakshin; torana — uttar), mandapa (khambhon wale hall), garbhagriha (sabse andar, jahan murti). Garbhagriha ke upar shikhar: uttar mein shikhara, dakshin mein vimana. Disha aam taur par poorv-pashchim, garbhagriha poorv ki or. Mandir Purusha (brahmaandiya purush) ka roop — shikhar sir, chabootra pair. Mandir mein jaana = bahar ki duniya se andar ki duniya ki yaatra. Badi chaardeewaari = prakara. Mandir pooja ke saath shiksha, kala, nritya-sangeet, samaaj aur vyapaar ke kendra bhi the.

Chaar mashhoor mandir

Brihadishvara (Thanjavur) — Rajaraja Chola I, ~1010; 60 m+ ooncha piramid jaisa vimana; ek hi pathar ka vishaal Nandi; granite par nakkaashi. Lingaraja (Bhubaneswar) — 11th sadi ki shuruaat; 3 bade mandapa; sandstone aur laterite. Dono Shiv ko samarpit. Dilwara (Mount Abu) — 5 Jain mandir, 11th–13th sadi, Solanki/Chaulukya; Aravalli ka safed marble, har satah par nakkaashi. Bishnupur (West Bengal) — 17th–18th sadi ke terracotta (pakki mitti) mandir; Radha-Krishna, Ram ke drishya; haath se banakar bhatti mein pakaaye. Inke liye ganit aur advanced geometry chahiye thi — jaise Egypt ke pyramid ya Europe ke Gothic girjaghar.

Masjid ki banawat

Masjid mein prarthana hall Mecca ki taraf; gumbad (dome) aur minaar (jahan se muezzin azaan deta). Diwaar mein mihrab — Mecca ki disha batane wala aala. Shukrawar ki namaaz wali badi masjid = Jama Masjid (jama = jamaavda, masjid = masjid). Ahmedabad Jama Masjid — Sultan Ahmed Shah I, 15th sadi; peela sandstone; mandir jaise khambhe, jaali — Hindu-Jain kaarigaron ki madad; bhookamp mein minaaron ke upari hisse gire. Sidi Saiyyed jaali mashhoor. Ise Indo-Islamic shaili kehte hain — gumbad, mehraab (nukili aur corbelled), jaali, phool-patti; Rajasthan mein jharokha aur chhatri bhi. Delhi Jama Masjid — India ki sabse badi; Shah Jahan (mid-17th sadi, Shahjahanabad); ~5,000 mazdoor; laal sandstone + safed marble; 3 darwaaze, 40 m ke 2 minaar, 3 gumbad; aangan mein 25,000 log.

Qile aur mahal

Qile mahatvapurn raaston ke paas, aksar pahad par. Mehrangarh (Jodhpur) — 15th sadi ke beech se; laal sandstone; andar mahal, zanana-mardana, astabal; ab museum. Saamne Jaswant Thada — cenotaph (smarak jahan dafn nahi kiya gaya). Jodhpur ke neele ghar — chune mein copper sulphate, deemak bhagaane ke liye! Agra Red Fort — Akbar ne punarnirman kiya; laal sandstone Dholpur se; Shah Jahan ne marble mahal banaaye (Musamman Burj — wahin Aurangzeb ne unhe qaid rakha, Khas Mahal); Yamuna ka paani Persian wheel se tanki mein, phir mitti ke pipe se phuhaare — 'eco-friendly AC'! Orchha (MP, Betwa kinaare) — Bundela Rajput, 16th sadi; Raja Mahal, Jahangir Mahal, Rai Praveen Mahal; Ram Raja mandir. Mysuru Palace (Amba Vilas) — Wadiyar; 20th sadi ki shuruaat (purana lakdi ka mahal jal gaya); Dasara mein sone ka singhasan; ~1 lakh bulb.

Maqbare — Humayun aur Taj

Islam aur Isaai dharm mein shav dafnaaye jaate hain, isliye mausoleum (bade maqbare) bane; Buddh, Jain, Hindu mein daah-sanskaar — isliye prachin rajaon ke maqbare nahi. Maqbaron mein samaroopta (symmetry), baag bhi. Humayun's Tomb (Delhi) — patni Bega Begum ne 1558 mein; Persian vaastukaar; Dara Shikoh ki qabr bhi; chhe-konon wala taara — Yahudi 'Star of David', Islam mein 'Seal of Solomon', Hindu tantra mein Purusha-Prakriti ka mel. Taj Mahal — Shah Jahan, Mumtaz Mahal ke liye, mid-17th sadi; teen sanskritiyon ka sangam: charbagh aur bade mehraab (Persian), Quran ki calligraphy (Arabi), chhatri-kamal-jaali (Bharatiya); marble mein rangeen pathar jade (inlay). 1970s mein pradooshan se peela pada → Supreme Court mein PIL, 1990s ke beech udyogon par rok.

Ghar, church aur angrezi daur

Purane ameer gharon mein beech mein aangan aur chaaron taraf kamre — ye design Harappa ke bade gharon mein bhi tha (4,500 saal pehle)! Shekhawati havelis (Rajasthan) — rangeen frescoes; ab kai hotel/museum. Chettinad houses (Tamil Nadu) — Southeast Asia se vyapaar karne wale vyapaari; Burma ka sagwan, Italy ka marble; aangan mein papad-mirch sukhaana! Churches: kuch European shaili mein — St. Philomena's (Mysuru), Gothic; Kerala mein sthaaniya roop — St. Thomas Orthodox, Mulanthuruthy (saamne mandir jaisa stambha), Kalloopara (dhalwaan khaprail chhat). Indo-Saracenic (British Raj, mid-19th–20th sadi) — Islamic + Hindu + Gothic: Madras High Court (1892), Victoria Memorial (Kolkata), Mysuru Palace. Hamara Fundamental Duty: 'hamari mili-juli sanskriti ki samriddh virasat ko mahatva dena aur bachaana'.

The Role of the Judiciary in Our Society

Nyay kya hai, kaanoon aur nyay ka rishta, Supreme Court–High Court–District Court ka dhaancha, PIL, tribunals aur Lok Adalat.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Kisi ne tumhare saath galat kiya — ab kya?

Socho kisi ne tumhara tiffin chura liya. Tum chahoge — use saza mile, wo maafi maange, ya tumhe tiffin wapas mile. Par jis par ilzaam hai, wo kahega — 'pehle meri baat to suno!' Yahi hai nyay (justice) ka saar. Hamari Preamble har naagrik ko saamajik, aarthik aur raajnaitik nyay ka vaada karti hai. Judiciary (nyaypaalika) sarkar ke teen stambhon mein se ek hai — legislature aur executive se swatantra. Ye jhagde kaanoon ke hisaab se suljhaati hai, dekhti hai ki sansad ke kaanoon samvidhan ke andar hon, aur executive apni seema na laanghe.

Kaanoon aur nyay ka rishta

Samaaj bade hue to nyay dilaane ka kaam sarkar ko mila. Kaanoon (law) = sarkar ke banaaye saamaanya niyam; todne par nateeja — jurmaana, muaavza, ya jail. Shikaayat karne aur apni baat rakhne ka system = judicial system. Azaadi ke baad angrezon ke 1,500 se zyada bhedbhaav wale kaanoon radd (repeal) kiye gaye — jaise Criminal Tribes Act, 1871 (kuch samudaayon ko janm se apraadhi maanta tha) aur Vernacular Press Act, 1878. Doosre deshon mein bhi aise kaanoon the: USA ke 'Jim Crow laws' (kaale logon ke liye alag paani ke nal, pool — 1960s mein khatam), South Africa ka apartheid (1991 mein khatam).

Nyay ke do pehlu

Substantive justice — nateeja sahi ho: kya faisla sach mein nyaypoorn aur uchit hai? Procedural justice — tareeka sahi ho: faisle se pehle har paksh ko poori baat rakhne ka mauka mile. Aur ek bada niyam: jab tak doshi saabit na ho, har vyakti nirdosh maana jaata hai.

Adaalaton ki seedhi

India mein ekikrit (unified) nyay vyavastha hai — sabse upar Supreme Court, rajya star par High Courts, zila star par Subordinate/District Courts. Jaise school mein class teacher → principal → board, waise hi neeche ke faisle se santusht na ho to upar appeal.

Supreme Court — desh ki sabse oonchi adaalat

26 January 1950 (samvidhan lagu hone ke din) bani; Article 124; New Delhi mein. Samvidhan ki antim vyakhyakaar. Motto: 'Yato dharmastato jayah' — jahan dharm, wahan vijay. Pramukh = Chief Justice of India; kul 33 + 1 (CJI + 33 jaj). Niyukti President karte hain. Yogyata: Bharatiya naagrik + (High Court jaj 5 saal, ya High Court vakeel 10 saal, ya President ki nazar mein vishisht vidhivetta). Retirement 65 saal.

Writ aur PIL — aam aadmi ki taakat

Agar Fundamental Rights (barabari, swatantrata, jeevan, bolne ki azaadi, dharm ki azaadi) ka ullanghan ho to naagrik writ petition daal sakta hai — Supreme Court (Article 32) ya High Court (Article 226) mein. PIL (Public Interest Litigation) — Supreme Court ka naya prayog: janhit ke mudde par koi bhi adaalat ja sakta hai, Article 21 (jeevan ka adhikaar) ko arth dene ke liye. Pehla PIL: vakeel Kapila Hingorani ne akhbaar mein padha ki Bihar mein undertrial qaidi apni sambhavit saza se zyada samay se jail mein hain → Supreme Court ne unhe chhudwaaya aur poore desh ke ~40,000 undertrials ke liye guidelines. M.C. Mehta v. Union of India — Ganga ki safaai, Taj Mahal ki raksha. Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan (1997) — kaamkaaj ki jagah yaun utpeedan ke khilaaf guidelines → 2013 ka kaanoon.

Supreme Court ke kaam ke 'darwaaze' (jurisdiction)

Original (Art. 131) — sirf Supreme Court mein: Centre vs State, ya rajyon ke aapsi jhagde (jaise nadi ka paani). Appellate — High Court ke faisle ke khilaaf appeal (samvidhan ki vyakhya ya kaanoon ka bada sawaal; maut ki saza wale case — Art. 132–134A); NGT, NCLAT jaise tribunals se bhi. Advisory (Art. 143) — President salaah maangein to kam se kam 5 jajon ki Constitution Bench raay deti hai. Special Leave (Art. 136) — kisi bhi adaalat/tribunal ke faisle par vishesh anumati se appeal. Supreme Court ka faisla sab adaalaton par laagu (Art. 141); 'poorn nyay' ke liye koi bhi aadesh (Art. 142); sab adhikaari uski madad karein (Art. 144).

High Court aur neeche ki adaalatein

Article 214 — har rajya mein High Court; par 28 rajyon ke liye 25 High Courts, kyonki kuch saajha hain — jaise Gauhati (Assam, Nagaland, Mizoram, Arunachal), Bombay (Maharashtra, Goa, Dadra-Nagar Haveli & Daman-Diu), Punjab & Haryana (+ Chandigarh), Madras (+ Puducherry), Calcutta (+ Andaman-Nicobar), Kerala (+ Lakshadweep). Jaj: President niyukt karte — CJI, Governor aur us HC ke Chief Justice se salaah ke baad; yogyata — 10 saal nyayik pad ya HC vakaalat (Art. 217); retirement 62 saal. Kaam: writ (226), neeche ki adaalaton se appeal, aur unki nigraani (Art. 227). Subordinate courts (Art. 233–237) — District, Sessions, Revenue, Small Causes courts; yahin judiciary aam logon se sabse kareeb milti hai. District Judge ko Governor HC ki salaah se niyukt karte hain.

Tribunals — khaas jhagdon ke liye khaas adaalatein

Tribunal = quasi-judicial sanstha jo ek khaas tarah ke jhagde suljhaaye: NCLT (company aur diwaaliyapan), Consumer Fora (upbhokta), National Green Tribunal (paryavaran), APTEL (bijli), Armed Forces Tribunal, Central Administrative Tribunal (sarkari karmachaariyon ki seva). Misaal: ek parivaar ne builder ko flat ke liye ~₹65 lakh diye; 4 saal tak building ka naam-o-nishaan nahi → National Consumer Commission ne builder ko byaaj samet paisa aur mukadme ka kharcha lautaane ka aadesh diya.

Naye kaanoon aur digital adaalat

Purane angrezi kaanoon badle: Indian Penal Code 1860 → Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023; CrPC 1973 → Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 2023; Indian Evidence Act 1872 → Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023 — tez nyay, digital saboot, peedit ki behtar raksha. Digital kadam: e-Filing, hybrid hearing, live streaming, kai bhashaon mein faislon ka anuvaad, National Judicial Data Grid (koi bhi case ki jaankaari dekh sake). 2024–25 se Supreme Court ke gate par 'Virtual Justice Clock'. Litigant = case ladne wala (mukadma karne wala aur jis par mukadma, dono).

Court jaaye bina jhagda suljhaao — ADR

Adaalaton par bojh kam karne ke liye Alternate Dispute Resolution: Arbitration — dono paksh ek swatantra arbitrator (retired jaj, vakeel, visheshagya) ko maante hain; uska faisla antim aur baadhyakaari (Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996). Mediation — mediator sirf samjhauta karwaata hai, faisla thop nahi sakta (Mediation Act 2023). Lok Adalat — 'janta ki adaalat', aapsi sahmati se; court mein pade case bhi, aur court jaane se pehle bhi (Legal Services Authorities Act 1987). Gram Nyayalaya — gaon star ki ghoomti (mobile) adaalat, saral prakriya (Gram Nyayalayas Act 2008). Aur haan — naagrik bhi PIL ke zariye logon, jaanwaron aur prakriti ke adhikaaron ki awaaz utha sakte hain.

Citizenship: Rights and Duties

Naagrik kaun hai, 6 Fundamental Rights, 11 Fundamental Duties, bhedbhaav ki pehchaan aur sabko saath lena (inclusion).

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Kruti ki kahani — achchha naagrik kaun?

Nayi student Kruti ko uske bolne ke lehje (accent) par chidhaya ja raha tha. Sonali aur Harshada ne bullies ko roka aur Kruti ko apni badminton team mein bula liya. Yahi hai achchha naagrik — galat ke khilaaf khada hona aur sabko apnaana. Citizen (naagrik) = jo kaanooni roop se desh ka hissa hai aur uski raajnaitik vyavastha (faisle lene) mein bhaag le sakta hai. Naagrikta = kaanooni pehchaan + saamajik zimmedari. Dhyaan do: kahin rehne wala (resident) hona aur naagrik hona alag hai — har resident naagrik nahi, aur naagrik videsh mein bhi reh sakta hai. Niyam: Citizenship Act 1955 aur Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2019.

Mele ki kahani — adhikaar kya hote hain?

Shehar ke mele mein: Meena ko debate se roka gaya ('ladkiyan stage par nahi bolengi'), thele wale ke bete Ravi ko library stall mein ghusne nahi diya jabki ameer ladke ka swaagat hua, kuch bachche school ki jagah safaai kar rahe the, aur wheelchair wale Gaganjit ke liye ramp nahi tha. Teacher ne samjhaaya ki samvidhan sabko Fundamental Rights deta hai. Bachchon ne aayojakon se baat ki — Meena debate mein, Ravi stall mein, ramp bana, bachche school mein! Adhikaar (rights) = wo cheezein jo izzat, suraksha aur samman ke saath jeene ke liye zaroori hain — 'justified claims'. Jo kaam doosron ya khud ko nuksaan pahunchaaye, wo adhikaar nahi.

6 Fundamental Rights

Samvidhan mein chhe moolbhoot adhikaar — court mein lagu karwaaye ja sakte hain, aur sarkar ki galat kaarwai se bhi bachaate hain: (1) Equality (Art. 14–18) — kaanoon ke saamne sab barabar; dharm, jaati, ling, janmsthaan par bhedbhaav nahi; chhuaachhoot khatam (17); sena/shiksha ke alaawa koi upaadhi nahi (18). (2) Freedom (19–22) — bolna, ghoomna, shaant sabha; par uchit pratibandh ke saath. (3) Against exploitation (23–24). (4) Freedom of religion (25–28). (5) Cultural & educational (29–30). (6) Constitutional remedies (32).

Equality vs Equity — teen bachche aur deewaar

Socho teen alag lambaai ke bachche deewaar ke paar match dekh rahe hain. Equality — sabko ek jaisa stool do; lamba waala to dekh lega, chhota phir bhi nahi. Equity — har ek ko zaroorat ke hisaab se madad do; chhote ko do stool. Kyonki kuch log itihaas ke bhedbhaav ki wajah se peeche hain, unhe barabari tak laane ke liye equity chahiye — jaise Beti Bachao Beti Padhao.

Jeevan ka adhikaar — sirf saans lena nahi

Article 21 — jeevan aur swatantrata; matlab izzat ke saath jeena: rozi-roti, shiksha, swasthya, saaf paryavaran, nyaypoorn vyavhaar. Divyang bachchon ko barabari se seekhne-khelne ki suvidha (jaise dristihin bachchon ke liye tactile map). Article 21A — 6–14 saal ki muft aur anivarya shiksha. Mazedaar: New Zealand ki Whanganui nadi ko kaanooni roop se 'jeevit' maana gaya; Costa Rica ke samvidhan mein prakriti ke adhikaar. 2017 mein Uttarakhand High Court ne Ganga-Yamuna ko 'legal entity' kaha, par Supreme Court ne ise 'legally unsustainable' keh kar palat diya.

Shoshan ke khilaaf — Childline 1098

Exploitation = kisi ka galat fayda uthaana — bandhua mazdoori (bina uchit vetan zabardasti kaam), maanav taskari (khareed-farokht), mahilaaon-bachchon ka shoshan, baal shram. Niyam: 14 saal se kam bachchon ko naukri nahi; sirf gair-khatarnaak parivaarik kaam mein, chhuttiyon ya school ke baad madad kar sakte hain; factory, khaan, restaurant mein bilkul nahi. Childline 1098 — 10 se ulti ginti! 1990s ke ant mein ek shikshan sanstha ke project se shuru. 2015 mein ek call par kapdon par kadhai karne wali factory se 20 bachche chhudaaye gaye; doshi giraftaar.

Dharm, sanskriti aur 'aatma' wala adhikaar

Dharm ki swatantrata — har koi apna dharm chune, maane, failaaye; kisi ko zabardasti dharm maanne ya uske liye paisa dene par majboor nahi kiya ja sakta; sarkari ya sarkari-sahaayata wale school kisi ek dharm ka prachaar nahi kar sakte. Saanskritik-shaikshik adhikaar — samudaay apni bhasha, lipi, parampara bachaayein; sarkari sanstha mein dharm/jaati/bhasha par daakhila mana nahi; dharmik aur bhaashaai alpsankhyak apne school chala sakte hain (kuch log ise secularism se takraav maante hain — bahas chalti hai). Constitutional remedies (Art. 32) — adhikaar toote to seedhe Supreme Court. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar: ye samvidhan ki 'aatma aur hriday' hai.

Green Park ki safaai — kartavya kya hain?

Ek Sunday ko Green Park Colony ke log milkar safaai karne lage — bachchon ne wrapper uthaaye, naaliyan saaf hui, paudhe lage, deewaron par achchhe sandesh, kutton ke liye paani-khaana. Reporter ne ek chhoti bachchi se poochha kyon? Jawab: 'Apne mohalle ki dekhbhaal karna hamara kartavya hai.' Fundamental Duties samvidhan mein shuru mein nahi the; 1976 mein Article 51A (Part IV-A) mein 10 jode gaye; 2002 mein 11th — maata-pita 6–14 saal ke bachchon ki shiksha sunishchit karein.

11 Fundamental Duties — ek-ek misaal ke saath

(1) Samvidhan, tiranga, rashtragaan ka samman — assembly mein rashtragaan par seedha khade raho. (2) Swatantrata sangram ke aadarsh — bullying ke khilaaf bolna bhi anyaay se ladna hai. (3) Desh ki ekta-akhandta-samprabhuta — ek-doosre ki bhasha ke shabd seekho. (4) Desh ki raksha, aapda mein madad — baadh mein NCC cadets. (5) Bhaichaara aur mahilaaon ka samman. (6) Sanskriti-virasat bachaao — qile ki deewaar par naam mat likho! (7) Paryavaran aur jaanwaron par daya. (8) Vaigyanik soch (scientific temper) aur jigyaasa — paani saaf karne ka tareeka sikhaao. (9) Sarkari sampatti bachaao, hinsa nahi — match haarne par bench mat todo. (10) Har kaam mein utkrishtata. (11) 6–14 saal ke bachchon ki shiksha.

Purana Bharat — dharma, rina, adhikaar

Dharma ka anuvaad aksar 'duty' hota hai, par iska arth bahut bada hai — jo vyakti aur samaaj ke liye hitkaari ho. Rina (karz) — janm lete hi devtaaon, poorvajon-maata-pita, guruon, saathi insaanon aur prakriti ka 'karz'; use chukaana kartavya. Adhikaar purane granthon mein kaabiliyat/yogyata ka arth deta tha — jo kaam karne yogya ho, uska haq. Aaj samvidhan adhikaar sabko barabar deta hai.

Adhikaar aur kartavya — ek sikke ke do pehlu

Adhikaar kaanoon se surakshit hain (court ja sakte ho); kartavya naitik-saamajik zimmedari hain (na nibhaane par hamesha kaanooni kaarwai nahi). Par har adhikaar ke saath ek kartavya: mujhe samman ka haq → doosron ka samman karna mera farz; mujhe bolne ki azaadi → doosron ki raay sunna, bina nuksaan pahunchaaye bolna. Shiksha ka adhikaar → padhna aur doosron ko padhne dena!

Bhedbhaav — dikhne wala aur na dikhne wala

Sanya ko kaha gaya wo lead role ke 'layak nahi dikhti' — par usne audition diya aur chuni gayi; roop se aankna bhedbhaav hai. Discrimination = jaati, dharm, nasl, divyangata, roop, ling, ya aarthik sthiti ke kaaran galat vyavhaar — anaitik aur gairkaanooni. Ye chhupa bhi ho sakta hai: kisi ko group project mein na lena, bus mein bagal mein na baithne dena, taane maarna. Rohan ko mota keh kar 'Drummy' bulaana; Mina trip ka paisa nahi de saki to use 'status ka nahi' keh kar alag karna. Chhupa bhedbhaav saabit karna mushkil hai. Prerna: Savitribai Phule — ladkiyon ka school kholne par logon ne keechad phenka, phir bhi nahi rukeen; Anandibai Joshi — 9 saal mein shaadi, baby ki maut, phir America (Pennsylvania) jaakar doctor bani.

Inclusion — koi peeche na chhoote

Smt. Amita ne dekha library ke books kam ja rahe the; reading club banaya to sirf angrezi mein tez bachche aaye. Unhone sthaaniya bhasha ki kitaabein, kahaani aur kathputli jodi — ab sab bachche shaamil! Inclusion = aisa maahaul jahan har koi swaagat, samman, mahatva mehsoos kare aur avsar barabar mile — sirf aarakshan se aage: braille kitaab, ramp, kaksha mein madad, alag bhasha walon se baat karne ke tareeke. Jo classmate dheere padhta hai, uska intezaar karo, use bolne ka mauka do — yahi inclusion hai.

Dynamics of Population

Demography kya hai — janm dar, fertility, mrityu dar, migration, demographic transition ke 3 stage, population pyramid aur India ka demographic dividend.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Demography — logon ki 'ginti' se zyada

Duniya mein kitne log hain? Unme bachche kitne, buzurg kitne, ladke-ladkiyan, padhe-likhe, kaun kya kaam karta hai? Aur ye sab samay ke saath kaise badalta hai — janm, mrityu aur logon ke ek jagah se doosri jagah jaane se? Is adhyayan ko demography (janankiki) kehte hain. Iske hisse: size (kitne), structure aur composition (umar, ling, shiksha…), pattern aur trend (badlaav). Fayda: zyada bachche → zyada school; zyada buzurg → zyada aspataal; zyada yuva → zyada naukriyan chahiye. Companiyan bhi umar-ling-aay dekh kar product banaati hain. Working-age population = 15–64 saal. India mein Census (janganana) aam taur par har 10 saal — umar, ling, vivaah, shiksha, bhasha, dharm, parivaar, kaam, aay.

Duniya aur India — ek nazar

2023 mein India ne China ko peechhe chhoda — ab duniya ka sabse zyada aabadi wala desh; duniya ke ~17% log Bharatiya (2024: India ~144 crore, China ~140 crore, USA ~33 crore). Duniya: 1950 mein 2.5 arab → 2025 mein 8.2 arab → 2050 tak 9.7 arab. UN ke anusaar 2080s ke beech ~10.3 arab par shikhar, phir ghatega. 1950s ke baad tez badhotri kyon? Antibiotics, vaccine, ORS, saaf paani, malaria-dast se bachchon ki maut kam. China, Japan, Italy ki aabadi ghat rahi; Nigeria, Ethiopia mein tez badh rahi. 2025 mein Asia ~4.8 arab, Africa ~1.5 arab.

Aabadi kis se tay hoti hai? — 4 'measurement'

Birth rate — saal mein prati 1,000 log par kitne jeevit janm. Fertility rate — ek mahila apne jeevan mein ausatan kitne bachche paida karegi. Death/mortality rate — saal mein prati 1,000 par kitni maut. Migration — logon ka desh ke andar ya bahar jaana. Socho ek baalti mein paani: janm = nal se aata paani, maut = chhed se nikalta paani, migration = koi baalti mein daale ya nikaale!

2.1 ka jaadu — replacement level

Fertility 2.1 = 'replacement level' — itne bachche ki maata-pita ki jagah le sakein aur aabadi sthir rahe. 2 kyon nahi, 2.1 kyon? Kyonki kuch bachche badaa hone se pehle hi mar jaate hain, aur ladke-ladkiyon ka anupaat bhi poora barabar nahi — isliye thoda extra. 2.1 se upar = aabadi badhegi; neeche = dheere-dheere ghategi. Fertility ghatne se birth rate bhi ghatta hai.

China ki One-Child Policy — ek sabak

1970s mein China ko khaana, naukri, ghar ki kami ka darr tha, to 1979 mein One-Child Policy — zyaadatar parivaar sirf ek bachcha; niyam todne par jurmaana ya naukri gayi. Fertility 7.5 (1963) → 1 (2025). Aabadi 2022 mein 1.43 arab par shikhar, ab ghat rahi; 2050 tak 1.31 arab. Buzurg (65+) ka hissa 14% (2023) → 26% (2050) — kaam karne wale kam, arthvyavastha dheemi. Ek poori peedhi ke bhai-behen nahi, rishton ka jaal (kinship network) chhota. 2015 mein policy khatam, ab 3 bachche tak ki chhoot — par fertility ab bhi 2.1 se neeche! India mein 'Hum do hamare do' (1952 se); fertility 5.7 (1950) → 1.9 (2025).

Fertility kyon gir rahi hai?

Sabse badi wajah — ladkiyon ki achchhi shiksha. Padhi-likhi mahilaayein naukri karti hain, der se shaadi karti hain aur kam bachche chahti hain. Shehron mein rehna mehenga, isliye chhote parivaar. Rajyon ki tulna dekho: Bihar — fertility 3.0, mahila saaksharta 55%; UP 2.4 / 66.1%; Jharkhand 2.3 / 61.7%; Karnataka 1.7 / 73.4%; Punjab 1.6 / 79.4%; Delhi 1.6 / 83.7% — jahan mahila saaksharta zyada, wahan fertility kam!

Mrityu dar, umar aur migration

Kam mrityu dar = achchha swasthya aur poshan. India mein child mortality (5 saal se kam umar ki maut prati 1,000 janm) 271 (1950) → 28 (2023)! Life expectancy (ek naye bachche ki ausat umar) azaadi ke samay sirf ~30 saal, 2024 mein ~70 saal. Iska matlab ye nahi ki har koi theek 70 jiyega — ye ausat hai. Migration — naukri, padhai ke liye; aapda ya ashaanti se bhi. Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru migration se bahut badhe — par paani, hawa, zameen, ghar, transport par dabaav. Socho — jab gaon ke yuva shehar chale jaayein to gaon mein kya badlega?

Demographic transition — desh ki 'umar' ke 3 stage

Jaise insaan bachpan-jawaani-budhaapa dekhta hai, waise hi desh: Stage 1 — janm dar bhi oonchi, mrityu dar bhi oonchi → aabadi lagbhag sthir (India 1901–1921, kharab swasthya, kam saaksharta). Stage 2 — mrityu dar giri, janm dar ab bhi oonchi → tez badhotri (India 1951 ke baad). Stage 3 — mrityu dar kam, janm dar bhi girne lagi (shehrikaran, aay, shiksha, mahilaaon ka kaam) → badhotri dheemi; bahut gire to aabadi ghate (Italy, Japan, South Korea). India mein 1980s ke beech se fertility giri, ab replacement se neeche.

India kaise bikhra hai aur sex ratio

Rajyon mein hissa: UP ~16%, Maharashtra ~9%, Bihar ~9%, West Bengal ~8%, MP-TN-Rajasthan ~6% har ek… Sex ratio = prati 1,000 purush par mahilaaon ki sankhya. Census 2001: 933 → 2011: 943 (mahilaayein 48.5%). Kerala, Puducherry, Tamil Nadu mein 1,000 ke aas-paas ya upar. Santulit sex ratio barabar vikas aur samaajik sthirta ke liye zaroori.

Population pyramid — aabadi ki tasveer

Ek graph jisme baayein purush, daayein mahila, neeche bachche, beech mein vayask, upar buzurg. Chauda neeche = zyada bachche, yuva aur badhti aabadi (India pehle, Nigeria). Beech chauda = zyaadatar kaamkaaji log, sthir aabadi. Upar chauda = budhaati aabadi (Japan). India 1950 mein patla tikona (bahut bachche, umar 30–35). Ab ghanti (bell) jaisa — neeche patla (kam bachche), beech mota (15–60 wale zyada), upar dheere-dheere mota. Bhavishya mein Japan-China ki tarah budhaapa aayega.

Demographic dividend — seemit 'khidki'

Youth bulge = 16–30 saal ke yuva bahut zyada. India mein ~27% 15 se kam, ~65% kaamkaaji umar (15–59). Median age (beech wali umar): India 28, Japan 50, Italy 48, Germany 47, China 40, USA 39. Zyada log kaam karein → zyada utpaadan aur tax → school, aspataal, sadak. Isi ko demographic dividend kehte hain. Par ye khidki seemit hai: 5 saal se kam ke bachche 2007 mein shikhar par the, 15 se kam 2011 mein; 2050 ke baad India budhaane lagega. Kerala, Tamil Nadu mein buzurg pehle hi zyada (Bihar-UP se). Budhaati aabadi = zyada swasthya kharcha, pension ka bojh, kam taxpayer.

Buzurgon ka mahatva aur aage ki taiyaari

Bharatiya ashrama dharma — jeevan ke 4 charan: brahmacharya, grihastha, vanaprastha, sanyasa. Buzurg kaam se hat-te hain par gyaan, kahaaniyan, reeti-riwaaj aur anubhav se parivaar-samaaj ko dishaa dete hain. Vikasit desh: Japan ne retirement umar badhaayi, robot aur AI se buzurgon ki dekhbhaal. Vikaasheel desh (India): shiksha, skill, swasthya mein nivesh. Kaamkaaji mahilaaon ka 'double burden' (naukri + ghar) — daycare, flexible samay, parivaar ka saath chahiye. Kai deshon ne fertility badhaane ki koshish ki, par zyaada safalta nahi mili.

India's Urban Landscape

Shehar kaise bante hain, aarthik aur saanskritik kendra kyon hain, unki chunautiyaan, aur Jaipur-Singapore jaisi achchhi planning.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Shehar vs gaon — farak kya hai?

India ki shehri parampara bahut purani hai — Harappa ke Dholavira, Kalibangan, Rakhigarhi se lekar Vijayanagara, Varanasi, Ujjain tak. Urban (shehri) ilaake = zyada aabadi, alag-alag dhandhe aur naukriyan, sarkari sansthaayein, bada transport-communication jaal; chune hue local bodies (nagar palika/nigam) chalaate hain. Rural (graamin) = gaon, kam aabadi, kareebi samudaay, zyaadatar kheti; gram panchayat chalaati hai. Shehar ki paribhasha har desh mein alag: Denmark — 220 log se zyada, Japan — 50,000, India — 5,000!

Population density — kamre mein kitne log?

Ghar par kuch dost aaye to aaram se ghoom sakte ho; par usi kamre mein 30 log thoons do to bheed! Kisi jagah kitni 'bheed' hai, ye maapta hai population density = kul aabadi ÷ kshetrafal (sq km) — yani prati sq km kitne log. Haryana ke gaon Asadpur Khera ki satellite photo mein hare-bhoore khule khet; Gurugram mein bhoore-grey imaaraton ka jaal. 2025 ke sabse bade shehron mein Delhi (~4.19 crore — sabse upar!) aur Kolkata bhi hain.

Shehar badhte kyon hain?

Itihaas mein shehar wahan bane jahan nadi (aasaan transport), vyapaari raaston ka chauraaha (bazaar), aur kheti ki upaj aur kachcha maal paas ho (kaarigar aur udyog). UN ke anusaar 2050 tak duniya ke 68% log shehron mein (abhi 55%). India mein shehri aabadi 17% (1951) → ~40% (2025). Urbanisation ka safar: kheti wala gaon → nayi factory, hotel, bank, sarkari daftar → doosre-teesre sector (udyog, transport, sevaayein) → log aate hain, kaam ki vividhta → chhota kasba/vyapaari kendra → metropolitan shehar (oonchi imaaratein, mall, metro).

Sakchi kaise bana Jamshedpur?

Chota Nagpur Plateau ka chhota gaon Sakchi — paas mein loha (iron ore) — isliye yahan Asia ka pehla steel plant laga. Tata parivaar ka sapna tha ki karmachaariyon ke liye aadhunik suvidhaon wala shehar bane; Sir Dorabji Tata ne steel banne se pehle hi aspataal bana diya! 1919 mein naam Jamshedpur — aaj Jharkhand ka sabse bada audyogik shehar; chaudi ped-lagi sadkein, baag, khuli jagah.

Har shehar ki apni pehchaan

Mumbai — Arabian Sea ka bandargah; 1875 mein Bombay Stock Exchange → desh ki aarthik raajdhani; film udyog 1910s mein shuru, 1930s tak chamka; Bandra-Worli Sea Link, Coastal Road, BKC. Kolkata — poorvi tat ka bandargah. Bengaluru — 'Silicon Valley of India'; software, startups, university; AI tak. Hyderabad — technology. Chhote vishesh shehar: Moradabad (peetal), Ludhiana, Tirupur (kapda), Jaipur (hastkala, ratna-gehne), Channapatna (lakdi ke khilone), Kanchipuram (resham saadi). Kochi, Indore, Lucknow, Nagpur — kharcha kam, isliye companiyan godaam-daftar kholti hain. Paryatan/teerth: Agra (Taj — 70–80 lakh log/saal), Amritsar, Varanasi.

Delhi aur NCR

Delhi Iron Age se sanskriti, vyapaar aur prashasan ka kendra — Rajput, Turk, Mughal ne qile-sadak-bazaar banaaye; angrezon ne nayi raajdhani, rail, bijli. Azaadi ke baad mantralay, udyog, university — log umad pade; ghar, paani, sewer par dabaav. Isliye National Capital Region (NCR) — NOIDA (New Okhla Industrial Development Authority), Ghaziabad; highway aur metro. 1990s se Gurugram — IT aur BPO (Business Process Outsourcing — kisi company ka kaam doosri company se karwaana) ka hub.

Shehar = sanskritiyon ki khichdi

Alag-alag ilaakon ke log aate hain to bhasha, khaana, tyohaar mil jaate hain. Vada pav — Maharashtrian aaloo vada + pav (pav aur aaloo Portuguese laaye the!); Mumbai ke kapda mill mazdooron ka pasandeeda — banana, le jaana, khaana sab aasaan. Kolkata — Indian Museum, Victoria Memorial; Durga Puja — 2021 mein UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage. Shehar library, museum, stadium, music festival banaate hain; Delhi ka Pradhanmantri Sangrahalaya — pradhan mantriyon ki kahani.

Shehron ki chunautiyaan

Zyada log → khaana, paani, bijli, ghar ki zyada maang. Tooti-patli sadkein, bina planning ke bastiyaan, gaadiyon ki bheed, 'heat islands' (shehar gaon se zyada garam), kachra. NCR mein AQI aksar 400 se upar, jabki surakshit seema 50! (AQI = PM2.5, PM10, CO, SO₂ jaise pradooshakon ko ek aasaan number mein batata hai — jitna zyada, utna khatarnaak.) Zyada ghanatva aur aarthik asmaanta se apraadh bhi zyada. Slums (jhuggi) — bheed, kachche ghar, paani-safaai ki kami; railway line, factory ke paas khaali zameen par, kyonki sasta ghar nahi. Par jhuggi wale hi sabzi bechte, ghar ka kaam, kapde dhona, construction, plumbing karte hain — shehar chalaate hain! Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana — gareeb parivaaron ko sasta ghar.

Bengaluru ki jheelein — milkar bachaao

Bengaluru mein garmi mein paani ki kami aur barish mein baadh — kharab planning se. Zyada groundwater nikaalna, rainwater harvesting na karna, jheelon par qabza. Kabhi ~1,000 jheelein thin, ab ~200; kai mein sewage-chemical; Bellandur aur Varthur jheelon par jhaag chha gaya, zehreeli gas, yahan tak ki aag lag gayi! Ab NGOs, RWAs (Resident Welfare Associations) aur log milkar gaad nikaal rahe, prakritik cheezon se baandh sudhaar rahe, naaliyan saaf, desi paudhe, machhliyan wapas — jaise Yelahanka Lake.

Jaipur — 'Pink City' ki planning (1727)

Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II ne 1727 mein Vidyadhar Bhattacharya ko mukhya vaastukaar banaya — vaastu shastra aur shilpa shastra ke gyaata. Shehar grid pattern mein — aayataakaar block (chowkri), disha ke hisaab se. Chaudi sadkein, khuli jagah, baag, 9 public chowk (vyapaar-milan, mandir). Suraksha ke liye 20 foot oonchi deewaar, 7 darwaaze. Mukhya sadkon ki imaaratein gulaabi sandstone se — ek jaisi khoobsurat pehchaan → 'Pink City'. Dhoop, taazi hawa, jalaashay-nahar se paani (sookha mausam); bazaaron mein chhaaya wale baraamde; Panna Meena Kund stepwell. Aaj bhi waise hi kaam karta hai!

Singapore — badalti planning ka model

Chhota dweep-desh, 8,000+ log prati sq km, phir bhi achchhi zindagi. Concept Plan — 40 saal aage tak zameen ka upyog; Master Plan — har 5 saal mein update. 1960s ke bheed bhare bandargah se aadhunik shehar. Mixed-use — ghar, daftar, park, museum paas-paas (Marina Bay Sands aur Raffles Place kuch hi km door). MRT metro, bus, cycle path, paidal hare raaste → car kam. Chhaton aur deewaaron par vertical garden → 'garden city', garmi kam. Buzurgon ke ghar aspataal ke paas, childcare, milan-jagah — ise 'blue zone' (jahan log lambi, swasth zindagi jeete hain, jaise Okinawa, Japan) maana jaata hai.

India ke achchhe udaharan aur kal ka shehar

Sabarmati Riverfront (Ahmedabad) — do-manzila nadi kinaare ki sair, park, bazaar, naukaayan, paidal-cycle wala Atal Bridge. M.G. Road (Gangtok) — sirf paidal zone, bahut saaf. Achchhe shehar ke liye: technology (smart traffic, online shikaayat, solar), naagrik bhaagidaari, kachra prabandhan, paani, public transport, sasta ghar, e-governance. Shehar 'jeevit' hote hain — badhte, badalte. Kal ka shehar wo jo vikas aur prakriti ka santulan rakhe, kamzor logon ko na chhode, aur sansaadhan khatam kiye bina suvidha de.

Cultural Currents: 13th to 17th Centuries

Ashaant raajneeti ke beech bhi sanskriti ka phalna — Bhakti-Sufi, sangeet, miniature chitra, baag, mahilaayein, shiksha, sahitya, vigyaan, khaana aur kapde.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Sanskriti kya hai aur Bharat ki sanskriti itni mazboot kyon?

Sanskriti (culture) = kisi samooh ka saajha jeevan-dhang jo peedhi dar peedhi chalta hai — moolya, reeti-riwaaj, parampara, kala, sangeet, sahitya, darshan, vigyaan. Mesopotamia, Misr, Greece, Rome ki prachin sanskritiyaan samay ke saath dhundhli ya khatam ho gayin, par Bharatiya sanskriti aaj bhi jeevit hai — gehri jadein, kaalateet moolya, vaigyanik soch, samriddh kala-darshan. Gandhi ji ne kaha: 'main chahta hoon sab deshon ki sanskriti ki hawa mere ghar mein aaye, par main kisi se ud na jaaun.' India ne bahar se aaye (Greece ke Megasthenes, Persia, Central Asia, Arab vyapaari) logon ki achchhi cheezein apnaayi ya apne hisaab se dhaali. 13th sadi se naye shaasak naye vichaar laaye; phir bhi teen baatein chalti rahin: (1) paramparaayein jaari, (2) har tarah ke shaasakon ka sanrakshan, (3) aam logon ki bhaagidaari.

Bhakti — bina purohit, bina reeti ke bhagwaan tak

Ek-ishwar aur ek kitaab wale Islam aur anek vichaardhaaraon wale Bharatiya dharmon mein bada farak tha; kai aakramankaariyon ne mandir aur pavitra sthal tode. Phir bhi samaaj tika — iska ek bada kaaran tha Bhakti. Bhakti marga = moksha (satya ki praapti / janm-mrityu se mukti) ka ek raasta jisme reeti-riwaaj ya purohit zaroori nahi; 6th sadi tak ye parampara ban chuki thi. Is daur mein phir lokpriya hui — sthaaniya bhashaon mein prem, daya aur barabari ke geet, sangeet aur nritya ke saath.

Sant aur unke sandesh

Kabirdas (15th sadi, UP; julaha dampati ne paala) — ek ishwar; Hindu aur Muslim dono ke dikhaave ki aalochana. Doha: 'gehu pis kar aata ban jaata hai to daanon ka bhed mit jaata hai — waise hi Ram aur Rahim ek ho jaate hain'. Ravidas (16th sadi ki shuruaat, chamda kaarigar parivaar) — barabari, varna-jaati ki deewaarein todo. Guru Nanak — ek ishwar, sabki barabari; Guru Granth Sahib mein kai Hindu santon ke bhajan bhi! Mirabai (Rajasthan), Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (poorv), Tukaram, Dnyaneshwar, Namdev (pashchim), Purandaradasa, Kanakadasa (dakshin). Tamil Nadu mein Alvar ka Divya Prabandham aur Nayanar ka Tiruvachakam (6th–9th sadi ke Vishnu-Shiv bhajan) gaaye jaate rahe. Sant ke chitra 200 saal baad bhi bane — unka asar itna lamba!

Sufi — prem se ishwar ki khoj

Sufism = Islam ka bhakti wala raasta — bheetar ki, vyaktigat khoj. Sufi sant qawwali gaate — sthaaniya shastriya sangeet ke saath; Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti, Nizamuddin Auliya. Whirling dervish (ghoomta dhyaan), zikr (ishwar ke naam ka baar-baar jaap). Sufiyon ne yog aur Vedanta ki takneekein bhi apnaayin — maun, tyaag, dhyaan. Rumi: 'kyon dhoondhoon? Main bhi wahi hoon…' Bhakti aur Sufi aksar mil jaate the. Purandaradasa (15th–16th sadi) — 'Carnatic sangeet ke pita'; heere ka vyapaar chhod Krishna bhakt bane; ~4,75,000 kirtan! Krishnadevaraya ke darbar tak pahunche; 1964 mein daak tikat.

Sangeet — do dhaaraayein

Natyashastra ne nritya, natak, sangeet, ganit, saundarya ko joda. Kahaani sunaane ke tareeke: Rajasthan ki kathputli, Odisha-Bengal-Andhra ka pattachitra (patta = kapda, chitra = tasveer) — Ramayana, Mahabharata, Purana ki kahaaniyan. Shastriya sangeet: uttar mein Hindustani, dakshin mein Carnatic. 15th sadi ke ant mein Gwalior ke Raja Mansingh Tomar ne dhrupad (dhruva = sthir, pada = pad) ko nayi oonchaai di. Tansen (Ramtanu Pandey) — Akbar ke navratnon mein; Sufi sant ka asar, par Krishna-Shiv ki rachnaayein bhi. Amir Khusro (13th–14th sadi) — Indo-Persian Sufi kavi; qawwali ka vikas. Carnatic sangeet Persian-Arabi asar se lagbhag alag vikasit hua. Sitar aur tabla ke aadhunik roop isi daur mein (tabla ki jad purana pakhawaj).

Deewaar se kaagaz tak — miniature chitra

Gufa chitra → Ajanta → 10th–11th sadi mein kaagaz aaya → chhote miniature chitra. Rajput chitrakala (15th–19th sadi) — Hindu vishay: kamal, bargad, haathi, gaay, mor, badi aankhon wali striyan. Abul Fazl ne Ain-i-Akbari mein Hindu chitrakaaron ke baare mein likha: 'duniya mein bahut kam unke barabar hain'. Mughal miniature — Persian asar; darbaar, drishya, portrait; Akbar ke atelier (studio) mein ek kalaakaar rekha, doosra rang, teesra finishing! Razmnama (Persian Mahabharata) ke chitra; Jahangir ke priya chitrakaar Abu al-Hasan. Dono shailiyon ne ek-doosre se seekha; kuch Mughal chitrakaar Bikaner chale gaye.

Baag — udyana se charbagh tak

Rigveda, Ramayana, Mahabharata mein udyana, vatika, nandana ka zikr — aksar mandir aur talaab ke saath. Mughal laaye Persian charbagh — chaar hisse, Quran ke swarg ki nadiyon ka prateek nahar, phuhaare, seedhiyaan (terrace). Nishat Bagh (Srinagar) — 17th sadi, terrace aur jharne. Amer Fort ka baag. Rashtrapati Bhavan ke Mughal Gardens (ab Amrit Udyan).

Mahilaaon ki sthiti — jagah ke hisaab se alag

10th sadi ke Arab itihaaskar Abu Zaidu-l Hasan: India ke rajaon ke darbar mein mahilaayein bina parde dikhti thin. 12th sadi ke baad hamlon mein mahilaaon-bachchon ko gulam bana kar becha gaya; kuch ne jauhar kiya. Suraksha ke liye uttar India ke kuch hisson mein purdah aur ghunghat badha (dakshin mein kam). Razia Sultan (13th sadi) — Delhi Sultanate ki pehli aur ekmaatra mahila shaasak; khud sena le gayin, school banaaye, bina parde darbar mein; saazish se raaj chhota raha. Nur Jahan (Jahangir ki patni) — farmaan, darbaar, unke naam ke sikke. Dakshin mein: Portuguese Fernão Nunes ne Vijayanagara mein mahilaaon ko pehalwaan, jyotishi, hisaab likhne wali, sangeetkaar dekha; raaniyon ne mandiron ko poore gaon daan kiye, kuein-dharmshaala banwaaye.

Shiksha aur gyaan — vishvavidyalay toote, par seekhna nahi ruka

Nalanda aur Vikramashila 12th sadi ke ant mein tode gaye — maanavta ka bada nuksaan. Phir bhi gurukul, math, mandir-school, vihar, Jain palli, gaon ki pathshala; dakshin mein Kanchipuram, Sringeri; madrasa (Arabi, Persian, Hindavi). Jati aur shreni se hunar ki apprenticeship jaari. Vijayanagara — 'purvamaryade' (purani dharm-maryada ka paalan). Italian Pietro Della Valle (1623) ne Karnataka ke tat par bachchon ko padhna-likhna-ganit seekhte dekha. Kapda: Koshti bunkar; Akbar ke protsaahan se Gujarat ke bunkar Banaras gaye; Mughalon ne zari/zardozi aur brocade diye; Banarasi saadi ko GI tag. Pashmina shawl (Ladakh-Kashmir ki bakri ka cashmere) par Persian buta (paisley).

Sahitya, bhasha aur Dara Shikoh

Sanskrit, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Odia, Hindavi, Bengali, Marathi — sab mein rachna. Chaitanya ka asar — Bengali Dhamali lok geet; Kavindra Paramesvara ne Mahabharata ka Bengali anuvaad. Odisha ke Gajapati raja bhi sanrakshak. Tirumalamba — Achyutadevaraya ki darbaari kavyitri; Sanskrit kavya 'Varadambika Parinaya'. Persian prashasan ki bhasha; Lilavati (Bhaskaracharya) ka Persian anuvaad Faizi ne, Rajatarangini ka bhi. Dara Shikoh — Varanasi ke vidwaanon ki madad se 52 Upanishad Persian mein; ye Europe tak gaye aur 19th sadi ke darshnikon ko prabhaavit kiya; Aurangzeb ne unhe maar diya. Urdu — Ganga-Yamuna ke maidaan ki Hindavi + Arabi-Persian shabd; Eighth Schedule mein; dakshin mein Dakhini. Amir Khusrau = 'Urdu sahitya ke pita'.

Vigyaan — Kerala ka calculus!

Shuddh zinc ka utpaadan shikhar par — duniya mein pehli baar distillation se. 14th sadi — Narayana Pandita ka Ganitakaumudi. Dakshin mein Madhava — trigonometric functions ki series expansion (duniya mein pehli baar) aur π ka anant shreni roop — 'Kerala School of Astronomy and Mathematics'. 1500 mein Nilakantha Somayaji ka model — Budh-Shukra Surya ki parikrama — Copernicus (1543) se dashakon pehle! Akbar ke samay shora (saltpetre) se baarood aur paani thanda karna; Itr Jahangir (gulaab ka itr). Chikitsa: Ayurveda, Siddha (Tamil Nadu), Unani (West Asia se).

Imaaratein, khaana aur kapde

Konark Surya Mandir (13th sadi, Eastern Ganga) — 24 pathar ke pahiye. Ranakpur Jain mandir (15th sadi, Dhanna Seth; Rishabhanatha). Sheesh Mahal, Amer (Raja Man Singh I) — ek deepak se taaron jaisi chamak. Srirangam ka Ranganathaswamy mandir — 14th sadi mein toda gaya, usi sadi ke ant tak phir bana. Khaana: elaichi, laung, haldi, dhaniya, kaali mirch; dahi mein marinate, ghee; khichdi naye swaad mein; Akbar ne Jain-Hindu prabhaav mein maans kam kiya; naan (Persian asar); samosa = Persian sambuseh (maans-meve ki jagah aaloo-matar!); jalebi = Persian zalabiyeh (shahad-gulaab jal ki jagah cheeni ki chaashni). Kapde: kapaas, malmal, resham badhe; darbaar mein jama aur pyjama; angrakha (Persian fashion se); saadi apne kshetriya roopon mein bani rahi.

C. Short-answer (model answers)

  1. 1Renewable resource kab non-renewable ban sakta hai? Do udaharan do.

    Jab hum use uski bharpaai (restoration-regeneration) se zyada tezi se use karein — jaise jungle ki lakdi itni tezi se kaatna ki ped ug na paayein, ya tuna machhli ka zyada shikaar. Punjab ka groundwater bhi isi tarah 'over-exploited' hua.

  2. 2Akbar ki neetiyon mein badlaav kaise aaya?

    Jawaani mein Chittor jaisi kathor jeet aur qatl-e-aam; baad mein Rajput gathbandhan, jizya khatam, sulh-i-kul, vidwaanon se charcha, anuvaad ghar (Razmnama) — samrajya sthir karne aur sweekarya banne ke liye raajnaitik samajh.

  3. 3'Angrezon ne India Mughalon se zyada Marathon se liya' — samjhaao.

    18th sadi mein Marathe sabse badi Bharatiya shakti the (Delhi tak niyantran); angrezon ke sabse bade dushman wahi the; teen Anglo-Maratha yuddh (1775–1818) ke baad hi angrez poore India ke maalik bane.

  4. 4'Indians funded their own subjugation' ka kya arth hai?

    Railway, telegraph, prashasan, angrezi sena aur ladaaiyon ka kharcha Bharatiya tax se wasoola gaya — jabki ye sab mukhya roop se angrezon ke vyapaar aur raaj ko mazboot karne ke liye the.

  5. 5Universal adult franchise kyon zaroori hai?

    Har 18+ naagrik ko ek vote, barabar mulya — jaati, dharm, ling, shiksha, aay ka bhed nahi; sabko desh ka bhavishya tay karne ka haq; pratinidhi janta ke prati jawabdeh.

  6. 6Legislature aur Executive mein farak?

    Legislature (Parliament: President + Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha) kaanoon banaati aur executive par nazar rakhti hai; Executive (President, VP, PM ki mantri parishad) kaanoon lagu karti, zyaadatar bill laati aur Lok Sabha ke prati jawabdeh hai.

  7. 7Demographic dividend kya hai aur India ko kya karna chahiye?

    Yuva kaamkaaji aabadi zyada hone se milne wala aarthik fayda (India ~65% 15–59). Khidki 2050 tak; isliye achchhi shiksha, skill, swasthya, naukri aur mahilaaon ke liye daycare jaisi suvidhaayein chahiye.

  8. 8Bhakti aur Sufi ne samaaj ko kaise joda?

    Dono ne prem, ek ishwar aur barabari ka sandesh sthaaniya bhashaon, sangeet aur nritya se diya; reeti-riwaaj aur jaati ki deewaarein kamzor kin; Sufiyon ne yog-Vedanta se seekha; Kabir jaise santon ne Ram-Rahim ki ekta gaayi.
