Theme I · Introduction

Understanding Social Science

Social Science kya hai, roz ki zindagi mein kaise dikhta hai, aur iske chaar mukhya hisse — Geography, History, Political Science, Economics.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Social Science — samaaj ka 'vigyaan'

Physics, Chemistry, Biology prakriti ko padhte hain; Social Science insaani samaaj ko — log kaise saath rehte hain, sarkar kaise chalti hai, kaam-dhandha kaise hota hai, aur beeta kal aaj ko kaise banata hai. Seedhi paribhasha: 'maanav samaaj ka vyavasthit (systematic) adhyayan'. Ye sirf 'kya hua' ya 'kahan hai' nahi batata, balki 'kyon hua' aur 'sab kaise jude hain' bhi samjhaata hai.

Tumhara ek din — Social Science se bhara

Subah uthe — ghar alag-alag jagah ke saamaan se bana. Khaana — kisi aur rajya ke khet se, kaata, process kiya, truck se aaya, khareeda-becha gaya. Sadak — sarkari vibhaag ne banaayi. School — shiksha neeti se chalta. Bijli — door ke power station se, bade jaal se aati. Yani sabse chhote kaam bhi sarkar, arthvyavastha, samaaj ke sahyog aur prakriti par tike hain. Aur sawaal socho: kuch log bheed wale shehar mein, kuch bikhre gaon mein kyon? Kuch ilaakon mein baadh zyada kyon? Kheti kahin achchhi, kahin kharab kyon? Samaaj sanyog (chance) se nahi chalta — itihaas, bhoogol, sansthaayein, sansaadhan aur insaani faisle use banaate hain.

Bharat ki purani 'jigyaasa'

Hamari parampara mein charcha, sawaal aur tark se duniya samajhne ki aadat thi. Panchamahabhuta — duniya 5 tatvon se bani: Prithvi (dharti), Apah (jal), Agni, Vayu, Akasha — aur insaan inse juda hai; isse basti, pesha, ghar ki banawat, khaana, swasthya samajh aate hain. Vasudhaiva kutumbakam — 'poori duniya ek parivaar'. ~2,300 saal pehle Kautilya ke Arthashastra mein prashasan, arthvyavastha, tax aur raja ke kartavya ka vyavasthit vichaar tha — aaj ke subjects banne se bahut pehle!

Social scientist kaam kaise karte hain?

Logon ki zindagi dekhte hain, interview aur survey karte hain, dastavez jaanchte hain, alag samay aur jagah ki tulna karte hain — aur saboot (evidence) se samjhaav banaate hain. Isse pata chalta hai kya tikta hai aur kya badalta hai.

Ek sookha, paanch nazariye

Samaaj itna ulajha hai ki koi ek subject poora nahi samjha sakta. Ek sookha (drought) socho: fasal par asar (paryavaran), kisaan ki kamaai (arthvyavastha), sarkari raahat (raajneeti), shehar ki or palaayan (samaaj), aur paani ki kami se nipatne ke purane tareeke (sanskriti). Isliye Social Science kai jude hue subjects ka parivaar hai. Class 9–10 mein 4 mukhya: Geography, History, Political Science, Economics. Baaki — Sociology, Philosophy, Anthropology, Psychology — aage.

Geography — 'kahan' aur 'kyon wahan'

Jagahon, cheezon aur logon ki sthiti aur failaav, aur insaan-prakriti ka rishta. Do nazariye: spatial (jagah aur uska mahatva) + temporal (samay ke saath badlaav). Misaal: India ki lambi tat-rekha ne Africa, Europe, SE Asia se sampark kaise badhaaya. Auzaar: map, globe, atlas, GIS, infographic. NCERT ka School Bhuvan portal — apna gaon-shehar map karo! Aage padhoge: dharti ki satah kaise banti, vaayumandal-jalvaayu, mahasagar, biomes, biosphere reserves.

History — beete kal ki kahaani, saboot ke saath

Bharat ki sabse purani parampara — itihasa-purana: kahaaniyon se jaankaari bhi aur moolya bhi. Aaj ka itihaas-lekhan empirical evidence (dekh kar/prayog se mila saboot) par zyada nirbhar — genetics, carbon-14 dating, archaeology. Sources: literary (Samaveda pandulipi, Tirukkural taad-patra), archaeological (moorti, terracotta), epigraphic (pathar-dhaatu par likhe lekh — jaise Brahmi stambh lekh, Krishnadevaraya ka Kannada lekh), numismatic (sikke — Samudragupta ka sikka, Jahangir ka Dhanu raashi wala sikka!), aur yaatra-vritaant, genealogical records (vansh-vriksh), lok-kathaayein.

Political Science — satta aur shaasan

Shaasan ka adhyayan: satta kaise bant-ti, faisle kaise hote, neetiyaan kaise lagu hoti; samvidhan, sarkar, sansthaayein, andolan, videsh neeti. Gaon ka Panchayati Raj dikhaata hai ki satta sirf sarkari imaaraton mein nahi, samaaj ke rishton mein bhi hai. Bharat mein raajneeti dharma (naitik kartavya), artha (aarthik kalyaan) aur rajadharma (raja ke kartavya) se judi thi — Veda, Upanishad, Mahabharata, Shukraniti, Arthashastra. Seekh: satta ek zimmedari hai, sirf haq nahi.

Economics — seemit saadhan, anant ichchhaayein

Log aur samaaj seemit sansaadhanon ko kaise use karein — kya banaayein, kaise baantein. Consumer kya khareedein, producer kitna banaayein, sarkar vikas-sthirta aur kushalta-nyaay mein santulan kaise rakhe. India sadiyon tak duniya ki agrani arthvyavasthaon mein tha; colonial raaj ne gareebi, akaal aur udyog-vinaash diya; azaadi ke baad punarnirmaan — aaj infrastructure, shiksha, technology, gareebi mein kami, badhti umar. Lakshya: sustainable vikas — aaj ki zaroorat poori karo, kal ki peedhi ka haq maare bina.

Kyon padhein? — samajhdaar naagrik banne ke liye

Ghar, paani, sadak, school, bazaar, internet — sab samaaj ke banaaye system hain; Social Science batata hai ye kaise bane aur kis par kya asar daalte hain. Alag bhasha-riwaaj ke peechhe bhoogol, itihaas, sanskriti, arthvyavastha hai — ye samajh samman aur sahyog laati hai, aur dikhaati hai ki vividhta ke neeche ek ekta hai. Paryavaran, swasthya, naukri jaise muddon par sahi sawaal poochhna seekhte ho. Ye ratne ka subject nahi — logon, jagahon, samaaj, sanskriti aur satta ko samajhne ka subject hai!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Social Science = maanav samaaj ka systematic study; 'kya' ke saath 'kyon' aur 'kaise jude'.
  • Natural sciences — prakriti; Social Science — samaaj, sansthaayein, sanskriti, insaani rishte.
  • Samaaj sanyog se nahi — itihaas, bhoogol, sansthaayein, sansaadhan, insaani faisle.
  • Panchamahabhuta: Prithvi, Apah, Agni, Vayu, Akasha. Vasudhaiva kutumbakam.
  • Arthashastra (Kautilya, ~2,300 saal pehle) — prashasan, arthvyavastha, tax, raja ke kartavya.
  • Tareeke: observation, interview, survey, dastavez, tulna → evidence-based explanation.
  • Drought ki misaal — paryavaran, economy, politics, society, culture.
  • Class 9–10 ke 4 core: Geography, History, Political Science, Economics; aur Sociology, Philosophy, Anthropology, Psychology.
  • Geography: spatial + temporal; auzaar — map, globe, atlas, GIS; School Bhuvan portal (NCERT).
  • History: itihasa-purana parampara; aadhunik — empirical evidence, genetics, carbon-14, archaeology.
  • Sources: literary (Samaveda, Tirukkural), archaeological, epigraphic (Brahmi, Krishnadevaraya Kannada lekh), numismatic (Samudragupta; Jahangir ka Sagittarius sikka), genealogical records, oral.
  • Political Science: shaasan, satta, samvidhan, andolan; Panchayati Raj; dharma, artha, rajadharma; Shukraniti, Mahabharata, Arthashastra; satta = zimmedari.
  • Economics: seemit sansaadhan; consumer-producer-government ke faisle; colonial vinaash → punarnirmaan; sustainable growth.
  • Aage ke vishay: biomes, Renaissance, Enlightenment, Industrial Revolution, civil society, GDP, Union Budget, entrepreneurship.

Yaad karne ki trick

4 subjects = "GHPE" — 'Ghar Pe'

Geography, History, Political Science, Economics — Social Science 'ghar pe' padho!

Panchamahabhuta = "PAAVA"

Prithvi, Apah, Agni, Vayu, Akasha.

History ke sources = "LAEN"

Literary, Archaeological, Epigraphic (lekh), Numismatic (sikke).

Raajneeti ke 3 = "DAR"

Dharma, Artha, Rajadharma — 'satta se DAR mat, zimmedari samjho'.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Social Science ki sabse sahi paribhasha kya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Maanav samaaj ka vyavasthit adhyayan — Ye samaaj, sansthaayein, sanskriti aur rishton ko samajhta hai.

  2. Q2. Panchamahabhuta mein kaunsa tatva NAHI hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (D) Lauh (iron) — Paanch: prithvi, jal (apah), agni, vaayu, aakaash.

  3. Q3. Sikkon se itihaas jaanne ko kya kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Numismatics — Numismatic source = sikke, mudra, medal.

  4. Q4. Pathar ya taamrapatra par likhe lekh kis prakaar ke source hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Epigraphic — Epigraphic — inscriptions.

  5. Q5. Arthashastra kisse juda hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Kautilya — ~2,300 saal purana prashasan-arthvyavastha granth.

  6. Q6. Geography ke do nazariye kaunse hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Spatial aur temporal — Jagah aur samay dono.

  7. Q7. NCERT ka kaunsa portal apna gaon/shehar map karne mein madad karta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) School Bhuvan — NRSC ke saath bana School Bhuvan.

  8. Q8. Drought ka asar kisaan ki kamaai par — ye kis discipline ka sawaal hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Economics — Kamaai, utpaadan — economics.
