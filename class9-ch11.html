Theme G · Geography

Life on Earth

Biomes — jungle, ghaas ke maidaan, registaan, tundra aur jaliya; biome pyramid, biodiversity ka sanrakshan, van aur van-vaasi, GI tag aur saajhe sansaadhan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Dharti — ek badi rang-birangi rajaai

Socho dharti ek patchwork quilt (godadi) hai — har tukde ka apna rang, mausam aur jeev. In bade tukdon ko biome kehte hain. Bio (Greek bios = jeevan) + -ome (samooh). Biome = duniya ke bade prakritik kshetra jahan milte-julte mausam mein khaas tarah ke paudhe-jaanwar rehte hain — barfeela tundra, garam registaan, hare jungle, vishaal ghaas ke maidaan. Farak samjho: biome = poora shehar, ecosystem = uska ek mohalla jahan jeev aur nirjeev cheezein aapas mein judti hain.

Biome kaise banta hai?

Kaun se jeev kahan panpenge — ye tay karte hain: taapmaan, varsha ka pattern, mukhya vanaspati aur unke ugne ka roop. Do bade varg: Terrestrial (zameeni) — van, ghaas ke maidaan, registaan, tundra; aur Aquatic (jaliya) — meetha paani aur samudri. Naqshe par biomes: tropical rainforest, grassland, desert, temperate deciduous (chaude patte jo mausam mein jhadte), taiga (shankudhaari — pine, spruce, larch), tundra.

Forest biome — prakriti ka hara chhaata

Tropical rainforest — Bhoomadhya rekha ke paas, 23.5°N se 23.5°S; saal bhar garam aur geela → ghane jungle. Sabse bade: Amazon (S. America), Congo (Africa), Indonesia-Malaysia. India mein: Andaman-Nicobar aur North-East. Ye duniya ka sabse ameer jaiv-bhandaar — isliye 'gene bank'. Oxygen aur jalvaayu ka santulan. Parten: Emergent layer (sabse oonche ped) → Upper canopy → Understory/Lower canopy → Forest floor.

Grassland biome

Khule bade maidaan — zyaadatar ghaas, ped bahut kam; madhyam varsha — ghane jungle ke liye kaafi nahi. Temperate grasslands — Prairies (N. America), Steppes (Asia) — upjaaoo mitti, kheti. Tropical grasslands = Savanna (Africa) — bikhre ped; sher, haathi, giraffe. Charne wale jaanwar, kheti aur pashupaalan ke liye zaroori.

Desert biome

Sabse sookhe biome — bahut kam baarish. Garam — Sahara (Africa); thande — Gobi (Asia). Rann of Kutch (Gujarat) — duniya ke sabse bade namak registaanon mein se; monsoon mein daldal, sookhe mausam mein safed namak ka registaan. Paudhe (cactus) aur jaanwar (oont, chhipkali, saanp) paani jama karne aur garmi sehne mein maahir. Khanij aur tel bhi. Socho: Rajasthan ka oont aur Nubra Valley (Ladakh) ka do-koohan wala oont — Nubra wale ke baal ghane kyon? Kyonki wahan thand hai!

Tundra biome

Sabse thanda — Arctic aur Antarctic ke paas. Lambi kathor sardi, chhoti garmi. Mitti lagbhag saal bhar jami — permafrost — isliye ped nahi ug paate. Vanaspati: moss, lichen, chhoti jhaadiyan. Jaanwar: reindeer, arctic fox, polar bear. Bahut naazuk — thoda taapmaan badla to poora tantra prabhavit. Himalaya (subtropics) mein to shankudhaari ped hain — kyonki wahan oonchaai se thand hai, par dhoop aur mitti tundra jaisi kathor nahi.

Aquatic biomes

Dharti ka lagbhag teen-chauthaai (3/4) hissa. Meetha paani (freshwater) — nadi, jheel, taalaab, wetlands; kheti, peene ka paani, machhli-mendhak-keede. Samudri (marine) — mahasaagar, saagar, coral reefs; bhaari jaiv-vividhta, garmi jama-baant kar jalvaayu par asar; whale, dolphin, machhli, plankton; laakhon ki rozi-roti.

Biome pyramid

Jeevon ke rishte ko oorja, sankhya ya biomass ke roop mein dikhaane wala chitra. Neeche — producers (paudhe, shaival) jo photosynthesis se suraj ki oorja pakadte. Upar — herbivores (primary consumer) → carnivores (secondary) → top predators (tertiary). Upar jaate hue sankhya, biomass aur oorja ghatti — har star par oorja garmi ke roop mein nikal jaati. Ek aur pyramid: thande-dhruviya se garam-ushna, geela se sookha — tundra, coniferous, deciduous, chaparral, grassland, desert, rainforest, scrubland, tropical seasonal forest, savanna.

Sab jude hain — biodiversity

Biome jeevon ko ghar dete; jeev bhi biome badalte — jaanwar paraagan aur beej failaav, insaan kheti-shehar, sookshmjeev poshak tatva lautaate. Biodiversity = dharti par jeevan ki vividhta — paudhe, jaanwar, sookshmjeev, unke ecosystem; sirf prajaatiyon ki ginti nahi, prajaati ke andar genetic farak aur aavaason ki vividhta bhi. Hotspots — Himalaya, Western Ghats, North-East, Andaman-Nicobar — aisi prajaatiyan jo kahin aur nahi (Nilgiri Tahr, Himalayan Snowcock, yak, mangrove).

Sanrakshan mein bhoogol ki bhoomika

Biodiversity asamaan bati hai, isliye bhoogol zaroori. Ecosystem-based — tat par mangrove bachao, pahaad par ped lagao. Protected Areas — national park, wildlife sanctuary, biosphere reserve, World Heritage Site. Barriers aur corridors — nadi, pahaad, ghaati jaanwar ke aane-jaane aur gene flow ko rokte ya raasta dete → wildlife corridor. Insaan-paryavaran — pahaad mein seedhidaar kheti, maidaan mein agroforestry. Naqshe par dhoondho: Namdapha NP, Rann of Kutch, Gahirmatha beach, Gulf of Mannar, Pachmarhi, Achanakmar–Amarkantak.

Ecotourism — Thenmala ki misaal

Ecotourism = prakriti ko nuksaan pahunchaaye bina, sthaaniya logon ko sahara dete hue zimmedaar yatra. Swadesh Darshan 2.0 (Paryatan Mantralaya) — tikau paryatan sthal. Thenmala (Kollam, Keralam) — India ka pehla planned ecotourism sthal; naam ka matlab 'Honey Hill'; Western Ghats (UNESCO World Heritage) mein; Tourism + Forest + Irrigation vibhaag ne milkar banaaya; sthaaniya log guide, eco-guard bane — fayda samudaay mein.

Van aur rozi-roti

Gaon aur aadivaasi samudaay vanon se paate: khaana, eendhan, chaara, dawa, ghar, lakdi, baans. Non-timber forest products (NTFP) — shahad, phal, mewe, raal, jadibootiyan. Gond aur Bhil sansaadhan lene se pehle van-devtaaon ki pooja karte — tikau parampara. Sacred groves — India mein 1,00,000+; naam: Devrai, Devarakadu, Kavu, Sarna — chhote par biodiversity hotspot, jal-srot bhi bachaate. Rajya-vaar: tendu patta, mahua, sal beej (MP, Chhattisgarh); baans-lakdi (Assam, Tripura, Keralam); jangli phal, shahad, mushroom (Jharkhand, Odisha); ashwagandha, neem, tulsi (Himalaya, Western Ghats).

Van-vaasi — rakshak aur gyaani

Gyaan ke bhandaar — jadi-booti ka paaramparik gyaan peedhi-dar-peedhi. Kamaai — tendu, mahua, shahad, lac, baans. Ghar ki zaroorat — lakdi, chaara, kand-mool. Sanskriti — tyohaar-reeti van se jude. Van-raksha — Bishnoi aandolan (Rajasthan, Amrita Devi) ne prerit kiya Chipko aandolan (Uttarakhand) — mahilaayein pedon se lipat gayin! Mahilaaon ki bhoomika sabse badi.

GI Tag — jagah ki pehchaan

Geographical Indication (GI) = ek bauddhik sampada adhikaar jo batata hai ki utpaad khaas jagah ka hai aur uski khaasiyat usi jagah se judi hai. Van-aadhaarit GI: Ramban Sulai Honey (J&K), Tezpur Litchi (Assam), Channapatna Toys (Karnataka — 'Aale mara' ped ki lakdi), Kandhamal Haldi (Odisha — zyada curcumin), Erode Manjal (Tamil Nadu), Nilgiri Tea, Tripura Queen Pineapple, Khasi Mandarin (Meghalaya). Fayda: viraasat ki raksha, sthaaniya kamaai.

Van-vaasiyon ki mushkilein

Swasthya, shiksha, saaf paani tak pahunch kam; mahilaaon ko lakdi ke dhuen se bimaari (PM Ujjwala Yojana se LPG). Van-upyog par roak, katai, vyavsaayik dohan → kaanoon aur zaroorat mein takraav. Baadh, bhooskhalan, jungle ki aag — FSI ke anusaar ~36% van aag-prabhavit, ~4% ati-prabhavit. Maanav-vanyajeev sangharsh — haathi, bandar, jangli suar fasal ujaadte. Utpaad saste daam par bikte — processing aur marketing chahiye. Sadak par forest bridge se roadkill kam.

Sarkari kadam aur Mendha Lekha

Forest Rights Act (FRA) 2006 — paaramparik van-vaasiyon ke zameen aur van-sansaadhan ke haq; laghu van upaj (baans, shahad, jadi-booti) lene ka haq aur kheti ki chhoti zameen ka maalikana. Joint Forest Management (JFM) — samudaay raksha kare aur upaj ka laabh le. PESA, DAJGUA (Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan), PM JANMAN. Mendha Lekha (Gadchiroli, Maharashtra; Gond) — FRA ke tahat van-adhikaar paane wala pehla gaon; naara 'Mawa Nate Mawa Raj' (hamara gaon, hamara raj); Gram Sabha se faisle; 1,800 hectare van; baans katai se kamaai, shiksha-infrastructure par kharch.

Saajhe sansaadhan (Ecological Commons)

Van, nadi, jheel, charaagaah, hawa, jaiv-vividhta — jo poore samudaay ke hain, kisi ek ke nahi; paaramparik niyamon se chalte. Aabaadi, udyog, nijeekaran se ghat rahe. Bihar machhli: Indian Fisheries Act 1897 ke daur mein zameendaaron ka kabza, machhuaare haashiye par → Bihar Fish Jalkar Management Act, 2006 — samudaay-aadhaarit prabandh → machhli utpaadan badha. Taalaab: Chandella-Bundela raajaaon ne 800–1200 CE mein Bundelkhand mein ~8,000 taalaab; Karnataka kere, Tamil Nadu eri, Andhra-Odisha chevuru, Bihar ahar-pyne, Gond raajaaon ke kata (teen-taraf band jalaashay). Aaj India mein 15 lakh+ chaalu taalaab.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Biome — bios (jeevan) + ome (samooh); terrestrial (van, grassland, desert, tundra) + aquatic (freshwater, marine).
  • Rainforest: 23.5°N–23.5°S; Amazon, Congo, SE Asia; India — Andaman-Nicobar, North-East; 'gene bank'; parten — emergent, upper canopy, understory, floor.
  • Grassland: temperate — prairies, steppes (kheti); tropical — savanna (sher, haathi, giraffe).
  • Desert: garam Sahara, thanda Gobi; Rann of Kutch — namak registaan; Nubra oont ke ghane baal.
  • Tundra: permafrost; moss, lichen; reindeer, arctic fox, polar bear.
  • Aquatic ~3/4 satah; coral reef, plankton; jalvaayu niyantran.
  • Biome pyramid: producers → herbivores → carnivores → top predators; upar oorja/sankhya/biomass ghatte.
  • Hotspots: Himalaya, Western Ghats, North-East, Andaman-Nicobar.
  • Sanrakshan: ecosystem-based, protected areas, corridors, sthaaniya prathaayein (terrace, agroforestry).
  • Thenmala (Kollam) — pehla planned ecotourism; 'Honey Hill'. Swadesh Darshan 2.0.
  • Sacred groves 1,00,000+ — Devrai, Devarakadu, Kavu, Sarna.
  • Bishnoi (Amrita Devi, Rajasthan) → Chipko (Uttarakhand).
  • GI: Ramban Sulai Honey, Tezpur Litchi, Channapatna Toys, Kandhamal Haldi, Erode Manjal, Nilgiri Tea, Tripura Queen Pineapple, Khasi Mandarin.
  • FSI: ~36% van aag-prabhavit, ~4% ati. PM Ujjwala — mahila swasthya.
  • FRA 2006, JFM, PESA, DAJGUA, PM JANMAN. Mendha Lekha — pehla FRA gaon; 'Mawa Nate Mawa Raj'; 1,800 ha.
  • Bihar Fish Jalkar Management Act 2006; Bundelkhand ~8,000 taalaab (800–1200 CE); kere, eri, chevuru, ahar-pyne, kata; 15 lakh+ taalaab.

Yaad karne ki trick

Terrestrial biome = "Van Ghaas Ret Barf"

Forest, Grassland, Desert, Tundra — garam-geele se thande-sookhe tak.

Rainforest ki parten = "EUUF"

Emergent, Upper canopy, Understory, Forest floor — upar se neeche.

Taalaabon ke naam = "Kere-Eri-Chevuru-Ahar-Kata"

Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra-Odisha, Bihar, Gondwana — 'KECAK'.

Grassland = "Pra-Ste-Sav"

Prairies (America), Steppes (Asia) = temperate; Savanna (Africa) = tropical.

Mendha Lekha = "Mawa Nate Mawa Raj"

Hamara gaon, hamara raj — FRA 2006 ka pehla gaon, Gadchiroli.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. 'Biome' mein 'bio' kis Greek shabd se aaya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Bios — jeevan — Bios = life.

  2. Q2. Tropical rainforest ko 'gene bank' kyon kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Sabse zyada genetic vividhta — Duniya ka sabse ameer jaiv-bhandaar.

  3. Q3. Savanna kis prakaar ka biome hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Tropical grassland — Africa — bikhre ped, sher, giraffe.

  4. Q4. Saal bhar jami mitti ko kya kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Permafrost — Tundra mein; ped nahi ugte.

  5. Q5. Biome pyramid mein upar jaane par kya ghatta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Oorja, sankhya aur biomass — Har star par oorja garmi ban kar nikalti.

  6. Q6. India ka pehla planned ecotourism sthal —

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Thenmala — Kollam, Keralam — 'Honey Hill'.

  7. Q7. Chipko aandolan ko kis aandolan ne prerit kiya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Bishnoi aandolan — Amrita Devi, Rajasthan.

  8. Q8. Kandhamal Haldi kis rajya ka GI utpaad hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Odisha — Zyada curcumin, aadivaasi kheti.

  9. Q9. Forest Rights Act ke tahat van-adhikaar paane wala pehla gaon —

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Mendha Lekha — Gadchiroli, Maharashtra.

  10. Q10. Tamil Nadu ke paaramparik taalaab kya kehlaate hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Eri — Karnataka — kere; Andhra-Odisha — chevuru.
