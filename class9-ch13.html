Governance and Public Policy

Government aur governance ka farak, India mein shaasan ke mulya, good governance ke 7 gun, e-governance, public policy kya hai, kaun banaata hai aur policy cycle.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Baadh wala shehar — sab milkar

Socho tumhare shehar mein kai din bhaari baarish. Sadkein doobi, school band, bijli gul, bimaari ka darr. Ab dekho kaun kya karta: Nagar Nigam naaliyan saaf, PWD sadak theek, police traffic, school ki soochna parents ko, swasthya karmi bimaari roke, pratinidhi awaaz uthaayein, padosi kamzor logon ki madad karein. Koi ek vibhaag akela nahi kar sakta — taal-mel chahiye. Isi milkar faisla lene, laagu karne aur jawabdeh rehne ki prakriya ko Governance kehte hain.

Government vs Governance

Government = sansthaayein jo raajnaitik adhikaar chalaati — Legislature, Executive, Judiciary; sawaal: 'kaun shaasan karta hai?' Governance = prakriya — faisle kaise bante, laagu hote, nigraani hoti; isme sarkar ke saath nagrik, media, civil society, private sector bhi; sawaal: 'shaasan kaise hota hai?' Misaal: street light lagaana Nagar Nigam/Panchayat (government) ka kaam; par andhere spot pehchaanna, kharab batti ki shikaayat, feedback — ye poori prakriya governance. Loktantrik governance ke aadhaar: paardarshita, jawabdehi, bhaagidaari, rule of law — nagrik kendra mein.

Hamari apni jaden — mulya aur prateek

Upanivesh (colonialism) ne kai deshon par apne model thope; aazaadi ke baad bhi wo sansthaayein bani rahin. India do baaton mein alag: 1) apni purv-aupniveshik shaasan paramparaaon ka gyaan granthon aur prathaaon mein bacha raha; 2) sab sansthaayein ek saath badalna sambhav nahi tha, isliye Samvidhan Bharatiya mulyon ki bhaavna se bana. British 'Order of the Star of India' ka motto tha 'Heaven's Light, Our Guide'; humne apnaaya 'Satyamev Jayate' (Mundaka Upanishad). Supreme Court ka motto 'Yato Dharmastato Jayah' (Mahabharata) — jahan dharm, wahan vijay.

Sengol, Ram Rajya, Rajadharma

Sengol — nyaypoorn shaasan ka prateek, Chola kaal (1,000+ saal) se parampara. 15 Aug 1947 ki aadhi raat Nehru ne Tamil Nadu ke Adheenam santon se praapt kiya; 2023 mein naye Sansad bhavan mein PM Narendra Modi ne sthaapit kiya. Gandhi ka aadarsh — Ram Rajya: imaandaari, paardarshita, karuna, kalyan, jan-kendrit shaasan. Rajadharma — raja ka pehla kartavya praja ki raksha aur kalyan; shaasan pavitra zimmedaari, sirf taaqat nahi. Arthashastra: 'Prajasukhe sukham rajnah' — praja ke sukh mein raja ka sukh, praja ke hit mein raja ka hit. Rigveda ki Sabha-Samiti bhi yaad karo.

Good Governance ke 7 gun

1) Rule of Law — kaanoon ke saamne sab barabar. 2) Accountability — adhikaari apne kaam ke liye jawabdeh. 3) Participation — nagrik faisle mein bhaagidaar. 4) Transparency — faisle khule aur uplabdh. 5) Effectiveness — saarvajanik sansaadhan samajhdaari se. 6) Responsiveness — sarkar logon ki zaroorat par dhyaan de. 7) Inclusiveness — har nagrik ko barabar avsar. Sabki bhoomika: sarkar kaanoon-neeti-sevaayein; nagrik vote, kaanoon paalan, bhaagidaari; media mudde saamne laaye; civil society jaagrukta; private sector arthik gatividhi aur innovation. Good governance = sarkar aur nagrik ki saajhedaari.

Chunautiyan aur Good Governance Day

Logon ki zaroortein badalti rehti hain — shaasan ka kaam kabhi khatam nahi hota. Chunautiyan: vibhaagon mein kamzor taal-mel; sevaaon tak asamaan pahunch (samaajik, aarthik, bhaugolik rukaawat); bhrashtaachaar aur pad ka durupyog; galat soochna/fake news. Hal: majboot sansthaayein, zimmedaar adhikaari, sakriya nagrik. 25 December = National Good Governance Day (pata karo kyon yahi taareekh chuni gayi!).

E-Governance — technology se behtar shaasan

Sarkar ICT (soochna-sanchaar technology) se sevaayein de, jaankaari baante, nagrikon se baat kare, feedback le = e-governance. Fayde: paardarshi, aasaan, tez; kaagaz kam, daftar ke chakkar khatam. Misaalein: UMANG (ek app, kai sarkari sevaayein), Aadhaar (pehchaan), GeM (Government e-Marketplace — sarkari khareed), DigiLocker (dastavez surakshit), CPGRAMS (shikaayat nivaaran), APAAR (chhatron ki academic ID). Shart: device, internet aur digital kaushal — isliye sabko shaamil karna zaroori. Socho pehle aur ab: janm praman-patra, admit card, marksheet, vetan, rail ticket, gaadi registration!

Public Policy — school ki plastic-mukt neeti

Sadak, traffic signal, saaf paani, bijli, park, kooda uthaana — sab public policies ka nateeja. School misaal: bachche single-use plastic laate, campus ganda → principal, teacher, chhatra, parents ne milkar 'Plastic-Free Campus Policy' banaayi — apni bottle, plastic bag nahi, alag-alag dustbin, maasik jaagrukta, teacher nigraani. Ye niti hai kyonki samasya sulajhaane ki yojnabaddh kaaryavaahi hai. Public policy = sarkar dwara apnaaya gaya kaary-maarg — kya, kyon, kaun, kaise, aur safalta kaise naapein; phir feedback loop se sudhaar.

'Public' kya banaata hai?

Policy public tab — jab sabke liye ho, sarkar banaaye, public sansthaayein laagu karein, aksar tax ke paise se chale, jan-kalyan badhaaye aur nagrik bhaagidaar hon — shiksha, swasthya, transport, safaai, paryavaran, suraksha. Har policy public nahi: school ki mobile neeti, company ka work-from-home, society ke parking/guest niyam — sirf ek sangathan tak seemit. Pehchaano: sports policy (public), har ghar saaf paani abhiyaan (public), company WFH (policy), Nagar Nigam ka kooda-vibhaajan (public), housing society ke guest hours (policy), bachchon ka muft teekakaran (public).

Kaun-kaun banaata hai policy?

Nagrik — samasya batao, vote do, sujhaav-feedback (school ke paas zebra crossing ki maang). Chune pratinidhi (MP, MLA, local) — mudde uthaao, kaanoon, budget (MLA ne Vidhan Sabha mein paani ki kami uthaayi). Adhikaari/vibhaag/mantralaya — banaao, laagu karo, taal-mel, nigraani (District Collector yojana laagu kare). Civil society, NGO, visheshagya, media — adhyayan, sujhaav, jaagrukta, nigraani (NGO ka jal-sanrakshan abhiyaan). Private sector — technology, innovation, sansaadhan (PPP se school mein solar panel). Antarrashtriya sangathan — anubhav, technical aur aarthik madad (WHO sankraamak rog niyantran).

Case study — aspataal mein ramp

72 saal ki Shanti Devi ko OPD tak seedhiyan chadhni padin — na ramp, na railing. Wheelchair wale vyakti andar hi nahi ja paaye. Drishtibaadhit ke liye na tactile raasta, na Braille. Mareezon ne adhikaariyon aur pratinidhiyon se baat ki; viklang adhikaar sanghathan ne akhbaar-social media mein uthaaya. Zila prashasan ne PWD aur Health Department se jaanch karaayi; doctor, staff, architect, PwD sangathan, buzurg, nivaasiyon se salaah; sarkar ne paisa manzoor kiya — ramp, lift, railing, tactile path, accessible toilet, signboard. Ab buzurg, viklang, garbhvati mahilaayein, bachche sab aasaani se. Ye hai policy ka safar — samasya se samaadhaan tak!

Policy cycle — 7 kadam

1) Policy Desirability — kaunsi zaroorat/samasya? 2) Prioritising — kaunsa mudda pehle? 3) Formulation — hitdhaarakon se salaah, draft, aarthik sambhavna. 4) Adoption — kaanoon (legislation) aur niyam (regulations) banaana. 5) Implementation — adhikaari, local bodies, civil society, private, nagrik se amal. 6) Monitoring — pragati naapna, jawabdehi, kamiyaan pehchaanna. 7) Evaluation — nateeje aur asar ka aakalan, feedback → phir se cycle. Koi kadam chhoota to? Misaal: 1 lakh ped lagaaye, paisa diya, par paani-suraksha nahi → zyaadatar mar gaye — implementation/monitoring kamzor!

NEP 2020 — milkar bani neeti

Pichhli shiksha neeti 1986. Samaaj tezi se badla → 2015 mein nayi neeti ki prakriya shuru. 2017 mein visheshagya samiti — adhyaksh vaigyanik Dr K. Kasturirangan. Khaasiyat: vishaal jan-paraamarsh — 2.5 lakh Gram Panchayat, 6,600 block, 6,000 shehri nikaay, 676 zile; lagbhag 2 lakh sujhaav. Kai sujhaav shaamil; July 2020 mein Union Cabinet ne manzoori di.

Policy → Programme → Scheme

Policy = disha aur lakshya (kya aur kyon). Programme = yojana/raasta (kaise). Scheme = thos kaam jisse fayda logon tak pahunche. Misaal National Health Policy 2017 — lakshya: sabko achchhi swasthya seva, sarkari tantra majboot, samaanta-sasta-jawabdeh, rokthaam aur badhaava. Programmes: swasthya tantra majboot, sabko vyaapak seva, preventive-promotive, digital health. Schemes: Ayushman Bharat–PMJAY, Health and Wellness Centres, Mission Indradhanush, Janani Suraksha Yojana. Yaad rakho: policy disha, programme raasta, scheme kadam.

Khelo Bharat Niti 2025 — taal-mel ki misaal

Khel ko rashtra-nirmaan ka saadhan maankar 2025 mein Khelo Bharat Niti. Lakshya: block se rashtriya star tak khel sanskriti. Kendra — neeti dhaancha, lakshya, funding. Rajya — 'Model Sports State', rashtriya khel aayojan, PPP se infrastructure. Zila — 'Model Sports District'. Block — niyamit pratiyogita, pratibha khoj. Khel sansthaan — coaching, upkaran. Private — sponsorship, poshan-upkaran research. Nagrik/parivaar — bachchon, khaaskar betiyon ko protsaahan, feedback. Safalta = sabka taal-mel. Tum — vote (aage chalkar), kaanoon paalan, shikaayat, bhaagidaari, feedback se shaasan sudhaar sakte ho!

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Governance = decision-making + implementation + monitoring; aadhaar — transparency, accountability, participation, rule of law.
  • Government = 'kaun' (sansthaayein); Governance = 'kaise' (prakriya; sarkar + nagrik + media + civil society + private).
  • Mulya: Satyamev Jayate (Mundaka Upanishad); SC motto Yato Dharmastato Jayah (Mahabharata).
  • Sengol — Chola; 1947 Nehru ko Adheenam se; 2023 naye Sansad mein. Gandhi — Ram Rajya. Arthashastra — 'Prajasukhe sukham rajnah'.
  • Good governance ke 7 gun: rule of law, accountability, participation, transparency, effectiveness, responsiveness, inclusiveness.
  • Chunautiyan: kamzor taal-mel, asamaan pahunch, bhrashtaachaar, fake news. 25 Dec — National Good Governance Day.
  • E-gov: UMANG, Aadhaar, GeM, DigiLocker, CPGRAMS, APAAR; digital pahunch zaroori.
  • Public policy — kya, kyon, kaun, kaise, kaise naapein + feedback loop. 'Public' = sabke liye, sarkar dwara, tax se, jan-kalyan.
  • Stakeholders: nagrik, pratinidhi, adhikaari, civil society/media, private (PPP), antarrashtriya sangathan (WHO).
  • Policy cycle: desirability → prioritising → formulation → adoption → implementation → monitoring → evaluation.
  • NEP 2020: purani 1986; 2015 shuruaat; 2017 Kasturirangan samiti; 2.5 lakh GP, 6,600 block, 6,000 ULB, 676 zile; ~2 lakh sujhaav; July 2020 Cabinet.
  • National Health Policy 2017 → programmes → schemes (AB-PMJAY, HWC, Mission Indradhanush, JSY).
  • Khelo Bharat Niti 2025 — Model Sports State/District, block star par talent scouting, PPP.

Yaad karne ki trick

Government vs Governance = "Kaun vs Kaise"

Government = Maalik kaun; Governance = kaise chalta.

Good governance ke 7 = "RAP TERI"

Rule of law, Accountability, Participation, Transparency, Effectiveness, Responsiveness, Inclusiveness.

Policy-Programme-Scheme = "Disha, Raasta, Kadam"

Policy disha deti, programme raasta dikhaata, scheme kadam uthaati.

Do motto = "Upanishad ka Satya, Mahabharat ka Dharma"

Satyamev Jayate — Mundaka Upanishad; Yato Dharmastato Jayah — Mahabharata (SC).

Policy cycle = "Zaroorat, Pehle, Draft, Kaanoon, Amal, Nazar, Nateeja"

Desirability, Prioritising, Formulation, Adoption, Implementation, Monitoring, Evaluation.
