Theme E · Economics

From Ideas to Startups

Idea se business tak — entrepreneur kaun, Jataka ki 'mare choohe' wali kahaani, Lijjat aur Oberoi, business plan, 4 functional areas, profit-loss, Chettiar aur India ke startups.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Nimbu paani ka stall — business ki pehli class

School fest mein 'Young Entrepreneurs Challenge' — tumne doston ke saath nimbu paani ka stall lagaaya. Kya-kya sochna pada? Nimbu-cheeni par kitna kharch, glass kahan se, ek glass ka daam kitna, kitne logon ko pasand aaya, munaafa hua ya ghaata? Din ke ant mein teacher ko report di. Yahi hai business ka saar — yojana, sangathan aur karke seekhna. Entrepreneurship (udyamita) = ek vyakti ya samooh ka business idea ko asli udyam mein badalna. Profit = utpaadan aur bikri ka kharch nikaal kar bachi kamaai.

Entrepreneur kya karta hai?

Samasya pehchaanta aur nayi soch se hal karne ki thaan leta hai. Risk leta — paisa aur samay lagaata. Utpaadan ke saadhan jodta (zameen, mazdoor, poonji, technology). Business ke bade faisle leta. Apne innovation se samaaj ka bhala karta. Entrepreneur jigyaasu hote — jahan doosre chunauti dekhein, wahan avsar dekhte. Misaal: traffic nigraani ke liye drone banaane wali company, ya plastic ki jagah jute ke bag.

Mare choohe se dhanvaan — Jataka katha

Ek seth ne raja se kaha — 'mehnat aur dimaag ho to mara chooha bhi daulat ki shuruaat ban sakta hai'. Ek garib yuvak ne suna aur aazmaaya. Mara chooha ek dukaandaar ko uski bhookhi billi ke liye 2 sikkon mein becha → sikkon se phal-paani khareede, thake phool todne wale ko dekar phool liye → phool shehar mein beche → toofan ke baad giri tehniyan saaf karke eendhan becha → ghode ke vyapaariyon ko ghaas → jalpaan ki dukaan → videshi vyapaariyon se sauda. Bahut ameer bana; aabhaar mein seth ko aadhi daulat di aur shehar ke logon ki madad ki. Seekh: avsar pehchaano, smart kaam karo, kritagya raho.

Lijjat Papad — chhat se kraanti tak

1959, Mumbai ki ek chawl ki chhat — 7 grihiniyan, udhaar ka saamaan, ek recipe aur ₹80! Na degree, na investor — bas himmat aur ekta. Factory nahi thi: har mahila subah aata ghar le jaati, din mein papad belti, shaam ko laut aati — shart: quality ek jaisi. Cooperative — har sadasya barabar maalik, munaafa barabar. 1962 mein registration — Shri Mahila Griha Udyog Lijjat Papad ('lijjat' = Gujarati mein swaadisht). 1980–90s mein aata, masala, sabun, export; ₹100 crore paar. 2023: 45,000+ mahila sadasya, 83 shaakhaayein, ₹1,600 crore saalaana turnover; bus suvidha, bachchon ko scholarship. Papad ke saath hal ki — mahilaaon ki aarthik aazaadi!

Mohan Singh Oberoi — ₹50 ke clerk se hotel samrajya

1898, Punjab mein janm; bachpan mein pita gujar gaye. Shimla mein hotel desk clerk — ₹50/mahina. Sirf apna kaam nahi, har cheez seekhi — mehmaan seva, billing, prashasan. 1934 — patni ke gehne girvi (mortgage) rakhkar aur saari bachat lagaakar chhota hotel khareeda — bada risk! Ghaate wale hotel ko safal banaaya; luxury hotel, hotel management school, duniya bhar mein failaav. 2001 — Padma Bhushan; 2002 mein nidhan. Seekh: business sirf idea nahi — kushal staff ki bharti-training (HR), paise ka prabandh (finance), kushal sanchaalan (operations), bharosemand brand (marketing), grahak santushti sabse upar.

Business Plan — udyam ka naqsha

Doston ke saath bakery kholni hai? Pehle business plan — ek likhit dastavez/blueprint jo lakshya aur unhe paane ke kadam bataaye; idea se lekar chalaane tak roadmap. 8 sawaal: 1) Zaroorat aur idea — log kya chaahte, kaunsi samasya hal? (taaza bread, cake, ya whole-wheat jaise healthy?) 2) Sambhavit grahak — bachche, yuva, parivaar? 3) Paisa aur laagat — oven, kiraya, vetan, aata-mewe, packaging; paisa kahan se (bachat, bank loan, dost-rishtedaar, investor)? 4) Jagah — school-college-bazaar ke paas? online/app order? 5) Log aur kaushal — baker, helper, billing, delivery. 6) Daam aur munaafa — laagat nikle, munaafa ho, par grahak ke liye sasta; chhote mini cake jar jaise vikalp. 7) Prachaar — social media, poster, offer, achchhi packaging, grahak seva. 8) Chunautiyan aur risk — competition, kachche maal ka daam, badalti pasand, cash flow, jaldi kharab hone wala inventory.

4 functional areas — business ke khambhe

1) Management & Operations — roz ka kaam: yojana, sangathan, maargdarshan + maal/seva samay par, kushal tareeke se. Bakery: sahi upkaran, saamagri, quality nigraani, roz ka utpaadan bina der-barbaadi. Focus: kushal prakriya. 2) Finance & Accounting — paise ka samajhdaar prabandh; budget, report, cash flow. 3) Human Resources — sahi log, sahi kaushal: bharti, training (hygiene, baking standard), labh, jhagda suljhaana, achchha kaarya-sthal. People are the heart of business! 4) Marketing & Sales — jaagrukta aur bikri: catchy naam, social media photo-video, discount, mausami items (mango cake), online order-delivery, eco-friendly packaging.

Finance: fixed vs variable cost

Finance = paise ki yojana, intezaam aur upyog. Fixed cost — utpaadan kam ho ya zyada, wahi rehta (kam samay mein): kiraya, upkaran (oven, mixer, fridge), beema — 'overhead'. Variable cost — utpaadan ke saath ghatta-badhta: kachcha maal (aata, cheeni), packaging. (Kitaab mein bakery ke liye vetan ko bhi badalne wale kharch mein gina gaya hai.) Investment = bhavishya ki aamdani/munaafe ki ummeed mein paisa/sansaadhan lagaana. Accounting = len-den ko record, vyavasthit aur report karna — aamdani (revenue) aur kharch (expenses) ka hisaab; tax kaanoon ka paalan.

Profit & Loss Account — ABC Bakery

Profit = Total Revenue − Total Expenses. ABC Bakery (March 2026): Aamdani — bread-bun ₹1,20,000 + pastry-cookie ₹75,000 + cake order ₹85,000 + delivery ₹15,000 = ₹2,95,000. Kharch — kachcha maal ₹1,00,000, packaging ₹12,000, kiraya ₹40,000, vetan ₹30,000, bijli-gas ₹15,000, prachaar ₹8,000, vividh ₹7,000, loan byaaj ₹10,000 = ₹2,22,000. Profit before tax (PBT) = 2,95,000 − 2,22,000 = ₹73,000. Tax ₹13,000 → Profit after tax (PAT) = ₹60,000. (Categories ratne ki zaroorat nahi — tareeka samjho!)

Chettiar — paaramparik udyami samudaay

India mein udyamita ki jaden gehri — kaushal aur pesha peedhi-dar-peedhi. Nattukottai Chettiar (Tamil Nadu) — vyapaari, banker, saahookar; India ke saath Sri Lanka, Burma (Myanmar), Malaysia, Dakshin-Poorv Asia tak. Pehchaan: naitikta aur bharose par udhaar, behtareen hisaab-kitaab, munaafa phir se vyapar, mandir, shiksha, samaaj kalyan mein. Namak, chaawal, kapda aur vitt ka vyapar — aksar bina formal bank ke! Seekh: risk, anushaasan, lambi soch.

Startup — asafalta bhi seekh hai

Startup = naya, shuruaati daur ki company jo nayaa (innovative) utpaad/seva laaye. Kuch tezi se badhte, kuch band ho jaate — imaandaar koshish ke baad asafalta mein koi buraai nahi; har koshish seekh deti. Swami Vivekananda: 'Utho, jaago aur lakshya praapt hone tak ruko mat'. Aaj India duniya ka teesra sabse bada startup ecosystem; 2025 mein lagbhag 120 unicorn (1 billion dollar se zyada mulya). Kshetra: e-commerce, fin-tech (digital payment), health-tech, agri-tech. Hub: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR. Yojana: Startup India — Atmanirbhar Bharat (2020) ki soch; lakshya Viksit Bharat 2047 (aazaadi ke 100 saal).

Business samaaj ko kya deta hai?

Rozgaar aur aamdani parivaaron ko. Maal-seva ka utpaadan → arthik gatividhi. Tax → sadak, school, aspataal. Innovation → vikas aur nayi samasyaaon ke hal. Jeevan-star sudhaar — dawa, transport, technology. CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) — shiksha, paryavaran, swasthya. Ek aur misaal: 1931, Digambar aur Govind Dandekar ne South Mumbai mein ink powder se shuru kiya, purani kaanch ki botalon mein syaahi; artists ke liye achchhe rang nahi the → Glasgow jaakar colour chemistry padhi, lab banaayi → pencil, crayon, geometry box, paint — mashhoor brand bana. Agla udyami shaayad tumhari class mein baitha hai!

Yaad karne ki trick

4 functional areas = "OF HM" (Oh, Fun! Hum!)

Operations, Finance, HR, Marketing.

Fixed vs Variable = "Kiraya Kade, Maal Mulayam"

Kiraya (fixed) kada — badalta nahi; kachcha maal (variable) mulayam — utpaadan ke saath ghat-badh.

Lijjat ke number = "59 mein 7 ne 80 lagaaye"

1959, 7 mahilaayein, ₹80 — aaj ₹1,600 crore!

Profit ka formula = "Aaya − Gaya = Bacha"

Revenue − Expenses = Profit; phir tax ghatao to PAT.

Oberoi = "50 ki naukri, 34 ka jua, 2001 ka Bhushan"

₹50 vetan, 1934 mein risk, 2001 Padma Bhushan.
