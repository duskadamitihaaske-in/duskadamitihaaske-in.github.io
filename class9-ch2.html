Shaping of the Earth's Surface

Dharti ki parten, plate tectonics, weathering-erosion, nadi-hawa-glacier-lehron-bhoomigat jal se bane landforms, aur unse judi aapdaayein.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Dharti 'jeevit' hai — hamesha badalti

Dharti ki satah ek jaisi nahi rehti — andar ki aur bahar ki taaqatein use lagaataar badalti hain. Pahad, jwalamukhi, maidaan, ghaatiyan = landforms (prakritik bhoo-aakritiyaan). Inhe samajhne ki sabse badi chaabi hai plate tectonics ka siddhant.

Dharti ke andar — ek 'anda' jaisa

Ubla anda socho: patla chhilka, safed hissa, peela beech. Dharti mein bhi teen parten: Crust (sabse upar, jahan hum rehte — mahadweep ke neeche 30–40 km, samundar ke neeche sirf 5–7 km), Mantle (bahut moti aur garam, ~2,900 km), Core (sabse andar — outer core taral loha-nickel, inner core thos, ghoomti dhaatu ki gend, sabse ghani). Crust + upari mantle = Lithosphere (sakht, ~100 km) — yahi tooti hui hai plates mein. Uske neeche Asthenosphere — aadha pighla, jis par plates 'tairti' hain.

Plates kyon khisakti hain? — ubalti daal ki tarah

Siddhant W.J. Morgan se juda: dharti ki upari parat badi-chhoti plates mein bati hai jo kuch cm har saal khisakti hain. Prakaar: continental (mahadweep wali), oceanic (samundar tal wali), mixed. Badi plates: Pacific, Eurasian, African, North American, South American, Indo-Australian, Antarctic. Kyon khisakti? Patile mein ubalti daal dekho — neeche garam daal upar aati, upar ki thandi neeche jaati, aur upar tairti cheezein idhar-udhar khisakti hain. Mantle mein bhi aisi hi convection currents plates ko dhakelti-kheenchti hain.

Plates ki seemaayein — teen tarah ki mulaqat

Convergent — do plates aamne-saamne takraati: mahadweep-mahadweep → fold mountains (Himalaya!); samudri-mahadweepiya → samudri plate neeche dhans jaati → jwalamukhi aur bhookamp. Divergent — plates door hoti: neeche se magma upar aakar nayi crust banaata — Mid-Atlantic Ridge. Transform — plates ek-doosre ke bagal se ragadti: crust na banti na mitti, bas bhookamp — USA ka San Andreas Fault. Zyaadatar bhookamp-jwalamukhi Pacific ke chaaron or — 'Ring of Fire'.

India aur bhookamp

India mein bhi bade bhookamp aaye — 2001 Gujarat mein bhaari tabaahi. Ghani aabadi wale desh mein bada bhookamp bahut nuksaan karta hai. Purane samay mein ise 'bhookampa' kehte the; Varahamihira ne Brihatsamhita mein ek poora hissa likha — hawa, barish, baadal, jaanwaron ka vyavhaar aur grahon ki sthiti se sanket; kaaran chaar shaktiyon (Vayu, Agni, Indra, Varuna) se joda. India ka ekmaatra mud volcano — Baratang (Andaman), jahan aag nahi, zameen ki gas ke dabaav se keechad bulbule nikalta hai!

Weathering — todna; Erosion — tod kar le jaana

Weathering = chattaan ka wahin toot kar chhota hona (le jaana nahi). Teen prakaar: physical (garmi-thand, paala, hawa se phailna-sikudna), chemical (paani, hawa, tezaab se khanij badal jaana), biological (paudhon ki jadein daraar mein ghus kar pathar tod dein!). Isse mitti banti hai. Erosion (kataav) = toote hisse ko paani, hawa, barf, lehrein utha kar doosri jagah le jaana. Kisaan ki upjaau upari mitti beh jaaye to fasal ghat-ti; nadi-tat ke ghar, sadak beh jaate. Bachaav ke purane tareeke: contour trench, bunding (med), terracing (seedhidaar khet), check dam — Harappa se Arthashastra, Krishiparashara, Vrikshayurveda tak; Nagaland ka Zabo system.

Nadi ka safar — V se delta tak

Sabse badi 'moorti-kaar' — behta paani. Upari bhaag (dhalaan tez): V-shaped valley, waterfall (sakht chattaan upar, naram neeche kati → achaanak girna; paryatan aur bijli), rapids. Beech ka bhaag: nadi saanp jaisi ghoomti — meander (bahari kinaare kaatti, andar mitti jama karti); ek mod kat jaaye to bachi jheel = oxbow lake; upjaau floodplain. Neeche ka bhaag: nadi dheemi, mitti jama → tikona delta (chaawal, jute; machhli; par baadh ka khatra — jaise Sundarbans), levee. Kaveri par Kallanai — nadi ka sinchaai mein upyog.

Samundar ki lehrein aur glacier

Lehron se: beach (ret-kankad jama — paryatan, machhli, aur lehron se dhaal), sea cliff (khadi deewaar), wave-cut platform, sea cave → do taraf ki gufa mil kar arch → arch gire to akela khamba stack. Glacier (dheemi barf ki nadi) se: U-shaped valley, cirque (katora jaisa gadda), arete (dhaardar choti), hanging valley, fjord (samundar se bhari gehri patli ghaati — bandargah). Glacier jo patthar-mitti chhodta hai = moraine (lateral — kinaare, terminal — aakhir mein, medial — beech mein). Feb 2021 Chamoli (Uttarakhand) ki achaanak baadh — socho kyon aayi?

Hawa aur zameen ke neeche ka paani

Hawa (registan mein): yardang (hawa se tarasha lamba chattaani dhaancha), ventifact (ret ki maar se chikna pathar), deflation hollow (mitti udne se gadda — kabhi oasis bhi), desert pavement. Dunes: barchan (ardh-chandra), longitudinal, star (kai bhuja), parabolic. Bhoomigat jal (chuna pathar mein) — Karst topography: gufa, chhat se latakti stalactite (yaad rakho — 'tite' = chhat se tight latki), zameen se uthti stalagmite, sinkhole (zameen dhansna), bhoomigat nadi. Ye sab agents of gradation hain — oonchi jagah ghisaate, neechi bharte.

Landforms ne itihaas bhi banaaya

Ganga, Nile, Indus ke upjaau maidaan → kheti aur pehle shehar. Himalaya ne hamle roke par Khyber jaise darron se len-den bhi hua. Thar ne badi bastiyan nahi banne di par Silk Route jaise vyapaar raaste bane. Tat aur bandargahon se dakshin ke raajya door deshon se jude.

Landforms se judi 4 aapdaayein

Landslide — lagaataar barish se mitti bhaari aur phisalni, bhookamp, khadi dhalaan; insaani kaaran: jungle kaatna, khanan, sadak-nirmaan, kharab drainage. Avalanche — kam samay mein bhaari barf, achaanak garmi, tez hawa, skiing-trekking ka kampan. GLOF (Glacial Lake Outburst Flood) — glacier tezi se pighle → jheel badhe → moraine ka 'baandh' toote → neeche achaanak tabaahi. Dust storm — sookha, kam vanaspati, overgrazing, tez hawa; climate change se zyada. Seekh: dharti ki taaqat ko samjho aur pehle se taiyaari karo.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Plate tectonics (W.J. Morgan) — lithosphere kai plates mein; plates asthenosphere par kuch cm/saal khisakti.
  • Parten: crust (mahadweep 30–40 km, samundar 5–7 km), mantle (~2,900 km), outer core (taral Fe-Ni), inner core (thos, sabse ghani).
  • Plate prakaar: continental, oceanic, mixed. 7 badi: Pacific, Eurasian, African, N. American, S. American, Indo-Australian, Antarctic.
  • Kaaran: mantle mein convection currents.
  • Convergent → fold mountains (Himalaya), subduction → jwalamukhi/bhookamp; Divergent → mid-ocean ridge (Mid-Atlantic Ridge); Transform → bhookamp (San Andreas Fault).
  • Ring of Fire — Pacific ke chaaron or. 2001 Gujarat bhookamp.
  • Varahamihira — Brihatsamhita mein bhookamp ke sanket (Vayu, Agni, Indra, Varuna). Baratang — India ka ekmaatra mud volcano.
  • Weathering: physical, chemical, biological (sirf todna). Erosion: water, wind, glacial, coastal (le jaana).
  • Bachaav: contour trench, bunding, terracing, check dam; Nagaland — Zabo.
  • Agents of gradation: behta paani, glacier, hawa, lehrein, bhoomigat jal.
  • Nadi: upari — V-valley, waterfall, rapids; beech — meander, oxbow lake, floodplain; neeche — delta, levee, alluvial fan.
  • Lehrein: beach, sand bar, cliff, wave-cut platform, cave, arch, stack.
  • Glacier: U-valley, cirque, arete, hanging valley, fjord; moraine — lateral, terminal, medial.
  • Hawa: yardang, ventifact, deflation hollow, desert pavement; dunes — barchan, longitudinal, star, parabolic.
  • Bhoomigat jal (Karst): cave, stalactite (upar se), stalagmite (neeche se), sinkhole/doline, underground river.
  • Aapdaayein: landslide, avalanche, GLOF, dust storm; Chamoli flood Feb 2021.

Yaad karne ki trick

Teen seemaayein = "Milo, Bichhdo, Ragdo"

Convergent = milo (takraao), Divergent = bichhdo, Transform = ragdo (bagal se).

Stalactite vs Stalagmite

staCtite = Ceiling (chhat se latki); staGmite = Ground (zameen se uthi).

Coast ka kram = "Cave → Arch → Stack"

'Gufa bani, pul bana, phir khamba bacha.'

Nadi ka safar = "V–M–D"

Upar V-valley, beech Meander, neeche Delta.

Glacier ka nishaan = U, nadi ka = V

Barf U banaaye (chaudi), paani V banaaye (patli gehri).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Lithosphere kisse milkar bani hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Crust + upari mantle — Yahi parat plates mein bati hai.

  2. Q2. Plates ki gati ka mukhya kaaran kya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Mantle ki convection currents — Garam padarth upar, thanda neeche — chakra.

  3. Q3. Himalaya kis prakaar ki plate seema se bana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Convergent — Indian aur Eurasian plates ki takkar.

  4. Q4. San Andreas Fault kis seema ka udaharan hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Transform — Plates bagal se ragadti hain.

  5. Q5. India ka ekmaatra mud volcano kahan hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Baratang — Andaman-Nicobar ke Baratang dweep mein.

  6. Q6. Paudhon ki jadon se chattaan ka toot-na kaunsa weathering hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Biological — Jeevon dwaara — biological.

  7. Q7. Oxbow lake nadi ke kis landform se banti hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Meander — Mod kat kar alag ho jaata hai.

  8. Q8. Glacier dwaara chhode gaye patthar-mitti ke dher ko kya kehte hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Moraine — Lateral, terminal, medial moraine.

  9. Q9. Gufa ki chhat se latakne wala dhaancha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Stalactite — Stalagmite zameen se upar uthta hai.
