Early Humans and Beginning of Civilisation

Africa se nikle pehle insaan, Stone Age ke 3 daur, Neolithic kranti, Mehrgarh se Harappa tak, aur Mesopotamia-Egypt-China ki kaansa yug sabhyataayein.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Likhaavat se pehle ka itihaas

Maanav itihaas ka 99% se zyada hissa (~30 lakh saal pehle se 5,000 saal pehle tak) likhaavat se pehle ka hai! Uski jaankaari sirf auzaaron, haddiyon aur cheezon (artefacts) se — isliye tareekhein andaaze se. Likhaavat ke baad (pichhle 5,000 saal, <1%) — naam, ghatnaayein, raajya-tilak, yuddh ki tareekhein bhi milti hain. Sabse purani lipiyon mein Harappan (Sindhu lipi) — chitron wali (pictographic), par aaj tak padhi nahi gayi; Mesopotamia ki cuneiform aur Egypt ki hieroglyphic padh li gayin. ~400 BCE se Brahmi — Ashoka ne vyavasthit roop diya (Sanchi stupa ki railing par).

Hamare poorvaj — Africa se poori duniya tak

Biological evolution: Australopithecines (australis = dakshini, pithecus = primate) se dheere-dheere aaj ke Homo sapiens. Cultural evolution: badalte mausam mein auzaar aur takneek se jeena — Quaternary period (pichhle 26 lakh saal). Kram: Homo habilis ('handy man' — Olduvai Gorge, Tanzania-Kenya; chopper auzaar) → Homo erectus (~20 lakh saal pehle; seedha chalne wala; handaxe, cleaver; Africa se bahar jaane wala pehla) → Neanderthal (Europe-SW Asia, ~40,000 saal pehle tak) → Homo sapiens (Africa mein ~3 lakh saal pehle; ~1,25,000 saal pehle bahar nikle; aaj ki ekmaatra maanav prajaati). ~33 lakh saal pehle pehle pathar ke auzaar — 'insaani vyavhaar' ki shuruaat; isliye hum 'hominins' = auzaar banaane wale. Auzaar = 'extra-corporal limbs' (shareer ke bahar ke haath-pair!).

Archaeologist kaise jaante hain?

Khudai mein auzaar, haddiyan, fossils (zameen mein dab kar laakhon saal mein pathar bane avshesh) milte hain. Archaeologist khud waise auzaar banaa kar aur chalaa kar dekhte hain ki purane log kaise jeete the — jaise jasoos saboot jodta hai!

Stone Age ke daur — Palaeo, Meso, Neo

Palaeolithic (palaeo = purana, lithic = pathar) — shikaar-sangrah, saade pathar ke auzaar. India mein sabse purani basti ~20 lakh saal: Attirampakkam (TN) ~15–17 lakh saal, Isampur (Karnataka) ~12 lakh saal — handaxe, cleaver, scraper, chopper (quartzite, chuna pathar se); maans kaatna, kand khodna, khaal saaf karna, haddi tod kar majja (marrow) nikaalna. Baad mein chhote auzaar — scraper, borer, nukile points (bhaale ke sire); phir teer-kamaan, blade, microblade; gufa chitra, shareer rangna, pathar-haddi-shankh ke moti; burin se shuturmurg ke ande par nakkaashi. Homo sapiens 50,000–12,000 saal pehle Australia aur America tak faile. Mesolithic (~12,000 saal pehle, garam mausam) — nanhe microlith auzaar, machhli mukhya bhojan, itihaas ka pehla aabadi visfot; Bhimbetka (MP) — saikdon chitrit chattaan aashray, World Heritage.

Neolithic Revolution — kheti ki kranti

Mausam aur bhojan ko pehchaan kar insaan ne jaanwar aur paudhe paalna shuru kiya — bhojan khoje nahi, ugaaye! Isliye ise 'badlaav' nahi, 'kranti (revolution)' kehte hain. Kisaan bhojan banaane-process karne ke auzaar, chikne (polished) pathar ke auzaar, alag-alag mitti ke bartan, aur pehle gaon banaaye — shehron ki neev. Ye har jagah ek saath nahi hua: West Asia (gehu-jau), Africa (bajra), India (millets; Ganga maidaan mein chaawal), China (millets, Yangtze mein chaawal). Kram: Palaeolithic → Mesolithic → Neolithic → Chalcolithic (taamba + pathar) → Bronze Age (taamba + tin) → Iron Age.

Mehrgarh — India ka pehla kisaan gaon

Mehrgarh (Bolan nadi, aaj Pakistan) — sabse purana Neolithic sthal aur pehla kheti wala gaon, ~7000 BCE. Dhoop mein sukhaayi eenton ke ghar, anaaj godaam, kabrein, lapis lazuli, carnelian, shankh ke gehne; gehu-jau, bhed-bakri aur zebu (kubad wala desi bail). ~4000 BCE tak taamba — Chalcolithic. 2500 BCE tak upmahadweep ke zyaadatar hisse mein Neolithic kisaan samudaay; Sanganakallu (granite pahaadi par, ~2000 BCE).

Early Harappan se Harappa tak

Taambe ke auzaar + Sindhu aur Ghaggar-Sarasvati ke upjaau maidaan → zyada upaj aur samriddhi. Bhirrana, Kunal mein pre-Harappan daur shaayad 7000–5500 BCE tak! Bartan, moti, shankh ki choodiyan, terracotta, taamba — sab mein nirantarta; chaardeewaari, mohar, lipi ki shuruaat bhi shaayad yahin — isliye 'Early Harappan'. Kram: Neolithic 7000 → Chalcolithic 4500 → Bronze 3300 → Mature Harappan 2600–1900 BCE → Late 1300 BCE. Kalibangan ke khet mein aade-tirchhe kund — do fasal, jaise aaj Rabi-Kharif — 5,000 saal purani parampara! Baat (weights) binary (1, 2, 4, 8, 16…) chhoti ikaiyon ke liye, aur 10 ke guna badi ke liye — vyapaar ke liye ek maanak.

Mesopotamia — 'nadiyon ke beech ki dharti'

Chaar badi kaansa yug sabhyataayein nadi maidaanon mein: Harappa (Sindhu-Sarasvati), Mesopotamia (Euphrates-Tigris), Egypt (Nile), China (Huang He). Mesopotamia (Greek: 'beech ki dharti') — aaj Iraq, Kuwait, Turkey-Iran ke hisse; Fertile Crescent. Chaar daur: Sumerian (Ur; pehle baandh-nahar, kachchi-pakki eent; ziggurat — seedhidaar piramid mandir jiske chaaron or shehar basa; ~3200 BCE pehli likhaavat cuneiform — geeli mitti par keel jaise nishaan, Latin cuneus = keel; pahiya gaadi, paal naav; 60 ki ginti → 60 minute ka ghanta, 360° ka gola!), Akkadian (2334 BCE; duniya ka pehla vanshaanugat samrajya; raja Sargon ke lekhon mein Dilmun (Bahrain), Magan (Oman), Meluhha (= Harappa!) se vyapaar), Assyrian (2154 BCE, Assur), Babylonian (Hammurabi, 1792 BCE — Code of Hammurabi, niyamon ka sangrah; Hittites ke hamlon se patan). Usi samay Harappa ke gabarband (check dam), Dholavira ke jalaashay aur Lothal ka dockyard! Epic of Gilgamesh — sabse purani kahaaniyon mein.

Egypt — 'Kemet', kaali mitti ki dharti

Egyptian apne desh ko Kemet ('kaali') kehte — Nile ki baadh se bichhi kaali upjaau mitti. Itihaas papyrus (papyrus paudhe ki patti dabaa kar bana kaagaz) se; Papyrus Ebers mein 700+ ilaaj; 2000 BCE ki library mein 'Sindbad' aur Cinderella ke sabse purane roop! Rosetta Stone (1799, Pierre Bouchard) par teen lipiyan — ek Greek; 1822 mein Champollion ne hieroglyphs padhe. Calendar: 3 ritu × 4 mahine; 365 din (12×30 + 5), Sirius taare par. Nile ka paani khaaiyon-jalaashayon mein — saamuhik mehnat se sthaaniya sarkar. Pharaoh raja; mastaba ek ke upar ek → pyramid (Saqqara ka step pyramid); ka (aatma ka saathi) ke liye shav ka mummification. Samaaj ka pyramid: Pharaoh → afsar-pujaari → zameendaar-kaarigar-vyapaari → gulam. Mahilaayein sampatti rakh sakti; Cleopatra 18 ki umar mein rani.

China — Pili nadi ki sabhyata

Huang He (Yellow River) aur Yangtze; Neolithic ~7000 BCE; kaansa ~2000 BCE; shehar ~1600 BCE. Vansh: Shang (1600–1046 BCE), Zhou (1046–256 BCE; raja-pujaari, par praja khush na ho to hataaye ja sakte!; afsar teerandaazi, ghudsawari, ganit, likhaavat, sangeet ki pariksha se chune), Qin (221–206 BCE; desh ko joda; naam 'China' shaayad isi se), Han. Pehli jaankaari oracle bones — haddi/kachhue ke khol garam karke daraaron se bhavishya. Lipi logographic — chinh poore shabd (insaan, ped). Jade ki cheezein (bajne wali jade machhli!), kaansa ke bartan. Great Wall — 680 BCE se 17th sadi tak. Resham Neolithic se; Han kaal mein itna vyapaar ki raasta = Silk Route. Pehli kaagazi mudra aur pariksha se civil service — China ki den. Seekh: sabhi sabhyataayein alag-alag nadi maidaanon mein bani, par sabne kheti, shehar, likhaavat aur prashasan viksit kiye.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Likhaavat se pehle >99% itihaas — sirf archaeological saboot. Harappan lipi (pictographic) — undeciphered; cuneiform, hieroglyphic padhi gayin.
  • Brahmi ~400 BCE se; Ashoka ne formalise kiya.
  • Biological evolution: Australopithecines → Homo sapiens. Quaternary period = pichhle 26 lakh saal.
  • Homo habilis (Olduvai Gorge) → Homo erectus (~20 lakh saal; handaxe, cleaver; pehla Africa se bahar) → Neanderthal (~40,000 saal pehle tak) → Homo sapiens (~3 lakh saal, Africa; ~1,25,000 saal pehle bahar).
  • ~33 lakh saal pehle pehle pathar auzaar; tools = 'extra-corporal limbs'.
  • Periods: Palaeolithic → Mesolithic → Neolithic → Chalcolithic → Bronze → Iron.
  • India: ~20 lakh saal; Attirampakkam (TN, 15–17 lakh), Isampur (Karnataka, 12 lakh).
  • Upper Palaeolithic: teer-kamaan, blade, moti, gufa chitra, burin.
  • Mesolithic ~12,000 saal pehle: microliths, machhli, pehla aabadi visfot; Bhimbetka (MP).
  • Neolithic Revolution: domestication, pottery, gaon. Alag-alag jagah alag samay.
  • Mehrgarh (Bolan) ~7000 BCE; gehu-jau, zebu; ~4000 BCE taamba (Chalcolithic). Sanganakallu ~2000 BCE.
  • Early Harappan: Bhirrana, Kunal (7000–5500 BCE?); Mature 2600–1900 BCE. Kalibangan — double cropping. Binary weights.
  • Mesopotamia: Sumerian (ziggurat, cuneiform ~3200 BCE, pahiya, base-60), Akkadian (2334 BCE, Sargon; Dilmun, Magan, Meluhha), Assyrian (2154 BCE), Babylonian (Hammurabi 1792 BCE, Code).
  • Harappa: gabarband, Dholavira tanks, Lothal dockyard.
  • Egypt: Kemet; papyrus (Ebers); Rosetta Stone 1799, Champollion 1822; Sirius calendar 365 din; Pharaoh, mastaba → pyramid (Saqqara); mummification (ka); Sed festival; Cleopatra.
  • China: Huang He, Yangtze; Shang, Zhou, Qin (naam China), Han; oracle bones; logographic; jade; Great Wall (680 BCE–17th c.); Silk Route; paper currency, exam-based civil service.

Yaad karne ki trick

Poorvaj ka kram = "Haath, Seedha, Nean, Samajh"

Habilis (handy), Erectus (seedha), Neanderthal, Sapiens (samajhdaar).

Yug ka kram = "Pa-Me-Ne-Cha-Bro-I"

Palaeo, Meso, Neo, Chalco, Bronze, Iron — 'Paa Me Ne Chai Bro I'!

Mesopotamia ke 4 = "SAAB"

Sumerian, Akkadian, Assyrian, Babylonian.

Chaar nadi, chaar sabhyata

Sindhu-Sarasvati — Harappa; Euphrates-Tigris — Mesopotamia; Nile — Egypt; Huang He — China.

China ke vansh = "Shan-Zo-Qi-Han"

Shang, Zhou, Qin, Han — 'Shaan se Zor Ki Haan'.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Africa se bahar jaane wala pehla hominin kaun tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Homo erectus — ~20 lakh saal pehle, handaxe aur cleaver ke saath.

  2. Q2. India ke sabse purane Palaeolithic sthalon mein kaunsa hai (~15–17 lakh saal)?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Attirampakkam — Tamil Nadu mein.

  3. Q3. Bhimbetka kis liye prasiddh hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Chitrit chattaan aashray — Madhya Pradesh — World Heritage Site.

  4. Q4. Upmahadweep ka sabse purana Neolithic kheti gaon?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Mehrgarh — Bolan nadi par, ~7000 BCE.

  5. Q5. Taamba aur pathar dono ke istemal wala yug?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Chalcolithic — Chalco = taamba.

  6. Q6. Mesopotamian lekhon mein 'Meluhha' kise kaha gaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Sindhu-Sarasvati sabhyata — Dilmun = Bahrain, Magan = Oman.

  7. Q7. Cuneiform lipi kisne banaayi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Sumerians — ~3200 BCE, mitti ki takhtiyon par.

  8. Q8. Code of Hammurabi kis sabhyata se juda hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Babylonian — 1792 BCE se Hammurabi ka raaj.

  9. Q9. Egyptian hieroglyphs ko padhne mein kisne madad ki?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Rosetta Stone — Usme Greek bhi likha tha; Champollion ne 1822 mein padha.

  10. Q10. 'China' naam shaayad kis vansh se aaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Qin — Qin (Ch'in) ne desh ko ek kiya.
