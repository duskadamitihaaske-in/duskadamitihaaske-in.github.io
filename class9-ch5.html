State and Society up to 1000 CE

Vedic jana se mahajanapada aur samrajya tak — sabha-samiti, raja ke kartavya, prashasan, dharma, varna-jati, mahilaayein, shiksha aur arthvyavastha.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Society aur State — farak kya?

Society (samaaj) = ek jagah, sanskriti aur 'apnapan' saajha karne wale logon ke rishton ka jaal — parivaar, vivaah jaisi sansthaayein; ye mukhyatah reeti-riwaaj se chalta hai. State (raajya) = niyam-kaanoon par chalne wali sangathit raajnaitik vyavastha — shaasak aur praja ke haq-kartavya, shaasan ke tareeke, kaanoon lagu karne wali sansthaayein. Is daur ki khaas baat: 2nd millennium BCE se Rigveda jaise likhit/maukhik granth milte hain, jo archaeology ke saath milkar poori tasveer dikhaate hain.

Chaar Veda aur unke chaar hisse

Rigveda — sabse purana; 1,028 sukta; devtaaon ki stuti aur srishti, janm-mrityu jaise gehre vichaar. Yajurveda — yajna ki vidhi, gadya mein vyakhya. Samaveda — Rigveda ke mantra gaane ke liye sajaaye; Bharatiya sangeet ke saat swar ki neev! Atharvaveda — buraai door karne aur shaaririk-maansik rogon ke ilaaj se jude mantra. Har Veda ke 4 hisse: Samhita (mantra), Brahmana (anushthaan ki gadya vyakhya), Aranyaka (aranya = jungle; vanvaasi rishiyon ka chintan), Upanishad (Atman aur Brahman). Dhyaan: Brahman = vichaar, Brahmana = granth, Brahmin = varna! Rigveda ki rachna Sapta-Sindhu (Indus, uski 5 sahaayak, Sarasvati) mein; tithi par bahas (5th se 2nd millennium BCE).

Vedic raajneeti — raja bhi, sabha bhi

Samaaj jana (kul) mein — Rigveda mein ~30; paanch milkar Panchajana: Yadu, Turvasha, Puru, Anu, Druhyu. 'Bharata' naam bhi Bharata jana se. Raja = kul ka mukhiya — yuddh mein netritva, raksha. Teen sabhaayein: Sabha — chhoti, chune hue vishisht log, neeti-raajneeti; Samiti — badi, janta ki sabha, yuddh-raajneeti par charcha; Vidhata — lok-jamaavda, nyay ka kaam. Aaj jaise sansad PM par lagaam rakhti hai, waise hi tab sabhaayein raja par — logon ki bhaagidaari ka vichaar bahut purana hai!

Lohe ne badla khel — janapada se mahajanapada

1st millennium BCE mein Ganga kshetra mein bastiyan badhin — lohe ke auzaar, zyada anaaj, PGW (Painted Grey Ware) bartan; madhya Ganga mein chamakdaar kaale NBPW (Northern Black Polished Ware). Phir Janapada ('jahan jana ne pair rakha') — rishte ke saath zameen ki pehchaan (~1000–600 BCE). ~600 BCE–300 CE mein 16 Mahajanapada — Kamboja, Gandhara, Matsya, Kuru, Shurasena, Panchala, Vatsa, Kosala, Kashi, Malla, Vrijji, Magadha, Anga, Chedi, Avanti, Ashmaka. Magadha (Bihar) sabse taaqatwar → Maurya samrajya. Do tarah: rajya (raja) aur gana/sangha (ganrajya). Deccan mein Maurya ke baad Satavahana (~500 saal). Dakshin mein Chola (baagh chinh), Chera/Keralaputra (dhanush), Pandya (machhli), Satiyaputra — 'Tamilakam ke Vendar'; jaankaari Sangam sahitya (300 BCE–300 CE) se.

Raja ke kartavya aur 'chakravarti' ka sapna

Arthashastra: 'raja khud mehnati ho, tabhi afsar mehnat karte hain'. Yajurveda ki rajyabhishek shapath: taaqatwar-kamzor ka nishpaksh nyay, aapdaaon se raksha, praja ka bhala. Mahabharata ka Shanti Parva — naitik aacharan. Raja khaandaani hota, par chuna ya hataaya jaane ke bhi udaharan — satta poori nahi thi. Poore upmahadweep ki pehchaan: Jambudvipa, Bharatavarsha, Ashvamedha-Rajasuya yajna, Prithivi, chakravarti kshetra. Ashoka: meri koshishon se 'Jambudvipa' mein adhyaatmik badlaav. Arthashastra: Prithivi = Himalaya se samundar tak = chakravarti (sarvochch samraat) ka kshetra. Chera raja Nedunjeral Adan ne Himalaya tak jeet ka daava kiya; Chola Rajendra I — 'Gangaikonda'.

Saptanga aur mantri-parishad

Kautilya ka raajya ek jeevit shareer — 7 ang (Saptanga): swami (raja), amatya (mantri-afsar), janapada (zameen + log), durga (qile wale shehar), kosha (khazaana), danda (sena, kaanoon), mitra (dost raajya). 'Ek pahiya gaadi nahi chalaata' — raja akela nahi, mantri-parishad (khazaanchi, mukhya tax adhikaari, kaanooni salaahkaar, senapati) ke saath; Ashoka ke lekh mein raja ki anupasthiti mein parishad ke faisle! Junagadh shilalekh (Girnar) — ek hi chattaan par teen raajvansh: Ashoka (Prakrit, dhamma), Rudradaman I (~150 CE, Sanskrit; Sudarshana jheel ki marammat), Skandagupta (5th sadi; phir marammat) — ~700 saal ka itihaas ek pathar par!

Prashasan — praant se gaon tak

Satavahana: ahara (prabhaag) → gaon (gramika mukhiya). District afsar (pradeshika) bankers, karvaan netaon, kaarigaron, lekhakon se salaah lete. 300–800 CE vikendrikaran: praant — uttar mein bhukti, dakshin mein mandalam; prabhaag — vishaya/bhoga vs kottam/valanadu; zila — adhishthana/pattana vs nadu; tehsil jaise — vithi vs pattala/kurram; sabse neeche gaon. Gupta: naya mantri sandhivigrahika ('yuddh aur shaanti'), kumaramatya; Damodarpur taamrapatra — zila daftar mein 5: mukhya adhikaari, mukhya banker, mukhya karvaan vyapaari, mukhya kaarigar, lagaan pramukh! Pallava — kar-mukt Brahmadeya gaon; gaon sabha ki samitiyan variyam. Chalukya (Badami) — agraharam (Aihole, Badami shiksha kendra). Kannauj ke liye tripartite struggle (Pratihara, Pala, Rashtrakuta). Chola: mandalam → valanadu → nadu → ur. Uttaramerur (Parantaka I, 10th sadi) — kudavolai (matdaan ghada): taad patron par naam, bachcha nikaale; ummeedwar ki 'imaandaar kamaai' zaroori.

Dharma — 'jo sabko dhaaran kare'

Kaanoon aur jeevan ki neev naitikta. Samatva — sab shareer ek hi padarth se, sab chetna ek param chetna ka roop; acchha raja wo jo 'sab jeevon ke kalyaan' ke liye kaam kare. Rita — Rigveda mein brahmaand ki vyavastha, prakriti aur samaaj ka santulan. Dharma = dharm nahi, balki kartavya, nyay, sadaachaar; shabd 'dhri' (dhaaran karna) se — wahi jad 'dharati' ki! Bhishma: 'jo jeevon ko dhaaran kare, wahi dharma'; 'lohe ka dharma doobna, lakdi ka tairna'. Pali mein dhamma — Ashoka ne naitik aacharan, ahinsa, karuna failaayi. Kamandaka ka Nitisara, Banabhatta ki Kadambari.

Varna, jati aur badalte pesha

Shuru mein pehchaan kai baaton se — nasl, ilaaka, gaon, gotra, bhasha, aur khaas kar pesha; pesha lachila tha. Rigveda 9.112.3: 'main kavi, pita vaidya, maa chakki chalaane wali' — ek hi parivaar mein alag pesha! Chaar varna ka pehla zikr Purushasukta (Rigveda Book 10). Varna kaam par aadharit (functional) — gyaan sabse oopar, phir raajnaitik shakti, phir dhan. Sutta Nipata: 'koi janm se brahmin nahi, karm se hota hai'. Dheere-dheere lachak ghati aur jati bani (antar-vivaah, endogamy, ilaaka) — varna 4 hi, jati anginat. Social mobility: Nanda, Maurya, Shunga, Satavahana, Gupta — alag-alag prishthbhoomi ke raja; Mandsaur lekh (473 CE) — Gujarat se aaye resham bunkar teerandaazi aur jyotish bhi jaante the! Sangam ka Tolkappiyam — Arasar (raja), Vanigar (vyapaari), Velar (kisaan), Antanar.

Parivaar, ashrama aur purushartha

Seedhi: kula (parivaar) → grama → vish (vishpati) → jana (raja). Gotra — ek rishi se judi pitravanshi pehchaan. Jeevan ke 4 ashrama: brahmacharya (vidyarthi), grihastha, vanaprastha, sanyasa; aur 16 sanskaar. Chaar purushartha: dharma, artha, kama, moksha — dhan (artha) achchha hai, agar dharma ke raaste kamaaya jaaye.

Mahilaayein — Vedic rishika se Chola raani tak

Vedic kaal mein mahilaayein padhti, kuch anushthaan karti, rath daud aur sabha mein jaati. Rigveda ke sukta rishikaaon ke: Apala, Vishvavara, Ghosha, Lopamudra; devi Usha, Aditi. Manusmriti: 'yatra naryastu pujyante ramante tatra devatah'. Sthiti samay ke saath ghati-badhi. Prabhavati Gupta (regent, apne naam se zameen daan), votive lekhon mein daan-daatri mahilaayein. Sangam: kheti, pashupaalan, kataai, machhli, namak; kaviyitri Avvaiyar, kumhaarin kavi Vennikuyattiyar. Chola raani Sembiyan Mahadevi — mandir nirmaan.

Dharm, bhakti aur shiksha

Vedic devta prakriti se jude — suraj, barish, agni, dharti, usha; aaj bhi Chhath, Makar Sankranti par surya pooja. Prakriti = pavitra, sirf sansaadhan nahi. Madhya 1st millennium BCE mein sanyasi — parivrajaka, bhikshu, shramana; Buddha, Mahavira. Phir Vishnu, Shiv, Shakti jaise ishtadev → Bhakti — bina jatil reeti, sabke liye; 6th sadi Tamil kshetra mein 12 Alvar (Vishnu) aur 63 Nayanar (Shiv). Shiksha: satya, dhairya, vinamrata, indriya sanyam; Veda, vyakaran, tark, ganit, chikitsa, khagol + sangeet, nritya, chitrakala, teerandaazi, yoga. Guru-shishya parampara — gurukul. Kendra: Takshashila, Nalanda (427 CE), Vallabhi (480 CE), Vikramashila, Kanchipuram, Sharada Peeth… Granth: Panini ki Ashtadhyayi, Pingala ka Chhandashastra, Charaka, Sushruta Samhita, Kalidasa, Tirukkural, Silappadikaram, Manimekalai.

Arthvyavastha — kheti, sinchaai, vyapaar, guild

Maurya: zameen ka tax aam taur par 1/6; chaaragaah saajhe; kuch jungle kaanoon se surakshit. Milindapanho — kheti ke 8 charan; Deccan ki kaali mitti → kapaas; Amarakosha mein jungle, fasal, khaad ka adhyaay; Chera — kathal, kaali mirch, haldi. Sinchaai: Chandragupta ke governor Pushyagupta ne Sudarshana jheel par baandh banaaya; Karikala ka Kallanai aaj bhi chalu. Raaste: Uttarapatha, Dakshinapatha; bandargah: Muziris, Kaveripattinam, Arikamedu, Masulipatnam; Rome tak vyapaar. Guilds (shreni) — Jatakon mein 18 prakaar; quality aur daam tay, guild court, bank jaise kaam! Nashik lekh — Ushavadata ne bunkar guild mein 3,000 karshapana jama kiye, byaaj se vihar chale. Sanchi ke darwaaze par Vidisha ke haathi-daant kaarigaron ka lekh — wahi moortikaar the!

Yaad karne ki trick

Panchajana = "YaTu PAD"

Yadu, Turvasha, Puru, Anu, Druhyu.

Veda ke 4 hisse = "SaBA-U" (Sabha-U!)

Samhita, Brahmana, Aranyaka, Upanishad.

Vedic sabhaayein = "Sab Sam Vi"

Sabha (chhoti, elite), Samiti (badi, janta), Vidhata (nyay).

Junagadh = "A-R-S" — 'ek pathar, teen raja'

Ashoka, Rudradaman, Skandagupta.

Purushartha = "DAKM" — 'Dhaak Mein'

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Rigveda mein kitne sukta hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 1,028 — 1,028 sukta.

  2. Q2. Bharatiya sangeet ke saat swaron ki neev kis Veda ko maana jaata hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Samaveda — Samaveda ke mantra gaane ke liye sajaaye gaye.

  3. Q3. Panchajana mein kaun shaamil NAHI hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Bharata — Paanch: Yadu, Turvasha, Puru, Anu, Druhyu.

  4. Q4. Vedic kaal ki kaunsi sabha mukhyatah nyay ka kaam karti thi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Vidhata — Vidhata — lok jamaavda, nyayik kaam.

  5. Q5. Junagadh shilalekh par kin teen shaasakon ke lekh hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Ashoka, Rudradaman I, Skandagupta — ~700 saal ka itihaas ek chattaan par.

  6. Q6. Gupta kaal mein naya 'yuddh aur shaanti' mantri kya kehlaata tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Sandhivigrahika — Sandhi (shaanti) + vigraha (yuddh).

  7. Q7. Pallava kaal ke kar-mukt gaon kya kehlaate the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Brahmadeya — Chalukyon mein agraharam.

  8. Q8. Chaar varnon ka sabse pehla zikr kahan milta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Purushasukta (Rigveda) — Rigveda ke 10th mandal mein.

  9. Q9. Maurya kaal mein zameen ka saamaanya tax kitna tha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1/6 — Upaj ka chhatha hissa.

  10. Q10. Nashik guha lekh se guilds ke baare mein kya pata chalta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Wo bank ki tarah jama par byaaj dete the — Ushavadata ne bunkar guild mein paisa jama kiya.
