Theme P · Political Science

Democracy

Loktantra ki jaden Vedic sabha-samiti tak; iske siddhant, prakaar, duniya ke model, India ka 'jeevit loktantra', chunautiyan aur hamari zimmedaari.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Democracy matlab kya?

Democracy (loktantra) = aisi sarkar jismein taaqat ka asli srot janta hai. Log free aur fair elections se apne pratinidhi chunte hain. Iski neev: aazaadi, samaanta, nyay, adhikaar aur kartavya. India duniya ka sabse bada loktantra hai — 140 crore se zyada aabaadi aur 96.8 crore voters (2024). Hamara loktantra Samvidhan se chalta hai — 26 Nov 1949 ko apnaaya, 26 Jan 1950 se laagu.

Jaden bahut purani hain

Loktantra ka vichaar India mein achaanak nahi aaya. Vedic kaal mein Sabha, Samiti, Vidhata — milkar faisle. Gana/Sangha (ganrajya) bhi the. Raja akela nahi — sabha, mantri aur afsaron se salaah leta tha. Rigveda ka Aikyamatya Sukta kehta hai: 'tumhari salaah ek ho, sabha ek ho, man ek ho' — yani milkar sochna, charcha aur ekta. Buddha ke Bauddha Sangha mein bahas hoti, neta chuna jaata aur voting se faisle hote.

Gulaami se Samvidhan tak

Videshi hamlon aur phir British raj ne logon ki bhaagidaari chheen li. Par aazaadi ki ladaai ne loktantrik vichaar phir se jagaaye. 1946 mein Constituent Assembly bani; 2 saal, 11 mahine, 18 din mein duniya ka sabse lamba likhit samvidhan taiyaar. Drafting Committee ke chairman — Dr. B.R. Ambedkar. Samvidhan ko patthar ki lakeer nahi, badalne layak rakha — Article 368 se amendment ho sakta hai, par mool mulya nahi tootte.

Siddhant 1–2: Janta raja hai, kaanoon sabse upar

Popular Sovereignty — sarkar ki taaqat janta se aati hai. 18 saal+ har nagrik ko secret ballot se vote ka haq = Universal Adult Franchise. Rule of Law — koi bhi kaanoon se upar nahi; sab ke liye barabar kaanoon. Jhagde adaalat mein 'procedure established by law' se suljhte hain, dande ya pehchaan se nahi. Article 21: kisi ko bhi jeevan ya aazaadi se kaanooni prakriya ke bina vanchit nahi kiya ja sakta. Do shabd yaad rakho: Equality before law (sab barabar) aur Equal protection of law (ek jaisi sthiti walon se ek jaisa vyavhaar).

Siddhant 3: Chhe Fundamental Rights

1) Samaanta (Art 14–18) 2) Swatantrata (19–22) 3) Shoshan ke viruddh (23–24) 4) Dharm ki swatantrata (25–28) 5) Sanskritik aur shaikshik (29–30) 6) Samvaidhanik upchaar (Art 32). Haq toota to Article 32 (Supreme Court) ya 226 (High Court) mein ja sakte ho. Ye adaalat mein laagu hote hain, par reasonable restrictions ke saath. Right to Education (21A) 2009 mein jodha — 6–14 saal ke bachchon ko muft aur zaroori shiksha.

Siddhant 4: Shaktiyon ka bantwara

Teen ang: Legislature kaanoon banaati, Executive laagu karti, Judiciary vyakhya karti. Isse taaqat ek jagah jama nahi hoti — checks and balances. Sansad samvidhan badal sakti hai, par adaalat dekhti hai ki wo samvidhan ki aatma ke khilaaf to nahi. Galat kaanoon ko adaalat unconstitutional ghoshit kar sakti hai. Judiciary nishpaksh aur swatantra hai; PIL (Public Interest Litigation) se aam aadmi ke liye nyay aasaan hua. Socho — agar ek hi ang kaanoon banaaye, laagu kare aur faisla bhi de, to? Taanashahi!

Siddhant 5–7: Jawabdehi, kai parties, kamzor ki raksha

Accountability & Transparency — sarkar janta ko jawab deti hai; elections, bahas, civil society se jaanch. RTI Act 2005 se nagrik sarkari jaankaari maang sakte hain. Sansad ka Question Hour (pehla ghanta) — sawaal poochh kar sarkar par nazar. Multi-Party System — kai parties; jo party ya gathbandhan 50% se zyada seats laaye, sarkar banaaye; sabse bada virodhi samooh = opposition. Niyam: Representation of the People Act, 1951. India mein 2,800+ registered parties! Kamzor varg ki raksha — Article 46: SC/ST aur kamzor vargon ke shiksha-aarthik hit ka khaas dhyaan.

Kaun si sanstha kaunsa mulya bachaati hai

Kaanoon banana → Sansad/Vidhan Sabha/local bodies (pratinidhitva, bahas, asahmati). Chunaav → Election Commission (bhaagidaari, samaanta). Nyay → Courts (rule of law). Charcha → Media, civil society (abhivyakti ki aazaadi). Jawabdehi → CAG, CIC, Lokpal, CVC. Vikendrikaran → Gram/shehri local bodies. Federalism → Centre-State mein taaqat ka bantwara (likhit samvidhan se). Media ko 'loktantra ka chautha stambh' kehte hain — janta ki awaaz.

Democracy ke prakaar

Direct Democracy — nagrik khud zyaadatar faisle lete hain; bade desh mein mushkil; misaal Switzerland. Representative (indirect) — log pratinidhi chunte hain, samay-samay par chunaav; misaal India. Representative ke do roop: Parliamentary — Executive (PM aur mantri) legislature ka hissa aur usi ko jawabdeh; log executive ko seedha nahi chunte — India, Canada. Presidential — Executive legislature se alag; President ko janta chunti, wahi janta ko jawabdeh — USA. School misaal: sab bachche ek jagah vote karein = direct; har class pratinidhi bheje = representative; sirf chune hue ummeedwar ladein aur virodh dabaaya jaaye = asli loktantra nahi!

Duniya ke model ek nazar mein

India — parliamentary; President (Head of State), PM (Head of Government); likhit samvidhan, federalism. Canada — parliamentary; Governor-General (Monarch ki taraf se). UK — constitutional monarchy; Monarch Head of State, PM sarkar ka mukhiya; unlikhit samvidhan, sansad sarvochch. Switzerland — direct democracy; Federal Council ke paas executive. USA — presidential; President hi sarkar ka mukhiya; do badi parties. Sovereignty — Latin superanus = 'sarvochch'.

India ka 'jeevit loktantra' — numbers dekho

Ek MP lagbhag 25 lakh logon ka pratinidhi (2019). Chunaav 22 scheduled bhaashaon mein; chunaav chinh taaki anpadh bhi khud vote de sake. 10 lakh+ polling stations — pahaad, jungle, registaan, dweep — kabhi ek voter ke liye bhi! Sabse khaas — itni vividhta (bhaasha, dharm, aarthik sthiti) ke log ek hi loktantrik dhaanche mein. Ye sirf sankhya nahi, logon ki lagaataar bhaagidaari hai.

Zameen se loktantra — Gram Sabha ki kahaaniyan

Teen star: Union, State, Local. North-East mein Autonomous District Councils (ADCs) — jan-jaatiya reeti-riwaaj bachaane ke liye kaanoon aur nyay ki shakti. Baaki rajyon ke tribal ilaakon mein PESA Act 1996 — Gram Sabha sabse badi faisla-sanstha. Jethipura (Gujarat) — 2019 mein Nanaji Deshmukh Rashtriya Gaurav Gram Sabha Puraskar; special Gram Sabha, Mahila Sabha, poora quorum (kam se kam haaziri). South Manubankul (Tripura) — 'women-friendly panchayat'. Loktantra sirf vote nahi — cooperatives, trade unions, NGOs mein bhi.

Mahilaayein aur vote

Britain mein mahilaaon ko poora vote 1928, USA mein 1920 — lambi ladaai ke baad. India mein 1950 se hi sabko barabar vote — bina andolan ke! Par vote ka haq barabar bhaagidaari ki guarantee nahi — samaajik soch rukaawat banti hai. Article 243(d) — Panchayat mein kam se kam 1/3 seats mahilaaon ki; 21 rajya + 2 UT ne 50% kar diya. Article 243(t) — Municipality mein 1/3; 17 rajya + 1 UT mein 50% (2023).

Chunautiyan aur Emergency ka sabak

Chunautiyan: anpadhta, fake news (social media), garibi, kshetravaad, laingik asamaanta, bhedbhaav, kaanoon ka kamzor amal. Sarkari sampatti todna, afwaah failaana, udaaseenta — sab loktantra ko kamzor karte hain. Emergency 1975–77: Indira Gandhi sarkar ne 'aantarik ashaanti' ke naam par laagu ki; zyaadatar Fundamental Rights nilambit, press par censorship, netaaon ki giraftaari. Jayaprakash Narayan (Lok Nayak) ke andolan ne Bihar-Gujarat mein chhatron ko joda. 1977 ke chunaav mein sattadhari dal haara — loktantra ki taaqat dikhi. (Art 352 = National, 356 = President's Rule, 360 = Financial Emergency.)

Tum aur loktantra

Achchha nagrik = jaankaar nagrik — akhbaar padho, news dekho, internet zimmedaari se. NSS, NCC, Bharat Scouts & Guides se seva aur teamwork seekho. Samvidhan samjho, Fundamental Duties nibhaao, media literate bano (fake news pehchaano), school-mohalle mein netritva lo. Yaad rakho: loktantra ki asli taaqat uske nagrik hain.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Democracy = taaqat ka srot janta; neev — aazaadi, samaanta, nyay, adhikaar, kartavya. India: 96.8 crore voters (2024).
  • Samvidhan apnaaya 26 Nov 1949, laagu 26 Jan 1950; Constituent Assembly 1946; 2 saal 11 mahine 18 din; Drafting chairman Dr. B.R. Ambedkar; amendment Art 368.
  • Prachin jaden: Sabha, Samiti, Vidhata; gana-sangha; Aikyamatya Sukta (Rigveda 10.191.3); Bauddha Sangha mein voting.
  • Siddhant: Popular Sovereignty (UAF, 18+, secret ballot) · Rule of Law (Art 21) · Fundamental Rights · Separation of Powers · Accountability (RTI 2005) · Multi-party (RPA 1951) · Kamzor varg (Art 46).
  • 6 Fundamental Rights: Equality 14–18, Freedom 19–22, Against Exploitation 23–24, Religion 25–28, Cultural-Educational 29–30, Constitutional Remedies 32 (+226 HC). RTE 21A (2009).
  • Sansthaayein: EC (chunaav), Courts (nyay), CAG/CIC/Lokpal/CVC (jawabdehi), local bodies (vikendrikaran). Media = 4th pillar.
  • Prakaar: Direct (Switzerland) vs Representative (India). Parliamentary (India, Canada, UK) vs Presidential (USA).
  • UK — unlikhit samvidhan, constitutional monarchy; Canada — Governor-General; Switzerland — Federal Council.
  • Facts: 1 MP ≈ 25 lakh log; 22 bhaasha; 10 lakh+ polling stations; 2,800+ parties.
  • Grassroots: ADCs (North-East), PESA 1996, Jethipura (Gujarat, NDRGGSP 2019), South Manubankul (Tripura, women-friendly).
  • Mahila vote: UK 1928, USA 1920, India 1950 se. Reservation: Panchayat 243(d), Municipality 243(t) — kam se kam 1/3.
  • Emergency 1975–77 — rights nilambit, press censorship; JP andolan; 1977 chunaav mein sarkar haari.

Yaad karne ki trick

6 Fundamental Rights = "SaaS DhaSaS"

Samanta, Swatantrata, Shoshan ke viruddh, Dharm, Sanskriti-shiksha, Samvaidhanik upchaar. Articles ka kram: 14 → 19 → 23 → 25 → 29 → 32.

Teen ang = "Banao, Chalao, Samjhao"

Legislature banaati, Executive chalaati, Judiciary samjhaati (vyakhya).

Emergency articles = "352-356-360: Desh, Rajya, Paisa"

352 National (desh), 356 President's Rule (rajya), 360 Financial (paisa).

Vote ka saal = "USA 20, UK 28, India 50"

Mahilaaon ko vote: USA 1920, UK 1928, India 1950 se sabko ek saath.

Parliamentary vs Presidential

Parliamentary = PM Parliament ke andar (India). Presidential = President alag, janta seedha chunti (USA).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Samvidhan kis din laagu hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 26 Jan 1950 — 26 Nov 1949 ko apnaaya, 26 Jan 1950 se laagu.

  2. Q2. Samvidhan mein amendment kis Article ke tahat hota hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Article 368 — Art 368 — samvidhan ko lachila banaata hai.

  3. Q3. 'Universal Adult Franchise' ka matlab hai —

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 18+ har nagrik ko vote ka haq — Bina bhedbhaav, 18 saal+ sabko.

  4. Q4. Right to Constitutional Remedies kis Article mein hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (D) 32 — Art 32 — Supreme Court; Art 226 — High Court.

  5. Q5. Direct democracy ki misaal kaunsa desh hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Switzerland — Switzerland mein nagrik seedhe faisle lete hain.

  6. Q6. Kis desh mein unlikhit samvidhan hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (D) United Kingdom — UK — unwritten constitution, constitutional monarchy.

  7. Q7. RTI Act kab laagu hua?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 2005 — Right to Information Act, 2005.

  8. Q8. Panchayat mein mahilaaon ke liye 1/3 aarakshan kis Article mein hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) 243(d) — 243(d) Panchayat; 243(t) Municipality.

  9. Q9. PESA Act (1996) mein sabse badi faisla-sanstha kaun hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Gram Sabha — Tribal ilaakon mein Gram Sabha ko shakti.

  10. Q10. Emergency 1975–77 ke khilaaf andolan ke 'Lok Nayak' kaun the?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Jayaprakash Narayan — JP ne Bihar-Gujarat mein chhatron ko joda.
