Part 1 · Ch07

Theme P · Political Science

Elections

Chunaav kyon zaroori, vote ko seat mein badalne ke tareeke (FPTP, PR, STV), chunaavi kaanoon, Delimitation, Election Commission, political parties aur chunautiyan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Chunaav kyon? — CR wali kahaani

Socho Class 8 mein Gurmat CR chuni gayi. Kya wo bina naye chunaav ke Class 9 mein bhi CR rahe? Nahi! Waise hi sarkar bhi apna samay (term) khatam hone ke baad bina janta se poochhe nahi chal sakti. Aur agar sirf ek hi party lade, to chunaav ka matlab hi kya? Loktantra ka sabse zaroori hissa = samay-samay par chunaav (periodic elections). Isse milti hai — Accountability (jawabdehi), Representation (pratinidhitva), Legitimacy (sarkar ki maanyata) aur Equality (har vote barabar).

Direct aur Indirect chunaav

Direct — nagrik seedha vote dekar chunte hain: Lok Sabha, Vidhan Sabha, Panchayat/Nagar Nigam — har 5 saal. Indirect — nagrik pratinidhi chunte hain, phir wo pratinidhi neta chunte hain: President, Vice President, Rajya Sabha. Ek mazedaar shabd: Psephology = chunaav ka vaigyanik adhyayan; Greek psephos = kankad (pebble) — prachin Greece mein kankad se vote padte the!

Vote ko seat mein kaise badlein? — teen tareeke

Teen kaalpanik desh socho. Haritbhumi — FPTP (First-Past-The-Post): har kshetra mein jise sabse zyada vote mile wo jeete, chaahe 50% se kam ho. Party A 40%, B 35%, C 25% → A jeeti. Ratnadweep — Majority system: jeetne ke liye 50% se zyada chahiye; na mile to top-2 mein doosra round. Round 2 mein Y ne 55% laakar jeeta. Swarnalok — PR (Proportional Representation): log party ko vote dete hain, seats vote ke anupaat mein — M 40%, N 35%, O 15%, P 10% → 100 mein se 40, 35, 15, 10 seats. Taaqat sab mein bati.

India kaunsa tareeka apnaata hai?

Samvidhan banaane walon ne FPTP chuna — Lok Sabha aur Vidhan Sabha ke liye. Rajya Sabha, President, Vice President — PR se. Chhe rajyon mein do sadan (bicameral) hain — Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh. Upari sadan = Vidhan Parishad; neeche = Vidhan Sabha. Vidhan Parishad ke sadasya Vidhan Sabha, local bodies, graduates aur teachers chunte hain; kuch ko Governor kala, vigyaan, samaaj seva, sahitya, cooperative se manonit karta hai. Iska chunaav STV (Single Transferable Vote) se.

STV — preference wala vote

Voter ummeedwaron ko 1, 2, 3… preference deta hai. Pehle first preference gine jaate hain. Jo quota paar kare, jeet gaya. Quota = (kul valid vote ÷ (bharne wali seats + 1)) + 1. Sabse kam vote wala bahar, uske vote un ballot par likhe second preference ko transfer. Ye tab tak chale jab tak saari seats bhar na jaayein. Yani tumhara vote bekaar nahi jaata — aage 'transfer' ho jaata hai!

Chunaav ke kaanoon

RPA 1950 (Representation of the People Act) — seats ka bantwara, kshetron ki seema (delimitation), voter list banana-sudhaarna, 18+ sabko bina bhedbhaav vote. RPA 1951 — baaki sab: nomination, prachaar, voting, chunaavi jhagde; saath hi bhrasht aacharan ki list — vote ke badle gift/vaada; dharm, jaati, nasl, samudaay, bhaasha ke naam par vote maangna; sarkari karmchaari (gazetted officer, judge, sena, police, excise) se madad lena. Teesra: Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952.

Delimitation — seemaayein kyon badalti hain

Kshetron ki seema pakki nahi — aabaadi badalne par badalti hai. Warna ek MP 5 lakh ka aur doosra 25 lakh ka pratinidhi hoga — barabari khatam! Delimitation = har rajya mein Lok Sabha-Vidhan Sabha ki seats ki sankhya aur seemaayein tay karna, taaki seat : aabaadi ka anupaat lagbhag barabar rahe. Article 82 ke tahat Delimitation Commission. Ab tak chaar — 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002.

Election Commission of India (ECI)

ECI ek swatantra, sthaayi, samvaidhanik sanstha — 25 January 1950 ko bani. Articles 324–329. Ye Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, Vidhan Sabha, Vidhan Parishad, President, Vice President ke chunaav karaati hai. Rajya star par Chief Electoral Officer; zile mein DM, SDM, Tahsildar ban jaate hain District Election Officer, Returning Officer, Electoral Registration Officer. 25 January = National Voters' Day — voters' pledge: bina dar, bina laalach, bina dharm-jaati ke prabhaav ke vote denge.

ECI ke kaam 1: Voter list aur taareekh

Electoral roll — ghar-ghar enumerator bhejkar yogya voters ka data; har polling station ke hisaab se list. Naam list mein nahi = vote nahi! SIR (Special Intensive Revision) — list update, jaanch, sudhaar: naye 18 saal walon ko jodna; mrityu, ghar badalna, duplicate, laapata — naam hataana; dava-aapatti ka mauka, phir final list. Taareekh — 5 saal poore ya sadan bhang hone par; dhyaan: mausam, fasal ka mausam, exams, tyohaar.

ECI ke kaam 2: Parties, chinh, nishpakshta

Sirf registered parties lad sakti hain; nirdaliya (independent) bhi lad sakte hain. ECI parties ko andar chunaav karaane ko kehta hai (inner-party democracy). Parties ki shreni: National, State, Registered-Unrecognised (RUPP). National-State parties ko chunaav chinh. Chinh ke jhagdon mein ECI quasi-judicial (adhh-nyayik) kaam karta hai. Sabko saath lena: 85+ buzurg aur 40%+ viklangta walon ke liye ghar se voting — 2024 mein pehli baar poore desh mein; Braille wali EVM, wheelchair, PVTG aur transgender voters ka dhyaan.

ECI ke digital auzaar

ETPBS — service voters (sena aadi) ke liye electronic postal ballot, kahin se bhi. Voter Helpline App — registration, sudhaar, jaankaari. Saksham — viklang voters ke liye. cVIGIL — Model Code of Conduct ka ullanghan dekho to turant report; flying squad location se pahunchta hai. Suvidha — ummeedwaron ke liye nomination forms aur rally/loudspeaker ki permission. ERONET — forms aur e-roll database. Sugam — chunaav ke liye gaadiyon ka hisaab. IEVP — videshi chunaav sansthaaon se sahyog; 28 EMBs + IFES, International IDEA, UN se MoU.

Political parties — loktantra ki reedh

Voter ummeedwaron, unki neeti aur vision ke baare mein zyaadatar parties se jaanta hai. Parties: janmat sangathit karti hain, mudde uthaati, manifesto banaati, chunaav ladti; jeetne wali sarkar banaati, haarne wali opposition banke jawabdehi tay karti. India mein multi-party system — desh ki bhaasha, sanskriti, dharm, kshetra ki vividhta ka aaina. 1967 — ek-party dabdabe ka ant, gathbandhan ka daur; 1977 — Janata Party ki pehli kendriya gathbandhan sarkar. Baad mein NDA (BJP ke netritva) aur UPA (INC; 2023 mein bhang hokar INDIA gathbandhan).

Dal-badal (Defection) aur kaanoon

Kisi party ke ticket par jeetkar party chhodna ya party ke nirdesh (whip) ke khilaaf vote dena = Defection. Kuch ise avsarvaad maante, kuch antaraatma ki awaaz. Anti-Defection Law — 1985, 52nd Amendment — aisa karne wala sadasya ayogya ho sakta hai; faisla Speaker/Chairman karta hai. Maqsad: sarkar mein sthirta.

National vs State party — pehchaan ki shartein

National party (koi ek): 4+ rajyon mein 6% vote + Lok Sabha mein 4 seat; ya Lok Sabha ki 2% seats, kam se kam 3 rajyon se; ya 4 rajyon mein State party. State party (koi ek): Vidhan Sabha mein 6% vote + 2 seat; ya Lok Sabha mein us rajya se 6% vote + 1 seat; ya Vidhan Sabha ki 3% seats ya 3 seats (jo zyada); ya har 25 Lok Sabha seats par 1 seat; ya 8% vote. Baaki = RUPP.

Chunautiyan aur hal

96.8 crore voters, lakhon booth, sainkdon parties — kaam bahut bada. Chunautiyan: galat soochna, fake news, dhamki (intimidation), paise ki taaqat. Hal: RPA 1950-51, Model Code of Conduct, EVM, VVPAT (vote ki parchi dikhti hai), jaagrukta abhiyaan. Bahas ka mudda: 'One Nation, One Election' — fayde aur seemaayein dono socho. Vote dena adhikaar bhi, kartavya bhi — chunaav loktantra ki aatma hain.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Chunaav ke 4 laabh: Accountability, Representation, Legitimacy, Equality.
  • Direct: Lok Sabha, Vidhan Sabha, local bodies (5 saal). Indirect: President, VP, Rajya Sabha.
  • Psephology — Greek psephos = pebble.
  • FPTP → Lok Sabha, Vidhan Sabha. PR → Rajya Sabha, President, VP. STV → Vidhan Parishad.
  • Bicameral rajya (6): AP, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, UP.
  • Majority system: 50%+ chahiye, warna top-2 mein doosra round.
  • RPA 1950 — seats, delimitation, voter list. RPA 1951 — nomination, prachaar, voting, vivaad, bhrasht aacharan. Presidential & VP Elections Act 1952.
  • Delimitation — Art 82; commissions: 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002.
  • ECI — bani 25 Jan 1950; Art 324–329; 25 Jan = National Voters' Day.
  • ECI kaam: electoral roll + SIR, schedule, party registration + chinh, nishpaksh chunaav, ghar se voting (85+, 40% PwD — 2024).
  • Apps: ETPBS, Voter Helpline, Saksham, cVIGIL, Suvidha, ERONET, Sugam. IEVP — 28 EMBs + IFES, IDEA, UN.
  • Anti-Defection Law — 1985, 52nd Amendment; faisla Speaker/Chairman.
  • Gathbandhan: 1967 shuruaat; 1977 Janata Party; NDA (BJP), UPA (INC) → 2023 INDIA.
  • National party: 6% vote 4 rajyon + 4 LS seat / 2% LS seats 3 rajyon se / 4 rajyon mein State party.

Yaad karne ki trick

Teen system = "Sabse aage, Aadhe se upar, Anupaat"

FPTP = sabse aage wala jeete; Majority = 50% se upar; PR = vote ke anupaat mein seat.

Bicameral 6 rajya = "ABK MTU"

Andhra, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh.

RPA 50 vs 51 = "50 list, 51 ladaai"

1950 — list (voter list, seats, seema); 1951 — chunaav ki ladaai (nomination, prachaar, vivaad).

ECI = "324 se 329, 25 Jan janm"

Articles 324–329; 25 January 1950 ko bani, isliye usi din Voters' Day.

Delimitation saal = "52-63-73-02"

Chaar commission: 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002 (Art 82).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Lok Sabha ke chunaav kis system se hote hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) First-Past-The-Post — FPTP — sabse zyada vote wala jeetta hai.

  2. Q2. Inmein se kiska chunaav INDIRECT hota hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (D) Rajya Sabha — President, VP, Rajya Sabha — indirect.

  3. Q3. 'Psephology' shabd Greek ke kis shabd se bana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Psephos — kankad — Prachin Greece mein kankad se vote.

  4. Q4. Inmein se kis rajya mein Vidhan Parishad NAHI hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Rajasthan — Chhe rajya: AP, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, UP.

  5. Q5. Voter list banane ka praavdhan kis kaanoon mein hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) RPA 1950 — RPA 1950 — seats, delimitation, electoral roll.

  6. Q6. Delimitation Commission kis Article ke tahat banta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Article 82 — Art 82; ab tak 4 commission.

  7. Q7. Election Commission of India kab bana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 25 Jan 1950 — Isliye 25 Jan = National Voters' Day.

  8. Q8. Model Code of Conduct ka ullanghan report karne wala app —

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) cVIGIL — cVIGIL — minto mein report, flying squad pahunchta.

  9. Q9. Anti-Defection Law kis amendment se aaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 52nd (1985) — 1985, 52nd Constitutional Amendment.

  10. Q10. 2024 mein ghar se voting ki suvidha kinke liye poore desh mein di gayi?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) 85+ buzurg aur 40%+ viklangta wale — Pehli baar 2024 General Elections mein.
