Theme E · Economics

Building Blocks in Economics: The Problem of Choice

Ichchhaayein anant, saadhan seemit — isliye chunna padta hai; opportunity cost, PPC, teen mool sawaal aur teen arthik pranaaliyan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Roz ke chunaav hi Economics hain

Pocket money se snacks lo ya joote ke liye bachaao? Library mein kitaab ki 5 copy, padhna chaahte 20 bachche — pehle kise mile? Kisaan kaunsi fasal boye — mitti, baarish aur bazaar dekhkar? Company zyada mazdoor rakhe ya machine? Sarkar highway banaaye ya hospital? Ye sab arthik chunaav (economic choices) hain — vyakti, company aur sarkar sabko karne padte hain.

Needs vs Wants

Needs (zaroorat) — jinke bina jeena mushkil: khaana, paani, ghar. Wants (ichchha) — jo achchha lage par zaroori nahi: gadget, ghoomna, mehenga saamaan. Insaan ki ichchhaayein anant aur badalti rehti hain — cycle mili to bike chahiye, bike mili to car! Market = jahan cheezein aur sevaayein khareedi-bechi jaati hain — asli dukaan ya online.

Saadhan seemit, upyog anek

Ichchha poori karne ke liye resources (saadhan) chahiye — land, labour, capital, technology (Class 8 ke factors of production). Saadhan prakritik (paani, koyla) ya maanav-nirmit (machine, technology) — par dono seemit. Aur ek saadhan ke kai upyog: steel se medical upkaran, hawaai jahaz ya fridge — kuch bhi ban sakta hai. Ghar ho ya poora desh — sabko tay karna hai ki seemit saadhan ka sabse achchha upyog kaise ho.

Opportunity Cost — jo chhoda uski keemat

Jab ek vikalp chunte ho to baaki chhodne padte hain. Jo chhoda, uska mulya = Opportunity Cost. Misaal: ₹100 hain — notebook li to racket ke liye bachat chhodi; racket ki bachat hi notebook ki opportunity cost hai. Samay bhi seemit saadhan hai — 1 ghanta game khele to wo ghanta padhaai ka tha! Har faisle ki ek 'chhupi keemat' hoti hai.

PPC — kisaan ka jau aur gehun

Kisaan ke paas seemit zameen-paani-mazdoor. Wo jau (barley) ya gehun (wheat) uga sakta hai. Combinations: A 0 jau – 100 gehun; B 25–90; C 50–70; D 75–40; E 100–0. Graph par x = jau, y = gehun — neeche jaati hui curve = Production Possibility Curve (PPC). A se E jaate hue jau badhta, gehun ghatta — zyada jau ke liye gehun ki qurbaani = jau ki opportunity cost. Dhyaan do: A→B mein 25 kg jau ke liye sirf 10 kg gehun chhoda, par D→E mein 25 kg jau ke liye 40 kg gehun! Curve ke har bindu par saadhan poori tarah, bina barbaadi ke istemaal — maximum output.

Economics — shabd ka matlab

Greek oikonomia = oikos (ghar) + nemein (prabandh) → 'ghar ka prabandh'. Seemit saadhan aur anant ichchhaaon mein parivaar hi nahi, poore desh ko bhi yojana banaani padti hai. Economics padhta hai ki chunaav kaise hote hain aur arthik ikaaiyan — consumer, producer, sarkar, bank — aapas mein kaise judti hain: log kaise kamaate, daulat kaise batti, keemat kaise tay hoti, sarkari neeti bazaar ko kaise badalti.

Data se faisla, andaaze se nahi

Achchhe faisle data aur vishleshan se hote hain. Parivaar paisa baanta hai — zaroori (khaana, dawa, school), gair-zaroori (gehne, manoranjan, hotel) aur bachat. Sarkar tax ki kamaai se sadak aur kalyan yojanaaon ki yojana banaati. Company bazaar ke trend dekhti. Economist kaam karte hain — policy-making (sarkar ko salaah), business consulting, finance (niveshak ko salaah), research aur shiksha. Survey = data jama aur vishleshan ka vyavasthit tareeka.

Economic Survey — desh ka report card

Vitt Mantralaya (Ministry of Finance) har saal banaata, Union Budget se pehle Sansad mein rakha jaata. Isme pichhle saal ka pradarshan — kheti, udyog, sevaayein, rozgaar, mehngaai, shiksha, swasthya, infrastructure — aur aage ki chunautiyan. Ye budget ka blueprint hai aur aam nagrik ko bhi arthvyavastha seedhi bhaasha mein samjhaata hai.

Teen mool sawaal

Anant ichchha + seemit saadhan = Scarcity (kami) → Choices → teen sawaal: 1) Kya banaayein? 2) Kaise banaayein? 3) Kiske liye banaayein? Har arthvyavastha ko inka jawab dena padta hai.

Kya banaayein? — ganna ya mota anaaj

Kisaan paani-khau ganna aur dhaan ugaaye ya sookha-sahne wale millets aur daalein? Ganna = zyada munaafa, chini udyog ko sahara. Millets-daal = paani ki bachat, mitti ki sehat, tikau kheti. Ganna chuna to opportunity cost = bacha hua paani aur achchhi mitti. Yani aaj ka fayda vs kal ki sthirta ka trade-off.

Kiske liye banaayein? — joote ki misaal

Log alag — zaroorat, aamdani, pasand alag. School shoes — saade, majboot, saste. Office shoes — aaraam, formal, achchha leather. Sports shoes — khaas rubber sole, halke, pakad aur lachak. Chappal/casual — roz ke liye, aaraamdaayak aur saste. Grahak ke hisaab se maal bhi badalta — leather = office/zyada aamdani; rubber/synthetic = khilaadi, mazdoor, sasti majboot zaroorat.

Kaise banaayein? — mazdoor ya machine

Labour-intensive — zyada mazdoor, kam machine (kheti, hastshilp). Capital-intensive — zyada machine-technology, kam mazdoor (steel, automobile). Garment factory ki misaal: machine mehngi → mazdoor; machine sasti → automation. Technology achchhi → machine. Designer/customised kapde → kushal kaarigar; mass production → machine. Mazdoor saste aur aasaani se mile → labour-intensive; mehnge/kam → machine. Sarkari kaanoon (labour law, machine par chhoot) bhi asar daalte.

Teen arthik pranaaliyan

Planned economy — sarkar (planning authority) tay karti kya, kitna, kaise, kise, kis daam; zameen, factory, bank, transport zyaadatar sarkari; license-permit ki sakhti → competition kam, quality-innovation ki prerna kam. Misaal: purana Soviet Union, North Korea, Cuba. Market economy — maang aur poorti (demand-supply) se faisle; sarkar sirf football ke referee jaisi — suraksha, kaanoon-vyavastha; daam ya utpaadan control nahi; maalikana private; competition se behtar quality, kam daam. Misaal: USA, Japan, Hong Kong. Mixed economy — dono ka mel; private aur sarkari dono, bade public sector companies bhi. Misaal: India (1991 ke baad), China (1978 ke baad), Germany, Sweden. Sach mein lagbhag har arthvyavastha mixed hai.

Public goods aur India ka safar

Public goods — sabke liye, kisi ko roka nahi jaata, ek ke upyog se doosre ka kam nahi hota: park, sadak, police, street light, buniyaadi shiksha. Mixed economy mein sarkar ka kaam: fair competition ke niyam, consumer protection, paardarshita, public goods, kalyan yojanaayein; bazaar laata hai munaafa, innovation, competition. India — aazaadi ke baad planned jaisa (license, bade public sector); 1991 ke sankat ke baad reforms — kam niyantran, private ko badhaava, videshi vyapar-nivesh khula → zyada bazaar-unmukh, par sarkar ki bhoomika bani rahi.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Arthik chunaav vyakti, company, sarkar teeno karte hain. Needs = zaroorat; wants = anant aur badalti ichchha.
  • Factors of production: land, labour, capital, technology — sab seemit, upyog anek (steel → medical, aircraft, fridge).
  • Opportunity cost = chhode gaye vikalp ka mulya.
  • PPC table: A(0,100) B(25,90) C(50,70) D(75,40) E(100,0) — neeche jhukti curve; har bindu = saadhan ka poora, kushal upyog.
  • Economics ← Greek oikonomia = oikos (household) + nemein (management).
  • Economist ka kaam: policy-making, business consulting, finance, research-education.
  • Economic Survey — Ministry of Finance; Union Budget se pehle Sansad mein; budget ka blueprint.
  • Scarcity → Choices → 3 sawaal: What, How, For Whom.
  • What: ganna/dhaan (munaafa) vs millets/daal (paani, mitti, sthirta).
  • For whom: school, office, sports, casual shoes — alag grahak, alag maal.
  • How: labour-intensive (kheti, hastshilp) vs capital-intensive (steel, auto) — machine ki keemat, technology, product ka swabhav, mazdoor ki uplabdhta, kaanoon.
  • Planned — Soviet Union, North Korea, Cuba. Market — USA, Japan, Hong Kong. Mixed — India (post-1991), China (post-1978), Germany, Sweden.
  • India: aazaadi ke baad state-led → 1991 reforms → market-unmukh mixed economy.

Yaad karne ki trick

Teen sawaal = "KyaKaiseKise"

Kya banaayein, Kaise banaayein, Kise (kiske liye) — ek saans mein bolo!

Economics = "Ghar ka Manager"

Oikos = ghar (jaise 'eco' = ghar/vaatavaran), nemein = manage karna.

Teen system = "Sarkar, Bazaar, Dono"

Planned = Sarkar (Soviet, Korea, Cuba — 'SKC'); Market = Bazaar (USA, Japan, Hong Kong — 'UJH'); Mixed = Dono (India, China, Germany, Sweden).

Opportunity cost = "Jo chhoda, wahi daam"

Har chunaav ki keemat paise se nahi, chhode gaye agle best vikalp se naapi jaati hai.

Labour vs Capital

Labour = Log zyada (kheti, hastshilp); Capital = Car factory jaisi machine wali (steel, auto).

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Inmein se kaun 'need' hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Peene ka paani — Khaana, paani, ghar — buniyaadi zaroorat.

  2. Q2. ₹100 se notebook kharidi, racket ki bachat chhodi. Ye kaunsi dhaarna hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Opportunity cost — Chhode gaye vikalp ki keemat.

  3. Q3. 'Economics' shabd kis Greek shabd se aaya?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Oikonomia — Oikos (ghar) + nemein (prabandh).

  4. Q4. PPC table mein C se D jaane par 25 kg jau ke liye kitna gehun chhoda?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 30 kg — C mein 70, D mein 40 → 30 kg.

  5. Q5. Economic Survey kaun taiyaar karta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ministry of Finance — Union Budget se pehle Sansad mein rakha jaata.

  6. Q6. Steel aur automobile udyog aam taur par kaise hote hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Capital-intensive — Machine aur technology par zyada nirbhar.

  7. Q7. Market economy mein sarkar ki bhoomika ki tulna kisse ki gayi hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Referee — Suraksha aur kaanoon-vyavastha, daam control nahi.

  8. Q8. Planned economy ki misaal —

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Cuba — Purana Soviet Union, North Korea, Cuba.

  9. Q9. India kab se zyada market-unmukh mixed economy bana?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 1991 — 1991 ke arthik sankat ke baad reforms.

  10. Q10. Inmein se 'public good' kaunsa hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (A) Street light — Sabke liye, kisi ko roka nahi jaata.
