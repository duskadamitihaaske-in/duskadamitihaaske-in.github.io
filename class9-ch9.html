The Price Puzzle: What Drives the Market

Daam kyon badalte hain? — demand aur supply ke niyam, unhe badalne wale kaaran, market equilibrium, dynamic pricing aur sarkar ka dakhal.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Daam ki paheliyan

Pichhle hafte ke aam ab aadhe daam par kyon? Sabziyan subah mehngi, shaam ko sasti kyon? Pyaaz ka daam har kuch mahine kyon uchhalta? Ek hi flight seat kabhi ₹3,000, kabhi ₹9,000? Malls saal ke kuch samay discount kyon dete? Daam bina wajah nahi badalte — log kya chaahte, kitna maal hai, mausam, tyohaar, trend, kabhi afwaah bhi. Par sabke peeche do badi taaqatein: Demand aur Supply.

Demand kya hai?

Demand = kisi daam par log kitna maal khareedna chaahte HAIN aur khareed SAKTE HAIN. Sirf ichchha demand nahi — saath mein purchasing power (paise ki khareed-shakti) bhi chahiye. Mehel chaahna demand nahi, jab tak paise na hon! Law of Demand: daam badha → maang ghati; daam ghata → maang badhi. Yani ulta rishta (inverse relationship).

Srivalli ke aam — demand schedule aur curve

Season ki shuruaat: aam ₹150/kg → Srivalli ne 1 kg liya. Daam ₹100 → 2 kg. Daam ₹50 → 3 kg. Ye table = demand schedule. Graph par y = daam, x = maatra; bindu A, B, C jodo → neeche ki or jhukti line DD' = demand curve. Shart: baaki cheezein (aamdani, pasand) wahi rahein. Ek vyakti ki maang = individual demand.

Market demand — sabki maang jodo

Alex aur Israt bhi aam khareedte hain. ₹150 par: Srivalli 1 + Alex 2 + Israt 3 = 6 kg. ₹100 par: 2+4+6 = 12 kg. ₹50 par: 3+6+9 = 18 kg. Ye = market demand (sabki individual demand ka jod). Dhyaan do — market curve zyada chapti (flatter) hai: ₹150 se ₹50 hone par Srivalli ki maang sirf 2 kg badhi, par market ki 12 kg! Kai log mile to ek jaisa daam-badlaav bada asar daalta hai.

Daam ke alaawa maang kya badalta? (1)

Naya smartphone mehenga hai phir bhi lambi line! Matlab daam hi sab kuch nahi. Related goods: Substitute (ek doosre ki jagah) — chai-coffee, aam-kela; coffee mehngi → chai ki maang badhi. Complementary (saath istemaal) — phone-earphone, car-petrol, printer-cartridge, movie ticket-popcorn; ticket mehenga → popcorn ki maang bhi giri. Aamdani badhi → zyada ya behtar maal. Pasand — Srivalli ko aam pasand, santra sasta ho tab bhi nahi lengi. Aabaadi — zyada bachche → sports shoes; zyada kaamkaaji → formal shoes; zyada buzurg → aaraamdaayak/orthopaedic joote.

Daam ke alaawa maang kya badalta? (2)

Diminishing marginal utility: pehla aam — waah! doosra — achchha; teesra… ab mann nahi. Har agle aam se milne wala atirikt sukh ghatta, isliye uske liye paisa dene ki ichchha bhi ghatti. Mausam/season — session shuru → kitaabon ki dukaan par bheed; tyohaar → mithaai; sardi → sweater. Bhavishya ke daam ki ummeed — daam girne ki ummeed → khareed taalo (Diwali sale ka intezaar), daam badhne ki ummeed → abhi khareedo.

Supply aur uska niyam

Supply = kisi daam par vikreta kitna maal bechna chaahte aur bech sakte hain. Law of Supply: daam badha → supply badhi; daam ghata → supply ghati — seedha rishta (direct). Kyon? Zyada daam = zyada munaafa, utpaadak zyada banaate, nayi companies bhi aa jaati. Seller A: ₹50 → 1 kg, ₹100 → 2 kg, ₹150 → 3 kg — upar ki or chadhti supply curve. Teen sellers A+B+C: ₹50 → 6, ₹100 → 12, ₹150 → 18 kg = market supply.

Daam ke alaawa supply kya badalta?

Related goods ka daam — gehun sasta, chana mehenga → kisaan agle season zyada chana boyega. Vikretaaon ki sankhya — zyada seller → supply zyada, daam gire; kam seller → daam chadhe. Technology — drip irrigation, weather sensor se paidaavaar badhi; cold storage se aam door ke bazaar tak → supply badhi. Bhavishya ki ummeed — aage maang badhne ki ummeed → zyada banao; aloo ke thok vyapaari peak season mein daam badhne ki ummeed mein abhi maal rok lete. Baaki: input ki keemat, mausam, aapda.

Market Equilibrium — jahan dono mil jaayein

₹40 par: maang 38, supply 6 → Excess demand (kami/shortage) → daam chadhega. ₹150 par: maang 8, supply 43 → Excess supply (surplus) → daam girega. ₹100 par: maang = supply = 12 kg → Market Equilibrium! Graph par demand aur supply curve jahan kaatein (point E) — equilibrium price ₹100, quantity 12 kg. Yahan na kami, na faltu maal — bazaar 'clear'; daam badalne ka koi dabaav nahi.

Asli duniya mein equilibrium?

Kitaab mein equilibrium ek bindu hai, par asli bazaar chalta-firta (dynamic) hai — technology, mazdoori, byaaj dar, yuddh, raajneeti, mahamaari, mausam, aapdaayein — sab demand-supply badalte. Isliye equilibrium hamesha khisakta rehta hai. COVID-19 (2020): mask ki maang achaanak uchhli, supply peechhe → daam bahut badhe; dheere supply badhi → daam gire; mahamaari khatam → maang ghati → daam pehle jaise.

Hotel ka kiraya — dynamic pricing

Goa ka hotel, 100 kamre: July ka Monday (off-season) — ₹1,500; December ka Saturday — ₹8,000; New Year's Eve — ₹25,000! Group booking cancel → raaton-raat 40% kam. Din mein kai baar daam badalte — kitni tezi se booking ho rahi, paas ke hotel ka daam, tyohaar/conference, mausam ki bhavishyavaani, aane mein kitne din, pichhle trend. Revenue = kharche ghataane se pehle bikri se kul kamaai. Socho: fast fashion, zyada machhli pakadna, bhoojal ka atidohan — aaj ki maang kal ki supply ko nuksaan pahuncha sakti hai.

Sarkar kyon beech mein aati hai?

India aaj duniya ki 4th sabse badi arthvyavastha — bazaar-aadhaarit par niyantrit (regulated). Bazaar maal use deta hai jo paisa de sake — par dawa bahut mehngi ho jaaye to garib kya kare? Isliye: Price ceiling — adhiktam daam (jaise zaroori dawaayein). Price floor — nyuntam daam (jaise minimum wage); asar ke liye equilibrium se upar rakhna padta. Monopoly — ek hi vikreta, koi vikalp nahi → zyada daam, kharab quality, kam supply; sarkar rokti. Regulators: RBI (banking), CCPA (upbhokta adhikaar), TRAI (telecom), SEBI (share bazaar).

Sanitiser ki kahaani aur public goods

COVID mein sanitiser ki maang uchhli — stock khatam, daam aasmaan; kuch dukaandaaron ne hoarding (zaroorat se zyada jama karna) aur black marketing (gair-kaanooni bikri) ki. Sarkar ne ise Essential Commodities Act, 1955 mein zaroori vastu ghoshit kiya — 200 ml ka MRP max ₹100. Companies ne utpaadan badhaaya, sanitiser sahi daam par mil gaya. Public goods — sadak, pul, park, street light, raksha, safaai-naali; private companies nahi banaatin kyonki seedha munaafa nahi. Park ki misaal: har parivaar ₹5,000 de to park banega, par har koi soche 'doosre denge, main muft mein ghoomunga' → paisa jama hi nahi hota → isliye sarkar banaati hai.

Zyada dakhal ke nuksaan

1) Daam bigadna, prerna ghatna — gehun ka bazaar daam ₹30, sarkar ne max ₹20 kar diya → kisaan kam ugaayenge → kami. 2) Compliance ka bojh — chhote restaurant ko food safety, fire safety, pradushan, local clearance — kai permission; samay aur kharch se naye udyami darte → ease of doing business girta. 3) Innovation ruk jaata — sahi kamaai na ho to kisaan behtar beej, sinchaai, technology mein nivesh nahi karega. Loktantrik sarkar ko consumer, producer, mazdoor — sabki awaaz sunkar santulan banaana chahiye.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Demand = willingness + ability (purchasing power). Law of Demand: P↑ Q↓ (inverse); demand curve neeche jhukti.
  • Srivalli: ₹150→1, ₹100→2, ₹50→3 kg. Market (Srivalli+Alex+Israt): 6, 12, 18 kg — market curve flatter.
  • Demand ke anya kaaran: substitute/complementary goods ka daam, aamdani, pasand, aabaadi ka aakaar-banaawat, season, bhavishya ki ummeed.
  • Diminishing marginal utility — har agle aam se kam sukh → dene ki ichchha kam.
  • Law of Supply: P↑ Qs↑ (direct); supply curve upar chadhti. Market supply A+B+C: 6, 12, 18 kg.
  • Supply ke anya kaaran: related goods (gehun vs chana), sellers ki sankhya, technology (drip, sensor, cold storage), bhavishya ki ummeed, input cost, mausam.
  • Table 9.3: ₹40 → Qd 38, Qs 6 (excess demand); ₹100 → 12 = 12 (equilibrium); ₹150 → Qd 8, Qs 43 (excess supply).
  • Real world mein equilibrium hamesha khisakta — COVID mask ki misaal; Goa hotel ₹1,500 / ₹8,000 / ₹25,000.
  • India = 4th sabse badi arthvyavastha; market-based regulated.
  • Sarkari dakhal: price ceiling (dawa), price floor (minimum wage), monopoly par roak; regulators RBI, CCPA, TRAI, SEBI.
  • Sanitiser — Essential Commodities Act 1955; 200 ml MRP ₹100.
  • Public goods — sadak, park, raksha; 'free rider' soch ke kaaran sarkar banaati.
  • Zyada dakhal ke nuksaan: price distortion (gehun ₹20 vs ₹30), compliance burden, innovation mein kami.

Yaad karne ki trick

Demand ulta, Supply seedha

Demand = Downward (neeche jhukti); Supply = Stairs up (seedhi chadhti). Daam badhe to grahak bhaage, vikreta aaye!

Substitute vs Complement = "Ya / Aur"

Substitute = chai YA coffee; Complement = car AUR petrol.

Ceiling vs Floor

Ceiling = chhat — daam isse upar nahi ja sakta (dawa). Floor = farsh — isse neeche nahi (minimum wage).

Equilibrium numbers = "100 ka 12"

Aam ka equilibrium daam ₹100, maatra 12 kg. Neeche daam = kami, upar daam = faltu maal.

Regulators = "RBI ka paisa, TRAI ka phone, SEBI ka share, CCPA ka grahak"

Har kshetra ka apna 'chaukidaar'.

Practice MCQs option dabao, turant check karo

  1. Q1. Law of Demand ke anusaar daam aur maang mein kaisa rishta hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Ulta (inverse) — Daam badhe to maang ghate.

  2. Q2. Demand ke liye ichchha ke saath kya zaroori hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Purchasing power — Khareedne ki kshamata bhi chahiye.

  3. Q3. ₹100 par Srivalli 2, Alex 4, Israt 6 kg leti hain. Market demand?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) 12 kg — 2 + 4 + 6 = 12 kg.

  4. Q4. Car aur petrol kis tarah ke goods hain?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Complementary — Saath istemaal hote hain.

  5. Q5. Teesra aam pehle jitna achchha nahi lagta — ye kaunsa siddhant?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Diminishing marginal utility — Har agli unit se kam atirikt utility.

  6. Q6. Table 9.3 mein equilibrium daam kya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) ₹100 — ₹100 par Qd = Qs = 12 kg.

  7. Q7. ₹150 par Qd 8 aur Qs 43 ho to sthiti kya hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) Excess supply — Supply maang se zyada — surplus.

  8. Q8. Minimum wage kis cheez ki misaal hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Price floor — Nyuntam seema — isse neeche nahi.

  9. Q9. COVID mein sanitiser ka daam kis kaanoon ke tahat bandha?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (B) Essential Commodities Act 1955 — 200 ml ka MRP max ₹100.

  10. Q10. Telecom kshetra ka regulator kaun hai?

    Answer & solution

    Sahi uttar: (C) TRAI — Telecom Regulatory Authority of India.
