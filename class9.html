Part 1 · Ch01

Theme I · Introduction

Understanding Social Science

Social Science kya hai, roz ki zindagi mein kaise dikhta hai, aur iske chaar mukhya hisse — Geography, History, Political Science, Economics.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Social Science — samaaj ka 'vigyaan'

Physics, Chemistry, Biology prakriti ko padhte hain; Social Science insaani samaaj ko — log kaise saath rehte hain, sarkar kaise chalti hai, kaam-dhandha kaise hota hai, aur beeta kal aaj ko kaise banata hai. Seedhi paribhasha: 'maanav samaaj ka vyavasthit (systematic) adhyayan'. Ye sirf 'kya hua' ya 'kahan hai' nahi batata, balki 'kyon hua' aur 'sab kaise jude hain' bhi samjhaata hai.

Tumhara ek din — Social Science se bhara

Subah uthe — ghar alag-alag jagah ke saamaan se bana. Khaana — kisi aur rajya ke khet se, kaata, process kiya, truck se aaya, khareeda-becha gaya. Sadak — sarkari vibhaag ne banaayi. School — shiksha neeti se chalta. Bijli — door ke power station se, bade jaal se aati. Yani sabse chhote kaam bhi sarkar, arthvyavastha, samaaj ke sahyog aur prakriti par tike hain. Aur sawaal socho: kuch log bheed wale shehar mein, kuch bikhre gaon mein kyon? Kuch ilaakon mein baadh zyada kyon? Kheti kahin achchhi, kahin kharab kyon? Samaaj sanyog (chance) se nahi chalta — itihaas, bhoogol, sansthaayein, sansaadhan aur insaani faisle use banaate hain.

Bharat ki purani 'jigyaasa'

Hamari parampara mein charcha, sawaal aur tark se duniya samajhne ki aadat thi. Panchamahabhuta — duniya 5 tatvon se bani: Prithvi (dharti), Apah (jal), Agni, Vayu, Akasha — aur insaan inse juda hai; isse basti, pesha, ghar ki banawat, khaana, swasthya samajh aate hain. Vasudhaiva kutumbakam — 'poori duniya ek parivaar'. ~2,300 saal pehle Kautilya ke Arthashastra mein prashasan, arthvyavastha, tax aur raja ke kartavya ka vyavasthit vichaar tha — aaj ke subjects banne se bahut pehle!

Social scientist kaam kaise karte hain?

Logon ki zindagi dekhte hain, interview aur survey karte hain, dastavez jaanchte hain, alag samay aur jagah ki tulna karte hain — aur saboot (evidence) se samjhaav banaate hain. Isse pata chalta hai kya tikta hai aur kya badalta hai.

Ek sookha, paanch nazariye

Samaaj itna ulajha hai ki koi ek subject poora nahi samjha sakta. Ek sookha (drought) socho: fasal par asar (paryavaran), kisaan ki kamaai (arthvyavastha), sarkari raahat (raajneeti), shehar ki or palaayan (samaaj), aur paani ki kami se nipatne ke purane tareeke (sanskriti). Isliye Social Science kai jude hue subjects ka parivaar hai. Class 9–10 mein 4 mukhya: Geography, History, Political Science, Economics. Baaki — Sociology, Philosophy, Anthropology, Psychology — aage.

Geography — 'kahan' aur 'kyon wahan'

Jagahon, cheezon aur logon ki sthiti aur failaav, aur insaan-prakriti ka rishta. Do nazariye: spatial (jagah aur uska mahatva) + temporal (samay ke saath badlaav). Misaal: India ki lambi tat-rekha ne Africa, Europe, SE Asia se sampark kaise badhaaya. Auzaar: map, globe, atlas, GIS, infographic. NCERT ka School Bhuvan portal — apna gaon-shehar map karo! Aage padhoge: dharti ki satah kaise banti, vaayumandal-jalvaayu, mahasagar, biomes, biosphere reserves.

History — beete kal ki kahaani, saboot ke saath

Bharat ki sabse purani parampara — itihasa-purana: kahaaniyon se jaankaari bhi aur moolya bhi. Aaj ka itihaas-lekhan empirical evidence (dekh kar/prayog se mila saboot) par zyada nirbhar — genetics, carbon-14 dating, archaeology. Sources: literary (Samaveda pandulipi, Tirukkural taad-patra), archaeological (moorti, terracotta), epigraphic (pathar-dhaatu par likhe lekh — jaise Brahmi stambh lekh, Krishnadevaraya ka Kannada lekh), numismatic (sikke — Samudragupta ka sikka, Jahangir ka Dhanu raashi wala sikka!), aur yaatra-vritaant, genealogical records (vansh-vriksh), lok-kathaayein.

Political Science — satta aur shaasan

Shaasan ka adhyayan: satta kaise bant-ti, faisle kaise hote, neetiyaan kaise lagu hoti; samvidhan, sarkar, sansthaayein, andolan, videsh neeti. Gaon ka Panchayati Raj dikhaata hai ki satta sirf sarkari imaaraton mein nahi, samaaj ke rishton mein bhi hai. Bharat mein raajneeti dharma (naitik kartavya), artha (aarthik kalyaan) aur rajadharma (raja ke kartavya) se judi thi — Veda, Upanishad, Mahabharata, Shukraniti, Arthashastra. Seekh: satta ek zimmedari hai, sirf haq nahi.

Economics — seemit saadhan, anant ichchhaayein

Log aur samaaj seemit sansaadhanon ko kaise use karein — kya banaayein, kaise baantein. Consumer kya khareedein, producer kitna banaayein, sarkar vikas-sthirta aur kushalta-nyaay mein santulan kaise rakhe. India sadiyon tak duniya ki agrani arthvyavasthaon mein tha; colonial raaj ne gareebi, akaal aur udyog-vinaash diya; azaadi ke baad punarnirmaan — aaj infrastructure, shiksha, technology, gareebi mein kami, badhti umar. Lakshya: sustainable vikas — aaj ki zaroorat poori karo, kal ki peedhi ka haq maare bina.

Kyon padhein? — samajhdaar naagrik banne ke liye

Ghar, paani, sadak, school, bazaar, internet — sab samaaj ke banaaye system hain; Social Science batata hai ye kaise bane aur kis par kya asar daalte hain. Alag bhasha-riwaaj ke peechhe bhoogol, itihaas, sanskriti, arthvyavastha hai — ye samajh samman aur sahyog laati hai, aur dikhaati hai ki vividhta ke neeche ek ekta hai. Paryavaran, swasthya, naukri jaise muddon par sahi sawaal poochhna seekhte ho. Ye ratne ka subject nahi — logon, jagahon, samaaj, sanskriti aur satta ko samajhne ka subject hai!

Theme G · Geography

Shaping of the Earth's Surface

Dharti ki parten, plate tectonics, weathering-erosion, nadi-hawa-glacier-lehron-bhoomigat jal se bane landforms, aur unse judi aapdaayein.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Dharti 'jeevit' hai — hamesha badalti

Dharti ki satah ek jaisi nahi rehti — andar ki aur bahar ki taaqatein use lagaataar badalti hain. Pahad, jwalamukhi, maidaan, ghaatiyan = landforms (prakritik bhoo-aakritiyaan). Inhe samajhne ki sabse badi chaabi hai plate tectonics ka siddhant.

Dharti ke andar — ek 'anda' jaisa

Ubla anda socho: patla chhilka, safed hissa, peela beech. Dharti mein bhi teen parten: Crust (sabse upar, jahan hum rehte — mahadweep ke neeche 30–40 km, samundar ke neeche sirf 5–7 km), Mantle (bahut moti aur garam, ~2,900 km), Core (sabse andar — outer core taral loha-nickel, inner core thos, ghoomti dhaatu ki gend, sabse ghani). Crust + upari mantle = Lithosphere (sakht, ~100 km) — yahi tooti hui hai plates mein. Uske neeche Asthenosphere — aadha pighla, jis par plates 'tairti' hain.

Plates kyon khisakti hain? — ubalti daal ki tarah

Siddhant W.J. Morgan se juda: dharti ki upari parat badi-chhoti plates mein bati hai jo kuch cm har saal khisakti hain. Prakaar: continental (mahadweep wali), oceanic (samundar tal wali), mixed. Badi plates: Pacific, Eurasian, African, North American, South American, Indo-Australian, Antarctic. Kyon khisakti? Patile mein ubalti daal dekho — neeche garam daal upar aati, upar ki thandi neeche jaati, aur upar tairti cheezein idhar-udhar khisakti hain. Mantle mein bhi aisi hi convection currents plates ko dhakelti-kheenchti hain.

Plates ki seemaayein — teen tarah ki mulaqat

Convergent — do plates aamne-saamne takraati: mahadweep-mahadweep → fold mountains (Himalaya!); samudri-mahadweepiya → samudri plate neeche dhans jaati → jwalamukhi aur bhookamp. Divergent — plates door hoti: neeche se magma upar aakar nayi crust banaata — Mid-Atlantic Ridge. Transform — plates ek-doosre ke bagal se ragadti: crust na banti na mitti, bas bhookamp — USA ka San Andreas Fault. Zyaadatar bhookamp-jwalamukhi Pacific ke chaaron or — 'Ring of Fire'.

India aur bhookamp

India mein bhi bade bhookamp aaye — 2001 Gujarat mein bhaari tabaahi. Ghani aabadi wale desh mein bada bhookamp bahut nuksaan karta hai. Purane samay mein ise 'bhookampa' kehte the; Varahamihira ne Brihatsamhita mein ek poora hissa likha — hawa, barish, baadal, jaanwaron ka vyavhaar aur grahon ki sthiti se sanket; kaaran chaar shaktiyon (Vayu, Agni, Indra, Varuna) se joda. India ka ekmaatra mud volcano — Baratang (Andaman), jahan aag nahi, zameen ki gas ke dabaav se keechad bulbule nikalta hai!

Weathering — todna; Erosion — tod kar le jaana

Weathering = chattaan ka wahin toot kar chhota hona (le jaana nahi). Teen prakaar: physical (garmi-thand, paala, hawa se phailna-sikudna), chemical (paani, hawa, tezaab se khanij badal jaana), biological (paudhon ki jadein daraar mein ghus kar pathar tod dein!). Isse mitti banti hai. Erosion (kataav) = toote hisse ko paani, hawa, barf, lehrein utha kar doosri jagah le jaana. Kisaan ki upjaau upari mitti beh jaaye to fasal ghat-ti; nadi-tat ke ghar, sadak beh jaate. Bachaav ke purane tareeke: contour trench, bunding (med), terracing (seedhidaar khet), check dam — Harappa se Arthashastra, Krishiparashara, Vrikshayurveda tak; Nagaland ka Zabo system.

Nadi ka safar — V se delta tak

Sabse badi 'moorti-kaar' — behta paani. Upari bhaag (dhalaan tez): V-shaped valley, waterfall (sakht chattaan upar, naram neeche kati → achaanak girna; paryatan aur bijli), rapids. Beech ka bhaag: nadi saanp jaisi ghoomti — meander (bahari kinaare kaatti, andar mitti jama karti); ek mod kat jaaye to bachi jheel = oxbow lake; upjaau floodplain. Neeche ka bhaag: nadi dheemi, mitti jama → tikona delta (chaawal, jute; machhli; par baadh ka khatra — jaise Sundarbans), levee. Kaveri par Kallanai — nadi ka sinchaai mein upyog.

Samundar ki lehrein aur glacier

Lehron se: beach (ret-kankad jama — paryatan, machhli, aur lehron se dhaal), sea cliff (khadi deewaar), wave-cut platform, sea cave → do taraf ki gufa mil kar arch → arch gire to akela khamba stack. Glacier (dheemi barf ki nadi) se: U-shaped valley, cirque (katora jaisa gadda), arete (dhaardar choti), hanging valley, fjord (samundar se bhari gehri patli ghaati — bandargah). Glacier jo patthar-mitti chhodta hai = moraine (lateral — kinaare, terminal — aakhir mein, medial — beech mein). Feb 2021 Chamoli (Uttarakhand) ki achaanak baadh — socho kyon aayi?

Hawa aur zameen ke neeche ka paani

Hawa (registan mein): yardang (hawa se tarasha lamba chattaani dhaancha), ventifact (ret ki maar se chikna pathar), deflation hollow (mitti udne se gadda — kabhi oasis bhi), desert pavement. Dunes: barchan (ardh-chandra), longitudinal, star (kai bhuja), parabolic. Bhoomigat jal (chuna pathar mein) — Karst topography: gufa, chhat se latakti stalactite (yaad rakho — 'tite' = chhat se tight latki), zameen se uthti stalagmite, sinkhole (zameen dhansna), bhoomigat nadi. Ye sab agents of gradation hain — oonchi jagah ghisaate, neechi bharte.

Landforms ne itihaas bhi banaaya

Ganga, Nile, Indus ke upjaau maidaan → kheti aur pehle shehar. Himalaya ne hamle roke par Khyber jaise darron se len-den bhi hua. Thar ne badi bastiyan nahi banne di par Silk Route jaise vyapaar raaste bane. Tat aur bandargahon se dakshin ke raajya door deshon se jude.

Landforms se judi 4 aapdaayein

Landslide — lagaataar barish se mitti bhaari aur phisalni, bhookamp, khadi dhalaan; insaani kaaran: jungle kaatna, khanan, sadak-nirmaan, kharab drainage. Avalanche — kam samay mein bhaari barf, achaanak garmi, tez hawa, skiing-trekking ka kampan. GLOF (Glacial Lake Outburst Flood) — glacier tezi se pighle → jheel badhe → moraine ka 'baandh' toote → neeche achaanak tabaahi. Dust storm — sookha, kam vanaspati, overgrazing, tez hawa; climate change se zyada. Seekh: dharti ki taaqat ko samjho aur pehle se taiyaari karo.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Plate tectonics (W.J. Morgan) — lithosphere kai plates mein; plates asthenosphere par kuch cm/saal khisakti.
  • Parten: crust (mahadweep 30–40 km, samundar 5–7 km), mantle (~2,900 km), outer core (taral Fe-Ni), inner core (thos, sabse ghani).
  • Plate prakaar: continental, oceanic, mixed. 7 badi: Pacific, Eurasian, African, N. American, S. American, Indo-Australian, Antarctic.
  • Kaaran: mantle mein convection currents.
  • Convergent → fold mountains (Himalaya), subduction → jwalamukhi/bhookamp; Divergent → mid-ocean ridge (Mid-Atlantic Ridge); Transform → bhookamp (San Andreas Fault).
  • Ring of Fire — Pacific ke chaaron or. 2001 Gujarat bhookamp.
  • Varahamihira — Brihatsamhita mein bhookamp ke sanket (Vayu, Agni, Indra, Varuna). Baratang — India ka ekmaatra mud volcano.
  • Weathering: physical, chemical, biological (sirf todna). Erosion: water, wind, glacial, coastal (le jaana).
  • Bachaav: contour trench, bunding, terracing, check dam; Nagaland — Zabo.
  • Agents of gradation: behta paani, glacier, hawa, lehrein, bhoomigat jal.
  • Nadi: upari — V-valley, waterfall, rapids; beech — meander, oxbow lake, floodplain; neeche — delta, levee, alluvial fan.
  • Lehrein: beach, sand bar, cliff, wave-cut platform, cave, arch, stack.
  • Glacier: U-valley, cirque, arete, hanging valley, fjord; moraine — lateral, terminal, medial.
  • Hawa: yardang, ventifact, deflation hollow, desert pavement; dunes — barchan, longitudinal, star, parabolic.
  • Bhoomigat jal (Karst): cave, stalactite (upar se), stalagmite (neeche se), sinkhole/doline, underground river.
  • Aapdaayein: landslide, avalanche, GLOF, dust storm; Chamoli flood Feb 2021.

Theme G · Geography

Atmosphere and Climate

Vaayumandal ki gaisen aur 5 parten, mausam-jalvaayu ke tatva, India ki ritu, monsoon kaise aata hai, climate change aur carbon footprint.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Dharti ka 'kambal' — vaayumandal

Aasmaan mein baadal, dhoop, hawa — ye sab ek hawa ki chaadar ki wajah se hai: atmosphere (vaayumandal). Ise dharti ka gurutvaakarshan (gravity) pakde rakhta hai. Ye suraj ki ultraviolet jaisi haanikaarak kirnon se bachaata hai aur garmi ka kuch hissa rok kar taapmaan theek rakhta hai. Socho — vaayumandal na ho to din mein bahut garmi, raat mein jamaa dene wali thand, aur saans bhi nahi!

Hawa mein kya-kya hai?

Nitrogen 78%, Oxygen 21%, Argon 0.93%, Carbon dioxide 0.04%, baaki ~0.03% (helium, neon, krypton, xenon, ozone, hydrogen). Saath mein bhaap (0.1–0.4%) — baadal aur barish ke liye zaroori — aur nanhe dhool kan. Oonchaai ke saath banawat badalti hai. Trick: 100 mein se 78 kursi Nitrogen ki, 21 Oxygen ki, 1 baaki sabki!

Paanch manzil — vaayumandal ki parten

Neeche se upar: (1) Troposphere (~12 km) — hum yahin saans lete; baadal, barish, kohra, ole — saara mausam yahin; oonchaai badhe to thand badhe; upar ki seema tropopause. (2) Stratosphere (50 km tak) — baadal-toofan nahi, isliye hawai jahaaz yahan udte; isme ozone parat — UV kirnon ki chhalni. Seema stratopause. (3) Mesosphere (80 km tak) — oonchaai ke saath thand; ulkaapind (meteors) yahin jal jaate. (4) Thermosphere (80–700 km) — X-ray, UV sokh kar tezi se garam; radio tarangen wapas dharti par bhejta (ionosphere isi ka hissa); aurora (dhruvon ke paas rangeen roshni — North mein Aurora Borealis, South mein Australis; 'aurora' = Latin mein 'bhor'). (5) Exosphere — bahut patli hawa; helium-hydrogen antariksh mein nikal jaate. Dhyaan: taapmaan oonchaai ke saath sirf troposphere aur mesosphere mein ghat-ta hai.

Hawa ka dabaav — mehsoos kyon nahi hota?

Hamare upar hawa ka bhaari bojh hai, phir bhi hum dabte nahi — kyonki hawa har taraf se dabaati hai aur hamare shareer ke andar ka dabaav bhi utna hi hai, dono barabar ho jaate hain!

Mausam vs jalvaayu aur 5 tatva

Weather = ghante-ghante, din-din ki haalat. Climate = bade ilaake mein 30 saal ya zyada ka ausat. Tatva: (1) Temperature — mukhya kaaran insolation (suraj se aane wali urja), jo equator se dhruvon ki taraf ghat-ti hai → teen zone: Torrid (23½°N–23½°S, garam), Temperate, Frigid (66½° ke paar). (2) Humidity — hawa mein bhaap; garam hawa zyada bhaap rakh sakti; kapde der se sookhte, paseena nahi udta. (3) Precipitation — drizzle, rain, snow, sleet, hail; zyaadatar groundwater barish se. (4) Air pressure — samundar tal par sabse zyada; garam jagah hawa upar uthe → kam dabaav (baadal, barish); thandi jagah hawa neeche baithe → zyada dabaav (saaf aasmaan). (5) Wind — hamesha zyada se kam dabaav ki or; naam jis disha se aaye (pashchim se = westerly).

Samundar kinaare ki do hawaayein

Sea breeze — din mein (khaas kar dopahar) zameen jaldi garam → zameen par kam dabaav → hawa samundar se zameen ki or. Land breeze — raat mein zameen jaldi thandi → hawa zameen se samundar ki or (dheemi). Isliye tat par mausam sukhad rehta hai. Hawa ki gati: calm (0–1 km/h, dhuan seedha upar), light breeze (6–11, patte sarsaraayein), strong breeze (39–49, chhaata pakadna mushkil), storm (103–117, tabaahi).

India ki ritu — IMD ke 4, parampara ke 6

India ki jalvaayu = tropical monsoon. IMD ke 4 mausam: Winter (Dec–early April; Dec-Jan sabse thande, uttar-pashchim 10–15°C), Summer/pre-monsoon (April–June; dakshin-pashchim mein April sabse garam, uttar mein May; 32–40°C), Monsoon (June–Sept; south-west monsoon), Post-monsoon (Oct–Dec; lautta monsoon). Paramparik 6 ritu (har ek ~2 mahine): Vasanta (Chaitra-Vaishakh, Mar–Apr), Grishma (Jyeshtha-Ashadh, May–Jun), Varsha (Shravan-Bhadrapad, Jul–Aug), Sharad (Ashvin-Kartik, Sep–Oct), Hemanta (Margashirsh-Paush, Nov–Dec), Shishira (Magh-Phalgun, Jan–Feb). Har ritu ke apne raag bhi! Arthashastra mein barish naapne ke record — lagaan aur raahat ke liye.

Monsoon — hawa ka saalaana 'U-turn'

Monsoon = hawa ki disha ka mausam ke saath palat jaana; Arabi 'mausim' (mausam). Purane naavik isi hawa ke sahaare yaatra karte the. South-west (garmi) monsoon (June–Sept): garmi mein zameen samundar se jaldi garam → upmahadweep par kam dabaav, samundar par zyada → nami bhari hawa samundar se zameen ki or → barish; saal ki zyaadatar barish isi se. Kerala ke paas ~1 June, poore desh mein ~8 July tak; Sept–Oct mein lautna. North-east (sardi) monsoon (Oct–Feb): zameen jaldi thandi → hawa zameen se samundar → aam taur par sookhi; par Bay of Bengal se nami lekar Tamil Nadu, Andhra, Karnataka ke hisson mein barish. Purana gyaan: Krishiparashara (suraj-chaand ki sthiti) aur Brihatsamhita (nakshatra — aasmaan ke 27 'chaand ke ghar') se barish ka anumaan; Kalidasa ke Meghadutam mein madhya India mein monsoon aane ki tithi aur baadlon ka raasta!

Monsoon = zindagi

Zyaadatar kheti monsoon par; achchha monsoon = achchhi fasal aur bhare jalaashay; tyohaar, rozgaar sab juda. Zyada barish → baadh; kam → sookha. Sarkar ka National Monsoon Mission aur 'Mission Mausam' — desh ko 'Weather Ready' aur 'Climate Smart' banaane ke liye.

Climate change aur tumhara 'carbon footprint'

Climate change = mausam ke pattern mein lamba badlaav, mukhyatah insaan ki wajah se — fossil fuel jalaana, jungle kaatna, udyog pradooshan → greenhouse gases (CO₂, methane, nitrous oxide, bhaap) badhti, garmi phansti. Nateeja: zyada baadh-sookha, glacier pighalna, samundar ka star badhna, jeevon ka nuksaan; mahilaaon-bachchon par bhi asar. Carbon footprint = tumhari gatividhiyon se nikli kul greenhouse gas. Khud jaancho: paidal/cycle (kam), bus/carpool (madhyam), chhoti doori bhi car (zyada), saal mein 2+ flight (bahut zyada); pankha-bulb band karna, ek baalti se nahaana, plastic na lena. Do chhote pledge likho aur nibhao!

Case study — Punjab baadh 2025

Kudrati kaaran: bahut bhaari monsoon + western disturbances; Himachal-J&K mein bhi barish; Satluj, Beas, Ravi, Ghaggar pehle se bhari. Insaani kaaran: purane kamzor tatbandh (dhussi bandh), nadi ke bahut paas ghar-khet (baadh ke failne ki jagah khatam), nadiyon-baandhon mein gaad bharna, der se ya saaf na di gayi chetaavni. Asar: jaanein gayin, log raahat camp mein, dhaan barbaad, dairy-poultry nasht, sadak-pul toote, ganda paani se beemaariyan. Sawaal: behtar planning se nuksaan kam ho sakta tha? Early Humans and Beginning of Civilisation

Africa se nikle pehle insaan, Stone Age ke 3 daur, Neolithic kranti, Mehrgarh se Harappa tak, aur Mesopotamia-Egypt-China ki kaansa yug sabhyataayein.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Likhaavat se pehle ka itihaas

Maanav itihaas ka 99% se zyada hissa (~30 lakh saal pehle se 5,000 saal pehle tak) likhaavat se pehle ka hai! Uski jaankaari sirf auzaaron, haddiyon aur cheezon (artefacts) se — isliye tareekhein andaaze se. Likhaavat ke baad (pichhle 5,000 saal, <1%) — naam, ghatnaayein, raajya-tilak, yuddh ki tareekhein bhi milti hain. Sabse purani lipiyon mein Harappan (Sindhu lipi) — chitron wali (pictographic), par aaj tak padhi nahi gayi; Mesopotamia ki cuneiform aur Egypt ki hieroglyphic padh li gayin. ~400 BCE se Brahmi — Ashoka ne vyavasthit roop diya (Sanchi stupa ki railing par).

Hamare poorvaj — Africa se poori duniya tak

Biological evolution: Australopithecines (australis = dakshini, pithecus = primate) se dheere-dheere aaj ke Homo sapiens. Cultural evolution: badalte mausam mein auzaar aur takneek se jeena — Quaternary period (pichhle 26 lakh saal). Kram: Homo habilis ('handy man' — Olduvai Gorge, Tanzania-Kenya; chopper auzaar) → Homo erectus (~20 lakh saal pehle; seedha chalne wala; handaxe, cleaver; Africa se bahar jaane wala pehla) → Neanderthal (Europe-SW Asia, ~40,000 saal pehle tak) → Homo sapiens (Africa mein ~3 lakh saal pehle; ~1,25,000 saal pehle bahar nikle; aaj ki ekmaatra maanav prajaati). ~33 lakh saal pehle pehle pathar ke auzaar — 'insaani vyavhaar' ki shuruaat; isliye hum 'hominins' = auzaar banaane wale. Auzaar = 'extra-corporal limbs' (shareer ke bahar ke haath-pair!).

Archaeologist kaise jaante hain?

Khudai mein auzaar, haddiyan, fossils (zameen mein dab kar laakhon saal mein pathar bane avshesh) milte hain. Archaeologist khud waise auzaar banaa kar aur chalaa kar dekhte hain ki purane log kaise jeete the — jaise jasoos saboot jodta hai!

Stone Age ke daur — Palaeo, Meso, Neo

Palaeolithic (palaeo = purana, lithic = pathar) — shikaar-sangrah, saade pathar ke auzaar. India mein sabse purani basti ~20 lakh saal: Attirampakkam (TN) ~15–17 lakh saal, Isampur (Karnataka) ~12 lakh saal — handaxe, cleaver, scraper, chopper (quartzite, chuna pathar se); maans kaatna, kand khodna, khaal saaf karna, haddi tod kar majja (marrow) nikaalna. Baad mein chhote auzaar — scraper, borer, nukile points (bhaale ke sire); phir teer-kamaan, blade, microblade; gufa chitra, shareer rangna, pathar-haddi-shankh ke moti; burin se shuturmurg ke ande par nakkaashi. Homo sapiens 50,000–12,000 saal pehle Australia aur America tak faile. Mesolithic (~12,000 saal pehle, garam mausam) — nanhe microlith auzaar, machhli mukhya bhojan, itihaas ka pehla aabadi visfot; Bhimbetka (MP) — saikdon chitrit chattaan aashray, World Heritage.

Neolithic Revolution — kheti ki kranti

Mausam aur bhojan ko pehchaan kar insaan ne jaanwar aur paudhe paalna shuru kiya — bhojan khoje nahi, ugaaye! Isliye ise 'badlaav' nahi, 'kranti (revolution)' kehte hain. Kisaan bhojan banaane-process karne ke auzaar, chikne (polished) pathar ke auzaar, alag-alag mitti ke bartan, aur pehle gaon banaaye — shehron ki neev. Ye har jagah ek saath nahi hua: West Asia (gehu-jau), Africa (bajra), India (millets; Ganga maidaan mein chaawal), China (millets, Yangtze mein chaawal). Kram: Palaeolithic → Mesolithic → Neolithic → Chalcolithic (taamba + pathar) → Bronze Age (taamba + tin) → Iron Age.

Mehrgarh — India ka pehla kisaan gaon

Mehrgarh (Bolan nadi, aaj Pakistan) — sabse purana Neolithic sthal aur pehla kheti wala gaon, ~7000 BCE. Dhoop mein sukhaayi eenton ke ghar, anaaj godaam, kabrein, lapis lazuli, carnelian, shankh ke gehne; gehu-jau, bhed-bakri aur zebu (kubad wala desi bail). ~4000 BCE tak taamba — Chalcolithic. 2500 BCE tak upmahadweep ke zyaadatar hisse mein Neolithic kisaan samudaay; Sanganakallu (granite pahaadi par, ~2000 BCE).

Early Harappan se Harappa tak

Taambe ke auzaar + Sindhu aur Ghaggar-Sarasvati ke upjaau maidaan → zyada upaj aur samriddhi. Bhirrana, Kunal mein pre-Harappan daur shaayad 7000–5500 BCE tak! Bartan, moti, shankh ki choodiyan, terracotta, taamba — sab mein nirantarta; chaardeewaari, mohar, lipi ki shuruaat bhi shaayad yahin — isliye 'Early Harappan'. Kram: Neolithic 7000 → Chalcolithic 4500 → Bronze 3300 → Mature Harappan 2600–1900 BCE → Late 1300 BCE. Kalibangan ke khet mein aade-tirchhe kund — do fasal, jaise aaj Rabi-Kharif — 5,000 saal purani parampara! Baat (weights) binary (1, 2, 4, 8, 16…) chhoti ikaiyon ke liye, aur 10 ke guna badi ke liye — vyapaar ke liye ek maanak.

Mesopotamia — 'nadiyon ke beech ki dharti'

Chaar badi kaansa yug sabhyataayein nadi maidaanon mein: Harappa (Sindhu-Sarasvati), Mesopotamia (Euphrates-Tigris), Egypt (Nile), China (Huang He). Mesopotamia (Greek: 'beech ki dharti') — aaj Iraq, Kuwait, Turkey-Iran ke hisse; Fertile Crescent. Chaar daur: Sumerian (Ur; pehle baandh-nahar, kachchi-pakki eent; ziggurat — seedhidaar piramid mandir jiske chaaron or shehar basa; ~3200 BCE pehli likhaavat cuneiform — geeli mitti par keel jaise nishaan, Latin cuneus = keel; pahiya gaadi, paal naav; 60 ki ginti → 60 minute ka ghanta, 360° ka gola!), Akkadian (2334 BCE; duniya ka pehla vanshaanugat samrajya; raja Sargon ke lekhon mein Dilmun (Bahrain), Magan (Oman), Meluhha (= Harappa!) se vyapaar), Assyrian (2154 BCE, Assur), Babylonian (Hammurabi, 1792 BCE — Code of Hammurabi, niyamon ka sangrah; Hittites ke hamlon se patan). Usi samay Harappa ke gabarband (check dam), Dholavira ke jalaashay aur Lothal ka dockyard! Epic of Gilgamesh — sabse purani kahaaniyon mein.

Egypt — 'Kemet', kaali mitti ki dharti

Egyptian apne desh ko Kemet ('kaali') kehte — Nile ki baadh se bichhi kaali upjaau mitti. Itihaas papyrus (papyrus paudhe ki patti dabaa kar bana kaagaz) se; Papyrus Ebers mein 700+ ilaaj; 2000 BCE ki library mein 'Sindbad' aur Cinderella ke sabse purane roop! Rosetta Stone (1799, Pierre Bouchard) par teen lipiyan — ek Greek; 1822 mein Champollion ne hieroglyphs padhe. Calendar: 3 ritu × 4 mahine; 365 din (12×30 + 5), Sirius taare par. Nile ka paani khaaiyon-jalaashayon mein — saamuhik mehnat se sthaaniya sarkar. Pharaoh raja; mastaba ek ke upar ek → pyramid (Saqqara ka step pyramid); ka (aatma ka saathi) ke liye shav ka mummification. Samaaj ka pyramid: Pharaoh → afsar-pujaari → zameendaar-kaarigar-vyapaari → gulam. Mahilaayein sampatti rakh sakti; Cleopatra 18 ki umar mein rani.

China — Pili nadi ki sabhyata

Huang He (Yellow River) aur Yangtze; Neolithic ~7000 BCE; kaansa ~2000 BCE; shehar ~1600 BCE. Vansh: Shang (1600–1046 BCE), Zhou (1046–256 BCE; raja-pujaari, par praja khush na ho to hataaye ja sakte!; afsar teerandaazi, ghudsawari, ganit, likhaavat, sangeet ki pariksha se chune), Qin (221–206 BCE; desh ko joda; naam 'China' shaayad isi se), Han. Pehli jaankaari oracle bones — haddi/kachhue ke khol garam karke daraaron se bhavishya. Lipi logographic — chinh poore shabd (insaan, ped). Jade ki cheezein (bajne wali jade machhli!), kaansa ke bartan. Great Wall — 680 BCE se 17th sadi tak. Resham Neolithic se; Han kaal mein itna vyapaar ki raasta = Silk Route. Pehli kaagazi mudra aur pariksha se civil service — China ki den. Seekh: sabhi sabhyataayein alag-alag nadi maidaanon mein bani, par sabne kheti, shehar, likhaavat aur prashasan viksit kiye.

State and Society up to 1000 CE

Vedic jana se mahajanapada aur samrajya tak — sabha-samiti, raja ke kartavya, prashasan, dharma, varna-jati, mahilaayein, shiksha aur arthvyavastha.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Society aur State — farak kya?

Society (samaaj) = ek jagah, sanskriti aur 'apnapan' saajha karne wale logon ke rishton ka jaal — parivaar, vivaah jaisi sansthaayein; ye mukhyatah reeti-riwaaj se chalta hai. State (raajya) = niyam-kaanoon par chalne wali sangathit raajnaitik vyavastha — shaasak aur praja ke haq-kartavya, shaasan ke tareeke, kaanoon lagu karne wali sansthaayein. Is daur ki khaas baat: 2nd millennium BCE se Rigveda jaise likhit/maukhik granth milte hain, jo archaeology ke saath milkar poori tasveer dikhaate hain.

Chaar Veda aur unke chaar hisse

Rigveda — sabse purana; 1,028 sukta; devtaaon ki stuti aur srishti, janm-mrityu jaise gehre vichaar. Yajurveda — yajna ki vidhi, gadya mein vyakhya. Samaveda — Rigveda ke mantra gaane ke liye sajaaye; Bharatiya sangeet ke saat swar ki neev! Atharvaveda — buraai door karne aur shaaririk-maansik rogon ke ilaaj se jude mantra. Har Veda ke 4 hisse: Samhita (mantra), Brahmana (anushthaan ki gadya vyakhya), Aranyaka (aranya = jungle; vanvaasi rishiyon ka chintan), Upanishad (Atman aur Brahman). Dhyaan: Brahman = vichaar, Brahmana = granth, Brahmin = varna! Rigveda ki rachna Sapta-Sindhu (Indus, uski 5 sahaayak, Sarasvati) mein; tithi par bahas (5th se 2nd millennium BCE).

Vedic raajneeti — raja bhi, sabha bhi

Samaaj jana (kul) mein — Rigveda mein ~30; paanch milkar Panchajana: Yadu, Turvasha, Puru, Anu, Druhyu. 'Bharata' naam bhi Bharata jana se. Raja = kul ka mukhiya — yuddh mein netritva, raksha. Teen sabhaayein: Sabha — chhoti, chune hue vishisht log, neeti-raajneeti; Samiti — badi, janta ki sabha, yuddh-raajneeti par charcha; Vidhata — lok-jamaavda, nyay ka kaam. Aaj jaise sansad PM par lagaam rakhti hai, waise hi tab sabhaayein raja par — logon ki bhaagidaari ka vichaar bahut purana hai!

Lohe ne badla khel — janapada se mahajanapada

1st millennium BCE mein Ganga kshetra mein bastiyan badhin — lohe ke auzaar, zyada anaaj, PGW (Painted Grey Ware) bartan; madhya Ganga mein chamakdaar kaale NBPW (Northern Black Polished Ware). Phir Janapada ('jahan jana ne pair rakha') — rishte ke saath zameen ki pehchaan (~1000–600 BCE). ~600 BCE–300 CE mein 16 Mahajanapada — Kamboja, Gandhara, Matsya, Kuru, Shurasena, Panchala, Vatsa, Kosala, Kashi, Malla, Vrijji, Magadha, Anga, Chedi, Avanti, Ashmaka. Magadha (Bihar) sabse taaqatwar → Maurya samrajya. Do tarah: rajya (raja) aur gana/sangha (ganrajya). Deccan mein Maurya ke baad Satavahana (~500 saal). Dakshin mein Chola (baagh chinh), Chera/Keralaputra (dhanush), Pandya (machhli), Satiyaputra — 'Tamilakam ke Vendar'; jaankaari Sangam sahitya (300 BCE–300 CE) se.

Raja ke kartavya aur 'chakravarti' ka sapna

Arthashastra: 'raja khud mehnati ho, tabhi afsar mehnat karte hain'. Yajurveda ki rajyabhishek shapath: taaqatwar-kamzor ka nishpaksh nyay, aapdaaon se raksha, praja ka bhala. Mahabharata ka Shanti Parva — naitik aacharan. Raja khaandaani hota, par chuna ya hataaya jaane ke bhi udaharan — satta poori nahi thi. Poore upmahadweep ki pehchaan: Jambudvipa, Bharatavarsha, Ashvamedha-Rajasuya yajna, Prithivi, chakravarti kshetra. Ashoka: meri koshishon se 'Jambudvipa' mein adhyaatmik badlaav. Arthashastra: Prithivi = Himalaya se samundar tak = chakravarti (sarvochch samraat) ka kshetra. Chera raja Nedunjeral Adan ne Himalaya tak jeet ka daava kiya; Chola Rajendra I — 'Gangaikonda'.

Saptanga aur mantri-parishad

Kautilya ka raajya ek jeevit shareer — 7 ang (Saptanga): swami (raja), amatya (mantri-afsar), janapada (zameen + log), durga (qile wale shehar), kosha (khazaana), danda (sena, kaanoon), mitra (dost raajya). 'Ek pahiya gaadi nahi chalaata' — raja akela nahi, mantri-parishad (khazaanchi, mukhya tax adhikaari, kaanooni salaahkaar, senapati) ke saath; Ashoka ke lekh mein raja ki anupasthiti mein parishad ke faisle! Junagadh shilalekh (Girnar) — ek hi chattaan par teen raajvansh: Ashoka (Prakrit, dhamma), Rudradaman I (~150 CE, Sanskrit; Sudarshana jheel ki marammat), Skandagupta (5th sadi; phir marammat) — ~700 saal ka itihaas ek pathar par!

Prashasan — praant se gaon tak

Satavahana: ahara (prabhaag) → gaon (gramika mukhiya). District afsar (pradeshika) bankers, karvaan netaon, kaarigaron, lekhakon se salaah lete. 300–800 CE vikendrikaran: praant — uttar mein bhukti, dakshin mein mandalam; prabhaag — vishaya/bhoga vs kottam/valanadu; zila — adhishthana/pattana vs nadu; tehsil jaise — vithi vs pattala/kurram; sabse neeche gaon. Gupta: naya mantri sandhivigrahika ('yuddh aur shaanti'), kumaramatya; Damodarpur taamrapatra — zila daftar mein 5: mukhya adhikaari, mukhya banker, mukhya karvaan vyapaari, mukhya kaarigar, lagaan pramukh! Pallava — kar-mukt Brahmadeya gaon; gaon sabha ki samitiyan variyam. Chalukya (Badami) — agraharam (Aihole, Badami shiksha kendra). Kannauj ke liye tripartite struggle (Pratihara, Pala, Rashtrakuta). Chola: mandalam → valanadu → nadu → ur. Uttaramerur (Parantaka I, 10th sadi) — kudavolai (matdaan ghada): taad patron par naam, bachcha nikaale; ummeedwar ki 'imaandaar kamaai' zaroori.

Dharma — 'jo sabko dhaaran kare'

Kaanoon aur jeevan ki neev naitikta. Samatva — sab shareer ek hi padarth se, sab chetna ek param chetna ka roop; acchha raja wo jo 'sab jeevon ke kalyaan' ke liye kaam kare. Rita — Rigveda mein brahmaand ki vyavastha, prakriti aur samaaj ka santulan. Dharma = dharm nahi, balki kartavya, nyay, sadaachaar; shabd 'dhri' (dhaaran karna) se — wahi jad 'dharati' ki! Bhishma: 'jo jeevon ko dhaaran kare, wahi dharma'; 'lohe ka dharma doobna, lakdi ka tairna'. Pali mein dhamma — Ashoka ne naitik aacharan, ahinsa, karuna failaayi. Kamandaka ka Nitisara, Banabhatta ki Kadambari.

Varna, jati aur badalte pesha

Shuru mein pehchaan kai baaton se — nasl, ilaaka, gaon, gotra, bhasha, aur khaas kar pesha; pesha lachila tha. Rigveda 9.112.3: 'main kavi, pita vaidya, maa chakki chalaane wali' — ek hi parivaar mein alag pesha! Chaar varna ka pehla zikr Purushasukta (Rigveda Book 10). Varna kaam par aadharit (functional) — gyaan sabse oopar, phir raajnaitik shakti, phir dhan. Sutta Nipata: 'koi janm se brahmin nahi, karm se hota hai'. Dheere-dheere lachak ghati aur jati bani (antar-vivaah, endogamy, ilaaka) — varna 4 hi, jati anginat. Social mobility: Nanda, Maurya, Shunga, Satavahana, Gupta — alag-alag prishthbhoomi ke raja; Mandsaur lekh (473 CE) — Gujarat se aaye resham bunkar teerandaazi aur jyotish bhi jaante the! Sangam ka Tolkappiyam — Arasar (raja), Vanigar (vyapaari), Velar (kisaan), Antanar.

Parivaar, ashrama aur purushartha

Seedhi: kula (parivaar) → grama → vish (vishpati) → jana (raja). Gotra — ek rishi se judi pitravanshi pehchaan. Jeevan ke 4 ashrama: brahmacharya (vidyarthi), grihastha, vanaprastha, sanyasa; aur 16 sanskaar. Chaar purushartha: dharma, artha, kama, moksha — dhan (artha) achchha hai, agar dharma ke raaste kamaaya jaaye.

Mahilaayein — Vedic rishika se Chola raani tak

Vedic kaal mein mahilaayein padhti, kuch anushthaan karti, rath daud aur sabha mein jaati. Rigveda ke sukta rishikaaon ke: Apala, Vishvavara, Ghosha, Lopamudra; devi Usha, Aditi. Manusmriti: 'yatra naryastu pujyante ramante tatra devatah'. Sthiti samay ke saath ghati-badhi. Prabhavati Gupta (regent, apne naam se zameen daan), votive lekhon mein daan-daatri mahilaayein. Sangam: kheti, pashupaalan, kataai, machhli, namak; kaviyitri Avvaiyar, kumhaarin kavi Vennikuyattiyar. Chola raani Sembiyan Mahadevi — mandir nirmaan.

Dharm, bhakti aur shiksha

Vedic devta prakriti se jude — suraj, barish, agni, dharti, usha; aaj bhi Chhath, Makar Sankranti par surya pooja. Prakriti = pavitra, sirf sansaadhan nahi. Madhya 1st millennium BCE mein sanyasi — parivrajaka, bhikshu, shramana; Buddha, Mahavira. Phir Vishnu, Shiv, Shakti jaise ishtadev → Bhakti — bina jatil reeti, sabke liye; 6th sadi Tamil kshetra mein 12 Alvar (Vishnu) aur 63 Nayanar (Shiv). Shiksha: satya, dhairya, vinamrata, indriya sanyam; Veda, vyakaran, tark, ganit, chikitsa, khagol + sangeet, nritya, chitrakala, teerandaazi, yoga. Guru-shishya parampara — gurukul. Kendra: Takshashila, Nalanda (427 CE), Vallabhi (480 CE), Vikramashila, Kanchipuram, Sharada Peeth… Granth: Panini ki Ashtadhyayi, Pingala ka Chhandashastra, Charaka, Sushruta Samhita, Kalidasa, Tirukkural, Silappadikaram, Manimekalai.

Arthvyavastha — kheti, sinchaai, vyapaar, guild

Maurya: zameen ka tax aam taur par 1/6; chaaragaah saajhe; kuch jungle kaanoon se surakshit. Milindapanho — kheti ke 8 charan; Deccan ki kaali mitti → kapaas; Amarakosha mein jungle, fasal, khaad ka adhyaay; Chera — kathal, kaali mirch, haldi. Sinchaai: Chandragupta ke governor Pushyagupta ne Sudarshana jheel par baandh banaaya; Karikala ka Kallanai aaj bhi chalu. Raaste: Uttarapatha, Dakshinapatha; bandargah: Muziris, Kaveripattinam, Arikamedu, Masulipatnam; Rome tak vyapaar. Guilds (shreni) — Jatakon mein 18 prakaar; quality aur daam tay, guild court, bank jaise kaam! Nashik lekh — Ushavadata ne bunkar guild mein 3,000 karshapana jama kiye, byaaj se vihar chale. Sanchi ke darwaaze par Vidisha ke haathi-daant kaarigaron ka lekh — wahi moortikaar the!

Democracy

Loktantra ki jaden Vedic sabha-samiti tak; iske siddhant, prakaar, duniya ke model, India ka 'jeevit loktantra', chunautiyan aur hamari zimmedaari.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Democracy matlab kya?

Democracy (loktantra) = aisi sarkar jismein taaqat ka asli srot janta hai. Log free aur fair elections se apne pratinidhi chunte hain. Iski neev: aazaadi, samaanta, nyay, adhikaar aur kartavya. India duniya ka sabse bada loktantra hai — 140 crore se zyada aabaadi aur 96.8 crore voters (2024). Hamara loktantra Samvidhan se chalta hai — 26 Nov 1949 ko apnaaya, 26 Jan 1950 se laagu.

Jaden bahut purani hain

Loktantra ka vichaar India mein achaanak nahi aaya. Vedic kaal mein Sabha, Samiti, Vidhata — milkar faisle. Gana/Sangha (ganrajya) bhi the. Raja akela nahi — sabha, mantri aur afsaron se salaah leta tha. Rigveda ka Aikyamatya Sukta kehta hai: 'tumhari salaah ek ho, sabha ek ho, man ek ho' — yani milkar sochna, charcha aur ekta. Buddha ke Bauddha Sangha mein bahas hoti, neta chuna jaata aur voting se faisle hote.

Gulaami se Samvidhan tak

Videshi hamlon aur phir British raj ne logon ki bhaagidaari chheen li. Par aazaadi ki ladaai ne loktantrik vichaar phir se jagaaye. 1946 mein Constituent Assembly bani; 2 saal, 11 mahine, 18 din mein duniya ka sabse lamba likhit samvidhan taiyaar. Drafting Committee ke chairman — Dr. B.R. Ambedkar. Samvidhan ko patthar ki lakeer nahi, badalne layak rakha — Article 368 se amendment ho sakta hai, par mool mulya nahi tootte.

Siddhant 1–2: Janta raja hai, kaanoon sabse upar

Popular Sovereignty — sarkar ki taaqat janta se aati hai. 18 saal+ har nagrik ko secret ballot se vote ka haq = Universal Adult Franchise. Rule of Law — koi bhi kaanoon se upar nahi; sab ke liye barabar kaanoon. Jhagde adaalat mein 'procedure established by law' se suljhte hain, dande ya pehchaan se nahi. Article 21: kisi ko bhi jeevan ya aazaadi se kaanooni prakriya ke bina vanchit nahi kiya ja sakta. Do shabd yaad rakho: Equality before law (sab barabar) aur Equal protection of law (ek jaisi sthiti walon se ek jaisa vyavhaar).

Siddhant 3: Chhe Fundamental Rights

1) Samaanta (Art 14–18) 2) Swatantrata (19–22) 3) Shoshan ke viruddh (23–24) 4) Dharm ki swatantrata (25–28) 5) Sanskritik aur shaikshik (29–30) 6) Samvaidhanik upchaar (Art 32). Haq toota to Article 32 (Supreme Court) ya 226 (High Court) mein ja sakte ho. Ye adaalat mein laagu hote hain, par reasonable restrictions ke saath. Right to Education (21A) 2009 mein jodha — 6–14 saal ke bachchon ko muft aur zaroori shiksha.

Siddhant 4: Shaktiyon ka bantwara

Teen ang: Legislature kaanoon banaati, Executive laagu karti, Judiciary vyakhya karti. Isse taaqat ek jagah jama nahi hoti — checks and balances. Sansad samvidhan badal sakti hai, par adaalat dekhti hai ki wo samvidhan ki aatma ke khilaaf to nahi. Galat kaanoon ko adaalat unconstitutional ghoshit kar sakti hai. Judiciary nishpaksh aur swatantra hai; PIL (Public Interest Litigation) se aam aadmi ke liye nyay aasaan hua. Socho — agar ek hi ang kaanoon banaaye, laagu kare aur faisla bhi de, to? Taanashahi!

Siddhant 5–7: Jawabdehi, kai parties, kamzor ki raksha

Accountability & Transparency — sarkar janta ko jawab deti hai; elections, bahas, civil society se jaanch. RTI Act 2005 se nagrik sarkari jaankaari maang sakte hain. Sansad ka Question Hour (pehla ghanta) — sawaal poochh kar sarkar par nazar. Multi-Party System — kai parties; jo party ya gathbandhan 50% se zyada seats laaye, sarkar banaaye; sabse bada virodhi samooh = opposition. Niyam: Representation of the People Act, 1951. India mein 2,800+ registered parties! Kamzor varg ki raksha — Article 46: SC/ST aur kamzor vargon ke shiksha-aarthik hit ka khaas dhyaan.

Kaun si sanstha kaunsa mulya bachaati hai

Kaanoon banana → Sansad/Vidhan Sabha/local bodies (pratinidhitva, bahas, asahmati). Chunaav → Election Commission (bhaagidaari, samaanta). Nyay → Courts (rule of law). Charcha → Media, civil society (abhivyakti ki aazaadi). Jawabdehi → CAG, CIC, Lokpal, CVC. Vikendrikaran → Gram/shehri local bodies. Federalism → Centre-State mein taaqat ka bantwara (likhit samvidhan se). Media ko 'loktantra ka chautha stambh' kehte hain — janta ki awaaz.

Democracy ke prakaar

Direct Democracy — nagrik khud zyaadatar faisle lete hain; bade desh mein mushkil; misaal Switzerland. Representative (indirect) — log pratinidhi chunte hain, samay-samay par chunaav; misaal India. Representative ke do roop: Parliamentary — Executive (PM aur mantri) legislature ka hissa aur usi ko jawabdeh; log executive ko seedha nahi chunte — India, Canada. Presidential — Executive legislature se alag; President ko janta chunti, wahi janta ko jawabdeh — USA. School misaal: sab bachche ek jagah vote karein = direct; har class pratinidhi bheje = representative; sirf chune hue ummeedwar ladein aur virodh dabaaya jaaye = asli loktantra nahi!

Duniya ke model ek nazar mein

India — parliamentary; President (Head of State), PM (Head of Government); likhit samvidhan, federalism. Canada — parliamentary; Governor-General (Monarch ki taraf se). UK — constitutional monarchy; Monarch Head of State, PM sarkar ka mukhiya; unlikhit samvidhan, sansad sarvochch. Switzerland — direct democracy; Federal Council ke paas executive. USA — presidential; President hi sarkar ka mukhiya; do badi parties. Sovereignty — Latin superanus = 'sarvochch'.

India ka 'jeevit loktantra' — numbers dekho

Ek MP lagbhag 25 lakh logon ka pratinidhi (2019). Chunaav 22 scheduled bhaashaon mein; chunaav chinh taaki anpadh bhi khud vote de sake. 10 lakh+ polling stations — pahaad, jungle, registaan, dweep — kabhi ek voter ke liye bhi! Sabse khaas — itni vividhta (bhaasha, dharm, aarthik sthiti) ke log ek hi loktantrik dhaanche mein. Ye sirf sankhya nahi, logon ki lagaataar bhaagidaari hai.

Zameen se loktantra — Gram Sabha ki kahaaniyan

Teen star: Union, State, Local. North-East mein Autonomous District Councils (ADCs) — jan-jaatiya reeti-riwaaj bachaane ke liye kaanoon aur nyay ki shakti. Baaki rajyon ke tribal ilaakon mein PESA Act 1996 — Gram Sabha sabse badi faisla-sanstha. Jethipura (Gujarat) — 2019 mein Nanaji Deshmukh Rashtriya Gaurav Gram Sabha Puraskar; special Gram Sabha, Mahila Sabha, poora quorum (kam se kam haaziri). South Manubankul (Tripura) — 'women-friendly panchayat'. Loktantra sirf vote nahi — cooperatives, trade unions, NGOs mein bhi.

Mahilaayein aur vote

Britain mein mahilaaon ko poora vote 1928, USA mein 1920 — lambi ladaai ke baad. India mein 1950 se hi sabko barabar vote — bina andolan ke! Par vote ka haq barabar bhaagidaari ki guarantee nahi — samaajik soch rukaawat banti hai. Article 243(d) — Panchayat mein kam se kam 1/3 seats mahilaaon ki; 21 rajya + 2 UT ne 50% kar diya. Article 243(t) — Municipality mein 1/3; 17 rajya + 1 UT mein 50% (2023).

Chunautiyan aur Emergency ka sabak

Chunautiyan: anpadhta, fake news (social media), garibi, kshetravaad, laingik asamaanta, bhedbhaav, kaanoon ka kamzor amal. Sarkari sampatti todna, afwaah failaana, udaaseenta — sab loktantra ko kamzor karte hain. Emergency 1975–77: Indira Gandhi sarkar ne 'aantarik ashaanti' ke naam par laagu ki; zyaadatar Fundamental Rights nilambit, press par censorship, netaaon ki giraftaari. Jayaprakash Narayan (Lok Nayak) ke andolan ne Bihar-Gujarat mein chhatron ko joda. 1977 ke chunaav mein sattadhari dal haara — loktantra ki taaqat dikhi. (Art 352 = National, 356 = President's Rule, 360 = Financial Emergency.)

Tum aur loktantra

Achchha nagrik = jaankaar nagrik — akhbaar padho, news dekho, internet zimmedaari se. NSS, NCC, Bharat Scouts & Guides se seva aur teamwork seekho. Samvidhan samjho, Fundamental Duties nibhaao, media literate bano (fake news pehchaano), school-mohalle mein netritva lo. Yaad rakho: loktantra ki asli taaqat uske nagrik hain.

Theme P · Political Science

Elections

Chunaav kyon zaroori, vote ko seat mein badalne ke tareeke (FPTP, PR, STV), chunaavi kaanoon, Delimitation, Election Commission, political parties aur chunautiyan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Chunaav kyon? — CR wali kahaani

Socho Class 8 mein Gurmat CR chuni gayi. Kya wo bina naye chunaav ke Class 9 mein bhi CR rahe? Nahi! Waise hi sarkar bhi apna samay (term) khatam hone ke baad bina janta se poochhe nahi chal sakti. Aur agar sirf ek hi party lade, to chunaav ka matlab hi kya? Loktantra ka sabse zaroori hissa = samay-samay par chunaav (periodic elections). Isse milti hai — Accountability (jawabdehi), Representation (pratinidhitva), Legitimacy (sarkar ki maanyata) aur Equality (har vote barabar).

Direct aur Indirect chunaav

Direct — nagrik seedha vote dekar chunte hain: Lok Sabha, Vidhan Sabha, Panchayat/Nagar Nigam — har 5 saal. Indirect — nagrik pratinidhi chunte hain, phir wo pratinidhi neta chunte hain: President, Vice President, Rajya Sabha. Ek mazedaar shabd: Psephology = chunaav ka vaigyanik adhyayan; Greek psephos = kankad (pebble) — prachin Greece mein kankad se vote padte the!

Vote ko seat mein kaise badlein? — teen tareeke

Teen kaalpanik desh socho. Haritbhumi — FPTP (First-Past-The-Post): har kshetra mein jise sabse zyada vote mile wo jeete, chaahe 50% se kam ho. Party A 40%, B 35%, C 25% → A jeeti. Ratnadweep — Majority system: jeetne ke liye 50% se zyada chahiye; na mile to top-2 mein doosra round. Round 2 mein Y ne 55% laakar jeeta. Swarnalok — PR (Proportional Representation): log party ko vote dete hain, seats vote ke anupaat mein — M 40%, N 35%, O 15%, P 10% → 100 mein se 40, 35, 15, 10 seats. Taaqat sab mein bati.

India kaunsa tareeka apnaata hai?

Samvidhan banaane walon ne FPTP chuna — Lok Sabha aur Vidhan Sabha ke liye. Rajya Sabha, President, Vice President — PR se. Chhe rajyon mein do sadan (bicameral) hain — Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh. Upari sadan = Vidhan Parishad; neeche = Vidhan Sabha. Vidhan Parishad ke sadasya Vidhan Sabha, local bodies, graduates aur teachers chunte hain; kuch ko Governor kala, vigyaan, samaaj seva, sahitya, cooperative se manonit karta hai. Iska chunaav STV (Single Transferable Vote) se.

STV — preference wala vote

Voter ummeedwaron ko 1, 2, 3… preference deta hai. Pehle first preference gine jaate hain. Jo quota paar kare, jeet gaya. Quota = (kul valid vote ÷ (bharne wali seats + 1)) + 1. Sabse kam vote wala bahar, uske vote un ballot par likhe second preference ko transfer. Ye tab tak chale jab tak saari seats bhar na jaayein. Yani tumhara vote bekaar nahi jaata — aage 'transfer' ho jaata hai!

Chunaav ke kaanoon

RPA 1950 (Representation of the People Act) — seats ka bantwara, kshetron ki seema (delimitation), voter list banana-sudhaarna, 18+ sabko bina bhedbhaav vote. RPA 1951 — baaki sab: nomination, prachaar, voting, chunaavi jhagde; saath hi bhrasht aacharan ki list — vote ke badle gift/vaada; dharm, jaati, nasl, samudaay, bhaasha ke naam par vote maangna; sarkari karmchaari (gazetted officer, judge, sena, police, excise) se madad lena. Teesra: Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952.

Delimitation — seemaayein kyon badalti hain

Kshetron ki seema pakki nahi — aabaadi badalne par badalti hai. Warna ek MP 5 lakh ka aur doosra 25 lakh ka pratinidhi hoga — barabari khatam! Delimitation = har rajya mein Lok Sabha-Vidhan Sabha ki seats ki sankhya aur seemaayein tay karna, taaki seat : aabaadi ka anupaat lagbhag barabar rahe. Article 82 ke tahat Delimitation Commission. Ab tak chaar — 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002.

Election Commission of India (ECI)

ECI ek swatantra, sthaayi, samvaidhanik sanstha — 25 January 1950 ko bani. Articles 324–329. Ye Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, Vidhan Sabha, Vidhan Parishad, President, Vice President ke chunaav karaati hai. Rajya star par Chief Electoral Officer; zile mein DM, SDM, Tahsildar ban jaate hain District Election Officer, Returning Officer, Electoral Registration Officer. 25 January = National Voters' Day — voters' pledge: bina dar, bina laalach, bina dharm-jaati ke prabhaav ke vote denge.

ECI ke kaam 1: Voter list aur taareekh

Electoral roll — ghar-ghar enumerator bhejkar yogya voters ka data; har polling station ke hisaab se list. Naam list mein nahi = vote nahi! SIR (Special Intensive Revision) — list update, jaanch, sudhaar: naye 18 saal walon ko jodna; mrityu, ghar badalna, duplicate, laapata — naam hataana; dava-aapatti ka mauka, phir final list. Taareekh — 5 saal poore ya sadan bhang hone par; dhyaan: mausam, fasal ka mausam, exams, tyohaar.

ECI ke kaam 2: Parties, chinh, nishpakshta

Sirf registered parties lad sakti hain; nirdaliya (independent) bhi lad sakte hain. ECI parties ko andar chunaav karaane ko kehta hai (inner-party democracy). Parties ki shreni: National, State, Registered-Unrecognised (RUPP). National-State parties ko chunaav chinh. Chinh ke jhagdon mein ECI quasi-judicial (adhh-nyayik) kaam karta hai. Sabko saath lena: 85+ buzurg aur 40%+ viklangta walon ke liye ghar se voting — 2024 mein pehli baar poore desh mein; Braille wali EVM, wheelchair, PVTG aur transgender voters ka dhyaan.

ECI ke digital auzaar

ETPBS — service voters (sena aadi) ke liye electronic postal ballot, kahin se bhi. Voter Helpline App — registration, sudhaar, jaankaari. Saksham — viklang voters ke liye. cVIGIL — Model Code of Conduct ka ullanghan dekho to turant report; flying squad location se pahunchta hai. Suvidha — ummeedwaron ke liye nomination forms aur rally/loudspeaker ki permission. ERONET — forms aur e-roll database. Sugam — chunaav ke liye gaadiyon ka hisaab. IEVP — videshi chunaav sansthaaon se sahyog; 28 EMBs + IFES, International IDEA, UN se MoU.

Political parties — loktantra ki reedh

Voter ummeedwaron, unki neeti aur vision ke baare mein zyaadatar parties se jaanta hai. Parties: janmat sangathit karti hain, mudde uthaati, manifesto banaati, chunaav ladti; jeetne wali sarkar banaati, haarne wali opposition banke jawabdehi tay karti. India mein multi-party system — desh ki bhaasha, sanskriti, dharm, kshetra ki vividhta ka aaina. 1967 — ek-party dabdabe ka ant, gathbandhan ka daur; 1977 — Janata Party ki pehli kendriya gathbandhan sarkar. Baad mein NDA (BJP ke netritva) aur UPA (INC; 2023 mein bhang hokar INDIA gathbandhan).

Dal-badal (Defection) aur kaanoon

Kisi party ke ticket par jeetkar party chhodna ya party ke nirdesh (whip) ke khilaaf vote dena = Defection. Kuch ise avsarvaad maante, kuch antaraatma ki awaaz. Anti-Defection Law — 1985, 52nd Amendment — aisa karne wala sadasya ayogya ho sakta hai; faisla Speaker/Chairman karta hai. Maqsad: sarkar mein sthirta.

National vs State party — pehchaan ki shartein

National party (koi ek): 4+ rajyon mein 6% vote + Lok Sabha mein 4 seat; ya Lok Sabha ki 2% seats, kam se kam 3 rajyon se; ya 4 rajyon mein State party. State party (koi ek): Vidhan Sabha mein 6% vote + 2 seat; ya Lok Sabha mein us rajya se 6% vote + 1 seat; ya Vidhan Sabha ki 3% seats ya 3 seats (jo zyada); ya har 25 Lok Sabha seats par 1 seat; ya 8% vote. Baaki = RUPP.

Chunautiyan aur hal

96.8 crore voters, lakhon booth, sainkdon parties — kaam bahut bada. Chunautiyan: galat soochna, fake news, dhamki (intimidation), paise ki taaqat. Hal: RPA 1950-51, Model Code of Conduct, EVM, VVPAT (vote ki parchi dikhti hai), jaagrukta abhiyaan. Bahas ka mudda: 'One Nation, One Election' — fayde aur seemaayein dono socho. Vote dena adhikaar bhi, kartavya bhi — chunaav loktantra ki aatma hain.

Theme E · Economics

Building Blocks in Economics: The Problem of Choice

Ichchhaayein anant, saadhan seemit — isliye chunna padta hai; opportunity cost, PPC, teen mool sawaal aur teen arthik pranaaliyan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Roz ke chunaav hi Economics hain

Pocket money se snacks lo ya joote ke liye bachaao? Library mein kitaab ki 5 copy, padhna chaahte 20 bachche — pehle kise mile? Kisaan kaunsi fasal boye — mitti, baarish aur bazaar dekhkar? Company zyada mazdoor rakhe ya machine? Sarkar highway banaaye ya hospital? Ye sab arthik chunaav (economic choices) hain — vyakti, company aur sarkar sabko karne padte hain.

Needs vs Wants

Needs (zaroorat) — jinke bina jeena mushkil: khaana, paani, ghar. Wants (ichchha) — jo achchha lage par zaroori nahi: gadget, ghoomna, mehenga saamaan. Insaan ki ichchhaayein anant aur badalti rehti hain — cycle mili to bike chahiye, bike mili to car! Market = jahan cheezein aur sevaayein khareedi-bechi jaati hain — asli dukaan ya online.

Saadhan seemit, upyog anek

Ichchha poori karne ke liye resources (saadhan) chahiye — land, labour, capital, technology (Class 8 ke factors of production). Saadhan prakritik (paani, koyla) ya maanav-nirmit (machine, technology) — par dono seemit. Aur ek saadhan ke kai upyog: steel se medical upkaran, hawaai jahaz ya fridge — kuch bhi ban sakta hai. Ghar ho ya poora desh — sabko tay karna hai ki seemit saadhan ka sabse achchha upyog kaise ho.

Opportunity Cost — jo chhoda uski keemat

Jab ek vikalp chunte ho to baaki chhodne padte hain. Jo chhoda, uska mulya = Opportunity Cost. Misaal: ₹100 hain — notebook li to racket ke liye bachat chhodi; racket ki bachat hi notebook ki opportunity cost hai. Samay bhi seemit saadhan hai — 1 ghanta game khele to wo ghanta padhaai ka tha! Har faisle ki ek 'chhupi keemat' hoti hai.

PPC — kisaan ka jau aur gehun

Kisaan ke paas seemit zameen-paani-mazdoor. Wo jau (barley) ya gehun (wheat) uga sakta hai. Combinations: A 0 jau – 100 gehun; B 25–90; C 50–70; D 75–40; E 100–0. Graph par x = jau, y = gehun — neeche jaati hui curve = Production Possibility Curve (PPC). A se E jaate hue jau badhta, gehun ghatta — zyada jau ke liye gehun ki qurbaani = jau ki opportunity cost. Dhyaan do: A→B mein 25 kg jau ke liye sirf 10 kg gehun chhoda, par D→E mein 25 kg jau ke liye 40 kg gehun! Curve ke har bindu par saadhan poori tarah, bina barbaadi ke istemaal — maximum output.

Economics — shabd ka matlab

Greek oikonomia = oikos (ghar) + nemein (prabandh) → 'ghar ka prabandh'. Seemit saadhan aur anant ichchhaaon mein parivaar hi nahi, poore desh ko bhi yojana banaani padti hai. Economics padhta hai ki chunaav kaise hote hain aur arthik ikaaiyan — consumer, producer, sarkar, bank — aapas mein kaise judti hain: log kaise kamaate, daulat kaise batti, keemat kaise tay hoti, sarkari neeti bazaar ko kaise badalti.

Data se faisla, andaaze se nahi

Achchhe faisle data aur vishleshan se hote hain. Parivaar paisa baanta hai — zaroori (khaana, dawa, school), gair-zaroori (gehne, manoranjan, hotel) aur bachat. Sarkar tax ki kamaai se sadak aur kalyan yojanaaon ki yojana banaati. Company bazaar ke trend dekhti. Economist kaam karte hain — policy-making (sarkar ko salaah), business consulting, finance (niveshak ko salaah), research aur shiksha. Survey = data jama aur vishleshan ka vyavasthit tareeka.

Economic Survey — desh ka report card

Vitt Mantralaya (Ministry of Finance) har saal banaata, Union Budget se pehle Sansad mein rakha jaata. Isme pichhle saal ka pradarshan — kheti, udyog, sevaayein, rozgaar, mehngaai, shiksha, swasthya, infrastructure — aur aage ki chunautiyan. Ye budget ka blueprint hai aur aam nagrik ko bhi arthvyavastha seedhi bhaasha mein samjhaata hai.

Teen mool sawaal

Anant ichchha + seemit saadhan = Scarcity (kami) → Choices → teen sawaal: 1) Kya banaayein? 2) Kaise banaayein? 3) Kiske liye banaayein? Har arthvyavastha ko inka jawab dena padta hai.

Kya banaayein? — ganna ya mota anaaj

Kisaan paani-khau ganna aur dhaan ugaaye ya sookha-sahne wale millets aur daalein? Ganna = zyada munaafa, chini udyog ko sahara. Millets-daal = paani ki bachat, mitti ki sehat, tikau kheti. Ganna chuna to opportunity cost = bacha hua paani aur achchhi mitti. Yani aaj ka fayda vs kal ki sthirta ka trade-off.

Kiske liye banaayein? — joote ki misaal

Log alag — zaroorat, aamdani, pasand alag. School shoes — saade, majboot, saste. Office shoes — aaraam, formal, achchha leather. Sports shoes — khaas rubber sole, halke, pakad aur lachak. Chappal/casual — roz ke liye, aaraamdaayak aur saste. Grahak ke hisaab se maal bhi badalta — leather = office/zyada aamdani; rubber/synthetic = khilaadi, mazdoor, sasti majboot zaroorat.

Kaise banaayein? — mazdoor ya machine

Labour-intensive — zyada mazdoor, kam machine (kheti, hastshilp). Capital-intensive — zyada machine-technology, kam mazdoor (steel, automobile). Garment factory ki misaal: machine mehngi → mazdoor; machine sasti → automation. Technology achchhi → machine. Designer/customised kapde → kushal kaarigar; mass production → machine. Mazdoor saste aur aasaani se mile → labour-intensive; mehnge/kam → machine. Sarkari kaanoon (labour law, machine par chhoot) bhi asar daalte.

Teen arthik pranaaliyan

Planned economy — sarkar (planning authority) tay karti kya, kitna, kaise, kise, kis daam; zameen, factory, bank, transport zyaadatar sarkari; license-permit ki sakhti → competition kam, quality-innovation ki prerna kam. Misaal: purana Soviet Union, North Korea, Cuba. Market economy — maang aur poorti (demand-supply) se faisle; sarkar sirf football ke referee jaisi — suraksha, kaanoon-vyavastha; daam ya utpaadan control nahi; maalikana private; competition se behtar quality, kam daam. Misaal: USA, Japan, Hong Kong. Mixed economy — dono ka mel; private aur sarkari dono, bade public sector companies bhi. Misaal: India (1991 ke baad), China (1978 ke baad), Germany, Sweden. Sach mein lagbhag har arthvyavastha mixed hai.

Public goods aur India ka safar

Public goods — sabke liye, kisi ko roka nahi jaata, ek ke upyog se doosre ka kam nahi hota: park, sadak, police, street light, buniyaadi shiksha. Mixed economy mein sarkar ka kaam: fair competition ke niyam, consumer protection, paardarshita, public goods, kalyan yojanaayein; bazaar laata hai munaafa, innovation, competition. India — aazaadi ke baad planned jaisa (license, bade public sector); 1991 ke sankat ke baad reforms — kam niyantran, private ko badhaava, videshi vyapar-nivesh khula → zyada bazaar-unmukh, par sarkar ki bhoomika bani rahi.

The Price Puzzle: What Drives the Market

Daam kyon badalte hain? — demand aur supply ke niyam, unhe badalne wale kaaran, market equilibrium, dynamic pricing aur sarkar ka dakhal.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Daam ki paheliyan

Pichhle hafte ke aam ab aadhe daam par kyon? Sabziyan subah mehngi, shaam ko sasti kyon? Pyaaz ka daam har kuch mahine kyon uchhalta? Ek hi flight seat kabhi ₹3,000, kabhi ₹9,000? Malls saal ke kuch samay discount kyon dete? Daam bina wajah nahi badalte — log kya chaahte, kitna maal hai, mausam, tyohaar, trend, kabhi afwaah bhi. Par sabke peeche do badi taaqatein: Demand aur Supply.

Demand kya hai?

Demand = kisi daam par log kitna maal khareedna chaahte HAIN aur khareed SAKTE HAIN. Sirf ichchha demand nahi — saath mein purchasing power (paise ki khareed-shakti) bhi chahiye. Mehel chaahna demand nahi, jab tak paise na hon! Law of Demand: daam badha → maang ghati; daam ghata → maang badhi. Yani ulta rishta (inverse relationship).

Srivalli ke aam — demand schedule aur curve

Season ki shuruaat: aam ₹150/kg → Srivalli ne 1 kg liya. Daam ₹100 → 2 kg. Daam ₹50 → 3 kg. Ye table = demand schedule. Graph par y = daam, x = maatra; bindu A, B, C jodo → neeche ki or jhukti line DD' = demand curve. Shart: baaki cheezein (aamdani, pasand) wahi rahein. Ek vyakti ki maang = individual demand.

Market demand — sabki maang jodo

Alex aur Israt bhi aam khareedte hain. ₹150 par: Srivalli 1 + Alex 2 + Israt 3 = 6 kg. ₹100 par: 2+4+6 = 12 kg. ₹50 par: 3+6+9 = 18 kg. Ye = market demand (sabki individual demand ka jod). Dhyaan do — market curve zyada chapti (flatter) hai: ₹150 se ₹50 hone par Srivalli ki maang sirf 2 kg badhi, par market ki 12 kg! Kai log mile to ek jaisa daam-badlaav bada asar daalta hai.

Daam ke alaawa maang kya badalta? (1)

Naya smartphone mehenga hai phir bhi lambi line! Matlab daam hi sab kuch nahi. Related goods: Substitute (ek doosre ki jagah) — chai-coffee, aam-kela; coffee mehngi → chai ki maang badhi. Complementary (saath istemaal) — phone-earphone, car-petrol, printer-cartridge, movie ticket-popcorn; ticket mehenga → popcorn ki maang bhi giri. Aamdani badhi → zyada ya behtar maal. Pasand — Srivalli ko aam pasand, santra sasta ho tab bhi nahi lengi. Aabaadi — zyada bachche → sports shoes; zyada kaamkaaji → formal shoes; zyada buzurg → aaraamdaayak/orthopaedic joote.

Daam ke alaawa maang kya badalta? (2)

Diminishing marginal utility: pehla aam — waah! doosra — achchha; teesra… ab mann nahi. Har agle aam se milne wala atirikt sukh ghatta, isliye uske liye paisa dene ki ichchha bhi ghatti. Mausam/season — session shuru → kitaabon ki dukaan par bheed; tyohaar → mithaai; sardi → sweater. Bhavishya ke daam ki ummeed — daam girne ki ummeed → khareed taalo (Diwali sale ka intezaar), daam badhne ki ummeed → abhi khareedo.

Supply aur uska niyam

Supply = kisi daam par vikreta kitna maal bechna chaahte aur bech sakte hain. Law of Supply: daam badha → supply badhi; daam ghata → supply ghati — seedha rishta (direct). Kyon? Zyada daam = zyada munaafa, utpaadak zyada banaate, nayi companies bhi aa jaati. Seller A: ₹50 → 1 kg, ₹100 → 2 kg, ₹150 → 3 kg — upar ki or chadhti supply curve. Teen sellers A+B+C: ₹50 → 6, ₹100 → 12, ₹150 → 18 kg = market supply.

Daam ke alaawa supply kya badalta?

Related goods ka daam — gehun sasta, chana mehenga → kisaan agle season zyada chana boyega. Vikretaaon ki sankhya — zyada seller → supply zyada, daam gire; kam seller → daam chadhe. Technology — drip irrigation, weather sensor se paidaavaar badhi; cold storage se aam door ke bazaar tak → supply badhi. Bhavishya ki ummeed — aage maang badhne ki ummeed → zyada banao; aloo ke thok vyapaari peak season mein daam badhne ki ummeed mein abhi maal rok lete. Baaki: input ki keemat, mausam, aapda.

Market Equilibrium — jahan dono mil jaayein

₹40 par: maang 38, supply 6 → Excess demand (kami/shortage) → daam chadhega. ₹150 par: maang 8, supply 43 → Excess supply (surplus) → daam girega. ₹100 par: maang = supply = 12 kg → Market Equilibrium! Graph par demand aur supply curve jahan kaatein (point E) — equilibrium price ₹100, quantity 12 kg. Yahan na kami, na faltu maal — bazaar 'clear'; daam badalne ka koi dabaav nahi.

Asli duniya mein equilibrium?

Kitaab mein equilibrium ek bindu hai, par asli bazaar chalta-firta (dynamic) hai — technology, mazdoori, byaaj dar, yuddh, raajneeti, mahamaari, mausam, aapdaayein — sab demand-supply badalte. Isliye equilibrium hamesha khisakta rehta hai. COVID-19 (2020): mask ki maang achaanak uchhli, supply peechhe → daam bahut badhe; dheere supply badhi → daam gire; mahamaari khatam → maang ghati → daam pehle jaise.

Hotel ka kiraya — dynamic pricing

Goa ka hotel, 100 kamre: July ka Monday (off-season) — ₹1,500; December ka Saturday — ₹8,000; New Year's Eve — ₹25,000! Group booking cancel → raaton-raat 40% kam. Din mein kai baar daam badalte — kitni tezi se booking ho rahi, paas ke hotel ka daam, tyohaar/conference, mausam ki bhavishyavaani, aane mein kitne din, pichhle trend. Revenue = kharche ghataane se pehle bikri se kul kamaai. Socho: fast fashion, zyada machhli pakadna, bhoojal ka atidohan — aaj ki maang kal ki supply ko nuksaan pahuncha sakti hai.

Sarkar kyon beech mein aati hai?

India aaj duniya ki 4th sabse badi arthvyavastha — bazaar-aadhaarit par niyantrit (regulated). Bazaar maal use deta hai jo paisa de sake — par dawa bahut mehngi ho jaaye to garib kya kare? Isliye: Price ceiling — adhiktam daam (jaise zaroori dawaayein). Price floor — nyuntam daam (jaise minimum wage); asar ke liye equilibrium se upar rakhna padta. Monopoly — ek hi vikreta, koi vikalp nahi → zyada daam, kharab quality, kam supply; sarkar rokti. Regulators: RBI (banking), CCPA (upbhokta adhikaar), TRAI (telecom), SEBI (share bazaar).

Sanitiser ki kahaani aur public goods

COVID mein sanitiser ki maang uchhli — stock khatam, daam aasmaan; kuch dukaandaaron ne hoarding (zaroorat se zyada jama karna) aur black marketing (gair-kaanooni bikri) ki. Sarkar ne ise Essential Commodities Act, 1955 mein zaroori vastu ghoshit kiya — 200 ml ka MRP max ₹100. Companies ne utpaadan badhaaya, sanitiser sahi daam par mil gaya. Public goods — sadak, pul, park, street light, raksha, safaai-naali; private companies nahi banaatin kyonki seedha munaafa nahi. Park ki misaal: har parivaar ₹5,000 de to park banega, par har koi soche 'doosre denge, main muft mein ghoomunga' → paisa jama hi nahi hota → isliye sarkar banaati hai.

Zyada dakhal ke nuksaan

1) Daam bigadna, prerna ghatna — gehun ka bazaar daam ₹30, sarkar ne max ₹20 kar diya → kisaan kam ugaayenge → kami. 2) Compliance ka bojh — chhote restaurant ko food safety, fire safety, pradushan, local clearance — kai permission; samay aur kharch se naye udyami darte → ease of doing business girta. 3) Innovation ruk jaata — sahi kamaai na ho to kisaan behtar beej, sinchaai, technology mein nivesh nahi karega. Loktantrik sarkar ko consumer, producer, mazdoor — sabki awaaz sunkar santulan banaana chahiye.

Exam ke liye short notes

  • Demand = willingness + ability (purchasing power). Law of Demand: P↑ Q↓ (inverse); demand curve neeche jhukti.
  • Srivalli: ₹150→1, ₹100→2, ₹50→3 kg. Market (Srivalli+Alex+Israt): 6, 12, 18 kg — market curve flatter.
  • Demand ke anya kaaran: substitute/complementary goods ka daam, aamdani, pasand, aabaadi ka aakaar-banaawat, season, bhavishya ki ummeed.
  • Diminishing marginal utility — har agle aam se kam sukh → dene ki ichchha kam.

Oceans and Life

Samudra ka farsh, lehrein-jwaar-dhaaraayein, cyclone-tsunami ki early warning, samudri sansaadhan, Blue Economy aur maritime zones ke niyam.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Samudra — rahasyon ka khazaana

Bharatiya sanskriti mein Samudra shaktishaali aur pavitra maana gaya. Dharti ka zyaadatar hissa samudra se dhaka hai. Is chapter ke 6 bade vichaar: 1) samudra ka paani hamesha chalta (lehrein, jwaar, dhaaraayein); 2) gurutvakarshan se jwaar-bhaata; 3) samudra ka farsh ek chhupa landscape; 4) Blue Economy — khaana, oorja, rozgaar ka tikau upyog; 5) early warning se tat ki raksha; 6) maritime zones — 'samudra ke niyam' (UNCLOS).

Samudra ke neeche ke pahaad-maidaan (Ocean Relief)

Zameen ki tarah samudra ke farsh par bhi pahaad, ghaati, pathaar, khaai — inhe submarine/ocean relief kehte hain. Mariana Trench (Pacific) — dharti ki sabse gehri jagah. Mid-Ocean Ridge — duniya ki sabse lambi parvat-shrinkhla, paani ke neeche chhupi! Mauna Kea (Hawai'i) — farsh se naapo to Everest se bhi ooncha.

Mukhya relief features

Continental Shelf — mahadweep ka doobta, uthla kinaara; samudri jeevan aur tel-gas se bharpoor. Continental Slope — teekhi dhalaan, mahadweep ka asli kinaara. Abyssal Plain — vishaal, samtal, gehra farsh. Mid-Ocean Ridge — jwaalamukhi se bani vishaal parvatmaala. Trench — sabse gehre hisse. Seamount — paani ke neeche jwaalamukhi pahaad jo satah tak nahi pahunchta. Guyot — chapte sir wala seamount (lehron ne choti ghis di, phir doob gaya).

Lehrein (Waves) — tat ki 'architect'

Satah ka baari-baari uthna-girna = wave; mukhya kaaran hawa. Upar ka bindu crest, neeche trough; do crest ke beech ki doori = wavelength; ek second mein kitne crest guzre = frequency; crest ko A se B tak jaane ka samay = wave period. Lehrein tat ko kaat-kaat kar aakaar deti: headland mein darar → gufa (cave) → mehraab (arch) → upar gira to stambh (stack) → ghis kar thoonth (stump); blow hole bhi. Jama karke beach banaati. Hydraulic action = behte paani ke zor se chattaan-mitti ka katna.

Tsunami — bhookamp ki lehar

Toofan mein tez hawa vishaal lehrein uthaati. Par samudra ke neeche bhookamp se uthi dev-lehar = Tsunami. Tat par pahunchkar ye 30 metre ya usse bhi oonchi (run-up height) ho sakti hai — bahut vinaashkaari.

Jwaar-Bhaata (Tides)

Din mein (zyaadatar jagah) do baar samudra ka niyamit chadhna-utarna. Sabse ooncha = high tide (flood tide), sabse neecha = low tide (ebb tide). Kaaran: Chandrama aur Surya ka gurutvakarshan. Spring tide — Surya, Prithvi, Chandra ek seedh mein (Poornima aur Amaavasya) → ooncha jwaar bahut ooncha, bhaata bahut neecha. Neap tide — Chandra pehla/teesra quarter (ashtami), kheenchaav alag dishaaon mein → farak kam.

Jwaar ke fayde

Jahaaz — high tide mein paani ooncha, bandargaah mein ghusna aasaan. Kai bandargaahon mein tidal range 4–5 m; Gulf of Khambhat mein lagbhag 12 m! Singapore Strait jaise uthle raaste mein bade tanker (VLCC) high tide ka intezaar karte. Tidal current 5–6 knot (9–11 km/h). Machhli — high tide mein machhliyan kinaare aati, achchha shikaar. Bijli — India ka tat 11,098.81 km; tidal energy ke sthal: Gulf of Kutch, Gulf of Khambhat, Sundarbans. Mazedaar: Dwarka ka Bhadkeshwar Mahadev mandir — sirf low tide mein pahunch sakte, high tide mein raasta doob jaata!

Samudri dhaaraayein (Currents)

Tay dishaa mein behti lagaataar jal-dhaara. Kaaran: hawa, Prithvi ka ghoomna (Coriolis force — uttari golaardh mein daayein, dakshini mein baayein mod), taapmaan aur khaarepan ka farak, mahadweepon ka aakaar. Garam jagah se aaye = garam dhaara (naqshe par laal); thandi se = thandi (neela). Gulf Stream (garam, Gulf of Mexico → Norway) — 60–70° par bhi Norway ka samudra nahi jamta! Labrador (thandi, Canada ka poorvi tat) — kam akshansh par bhi barf aur hima-shail. Jahan garam-thandi milein = fishing grounds (poshak tatva bharpoor).

Early warning — cyclone se bachaav

Cyclone = garam samudra par bane ghoomte toofan. India mein Cyclone Warning aur Marine Weather Services dono taton par. NCRMP (National Cyclone Risk Mitigation Project) — cyclone shelter, warning system, Decision Support System (DSS). INCOIS + IMD ka SSEWS (Storm Surge Early Warning System) — toofan se uthne waali lehar aur doob-kshetra ki bhavishyavaani. Naam: North Indian Ocean ke cyclone 13 deshon ke diye 169 naamon ki list se kram se — jaise Biparjoy (2023), Remal (2024).

GEMINI — machhuaaron ka saathi

GAGAN-Enabled Mariner's Instrument for Navigation and Information — chhota haath ka device. GAGAN satellite se data lekar Bluetooth se mobile par; INCOIS app use 9 kshetriya bhaashaon mein dikhaata — oonchi lehar, tez hawa, cyclone-tsunami alert, potential fishing zone, dhaara ki gati, bathymetry (paani ke neeche ki gehraai-naksha). Abhi 50 nautical mile tak; naya version 300 NM tak, do-tarfa baat. Madad: PM Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY).

2004 Tsunami aur tsunami warning

26 December 2004 — Indian Ocean Tsunami; lagbhag 2,30,000 mautein. India mein Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Andhra, Keralam, Andaman-Nicobar sabse prabhavit. Indonesia ka Simeulue dweep bach gaya — lok-kathaaon ne log sikhaaye the ki chetaavni ke sanket kya hain! Baad mein ITEWC (Indian Tsunami Early Warning Centre) — tsunami buoys ka jaal. 4 kadam: Detection (seismic stations bhookamp pakadte) → Confirmation (samudra-tal par OBPR) → Tracking (tide gauge, radar) → Warning (satellite, SMS, email, tat ke display board). Technology ke saath jaagrukta aur training bhi zaroori.

Samudri sansaadhan

Jeevit (living): machhli — coastal/neritic (shelf par 0–200 m) aur oceanic (200 m se gehre), samudri vanaspati, bioactive padaarth, biofuel. Nirjeev (non-living): khanij, ret-bajri; oorja — paramparik (tel, prakritik gas) aur gair-paramparik (gas hydrates, offshore wind, tidal, wave, current energy, OTEC — taapmaan ke farak se bijli). Mahatva: protein aur rozgaar; duniya ka 90% vyapar (maatra se) samudra se; tat paryatan; sanskriti — shankh aur kapardika (kaudi), kabhi mudra, Samudra Manthan ke ratna maane gaye.

Blue Economy aur SDG 14

World Bank: 'samudri sansaadhanon ka tikau upyog — vikas, behtar aajeevika aur rozgaar ke liye, samudri tantra ki sehat bachaate hue'. Teen khambhe: ecosystem health, economic growth, livelihoods & jobs. UN SDG 14: Life Below Water — pradushan kam, tantra ki raksha, ocean acidification se nipatna, machhli pakadne ka niyam, samudri kshetron ka sanrakshan, haanikaarak subsidy khatam.

Maritime zones — samudra kiska?

Nautical mile = 1,852 m (akshansh ka 1 minute). Baseline se: Territorial sea — 12 NM tak; lagbhag poora niyantran (customs, immigration, pradushan ke kaanoon), par videshi jahaazon ko 'innocent passage' — bina ruke, shaanti se guzar sakte; machhli, sainik kaam nahi. Contiguous zone — 24 NM tak; customs, tax, avaidh pravaas par seemit niyantran. EEZ — 200 NM tak (UNCLOS); machhli, tel, gas par vishesh adhikaar, par poora maalikana nahi; doosre jahaaz guzar sakte. Uske baad High seas — samudra ka ~64%, global commons; sabko barabar haq; wahan flag state (jahaz jis desh mein registered) ka kaanoon.

Samudra ke traffic niyam

High seas ki samasya: zyada/avaidh machhli pakadna, sansaadhan par jhagde, suraksha mushkil. Isliye maritime law: COLREGs — 'samudra ke traffic niyam' — sahi nigraani, kis taraf se guzrein, kaun raasta de (give-way) kaun seedha chale (stand-on), surakshit gati, batti-awaaz-aakaar ke sanket. MARPOL — jahaazon se pradushan rokne ka samjhauta. Niyam na hon to takkar, pradushan aur jhagde badhenge.

Life on Earth

Biomes — jungle, ghaas ke maidaan, registaan, tundra aur jaliya; biome pyramid, biodiversity ka sanrakshan, van aur van-vaasi, GI tag aur saajhe sansaadhan.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Dharti — ek badi rang-birangi rajaai

Socho dharti ek patchwork quilt (godadi) hai — har tukde ka apna rang, mausam aur jeev. In bade tukdon ko biome kehte hain. Bio (Greek bios = jeevan) + -ome (samooh). Biome = duniya ke bade prakritik kshetra jahan milte-julte mausam mein khaas tarah ke paudhe-jaanwar rehte hain — barfeela tundra, garam registaan, hare jungle, vishaal ghaas ke maidaan. Farak samjho: biome = poora shehar, ecosystem = uska ek mohalla jahan jeev aur nirjeev cheezein aapas mein judti hain.

Biome kaise banta hai?

Kaun se jeev kahan panpenge — ye tay karte hain: taapmaan, varsha ka pattern, mukhya vanaspati aur unke ugne ka roop. Do bade varg: Terrestrial (zameeni) — van, ghaas ke maidaan, registaan, tundra; aur Aquatic (jaliya) — meetha paani aur samudri. Naqshe par biomes: tropical rainforest, grassland, desert, temperate deciduous (chaude patte jo mausam mein jhadte), taiga (shankudhaari — pine, spruce, larch), tundra.

Forest biome — prakriti ka hara chhaata

Tropical rainforest — Bhoomadhya rekha ke paas, 23.5°N se 23.5°S; saal bhar garam aur geela → ghane jungle. Sabse bade: Amazon (S. America), Congo (Africa), Indonesia-Malaysia. India mein: Andaman-Nicobar aur North-East. Ye duniya ka sabse ameer jaiv-bhandaar — isliye 'gene bank'. Oxygen aur jalvaayu ka santulan. Parten: Emergent layer (sabse oonche ped) → Upper canopy → Understory/Lower canopy → Forest floor.

Grassland biome

Khule bade maidaan — zyaadatar ghaas, ped bahut kam; madhyam varsha — ghane jungle ke liye kaafi nahi. Temperate grasslands — Prairies (N. America), Steppes (Asia) — upjaaoo mitti, kheti. Tropical grasslands = Savanna (Africa) — bikhre ped; sher, haathi, giraffe. Charne wale jaanwar, kheti aur pashupaalan ke liye zaroori.

Desert biome

Sabse sookhe biome — bahut kam baarish. Garam — Sahara (Africa); thande — Gobi (Asia). Rann of Kutch (Gujarat) — duniya ke sabse bade namak registaanon mein se; monsoon mein daldal, sookhe mausam mein safed namak ka registaan. Paudhe (cactus) aur jaanwar (oont, chhipkali, saanp) paani jama karne aur garmi sehne mein maahir. Khanij aur tel bhi. Socho: Rajasthan ka oont aur Nubra Valley (Ladakh) ka do-koohan wala oont — Nubra wale ke baal ghane kyon? Kyonki wahan thand hai!

Tundra biome

Sabse thanda — Arctic aur Antarctic ke paas. Lambi kathor sardi, chhoti garmi. Mitti lagbhag saal bhar jami — permafrost — isliye ped nahi ug paate. Vanaspati: moss, lichen, chhoti jhaadiyan. Jaanwar: reindeer, arctic fox, polar bear. Bahut naazuk — thoda taapmaan badla to poora tantra prabhavit. Himalaya (subtropics) mein to shankudhaari ped hain — kyonki wahan oonchaai se thand hai, par dhoop aur mitti tundra jaisi kathor nahi.

Aquatic biomes

Dharti ka lagbhag teen-chauthaai (3/4) hissa. Meetha paani (freshwater) — nadi, jheel, taalaab, wetlands; kheti, peene ka paani, machhli-mendhak-keede. Samudri (marine) — mahasaagar, saagar, coral reefs; bhaari jaiv-vividhta, garmi jama-baant kar jalvaayu par asar; whale, dolphin, machhli, plankton; laakhon ki rozi-roti.

Biome pyramid

Jeevon ke rishte ko oorja, sankhya ya biomass ke roop mein dikhaane wala chitra. Neeche — producers (paudhe, shaival) jo photosynthesis se suraj ki oorja pakadte. Upar — herbivores (primary consumer) → carnivores (secondary) → top predators (tertiary). Upar jaate hue sankhya, biomass aur oorja ghatti — har star par oorja garmi ke roop mein nikal jaati. Ek aur pyramid: thande-dhruviya se garam-ushna, geela se sookha — tundra, coniferous, deciduous, chaparral, grassland, desert, rainforest, scrubland, tropical seasonal forest, savanna.

Sab jude hain — biodiversity

Biome jeevon ko ghar dete; jeev bhi biome badalte — jaanwar paraagan aur beej failaav, insaan kheti-shehar, sookshmjeev poshak tatva lautaate. Biodiversity = dharti par jeevan ki vividhta — paudhe, jaanwar, sookshmjeev, unke ecosystem; sirf prajaatiyon ki ginti nahi, prajaati ke andar genetic farak aur aavaason ki vividhta bhi. Hotspots — Himalaya, Western Ghats, North-East, Andaman-Nicobar — aisi prajaatiyan jo kahin aur nahi (Nilgiri Tahr, Himalayan Snowcock, yak, mangrove).

Sanrakshan mein bhoogol ki bhoomika

Biodiversity asamaan bati hai, isliye bhoogol zaroori. Ecosystem-based — tat par mangrove bachao, pahaad par ped lagao. Protected Areas — national park, wildlife sanctuary, biosphere reserve, World Heritage Site. Barriers aur corridors — nadi, pahaad, ghaati jaanwar ke aane-jaane aur gene flow ko rokte ya raasta dete → wildlife corridor. Insaan-paryavaran — pahaad mein seedhidaar kheti, maidaan mein agroforestry. Naqshe par dhoondho: Namdapha NP, Rann of Kutch, Gahirmatha beach, Gulf of Mannar, Pachmarhi, Achanakmar–Amarkantak.

Ecotourism — Thenmala ki misaal

Ecotourism = prakriti ko nuksaan pahunchaaye bina, sthaaniya logon ko sahara dete hue zimmedaar yatra. Swadesh Darshan 2.0 (Paryatan Mantralaya) — tikau paryatan sthal. Thenmala (Kollam, Keralam) — India ka pehla planned ecotourism sthal; naam ka matlab 'Honey Hill'; Western Ghats (UNESCO World Heritage) mein; Tourism + Forest + Irrigation vibhaag ne milkar banaaya; sthaaniya log guide, eco-guard bane — fayda samudaay mein.

Van aur rozi-roti

Gaon aur aadivaasi samudaay vanon se paate: khaana, eendhan, chaara, dawa, ghar, lakdi, baans. Non-timber forest products (NTFP) — shahad, phal, mewe, raal, jadibootiyan. Gond aur Bhil sansaadhan lene se pehle van-devtaaon ki pooja karte — tikau parampara. Sacred groves — India mein 1,00,000+; naam: Devrai, Devarakadu, Kavu, Sarna — chhote par biodiversity hotspot, jal-srot bhi bachaate. Rajya-vaar: tendu patta, mahua, sal beej (MP, Chhattisgarh); baans-lakdi (Assam, Tripura, Keralam); jangli phal, shahad, mushroom (Jharkhand, Odisha); ashwagandha, neem, tulsi (Himalaya, Western Ghats).

Van-vaasi — rakshak aur gyaani

Gyaan ke bhandaar — jadi-booti ka paaramparik gyaan peedhi-dar-peedhi. Kamaai — tendu, mahua, shahad, lac, baans. Ghar ki zaroorat — lakdi, chaara, kand-mool. Sanskriti — tyohaar-reeti van se jude. Van-raksha — Bishnoi aandolan (Rajasthan, Amrita Devi) ne prerit kiya Chipko aandolan (Uttarakhand) — mahilaayein pedon se lipat gayin! Mahilaaon ki bhoomika sabse badi.

GI Tag — jagah ki pehchaan

Geographical Indication (GI) = ek bauddhik sampada adhikaar jo batata hai ki utpaad khaas jagah ka hai aur uski khaasiyat usi jagah se judi hai. Van-aadhaarit GI: Ramban Sulai Honey (J&K), Tezpur Litchi (Assam), Channapatna Toys (Karnataka — 'Aale mara' ped ki lakdi), Kandhamal Haldi (Odisha — zyada curcumin), Erode Manjal (Tamil Nadu), Nilgiri Tea, Tripura Queen Pineapple, Khasi Mandarin (Meghalaya). Fayda: viraasat ki raksha, sthaaniya kamaai.

Van-vaasiyon ki mushkilein

Swasthya, shiksha, saaf paani tak pahunch kam; mahilaaon ko lakdi ke dhuen se bimaari (PM Ujjwala Yojana se LPG). Van-upyog par roak, katai, vyavsaayik dohan → kaanoon aur zaroorat mein takraav. Baadh, bhooskhalan, jungle ki aag — FSI ke anusaar ~36% van aag-prabhavit, ~4% ati-prabhavit. Maanav-vanyajeev sangharsh — haathi, bandar, jangli suar fasal ujaadte. Utpaad saste daam par bikte — processing aur marketing chahiye. Sadak par forest bridge se roadkill kam.

Sarkari kadam aur Mendha Lekha

Forest Rights Act (FRA) 2006 — paaramparik van-vaasiyon ke zameen aur van-sansaadhan ke haq; laghu van upaj (baans, shahad, jadi-booti) lene ka haq aur kheti ki chhoti zameen ka maalikana. Joint Forest Management (JFM) — samudaay raksha kare aur upaj ka laabh le. PESA, DAJGUA (Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan), PM JANMAN. Mendha Lekha (Gadchiroli, Maharashtra; Gond) — FRA ke tahat van-adhikaar paane wala pehla gaon; naara 'Mawa Nate Mawa Raj' (hamara gaon, hamara raj); Gram Sabha se faisle; 1,800 hectare van; baans katai se kamaai, shiksha-infrastructure par kharch.

Saajhe sansaadhan (Ecological Commons)

Van, nadi, jheel, charaagaah, hawa, jaiv-vividhta — jo poore samudaay ke hain, kisi ek ke nahi; paaramparik niyamon se chalte. Aabaadi, udyog, nijeekaran se ghat rahe. Bihar machhli: Indian Fisheries Act 1897 ke daur mein zameendaaron ka kabza, machhuaare haashiye par → Bihar Fish Jalkar Management Act, 2006 — samudaay-aadhaarit prabandh → machhli utpaadan badha. Taalaab: Chandella-Bundela raajaaon ne 800–1200 CE mein Bundelkhand mein ~8,000 taalaab; Karnataka kere, Tamil Nadu eri, Andhra-Odisha chevuru, Bihar ahar-pyne, Gond raajaaon ke kata (teen-taraf band jalaashay). Aaj India mein 15 lakh+ chaalu taalaab.

Theme H · History

Resistance and Resilience (1000–1700 CE)

Hamlon ke daur mein Bharat ne kaise lada (resistance) aur kaise tika raha (resilience) — Rajput, Ahom, Vijayanagara, Maratha, Sikh, Jat; Bhakti, mandir aur vyapar.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Do shabd samjho — Resistance aur Resilience

1000–1700 CE — baar-baar hamle, yuddh, dharmik bhedbhaav. Resistance (pratirodh) = kisi taaqat ya khatre ka sakriya virodh — apni satta, zameen, hit ya sanskritik-aadhyaatmik pehchaan bachaane ke liye; roop: yuddh, gathbandhan, kootneeti, baatcheet. Resilience (lacheelaapan/tikaav) = mushkil mein tikne aur dhalne ki kshamata, apni parampara bachaate hue — jaise mandiron ki marammat, reeti-riwaaj mein badlaav, saath rehne ke tareeke. Rishta: resilience resistance ki reedh ki haddi hai — neta ladne ki sthiti banaata hai, par tikne ki taaqat resilience deti hai. Ye daur sirf hamle-virodh nahi, sangharsh aur sahyog dono ka hai.

Pehli lahar — Arab, Ghazni, Ghur

Upjaaoo maidaanon ka atirikt anaaj samriddhi bhi tha aur hamlon ka kaaran bhi. 7th sadi ke beech se Arab hamle (samudra aur zameen) — Deccan, Malwa, Kashmir, Rajasthan ke raajaaon ne khadeda. Mahmud of Ghazni — pehla hamla 1001 mein Jayapala (Hindu Shahi) par. Hindu Shahi, Chandella, Solanki, Chauhan lade. 1178 — Rani Naiki Devi (Chaulukya) ne Kasahrada (Mount Abu ke paas) mein Muhammad Ghuri ko haraaya! 1192 — Tarain ki doosri ladaai mein Ghuri ne Prithviraj Chauhan ko haraaya. Gujarat, Malwa, Bundelkhand (Chandella) ~100 saal tak ade rahe; Odisha aur Assam ke hisse Turk jeet hi nahi paaye; prayadweepiya Bharat 11–12 sadi mein aazaad raha.

Delhi Sultanate aur virodh (1200–1500)

1206 — Delhi Sultanate; satta ka kendrikaran. Iqta — vetan ki jagah sena-naayakon ko ilaake; lagaan vasooli, kisaan par bojh badha. Pavitra sthal tode gaye → kade virodh. Virodh karne wale: Yadava (Devagiri), Rajasthan ke Rajput, Kakatiya (Warangal) — Rani Rudrama Devi (1262–89), Hoysala (Karnataka). Mewar, Gwalior, Malwa, Awadh, Etawah, Ajmer, Jalore, Kalinjar, Kannauj ne bhi chunauti di. Ghatnaayein: 1298 Alauddin ne Somnath loota; 1301 Ranthambore, 1303 Chittor ki gheraabandi; 1307 Malik Kafur ka Devagiri par hamla; ~1325 Sisodia Rana Hammir ne Mewar wapas liya; 1433–68 Maharana Kumbha ne kilon ka jaal banaaya. Aur veer: Kanhada Deva (Jalore), Hammira Deva (Ranthambore), Rao Surjan Hada (Bundi).

Rajput–Mughal: ladaai bhi, samjhauta bhi

Ibrahim Lodi kamzor → 1526 Panipat mein Babur jeeta, Sultanate khatam. 1527 Khanua — Rana Sanga ne Rajput sangh banaakar Babur se takkar li; Babur jeeta — bada mod. Rishte rananeeti se bane: Mewar ne kada virodh kiya, Amber ke Kachhwaha ne sahyog ka raasta chuna. Mughalon ko Rajputon ki sainik taaqat, wafaadaari aur prashaasnik kshamata chahiye thi — isliye mansab diye. Mansabdari (Akbar) = pad/rank wala tantra jo sena aur prashasan jodta.

Mewar — jhuka nahi

1567–68 — Akbar ne Chittor ghera; Rana Udai Singh ki sena 5 mahine se zyada ladi; phir praja ke saath Aravalli mein chale gaye. 1576 Haldighati — Maharana Pratap vs Mughal. Phir guerrilla yuddh — Aravalli ke beehad mein achaanak hamle, chhaape, ghaat. 1582 Dewair (Udaipur ke paas) — Mughal chaukiyan dhahin; 1580s tak zyaadatar zameen wapas. Bhil samudaay — raaste ka gyaan, teerandaazi, guerrilla — isliye Mewar ke rajchinh par Rajput aur Bhil yoddha, beech mein Eklingji. Amar Singh ne Jahangir ke hamle (1605, 1608, 1609) roke. Aakhir sandhi — Mewar ke shaasak ko Mughal darbaar mein khud haazir hone se chhoot (Surat ke vyapar raaston ki ahmiyat bhi). Kavi Dursa Arha: Pratap ne ghodon par shaahi daag nahi lagne diya, Nauroz mein nahi gaye, Jharokha Darshan mein pehra nahi diya.

Ahom — chhe sadiyon ki aazaadi

Assam ka Ahom rajya 1228–1826 — lagbhag 600 saal aazaad! Rananeeti: seedhi takkar nahi, zameen ka faayda — jungle, nadiyan, monsoon chakra ka gyaan. 1615 Samdhara — pehli takkar, 1616 tak zameen wapas. 1662 Mir Jumla ne raajdhaani Garhgaon le li, raja Jayadhvaj Singha par sandhi thopi — par saal bhar mein raja ne todi. 1671 Saraighat — Lachit Borphukan ne Mughal bede ko haraaya; Brahmaputra seema bani. Taaqat ke raaz: gathbandhan, guerrilla, kathin bhoogol, Paik system, aur tez halki naavein 'bachari'.

Vijayanagara — 'Jeet ka Shehar'

Malik Kafur ne Warangal, Belur, Madurai ke shehar-mandir loote; Tughlaq ke samay Madurai Sultanate bana. Jawab mein 1336 — Harihara aur Bukka ne Vijayanagara (Hampi ke paas) basaaya. Nadi aur pathreeli zameen = prakritik suraksha. Krishna-Tungabhadra ke dakshin tak failaav; Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Sanskrit sahitya khila. 1342 Kannanur — Hoysala Vira Ballala III lade par maare gaye. Kumara Kampana (Bukka ke bete) ne Madurai Sultanate khatam kiya; Kanchipuram (Rajasimheshwara) aur Srirangam mein moortiyan wapas sthaapit kin. Shikhar: Krishnadevaraya (mrityu 1529). 1565 Talikota — Deccan sultanaton ke gathbandhan se haar, shehar ujda; Nayaka rajya bane.

Madurai ke Nayak ki 'chappal' wali kahaani

Lok-parampara: Mughal baadshah adheen raajaaon ko shaahi chappal bhejta; raja seema par jaakar swaagat kare aur kar de. Muthu Virappa Nayak III ne seema par jaana mana kiya — doot ko mahal tak laana pada. Wahan bhi jhuka nahi; chappal zameen par rakhwaayi, apna pair daala aur mazaak udaaya — 'Badshah ne joda kyon nahi bheja?' — aur doot ko rajya se nikaal diya. Virodh sirf talwaar se nahi, saahas bhare ishaaron se bhi!

Maratha — Swarajya ka sapna

1674 — Chhatrapati Shivaji ka rajyabhishek, aazaad Maratha rajya. Swarajya = bina baahari niyantran ke swa-shaasan. 1659 Pratapgarh mein Afzal Khan ka ant. Lambi ladaai ne Mughalon ko thaka diya — Aurangzeb 20+ saal Deccan mein atka raha. Gathbandhan aur kar-dene wale rishton ka jaal. Kile — Raigad, Sinhagad — guerrilla ke kendra; kuch aage ki chaukiyan, seema wale fallback raajdhaani; paani, hathiyaar, prashasan aur nyay ke kendra — lambi gheraabandi jhel sakein. Gingee/Senji (Villupuram, Tamil Nadu) — ek darwaaza itna khaas ki thode sainik 10,000 ki sena rok saken; Nayak, Bijapur, Mughal, Maratha, French, English sabke haath raha; 2025 mein UNESCO World Heritage.

Sikh — aadhyaatmik aur raajnaitik tikaav

Guru Nanak (15th sadi ke ant) — ek Ishwar, samaanta, karmkaand ka tyaag. Prabhaav badha to Mughal dakhal: Jahangir ne Guru Arjan Dev (5th) ko Khusrau ki madad ke bahaane shaheed karaaya — bada mod. Guru Hargobind (6th) Gwalior kile mein qaid; unhone Akal Takht banaaya — meeri (saansaarik) aur peeri (aadhyaatmik): Harmandir Sahib = peeri, Akal Takht = meeri. 1675 — Aurangzeb ne Guru Tegh Bahadur (9th) ko shaheed karaaya. 1699 — Guru Gobind Singh (10th) ne Khalsa banaaya. Sahibzaadon ki shahaadat ke baad Sikh misal (hathiyaarband dal → sangh) mein sangathit; kisaan sainik bane.

Jat virodh

Ganga-Yamuna doab ke kisaan Jat — bhaari kar aur shoshan + Aurangzeb ki dharmik neeti. 1669 — Gokul Jat ke netritva mein vidroh; Mughal mahino jooojhe. 1686 — Aurangzeb Deccan mein vyast, Sinsani aur Sogar ke zamindaaron ke netritva mein phir vidroh. 1691 tak Mughal haavi, par virodh jaari. Italian yaatri Niccolao Manucci ne iska varnan likha.

Bhakti — tikaav ki aatma

Bhakti = Ishwar se vyaktigat prem, sthaaniya bhaashaon mein bhajan. 7th sadi se dakshin mein (Nayanar — Shiv bhakt, jaise Karaikal Ammaiyar; Alvar — Vishnu bhakt, jaise Andal); 14th sadi se uttar mein — Ramananda ne dakshin-uttar ko joda. Do dhaaraayein: Nirguna (niraakaar) aur Saguna (saakaar). Mandir tode gaye to naam-kirtan — aisi pooja jise koi tod nahi sakta! Satsang, sankirtan se samaaj juda. Sankardev — Kirtana Ghosha; Chaitanya ne Bengal mein Qazi ki roak ke virodh mein sankirtan nikaala; Rajasthan mein Dadu Panth, Niranjani — yoddha-sanyaasi vyapar raaste bachaate. Tulsidas — Ramcharitmanas (Awadhi) — Ram katha ghar-ghar.

Bhakti mein sab — mahilaayein bhi, sabhi dharm bhi

Atukuri Molla (kumhaar ki beti, Gopavaram, Andhra) — Molla Ramayanam Telugu mein; Krishnadevaraya ke darbaar mein paath. Akka Mahadevi (Karnataka) aur Lal Ded (Kashmir) — Shaiv, sanyaas; Bahinabai (Maharashtra) — ghar sambhaalte hue Vithoba bhakti. Lal Ded ka vakh: 'shaastra seekhna aasaan, jeena kathin'. Raskhan (Braj — Prem Vatika), Rahim (Mughal senapati, Krishna bhakti), Bibi Lachimar (Turk mool, Vishnu bhakti). Kshetra-vaar: Kabir, Mirabai, Surdas, Raidas (uttar); Chaitanya, Jayadeva, Sankardev (poorv); Basavanna, Annamacharya, Madhvacharya (dakshin); Jnanadeva, Namdev, Tukaram, Janabai, Chokhamela (pashchim). Devi-shakti: Shivaji ko Tulja Bhavani, Bastar ke Annamaraja ko Danteshwari se talwaar ki maanyata.

Sulh-i-Kul aur Sufi

Akbar ke baad ke shaasan mein Sulh-i-Kul = 'sabse shaanti', saarvbhaumik sahishnuta — raajnaitik samarthan jutaane ki neeti bhi; Akbar ne Surya ki vandana jaisi prathaayein apnaayin. Sufi — Islam ka rahasyavaadi, udaar roop; silsila: Sultanate mein Chishti, Suhrawardi; Mughal kaal mein Qadiriyya, Naqshbandiya. Khanqah aur dargaah jan-jeevan ke kendra; Hindavi, Bangla ka prayog; kuch ne pranayam ('habs-i-dam') bhi apnaaya.

Mandir — sirf pooja-sthal nahi

Mandir = arthik kendra (zameen, rozgaar — vaastukaar, kaarigar, moortikaar, pujari, sangeetkaar), bank (kisaan, gram sabha, vyapaariyon ko udhaar), shiksha-kala (Bharatanatyam), nyay-kar ke adhikaar. Devta hi raja: Odisha ke Gajapati khud ko Jagannath ka Rauta (pratinidhi) kehte; Jaipur ke Kachhwaha Govinddevji ki ore se, Kota ke Rajput Ram ke pratinidhi. Thanjavur Brihadeeswara ko sone-gehnon ka daan → sinchaai par kharch. Mandir-nagar: Thanjavur, Kanchipuram, Puri. 11th sadi se Ghaznavi-Ghuri hamlon mein todphod; 12th se kai jagah masjid. Par punarnirmaan: Kannanur ka mandir (masjid bana tha) Kampana ne wapas mandir banaaya; Kashi Vishwanath — Todar Mal aur Raja Man Singh ne banaaya (Aurangzeb ne toda; aaj ka mandir Ahilyabai Holkar, 1780); Govind Devji (Vrindavan) — Man Singh; Keshav Dev (Mathura) — Bundela Vir Singh.

Haveli mandir — chhupkar bachna

16th sadi se mandir tang galiyon mein, darwaaza mukhya sadak par nahi, ghar jaise dikhte, ooncha shikhar nahi — 'haveli mandir'; Delhi, Varanasi, Rajasthan mein. Jagat Shiromani (Meerabai) Mandir, Amer — Rani Kanakwati (Man Singh I ki patni) ne Krishna-Meera ko samarpit kiya. Mewar ke Jagat Singh ne Chittor ki kilebandi ki aur Udaipur mein bada Jagdish Mandir — Shah Jahan ki Jama Masjid ka dharmik-raajnaitik jawab maana jaata.

Kheti, hundi aur Hind Mahasagar ka vyapar

Rajya ki aamdani = lagaan; Alauddin aur Muhammad bin Tughlaq ke samay 50% tak upaj + anya kar. Delhi aur Mughal samudri vyapar mein kam ruchi — tat ka vyapar sthaaniya shaktiyon ke haath. Sinchaai: rassi-ghirni, Persian wheel (rahat); Vijayanagara ka chhoti sinchaai mein nivesh. Governance and Public Policy

Government aur governance ka farak, India mein shaasan ke mulya, good governance ke 7 gun, e-governance, public policy kya hai, kaun banaata hai aur policy cycle.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Baadh wala shehar — sab milkar

Socho tumhare shehar mein kai din bhaari baarish. Sadkein doobi, school band, bijli gul, bimaari ka darr. Ab dekho kaun kya karta: Nagar Nigam naaliyan saaf, PWD sadak theek, police traffic, school ki soochna parents ko, swasthya karmi bimaari roke, pratinidhi awaaz uthaayein, padosi kamzor logon ki madad karein. Koi ek vibhaag akela nahi kar sakta — taal-mel chahiye. Isi milkar faisla lene, laagu karne aur jawabdeh rehne ki prakriya ko Governance kehte hain.

Government vs Governance

Government = sansthaayein jo raajnaitik adhikaar chalaati — Legislature, Executive, Judiciary; sawaal: 'kaun shaasan karta hai?' Governance = prakriya — faisle kaise bante, laagu hote, nigraani hoti; isme sarkar ke saath nagrik, media, civil society, private sector bhi; sawaal: 'shaasan kaise hota hai?' Misaal: street light lagaana Nagar Nigam/Panchayat (government) ka kaam; par andhere spot pehchaanna, kharab batti ki shikaayat, feedback — ye poori prakriya governance. Loktantrik governance ke aadhaar: paardarshita, jawabdehi, bhaagidaari, rule of law — nagrik kendra mein.

Hamari apni jaden — mulya aur prateek

Upanivesh (colonialism) ne kai deshon par apne model thope; aazaadi ke baad bhi wo sansthaayein bani rahin. India do baaton mein alag: 1) apni purv-aupniveshik shaasan paramparaaon ka gyaan granthon aur prathaaon mein bacha raha; 2) sab sansthaayein ek saath badalna sambhav nahi tha, isliye Samvidhan Bharatiya mulyon ki bhaavna se bana. British 'Order of the Star of India' ka motto tha 'Heaven's Light, Our Guide'; humne apnaaya 'Satyamev Jayate' (Mundaka Upanishad). Supreme Court ka motto 'Yato Dharmastato Jayah' (Mahabharata) — jahan dharm, wahan vijay.

Sengol, Ram Rajya, Rajadharma

Sengol — nyaypoorn shaasan ka prateek, Chola kaal (1,000+ saal) se parampara. 15 Aug 1947 ki aadhi raat Nehru ne Tamil Nadu ke Adheenam santon se praapt kiya; 2023 mein naye Sansad bhavan mein PM Narendra Modi ne sthaapit kiya. Gandhi ka aadarsh — Ram Rajya: imaandaari, paardarshita, karuna, kalyan, jan-kendrit shaasan. Rajadharma — raja ka pehla kartavya praja ki raksha aur kalyan; shaasan pavitra zimmedaari, sirf taaqat nahi. Arthashastra: 'Prajasukhe sukham rajnah' — praja ke sukh mein raja ka sukh, praja ke hit mein raja ka hit. Rigveda ki Sabha-Samiti bhi yaad karo.

Good Governance ke 7 gun

1) Rule of Law — kaanoon ke saamne sab barabar. 2) Accountability — adhikaari apne kaam ke liye jawabdeh. 3) Participation — nagrik faisle mein bhaagidaar. 4) Transparency — faisle khule aur uplabdh. 5) Effectiveness — saarvajanik sansaadhan samajhdaari se. 6) Responsiveness — sarkar logon ki zaroorat par dhyaan de. 7) Inclusiveness — har nagrik ko barabar avsar. Sabki bhoomika: sarkar kaanoon-neeti-sevaayein; nagrik vote, kaanoon paalan, bhaagidaari; media mudde saamne laaye; civil society jaagrukta; private sector arthik gatividhi aur innovation. Good governance = sarkar aur nagrik ki saajhedaari.

Chunautiyan aur Good Governance Day

Logon ki zaroortein badalti rehti hain — shaasan ka kaam kabhi khatam nahi hota. Chunautiyan: vibhaagon mein kamzor taal-mel; sevaaon tak asamaan pahunch (samaajik, aarthik, bhaugolik rukaawat); bhrashtaachaar aur pad ka durupyog; galat soochna/fake news. Hal: majboot sansthaayein, zimmedaar adhikaari, sakriya nagrik. 25 December = National Good Governance Day (pata karo kyon yahi taareekh chuni gayi!).

E-Governance — technology se behtar shaasan

Sarkar ICT (soochna-sanchaar technology) se sevaayein de, jaankaari baante, nagrikon se baat kare, feedback le = e-governance. Fayde: paardarshi, aasaan, tez; kaagaz kam, daftar ke chakkar khatam. Misaalein: UMANG (ek app, kai sarkari sevaayein), Aadhaar (pehchaan), GeM (Government e-Marketplace — sarkari khareed), DigiLocker (dastavez surakshit), CPGRAMS (shikaayat nivaaran), APAAR (chhatron ki academic ID). Shart: device, internet aur digital kaushal — isliye sabko shaamil karna zaroori. Socho pehle aur ab: janm praman-patra, admit card, marksheet, vetan, rail ticket, gaadi registration!

Public Policy — school ki plastic-mukt neeti

Sadak, traffic signal, saaf paani, bijli, park, kooda uthaana — sab public policies ka nateeja. School misaal: bachche single-use plastic laate, campus ganda → principal, teacher, chhatra, parents ne milkar 'Plastic-Free Campus Policy' banaayi — apni bottle, plastic bag nahi, alag-alag dustbin, maasik jaagrukta, teacher nigraani. Ye niti hai kyonki samasya sulajhaane ki yojnabaddh kaaryavaahi hai. Public policy = sarkar dwara apnaaya gaya kaary-maarg — kya, kyon, kaun, kaise, aur safalta kaise naapein; phir feedback loop se sudhaar.

'Public' kya banaata hai?

Policy public tab — jab sabke liye ho, sarkar banaaye, public sansthaayein laagu karein, aksar tax ke paise se chale, jan-kalyan badhaaye aur nagrik bhaagidaar hon — shiksha, swasthya, transport, safaai, paryavaran, suraksha. Har policy public nahi: school ki mobile neeti, company ka work-from-home, society ke parking/guest niyam — sirf ek sangathan tak seemit. Pehchaano: sports policy (public), har ghar saaf paani abhiyaan (public), company WFH (policy), Nagar Nigam ka kooda-vibhaajan (public), housing society ke guest hours (policy), bachchon ka muft teekakaran (public).

Kaun-kaun banaata hai policy?

Nagrik — samasya batao, vote do, sujhaav-feedback (school ke paas zebra crossing ki maang). Chune pratinidhi (MP, MLA, local) — mudde uthaao, kaanoon, budget (MLA ne Vidhan Sabha mein paani ki kami uthaayi). Adhikaari/vibhaag/mantralaya — banaao, laagu karo, taal-mel, nigraani (District Collector yojana laagu kare). Civil society, NGO, visheshagya, media — adhyayan, sujhaav, jaagrukta, nigraani (NGO ka jal-sanrakshan abhiyaan). Private sector — technology, innovation, sansaadhan (PPP se school mein solar panel). Antarrashtriya sangathan — anubhav, technical aur aarthik madad (WHO sankraamak rog niyantran).

Case study — aspataal mein ramp

72 saal ki Shanti Devi ko OPD tak seedhiyan chadhni padin — na ramp, na railing. Wheelchair wale vyakti andar hi nahi ja paaye. Drishtibaadhit ke liye na tactile raasta, na Braille. Mareezon ne adhikaariyon aur pratinidhiyon se baat ki; viklang adhikaar sanghathan ne akhbaar-social media mein uthaaya. Zila prashasan ne PWD aur Health Department se jaanch karaayi; doctor, staff, architect, PwD sangathan, buzurg, nivaasiyon se salaah; sarkar ne paisa manzoor kiya — ramp, lift, railing, tactile path, accessible toilet, signboard. Ab buzurg, viklang, garbhvati mahilaayein, bachche sab aasaani se. Ye hai policy ka safar — samasya se samaadhaan tak!

Policy cycle — 7 kadam

1) Policy Desirability — kaunsi zaroorat/samasya? 2) Prioritising — kaunsa mudda pehle? 3) Formulation — hitdhaarakon se salaah, draft, aarthik sambhavna. 4) Adoption — kaanoon (legislation) aur niyam (regulations) banaana. 5) Implementation — adhikaari, local bodies, civil society, private, nagrik se amal. 6) Monitoring — pragati naapna, jawabdehi, kamiyaan pehchaanna. 7) Evaluation — nateeje aur asar ka aakalan, feedback → phir se cycle. Koi kadam chhoota to? Misaal: 1 lakh ped lagaaye, paisa diya, par paani-suraksha nahi → zyaadatar mar gaye — implementation/monitoring kamzor!

NEP 2020 — milkar bani neeti

Pichhli shiksha neeti 1986. Samaaj tezi se badla → 2015 mein nayi neeti ki prakriya shuru. 2017 mein visheshagya samiti — adhyaksh vaigyanik Dr K. Kasturirangan. Khaasiyat: vishaal jan-paraamarsh — 2.5 lakh Gram Panchayat, 6,600 block, 6,000 shehri nikaay, 676 zile; lagbhag 2 lakh sujhaav. Kai sujhaav shaamil; July 2020 mein Union Cabinet ne manzoori di.

Policy → Programme → Scheme

Policy = disha aur lakshya (kya aur kyon). Programme = yojana/raasta (kaise). Scheme = thos kaam jisse fayda logon tak pahunche. Misaal National Health Policy 2017 — lakshya: sabko achchhi swasthya seva, sarkari tantra majboot, samaanta-sasta-jawabdeh, rokthaam aur badhaava. Programmes: swasthya tantra majboot, sabko vyaapak seva, preventive-promotive, digital health. Schemes: Ayushman Bharat–PMJAY, Health and Wellness Centres, Mission Indradhanush, Janani Suraksha Yojana. Yaad rakho: policy disha, programme raasta, scheme kadam.

Khelo Bharat Niti 2025 — taal-mel ki misaal

Khel ko rashtra-nirmaan ka saadhan maankar 2025 mein Khelo Bharat Niti. Lakshya: block se rashtriya star tak khel sanskriti. Kendra — neeti dhaancha, lakshya, funding. Rajya — 'Model Sports State', rashtriya khel aayojan, PPP se infrastructure. Zila — 'Model Sports District'. Block — niyamit pratiyogita, pratibha khoj. Khel sansthaan — coaching, upkaran. Private — sponsorship, poshan-upkaran research. Nagrik/parivaar — bachchon, khaaskar betiyon ko protsaahan, feedback. Safalta = sabka taal-mel. Tum — vote (aage chalkar), kaanoon paalan, shikaayat, bhaagidaari, feedback se shaasan sudhaar sakte ho!

Personal finance ke 5 khambhe

1) Income — jo paisa milta hai (vetan, vyapar). 2) Budgeting — kitna bachaana, kitna kharchna, zaroorat-ichchha par kaise. 3) Saving — bhavishya ya aapaat ke liye alag rakhna. 4) Investing — bachat ko share, mutual fund jaise assets mein lagaana. 5) Protection & risk management — beema aur karz prabandh se paise ki raksha.

Khambha 1: Aamdani (Income)

Saamaan bechne ya seva dene ke badle milne wala paisa — shuruaati bindu; bina aamdani na kharch, na bachat, na nivesh. Misaalein: teacher ka maasik vetan, dukaandaar ka munaafa, makaan ka kiraya, bank jama par byaaj, share-dhaarak ka dividend. College mein 'Earn While You Learn' — part-time, internship, apprenticeship se padhaai ke saath kamaai aur anubhav.

Khambha 2: Budget banaao

Budget = aamdani aur kharch ka likhit santulit plan. Lakshya tay karne ke 4 sawaal: kya chahiye? kitna paisa? kab tak? kaunse kadam? 4 steps: 1) Saare kharch likho — chhote-bade. 2) Bachat lakshya — jaise 6 mahine mein ₹3,000 ki cycle → ₹500/mahina. 3) Kharch track karo — budget se tulna. 4) Samiksha aur badlaav — kiraya badha, dawa ka achaanak kharch aaya to budget badlo. Fayda: faltu kharch (baar-baar bahar khaana, impulse shopping) par lagaam, chaadar dekhkar pair failaao.

Khambha 3: Bachat (Saving)

Aamdani ka hissa bhavishya ke liye alag rakhna — samay ke saath jama arthik adhikta. Kyon? Zindagi anishchit, aapaat kabhi bhi; aur aage nivesh (FD, share, mutual fund, zameen-makaan) ke liye. Visheshagya kehte: 'Pehle bachao, bacha hua kharcho' — Kharch = Aamdani − Bachat. ₹1,00,000 mein se pehle ₹20,000 bachaaye to ₹80,000 hi kharch ke liye. Kam aamdani wala bhi thoda-thoda bacha sakta hai!

Khambha 4: Nivesh — Priya vs Sameer

Dono ko ₹500 mile. Priya ne gullak mein rakhe — saal baad bhi ₹500. Sameer ne 8% byaaj wali yojana mein — saal baad ₹540. Dono zimmedaar, par Sameer ka paisa badh raha hai. Investing = bachat ko assets mein lagaana taaki mulya bache aur return mile — jaise aam ka ped lagaana: phal turant nahi, par saalon tak milta. Nakad mein liquidity zyada (turant istemaal), par nivesh mehngaai ko maat deta hai.

Mehngaai aur khareed-shakti

Inflation (mehngaai) = samay ke saath cheezon ke daam badhna. School bag aaj ₹1,000, mehngaai 6% → agle saal ~₹1,060. Paisa ghar mein pada raha to utne mein bag nahi aayega — purchasing power ghat gayi! Purchasing power = ek rupaye se kitna maal-seva khareed sakte ho; yahi jeevan-star tay karta. Liquidity = asset kitni jaldi aur aasaani se bina mulya ghataaye nakad mein badle.

Compounding — jaldi shuru karo!

Compounding = return par bhi return — kamaai phir se nivesh hokar aur kamaai karti (Class 7 ki raja-saadhu ki kahaani yaad karo). Bhavisha aur Dhruv — dono har saal ₹1,000, 7% byaaj. Bhavisha ne 25 saal ki umr se shuru kiya, Dhruv ne 35 se. 65 ki umr tak: Bhavisha ₹1,99,635, Dhruv sirf ₹94,460 — lagbhag 40% hi! Sirf 10 saal ki der ne aadhe se zyada nuksaan kiya. Graph shuru mein chapta, baad mein teekha — kyonki return par return ka asar samay ke saath badhta. Formula: A = P(1 + r)ⁿ.

Nivesh ke 4 aam vikalp

a) Fixed Deposit (FD) — bank/post office mein ek-musht raashi tay samay (1, 3, 5 saal) ke liye, tay byaaj; sabse surakshit, return nishchit par kam. Sarkari vikalp: PPF, NSC, Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana. b) Bonds — sarkar ya company ko udhaar dena; niyamit byaaj; ₹10,000 ka 5% bond → saal mein ₹500, maturity par ₹10,500; avadhi 1 din se 30+ saal; sarkari bond corporate se surakshit. c) Shares/Equity — company mein hissedaari; dividend + daam badhne ka laabh; risk sabse zyada, lambi avadhi mein return bhi sabse zyada. BSE SENSEX — Bombay Stock Exchange ki 30 badi companies ka suchkaank; chhote samay mein upar-neeche, lambe samay mein badhta. d) Mutual Fund — kai niveshakon ka paisa milakar professional fund manager share-bond ki 'tokri' mein lagaata; portfolio diversification se risk kam; 'Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks…'!

Khambha 5: Beema (Insurance)

Bimaari, durghatna, aamdani jaana, sampatti nuksaan — anishchitta. Insurance = kaanooni anubandh: tum niyamit premium do, company tay nuksaan ki bharpaai kare. Misaal: parivaar ₹500/mahina health insurance → aspataal ka bada kharch company bhare. Siddhant — risk sharing: bahut log premium ek saajhe kosh mein, nuksaan hone par madad. Prakaar: Health (aspataal, dawa, jaanch; garib parivaaron ke liye Ayushman Bharat–PMJAY); Life (kamaane wale ki mrityu par parivaar ko sahara — kharch, padhaai, karz); General — motor, property (aag, chori, baadh, bhookamp), crop (aapda, keede, daam), cattle (pashu ki mrityu/viklangta).

Udhaar aur EMI — samajhdaari se

Udhaar utpaadak kaam ke liye achchha — jaise education loan. Faltu saamaan ke liye udhaar → karz ka bojh. Zimmedaar udhaar = sirf zaroorat par, aur samay par chukaana. EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) = loan chukaane ki tay maasik kisht — mool (principal) + byaaj. ₹30,000 ka fridge → ₹3,000 x 12 mahine = ₹36,000 — yani ₹6,000 byaaj! Phone, bike, ghar, car — EMI aam. Bahut saari EMI = bachat aur aapaat-kosh khatre mein. Debt = kisi ka bakaaya paisa.

Risk aur Return — 4 baatein socho

Nivesh se pehle: Risk (paisa doobne ki sambhavna), Return (munaafa), Time horizon (kitne samay tak paisa lagaaye rakh sakte ho), Liquidity (kitni jaldi nakad mein badle). Niyam: kam risk → kam return; zyada risk → zyada sambhavit return (risk-return trade-off). Seedhi: FD (kam risk, sthir kam return) → Mutual fund (zyada) → Shares (sabse zyada risk aur sambhavit return). Chunaav apne lakshya, risk sehne ki kshamata aur paise ki zaroorat ke samay se karo.

Tax — desh ke liye yogdaan

Tax = sarkar ko anivaarya bhugtaan — school, aspataal, transport, raksha ke liye. Direct tax — seedha aamdani/munaafe par (income tax). Indirect tax — saamaan-seva ke daam mein juda (GST). Personal income tax — ek financial year (1 April – 31 March) ki aamdani par; ek seema se kam kamaane wale chhoot. Progressive taxation — aamdani badhe to dar bhi badhe; asamaanta kam. Slab Union Budget mein Vitt Mantralaya prastaavit karta; CBDT (Central Board of Direct Taxes) prashasan dekhta. Imaandaari se samay par tax = nagrik kartavya.

Naya Tax Regime (FY 2025–26) + Priya ka hisaab

Slabs: ₹4 lakh tak — 0; 4–8 lakh — 5%; 8–12 lakh — 10%; 12–16 lakh — 15%; 16–20 lakh — 20%; 20–24 lakh — 25%; 24 lakh se upar — 30%. Priya (business analyst) ki aamdani ₹14,00,000. Step 1: Standard deduction ₹75,000 ghatao → taxable ₹13,25,000. Step 2: pehle ₹4 lakh = 0; agle ₹4 lakh x 5% = ₹20,000; agle ₹4 lakh x 10% = ₹40,000; bache ₹1,25,000 x 15% = ₹18,750. Kul tax = ₹78,750. Har tukde par alag dar — poori aamdani par ek dar nahi! Sawaal: Sai ki taxable aamdani ₹3,80,000 → tax? Shunya (4 lakh se kam).

From Ideas to Startups

Idea se business tak — entrepreneur kaun, Jataka ki 'mare choohe' wali kahaani, Lijjat aur Oberoi, business plan, 4 functional areas, profit-loss, Chettiar aur India ke startups.

Aasaan bhasha mein samjho poora chapter, kahani ki tarah

Pehle ye hissa aaraam se padho — har baat udaharan ke saath samjhaayi gayi hai. Phir neeche short notes se revision karo.

Nimbu paani ka stall — business ki pehli class

School fest mein 'Young Entrepreneurs Challenge' — tumne doston ke saath nimbu paani ka stall lagaaya. Kya-kya sochna pada? Nimbu-cheeni par kitna kharch, glass kahan se, ek glass ka daam kitna, kitne logon ko pasand aaya, munaafa hua ya ghaata? Din ke ant mein teacher ko report di. Yahi hai business ka saar — yojana, sangathan aur karke seekhna. Entrepreneurship (udyamita) = ek vyakti ya samooh ka business idea ko asli udyam mein badalna. Profit = utpaadan aur bikri ka kharch nikaal kar bachi kamaai.

Entrepreneur kya karta hai?

Samasya pehchaanta aur nayi soch se hal karne ki thaan leta hai. Risk leta — paisa aur samay lagaata. Utpaadan ke saadhan jodta (zameen, mazdoor, poonji, technology). Business ke bade faisle leta. Apne innovation se samaaj ka bhala karta. Entrepreneur jigyaasu hote — jahan doosre chunauti dekhein, wahan avsar dekhte. Misaal: traffic nigraani ke liye drone banaane wali company, ya plastic ki jagah jute ke bag.

Mare choohe se dhanvaan — Jataka katha

Ek seth ne raja se kaha — 'mehnat aur dimaag ho to mara chooha bhi daulat ki shuruaat ban sakta hai'. Ek garib yuvak ne suna aur aazmaaya. Mara chooha ek dukaandaar ko uski bhookhi billi ke liye 2 sikkon mein becha → sikkon se phal-paani khareede, thake phool todne wale ko dekar phool liye → phool shehar mein beche → toofan ke baad giri tehniyan saaf karke eendhan becha → ghode ke vyapaariyon ko ghaas → jalpaan ki dukaan → videshi vyapaariyon se sauda. Bahut ameer bana; aabhaar mein seth ko aadhi daulat di aur shehar ke logon ki madad ki. Seekh: avsar pehchaano, smart kaam karo, kritagya raho.

Lijjat Papad — chhat se kraanti tak

1959, Mumbai ki ek chawl ki chhat — 7 grihiniyan, udhaar ka saamaan, ek recipe aur ₹80! Na degree, na investor — bas himmat aur ekta. Factory nahi thi: har mahila subah aata ghar le jaati, din mein papad belti, shaam ko laut aati — shart: quality ek jaisi. Cooperative — har sadasya barabar maalik, munaafa barabar. 1962 mein registration — Shri Mahila Griha Udyog Lijjat Papad ('lijjat' = Gujarati mein swaadisht). 1980–90s mein aata, masala, sabun, export; ₹100 crore paar. 2023: 45,000+ mahila sadasya, 83 shaakhaayein, ₹1,600 crore saalaana turnover; bus suvidha, bachchon ko scholarship. Papad ke saath hal ki — mahilaaon ki aarthik aazaadi!

Mohan Singh Oberoi — ₹50 ke clerk se hotel samrajya

1898, Punjab mein janm; bachpan mein pita gujar gaye. Shimla mein hotel desk clerk — ₹50/mahina. Sirf apna kaam nahi, har cheez seekhi — mehmaan seva, billing, prashasan. 1934 — patni ke gehne girvi (mortgage) rakhkar aur saari bachat lagaakar chhota hotel khareeda — bada risk! Ghaate wale hotel ko safal banaaya; luxury hotel, hotel management school, duniya bhar mein failaav. 2001 — Padma Bhushan; 2002 mein nidhan. Seekh: business sirf idea nahi — kushal staff ki bharti-training (HR), paise ka prabandh (finance), kushal sanchaalan (operations), bharosemand brand (marketing), grahak santushti sabse upar.

Business Plan — udyam ka naqsha

Doston ke saath bakery kholni hai? Pehle business plan — ek likhit dastavez/blueprint jo lakshya aur unhe paane ke kadam bataaye; idea se lekar chalaane tak roadmap. 8 sawaal: 1) Zaroorat aur idea — log kya chaahte, kaunsi samasya hal? (taaza bread, cake, ya whole-wheat jaise healthy?) 2) Sambhavit grahak — bachche, yuva, parivaar? 3) Paisa aur laagat — oven, kiraya, vetan, aata-mewe, packaging; paisa kahan se (bachat, bank loan, dost-rishtedaar, investor)? 4) Jagah — school-college-bazaar ke paas? online/app order? 5) Log aur kaushal — baker, helper, billing, delivery. 6) Daam aur munaafa — laagat nikle, munaafa ho, par grahak ke liye sasta; chhote mini cake jar jaise vikalp. 7) Prachaar — social media, poster, offer, achchhi packaging, grahak seva. 8) Chunautiyan aur risk — competition, kachche maal ka daam, badalti pasand, cash flow, jaldi kharab hone wala inventory.

4 functional areas — business ke khambhe

1) Management & Operations — roz ka kaam: yojana, sangathan, maargdarshan + maal/seva samay par, kushal tareeke se. Bakery: sahi upkaran, saamagri, quality nigraani, roz ka utpaadan bina der-barbaadi. Focus: kushal prakriya. 2) Finance & Accounting — paise ka samajhdaar prabandh; budget, report, cash flow. 3) Human Resources — sahi log, sahi kaushal: bharti, training (hygiene, baking standard), labh, jhagda suljhaana, achchha kaarya-sthal. People are the heart of business! 4) Marketing & Sales — jaagrukta aur bikri: catchy naam, social media photo-video, discount, mausami items (mango cake), online order-delivery, eco-friendly packaging.

Finance: fixed vs variable cost

Finance = paise ki yojana, intezaam aur upyog. Fixed cost — utpaadan kam ho ya zyada, wahi rehta (kam samay mein): kiraya, upkaran (oven, mixer, fridge), beema — 'overhead'. Variable cost — utpaadan ke saath ghatta-badhta: kachcha maal (aata, cheeni), packaging. (Kitaab mein bakery ke liye vetan ko bhi badalne wale kharch mein gina gaya hai.) Investment = bhavishya ki aamdani/munaafe ki ummeed mein paisa/sansaadhan lagaana. Accounting = len-den ko record, vyavasthit aur report karna — aamdani (revenue) aur kharch (expenses) ka hisaab; tax kaanoon ka paalan.

Profit & Loss Account — ABC Bakery

Profit = Total Revenue − Total Expenses. ABC Bakery (March 2026): Aamdani — bread-bun ₹1,20,000 + pastry-cookie ₹75,000 + cake order ₹85,000 + delivery ₹15,000 = ₹2,95,000. Kharch — kachcha maal ₹1,00,000, packaging ₹12,000, kiraya ₹40,000, vetan ₹30,000, bijli-gas ₹15,000, prachaar ₹8,000, vividh ₹7,000, loan byaaj ₹10,000 = ₹2,22,000. Profit before tax (PBT) = 2,95,000 − 2,22,000 = ₹73,000. Tax ₹13,000 → Profit after tax (PAT) = ₹60,000. (Categories ratne ki zaroorat nahi — tareeka samjho!)

Chettiar — paaramparik udyami samudaay

India mein udyamita ki jaden gehri — kaushal aur pesha peedhi-dar-peedhi. Nattukottai Chettiar (Tamil Nadu) — vyapaari, banker, saahookar; India ke saath Sri Lanka, Burma (Myanmar), Malaysia, Dakshin-Poorv Asia tak. Pehchaan: naitikta aur bharose par udhaar, behtareen hisaab-kitaab, munaafa phir se vyapar, mandir, shiksha, samaaj kalyan mein. Namak, chaawal, kapda aur vitt ka vyapar — aksar bina formal bank ke! Seekh: risk, anushaasan, lambi soch.

Startup — asafalta bhi seekh hai

Startup = naya, shuruaati daur ki company jo nayaa (innovative) utpaad/seva laaye. Kuch tezi se badhte, kuch band ho jaate — imaandaar koshish ke baad asafalta mein koi buraai nahi; har koshish seekh deti. Swami Vivekananda: 'Utho, jaago aur lakshya praapt hone tak ruko mat'. Aaj India duniya ka teesra sabse bada startup ecosystem; 2025 mein lagbhag 120 unicorn (1 billion dollar se zyada mulya). Kshetra: e-commerce, fin-tech (digital payment), health-tech, agri-tech. Hub: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR. Yojana: Startup India — Atmanirbhar Bharat (2020) ki soch; lakshya Viksit Bharat 2047 (aazaadi ke 100 saal).

Business samaaj ko kya deta hai?

Rozgaar aur aamdani parivaaron ko. Maal-seva ka utpaadan → arthik gatividhi. Tax → sadak, school, aspataal. Innovation → vikas aur nayi samasyaaon ke hal. Jeevan-star sudhaar — dawa, transport, technology. CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) — shiksha, paryavaran, swasthya. Ek aur misaal: 1931, Digambar aur Govind Dandekar ne South Mumbai mein ink powder se shuru kiya, purani kaanch ki botalon mein syaahi; artists ke liye achchhe rang nahi the → Glasgow jaakar colour chemistry padhi, lab banaayi → pencil, crayon, geometry box, paint — mashhoor brand bana. Agla udyami shaayad tumhari class mein baitha hai!

C. Short-answer (model answers)

  1. 1Social Science ko 'jude hue subjects ka parivaar' kyon kehte hain? Sookhe ki misaal do.

    Samaaj itna ulajha hai ki ek subject poora nahi samjha sakta. Sookha fasal (paryavaran), kisaan ki kamaai (arthvyavastha), sarkari raahat (raajneeti), palaayan (samaaj) aur paani bachaane ke purane tareeke (sanskriti) — sabko ek saath chhoota hai.

  2. 2Rule of Law loktantra ke liye kyon zaroori hai?

    Koi bhi kaanoon se upar nahi; jhagde adaalat mein 'procedure established by law' se suljhte, dande ya pehchaan se nahi (Art 21). Isse taaqat ka durupyog rukta, adhikaar surakshit rehte aur sarkar par bharosa banta.

  3. 3FPTP, Majority aur PR system mein farak batao.

    FPTP — sabse zyada vote wala jeete (50% zaroori nahi). Majority — 50%+ chahiye, warna top-2 mein doosra round. PR — log party ko vote dete, seats vote ke anupaat mein.

  4. 4Opportunity cost ko PPC ki madad se samjhaao.

    Kisaan jau badhaaye to gehun ghatta (A 0–100 se E 100–0). Zyada jau ke liye chhoda gaya gehun hi jau ki opportunity cost. Curve neeche jhukti; har bindu par saadhan ka poora kushal upyog.

  5. 5Market equilibrium asli duniya mein sthir kyon nahi rehta?

    Technology, mazdoori, byaaj, yuddh, mahamaari, mausam demand-supply badalte rehte. COVID mein mask ki maang uchhli, daam badhe, phir supply badhi to gire. Goa hotel ka kiraya ₹1,500 se ₹25,000 tak badalta — dynamic market.

  6. 6Mewar aur Ahom ki resistance mein kya samaan tha?

    Dono ne apni bhoogol ka faayda uthaaya — Mewar ne Aravalli mein guerrilla yuddh (Bhil saathi), Ahom ne jungle-nadi-monsoon aur tez 'bachari' naavein. Dono ne sandhi todi ya apni shartein manwaayin aur aazaadi bachaayi.

  7. 7Bhakti parampara ne resilience kaise dikhaayi?

    Sthaaniya bhaashaon mein bhajan, naam-kirtan jaisi pooja jo todi nahi ja sakti, satsang-sankirtan se samudaay juda; mahilaaon aur sabhi vargon ki bhaagidaari; Tulsidas ne Ramcharitmanas Awadhi mein likh kar Ram katha ghar-ghar pahunchaayi.

  8. 8Policy, programme aur scheme mein farak — National Health Policy 2017 se.

    Policy disha deti (sabko sasti achchhi swasthya seva). Programme raasta (swasthya tantra majboot, digital health). Scheme thos kaam (Ayushman Bharat–PMJAY, Mission Indradhanush, Janani Suraksha Yojana).

  9. 9Jaldi nivesh shuru karna kyon faaydemand hai?

    Compounding — return par return. Bhavisha (25 se) aur Dhruv (35 se) dono ₹1,000/saal 7% par; 65 tak Bhavisha ₹1,99,635, Dhruv sirf ₹94,460. Zyada samay = zyada badhat.

  10. 10Business ke 4 functional areas bakery ki misaal se.

    Operations — upkaran, saamagri, quality, roz ka utpaadan. Finance — fixed (kiraya) aur variable (kachcha maal) kharch, cash flow, P&L. HR — baker, helper bharti aur hygiene training. Marketing — naam, social media, discount, online order.
