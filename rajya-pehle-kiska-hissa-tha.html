Exam me sabse zyada poocha jaane wala sawal: "Ye rajya pehle kiska hissa tha?" Neeche seedhe jawab hain. Pehle chhota sa background, phir poori table, phir sawal-jawab.

Rajya kaise bante gaye? (3 kadam)

  1. 1947-50: Riyasaton ka vilay (Sardar Patel). Dar Commission (1948) aur JVP Committee (1948) ne bhasha ke aadhar par rajya banane se abhi mana kiya.
  2. 1953-56: Andhra (1953) ke baad Rajya Punargathan Aayog (SRC) bana — Fazl Ali, K.M. Panikkar, H.N. Kunzru. 1 Nov 1956 ko 14 rajya aur 6 Union Territory bane.
  3. 1960 ke baad: naye rajya alag-alag wajah se bane — bhasha (Gujarat-Maharashtra 1960), prashasanik/jan-andolan (Haryana 1966, Telangana 2014), aur 2000 ke 3 naye rajya.

Sawal-Jawab (FAQ)

Odisha pehle kiska hissa tha?

Odisha pehle Bihar aur Odisha Province ka hissa tha. 1 April 1936 ko ise alag province banaya gaya — ye bhasha (Odia) ke aadhar par bana pehla prant tha. Naam "Orissa" se "Odisha" 2011 mein hua. Isiliye 1 April ko Utkal Divas manate hain.

Telangana pehle kiska hissa tha?

Telangana pehle Andhra Pradesh ka hissa tha. Ye Hyderabad State ka Telugu-bhashi ilaaka tha, jo 1956 mein Andhra ke saath milkar Andhra Pradesh bana. Lambe andolan ke baad 2 June 2014 ko Telangana alag rajya bana — Bharat ka 29th rajya.

Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh aur Uttarakhand pehle kiske hissa the?

Teeno 2000 mein bane: Chhattisgarh (1 Nov) Madhya Pradesh se, Uttarakhand (9 Nov) Uttar Pradesh se, aur Jharkhand (15 Nov) Bihar se. Kram yaad rakho: 26th, 27th, 28th rajya.

Haryana pehle kiska hissa tha?

Haryana pehle Punjab ka hissa tha. 1 November 1966 ko Punjab Punargathan Act ke tahat Hindi-bhashi ilaake Haryana bane, aur kuch pahadi ilaake Himachal Pradesh ko mile. Chandigarh dono rajyon ki rajdhani aur Union Territory hai.

Gujarat aur Maharashtra pehle kiska hissa the?

Dono Bombay State ke hissa the. Bhasha andolan (Samyukta Maharashtra aur Mahagujarat) ke baad 1 May 1960 ko Maharashtra aur Gujarat alag rajya bane.

Bharat mein bhasha ke aadhar par bana pehla rajya kaunsa tha?

Andhra Pradesh — 1 October 1953 ko Madras State se alag hua, Potti Sriramulu ke 56 din ke anshan (unki maut December 1952) ke baad. Ye parivartan Rajya Punargathan Aayog (1953) ki neev bana.

Rajya Punargathan Aayog (SRC) kya tha?

1953 mein bana aayog, adhyaksh Fazl Ali, sadasya K.M. Panikkar aur H.N. Kunzru. Isne bhasha ke aadhar par rajyon ke punargathan ki sifarish ki. Rajya Punargathan Act 1956 ke baad 1 Nov 1956 ko 14 rajya aur 6 Union Territory bane.

Abhi Bharat mein kitne rajya aur Union Territory hain?

28 rajya aur 8 Union Territory. Telangana (2014) ke baad rajyon ki sankhya 29 hui thi, par 2019 mein Jammu & Kashmir rajya se Union Territory banne par 28 rajya reh gaye.
